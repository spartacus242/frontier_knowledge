## Provisional entries

**UQ-20261017-06** — Train k ≥ 3 sparse-MoE language models differing only in seed: how much of the token-to-expert partition is shared across runs after one-to-one expert matching, at the 1%-of-pretraining checkpoint and at the end, against permutation nulls and against the within-run reference?

Adversary 1 returned **Validated**; the judge did not, and verification explains why neither extreme was right. The cap was an opened 2026 paper, arXiv:2604.14419 "Equifinality", whose introduction claims "Cross-seed analysis reveals 500x-above-random functional overlap through entirely different weight parameterizations" — read as limb (i) in substance, with the method sitting in sections that would not serve. Those sections were served this time. The 500× is a best-match Jaccard between experts' **top-20 projected-vocabulary word sets** (0.15 Wide, 0.13 Deep, against a ~0.0003 null), matched "best-match across 1024 experts" with no one-to-one constraint, at **converged checkpoints only**, never related to loss — an output-side function overlap, not a routing-partition agreement. The companion arXiv:2604.14434 carries no cross-seed statistic at all, and arXiv:2607.28308's "expert subspaces overlap substantially" is a within-model measurement, so the adversaries' use of it to pre-empt a branch is struck. Promotion condition (a) is discharged in full; what remains is that ARI/NMI are undefined on a top-8 multi-assignment until a reduction is declared, and limb (iv) is not estimable at k ≥ 3 — one adversary showed this with its own 20,000-draw simulation (null R² mean 0.499 at n=3, p=1; R² identically 1.000 at n=3, p=2; below 0.10 only at n ≥ 11). Well-posedness 2/3 bars Validated on its own.

**UQ-20261017-07** — For one Common Crawl pool re-filtered at a matched keep rate by classifiers whose labels come from different annotator models, crossed with ≥3 architecture arms at matched FLOPs: how large is the annotator × arm interaction against within-cell seed variance, and how many pairwise architecture contrasts reverse sign?

Adversary 1 returned **Validated** here too. Its one Unresolved attack held that the candidate is the unrun cell of two public factorials — DataDecide's data axis crossed with arXiv:2609.11917's architecture axis. Two readers opened DataDecide. The three recipe names the record and the adversary both relied on are real, and the set they were said to form is not: QC Orig 10% and QC Tulu 10% share the Falcon+CC pool and a 10% rate but differ in the classifier's **training data**, while QC FW 10% sits on a different pool; the 25 recipes "differ in sources, deduplication, and filtering" at thresholds of 3/7/10/20%; the word "teacher" never appears; and seeds are not 3 across the ladder — "All 1B (target size) models have 3 full reruns with different seeds, while other model sizes have second and third seed runs that are terminated early after 25% of the target compute budget." The second parent crosses architecture with data **repetition**, not quality. Neither supplies the annotator axis, so the kill is not sourceable and attack 3 drops to Partial — which also restores both of the record's "Not Low" grounds that the entry had conceded were false.

With no Unresolved attack, 3/3 well-posedness and 2/3 significance, this came within one clause of the ledger's first Validated. Gate 5 requires novelty "≥ Medium **with the complete search record**", and the record is not complete — five items unread, one of them arXiv:2605.19407, whose abstract leaves an architecture axis possible. Gate 5 separately names this entry's exact case as Provisional: "Medium novelty with a close neighbor", and there are five. The objection now carried is the design's own power: an exact 95% interval for the variance ratio spanning ~285× at the declared minimum and >10× at 3×5×3, which is 45 matched-FLOP pretraining runs.

## Rejected entries

**UQ-20261017-09** (agent-run rework share) — rejected, on ground the batch never reached. Its single adversary killed it as a disguised known on two papers. Both were opened: arXiv:2512.02393 measures **actions** ("Number of times the agent decides to repeat a previously executed action"), defines no inefficiency rate, and the words *token*, *dollar* and *waste* do not appear in it; arXiv:2607.22585 reports tokens — not dollars — per solved task and presents no re-ranking. Asked directly, the checker answered **no** to both papers on both of the candidate's deliverables. The kill is withdrawn, and with it the Gate 2 regrade to Low.

What replaced it is worse for the candidate. Its Gate 1 reformulation — the one permitted — deliberately swapped the decidable rule ("no later step reads it") for counterfactual non-use, then proposed to apply it to released trace archives. Neither half survives. Every published step-attribution method obtains the counterfactual by **re-executing** the agent (arXiv:2606.08275, arXiv:2605.25338, arXiv:2608.19760), and none implements removal from later context, the exact intervention the rule names. And the predicate is false in the baseline: arXiv:2602.07150 (ICLR 2026, ~60,000 SWE-Bench-Verified trajectories, 10 runs per configuration) finds single-run pass@1 varying "by 2.2 to 6.0 percentage points depending on which run is selected", SDs above 1.5 points at temperature 0, and trajectories diverging at median token 56 and token 32 for two models — with a temperature-0 pair on django-9296 matching for 93 tokens, diverging at token 94, and ending in opposite outcomes. An ablated re-run therefore shows changed later actions whatever the removed step contributed. Attack 4 Unresolved is fatal; attacks 5, 7 and 8 are Unresolved alongside it, and well-posedness drops from 2/3 to 1/3. A second sweep also found four nearer 2026 papers the batch missed (arXiv:2509.23586, arXiv:2607.12161, arXiv:2608.01347, arXiv:2604.22750), with arXiv:2606.01365 unopened and recorded as Unverified.
