**2. So the kill needs an affirmative finding: a sentence in which the authors state that they isolated, adjusted for, or otherwise accounted for automatic adaptation. If the paper contains no such sentence it measures a quantity **without the clause that distinguishes the candidate**, and the candidate survives however clean the numbers are. Absence of such a sentence is the likely case — a clinical-management paper reporting what clinicians changed and what happened next is not usually built as a causal-identification paper — but likely is not established, and a guess is not recorded here as a finding.
