Domain(s): Biology — cell-culture methodology and in vitro model fidelity (non-clinical basic biology). Adjacent: comparative thermal physiology; measurement-condition specification and standards (GCCP 2.0, ALTEX, PRINCE); metascience of model-system external validity.

Question (canonical): For K >= 3 cell types pre-declared from tissues whose in vivo temperature is distinctly non-37 °C (at least one cooler and one warmer), in a 2x2 factorial crossing tissue-matched vs 37 °C temperature with physioxic vs atmospheric oxygen, with proliferation-rate-matched controls and a distance metric to a freshly isolated in vivo reference profile both declared before any data are taken: what fraction of the baseline in vitro-to-in vivo distance is closed by temperature correction, reported per cell type with confidence intervals alongside the fraction closed by oxygen correction measured on the same scale and in the same experiment, and the temperature x oxygen interaction?

Alternative formulations:
- V1, as received at intake (one Gate 1 reformulation was used; V2 above is the graded version): for cell types native to tissues whose in vivo temperature differs materially from 37 °C, how much of the phenotypic discordance between cultured cells and their in vivo counterparts is attributable to the universal 37 °C culture default? Reformulated because "how much is attributable" named no estimand, reference or metric; no change of subject matter.
- Is the standard 37 °C incubator a mis-specified condition for cells from cooler and warmer tissues, and how far do their transcriptomes, proliferation and differentiation move toward in vivo profiles at tissue-matched temperature?
- Does cell culture have a temperature problem of the same size as its documented oxygen problem?

Motivating gap: Silence mapping, not an anomaly. The oxygen default carries a named mis-specification critique (physioxia; Alva et al. 2022 calls the commonly cited 20-21% O2 figures "incorrect"), while the temperature default is dense in how-answers (incubator specification, calibration, QC logging) and near-empty of why-questions, though in vivo tissue temperature spans roughly 32-40 °C. Verified from sources opened: Klein et al. 2022 gives tissue-matched O2 values (13% lung, 1-3% uterus) and no tissue-matched temperature values, writing "It is implicitly assumed that culture temperature is controlled at 37°C" and that thermal regimes "may be reliably inferred from calibrated incubators" (quotes independently re-verified verbatim by adversaries 1 and 3); GCCP 2.0 files temperature under calibration/monitoring/records with thresholds framed as heat shock >39 °C and slowed replication <35 °C, and calls room air "hyperoxic, hypocapnic, and cold for cells in culture", taking 37 °C as the zero point.

Presuppositions:
1. Source-tissue in vivo temperature known to ~1 °C in the relevant state — CONTESTED for deep organs and across diurnal/activity variation; partially supported for the reproductive tract (Grinsted 1985; Bahat 2003/Hunter 1986, via Cochrane CD012192).
2. 37 °C is the near-universal default — UNVERIFIED as a number and now CONTRAINDICATED: no protocol-corpus survey exists (record query 9; adversary queries 1/8), and the only corpus datum in play, from the record's own load-bearing source, points softer — Klein et al. 2022: "42% reported temperature and CO2 incubator settings" and "a number of studies used different culture temperatures". Applied subfields have already moved off it (broiler SC standard is 38 °C; Hanwoo SC tested at 39 °C).
3. Cultured cells retain temperature-responsive programmes — SUPPORTED (Metzger et al. 2022, genome-wide transcript and bioenergetic responses at 35/39/41 vs 37 °C).
4. An in vivo reference profile exists against which discordance is measurable — LOAD-BEARING, weakest link, and now damaged: van den Brink et al. 2017 (Nat Methods 14:935-936, opened by adversary 3) documents the reference-side artefact as an HSP/IEG signature ("Fos expression is induced during the SC isolation procedure"; "Hspa1b is also higher expressed in 2-hour collagenase-treated than in 1-hour") whose validated fixes are selections on a stress phenotype (MitoTracker-high, FSC-H gating).
5. Temperature effects separable from the proliferation-rate change temperature causes — MAJOR CONFOUNDER; V2 requires rate-matched controls (GCCP 2.0: "growth below 35°C typically slows replication").
6. Incubators hold alternative setpoints without altering pH or dissolved gas — FEASIBILITY, contested: CO2/bicarbonate pH equilibrium is temperature-dependent, so pH must be instrumented, not assumed.
7. "Discordance attributable to temperature" is estimable as a distance reduction under a pre-declared metric — accepted only as a convention.
8. Media formulated at 37 °C do not become limiting at other temperatures — CONFOUNDER, unchecked; physiological-media x oxygen factorials already exist, so media is a live third factor.

What would count as an answer: Per cell type, the fraction of baseline in vitro-to-in vivo distance closed by tissue-matched temperature with CIs, reported beside the fraction closed by physioxia on the same scale in the same experiment, plus the interaction. A negligible temperature share beside a large oxygen share retires the question. A comparable or larger share, or a strong interaction, makes culture temperature a per-cell-type specification the standards and reporting-checklist community would have to justify rather than inherit. Genuine asymmetry: the oxygen side of that comparison does not exist either — Alva et al. 2022 is narrative with no pooled effect size or attribution estimate, and three independent answer-side sweeps (record query 6; adversary 1 query 7; adversary 2 query 4; adversary 3 query 1) found no variance share or distance-closed fraction for temperature or oxygen — so the scale would have to be built by the same experiment.

Significance — consequence: If the temperature share is comparable to or larger than oxygen's, or the interaction is strong, culture temperature becomes a per-cell-type specification rather than an inherited constant — GCCP 2.0, PRINCE and repository datasheets would each have to carry a justified temperature, and the published phenotype of every cool- or warm-tissue cell type acquires a named, estimable bias term. If negligible, the inheritance is vindicated cheaply and the question retires, while the same experiment still yields the first attribution estimate for oxygen, which does not exist. A null is not a wasted experiment.

(ii) The physioxia constituency (Stuart lab; Klein/Duarte), handed a second condition on their own scale.
