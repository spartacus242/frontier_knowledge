HALF ONE, limb (a) as a measured known. Prior A is real, open, correctly attributed and readable at both routes the entry names, so Gate 2's failure to find it was a retrieval failure and not an absence. The repeat-run control the record said no source supplies DOES exist and IS printed - "running GPT-3.5 March twice yields a disagreement rate of 2.8%" and "running GPT-3.5 June twice yields disagreement rate of 7.0%", Section 3.4 body text, each returned whole - and that much of the kill stands. But those two figures cannot carry the rejection on four counts. They are point estimates with no interval, no repeat count and no stated uncertainty, so they are not a BAND. They cover GPT-3.5 on OpinionQA alone, with nothing for GPT-4 and nothing for the other seven evaluation sets. They are measured where the paper says "We set the temperature to be 0.1 to reduce output randomness", so they are a floor under a deliberately chosen test setting rather than an operating-condition spread. And decisively, the design is "one snapshotted in March 2023 and another in June 2023" of the SAME MODEL NAME, both live and queryable - which is why both could be queried at all. There is no predecessor, no vendor-designated successor and no retirement anywhere in it, and no API identifier strings appear in either version. On how a retired predecessor stands to its designated successor the paper is SILENT, and silence is not support. Secondary corrections from the same check: the USMLE pair (27.9% GPT-3.5, 12.2% GPT-4, Section 3.7) and the happy-numbers pair (67.6% GPT-4, 77.2% GPT-3.5, Section 3.2) hold as recorded; OpinionQA's 27% is the sentence and 27.5% is a Figure 8(a) label; the LangChain per-model pair is withdrawn above; the paper's only scope-limiting sentence is in Section 2.2, not its conclusions; and claim (e) is a clean absence - no share of published conclusions reversing, in either version, the unit of analysis being the item and "mismatch" being a definition rather than an evaluation.
