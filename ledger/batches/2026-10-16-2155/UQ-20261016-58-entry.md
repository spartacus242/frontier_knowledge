Domain(s): Biology — invertebrate molecular biology / RNA quality metrology; biodiversity-genomics sample QC (EBP, Darwin Tree of Life). Method-owning neighbours: measurement metrology (calibration transfer, commutability), screening-bias epidemiology (verification bias by subgroup), psychometric measurement non-invariance.

Question (canonical): In a defined frame of RNA submissions carrying a recorded integrity score and a documented accept/reject/re-extract decision, with each source taxon assigned a 28S hidden-break status, (a) what is the break-free vs break-bearing difference in recorded integrity score and in per-sample rejection and re-extraction rate, adjusted for preservation route, tissue, protocol, input mass and body size with denominators stated; and (b) in matched RNA from one break-bearing and one break-free species degraded in the same graded series, what is the mapping RIN_break(d) → RIN_free(d) at equal true degradation d measured independently (DV200, spike-in fragment length, library 3' bias), and therefore what numeric correction does a RIN ≥ 7-type gate require in a break-bearing taxon? (c, secondary/exploratory) After conditioning on research effort, body size, collection difficulty and preservation, is break status associated with whether a taxon has any transcriptome at all?
(Gate 1 reformulation; one reformulation used, for well-posedness only. Gate 0 intake wording and the full limb specification are in batches/2026-10-16-2155/UQ-20261016-58-record.md.)

Alternative formulations:
- Has a vertebrate-trained RNA-quality metric been quietly selecting which invertebrates get sequenced?
- What is the mapping between RIN and actual RNA degradation in taxa whose 28S migrates as a single peak, and what has the absence of that mapping cost in rejected samples and missing transcriptomes?
(Adversary 3's attack 8 recommends the first wording NOT be carried as canonical: as a corpus-level causal claim it decomposes into clade-level confounds.)

Motivating gap: Anomaly mining. The hidden break is published and its QC consequence has now been asserted in print at least four times and measured never. Natsidis et al. 2019 (Sci Rep 9:19477) — RIN "will be consistently underestimated in the great majority of animal species with a break", training set "obtained from species without a hidden break", "an alternative to the standard RIN ... would be a valuable development" — computes no RIN value and measures no QC outcome. Casanova Gomez et al. 2026 (PeerJ 14:e20693) recommends "rRNA-aware quality control" with no RIN values, rates or metric. Deleo et al. 2018 (Biol Methods Protoc 3:bpy012) reports RINs "could not be estimated for a majority" of split-28S samples. McCarthy et al. 2015 (PeerJ 3:e1436) uses RIN only as an incidental ">8.0" descriptor. Found late by adversary 2: Agilent application note 5991-7903EN (Fabrick & Hull 2017) — 12 insect species, RIN uncomputable for 5, values 2.4–7.3 for the other 7, "the validity of RIN for insect total RNA was not proven", no break-free arm and RIN not reported on its own degradation series.

Presuppositions:
1. Break status assignable per taxon. ESTABLISHED in silico (Natsidis 2019: break in 17/19 testable protostome phyla); CONTESTED in detail — adversary 3 verified calls are made by eye from coverage drops, several phyla left uncertain, and only for species with ≥1 Gb existing RNA-Seq; Casanova Gomez 2026 (read by adversary 3) confirms graded, not binary, profile classes.
2. QC outcomes recoverable from facility/consortium logs or public submission metadata. REFUTED for the public-metadata fallback: adversary 1 read ERGA sample-manifest SOP v2.5.1 — no RIN/RQN/DV200 field, no rejection or re-collection record. Limb (a) must be a private-LIMS study.
3. Integrity thresholds functioned as accept/reject gates, not advisory numbers. UNESTABLISHED and now with direct counter-evidence: Sanger ToL core-lab paper over ~2,000 species (bioRxiv 2025.04.11.648334, read by adversary 2) names no integrity metric — Qubit/Nanodrop, one numeric gate on yield ("over the 100 ng input requirement"); DToL protocols.io RNA SOP (adversary 3) says only "the required QC"; Cornell TREx (adversary 1) uses RQN<7 as a protocol switch to ribo-depletion, not a gate; Reichel et al. 2026 Mol Ecol Resour 26:e70100 calls RIN ≥ 8 a "rule of thumb" and tells submitters not to apply rigid cut-offs to invertebrates.
4. Confounder-adjusted break-bearing vs break-free comparison possible. CONTESTED and, per adversary 3, near-fatal for limb (c): the break is homologous across protostomes and absent in deuterostomes, so the contrast is close to "protostome vs not" and confounded with body size, tissue, preservation and field logistics.
5. A reference measure of true degradation independent of the 28S peak exists (DV200, spike-in fragment length, 3' bias). ESTABLISHED within one matrix; never run across break status.
6. (Gate 1) Heat/denaturation artefacts can mimic a break-type profile, so denaturing treatment must be held fixed or limb (a) measures protocol, not biology.

What would count as an answer: (i) from facility or consortium QC logs, the integrity-score distribution and the rejection/re-extraction rate for break-bearing vs break-free taxa with denominators stated and preservation/tissue/protocol fixed or adjusted; (ii) a graded-degradation calibration experiment on matched RNA from one break-bearing and one break-free species, RIN regressed on an independent degradation axis, yielding the equivalence mapping and the numeric threshold correction. A credible null — break status unrelated to rejection once effort, preservation and denaturing protocol are accounted for, because facilities already compensate informally — is a real answer, not a failure.

Verification (post-batch, fresh-context checker, log UQ-20261016-58-verification.md): the cap on this entry is CONFIRMED, from documents rather than from argument, and it tells the candidate which limb to keep.
Of the operational documents opened, NONE mentions RIN or RQN at all. The Sanger Tree of Life core-laboratory paper (bioRxiv 2025.04.11.648334) checks RNA with Qubit and Nanodrop and gates on total yield only (over 100 ng); samples that fail are retried with a different individual, a different tissue, or more input. The Sanger manual TRIzol protocol and the sample-triage protocol contain no RIN, RQN or 28S text at all; their only numeric flags are A230/280 below 1.6 for a partially dissolved pellet and a tissue-weight rule. The EBP standards page reached was an abstract-only landing page with no RNA-integrity text. No programme or core-facility document opened acknowledges the hidden-break problem or tells submitters what to do about it.
So the primary limb — a break-correlated excess of rejections and re-extractions — has no documented decision channel in the very programmes the candidate names: in these documents the integrity score is not the gate, yield is. That is what holds the entry at Provisional, and it is now an evidenced finding rather than an adversary's suspicion. It also points at the limb worth keeping: the equivalence mapping (what RIN in a break-bearing taxon corresponds to the same true degradation state as a given RIN in a break-free taxon) needs no decision channel at all, and nothing opened measures it.
Standing exposure variable, unchanged: Natsidis, Schiffer, Salvador-Martinez and Telford 2019 (Sci Rep 9:19477) computes break status across eukaryotes and leaves the corrected metric as a printed desideratum; Deleo et al. 2018 reports the 28S gap deletion being misread as a degraded profile in about 90% of the species they sampled.
UNVERIFIED and listed as promotion conditions, not as checks that passed: the full EBP standards report (PMC blocked), the Sanger "RNA Decision Making Tree" appendix, the Darwin Tree of Life sample manifests, and generic core-facility submission thresholds.
