Domain(s): Other STEM — measurement science and standardisation (analytical chemistry, petroleum and materials testing). Method field: statistics of variance components (ISO 5725, ASTM E691/D6300).
Question (canonical): Over a frame F of (standard test method, material class) pairs declared before any data pull and admitted only when (a) two or more independent interlaboratory precision studies of the SAME designated method exist on materials matching a declared rule, and (b) each study's laboratory count p, material count m and replicate structure n are recoverable from a public or purchasable document, what is the distribution of log(R2/R1) of the two published reproducibility limits, and does its realized spread exceed the pair-specific spread implied by the sampling distribution of each study's own s_R at its own (p, m, n)? Pairs failing (b) are enumerated as a reported coverage gap rather than dropped.
[Adopted wording is the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation (R1 in the record); the Gate 0 intake wording V0 is listed below. Second limb: the joint distribution of (age of the study behind the precision statement, p, m) over an enumerated frame of currently active methods.]
Alternative formulations: (1) V0 intake wording — restricted to methods with two or more independent interlaboratory studies on comparable materials, is the published reproducibility limit R itself reproducible? (2) Is R a property of the test method, or of the laboratories, materials and decade that happened to be in its one interlaboratory study? (3) If the study behind a currently active precision statement were re-run today, by how much would r and R change, and in which direction?
Motivating gap: A published R is reused far outside its one study — accreditation uncertainty budgets, specification limits, PT scoring limits — while the study behind it is run once, with few laboratories, on the materials and instruments of its era. The intake framing ("nobody has tested this") is withdrawn by the record: the per-method version is a published International Standard (ISO 4259-3:2020) and ASTM D7372 defines a published-vs-achieved index. What survives is the aggregate: no source opened by the generator or by three adversaries prints a cross-method ratio distribution.

Significance — audience: accreditation bodies and assessed laboratories deriving uncertainty from method-performance data; PT providers setting scoring SDs from published precision; standards committees deciding when a precision statement needs re-study (ASTM D02/D04/E11, ISO TC28, TAPPI, AOAC, AASHTO); specification writers and dispute arbitration. Strongest remaining objection: Outcome-based selection into the frame. The only re-study instance anyone opened (ASTM WK89836, D5442) was commissioned because the existing precision "was unsatisfactory according to requirements of ASTM D6300", and WK67628 replaces a single-material statement found deficient — so F is enriched for pairs whose first R was already judged inadequate. If that judgement is outcome-based, first studies with a low-by-chance s_R1 are preferentially re-studied, log(R2/R1) is shifted positive under the null, and zero-centred design-implied intervals read a selection artefact as instability. This objection's own vulnerability, recorded because nobody has measured it: it follows from the DESIGN of the re-study trigger, not from any reported result — adversary 3 labels the shift "my own reasoning, not sourced", no opened page says whether the D6300 judgement is design-based (p or df minima) or outcome-based (achieved vs published), and no published R2/R1 exists against which the shift could be checked; the single unopened source most likely to settle it is the current edition of ASTM D6300 (-20a/-23a store pages returned to two adversaries and opened by neither; only the 2003/04 text was read, and in that edition there is no clause comparing new precision with previously published precision). The related C5 weakness is ALGEBRAIC, not empirical or bitwise: s_R^2 is a combination of two mean squares, so a chi-square interval at (p, m, n) is an approximation rather than an identity, and no primary source on its adequacy was opened by anyone. Which leg the verdict stands on: the Provisional stands on well-posedness 2/3 — the frame-non-empty presupposition is unverified after 39 queries and the design-field premise is partly falsified — and that leg would survive intact if both the selection objection and the Disguise residuals fell. The selection objection and the published-R-vs-research-report mismatch (WK91528) are the legs that would then matter; they wound the inferential limb and the decomposition, not the first-limb distribution.
