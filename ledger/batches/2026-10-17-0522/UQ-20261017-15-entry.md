Domain(s): Biology — interactomics / systems biology (PPI mapping, interactome coverage estimation). Method-owning neighbours: biophysical chemistry and pharmacology (equilibrium binding, assay window), analytical metrology (LOD, working range).
Question (canonical): For each assay class in current interactome-mapping use (HT-Y2H, AP-MS of lysates, binary recomposition assays of the MAPPIT/LUMIER/PCA type, proximity labelling, in-cell crosslinking MS), taking each assay's working concentration regime and detection limit from published protocols as bounded intervals and taking detectability to mean that a law-of-mass-action equilibrium complex fraction exceeds the assay's LOD at that working concentration: (a) where does each assay's detectability boundary lie in the (Kd, intracellular concentration) plane for one declared cell type; (b) what is the union of those regions, and hence the complement invisible to the whole toolkit; (c) what share of pairs falls in the complement under two declared denominators — a reference set with independently measured affinities, and all pairs among proteins expressed in that cell type weighted by a stated Kd prior; and (d) restated under the window, what does at least one published "fraction of the interactome mapped" figure become?
 [v1 at intake was the same question without a declared denominator or detectability criterion; v2 above is the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation and is the version graded.]
Alternative formulations:
 1. What is the detection window of the interactome-mapping toolkit, in Kd and copies per cell, and how much of the interactome lies outside it?
 2. Is the published "fraction of the human interactome mapped" a coverage figure for the interactome, or for the part of it the assays can physically reach?
 3. (metrology) What is the working range of the interactome-mapping method in the analyte's own units, and what is its coverage error as distinct from its sampling error?
Motivating gap: Silence mapping. The field's assay-sensitivity parameter is measured by running each assay against a literature-curated positive reference set — i.e. against previously detected interactions — so it is estimated from inside the detection window and has no slot for what lies outside. Absolute copy numbers per cell line and documented protocol parameters now make the window computable rather than estimable. The intake's claim that there is "essentially no literature asking what its instruments can see" is STRUCK: Hein 2015 reads its own plot's borders as method-set dynamic-range limits; Heo/Maslov/Shakhnovich 2011 imposes an explicit detection threshold on a proteome-wide mass-action solution; Huang/Jedynak/Bader 2007 splits Y2H false negatives into systematic and sampling terms; Lalonde 2008 states the window qualitatively per method. What survives is the narrower gap: no opened source computes the boundary in the cell's units from protocol parameters, unions the assay classes, or restates a coverage figure conditional on a window.
Presuppositions:
 1. Each assay class has a working regime and LOD recoverable from protocols — PARTLY CONTESTED (Y2H nuclear and AP-MS lysate effective concentrations poorly documented); v2 requires intervals.
 2. Abundances convert to concentrations with a defensible volume — CONTESTED (compartments, condensates); v2 requires a compartment model plus sensitivity analysis.
 3. Equilibrium occupancy is adequate to define detectability — CONTESTED, and narrowed by Gate 4: covalent, time-integrating classes (proximity labelling, in-cell XL-MS) accumulate signal over an exposure, and AP-MS is a dilution-and-wash kinetic survival problem, so (Kd, C) are not the governing coordinates for several listed classes.
 4. Reference positive sets are drawn from previously detected interactions — VERIFIED from Venkatesan et al. 2009 (17,297 curated pairs narrowed to 188 then 107; "It is impossible to generate a set of negative interacting pairs with absolute confidence"). Circularity limb stands.
 5. The invisible band is non-empty — CONTESTED and partly what is asked. Supported qualitatively (Hein: weak interactions "frequently gone undetected"; Heo: "missed weak functional interactions") but no interaction has been independently placed inside a computed region.
 6. "The interactome" is a population a share can be reported of — CONTESTED at intake, repaired in v2 by declaring two denominators; Gate 4 attack 5 shows the repair is declaration without a usable denominator.
What would count as an answer: A computed (Kd, abundance) map marked by which assay classes reach each region, with the invisible region's boundary and its uncertainty; the share inside it under both declared denominators; and at least one published coverage figure restated as coverage of the reachable region (e.g. Venkatesan's ~8% of ~130,000). A null stands on its own: if the union substantially covers the plausible plane, coverage statements are sound as stated and the silence was justified. Axes are measurable, so neither answer can be reached by rhetoric — but see the strongest remaining objection on limb (c).

Novelty confidence: Medium
Significance — consequence: If a large region is invisible to the whole toolkit, every published "fraction of the interactome mapped" is a statement about coverage of the reachable region — a coverage-error problem, not a sampling-error problem — and assay-development priorities shift from throughput inside the window to extending it, with a named target region; network statistics on interactome maps acquire a physically specified missingness model in place of uniform-sampling assumptions. If the union substantially covers the plane, the result is a clean null: coverage statements are sound and the inherited-sensitivity circularity is harmless in practice. Gate 4 correction: the coverage-vs-sampling distinction is not wholly blurred by the field — Huang 2007 made it in 2007 — so the novel part of the consequence is the *physical* relabelling, not the distinction itself.
Significance — audience: Interactome-mapping consortia and their coverage arithmetic (CCSB/HuRI lineage, quantitative AP-MS groups); assay developers, for whom a computed blind region is a specification in biophysical units (the proximity-labelling literature already aims at the weak/transient corner without a map of it); database curators and PRS/RRS benchmark builders, since a window implies the positive reference set samples only the reachable region; ML PPI prediction, where a computed window converts "generalises poorly" into a quantified domain-shift term. The audience is real and already contesting adjacent claims; Gate 4 doubts whether it would adopt a prior-dependent share, not whether it exists.
Significance — fecundity: What the degree distribution looks like after correction for a physically specified window (P4-at-Gate-2's question with the threshold measured rather than fitted); whether disease-gene hub enrichment survives window correction or is an abundance artefact; which structurally or genetically evidenced interactions sit inside the invisible region and whether they share properties (IDR-mediated, avidity-driven, condensate-resident); a principled negative set built from the visible region only; the analogous window of a computational predictor; and transfer of ecology's detectability/occupancy estimators to molecular networks, which three adversaries independently confirmed has never been attempted.
Significance — anchoring: The interactome-completeness problem (P3); the low overlap between datasets from different assays, which a window would partly explain as geometry rather than noise; the "weak and transient interactions are under-appreciated" thread (P1, P2); the compartment/condensate problem, which enters as a contested presupposition; benchmark bias in structure-prediction-era PPI inference; and non-identifiability of population size under heterogeneous detection (Link, Biometrics), which an adversary checked and found cuts for the candidate.
Reason for silence: A disciplinary boundary compounded by an instrument that cannot see its own blind spot. The field operationalises sensitivity as a hit rate against a literature-curated positive set, so its calibration instrument is structurally unable to sample outside the window and returns a sensitivity number whether or not a blind region exists — the quantity has no slot in the accounting and its absence produces no anomaly. The quantities needed live in biophysics, pharmacology and metrology, which own the method but not the data; the method-owning literature parameterises these assays by labelling radius and reactive-intermediate half-life and, verbatim from a review opened at Gate 4, "does not describe these methods in terms of affinity", so the question cannot be stated in its variables. Gate 4 weakened this twice: Huang 2007 did state roughly the right next step in the field's own flagship computational journal and nobody did it for ~19 years, and a plain input-availability explanation (no measured proteome-wide Kd distribution; Heo had to assign Kd by formula) fits the evidence at least as well. The ledger does not accept difficulty-of-answering as a reason for silence, so the mechanism survives only in its narrower form — why the units were never adopted — and the record's stronger version is withdrawn.

Verification (post-batch, two fresh-context checkers, logs UQ-20261017-15-verification-huri.md and -huri2.md): the verdict is unchanged, but the COULD NOT READ residual that independently blocked Validated is discharged — and what HuRI actually says supports the candidate's novelty rather than capping it.
**HuRI opened on the second attempt**, through the PMC author manuscript (PMC7169983); the Harvard DASH PDF, doi.org and nature.com all returned fetch errors, so the typeset Nature text is still unread. A first checker had reached only the 2019 bioRxiv preprint.
Three findings, each bearing directly on the entry. First, the paper **does** publish the "fraction of the interactome mapped" figure the candidate proposes to restate: "HuRI is estimated to represent 2-11% of the binary protein interactome", with the derivation in Supplementary Note 2 and Extended Data Fig. 3j (unread), varying minimum publications per gene and minimum Lit-BM evidence, and with the main text conceding that "Accurate estimation of the total size of the interactome remains challenging." Second, and decisively for novelty, that figure has **no affinity or abundance basis**. Sensitivity is framed purely empirically, as recovery of PRSv1 and RRSv1 reference pairs by Y2H assay version; no dissociation constant, affinity or concentration language is tied to sensitivity anywhere in the main text returned, the only neighbouring phrase being "a continuum of binding strength or stability". Third, the paper does **not** map a systematically invisible region: the closest statements are that "many PPIs remained undetected as Y2H, like all characterized assays" and that weak or transient interactions are difficult to detect — qualitative remarks, not a boundary. On complementarity it says "The Y2H versions were complementary", with three versions roughly doubling detected PPIs against one and MAPPIT and GPCA used as orthogonal validation, but it never quantifies how much of any space the union covers.
So the same-field neighbour that was blocking Validated as unread turns out to own the candidate's target rather than its answer: it supplies the percentage the candidate wants restated against a detection boundary, and it states the boundary only in assay-recovery terms. The Unverified cap is discharged, and the entry's "closest neighbours each own only a restricted sibling" judgement is confirmed for HuRI specifically.
