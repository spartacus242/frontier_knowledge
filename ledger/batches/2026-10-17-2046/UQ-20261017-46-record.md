## Gate 0 — Intake

Question (canonical, V0 as submitted): For one fixed open-weight checkpoint and one fixed exact-match or multiple-choice benchmark, run under a battery of P >= 4 independently-mechanised, batch-size-1, offline perturbations that the field treats as leaving the nominal model unchanged (weight-quantization family at matched bit width: GPTQ / AWQ / round-to-nearest; release-dtype cast bf16 <-> fp16; attention/kernel implementation: eager / SDPA / FlashAttention; decode seed at fixed temperature > 0), is the set of items whose correctness flips shared across perturbation types beyond what item-specific independent Bernoulli flipping predicts — i.e. does the benchmark carry a common "fragile stratum" — and what share of the headline score sits inside it?

Alternative formulations:
1. Do independent, semantically meaningless changes to how a model is run flip the same benchmark questions, or different ones?
2. What fraction of a reported benchmark score is carried by items whose correctness is decided by numerics rather than by the model, and is that set a property of the item or of the perturbation?

Motivating gap (as submitted, then corrected by Gate 2): arXiv:2407.09141, "Accuracy is Not All You Need" (Microsoft Research; NeurIPS 2024 poster), introduced "flips" — verbatim, "measures the % of answers that changed from correct -> incorrect and incorrect -> correct, between baseline and quantized model" — and reported double-digit flip rates for compressed LLMs whose aggregate accuracy was nearly unchanged. The intake, having read that paper at first hand on 2026-10-17, correctly establishes that *this* paper performs no item-level flip-set analysis, reports no concordance statistic between two methods' flip sets, tests against no independent-Bernoulli null, and has no non-compression arm. **That part of the gap statement verifies.** What does not verify is the implied wider claim that the item-level question is therefore open in the literature: Gate 2 found (a) a published fragile-share figure in an adjacent perturbation family, (b) a fitted, calibrated per-item margin -> flip-probability model in the quantization family, and (c) the shared-unstable-item existence claim already in print, qualitatively, in the numerics family. See Novelty below. Batch-size-1 offline execution remains a correct and material design choice: it removes the batch-composition mediator that made ledger entry UQ-20261017-26 non-identified.

Presuppositions (as submitted, with Gate 2 updates):
- P1. Per-item correctness is well defined and deterministically extractable by the harness. ESTABLISHED within the stated restriction (exact-match / multiple-choice); CONTESTED and therefore excluded for free-form or judge-scored benchmarks. Note arXiv:2608.21382 shows the extraction rule is itself a perturbation axis (generation-with-parsing vs likelihood selection move items), so "the harness" must be pinned, not merely assumed.
- P2. The listed perturbations are treated by the community as nominally semantics-preserving. CONTESTED for quantization, which is openly lossy; the design separates a lossy arm (quantization) from nominally lossless arms (kernel implementation, dtype cast at equal width, seed). Gate 2 confirms the lossless arms are indeed treated as nominally model-preserving and are documented not to be (HEAL, arXiv:2606.21023, measures a 6.2 +/- 0.4 answer-flip rate for BF16 vs an FP32 anchor on Qwen3-8B).
- P3. The perturbations are mechanistically independent in the sense a common-cause reading needs. STRONGLY CONTESTED, and Gate 2 has made it worse rather than better: all arms act through the same final logit margin, and arXiv:2608.06564 now supplies affirmative published evidence that pre-perturbation margin predicts per-item flip probability well (held-out flip rates to a median of 1.8 points, calibration error 0.004 over 131,758 predictions). So the most likely answer to the candidate's question — "yes, a shared stratum, and it is the small-margin stratum" — is close to already established for the quantization arm. The candidate's own margin-conditioning limb is the right response to this presupposition, but it is no longer an open design: it is a partially executed one.
- P4. Items have heterogeneous per-item flip propensity, so the null is non-trivial. SUPPORTED; arXiv:2608.21382 reports item-level fragility correlated with item discrimination at 0.28, and HEAL asserts flips "generally originate from highly unstable questions".

## Gate 1 — Well-posedness

V0 fails on identification, not on vagueness. The stated null is "each item i flips independently with its own rate p_i estimated from within-perturbation replicates". For three of the four arms (quantization method, dtype cast, kernel implementation) the perturbation is deterministic: there are no within-perturbation replicates, so p_i is not estimable for exactly the arms the question is about, and the null against which concordance is to be judged does not exist. P6 concedes this in the intake and then proceeds as if it were only a power limitation; it is an identification failure.

Reformulation R1 (the one permitted at Gate 1, for well-posedness only; both versions recorded):

> For one fixed open-weight checkpoint and one fixed exact-match / multiple-choice benchmark under a pinned extraction rule, run at batch size 1 offline under a crossed design of P >= 4 perturbation TYPES x k >= 5 exchangeable INSTANCES within each type (quantization at matched bit width across k calibration draws / orderings; k release-dtype cast paths; k attention-kernel implementations; k decode seeds at fixed T > 0): in a two-level model of the per-item flip indicator, is the BETWEEN-TYPE component of per-item flip propensity non-zero once the WITHIN-TYPE component has fixed each item's own rate p_i; does it survive conditioning on the measured top-two logit margin; and what share of the headline score is contributed by items in the top decile of estimated between-type fragility?

R1 makes the null estimable by instantiating each type with exchangeable instances, so p_i comes from within-type replicates and the estimand becomes the between-type variance component of a two-level decomposition, with the within-type component as the null's own calibration. R1 is well-posed in the sense the ledger requires: a two-level binomial/logit-normal fit with the between-type variance component bounded away from zero (and its residual after margin conditioning) settles it, and a between-type component indistinguishable from zero settles it the other way.

R1 retains one genuine defect, which is why well-posedness is not 3/3: the "exchangeable instances within type" claim is contestable per arm and is doing real inferential work. Two GPTQ calibration draws are plausibly exchangeable; two attention-kernel implementations (eager / SDPA / FlashAttention) are three fixed engineering artefacts, not draws from a population, so k >= 5 is not achievable there and the within-type component for that arm is not a sample from anything. The kernel arm therefore enters R1 as a fixed-effect contrast with no null of its own, and the P-way statistic is only as well-defined as the weakest arm's exchangeability claim.

It is a question, not a thesis: both named outcomes are stated in advance with their consequences, and the third ("fully explained by margin") is stated as determinate rather than as a failure.

What would count as an answer (R1): pairwise and P-way concordance of flip sets (Jaccard, Fleiss' kappa, and a tetrachoric common-factor fit) measured against the pre-registered within-type null; the between-type variance component with a CI; the share of the headline score contributed by items in the top decile of estimated fragility; and the residual concordance after conditioning on measured top-two logit margin. "A shared fragile stratum exists" if concordance exceeds the null's upper 95% bound AND survives margin conditioning; "it does not" if concordance falls to null once per-item rates are free; "margin explains it" if the residual vanishes. All three are answers.

## Gate 3 — Significance (argued)

Significance — consequence: If YES (shared stratum survives the within-type null and margin conditioning): a benchmark acquires a reportable fragile-share, and compression-robustness claims become conditional statements about which stratum a perturbation lands in rather than about a method. Concretely, a leaderboard delta smaller than the fragile share would be unreportable, and an eval release could ship a per-item fragility annotation that transfers across perturbations. If NO (concordance collapses to the within-type null once per-item rates are free): flips are a property of the perturbation, item-level fragility annotation is impossible, and every perturbation must be evaluated on its own — which is current practice, so a null ratifies practice rather than changing it. That asymmetry is the honest reading: the no-branch is informative but not action-changing. If MARGIN EXPLAINS IT: fragility is computable from logits alone with no perturbation sweep — the cheapest of the three for maintainers, and the branch B has already substantially delivered for quantization, which is where most of this candidate's expected value has already been spent.

Significance — audience: Eval-harness maintainers (lm-evaluation-harness, HELM, Open LLM Leaderboard) would add a fragile-share column or a robust-stratum rescore; this audience is demonstrably real and already moving — A is written from inside it, and 2604.03244 argues for standardized item-level releases. Quantization and compression researchers (the 2407.09141 line, 2606.19558, 2608.06564) would report flips against a numerics baseline rather than against the unquantized model alone. Inference-engine maintainers (vLLM, SGLang, llama.cpp) would gain a reason to treat kernel and dtype paths as evaluation-relevant configuration, which HEAL already argues for in the reproducibility register. Model-release evaluators and auditors would learn whether a headline score is portable across serving stacks. The audience is real and identified; what is discounted is the marginal change in its behaviour, since two of the three branches now have published partial answers it could act on already.

Significance — fecundity: Does fragile-stratum membership transfer across checkpoints of one model family, or across model families on the same items? Can a benchmark be rescored on its robust stratum only, and does that change rankings (A's Kendall 0.42 suggests it would)? Does fragile share predict leaderboard rank instability? Does the stratum coincide with the known-mislabelled or ambiguous items (MMLU-Redux) — i.e. is numeric fragility a cheap detector of item defects, which would connect this to 2603.24999's scalability coefficients? Is the between-type component itself a model property worth reporting (a "numeric brittleness" score)? These are live and none is answered by anything opened.

Significance — anchoring: The flips metric and its unanalysed structure (2407.09141). Numerical nondeterminism and batch invariance (2506.09501; HEAL 2606.21023; vLLM batch-invariance work). Config fragility in leaderboards (2608.21382). Fidelity-metric choice for compressed deployment (2606.19558). Prediction churn and predictive multiplicity (2102.03349; the Rashomon-set ambiguity measure), where the same statistic exists for a different object. Reliability engineering's common-cause failure machinery (IEC/IAEA; 2204.03717), untransplanted. Ledger entries UQ-20261016-06 (numerics noise-vs-bias on agent scores), UQ-20261017-26 (spec-decode fidelity), UQ-20261017-30 (harness-side failure share of the denominator).

Reason for silence: A disciplinary boundary with no shared unit of analysis — a credible mechanism, and one that is visibly eroding. Compression research owns the lossy arm and publishes per-METHOD aggregates or at most per-method flip rates (2407.09141, 2606.19558, 2608.06564); numerical-reproducibility and systems research owns the nominally-lossless arms and publishes per-STACK flip rates against a single FP32 anchor (HEAL). Each field's publication unit is the method or the stack; the item is a means, not an endpoint, so the cross-type concordance statistic only exists if someone pools the two literatures' arms onto one item frame — and neither field's reviewers would ask for the other's arm. The psychometric machinery that expresses the question (per-item propensity, tetrachoric common factor, Fleiss' kappa) is routine in a third field whose practitioners do not run inference stacks, and the reliability-engineering machinery (common-cause failure, beta-factor models) sits in a fourth. Against this: A shows the item-level unit has now arrived in eval methodology and A's own Section 6 names the next step, which is precisely why the silence is breaking and why the grade is Low rather than Medium. A secondary mechanism — the batch-composition confound documented in UQ-20261017-26 and left uncontrolled by HEAL — made the measurement unidentified until batch-invariant kernels and batch-size-1 offline harnesses became routine, which is recent.
