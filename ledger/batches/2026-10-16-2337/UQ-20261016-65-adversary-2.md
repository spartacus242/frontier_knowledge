# Gate 4 — Adversary #2 log — UQ-20261016-65
Date: 2026-10-17. Budget used: 9/9 web searches, 4/4 web fetches, plus 1 free GitHub code search. Second adversary; adversary #1's log not read.

## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

Of all the image series a scanner produces that were meant to be looked at for diagnosis — i.e. not scouts/localizers, not dose-report objects, not aborted or repeated runs, not machine-made reformats or parameter maps — what share are never actually put on a screen by anybody in the chain of people who read the case (trainee, fellow, attending, second reader) between the study landing in PACS and the report being signed? And if you pull a stratified sample of those never-opened series and hand them cold to two or more readers who cannot see the original report, how often is there something in them that someone would have to act on?

Two limbs: (i) a coverage number read off viewer display logs, with a pre-declared series taxonomy and a pre-declared provenance map that credits a displayed derived series as covering the source series it encodes; (ii) a blinded multi-reader actionable-finding yield in that never-displayed stratum.

## Recommended verdict
**Provisional** — nothing Unresolved (4 Resolved, 4 Partial: attacks 4, 5, 7, 8), but novelty is Medium with two unread novelty-bearing papers and an uncleared P8; promotion to Validated requires the full text of 10.1007/s10278-016-9881-1, a read of 10.1007/s10278-010-9337-y, and one vendor-analytics specification checked for a per-series display metric.

**Single strongest killing point (Partial, attack 8/5):** limb (i)'s headline fraction is a free parameter of the pre-declared provenance map, and the map choice is adversarial to limb (ii) — crediting a displayed thick reformat as coverage of an unopened thin-slice source stack writes off precisely the series where actionable yield would concentrate, so a map generous enough to be clinically defensible pushes the fraction toward zero and closes the question by convention, while a strict map inflates it with informationally redundant series. Nothing I or the record found bounds that sensitivity in either direction, and the question's three pre-named actions are all keyed to a threshold on a number that moves with the convention.
