## Process lessons

- **When every adversary's fatal attack rests on the same abstract-only or unopened document, Gate 4 has
  not tested the leg, and their agreement is not corroboration.** UQ-08's two Gate 4 adversaries were each
  Unresolved on attack 2 against an abstract-level read of the one paper the leg turned on. Two readers
  agreeing about a document neither has read is one unread document, not two supports.
- **A lead justified by kind is worth opening even when the pass that named it could not.** Verification
  named LaMotte 1977 as the right *kind* of document and did not open it; a cold reader's own query
  returned it, it opened in one fetch, and it replaced an entire rejection ground.
- **A document in the record's own "unopened" list can be the nearest prior.** JCI Insight 152598 was
  listed under "grounding nothing"; two independent readers opened it and it set the entry's novelty
  ceiling. This is the fourth batch running in which a paper the record itself surfaced and never fetched
  changed a grade.
- **An entry's own unopened list can double-count one paper.** PMC10530070 resolves in one `idconv` call
  to DOI 10.1111/trf.17468 — a paper this entry had already read at full text — so one of its three
  qualified absences closed in the candidate's favour.
- **A quotation-cap refusal is not an extraction failure, and a spent budget is not an unreadable
  source.** Both appeared in this batch, in two different entries, and both manufactured an absence.
- **An unsourced quotation presented with "states" is the gravest defect available**, because it is the
  one thing a reader cannot check without opening the file. It is struck, never reattributed to a guessed
  document and never converted into a promotion condition: no condition turns an unlocated string into a
  citation.
- **Two of this batch's four cold readers volunteered that their own load-bearing step was their own
  inference with the grounding search unrun**, one saying in terms that it would "rather hand back a
  Partial than an unsourced kill". That labelling is what makes a leg reviewable, and it is why the legs
  that fell could be seen to fall.
- **A "pushed" claim needs checking against the remote, not assumed.** This run reported the raw records
  pushed when `git log origin/<branch>..HEAD` still showed the commit local; a later push also needed a
  rebase because the research workstream had landed its own commits on the shared branch meanwhile.
