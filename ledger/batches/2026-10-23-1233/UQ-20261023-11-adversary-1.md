## Restatement in my own words

Seismic networks publish a capability (or completeness) map that implicitly treats stations as independently available. The candidate asks, for one named network and window: (a) do stations actually go dark TOGETHER more than an independent-outage null predicts, measured as the excess share of downtime station-hours sitting in multi-station coincident clusters at window w; (b) how much more likely does that correlation make a "blind window" — an interval of ≥T hours in which the realized detection threshold (computed from which channels are up, via a declared model) is worse than the named published map by ≥ΔM — than the independent null says, as a ratio; and (c) what share of the realized threshold's variance is due to correlated as against independent downtime. The grid (w, ΔM, T) is enumerated; the station frame is by rule (FDSN epoch covers ≥90% of W); a null answer (no excess, ratio ≈ 1) counts as an answer.

## Attack 4 first — conditioning clauses written out and tested

Measurand exemption applied: that station downtime is correlated (and that the independence idealisation fails) is what the question MEASURES, with a pre-named null; attack 4 is not run against it.

Clauses the question needs TRUE before it can be asked:

1. **Per-channel availability records exist and are retrievable, for ≥1 network, at a resolution finer than the smallest w (15 min) and T (1 h).** — **TRUE (sourced, S1).** The FDSN availability service's query method returns "full resolution time series listings" per channel with microsecond timestamps and a mergegaps tolerance in seconds; downtime intervals are the complements of the returned spans. The record had this at ONE opened instance (MUSTANG page) with no resolution; the spec settles resolution. Live service instances (NCEDC, SCEDC) returned but not opened — so the spec is one opened instance of the kind, two named instances unopened.
2. **The station frame rule (FDSN epoch covers ≥90% of W) is evaluable.** — **UNVERIFIED by me** (I opened no fdsnws-station document). Low risk; kills nothing.
3. **A NAMED published capability product for N exists on a declared magnitude scale and is commensurable with the realized threshold model's output (repair presupposition R1).** — **CONTESTED, now with a sourced number, but convertible by declaration.** S2's abstract states the detection-capability threshold sits above the TM threshold level "with the difference typically being 0.5-1 magnitude unit". MY OWN REASONING ON THAT NUMBER (not a source finding): if the declared realized model outputs the TM threshold level while the named reference is a 90% detection-capability map (or vice versa), the systematic offset between the two estimands exceeds every ΔM in the grid {0.2, 0.3, 0.5}, and V2(b) returns P ≈ 1 under both nulls with an uninformative ratio. The question survives this ONLY if the declared model and the named reference are the same estimand (S2 and S3 show the TM product is routinely converted to a ≥3-station 90% detection capability, so a commensurable pair exists for the IMS). Grade: CONTESTED-but-declarable; it tightens R1 and does not kill, because the question already requires both to be declared.
4. **"Downtime" read from an availability record equals the station's operational unavailability at time t.** — **CONTESTED (sourced, S1).** The service reflects what is "loaded into the repository" at query time; late-arriving data closes a gap that was, in real time, an outage. So FDSN-derived downtime is ARCHIVAL downtime. That is the right object for catalogue completeness (audience iii) and the wrong object for EEW alerting (audience ii), and V2 does not say which it means. Not fatal: the question's own words are "per-channel data-availability records", which fixes the archival reading; but the EEW audience claim in Gate 3 then rests on a different quantity than the one measured.
5. **Multi-station coincident clusters at window w are identifiable from the record's own time stamps (R3).** — **TRUE (sourced, S1):** per-channel spans with microsecond timestamps make coincidence at any w ≥ seconds a pure computation.
6. **A declared, reproducible threshold model exists mapping the up-channel set at t to a threshold field.** — **TRUE (sourced, S2, S3):** the TM method is an operating implementation producing hourly (S3) and finer (record's prior A) three-station detection-capability estimates.
7. **The "variance of the realized threshold" is decomposable into correlated and independent downtime shares.** — **UNVERIFIED and declaration-dependent.** No source opened bears on it. MY OWN REASONING: the decomposition is not unique (it depends on how the joint process is orthogonalised against the independent null), which is why V2 says "under a declared variance decomposition"; it is well-posed by fiat and comparable across studies only if the declaration is reproduced.

No clause the question cannot survive without came back FALSE. Two came back CONTESTED (3 and 4) and both are convertible into declarations the question already demands. Attack 4 therefore is **Partial**, not Unresolved.
