## Restatement (my words)

Sites that block AI crawlers are like survey nonrespondents whose answers we already hold from earlier crawls. The candidate asks three things: (i) how different is the blocked content from the unblocked content on a fixed list of corpus statistics, scaled by the blocked share (Groves bias = rate x difference); (ii) does that difference disappear once you reweight on domain features a crawler can see — i.e. is blocking MAR given observables, or MNAR; (iii) does a model trained with the blocked content removed behave differently from one trained with it in.
