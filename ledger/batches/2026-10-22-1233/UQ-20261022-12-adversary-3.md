## 1. Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

For standard test methods (ASTM, ISO, AOAC, TAPPI ...) that have been put through two or more independent interlaboratory precision studies on comparable materials, how far apart are the two PUBLISHED reproducibility limits R? Specifically: over a pre-declared frame F of (method, material-class) pairs, what is the distribution of log(R2/R1), and is its spread wider than the sampling noise that each study's own laboratory count p, material count m and replicate structure n would predict for its s_R (a chi-square-type interval)? Second limb: a census of how old, how small and how many-material the studies behind currently active precision statements are. Pairs for which p, m, n cannot be recovered are counted and reported as a coverage gap, not dropped.

What the question CONDITIONS ON (needed true before it can be asked), as literally worded — numbered for attack 4:
- C1. At least a non-trivial number of methods have TWO OR MORE completed, independent interlaboratory precision studies of the SAME designated method on materials satisfying the declared matching rule (frame F non-empty).
- C2. For each admitted study, p, m, n are recoverable from a public or purchasable document. (R1 converts failure of this clause into a reported coverage gap, so it is a MEASURAND, not a presupposition — attack 4 does not reach it. Said here so it is not graded around.)
- C3. "SAME designated method" means the same measurement procedure in both studies, so that R2/R1 measures the stability of R and not a change in the method.
- C4. The published R is the study's own output (2.8 x s_R, or the D6300/E691 model of it) and not a committee-adjusted number.
- C5. The sampling dispersion of s_R at (p, m, n) is adequately described by a chi-square-type interval.
- C6. Membership of a pair in F is independent of the outcome: that a method has been re-studied is not itself informative about the sign or size of log(R2/R1). Without C6, overlaying zero-centred design-implied intervals on realised ratios reads selection as instability.
- C7. "Comparable materials" is decidable across two studies run years apart under the declared rule (R1 makes this conditional on the rule; conditional, not presupposed).
