Question (canonical):
V2 (Gate 1 reformulation, the one permitted; scored as canonical) — In >=2 independent cohorts or trials holding per-patient time-stamped electronic dosing records over whole inter-visit intervals and a visit-measured biomarker whose pharmacodynamic response time is shorter than that interval (BP, LDL, HIV RNA, IOP, INR), under one pre-declared exposure-response model per biomarker with >=2 model forms as sensitivity: what is the signed difference, per randomised arm and in the arm contrast, between the treatment effect as the visit-measured analysis reports it and the effect predicted when each patient's realised doses are redistributed uniformly across the interval (dose-count-preserving; visit-entrained component removed, total exposure fixed), expressed as a fraction of the observed arm difference against a pre-declared margin, with HbA1c as the pre-specified falsification arm in which the difference must vanish?
V1 (as intaken) differs only in specification, not in object; full text of both in batches/2026-10-21-0452/UQ-20261021-01-record.md.

Alternative formulations:
- How much of a drug's measured effect is an artefact of patients taking it more faithfully in the days before the visit at which the effect is measured?
- Treating the visit calendar as a cause of the exposure used to evaluate it, what is the visit-entrainment bias in the ITT estimate, and does it differ between arms?

Motivating gap:
Anomaly mining. Entrainment established on one full-text primary (Poleon, Sabbagh & Racette, Front Med 2022;9:867884; 66 POAG patients, MEMS caps, 28 days bracketing one visit): adherence 85.5 -> 88.5% pre-visit (p=0.01), 87.0 -> 84.9% post (p=0.02); optimal-adherence subgroup (n=49) 94.0 -> 97.7% (p=0.001). Effect small (~3 pp) and carried by the already-adherent. The gap: entrainment is owned as a patient trait ("whitecoat adherence") and corrected out of the ADHERENCE estimate, never posed as a property of the measurement schedule that biases the EFFICACY estimate. The one efficacy-adjacent sentence in that literature is uncited, qualitative, and about bedside inference in care, not a trial's estimated effect.

Presuppositions:
1. A dataset holding dosing timestamps AND a fast biomarker AND a randomised arm. Dosing-record half established; the conjunction only HALF-established (Tousset et al. 2006: MEMS + office BP in 44 trial patients, no arm contrast stated). Binding feasibility risk.
2. Adherence entrained to visit timing, not merely noisy. Established in glaucoma at full-text read; magnitude ~3 pp; A2's re-read corrects the record — in the optimal subgroup the post-visit comparison is 95.2 -> 95.4%, a slight rise, so the rise-then-fall cycle holds only on the pre-visit side there. Generalisation to HIV/hypertension unverified.
3. Named biomarkers respond faster than the interval. Established; false for HbA1c, hence the falsification arm.
4. Electronic timestamps index ingestion. CONTESTED and sign-bearing: MEMS logs container openings as a dose proxy (stated in P1 itself), and curiosity or tidying openings have no reason to be stationary before an appointment. Unresolved by anything either adversary opened.
5. A time-averaged counterfactual is a well-defined estimand. Resolved by construction at V2, at the price of model-dependence.
6. Entrainment not already cancelled by the comparator arm. Contested; partial cancellation likely and the residual is the quantity of interest, which is why V2 reports per-arm and contrast separately.

What would count as an answer:
A signed, interval-estimated difference in >=2 independent datasets between the visit-measured effect and the effect re-estimated with the entrained dosing component removed under the dose-count-preserving regime and a pre-declared exposure-response model, per arm and as a fraction of the arm difference, judged against a pre-declared margin, plus the HbA1c falsification arm. Small and margin-compatible everywhere with the falsification arm behaving = a negative answer that retires the question. An interval too wide is declared in advance to be an answer about feasibility, not a null.

Significance — consequence:
If the entrained component is material in fast-biomarker indications, the visit schedule becomes an efficacy-relevant design parameter: part of what a registration trial estimates is a property of its own calendar, which no label reproduces. Non-inferiority margins and dose-ranging slopes would be mis-scaled; part of the efficacy-effectiveness gap would be mechanical schedule geometry rather than a fact about patients; an E9(R1) estimand would need to declare dependence on the assessment calendar. On a null, a threat currently retired by silence is retired on evidence.
