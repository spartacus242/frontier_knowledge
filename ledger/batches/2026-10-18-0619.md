## Scope is not a quotation: Appendix A names R1–R7, not 21 records

Gate 2 on UQ-14 wrote its own kill condition — "If that appendix reports several repetitions per record with dispersion, the candidate's limb (i) is substantially answered for the 2025 portion of the series and novelty falls to Low" — and adversary #3 opened the appendix and declared the condition met for 21 records.

Read cold, App. A's text names only R1–R7 and the R6→R7 transition. It states no total. It never says 21, and it never says May 2025: that figure is from Section 1 of the same paper, and the benchmark it describes has 19 tasks. Everything quantitative in the appendix is in **Figure A.1, which no agent in four passes could see**. And the environment is one common *upgraded* PyTorch — explicitly not period-correct, because the authors could not determine the earlier version for R1–R6 — which is the exact capability the candidate's distinctive arm depends on.

So the appendix answers a narrower question, in one environment, with no separation test, no failure census and no transfer arm. "The condition is met" became "the condition is met at a scope the source never claims", and the difference was the whole verdict.

**Rule: a scope claim is a claim, and it needs its own verbatim check. "App. A re-times every record" is not a quotation from App. A.**

## A quotation can be verbatim and the result still absent

UQ-15's kill said arXiv:2407.07890 "publishes the mediated share" of an LLM benchmark gain carried by MCQ-format ability. First-hand, from the PDF (100,000 of 101,411 characters), one string at a time:

- Sec. 3.2's "conflates knowledge-testing with testing a models' ability to answer multiple choice questions" — **verbatim**.
- Sec. 2.2's "over 7 accuracy points in MMLU" — **verbatim**.
- The 0.071 and 0.168 numbers — **real**, in Appendix B **Table 3** (the record cited Table 2; there is no Table 2).
- The mediated **share** — **does not exist**. Table 3 gives indirect effects in accuracy units with SEs; the total effect is absent from the same table, so no share is formable. The record's "close to the whole gap" was an inference presented as a reading.
- ">17 points GSM8K" — **does not exist**. The paper says **19**.
- The mediator — **training on the test task in general**, not multiple-choice-format ability.
- The unit — **56 released base models**, split by release date at matched pretraining compute. No within-run checkpoints. Cooldown, annealing and LR decay appear nowhere in the text read.
- The instrument — **a weight update** (fine-tuning every model on the MMLU auxiliary training set) plus prompt reformulation. Not a fixed-weight output transform.

Three accurate quotations and one real table of numbers, around a result that is not in the paper. This is the defect class the ledger logs as fit (`ledger-prior-art-fit`) rather than identity, and it is the most expensive one, because every cheap check passes.

## A number can be right and the checker's cut wrong

Late adversary 2 on UQ-14 derived a noise floor from the contributors' own published dispersion (n=20, CV 0.085%; n=13, CV 0.262%), computed the minimum detectable step-down under the candidate's own rule at 0.18%–0.56%, and reported **17 of 91 step-downs below the loose floor, carrying 1.6% of the cumulative log-speedup**.

This ledger's own arithmetic note called that figure unconfirmed, because no threshold in its table reproduced 17. **That was the checker's error, not the figure's**: 17 is at the adversary's own stated threshold of 0.56%, and the table's finest bucket was 1%. Recomputed at 0.56% on the raw README: **17 steps, 1.597%, and the same seventeen record numbers** — 23, 24, 28, 30, 37, 38, 41, 47, 63, 68, 69, 70, 71, 76, 80, 86, 91. **Rule: re-derive a derived number at the threshold ITS author stated. Checking a figure against the wrong cut manufactures a false negative exactly as a narrowed question manufactures a false defect — which is the same failure this ledger logged against itself two batches ago, from the other direction.**

## What to carry forward

3. **"Abstract-level only, grounds nothing" describes the reader, not the paper.** The paper UQ-12 dismissed from its title became the ground the rejection now stands on.

6. **The author line keeps failing by new mechanisms**: an invented author with a real one dropped, and a middle author promoted to first. Five instances in two batches, no two alike. Read the printed first page.
