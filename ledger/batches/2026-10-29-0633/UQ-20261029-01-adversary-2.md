## Restatement, in my own words

The fragments of rock returned from the asteroids Bennu and Ryugu are shot through with microscopic cracks, and those cracks are what make the rock conduct heat so badly. The question asks **what broke them**: did the cracks open because water-bearing minerals inside the parent asteroid changed volume as they formed or dried out (shrinkage/swelling cracking), or did an impact shock them open? The question proposes to decide it from crack *geometry*: volume-change cracking is asserted to give near-isotropic crack orientations, crack spacing set by the thickness of the clay-rich domains, and crack area density that rises with how aqueously altered the local material is; impact-shock cracking is asserted to give planar preferred orientations and a density that does not track alteration grade. The reformulation (V2) keeps the same two arms but drops to the ~3 µm voxel scale actually achieved and to a two-class alteration contrast.

## Recommended verdict

**Rejected** — Unresolved on attacks 2 and 4 (and 7): the question is asked in print in its own field and explicitly declared open by the paper it was mined from, and its shock arm is defined by a clause that the published relation between impact deformation and alteration grade in hydrated carbonaceous chondrites contests, in both V1 and the Gate 1 repair.

**Single strongest killing point.** Leg A of attack 4, on Lindgren et al. 2015 at **FULL TEXT** (doi 10.1016/j.gca.2014.09.014; Lindgren, Hanna, Dobson, Tomkinson, Lee; *GCA* 148:159–178): the candidate's shock arm is *density uncorrelated with alteration grade*, but in CM carbonaceous chondrites impact-produced fabric strength **increases** with degree of aqueous alteration (r² = 0.35, Sec. 3.1.3 / Sec. 4.7, Fig. 5b), and both mechanisms the authors offer — impact fracture networks tapping fluid reservoirs and so *enhancing* alteration, or more-altered rock deforming more readily through ductile phyllosilicates — predict the same positive sign the candidate reserves for volume-change cracking. Leg (b) of the discriminant therefore cannot separate the arms, and V2 keeps it.
