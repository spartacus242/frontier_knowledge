## Restatement (my own words)

Build a stochastic source whose Shannon entropy rate H we know to any wanted precision because we specified it (a small hidden-Markov source; a probabilistic grammar). Train sequence models from a fixed architecture class (decoder-only transformers; for contrast a gated-linear-recurrent / state-space class) on unlimited fresh samples, at ever larger parameter counts and token budgets. As both grow without bound, does the per-symbol cross-entropy they attain tend to H, or does it level off at H + Delta with Delta > 0? And if Delta > 0, is it fixed by the architecture class (the same across sources, different across classes) rather than by the source?
