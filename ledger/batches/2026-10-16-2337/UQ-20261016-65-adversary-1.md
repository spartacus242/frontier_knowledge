## Restatement (my own words)

Of the image series a scanner produces and the PACS archives for diagnostic use, what share does nobody on the reporting team (trainee, fellow, attending, second reader) ever actually put on a screen before the report is signed — and if you blind-re-read a sample of those unopened series, how often do they contain something that should have changed management? The record's instrument is the PACS/viewer display log; its two arms are (i) a log-defined "never displayed" fraction per diagnostically-intended series and (ii) a blinded multi-reader yield on that stratum.
