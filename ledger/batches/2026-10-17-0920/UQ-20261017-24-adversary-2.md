# UQ-20261017-24 — Adversary #2 log (independent; first log not read)

## Restatement in my own words

Several metals that burn off re-entering satellites and rocket bodies are now injected into the
upper atmosphere at rates that equal or beat the rate at which the same metals arrive in cosmic
dust. Some of the ways we weigh the cosmic-dust arrival rate work by measuring metals — in
stratospheric sulfuric-acid particles, in mesospheric metal layers, in meteoric smoke, in polar
snow and ice. The question asks, method by method: which element does each method actually count;
given the published per-element spacecraft injection rate and its error bars, how far wrong is
that method's inferred cosmic-dust influx; in what year (or at what re-entry tonnage) does that
error exceed the method's own stated precision; and can you still tell a spacecraft atom from a
meteoric one, particle by particle or snow-layer by snow-layer, with a separation rule fixed in
advance and its error rate measured on material archived before the space age.

So: not "how much space junk is in the stratosphere" (that is measured) and not "can the lithium
layer act as a re-entry sentinel" (that is demonstrated), but "is the instrument we weigh cosmic
dust with still calibrated, and when does it break."

## Prior spot-check summary (unit of analysis / endpoint / comparator / estimand)

- P1 Lasue et al. EGU24-14286 — VERIFIED REAL, read by me. Estimand = time trend in the
  anthropogenic share of *collected particles*. The candidate's estimand = fractional bias in an
  *inferred influx rate* per method, plus the date each method loses its quoted precision. These
  are different estimands, and P1 contains no influx number, no propagation, no error rate and no
  pre-space-age baseline (its series begins 1981, i.e. inside the space age, so it cannot supply

## Recommended verdict

**Provisional** — nothing Unresolved on attacks 1, 2 or 4, so no Gate-4 rejection is available to me;
but attack 7 (Reason for silence) is Unresolved, which caps the entry at Provisional, and the Gate 2
novelty grade of Low independently bars Validated. Promotion conditions: (i) read Schulz et al.
2025's results/discussion and EGU26-1032 and confirm neither poses the bias limb; (ii) supply a
non-circular mechanism for the silence that survives the fact that the field built a contaminant
class (TCA/AOS) for this in the 1980s; (iii) verify presupposition 6 (tracer bias -> influx bias)
for at least one named method.

Caution for the runner, stated because it cuts against my own brief: the Gate 2 "near-duplicate"
ground does NOT survive an estimand check. I opened P1 myself and confirmed it reports no influx
rate, no misattribution rate and no bias claim; its estimand is the anthropogenic share of
collected particles, not the bias in an inferred influx. If the verdict on this record is resting
on P1 as a near-duplicate of the candidate's central limb, that ground is overstated — the honest
kill here is attack 7 plus the already-published fractions, not a duplicate.
