Motivating gap:
Williamson's conjecture is false at odd 35, 47, 53, 59. The failures are recorded by exhaustive computation and, in the three papers read at full text at Gate 2, never explained: Bright-Kotsireas-Ganesh state their aim as raising the enumeration bound, and a dedicated term sweep of that paper finds no occurrence of "explanation", "pattern", "predict", "scarce" or "rare"; the two 2026 papers respond by weakening the object (near-Williamson, complex Hadamard). Since a quadruple at odd n yields a Hadamard matrix of order 4n, an obstruction would bear on the Hadamard conjecture's construction supply.
