Question (canonical): Over a pre-specified frame of K EMPIAR entries depositing an uncleaned particle stack alongside the published final stack, (a) in what share of entries does a reconstruction from the complement (deposited picks minus final stack) satisfy a pre-registered three-part distinct-state criterion — resolution better than a fixed threshold, map-to-model correspondence above a fixed cutoff, and deviation from the deposited map above the 95th percentile of deviations from size-matched random subsets of the retained stack; and (b) when M curators process the same micrographs independently and blind, what is the Jaccard overlap of their rejected sets, and does any resulting map carry a feature absent from the deposited map? (Gate 1 reformulation, one allowed, and the version graded.)
Alternative formulations:
- Gate 0 intake version: does the rejected complement of a deposited cryo-EM reconstruction support a distinct conformational or compositional state rather than noise, and is the retained/rejected partition a property of the data or of the curator?
- What is in cryo-EM's discard pile, and is the deposited structural record a biased subsample of the molecular ensemble?
Motivating gap: the field is dense with how-answers about discarding (2D/3D classification, vendor "junk cleaning", neural sorters such as ANTIDOTE, bioRxiv 2025.10.06.680814) and validates only the retained map (gold-standard FSC, independent-particle cross-validation), while the "the discard pile is junk" assertion is rarely measured against the alternative that it is enriched in the minority states classification removes. Gate 2 narrowed the gap: the existence limb is already demonstrated in the candidate's own literature and the complement/matched-subset mechanics already exercised; only the pooled corpus-level share and the curator-overlap statistic remain unmeasured.
Presuppositions: (1) enough EMPIAR entries pair raw/uncleaned picks with final lists — partly established (EMPIAR-10930 deposits ~18M uncleaned particles); the archive-wide count is unknown. (2) The deposited picking step is reproducible for entries lacking an uncleaned stack — contested and load-bearing. (3) "Distinct state" admits a non-circular pre-specified criterion — supplied at Gate 1; contested by adversary attack 5. (4) Enough signal in the complement for any reconstruction — ESTABLISHED (P1: 4.38 A from the lowest-scoring 90k of EMPIAR-10093 vs 4.08 A from the top 84k). (5) Rejection is at least partly operator-dependent — contested; supported anecdotally by P2; threatened from the opposite side by run-to-run algorithmic stochasticity (attack 4). (6) Specimens possess minority states at removed frequencies — demonstrated for ALK-ALKAL2 (2:1 beside 2:2). (7) Downstream users treat a deposited map as representative — only partly established; not load-bearing for significance.
What would count as an answer: per-entry pass/fail of the complement against the pre-registered criterion with matched-size random retained subsets as controls; the M-curator Jaccard of rejected sets plus any feature absent from the deposited map; and a pooled share of entries whose discarded material supported a reproducible distinct state. Negative if complement maps are indistinguishable from matched random subsets and curator overlap is high; positive even at a minority share, which would give EMDB/EMPIAR grounds to mandate deposition of the full pick list and rejection record (the analogue of unmerged reflections in crystallography).

Secondary: an archive gap (uncleaned stacks are recent, so the audit was technically impossible until lately) and map-centric validation culture (no metric whose denominator is the rejected set). The silence is partial, not total — consistent with the Low grade, and it does not explain silence on the curator-overlap limb, which needs only micrographs.

That arithmetic check did not pass; it could not be run. So "the candidate's own control has already been run on this pair" is withdrawn.
