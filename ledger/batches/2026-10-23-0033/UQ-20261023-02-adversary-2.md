## Restatement (my own words, before any of the generator's framing)

Is a "constant" circadian chamber actually constant? Specifically: does a nominally DD or LL recording chamber deliver a hidden once-per-day cycle — in air temperature, vibration, sound, CO2, humidity, or in when people walk in — big enough to weakly pull the clock around or to smear the behavioural readout; and if so, how much of the chamber-to-chamber and lab-to-lab scatter in published free-running period for one fixed genotype is really the building's day rather than the animal's clock?

As the question **now stands (V2, repair included)**, it asks someone to: hang passive multi-channel loggers (≤ 1 min) in ≥ 3 nominally constant chambers across ≥ 2 facilities for ≥ 2 complete experiments; report each channel's 24-h amplitude and phase with CIs; **compare the temperature amplitude against "the documented thermal entrainment threshold for the preparation under test"**; run an imposed-cue-versus-sham arm and report its effect on tau, phase angle and after-effects against the tau estimator's own precision; and estimate a chamber-level variance component for tau with its CI. I attack V2 and its repair, not V1.
