Question (canonical): For a declared, version-pinned high-throughput DFT attempt frame (every structure submitted, with per-task terminal states retained, or a frame re-run from scratch) and a pre-registered recovery protocol applied to a random sample of terminally failed tasks (escalated mixing/preconditioner, smearing, k-grid, initial magnetic configuration, symmetry, step limits) plus cross-code recovery: (a) conditional on composition and structure under a pre-declared featurization, is the distribution of formation energy, band gap, magnetic moment/ordering and dynamical stability the same among recovered-failed as among retained entries (the ignorability null), and what is the hand-adjudicated split of failures into numerics-driven and physics-driven (the latter a declared outcome category, not a missing value); and (b) when the recovered set is reinstated, by how much does at least one published downstream statistic move — a screening hit rate, the count of entries on or within X meV/atom of the convex hull, or the held-out error of a universal MLIP retrained with the recovered entries included?
