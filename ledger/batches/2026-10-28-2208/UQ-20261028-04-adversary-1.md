## Restatement (my own words)

Take every cube-free k with 0 < |k| <= 1000 and k not congruent to +/-4 mod 9. For each, count the integer solutions of x^3 + y^3 + z^3 = k below a fixed height 10^15 and divide that count by a reference prediction c_k log B, where c_k is supposed to be built from p-adic (local) densities of the equation alone. Then ask whether the resulting per-k ratios, ranked across k, line up with the class number, regulator or 3-rank of the class group of the pure cubic field Q(k^(1/3)), beyond what a permutation null allows once the mod-9 class is held fixed. The question conditions on (i) the per-k solution counts being complete to 10^15 under one stated convention, and (ii) a reference prediction that is "local alone" and carries none of the global invariants it is to be correlated against. A labelled prerequisite asks whether (ii) holds for the prediction actually in use.
