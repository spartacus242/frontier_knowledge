## Restatement (my own words)

Take one sparse-MoE language-model recipe and train it several times changing only the seed (router/expert init and data order). OLMoE reports that, inside a single run, about 60% of each token's top-8 expert choices are already the final choices after 1% of training. The candidate asks whether the thing that gets fixed that early is the SAME thing in every run once experts are optimally relabelled (a recipe-level, canonical partition), a lottery each run draws differently (agreement no better than a permutation null), or a measurable core plus noise — and, separately, whether the 1%-checkpoint routing state of a run predicts that run's terminal loss and benchmark scores. Measured as ARI/NMI of the cross-run token-to-expert assignment after Hungarian matching, against a permutation null and an unrelated-run null, with the within-run saturation S as a descriptive reference, plus a between-seed variance share and an aux-loss-weight sweep.
