# C1 adversary log

## Restatement
Do the numbers of distinct traditional dishes in regions follow D = cA^z (or scale with population N). Is the exponent for true islands different from the exponent for nested mainland subregions, once documentation effort is controlled? Put plainly: does the species-area relationship transplant to cuisine, and does the island effect show up?

## Extra queries run (Disguise and prior spot-check)
1. number of dishes species-area relationship cuisine richness island cultural diversity dishes area scaling: only ecological SAR papers.
2. culinary repertoire size population size cultural evolution island food traditions number of recipes Henrich Tasmania: Henrich 2004, Vaesen 2016, and "Population Size and Cultural Evolution in Nonindustrial Food-Producing Societies" (PMC3772076). The last is the nearest hit. It is a food-producing-societies paper on technology, not dish counts, and I did not open it.
3. intangible cultural heritage elements count per country area power law; geographical indications number per region scaling area: nothing relevant.
4. number of traditional foods per region vs area population species-area analogue food heritage registry count regression: ethnobotany and registry papers, no scaling fit.
5. cultural traits richness island area isolation power law species-area folktales OR music OR crafts OR cuisine repertoire: Barbosa & Fontanari again, plus assorted cultural-diversity papers. No cuisine.
6. Check on the record's priors: Barbosa & Fontanari arXiv:0902.1346 resolves and is real (published in Theory in Biosciences). Kline & Boyd (PMC2894932) was not directly re-fetched. The search surfaced the same PMC family, and the citation is consistent with the literature I know. I count the priors as real.

Translated vocabularies: (a) linguistics / lexical typology, "repertoire or inventory size vs. community size" (Hay & Bauer, Lupyan & Dale, phoneme inventory vs population); (b) ecology / macroecology, "richness-area, Arrhenius, small-island effect"; (c) heritage / registry studies, "GI and ICH counts per region". None returned a dish-level fit. The record's "culture-area" and "repertoire-size" template is, however, the same question in other vocabularies, only for non-food items.

## Overall
Nothing is knocked out cleanly, but no attack is fully resolved either. The decisive weaknesses are on attack 2 (it is a known template with a new trait) and attack 4 (the unit "distinct dish" is not operationalized, and every proposed source imposes its own taxonomy). Neither is Unresolved, because the instance is not found and the unit problem has a partial fix. The candidate is a modest, probably publishable empirical application, not a deep unasked question.

**Recommended verdict: Provisional** (no Unresolved on attacks 1, 2 or 4; all eight Partial, weakest on 2, 4 and 7).
