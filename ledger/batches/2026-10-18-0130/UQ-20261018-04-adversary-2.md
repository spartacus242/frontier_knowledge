# UQ-20261018-04 — Adversary #2 (fresh context; earlier adversary log not read)

## Restatement (my own words)

Take one reusable surgical instrument. It has a biography: how many times it has been through
decontamination/sterilisation, and how old it is. Barcode/RFID instrument-tracking systems now hold
that biography per item and can say which case the item was in. The question asks for three
separate associations between that biography at the moment of use and three different things:
(i) the item breaking or being swapped out during the case (unit: item-use); (ii) the sterilisation
load or tray that carried it failing process control — BI failure, wet pack, recall (unit: load/tray);
(iii) the patient in that case getting an NHSN surgical-site infection at 30/90 days (unit: case,
exposure = max or mean item cycle count in the case). Each adjusted for service line, surgeon,
complexity, tray type, with a pre-declared negative-control exposure and a stated minimum
detectable effect.

In plain terms: **does an instrument's wear-and-tear mileage predict that it fails in theatre, that
its sterilisation batch fails, or that its patient gets infected?** The novel content the record
claims is the per-item mileage counter as an exposure; the policy target is retirement rules.

## Recommended verdict

**Rejected** — no fatal Unresolved came out of my eight attacks (1, 2 and 4 are Partial), but attack 7 is Unresolved, which caps this at Provisional, and Provisional requires Medium novelty with a close neighbour, which this record does not have: Gate 2's Low grade stands on P1 (which I could not re-verify within cap, so I rest nothing on it) and is independently corroborated by a paper I did open — Pitschner 2007 — which implemented per-item barcode reprocessing history joined to patient outcomes nineteen years ago in another device class. A narrowed question restricted to limbs (ii) and (iii), with the falsified "newly measurable, never used as an exposure" premise removed and the OR-record endpoint replaced by something that exists, would deserve a fresh Gate 2 rather than inheriting this verdict.
