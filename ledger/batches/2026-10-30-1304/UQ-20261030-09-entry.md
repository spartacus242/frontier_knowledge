Domain(s): Medicine — cancer (incomplete tumour elimination by chemotherapy; intratumoural microbiota). Acting community: pancreatic/GI medical oncology, surgical pathology, cancer-microbiome groups.
Question (canonical): In pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma resected after a gemcitabine-containing neoadjuvant regimen, is the per-microregion probability that a tumour cell is residual-and-viable graded by local bacterial occupancy at the 10-100 um scale, net of necrosis fraction, hypoxia marker and distance to nearest perfused vessel?
This is the Gate 1 reformulation (the one permitted), not the submitted wording. As submitted: "In human tumours resected after neoadjuvant chemotherapy with a drug that intratumoural bacteria can inactivate, are the surviving viable tumour cells spatially co-located with bacteria-colonised microniches at the 10-100 um scale, and does local bacterial load grade the local probability of tumour-cell survival?" The repair was forced by a pre-declared, unenumerated drug set; the set is now one member (gemcitabine) under a stated generating rule (bacterial enzymatic inactivation of the parent compound with the inactivated metabolite identified by mass spectrometry; Geller et al. 2017, HPLC-MS/MS, Figs 1C and 2B-C).
Alternative formulations: (1) Do bacteria inside a human tumour create drug-free sanctuaries in which tumour cells live through chemotherapy? (2) At micrometre resolution in post-treatment human specimens, what is the distance-resolved cross-correlation between bacterial niche occupancy and residual viable tumour cells, net of necrosis, hypoxia and vessel distance?
Motivating gap: Multiplex placement of bacteria and host cells in the same human tumour section exists and has been applied in human PDAC and in OSCC/CRC; bacterial inactivation of gemcitabine is established in vitro and in mice. The spatial prediction that follows - residual disease should sit where the bacteria are - is tested in no post-treatment human series anyone in this pipeline opened, although treatment-response grading of post-neoadjuvant specimens is routine pathology. In both spatial papers read in full the served text does not state the specimens' treatment status.
Depth (object test; deletion test): Object - the answer would be a fact about human tumour tissue: the joint micrometre-scale arrangement of bacteria and surviving tumour cells inside a resected block (tissues, cells, bacteria are on the pass list). Deletion - if every paper, registry entry and protocol vanished tonight, an archived post-neoadjuvant block still has surviving tumour cells at some set of distances from bacteria-occupied microniches; the tissue's own geometry fixes the answer. Both Pass in the Gate 0-3 record (rows test: one row = one tissue microregion or one tumour cell with its distance to the nearest bacterial niche; clauses (a)-(e) matched none, the contamination/detection-floor limb carried as a labelled prerequisite), and both adversaries re-ran the tests on their own restatements and recorded Pass. No Depth Fail anywhere, so this is not a Gate 0 rejection.
Presuppositions: (1) bacteria present in a substantial share of the relevant tumours at surgery, after chemotherapy and peri-procedural antibiotics - CONTESTED both ways on opened sources (May et al. 2025: bulk signal "generally low, comparable to those seen in negative controls"; Nalluri et al. 2021 Table 2: 3/7 gemcitabine, 2/2 gem/paclitaxel, 1/1 gem/oxaliplatin colonised, P = 0.303), and declared a MEASURAND rather than a premise, since the design reports occupancy and its detection floor as primary and calls sub-floor effects indeterminate; (2) bacterially mediated inactivation reaches concentrations that matter in human tissue - NOT ESTABLISHED: Geller et al. report no gemcitabine or dFdC/dFdU concentration inside human tumour tissue, nor inside the mouse tumours; (3) viable tumour cells distinguishable from treatment-effect debris in the same section - CONTESTED, not operationalised; (4) contamination controllable enough for a spatial claim - CONTESTED, carried as the labelled prerequisite limb; (5) the association is not both features tracking necrosis, hypoxia or vessel distance - measured covariates, not assumptions; (6) enough post-neoadjuvant specimens in >=2 independent series - NOW ESTABLISHED, correcting the record: the record graded this "one series only" and nominated an unopened ESMO abstract, and adversary 2 closed it on Nalluri et al. 2021 (Minnesota, 10/27 neoadjuvant patients, every one on a gemcitabine-containing regimen, malignant tissue sampled) beside May et al. 2025 (Columbia).
What would count as an answer: in >=2 independent series of post-neoadjuvant gemcitabine-regimen PDAC resections, per-section maps of bacterial occupancy and of viable tumour cells, reported as the odds of a residual viable tumour cell lying within versus beyond a pre-specified distance of a bacterial niche, with necrosis fraction, hypoxia marker and distance to nearest perfused vessel as measured covariates and a null from label permutation preserving each section's spatial autocorrelation. A distance-graded excess surviving the covariates makes local bacterial inactivation a candidate mechanism of patchy residual disease; no excess removes a leading spatial explanation; an effect below the reported contamination/detection bound is reported as indeterminate. The prerequisite limb reports that bound before any association is computed.

Strongest remaining objection: Attack 5 (vagueness), Unresolved on adversary 2. The two terms that decide the answer carry no thresholds: the bacterial-occupancy positivity criterion relative to tissue-free and probe-free controls, and the viable-versus-treatment-effect marker rule. This objection has two legs and the verdict stands on the FIRST. Leg one is documentary and survives alone: the record itself states both definitions are "named as contested but never stated", the three primary studies in evidence use incomparable positivity criteria (cycle-30 amplification; UMI >=3; Ct to 42.1 against controls), so there is no field standard to inherit, and a question whose two decisive terms are unoperationalised is under-specified whatever a sensitivity analysis would show. Leg two would fall if tested: adversary 2's claim that threshold choice can flip the sign of a distance gradient is labelled in its own log as "MY OWN REASONING, not a source finding", with the grounding search (sensitivity of spatial cross-correlation to probe-positivity cut-off) never run - so on a stricter reading attack 5 is Partial, and adversary 1 graded it exactly that. VULNERABILITY OF THIS VERDICT, recorded as required: leg two is a thing NOBODY HAS MEASURED. It is an EMPIRICAL claim about an unperformed sensitivity analysis, not an algebraic identity - no identity makes a cross-correlation sign threshold-dependent, and no published analysis of this tissue class establishes it either; it is not a bitwise or design-forced consequence. The single unopened source most likely to settle it is the 16S rRNA in-situ-hybridisation STAR Methods section of Li et al. 2024 (Cell Reports Medicine 5:101397), which prints a Ventana Discovery Ultra ISH protocol, a nest hot/cold threshold and a 150 um margin; the record's read stopped before that section, so the page is identified and simply unread.
