## Restatement (my own words, not the generator's)

Take every pair of published phylogenies that overlap in at least k taxa after names are reconciled. Prune both trees to the shared taxa. Find the clades each paper labelled at >=95% (nonparametric bootstrap, UFBoot or posterior probability). Count how often a >=95% clade in one paper is incompatible, as a bipartition, with a >=95% clade in the other. Because at most one of two incompatible clades can be right, the candidate calls that rate a lower bound on the joint error of the two labels, and so a calibration audit of the field's "95%" that needs no reference truth; it is to be reported by support type, by how much data the two papers share, and by era, and compared with a nominal benchmark declared per method. In one sentence: audit phylogenetic 95% labels against each other across the literature, the way forecasts are audited against outcomes, with a second paper standing in for the outcome. The repair (V2) replaced "independent later study" with a declared data-overlap stratifier and made the statistic explicitly a pair-level joint-error bound.
