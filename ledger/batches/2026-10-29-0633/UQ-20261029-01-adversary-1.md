## Restatement (my own words)

Inside the rock fragments brought back from Bennu and Ryugu there are microcracks that are said to lower the rock's thermal conductivity. The question asks which of two histories those cracks carry in their geometry: cracking by volume change while hydrous silicates reacted on the parent body (predicted to give cracks with no preferred orientation, spacing set by the thickness of clay-rich domains, and more cracks where the rock is more altered), or cracking by impact shock (predicted to give planar sets with a preferred orientation and a crack density that does not track alteration). Gate 1's reformulation (V2) replaces the continuous alteration index with the two alteration classes already distinguishable on the fragments, fixes the isotropy threshold from a laboratory volume-change-cracked analogue of the same phyllosilicate assemblage, and accepts the ~3 µm voxel scale that exists.
