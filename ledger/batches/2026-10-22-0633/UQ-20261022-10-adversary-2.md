## Restatement (my own words, not the generator's)

Pick a country and a time window. Make a list of every occasion on which the company behind an active implant stopped being able or willing to keep that implant working — it went bust, left the market, or simply ended service — using a definition of "stopped supporting" written down before anyone looks at data. For each occasion, say how many people are alive right now with that implant inside them. Then, for the occasions where those people can still be traced, ask whether they lost their therapy, needed a re-operation, had the device taken out, or became ineligible for an MRI or some other treatment — more often than before the company left, and more often than comparable people whose implant still has a live manufacturer behind it. Finally, report honestly what fraction of the occasions you could not even count, and what fraction of implant types you could not follow at all.

So: two measured limbs (an event list with exposed-population counts; an outcome contrast), plus one meta-limb (the share that is uncountable) that the candidate promotes to a result.
