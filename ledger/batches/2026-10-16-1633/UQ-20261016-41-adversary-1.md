# UQ-20261016-41 — Adversary #1 log (Gate 4)

## Restatement (my own words)
Published libm accuracy numbers are maxima found by adversarial/exhaustive search over the whole representable format. The candidate asks: if you instead weight by the arguments that real scientific/ML programs actually pass to exp, log, pow, sin, cos, tan, erf, exp2, rsqrt, tanh (recorded by interposing on live calls in a pre-specified corpus), how often do deployed implementations (glibc, LLVM-libc, a vendor CPU library, a GPU device library and its fast-math variant) disagree with one another and with the MPFR correctly-rounded value, what does the ulp-error distribution look like, and by what factor does that differ from the same quantities computed under uniform weighting over the format? Sub-claim I am testing: nobody has computed either arm, in any vocabulary.
