# Adversary #1 log — UQ-20261015-15 (affinity-tag bias in published enzyme kinetics)

## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)
Take every case in the published kinetics literature where the *same* enzyme (same organism, same sequence span, same mutational state) acting on the *same* substrate was measured both with and without an affinity tag (or with the tag at the other terminus), under comparable temperature/pH/buffer/co-substrate conditions. Pool those paired measurements and ask two things. (a) Does the tagged member of each pair sit systematically higher or lower in log10 kcat and log10 KM than the untagged member — a direction-consistent shift with a confidence interval, estimated with tag type, terminus and laboratory as model terms — or is the tag only noise? (b) If you re-extract those labels from the source papers and refit a published turnover-number predictor (DLKcat / TurNuP / CatPred / RealKcat) once with and once without tag/terminus stratification, do held-out error or top-k rankings move more than resampling noise? Pre-registered bands decide YES / NO / NOISE-NOT-BIAS, and (a)-YES-with-(b)-NO is an allowed fourth answer. The declared exposure is the tag-plus-affinity-purification package, not the tag alone.
