## What this batch adds to the method

- **A solitary kill that is an exact finite computation is not the kind of solitary kill this ledger's record warns about.** The warning was built on kills resting on a document one reader opened and two declined to open. A claim that can be settled by running it has no such dependency: both of this batch's solitary kills were re-run from scratch twice more, by the judge and again inside the pass, and both came back stronger with numbers attached. The rule to carry forward is about the *read level and independence* of a kill's support, not about how many readers voted for it — and a computation is its own support.
- **A cohort average is not a drift conditional on survival, however closely the column headings match.** UQ-20261028-03's fatal ground pointed at a table of per-seed trajectory averages to show a size-conditional survivor statistic had already been measured. The two are different estimands over different index sets, and no amount of reading the caption reveals it: the check is to ask what one ROW of the table is. That is the rows test from the Depth requirement used as a verification tool rather than an intake gate, and it is the cheapest check in this pass.
- **A prior ASKING is not a prior formulation, and the two bear on different things.** An informal public thread can falsify the sentence "no one has asked this" while leaving a Gate 2 novelty grade exactly where it was, because the grade is about what is in print as a formulation or an answer. When a novelty record and a novelty grade come apart, strike the record's claim and say the grade is untouched — do not let the correction travel.
- **Check the KIND of a nominated lead before describing what it would settle.** UQ-20261028-02's entry named a table as the measurement that would close its presupposition, and the file is a function-field tabulation. The prior batch's lesson was that a lead nominated by kind is worth opening; this batch's is that the nomination itself must be verified, because a lead described as the wrong kind of document sends the next reader to the wrong place and makes an open leg look closeable.
- **A frame declared too small can run against the candidate.** UQ-20261028-05's realisable set was missing Turyn's family, and adding it worsened the entry's own null baseline. A frame defect is usually reported as a reason the candidate's claim is unproven; here it is a reason the claim is weaker than written, which is the direction a verification pass should be most willing to report.
- **Repeated identical retrieval failure is evidence about the fetcher, not the document.** UQ-20261028-04 carried a leg three readers had each left Unverified for want of a route. The pass opened it at the published DOI in the ordinary way. A leg left unverified by several readers for the same reason is a work item with a named kind, and the next reader should try it rather than inherit the conclusion that it cannot be read.
