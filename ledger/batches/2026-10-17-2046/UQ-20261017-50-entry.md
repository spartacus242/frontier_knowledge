Domain(s): AI architecture — training systems and optimization (pretraining recipe engineering; secondary: scaling-law and ablation methodology)

Question (canonical): Restricted to runs declaring clipping of a GLOBAL gradient norm at nominal threshold c (c = 1.0 the index case), with a step CLIPPED when the stack's clip coefficient is < 1: (a) what is the clipped-step fraction as a function of training progress, per run, from released pre-clip gradient-norm series or instrumented reproductions; and (b) under a band pre-declared from seed replicates, are BOTH the clipped-step fraction AND the realized-update deviation (relative L2 and cosine of the applied update from the update that clipping-disabled would have applied, at matched checkpoints) invariant to (i) model scale along a ladder at fixed c and (ii) the parameter grouping over which the stack reduces the norm — and if not, what c'(scale, grouping) reproduces a reference run's two endpoints? (V2; the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation of V1, for well-posedness only — V1 left "actually active" and "same intervention" undefined and let limb (b) rest on gradient-norm scaling.)

Alternative formulations:
- When a recipe says "gradient clipping 1.0", how often does it clip, and does it mean the same thing at 1B and at 70B?
- Is the clipping threshold a hyperparameter that ladders and ablations hold fixed in name while varying in effect?

Motivating gap: Dense-how, empty-why. The clip appears in nearly every published LLM recipe, almost always at 1.0, and the literature is thick with methods for clipping better (ZClip arXiv:2504.02507, AdaGC arXiv:2502.11034, SPAMP arXiv:2510.01578, spectral clipping arXiv:2605.11838), while nothing found characterises what the shipped constant does in the runs using it. AdaGC motivates adaptive clipping by two named mismatches of a single global threshold (temporal: "The optimal global clipping threshold typically decreases over the course of training"; spatial: across tensors) and compares granularities, yet reports no activation rate, no argument about tightness versus parameter count, and does not treat one nominal threshold under different groupings as a comparability problem.

Presuppositions: P1 activation rate archive-recoverable — LARGELY ESTABLISHED from code read at first hand (OLMo optim.py computes total_grad_norm, clip_coef, and logs clipping_rate; verified by adversary 1 against allenai/OLMo canonical repo), residual: gated on collect_param_metrics, and under global fixed clipping clipping_rate degenerates to a 0/1 per-step indicator. P2 recipes expose the grouping — partly contested (stack VERSION decides). P3 gradient norm scales with size — CONTESTED and deliberately removed by V2's move to the realized update; both adversaries endorse that treatment. P5 varying constraint is a defect for a ladder's purpose. P6 norm- not element-clipping — removed by V2's frame restriction.

What would count as an answer: Per-run clipped-step fraction versus training progress for >= 2 stacks at >= 3 scales under one nominal c; realized-update deviation (relative L2, cosine) against the clipping-disabled counterfactual at matched checkpoints; and an equivalence mapping c'(scale, grouping). "Same intervention" if both endpoints sit inside the pre-declared replicate band — a null that licenses copying the constant across a ladder. Otherwise the mapping is the deliverable. Near-zero activation in some regime answers limb (a) and makes the constant vestigial there. Grouping arm must be run WITHIN one stack (adversary 2's specification note), else grouping is confounded with reduction precision, kernel order and DP sharding.
