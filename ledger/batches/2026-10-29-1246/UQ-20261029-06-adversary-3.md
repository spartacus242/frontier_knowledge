## 1. Restatement (my own words, not the generator's)

Fix one network, one initialisation, one training recipe and one token budget. Generate Gaussian inputs whose covariance eigenvalues decay as i^-beta. Train with minibatch SGD at learning rate eta and batch size B over a 6 x 9 grid. Afterwards, for every hidden weight matrix with min(m,n) >= 512, Hill-fit the top 10% of its eigenvalues (of W^T W) to get a tail exponent alpha. Ask: is the function alpha(beta, eta, B) one-dimensional in beta, one-dimensional in eta/B, one-dimensional in a monomial beta^a (eta/B)^b, or irreducibly two-dimensional — and what are the two partial slopes at the grid centre, with CIs?
