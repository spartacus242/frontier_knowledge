Question (canonical): In one genome-scale CRISPRi single-cell atlas (index case: K562 genome-wide Perturb-seq, ~9,600 primary-transcript perturbations), what fraction of the declared measured gene set is moved by no perturbation at all — reported as a function of a declared log-fold-change grid δ under a union-over-perturbations FDR with k ≥ 2, with RESPONSIVE / UNRESPONSIVE-AND-POWERED / UNDERPOWERED held strictly separate — and does the unresponsive-and-powered set recur in a second line (RPE1) above a declared null? (Scored version = V2; V1 as intake lacked the union rule and the power-coverage rule.)
Alternative formulations: How much of a cell's transcriptome is reachable by perturbing one gene at a time, and is there a part no single-gene knockdown touches? / Reading a Perturb-seq response matrix along the GENE axis rather than the perturbation axis, how many measured genes show no detected response to any perturbation once each gene's own detection power is accounted for, and do the same genes recur in a second line?
Motivating gap: Genome-scale Perturb-seq made the full perturbation × gene matrix measurable; every published analysis reads the perturbation margin (31.1% of perturbations have a transcriptional phenotype) and the gene margin was claimed unread. Gate 4 falsified the second half of that claim: the measured-unit margin is read, named and published in at least two adjacent literatures (see priors). The surviving gap is narrower than intake stated — the per-gene power three-class partition, not the gene-axis reading itself.
Presuppositions: (1) an atlas with enough cells per perturbation to declare per-gene power — established for one line; (2) per-gene power is computable so "not moved" separates from "not measurable" — LOAD-BEARING, unvalidated, no opened source does it on this object; (3) CRISPRi is an adequate instance of perturbing a gene — contested, scoped into the estimand (and confirmed as a live failure mode: "CRISPRi did not work well for weakly expressed TF genes", Nat Commun 2023); (4) declared expression filter excludes unexpressed genes — fine, but the atlas filter is "expressed >0.05 UMI per cell", so G is dominated by near-zero-power genes; (5) the library is dense enough that "any perturbation" ≈ "any single gene" — contested (yeast redundancy ≈29%); (6) non-response is not a compositional-normalisation artefact — contested, no spike-in arm available retrospectively; (7) reachability rather than invariance is what matters — flagged, significance re-routed off it; (8) FALSIFIED: that the measured-gene margin is unread in the literature.
