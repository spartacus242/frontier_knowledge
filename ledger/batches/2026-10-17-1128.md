## Three kills, three different ways of being wrong

**UQ-28 mistook a bound for a measurement.** Both adversaries read CorgiPile's `h_D` as the candidate's estimand under another name, and rejected the question as a disguised known. `h_D` is real, and the quotes are verbatim — but it is "the upper bound of a block-wise data variance", a constant carried in Theorems 1 and 2, whose extreme values 1 and b are stated as hypothetical limits. Across the three papers there is no numeric value, table or plot of it for any dataset, no measured gradient variance, no occurrence of "gradient noise scale", "critical batch size" or "large-batch training", and no LLM pretraining workload. Even the comparator the adversary leaned on is version-dependent: "uniformly and independently with replacement" does not occur in the arXiv/SIGMOD paper at all, only in the VLDB Journal extension. A prior that defines a quantity without measuring it is not a disguised known.

**UQ-30 cited a source against its own authors.** The rejection rested on MathArena having posed the convention question and answered it. The sentence quoted as its convention — "If all attempts fail, the solution is marked as incorrect" — is verbatim present, and sits under the heading "Fixed number of retries:", the option the authors explicitly decline: "We prefer the first strategy and do not report model performance until a sufficiently stable implementation is available." The choice is posed as two options, retry-until-success against fixed-retries, and dropping the item is not one of them. Three of the four other sources behind that kill turned out to be something adjacent too, and the fifth has an unread section.

**UQ-26's kill fell and a better one replaced it.** Gate 2's novelty ground did not survive a first-hand read of the paper it named: arXiv:2607.17283 reports a non-rejection at a resolution it states as "about 9,200 tokens per method", with no deviation magnitude, no confidence interval, no power analysis, no permutation null, no calibration and — decisively — no baseline-versus-baseline arm. But the late Gate 4 found what two adversaries and eleven Gate 2 queries had not: the candidate's estimand is not identified. Enabling speculation submits k+1 query rows where decoding submits one, changing the kernel path and reduction order even for a request alone in its batch, so batch composition is a post-treatment mediator and "the excess over a composition-matched baseline" has no estimand in a production engine. Attack 4 Unresolved is fatal, and the entry stays Rejected with its well-posedness lowered rather than its novelty.

## What to carry forward

1. **Every reversal in this batch and the last came from a kill stated about a source nobody had opened.** Six rejections across two batches, five withdrawn on first-hand reads. The adversaries were not careless — their quotes were verbatim each time. What they got wrong was what the quoted sentence was doing in its paper: a bound read as a measurement, a declined option read as a convention, a resolution limit read as a result. Gate 4 should keep asking for the quote, and Gate 5 should keep refusing to act on a quote whose surroundings nobody read.
2. **`[reasoning_extraction]` is now the dominant operational failure.** Twelve agents died at launch in this batch, including every cold adversary. The documented fix works, but it downgrades the instrument. If this continues, the eight-attack adversary brief needs splitting into short per-attack agents by default rather than as a fallback.
3. **The late Gate 4 is earning its place.** It produced the only rejection in this batch and the sourced cap on two of the four survivors. Three of its four runs changed the entry.
4. **"The nuisance exceeds the treatment" is a reusable kill.** It ended UQ-26 and it nearly ended UQ-30. Where a candidate proposes to measure an effect against a baseline the treatment itself moves, ask for the estimand before asking for the number.
