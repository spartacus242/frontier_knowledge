# UQ-20261015-10 — Gate 4 adversary #2 log

## Restatement (own words)
Take the open knockout-based antibody tests (YCharOS: about 1,200 commercial antibodies, about 120 human proteins). Analyse WB, IP and IF separately. After allowing for antibody type, vendor, host, immunogen (where known), target abundance and how well studied the target is, how much of the variation in whether an antibody works comes from which protein it targets? Then: do six protein properties explain that target share? The six are TM segment count, compartment, disorder, closest-paralog identity, PTM density and length. In short: are some proteins intrinsically "hard to antibody", and is that because of membrane topology, paralogs, disorder or modification?

## Fetches (spot-checks of the record's cited priors)
- bioRxiv 2023.06.01.543292 (Ayoubi et al.), full text -> CONFIRMED: 614 antibodies / 65 targets; 56 intracellular and 9 secreted; about 40% of targets lack a successful IF antibody; 9/65 have only non-selective WB antibodies; no analysis by target property.
- bioRxiv 2022.11.24.517810 (anti-GPCR screen) -> CONFIRMED: 407 antibodies, 215 GPCRs (205 assessed), 61% (248/407) selective. Cross-reactivity was attributed to homology or expression ratio, and on-target antigens were longer and more disordered. NEW DETAIL: cross-reactivity was similar (10-20%) across subfamilies. That is a cross-subfamily null at the family level, and the record omits it.
- bioRxiv 2024.10.07.617016 (Waury et al. 2025) -> PARTLY CONTRADICTS THE RECORD. The paper states that "the presence of a transmembrane region within the protein to be detected led to a lower percentage of successful antibodies". It analyses this at the level of the protein, separately from TM overlap of the immunogen (Fig. 5A). It also tests disulfide-bridge presence (103 proteins; p = 0.687). The record says Waury's target-level TM finding "concerns coverage (whether any antibody was made), not pass rate". That is false. Waury reports target-level pass-rate effects of target features (TM, disulfides) on HPA validation outcomes.
- doi.org/10.1007/s00210-009-0395-y (Michel 2009) -> NOT READ (proxy 429). The record's description is unverified by this adversary.

## Recommended verdict
**Rejected.** Attack 2 (Disguise) is Unresolved, and attack 3 is also Unresolved. Rejection is mandatory under Gate 4. The decisive point: Waury et al. 2025 already reports that target-protein TM presence lowers antibody success rates at the protein level. The record's differentiator ("coverage, not pass rate") is a misreading. Also, nobody has run the Disguise search in the unrun vocabularies (0 of 8 adversary queries ran, and the generator ran 7 of 12). Re-entry would require a complete Gate 2 rerun, re-scoping to part (i) only (knockout-ground-truth target ICC across vendors), and explicitly positioning the candidate as a knockout-based replication and extension of Waury.
