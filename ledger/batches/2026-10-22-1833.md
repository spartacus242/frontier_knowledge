## The two rejections whose ground survived

**UQ-20261022-17** (round-trip failure rate of shipped multilingual tokenizers) was rejected at Gate 2, novelty Low, and confirmed independently at Gate 4 by the second adversary's Unresolved on attack 2. It also GOT STRONGER on a leg the batch never recorded: Tables 2 and 3, read in full, OMIT Reconstruction Exact Match and CER from their 14 and 9 intrinsic metrics — a NOT FOUND in those words, so the paper names the metric and then does not report it.

Three defects in the entry's own prose were fixed in place: §3.4 NAMES AND INTERPRETS the metrics while Table 1 DEFINES them (the entry credited 3.4 with both); the paper NAMES THREE MECHANISMS with "or" rather than enumerating a closed taxonomy; and the entry's quotation was the left half of a colon-joined sentence given without an ellipsis. And the sentence the entry was missing is now in it: the second adversary's nine sweeps found no cross-field disguise at all, so this Unresolved rests WHOLLY on a definitional prior in a single unvenued preprint with no measurement behind the metric. The kill lands against a candidate that would RAISE the question; it does not reach one that would MEASURE a script-stratified fidelity rate.

**UQ-20261022-19** (within-item against between-item decomposition of the accuracy-versus-reasoning-token curve) was rejected as asked-but-still-open on arXiv:2606.26502 §4.2, and both of the entry's quoted sentences are verbatim in the paper at full text with the grammar intact on both sides of the cut. **But the entry's central absence claim was true only on a wording artefact, and that is this batch's sharpest finding.** The entry demanded a RANDOMIZED budget experiment. Zhou, Ling, Chen, Wang, Fan & Wang 2026 (arXiv:2604.10739v1 §4.1.3) had already run a SATURATED complete crossing — 32 budget levels against every problem across AIME 2024 and 2025, MATH-500 and GPQA Diamond — which identifies a within-item slope BETTER than randomization. ## The signature defect: an unenumerated universal

Two entries on unrelated subjects asserted "every" about a KIND of artifact without anyone enumerating instances, and both were wrong on the instance nobody opened.

- UQ-20's "every public production request-length corpus strips prompt content" is true of every corpus CHECKED and false as a universal: WildChat-1M and LMSYS-Chat-1M retain content. That one cost the kill.
- UQ-18's claim that limb (ii)'s denominator is ambiguous by a factor of two is real in three of four repositories opened (Qwen's 14 against 28 px, InternVL's 32x32 against 16x16 pre- and post-shuffle) and false for LLaVA-1.5-7B, which has one grid and exactly one defensible interval.

Both were narrowed from "every" to "every checked", with the instances named. The cheapest guard is the one the runner already carries for document kinds: before writing a universal, open two or three actual instances — and when the claim is enumerative rather than proved, say so in the claim.

A second defect worth recording: **two checkers misdescribed the entry they were testing.** One read a Low-grade trigger as a Reject trigger and misstated the entry's read level; one twice named the wrong paper as an entry's rejection ground. Both were corrected rather than inherited, and the amending agent caught a third — a provenance error in the brief it was given, which attributed Mooncake's synthetic-trace file to ServeGen — and declined to propagate it, citing the artifact it had read at first hand.
