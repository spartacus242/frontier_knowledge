Domain(s): Other STEM — civil/environmental engineering; LCA; construction-materials standardisation (EN 15804 / ISO 21930 and its PCRs); public procurement policy. Method-owning neighbours: metrology (variance components / MSA), composite-indicator sensitivity analysis (rank reversal).

Question (canonical):
On a pre-declared, machine-extracted frame of published EPDs for construction products, partitioned into functional classes by a normalisation fixed before any data pull (declared unit, performance/strength class, reference service life, stage set A1-A3 minimum), with the method-effect estimator declared in advance as the within-product contrast across repeated declarations of the SAME product under differing method configurations (PCR version, allocation rule, background-database identity and vintage, declared-unit conversion, programme operator, verifier): (i) what share of within-class variance in declared GWP is attributable to method/background-data configuration versus producer/plant identity; (ii) under harmonised recomputation at one declared reference configuration, what fraction of pairwise within-class comparisons reverse sign and what fraction of compliance determinations against the threshold in force at declaration date (e.g. a Buy Clean maximum acceptable GWP) cross the threshold; (iii) what fraction of the frame was dropped for missing method metadata, by field?
(V1 intake wording and the Gate 1 reformulation rationale are in batches/2026-10-17-1823/UQ-20261017-45-record.md.)

Alternative formulations:
- Do EPDs measure factories or methods, and does the answer change who wins a Buy Clean procurement?
- For products carrying multiple declarations, how large is the method-induced component of declared GWP relative to the plant-to-plant component, and does it exceed the margin at which procurement thresholds are set?

Motivating gap:
Silence mapping. The standards regime specifies how to compute and verify a declaration; every declaration prints a non-comparability caveat; procurement then compares declared values against numerical thresholds anyway. California DGS's statutory *Buy Clean California Act GWP adjustment study* (1 Jan 2025) names programme-operator plurality, inconsistent review periods, modelling assumptions and datasets/software as what its own discrepancies "could be attributed to", quantifies none of them, and selects industry-wide EPDs for "consistency"; EC3, which mediates most US low-carbon material selection, assigns a flat 3% LCIA-method uncertainty term with allocation "not modeled" and states its methodology "has not been extensively peer-reviewed". The corpus is now machine-readable at the scale of tens of thousands of declarations.

Presuppositions:
1. Enumerable, linkable corpus with extractable method metadata and adequate per-class n. CONTESTED and binding (metadata completeness, not corpus size).
2. Functional equivalence enforceable by declared normalisation. Supported.
3. Harmonised recomputation / identified method-correction possible; EPDs publish results not inventories, so identification may rest on the repeat-declared subset, which may be unrepresentative. CONTESTED — binding threat to limb (i).
4. Plant-level production differences real and non-negligible. Supported (DGS facility counts).
5. Thresholds known, binding, applied to declared values as published. Supported (BCCA limits statutory and changed 1 Jan 2025; rebar 890->755).
6. Declared values not already strategically selected by producers (PCR, verifier, declaring plant). CONTESTED — makes part of the "method component" behavioural.
7. PCR committees, programme operators and agencies would act on a measured split. CONTESTED.

What would count as an answer:
(i) a within-class variance decomposition of declared GWP into method/background-data and producer/plant components, estimated from repeated declarations of the same product under differing configurations, with transportability from the repeat-declared subset reported as a sensitivity rather than assumed; (ii) the share of pairwise within-class comparisons and of threshold determinations that reverse under harmonised recomputation, with uncertainty; (iii) frame dropout by metadata field. An explicit null — small method component, few reversals — counts as an answer.

Significance — consequence:
Unchanged by the rejection and genuine in outline: if method components dominated and determinations crossed under harmonisation, Buy Clean-style limits would be sorting producers partly by LCA consultant, PCR vintage and database licence rather than by kiln, furnace or grid — fixable by harmonised recomputation as a procurement requirement or method-robust threshold bands. If the null held, the printed non-comparability caveat would be shown over-conservative in practice and EC3's assigned factors replaceable by measured ones. The opened corpus evidence (P4, Anderson & Moncaster, AzariJafari) already points toward the null for the A1-A3 stage set that Buy Clean regulates, which is what deflates the magnitude case built on P1's 12-50% swings.

V1. CONFIRMED, read as full text (MDPI HTML; the PDF route returned 403, and table cell values were not visible). The corpus is as described: EPS 97 EPDs, stone wool 41, bricks 60, ready-mix concrete 79 GWP values, cement 64 GWP values, natural stone 58 certifications, steel sections 59 plus rebar 20, windows 70 valid EPDs giving 178 GWP values, wood 101, plus PV 24 and heat pumps 7 excluded as too few; Section 2.1 lists 25 sources plus the ECO Platform umbrella, with no stated total, and the concrete and cement counts are GWP VALUES rather than EPDs. The paper does test programme operator, background database, country and producer organisation against physical and process variables, per product class, using t-tests, bootstrapped t-tests, ANOVA, Kruskal-Wallis, Spearman, linear regression with R-squared, a White heteroscedasticity test and k-means. All five quoted statistics are present in the running text: concrete strength class F = 15.03, p = 3.57e-15; cement strength class F = 8.4, p = 0.0006; steel basic-oxygen-furnace against electric-arc-furnace Kruskal-Wallis p = 2.73e-8; concrete programme operator F = 6.84, p = 0.0002; concrete country F = 4.42, p = 0.0008. The concluding sentence is there, in the Discussion's final paragraph: the programme operator and the LCA background database "did not play a very relevant role in determining embodied climate change impacts of all products considered", attributed to European methodological harmonisation. So the kill's central claim — that this question is asked and answered in the candidate's own literature, on the candidate's own corpus type — is CORRECT and first-hand, and the adversary that found it had genuinely read it.
V2. CORRECTION: the paper performs NO VARIANCE DECOMPOSITION. Checked across the whole body: NOT FOUND for any share of variance, eta-squared, or split of the declared-GWP spread into a methodological and a real-performance component; the only R-squared is single-predictor (stone wool density against GWP, 0.818). So it answers "is this factor significant?" and not "how much of the difference is attributable to it?", which is the candidate's estimand. That is an estimand difference, not merely an estimator one.
V3. CORRECTION: it tests TWO of the candidate's four methodological factors. Product category rule version is NOT tested as a factor — Section 2.1 treats EN 15804 +A1 and +A2 as substantially equivalent — the allocation method is not examined at all, and the declared-unit conversion appears only as a harmonisation STEP the authors perform (tonnes via density, square metres via thickness, insulation at 1 m2 and R = 1 m2K/W over 50 years), never as a factor whose effect is estimated. Procurement appears only as an Introduction citation to EU Green Public Procurement, with no conclusion that methodological choices change a procurement decision.
V4. CORRECTION, and the most useful one: the paper's headline does not hold for concrete, and the paper does not say so. For ready-mix concrete the programme operator (F = 6.84, p = 0.0002) and the country (F = 4.42, p = 0.0008) ARE significant; Section 3.3 explains this by an overlap between programme operators and geography and does not test that explanation or control for it, and the Discussion's "all products considered" sentence carries no carve-out for concrete. The paper also calls the result "substantial equivalence" immediately after reporting significance. So within the very corpus the kill cites, the one highest-volume product class shows a significant methodological-factor effect that is left unexplained and unquantified.
