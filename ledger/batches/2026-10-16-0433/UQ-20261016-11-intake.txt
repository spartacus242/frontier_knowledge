Source method: anomaly mining
Domain(s): STEM — pharmaceutical solid-state chemistry / crystallization science (with aerosol-particle analysis as the measuring discipline)
Question: In laboratories and pilot plants with a documented handling history of a compound that has a metastable and a stable polymorph, is crystallographically active seed material of the stable form recoverable from air and surfaces at quantities above the threshold that determines which form crystallizes, and with what persistence after handling stops?
Alternatives: Is the 'disappearing polymorph' phenomenon caused by an ambient seed burden that can be measured directly — swabbed, counted, form-identified, and shown to decay — rather than inferred after the fact? / For a given compound and room, what is the dose–response between recovered environmental residue (mass and polymorphic form) and the probability that an unseeded crystallization yields the stable rather than the metastable form?
Motivating gap: Disappearing polymorphs are a repeatedly reported, named anomaly: once the thermodynamically stable form of a compound has been obtained somewhere, the previously reproducible metastable form often becomes impossible to prepare, across unrelated laboratories (Dunitz & Bernstein, 'Disappearing Polymorphs', Acc. Chem. Res. 1995, 28:193–200; Bučar, Lancaster & Bernstein, 'Disappearing Polymorphs Revisited', Angew. Chem. Int. Ed. 2015, 54:6972–6993; the canonical case is ritonavir Form II, Bauer et al., Pharm. Res. 2001, 18:859–866 — all three recalled from memory, NOT re-verified this run, see search note). Deliberate seeding is quantitatively engineered in industry and cleaning validation already swabs surfaces for drug residue by HPLC, so both halves of the measurement exist; what has never been put together is a measurement of the involuntary seed burden of a working room — mass, polymorphic form, viability as a seed, and decay time. The anomaly is attributed to airborne/fomite seeding in review after review and the attribution is treated as untestable folklore. Presuppositions: A compound exists with a documented metastable form that became hard to prepare after the stable form appeared, and laboratories with contrasting handling histories can still be identified and sampled.; Seed-free (or seed-controlled) crystallization conditions reproducibly give the metastable form in a naive environment — CONTESTED: the historical records that establish this are often informal, and the original conditions may not be fully recoverable.; Seeding by trace environmental material is a real causal route for the disappearance — CONTESTED: competing explanations include unrecognized impurities acting as nucleation inhibitors or templates, humidity and scale changes, and publication-side attrition of negative results.; Recovered residue can be form-identified at the quantities present (nanogram-to-microgram on a swab), by Raman/XRD on collected particles or by a seeding bioassay — CONTESTED for XRD; the bioassay route makes it a functional rather than structural assay.; 'Crystallographically active' is operationalizable as a flip in the outcome of a standardized supersaturated crystallization, not merely as detected mass.; Handling history of candidate rooms is documentable well enough to rank exposure.
What would count as an answer: A sampling campaign over rooms stratified by documented exposure to the stable form: surface swabs and air samples, extracted, quantified by HPLC for mass, and assayed for seeding activity by introduction into a standardized supersaturated solution held under conditions that give the metastable form in naive, seed-controlled apparatus. An answer is a dose–response curve from recovered quantity to probability of the stable form, with (i) a threshold quantity, (ii) a persistence half-life measured by re-sampling rooms after handling ceased and after defined cleaning protocols, and (iii) form identification of recovered particles where mass permits. 'Yes' = exposed rooms carry recoverable seeding activity above the flip threshold and naive/cleaned rooms do not, with the dose–response monotone. 'No' = exposed and naive rooms are indistinguishable in both recovered activity and crystallization outcome, which would redirect the explanation of disappearance to impurity, humidity or scale and make the seeding account unsupported rather than merely unproven.
