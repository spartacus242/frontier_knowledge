Verification (post-batch, fresh-context checker, log UQ-20261017-10-verification.md): the kill stands in the regime that carries the entry's significance, but two of the three legs it was argued from are mis-sourced, and the entry's own arithmetic was being presented as a published figure.
Leg by leg. **(1) The padding ratio.** The 0.01-0.08% range is real and is Table 1 of arXiv:2407.07495 (bucket pre-training), whose fixed-length rows read 1024: 0.01%, 2048: 0.01%, 4096: 0.02%, 8192: 0.04%, 16384: 0.08%, against the paper's own bucketing method at 0.12% and document-level at 0.0% ("Padding ratio: The proportion of padding tokens in training sequences"). But it is **not a padding ratio published from a large pretraining run**: it is a data-composition statistic computed on "a representative subset consisting of 100B tokens (approximately 98M documents)" of FineWeb-Edu, and that paper's own 1B Llama3.1-architecture experiments report no padding figure. No lab technical report with a published padding figure was found. The entry's phrasing is corrected accordingly: the figure predicts what a fixed-length-packed run's ratio would be, over a realistic 98M-document corpus at exactly the sequence lengths such runs use, and that is what the kill needs — but it is a computed statistic from one 2024 paper, not a reported property of any published run.
**(2) The "uniform by construction" step.** The checker reports plainly that the clause "so essentially every token carries equal weight in the loss" **is stated by no source it read**. That step is therefore the ledger's own algebra, not a sourced claim, and is relabelled here as such: with sequences of equal length, no masking, and a padded share of order 1e-4, per-micro-batch mean normalisation gives per-token weights uniform to within that order, so limb (b)(i)'s zero-weight share is ~0, the Kish effective-to-nominal ratio is ~1 and the induced mixture TV distance is ~0. The arithmetic is elementary and the inputs are sourced; the inference is the ledger's.
**(3) The October 2024 diagnosis.** CONFIRMED and dated: Unsloth's blog of October 15, 2024 and Hugging Face's of October 16, 2024 both identify the gradient-accumulation loss-normalisation defect, quoting "naively averaging over each gradient accumulation step is wrong" and a correct denominator of "the total number of all non padding tokens in those batches". The checker adds the qualification that matters: those posts concern **padded, variable-length** settings, mostly fine-tuning, and are "not about fixed-length packing", so they do not themselves establish that packed pretraining is free of the defect. That is consistent with the entry's regime split rather than against it — the posts answer the SFT/RL limb, the padding ratio answers the packed-pretraining limb — but the entry no longer claims they cover both. The practice premise is separately supported: arXiv:2404.10830 states that "input documents are typically concatenated together and then split into sequences of equal length to avoid padding tokens", with its own best-fit packing leaving a "negligible amount of padding" (no number given).
