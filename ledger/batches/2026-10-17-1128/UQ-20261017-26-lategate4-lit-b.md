## Q1 reasoning (mine, not sourced)
Let y(r,s,c) = output of request r under speculation flag s and batch context c. In exact arithmetic y(r,1,c)=y(r,0,c) (greedy) or equal in distribution (sampling), so every observed deviation is a numerics-through-shape effect. c is not exogenous: s changes c at the row level (k+1 rows/query-len k+1 vs 1; different attention and GEMM kernel paths), and at the schedule level (variable accepted tokens per step => request trajectories, context lengths and co-residency drift apart across arms, data-dependently). So s -> c -> y is a mediated path and the "direct" path cannot be separated in the production instrument. Survives: (a) total contrast between two full serving configs under a fixed workload; (b) noise floor within each arm over compositions and launches; (c) excess-over-noise as a distributional comparison, not subtraction (divergence is thresholded/chaotic, rates are not additive); (d) teacher-forced logit deviation at fixed prefixes, which fixes the prefix and lets co-resident composition be varied independently. Does not survive: row-level composition matching; a "net of baseline" scalar by subtraction; pairing sampled outputs token-by-token (RNG coupling differs even in exact arithmetic); the direct algorithmic effect, which is zero by the proof and non-zero numbers are shape effects. The 53.8% vs 96.9% figures come from different models/engines so cannot be compared as noise-reduction evidence.
