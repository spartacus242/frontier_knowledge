Confounding by exit cause and by exit-triggered ascertainment/policy (the clause "in the post-exit period, compared with the same recipients' pre-exit period and with matched recipients of still-supported devices") — TESTED on the largest real instance of a liquidated manufacturer's implant population (F1). The candidate assigns exposure at the device-model level, so for passive classes it would mis-assign the support loss to the wrong exiting party. Sourced to paras 2(x) and 34 as served.
