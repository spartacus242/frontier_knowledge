## Restatement (my own words)

Take one high-resolution capture (Visium HD 2 µm or Stereo-seq DNB-level) and re-aggregate it to a ladder of square bin sizes. Run the SAME spatially-variable-gene test and the SAME domain clustering at fixed k on each rung. Question: how much of the variation in the SVG rank list and in the domain partition is explained by which rung you picked, versus by the tissue, once you subtract how much those outputs wobble under resampling at a single rung — and is there a rung beyond which the bin-size share drops under that wobble? Cell-level pipelines (segmentation, ligand–receptor) are set aside as a separate limb because they presuppose cells.

Unit of analysis: a (dataset, method, output) triple evaluated across supports. Comparator: other supports of the same tissue. Estimand: support-attributable share of output variance, against a within-support resampling floor. Endpoint: a support-output curve with a noise band; existence of s*.
