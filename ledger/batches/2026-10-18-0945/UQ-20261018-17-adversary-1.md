## Restatement (my own words)

Take the published papers in which someone claims to have rebuilt a cellular process out of purified parts in a tube. For each such paper and each part, divide the concentration the part was used at in the tube by the concentration that same molecule is actually measured at in the cell where the process is said to happen. Report the whole spread of those ratios (log scale, with error bars on both the numerator and the denominator), and say in what fraction of papers the worst-offending component was pushed more than ~3x, 10x or 100x past its cellular level. Then the hard limb: take some of those papers back to the bench, set *every* component to its cellular concentration, and ask whether the paper's own main readout still appears above a pre-declared detection floor — i.e. whether "these parts suffice" survives being run at the cell's own doses. A third limb asks whether a big fold-excess at publication predicts that someone later found an extra component was required after all.

In one line: is "purified components are sufficient" in practice "purified components are sufficient when overdriven", and by how much?

## Spot-check of the record's cited priors

- **Prior 1 (Farahi et al., IJMS 2021;22(6):3017) — REAL, read on the publisher page, numbers confirmed verbatim.** "only 25 of the 75 proteins (33.3%) could undergo LLPS in vitro at a concentration that is within the range"; "by less than (17 proteins; 22.7%)"; "more than (33 proteins; 44%) an order of magnitude"; frame "a high-confidence dataset of 89 human LLPS drivers", "the subset of 78 proteins", PaxDb "available for 75 proteins"; and the comparator caveat the record quotes — PaxDb values "should, therefore, be considered as theoretical lower limits." The record transcribed this prior accurately.
- **One discrepancy I found that the record does not flag:** the same page states "for 30 of these proteins, it was not possible to relate the reported concentrations to the LLPS Csat values", yet the three bins sum to 75. So Prior 1's bins are built on *lowest reported in-vitro LLPS concentration* for a substantial subset, not on Csat throughout — a heterogeneous numerator. This is a real weakness in the existing answer and it enlarges the candidate's residual slightly. It does **not** restore the motivating gap: the ratio, the frame, the PaxDb comparator and the fraction-above-threshold shape are all still published.
- Priors 2 and 3 were not re-verified (budget); both were surfaced independently by my D4 and the record's own routes, and nothing in my attacks turns on their contents beyond what the record quotes.
