Question (canonical): At a fixed pretraining checkpoint theta, what is the design effect deff(theta, B) of a stack's shipped batch formation (shard assignment + shuffle buffer + rank sharding) — the variance of the minibatch gradient's scalar projection on the full-corpus gradient direction under the real sampling scheme divided by the same variance under i.i.d.-with-replacement resampling of the same corpus at identical nominal B, the two arms differing in nothing but design — what within-batch gradient ICC rho does it imply under deff = 1 + (m-1)rho for a declared m, does multiplying tr(Sigma) by deff move B_simple = tr(Sigma)/|G|^2 and the critical-batch-size prescription past a pre-registered band (NULL <= 1.1 / MODEST 1.1-2 / MATERIAL > 2), and is the correction stable across checkpoints and shuffle-buffer sizes? (V1, the single permitted Gate 1 reformulation; V0 and the reason for reformulating are in the record.)
Alternative formulations: (1) How much does shard-and-shuffle-buffer clustering inflate pretraining gradient variance, and does the inflation bias the batch-size rules read off measured gradient noise? (2) What is the design-corrected effective batch size of a large pretraining run — how many i.i.d. examples is a nominal batch of B worth? (3) When a GNS tracker reports B_simple, is it reporting the noise of the run's actual sampling design or of a counterfactual i.i.d. one?

Footnote 6 is verbatim and is the sixth footnote in the PDF numbering: "This is strictly true only when training examples are sampled independently from the same data distribution." and "but our conclusions can be altered straightforwardly to account for correlated samples." The bias, however, is not signed a priori. Writing h(B) for the ratio of realized to independent per-example variance, the two-batch estimator's bias in |G|^2 is tr(Sigma)(h_b - h_s)/(B_b - B_s): exactly ZERO when h is constant in B (batches spanning many whole clusters, h = 1 + (m-1)rho, the whole effect landing in S instead), POSITIVE when both batches sit inside one cluster, and NEGATIVE for without-replacement or stratified designs (h = 1 - B/D gives -tr(Sigma)/D exactly). The adversary's determinate downward sign holds only under whole-batch exchangeable correlation, and the paper derives no sign at all. So the clause does not break the estimand: which way a given loader biases a reported noise scale is a property of that loader's design, which is the thing the candidate proposes to measure.
