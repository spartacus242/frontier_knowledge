"Observed rates of surgical instrument errors point to visualization tasks as being a critically vulnerable point in sterile processing and a significant cause of lost chargeable OR minutes." BMC Surgery 2024;24(1) (April), DOI 10.1186/s12893-024-02407-1. Pediatric ORs, two-hospital campus, summer 2021.
Q2. Not stated in those words. No sentence says malfunction is "not routinely recorded in OR documentation". Nearest, Introduction: "staff reporting of these problems is burdensome, frequently incomplete, delayed, and underreports". Discussion: "the current staff driven reporting mechanisms appear to greatly underreport these events". Abstract: historical reporting via "a computer database that is not integrated into the electronic medical record". Paper is about sterile-processing instrument errors (missing, broken/poorly functioning, tray problems), not an OR-record malfunction endpoint. The earlier reader's claim is a loose paraphrase at best.
