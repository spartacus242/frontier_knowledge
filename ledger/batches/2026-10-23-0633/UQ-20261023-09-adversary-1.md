## Restatement (my own words)

Take a device the FDA has cleared to issue a diabetic-retinopathy screening decision on its own (refer / do not refer / cannot grade). Photograph a patient once, then have a second member of staff photograph the same patient again on another unit of the same camera model during the same visit, and feed both sets to the device. In how many patients does the decision change? And is that change rate larger or smaller than the width of the confidence intervals the FDA accepted for the device's sensitivity and specificity? The candidate wants this measured in ordinary clinical deployment, not in a trial.

Regime check written before anything else: the candidate's own regime is (population) patients in routine deployment of a cleared autonomous device; (unit) the patient-visit decision; (condition) re-acquisition by a different operator on the same camera model within one visit; (instrument) the cleared device + named camera; (threshold) the device's own referral categories incl. insufficient quality; (category) self-agreement, compared with clearance accuracy margins.
