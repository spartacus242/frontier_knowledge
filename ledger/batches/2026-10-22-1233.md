## Did the three v12 runner rules fire? All three, and the third one earned its place

The v12 runner carried three rules written from the previous batch's defects. All three fired on their first run.

**2. The clause-by-clause attack 4** (write the question's conditioning clauses as a numbered list, then grade each TRUE / FALSE / CONTESTED / UNVERIFIED, each sourced). Run in all 13 adversary logs — 39 CONTESTED and 63 UNVERIFIED gradings between them. Both of the batch's kills came out of it, and both came out of it twice independently.

**3. The measurand-not-presupposition exemption** (if the question already converts a contested clause into a reported quantity rather than assuming it, that clause is a measurand and attack 4 does not reach it). **Applied — not merely considered — in 10 of the 13 logs**, several of which say in terms that they are declaring the exemption "rather than grading around it". ## The two rejections

**UQ-20261022-11** dies on a definitional referent. The frame's members do not carry the quantity the question bounds. `current-utc-offset` in `ietf-ptp@2019-05-07.yang` (RFC 8575) is declared, in the module's own words, "The offset between TAI and UTC when the epoch of the PTP system is the PTP epoch in units of seconds, i.e., when ptp-timescale is TRUE; otherwise, the value has no meaning." A checker read the module byte-exact from raw.githubusercontent.com, so the quotation is uncapped and whole. The same `Integer16 currentUtcOffset` appears in linuxptp's announce struct with `#define CURRENT_UTC_OFFSET 37 /* 1 Jan 2017 */`, assigned straight into the Linux `ADJ_TAI` offset under the comment "Update TAI-UTC offset of the system clock if valid and traceable"; NTP's leap indicator is two bits and four codes with no magnitude field at all. So a largest-representable-UT1−UTC has no referent for three of the four named instances and no field whatever for the fourth. Both conditions the brief named for this kill falling — that the definition is not what the entry says, or that the named instances do carry UT1−UTC — came back **false on the documents**.

**UQ-20261022-14** dies on an operator conversion that does not exist. The fatal leg was re-tested on all three named instances *and* on the document class the entry itself nominated as its best hope of a counterexample. USGS 07312109 publishes "0.00 ft Above NGVD 29" plus "add 0.82 feet" to NAVD 1988 and no geoid model; the Hudson River – Black River Regulating District publishes NGVD29-to-NAVD88 numbers only; the USACE Kaw Lake page names no datum at all, confirmed verbatim against every search term. ## What the pass changed without moving a verdict

**One claim falsified outright.** UQ-14 asserted that nobody has stated the magnitude of the NAVD88-to-geopotential difference. That is false, and it is falsified by the very document the entry recorded as never opened: NOS NGS 72 (Ahlgren, Scott, Zilkoski, Shaw, Paudel, May 2020) prints 56.2 cm — Table 13 mean 56.22 cm over 32,357 bench marks — alongside the hedged, cited estimate putting NAVD 88 "55 +/- 2 cm below the W0 = 62 636 856.0 m2/s2 surface (Bursa, et al 2004)", and NOS NGS 64 prints a 50 cm average CONUS bias with about 1 m of tilt. The entry now carries the figure with its hedges and three margin warnings, the first being that the offset is not national: the same table holds **−42.74 cm** for Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands, so "a ~50 cm bias in NAVD 88" stated as a general property overreaches its own source.

**Three misquotations, each verbatim and each cut where the surrounding clause changes its force.** This is the batch's signature defect, and it is the same mechanism three times over.

- UQ-15's selection-policy quotation drops the opening **"In such cases,"** that scopes it to discrepancy resolution, drops the preceding "a small number of discrepancies in the transition frequencies", and drops the trailing "and also considered the type of experiment used to derive the value in the empirical line list" — which makes it a two-criterion judgment about one parameter class of one molecule, not a selection rule. The universal form of that leg is struck: a merge policy selecting a single source per parameter is **not stated at all**, as a policy, anywhere in either HITRAN2020 or HITRAN2024's readable window, and several per-molecule statements in HITRAN2024 are explicitly two-source hybrids, which cuts directly against it.
- UQ-11 quotes four verbatim words, "UT1 − UTC", from a Steigenberger et al. clause that says the opposite of what they were used for: for GPS the paper records that "∆UT1 = UT1 − UTC has been replaced by ∆UT1GPS = UT1 − GPST", GPST being "a continuous time scale" that "avoids potential problems in the vicinity of leap second changes", and it states that "no details about the origin of the ERP data and the parameterization of the ERP prediction model are provided for BDS, QZSS, and IRNSS". The paper does supply something: a **third** incommensurable offset kind, UT1 − GPST, so the fatal leg now reads "at least three", not two.
- UQ-15's PriSpecTemp quotation is cut before "At the time of the Oyafuso article publication," which restricts an "only source" assertion to a point in time.

**Two legs of a fatal clause struck, with the clause surviving on the rest.** UQ-14 claimed the SWOT reference-frame deck assigns the vertical transformation to the data user. It does not: slide 10 gives the permanent-tide transformation in closed form with its constants (Hperm = −0.31460 m, k2 = 0.3) and an error bound under 1.3 mm, and slide 15 gives two worked conversion identities with a handbook pointer. **A number-pairing struck for misstating its own source.** UQ-14 had it that the frame shift flagged in the ISPRS reservoir paper (−0.18 m) is "larger than the residual it would explain (−0.08 m)". The residual it is offered to explain is **−0.17 m** (UAV-DEM minus gauge, single epoch); against its actual comparand the shift is the same size to within 0.01 m, which is precisely why the authors propose it. The −0.08 m is a separate quantity (SWOT minus gauge, mean and median, SD 0.15 m, RMSE 0.17 m, n = 36 epochs). Cutting against the entry in the same breath: that paper's Table 3 **is** a dispersion summary of exactly the candidate's residual — at n = 1 reservoir, with no stationarity test and no threshold comparison.

**The highest-stakes check in the batch came back half against its entry.** UQ-13 named WCEE2017-193.pdf as the one unopened document most likely to flip it to Rejected, on the ground that a signed population-level early-versus-later magnitude bias would move novelty toward Low.
