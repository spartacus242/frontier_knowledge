# UQ-20261017-14 — Gate 0–3 record

ID: UQ-20261017-14
Date: 2026-10-17
Status: (Gate 5 pending — this record covers Gates 0–3 only; Gate 2 finding is Low novelty)
Domain(s): Biology — somatic mutagenesis, DNA repair and epigenomics (single-molecule, single-strand-resolved sequencing with per-read base-modification calling). Adjacent: cancer genomics / liquid biopsy, plant epigenomics, sequencing methodology.

## Question (canonical)

V1 (as intaken): Do the individual DNA molecules that carry a somatic mutation differ in their own local methylation state from matched molecules without a mutation at the same locus in the same sample?

V2 (Gate 1 reformulation — the one permitted reformulation, made solely for well-posedness; both versions recorded): In single-molecule, single-strand-resolved sequencing of a non-clonal somatic tissue, is local 5mC density on a molecule — measured over a pre-specified window that **excludes the mutated base and every CpG created or destroyed by the mutation** — associated with whether that molecule carries a somatic mutation, relative to mutation-free molecules matched on locus, sample and strand, estimated by matched conditional logistic regression, stratified by mutation class (C>T at CpG / other C>T / non-C>T) with a pre-declared negative-control class and an explicit per-read methylation-call error model?

Reason the reformulation was required: at the mutated base itself the comparison is **degenerate, not empirical** — a C>T at a CpG destroys the CpG and a mutation that creates a CpG creates the only methylatable site — so a "difference in methylation between mutant and wild-type molecules" at that position is a sequence tautology. V1 is answerable in the affirmative from arithmetic on the genetic code plus any per-molecule assay, and it **has been so answered in print** (see Gate 2, prior P1). Only V2 carries empirical content.

## Alternative formulations

- Is the methylation–mutation association a molecule-level fact, or only a regional correlation?
- On single reads, what is the odds ratio for local 5mC density on mutation-bearing versus matched mutation-free molecules, by mutation class and strand?

## Motivating gap (with one intake claim corrected)

The newly measurable quantity is the joint state of one molecule: single-strand-resolved single-molecule sequencing (HiDEF-seq, Liu et al. 2024 Nature; NanoSeq-class and duplex designs) calls somatic mutations, mismatches and damage on individual molecules, and bisulfite- or modification-aware chemistries call 5mC on the same template. Everything the field believes about methylation and chromatin shaping mutation rates rests on **regional** correlation — methylation density against mutation density over kb–Mb windows — a design that cannot separate a molecule-level mechanism from regional confounders (replication timing, chromatin state, repair access) that covary with methylation at the same scale.

**Correction to the intake, from Gate 2:** the intake asserted that nothing pairs a mutation with the modification state of its own molecule. That is false. MethylSaferSeqS (prior P1) reports exactly that pairing — mutant versus wild-type molecules at the same locus in the same sample, with per-molecule methylation — in 2023, in the candidate's own field. The screening search that found "only co-profiling of methylation with histone marks, DamID and chromatin accessibility" missed it. What remains unfound is the **systematic, class-stratified estimate on neighbouring CpGs** (V2), not the measurement.

## Presuppositions

1. Per-read 5mC calls are accurate enough for the comparison. **CONTESTED, unresolved here:** no measured per-read error rate was obtained in this Gate 2; P1's own data show residual apparent methylation at non-CpG C's which the authors attribute to "presumably... incomplete bisulfite conversion" (1.3% of 398 C's; 3.1% of 65 C's), i.e. a per-molecule false-positive floor of a few percent exists and must be modelled. The estimand must therefore be window-level 5mC density, not a single CpG's state.
2. A molecule's present methylation state is informative about its state when the mutation arose. **STRONGLY CONTESTED — the main vulnerability, and now doubly so:** for C>T at CpG the mark is physically destroyed, which is why V2 must use neighbouring CpGs; and post-event remodelling means the measurement is of the mutated molecule's current microenvironment. The question is therefore an **association**, and any mechanistic reading is a further inference requiring extra assumptions.
3. Matched mutation-free molecules from the same locus and sample are a valid comparator (same cell-type mixture, amplification and strand history). **PARTLY CONTESTED:** P1 used exactly this comparator at amplicon scale, so it is operational; the matching rule (locus, sample, strand, and duplex-family size) must be declared in advance.
4. Single-molecule somatic mutation calls are not artefacts of damage or library chemistry. **Addressed in principle** by single-strand-resolved designs; must be shown, not assumed, and is harder under bisulfite/enzymatic conversion, which itself damages template.
5. Enough mutation-bearing molecules at CpG-containing loci can be sequenced for power. **FEASIBILITY LIMB, undemonstrated:** P1 recovered 11 and 65 mutant molecules at single hotspot loci in plasma — orders of magnitude short of a class-stratified genome-wide estimate. An explicit power calculation from measured somatic mutation frequencies and per-molecule yields is required; if it fails, the question is premature rather than wrong.
6. The sample is non-clonal, so matched mutation-free molecules exist in quantity. Established for normal somatic tissue; fails for clonal material, which bounds the frame.

## What would count as an answer

A per-molecule matched odds ratio (conditional logistic, matched on locus, sample and strand) for local 5mC density on mutation-bearing versus mutation-free molecules, over a pre-specified window excluding the mutated base and any CpG it creates or destroys, stratified by mutation class (C>T at CpG / other C>T / non-C>T), with a pre-specified per-read methylation-call error model and a negative-control mutation class in which no molecule-level association is expected. A **bounded null is as informative as a positive result**: it would say the well-known regional association is regional — mediated by something other than the mark on the molecule — a direct constraint on mechanism for the mutational-signature and DNA-repair communities.

## Novelty — how the candidate differs from each

**vs P1 (the near-duplicate).** P1 matches the candidate on unit (single molecule), comparator (mutation-free molecules at the same locus in the same sample) and endpoint family (per-molecule methylation) — which is why this grade is Low rather than Medium. It differs on **estimand** in a way that matters: P1's interrogated cytosine **is the mutated base itself**, the CpG that the mutation creates (CAT→CGT) or destroys (CCG→CCA), so its result is a deterministic consequence of the sequence change and carries no information about whether methylation and mutation co-occur beyond the base that changed — exactly the degeneracy V2 excludes. P1 reports no odds ratio and no statistical test; it is a descriptive check at **2 loci in 2 patients** with 11 and 65 mutant molecules; there is no strand matching, no mutation-class stratification, no neighbouring-CpG window, no per-read error model, no negative-control class, no power calculation, and no somatic-tissue (non-plasma, non-clonal) frame. The candidate's residual content is therefore the **systematic class-stratified matched association on neighbouring CpGs** — a different estimand on the same instrument, not a new measurement.

**vs P2.** P2 reverses the conditioning and changes the unit: it conditions on a **site's** methylation and asks about mutability, aggregating over molecules and across individuals, often with methylation and mutation measured in different samples. It is the best-executed version of precisely the design the candidate says cannot distinguish a molecule-level mechanism from a regional confounder, and its neighbourhood estimand (±1–3 bp around a focal CpG) is the site-level analogue of V2's window. The candidate conditions on the **molecule's** mutation status and asks about that molecule's own 5mC, within sample and within locus, so replication timing, chromatin state and cell-type mixture are matched out by design rather than adjusted for.

**vs P3.** P3's phasing variants are **germline heterozygous SNVs** — present on roughly half of all molecules and used only as labels to pool reads into two haplotypes; its comparator is the other haplotype, not mutation-free molecules at the same locus, and its estimand is haplotype-level methylation difference. A somatic mutation is a property of a tiny minority of molecules and cannot define a haplotype, so the matched-molecule design the candidate needs is not an instance of allele-specific-methylation phasing. (Attributes abstract-level only; the Low grade does not rest on P3.)

**Novelty confidence: Low.**
Grounds, stated plainly: an on-point paper in the candidate's own field and own words — found by the mandatory own-field query, on a single-molecule platform, matched on locus and sample — performs the mutant-versus-wild-type per-molecule methylation comparison that V1 asks for, and reports the numbers. The intake's claim that nobody has paired a mutation with the modification state of its own molecule is false. The sharpened core (V2: neighbouring-CpG local 5mC density, matched conditional-logistic OR, class-stratified, negative control, error model) was **not** found in 11 queries across cancer genomics, plant epigenomics, liquid biopsy, germline/evolutionary genomics, sequencing methodology and WGBS-analysis vocabularies, and would grade **Medium** if the candidate were restricted to it — but that restriction is a narrowing of the question, and on the question as intaken the honest grade is Low.

## Significance — consequence (argued)

If the answer is **yes** (OR away from 1 for C>T at CpG and other C>T but not for the negative-control class), the methylation–mutation relationship becomes a molecule-level fact: the mark travels with the molecule that mutated, which licenses mechanistic models in which the mark (or what binds it) acts in cis on that duplex — deamination of 5mC, TET intermediates, MBD-protein occlusion of repair — and makes per-molecule methylation a legitimate covariate in mutation-rate models. If the answer is a **bounded no** (a CI tight around OR = 1 at adequate power), the large regional correlation that the mutational-signature and chromatin-mutation literature rests on is shown to be **regional in nature** — mediated by replication timing, chromatin accessibility or repair targeting rather than by the mark on the mutated molecule — and models that attribute SBS1-like CpG>TpG burden to the methylation of the individual molecule lose their warrant. Both branches are decision-relevant, which is the mark of a usable question; a null here is not a failed experiment.

Significance is nonetheless **dented by the estimand's own structure**: at the very class where the mechanism is cleanest (C>T at CpG) the mark at the mutated base is destroyed, so the test runs on neighbouring CpGs, whose methylation is correlated with but not identical to the destroyed site's. A positive result is therefore evidence about the molecule's local methylation microenvironment, not about the state of the deaminated cytosine itself.

## Significance — audience

- **Mutational-signature modellers** (COSMIC/SBS curators, PCAWG-lineage groups): would change whether per-region methylation enters signature-attribution models as a mechanistic term or only as a regional nuisance covariate.
- **DNA-repair biochemists** (MMR, BER/TDG, MBD-protein and TET communities): a molecule-level OR is the kind of in-cell evidence their in-vitro cis models currently lack.
- **The somatic-mosaicism and single-molecule-sequencing community** (HiDEF-seq, NanoSeq and duplex-method developers; the somatic-mosaicism atlas programmes): the analysis is a reanalysis of data they already generate, and the per-read methylation error model it forces is a methods deliverable in its own right.
- **Liquid-biopsy developers** (the P1 lineage): per-molecule mutation+methylation joint state is already their instrument; whether mutant molecules carry a distinguishable methylation microenvironment bears directly on mutation-plus-methylation classifier design.

## Significance — fecundity

Answering it makes askable: the same question for other per-read marks (6mA, oxidative damage, abasic sites, nick position) and for the strand asymmetry of the association on single-strand-resolved reads; whether repair-tract footprints are visible as methylation discontinuities on mutation-bearing molecules; whether the OR differs between mutation classes whose signatures are attributed to different processes; and the clonal-hematopoiesis analogue (do driver-bearing molecules differ in methylation microenvironment at the driver locus?). It also defines a reusable template: condition on a rare per-molecule event and compare matched molecules within locus and sample, rather than correlating densities across windows.

## Significance — anchoring

Touches (i) the mechanism of SBS1/CpG>TpG and the long-standing attribution of it to 5mC deamination; (ii) the regional methylation-and-chromatin-versus-mutation-rate literature, whose confounding by replication timing is a standing anomaly (P2's RR_met is its careful site-level form); (iii) the open problem of why somatic mutation rate varies along the genome at kb–Mb scale; (iv) the methods-level open problem of per-read modification-call accuracy, which this question forces into the open as a measured quantity rather than a tool default.

**Scores: Well-posedness 2/3 · Significance 2/3**

Well-posedness 2/3: V2 states exactly what evidence would settle it and what a null looks like, and the matching rule and strata are declarable in advance — but two presuppositions remain load-bearing and unsatisfied. No measured per-read 5mC error rate is in hand (P1's own non-CpG residuals suggest a few-percent floor), and the feasibility limb is undemonstrated (P1 recovered 11 and 65 mutant molecules at single loci). 3/3 would require a fixed window, a measured error model and a power calculation from published per-molecule yields.

Significance 2/3: both branches change what an identifiable community does, and the null branch is as informative as the positive one — but the answer speaks to the mutated molecule's current methylation microenvironment rather than to the state of the base that mutated, so the mechanistic payoff needs an extra assumption that the question itself cannot supply.

## Reason for silence

Two mechanisms, both credible, and one of them is directly evidenced by P1.

1. **New instrument, used for the thing it was built for.** Per-molecule mutation + methylation co-measurement arrived in 2023–2024 (P1; duplex and single-strand-resolved designs; per-read modification basecalling). P1's authors, having built the capability, used the joint readout for **assay validation and tumour-fraction estimation** — and their one per-molecule mutation–methylation comparison is a positive control at the mutated base, not an association estimate. The question requires seeing the joint readout as an estimand rather than as proof that the library worked.
2. **The standard QC of each field discards exactly the molecules the question is about.** Methylation pipelines treat reads bearing mismatches as sequencing error and filter them; somatic-mutation pipelines treat methylation (and bisulfite/enzymatic conversion) as a nuisance that corrupts base calls, and regress methylation out at the window level. Neither pipeline's default output retains the mutation-bearing molecule's own modification calls, so the quantity is not in anyone's results tables even when it is in their raw reads. That is a disciplinary-boundary-plus-pipeline-artefact gap, not "no one thought of it".

Note for Gate 4/5: mechanism (1) is weakened by the fact that P1 did pose a version of the comparison, which is part of why Gate 2 graded Low. A reason for silence that has to explain away an existing partial instance is, per the ledger's own rule, a signal to treat the novelty finding as suspect — and here the suspicion resolved in the direction of Low.
