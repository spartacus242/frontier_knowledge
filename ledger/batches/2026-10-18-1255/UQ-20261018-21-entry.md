Domain(s): Medicine — hospital medicine, diagnostic safety, deterioration detection. Method-owning neighbours: econometrics of diagnostic testing (implied-threshold recovery), medical decision analysis (testing threshold / cost-loss), forecast verification (calibration intercept-slope), ML-for-health (informative sampling).
Question (canonical): In hospitalised adults on general wards, treating each discretionary order for a pre-declared index test (lactate, troponin, blood culture, repeat CBC/chemistry) as a revealed forecast of near-term deterioration (RRT activation, unplanned ICU transfer, or death within 24-48 h): (i) estimate the clinician-specific ordering-threshold distribution on the deterioration-probability scale and compare the realised deterioration rate of the lowest-propensity ordered patients against both that revealed threshold and a pre-declared cost-loss threshold; (ii) report calibration intercept and slope, with intervals, overall and in pre-declared strata (night/day, staffing, service, age, language, race, weekday/weekend) under a declared intention-to-forecast estimand plus a treatment-adjustment arm; (iii) run a falsification arm on protocol/order-set-mandated orders.
Alternative formulations:
- Treating the decision to order a test as a revealed probability statement rather than a data-generating nuisance, how well calibrated are clinicians' implicit forecasts of deterioration, and where do they fail?
- Can the testing-threshold model be inverted on inpatient order streams to recover a clinician's subjective deterioration risk, and does the marginal ordered patient's realised event rate equal the recovered threshold?
Motivating gap: Agniel, Kohane & Weber (BMJ 2018;361:k1479, opened in full) find the presence and timing of a lab test predicts survival better than its value, and state the timing "captures the physician's experience, intuition, and assessment"; nobody then measures whether that judgement is right. Two intake corrections established first-hand: the anomaly's endpoint is three-year survival, not 24-48 h deterioration (so the replication limb is unexamined ground, not a replication check); and the anchor does not treat order features as leakage to be excluded — it recommends modelling process explicitly.
Presuppositions:
- P1. A discretionary component of ordering is separable from protocol-driven ordering. CONTESTED, unresolved; arm (iii) is mitigation, not evidence.
- P2. Ordering is described by a threshold model with a stable cost-loss ratio pinning an implied probability. FALSIFIED as stated in V0 (Abaluck et al.: threshold mean 0.056, SD 0.054); V1 estimates the threshold distribution instead. Djulbegovic 2014 (unread at Gate 2) opened by both adversaries: supports the threshold model directionally, but on 41 physicians' hypothetical vignettes, and finds expected-utility — the form limb (i)(b) uses — the worst-fitting of three descriptive models (DP vs EUT OR 1.75, p<0.001).
- P3. The forecast can be separated from its own consequences (order -> treatment -> altered event rate). CONTESTED; the record calls this the central structural weakness and concedes no observational design identifies the split.
- P4. Marginal patients are identifiable from pre-order observables. ESTABLISHED in an adjacent setting (Abaluck et al.); transfer to wards assumed.
- P5. The deterioration outcome is ascertainable and not definitionally entangled with the index order. NOT RESOLVED; two of three components are decisions by the same team.
- P6. The anchoring anomaly replicates for this endpoint. NOT ESTABLISHED.
What would count as an answer: a pre-registered analysis in >=2 systems with order-level timestamps reporting (i) the threshold distribution and the marginal-patient realised rate against revealed and cost-loss thresholds; (ii) calibration intercept and slope with intervals in pre-declared strata under the declared estimand with the treatment-adjustment arm; (iii) the protocol-order falsification arm. Signed interval-estimated miscalibration naming the failing strata is a clear answer; calibration answers in the opposite direction (the order stream is the best available human prior, and stripping order features discards information); "not estimable clinician-by-clinician" is a pre-declared third answer that would close the question.
