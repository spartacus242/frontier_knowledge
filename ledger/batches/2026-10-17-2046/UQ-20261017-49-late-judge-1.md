## Arithmetic that frames the whole audit (mine, two lines, from the established facts)

E(F,S) = prev_kept / prev_pool = s_AI / (p*s_AI + (1-p)*s_H) = RR / (1 + p*(RR-1)),
where RR = s_AI/s_H is the published survival ratio and p the pool prevalence.
Consequences:
 - E > 1 iff RR > 1, and RR is an UPPER bound on E (E -> RR as p -> 0, E -> 1 as p -> 1).
   So the SIGN of the candidate's limb 1 is fixed by the published RR for the two pipelines tested.
 - The MAGNITUDE needs p, which arXiv:2609.40295 does not print (established fact: no pre-filter
   token share; and the 27.5%/31.1% pair is a June-vs-August time trend, not pre-vs-post filter).
   The record's own back-solve of p ~ 0.142 is therefore void: it mixed a token share with
   document pass rates and treated a time trend as a before/after contrast.
 - UNIT/OBJECT narrowing that the established facts force and the record missed: the paper runs a
   "FineWeb v1.4.0 pipeline replication" and "DCLM's full pipeline". FineWeb v1.4.0 is URL/extraction/
   langID/Gopher/C4/custom-heuristic/MinHash — it contains NO model-based quality classifier
   (FineWeb-Edu is a downstream derivative). So the 2.3x is not evidence about ANY model-based filter.
   DCLM's full pipeline does contain the fastText OH-2.5+ELI5 top-10% stage, but the 9.8x is
   confounded with the heuristic and dedup stages ahead of it. The candidate's object — enrichment
   attributable to a named model-based filter F at its own operating point — has at most ONE
   confounded published data point, and zero for FineWeb-Edu and Nemotron-CC.
 - Dissolution reader's algebra (independent): under nondifferential error with FPR f,
   R = (a_k - f)/(a_0 - f), sensitivity cancels; uncorrected a_k/a_0 is a LOWER bound on R for
   f < a_0. So the correction limb only changes an answer if error is DIFFERENTIAL across strata.

## 1 Triviality — PARTIAL

plug-in that at Se=0.9801/Sp=0.9995 moves a prevalence by ~0.5 points, with sensitivity cancelling

distinguishes the candidate is a corollary, not a question.
Why it bites: the correction sub-limb really is near-trivial in the regime at issue, and the
uncorrected ratio is already a valid lower bound, so the corrected number is pre-bounded.

at all); no reader found any prevalence-corrected kept-vs-pool ratio for any web-text quality filter

an empirical fact no algebra delivers — no reader found such a ladder for machine-generated share.

## 3 Combinatorial novelty — RESOLVED
Objection: the candidate is only (known: filter keep-rate ladder) x (known: misclassification-
corrected prevalence). Both halves are mature — Gao 2021 (arXiv:2109.00698) moves a GPT-3-style
filter's own Pareto alpha over 8 discard fractions and reports kept-set composition for domain-style
attributes; Longpre 2023 sweeps a quality classifier's threshold (91/84/73/46% kept); AboutMe gives
two-point retention by social attribute; Rogan-Gladen is textbook and has already been applied to
AI-text prevalence (Elazar & Antoniak 2601.17036).
Why the objection fails:
 - the crossing has been shown to matter where it was tried: the one measurement in existence returns
   2.3x and 9.8x, i.e. a large effect, not a null;
 - a published MECHANISM predicts it: a style-only Wikipedia-style rewrite flips ~7% of FineWeb-Edu
   keep decisions (2605.23721), and NemoCurator Mixtral admits >7% of rephrased documents that scored
   <=2 raw — filters key on surface fluency, which is what AI text has;
 - and a published prediction of the OPPOSITE sign exists: Bahri et al. 2020, over 500M CC pages,
   found high P(machine-written) tracks LOW language quality. Two live hypotheses with opposite signs
   for the ladder means the crossing's outcome is not forecastable from the parts, which is exactly
   what combinatorial novelty denies;
 - no reader, across 8 attacks, found any ladder reporting machine-generated share against a filter's
   own threshold, so the cross-product's output is unmeasured as well as unpredictable.
Status: Resolved.

## 5 Vagueness — PARTIAL
Objection: "machine-generated documents" is not a settled binary. The vagueness reader found practice
split: Pew collapses a 0-1 score at 0.2 (chosen to calibrate an open model to Pangram 3.3); Graphite
calls an article AI at <=50% human; Ahrefs reports pure AI 2.5%, pure human 25.8%, MIXED 71.7%;
Pangram 3.3's own card ships four bands (0.25/0.5/0.75); EditLens says binary detectors are "not
well-suited" to mixed authorship. No study measures how a prevalence estimate moves with mixed
assignment; Ahrefs' categories imply 2.5% vs 74.2% depending on where mixed goes — ~72 points.
Why it bites: the candidate names no coding rule, so two faithful answerers (Pew's 0.2 and Pangram's
0.75) can return very different prevalences; and because filters may preferentially retain LIGHTLY
EDITED human text, the mixed stratum is plausibly where the action is, so the cutoff could move not
just E's magnitude but the monotonicity direction. Nothing published constrains that.
Why not fatal: E is a ratio, which damps a cutoff shift common to numerator and denominator; the
reformulation already fixed the unit (documents, token figures reported separately); and the
question's own demand for a hand-labelled validation subsample FORCES an explicit coding rule to be
written down before any labelling can occur. One declared band boundary repairs it, and the sign and
monotonicity limbs are answerable under any fixed rule.
Status: Partial.

## 6 Significance inflation — PARTIAL

mixing fraction while models train on the FILTERED subset, so a post-filter fraction licenses or
unlicenses that literature's quantitative use.
Why it bites: the significance reader found the fraction is load-bearing in only one branch. Shumailov
2305.17493 and the OLS paper 2402.07712 are indexed by GENERATION COUNT; Gerstgrasser 2404.01413 by

## 8 Dissolution — PARTIAL
Split the question and grade each part:
 (a) "do filters keep AI-labelled text at a higher rate" — SETTLED in sign for two whole pipelines
     (2.3x, 9.8x), unsettled for every named model-based filter (FineWeb v1.4.0 has no model-based
     classifier; DCLM's 9.8x is pipeline-confounded; Nemotron-CC and FineWeb-Edu untested).
 (b) "correct for detector error" — near-settled as method: sensitivity cancels under nondifferential
     error, the uncorrected ratio is a lower bound, and at Sp=0.9995 the move is sub-point.
 (c) "estimate Se/Sp in-stratum on hand-labelled provenance-anchored data" — plausibly UNMEASURABLE:
     no provenance oracle for positives in a wild 2026 snapshot (dating anchors negatives only, on the
     ~10-15% of dated pages), and the asht/Gelman-Carpenter/Kunz results say prevalence, Se and Sp are
     not jointly identified from positive counts alone, so external calibration is mandatory.
 (d) "intervals propagating the correction" — dominated by the specificity validation sample size
     (Cai et al.: exact lower bound 0.00-2.06% with 371 known negatives vs 0.68-1.87% with 3,324;
     delta-method and bootstrap under-cover at high specificity, coverage 0.886 at Sp 99.6%), and the
     Lang-Reiczigel simulations asht cites cover Se/Sp ~70-90%, nowhere near a 0.05% FPR regime. So
     (d) inherits (c)'s problem.
 (e) "is E monotone in stringency along F's own threshold" — MEASURABLE (FineWeb-Edu weights are
     released and runnable at a documented cutoff; DCLM's rule is a documented percentile; archived
     snapshots are pinned) and UNSETTLED (no ladder of machine-generated share exists; two published
     mechanisms predict opposite signs). Also note the near-FPR instability the dissolution reader
     flags does not bite at the observed 12.8-29.3% label rates, only in very low-rate strata.
Because (e) is both measurable and unsettled — and (a) is unsettled for the actual objects named —
the question does not dissolve. But the bite is severe and precisely aimed: the parts that are
measurable are the parts that do not need the correction, and the part that distinguishes this
candidate from the published work is the part that may not be executable. No source anywhere states an
identifiability condition for a RATIO of two corrected prevalences; that gap rests on unsourced algebra.
Status: Partial.
