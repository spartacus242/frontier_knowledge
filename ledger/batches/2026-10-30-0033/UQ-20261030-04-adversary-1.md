# UQ-20261030-04 — Gate 4, adversary #1

## Restatement (my own words)

Take one cell. Sequence its mitochondrial genomes one physical molecule at a time, near full length, each molecule individually tagged. For each molecule, count how many de-novo somatic mutations it carries (haplogroup-fixed and recurrent-across-cells sites excluded by a stated rule). Now ask whether those per-molecule counts look like independent Poisson draws with mean = (this cell's own de-novo per-base frequency x called molecule length), or whether they are overdispersed — variance/mean > 1, a few molecules carrying far more than their share — which is what you would expect if some genome copies are templates that get copied over and over while most are copied rarely. Headline number: the within-cell index of dispersion with a CI, plus a negative-binomial shape parameter, in >=50 cells each of two enumerated cell types (muscle fibre segments; primary dermal fibroblasts), at >=10^3 molecules per cell, with a molecule-level error model from a matched-length known-sequence spike-in.
