Significance — consequence:
If every correlation is positive, there is a single junk-sorting axis: one physical process decides where all damage goes, and the division-coupled-rejuvenation literature collapses from a per-organelle collection of mechanisms to one mechanism with one knob. That would make asymmetric damage segregation a cell-level program rather than a per-organelle accident, and would predict that in each division one daughter is a disposable sink — a mammalian analogue of the yeast mother. If the correlations are indistinguishable from the null, each species has its own mechanism, every single-species result in this literature is a local finding that cannot be generalised across species, and the rejuvenation benefit per division is the sum of independent small asymmetries rather than one large aligned one — scaling as sqrt(k) rather than k across k species. THAT SCALING IS MY OWN ARITHMETIC ABOUT INDEPENDENT VERSUS ALIGNED SUMS, NOT A SOURCE FINDING, and no source I opened states it. If any correlation is negative, cells actively route different damage to different daughters, which no model I encountered predicts and which would make "the damaged daughter" a category error.

Strongest remaining objection:
That a positive answer is close to forced by geometry, and for a nuisance reason the candidate's own null does not remove. Any damage species carried by many dispersed objects partitions near-binomially, and all species share one cleavage plane and one pair of daughter volumes, so some positive correlation is expected before any sorting mechanism exists. Prior 2 makes this concrete rather than hypothetical: it prints a mitochondria-vs-cytoplasm correlation of "≃ 0.35" (Fig. 4e), with the cytoplasm channel being the one it says "is expected to correlate with the cell volume". Volume normalisation is supposed to handle this, but normalising by a measured volume removes only the part of the geometric nuisance that volume captures, and the species-label-permutation null cannot help because permuting labels across divisions destroys the shared within-division geometry as well. The fix is a within-division geometric control — a fourth channel marking a NON-damage organelle of matched copy number and size, whose correlation with each damage species is the real null — and V2 as written does not specify one. Without it the study risks reporting "all-positive, therefore one sorting axis" when the honest reading is "all-positive, therefore one cleavage plane".

Suggested first step toward an answer (pointer only — not taken):
A two-species pilot in one symmetrically dividing line (a depolarisation-resolved mitochondrial channel plus a stress-free inclusion reporter) over ~50 tracked divisions, run WITH the matched-abundance non-damage control organelle as a fourth channel, reporting the signed rank correlation between the two damage species and the control-versus-each correlation side by side. That is enough to see whether the geometric nuisance dominates before committing to five channels and 300 divisions.
