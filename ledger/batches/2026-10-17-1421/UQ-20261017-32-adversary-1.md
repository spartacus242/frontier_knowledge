## Restatement (my words, before attacking)

Species-level trait compilations (AnAge, PanTHERIA, FishBase, TRY, Genoud's BMR table) put one row per species and treat each row as one observation, correcting only for phylogeny. Many rows were copied from handbooks or earlier compilations, so several rows may ultimately rest on the same original measurement or the same original publication. The candidate asks, for pre-declared databases and pre-registered fits: (i) what fraction of rows in a fit trace back to an original source that another row in the same fit also traces to, and what fraction cannot be traced; (ii) what design effect and effective sample size that clustering implies; (iii) how far the allometric exponent and its interval move when the fit is redone with errors clustered by original source; (iv) how much of that clustering the phylogenetic covariance in PGLS already soaks up.
