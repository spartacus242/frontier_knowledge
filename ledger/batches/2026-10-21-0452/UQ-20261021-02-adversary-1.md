## Restatement (my own words)

Does the clinician whose name sits on a drug order actually make the choice? Specifically: (a) how often is the recorded prescriber not the person who decided, per drug class, measured against an observed gold standard, with the share of orders that have no single decider reported separately; (b) how well do EHR order fields and audit-log events recover the real chooser (sensitivity/specificity against that gold standard); and (c) on one cohort, how much do a prescriber-preference IV estimate (point estimate, conditional F, partial R-squared) and a prescriber-targeted audit-and-feedback effect move when exposure is reassigned from the name on the order to the real chooser, against a pre-declared equivalence margin?
