## Question (canonical)

**V1 (as intake):** In a genome-scale single-gene perturbation atlas with single-cell transcriptomic readout, what fraction of the robustly expressed transcriptome is not moved by ANY single-gene perturbation at a declared per-gene power and declared minimum effect size, and does that non-responsive set recur across cell lines?

**V2 (the one reformulation permitted at Gate 1, for well-posedness only; this is the version scored):** Fix one genome-scale CRISPRi single-cell atlas (index case: the K562 genome-wide Perturb-seq screen, ~9,600 perturbations targeting a primary transcript) and declare, before any testing: (i) an expression filter defining the measured gene set G; (ii) a grid of minimum effect sizes δ on the log-fold-change scale rather than one value; (iii) a family-wise error rule for the UNION over perturbations, so that "gene g responded" means g was called at δ in at least k ≥ 2 independently-assigned perturbation guides/perturbations under a declared FDR on the (gene × perturbation) matrix, not any one of ~10⁴ marginal tests; (iv) a per-(gene, perturbation) power function and a declared coverage rule for "powered" (g is powered iff power ≥ 1−β at δ for at least a declared proportion p of perturbations). Then report, as a function of δ: |G| and the counts and fractions of three classes kept separate — RESPONSIVE; UNRESPONSIVE-AND-POWERED; UNDERPOWERED — plus the same computation in a second line (RPE1 essential-gene screen) restricted to the intersection of the two expression filters, the overlap of the unresponsive-and-powered sets against a declared null for that overlap given each line's marginal class sizes, and a characterisation of any recurring set against pre-declared covariates (expression level, housekeeping status, chromatin state, transcript half-life, membership in the perturbation library). Declare the estimand as reachability-under-CRISPRi-knockdown, with CRISPRa and knockout as separate declared limbs, and report a compositional-normalisation sensitivity arm.

Alternative formulations (from intake, retained): How much of a cell's transcriptome is reachable by perturbing one gene at a time, and is there a part of it that no single-gene knockdown touches? / Reading a genome-scale Perturb-seq response matrix along the GENE axis rather than the perturbation axis, how many measured genes show no detected response to any perturbation after each gene's own detection power is accounted for, and do the same genes recur in a second line?

## Motivating gap (as intake, with two Gate 2 corrections)

Intake: genome-scale Perturb-seq made the full perturbation × gene response matrix measurable, every analysis of it reads the perturbation axis, and the gene axis has not been read; the size of the unreached part is an unmeasured ceiling on GRN inference, co-expression module reasoning and the virtual-cell programme.

**Correction 1 (narrows the gap, does not close it).** Intake stated that TRADE "contains no … overall fraction of perturbations with a detectable phenotype". True of TRADE, but that quantity *is* published, by the atlas paper itself: Replogle et al. report "2,987 of 9,608 genetic perturbations targeting a primary transcript (31.1%)" by energy-distance test, "2,935 … (30.5%)" by Anderson–Darling with >10 DEGs, and "86.6% of essential genetic perturbations (gamma < –0.1) leading to a significant transcriptional response in K562 cells", with the caveat that "our estimate of the fraction of genetic perturbations that cause a transcriptional phenotype is likely to be a lower bound". So the gap claim must be stated as gene-axis-only; the perturbation-axis fraction is on the record and is the candidate's formal mirror image.

**Correction 2 (supports the gap).** The same paper, read in full this pass, has: fraction of measured genes differentially expressed in at least one perturbation — ABSENT; genes responding to no perturbation — ABSENT; per-gene power analysis — ABSENT; cross-cell-line comparison at the level of which GENES respond — ABSENT (cross-line comparison is perturbation-level, e.g. "CIN scores were strongly correlated across K562 and RPE1 cell lines"). Its nearest gene-ish statements, "knockdown of a significant fraction of expressed genes causes a transcriptional response" and "highly expressed genes were more likely to produce transcriptional phenotypes", are both about the *perturbed* gene, not the *measured* gene. The same three absences hold in CausalBench and in the stoichiometry-propagation preprint (both opened, below).

## Presuppositions (intake list, with Gate 1/2 status)

1. A genome-scale atlas exists with enough cells per perturbation to declare a per-gene power. **ESTABLISHED for at least one line** (162,751 K562 and 162,733 RPE1 samples in the CausalBench tabulation of the Replogle data). Per-gene power at low expression is the binding constraint and is what V2's three-class partition exists to absorb.
2. Per-gene detection power is computable, so "not moved" is separable from "not measurable". **LOAD-BEARING, and only partly established.** Multi-sample scRNA-seq power-design machinery exists (bioRxiv 2020.04.01.019851 and successors, surfaced at query 10) but no opened source applies a per-gene power curve to classify genes of an atlas as unresponsive-versus-underpowered. The effect size must be declared in advance; V2 makes it a grid, because the answer is a curve in δ and a single δ would make the headline number an artefact of a free parameter.
3. CRISPRi knockdown is an adequate instance of "perturbing a gene". **CONTESTED** — incomplete knockdown, essential-gene dropout, no gain-of-function. V2 states the estimand as reachability-under-CRISPRi with CRISPRa/knockout as separate declared limbs.
4. Genes not expressed in the line are excluded by a declared expression filter. Fine, and declared; note the cross-line limb must run on the intersection of two filters, which shrinks the denominator.
5. The library's perturbation set is dense enough that "any perturbation" approximates "any single gene". **CONTESTED** for genes absent from the library and for redundant gene families; the historical yeast work (Prior 4) puts redundancy at roughly 29% of non-responsiveness on the perturbation side, which is the right order of magnitude for the worry.
6. Non-response is not an artefact of compositional normalisation. **CONTESTED** — V2 carries a declared normalisation plus a sensitivity arm; a spike-in or total-RNA arm would be better and is not available retrospectively in the existing atlases, so this is a real limitation of answering the question on archived data.
7. Reachability is what matters for inference — a non-responsive gene is genuinely uninformative rather than informative through its invariance. **FLAGGED and not resolved.** Invariance is itself information (it is the premise of invariant-causal-prediction-style methods), so the significance argument below is made on the identifiability-ceiling and benchmark-floor limbs, which do not depend on invariance being uninformative, rather than on the "uninformative" claim.

## What would count as an answer

As V2: the declared filter, the δ-grid, the union rule, the per-gene power function; then the three class counts and fractions reported as functions of δ at a declared FDR, never merged; the second-line replication with the overlap of unresponsive-and-powered sets tested against a declared null; and the covariate characterisation. A fraction near zero at a biologically meaningful δ licenses the reachability assumption GRN inference and virtual-cell work rest on. A large, cell-line-shared unresponsive-and-powered fraction sets a hard identifiability ceiling on single-perturbation atlases. Equally decisive, and the likeliest outcome on the read evidence: most non-responders fall in the UNDERPOWERED class, which converts the question into a sample-size statement about what a given atlas depth can resolve — still an answer, and still actionable, which is why V2 forbids merging the two non-responsive classes.

## Novelty — how this differs from each

- **vs Prior 1:** same response matrix, orthogonal margin. Replogle's unit is the perturbation, endpoint "does this perturbation move anything", reference the control cells, estimand 31.1%. The candidate's unit is the MEASURED gene, endpoint "is this gene moved by at least one perturbation under a declared union rule", reference the gene's own power curve at a declared δ, estimand the fraction unresponsive-and-powered with underpowered held separate. The one sentence that could be mistaken for the candidate's quantity — "highly expressed genes were more likely to produce transcriptional phenotypes" — is about the perturbed gene's expression predicting its own phenotype, not about measured genes responding. Cross-line comparison exists but at the perturbation level only.
- **vs Prior 2:** CausalBench is the consumer whose ceiling the candidate would measure, and it is a clean demonstration of the blind spot: it filters weak PERTURBATIONS out by an explicit three-part rule and leaves gene selection entirely undeclared. Unit (edge) and estimand (inference performance) differ from the candidate's at both ends; it reports neither the fraction of genes moved nor any power analysis. A large unresponsive-and-powered fraction would be a hard cap on this benchmark's achievable recall, which the benchmark itself does not compute.
- **vs Prior 3:** the closest mechanistic claim — why effects propagate to the genes they do — and still perturbation-indexed. Its 10–15% is DEGs **per strain** in three growth-defective mutants, not the union over perturbations; no unresponsive-gene set, no per-gene power, no gene-level cross-system comparison (its cross-organism claim is about the propagation pattern being "universal", not about which genes respond). Its stoichiometry-conservation axis is a strong candidate covariate for the candidate's characterisation limb, which is a complementary relation, not an overlap.
- **vs Prior 4:** the word "non-responsive" is the same and the referent is the opposite object. Prior 4's non-responders are MUTANTS (perturbations) that fail to show a defect, and its explanatory estimand partitions that failure into redundancy and condition dependency; the candidate's non-responders are measured GENES that no perturbation moves. Its redundancy share is nonetheless the best available prior on presupposition 5. **Unchecked:** whether its full text contains any gene-axis count — unread, so no absence is claimed.

## Significance — consequence

If the unresponsive-and-powered fraction is near zero at a biologically meaningful δ: the reachability premise under which single-perturbation atlases are collected and under which GRN inference and virtual-cell training operate is licensed, and the field's current effort allocation (deeper single-gene atlases) is vindicated against the combinatorial alternative. If it is large and recurs across lines: the recurring set is, by construction, invisible to any method that learns only from single-gene interventions in those lines, which is an identifiability ceiling rather than a method deficiency — no amount of model capacity recovers an edge into a gene that no single intervention moves — and combinatorial perturbation moves from refinement to necessity. A third outcome is live and arguably most likely: most non-responders land in the UNDERPOWERED class, in which case the answer is a statement about what atlas depth buys, directly usable in designing the next screen's cells-per-perturbation. All three outcomes change a decision; none of them is the null result that TRADE's perturbation-side finding (most transcriptome-wide impact sits below per-gene significance) might lead one to expect, because V2 reports the three classes separately rather than merging them.

Second, independent consequence, and the sharpest: any gene that never moves is predicted correctly by the control mean. A measured unresponsive fraction is therefore the floor on every perturbation-prediction metric computed over all genes — it quantifies, rather than merely alleges, the inflation that "Virtual Cell Models Inflate Perturbation Effect Sizes and Undermine Causal GRN Recovery" (ICML 2026) and "Virtual Cell Perturbation Metrics Reloaded" are currently arguing about. That limb does not depend on presupposition 7 (invariance being uninformative) and so survives the flag on it.

## Significance — audience

Three communities, all currently active and all with a decision in front of them. (1) Perturbation-atlas designers (the groups producing genome-scale Perturb-seq and its successors, and the consortia funding them): the choice is cells-per-perturbation versus perturbations-per-screen versus combinatorial pairs; the three-class split is directly a per-cell-count argument. (2) GRN-benchmark builders — concretely the CausalBench line, which declares no gene-side rule and whose achievable recall the answer bounds: they would gain a principled gene-inclusion rule and a ceiling to score against instead of an unbounded one. (3) Virtual-cell model and metric designers (Virtual Cell Challenge organisers and the metric-reloaded/inflation critics): they would recompute headline metrics on the responsive subset, or report the floor alongside. The reality of audience (3) is evidenced rather than asserted: an ICML 2026 paper and a public metrics critique are both arguing about effect-size inflation in exactly these models, which is the problem the candidate's quantity dimensions.

## Significance — fecundity

Opens, in order of immediacy: is the unresponsive-and-powered set reachable by a different modality (CRISPRa, knockout, degron, environmental perturbation) — i.e. is "unreachable" a property of the gene or of CRISPRi? Are unresponsive genes the buffered genes — testable against Prior 3's stoichiometry-conservation architecture as a pre-declared covariate? How much of current perturbation-prediction benchmark performance is carried by unresponsive genes, by direct recomputation? Does the recurring set coincide with the genes whose co-expression modules are inferred but never interventionally confirmed? And the design question the answer makes askable: how many combinatorial pairs are needed before the unreached fraction falls below a target, which is a sample-size calculation nobody can currently set up.

## Significance — anchoring

Touches (a) the open argument about whether perturbation-prediction models beat trivial baselines, where the unresponsive fraction is the mechanism by which a trivial baseline can look strong; (b) identifiability in GRN inference, where the existing literature (Prior 1 of query 7's hits: practical indistinguishability, arXiv 2508.21006) bounds parameters and edges but has no vocabulary for an unreached response variable; (c) genetic buffering and robustness, where the perturbation-side version is classical (Prior 4's redundancy share) and the gene-side version is not; (d) the design premise of the virtual-cell programme.

**Significance score: 2/3.** Argued down from 3 for one reason: the chief decision the "large fraction" branch would force — invest in combinatorial perturbation — is already being taken for independent reasons, so that branch confirms a direction rather than redirecting it. What holds it at 2 rather than 1 is the benchmark-floor limb, which is a specific recomputation an identified community would perform on a specific published number, and the cells-per-perturbation design limb, which is currently decided without this quantity.

## Reason for silence

Four mechanisms, compounding, none of them "no one thought of it".

1. **New instrument, recent.** Before genome-scale atlases the union over perturbations was not computable at all: the yeast compendium had ~1,500 deletions and bulk arrays, enough for the perturbation axis and not for a per-gene power curve. The matrix has existed for roughly four years.
2. **Analysis-template inheritance — the operative mechanism.** A screen's deliverable is a hit list, so every tool in the stack is perturbation-indexed: DE is computed per perturbation, quality filters are per perturbation (CausalBench's ≥50 DEGs / ≥25 cells / ≥30% knockdown), and the standard efficacy tooling asks "did this perturbation work". Reading the other margin requires inverting a pipeline whose unit of analysis is wired in from the screening tradition that produced it. Prior 4 shows the inheritance is older than the instrument: the word "non-responsive" was already attached to the perturbation in 2017, and the GEO series name carries it.
3. **A discipline split between method and data.** The three-class partition "signal / no signal with adequate power / inadequate power" is a measurement-science and statistical-design template, owned by the people who build power calculators, not by the people who build atlases; searching the method-owning vocabulary (query 10) found the machinery sitting unused on this object, and searching the identifiability-owning vocabulary (query 7) found the limit concept expressed only over parameters.
4. **The question looks like a guaranteed null.** TRADE's finding that most transcriptome-wide impact sits below per-gene significance makes a gene-axis reading look hopeless — most genes will fail to clear significance for reasons of power — which is a reason not to attempt it *unless* power is modelled per gene. That is precisely what the question requires and what makes it askable; the discouraging result and the enabling requirement are the same fact.

## Scores

**Scores: Well-posedness 3/3 (on V2) · Significance 2/3**

Well-posedness, V1 would be 2/3: as intake, "not moved by ANY single-gene perturbation" leaves the union over ~10⁴ perturbations untreated, so under naive per-(gene, perturbation) testing almost every gene is "responsive" by chance and the headline fraction is set by the multiplicity rule rather than by biology; and "power above the declared floor" is undefined without saying over how many perturbations. V2 repairs both (union FDR plus k ≥ 2; power coverage proportion p) and makes δ a reported grid rather than a free parameter, which is what takes it to 3/3. The residual that does not cost a point but should be stated: the three-class answer is a function of (δ, FDR, p, atlas depth), so the deliverable is a surface, and a reader wanting one number will have to be given a declared operating point.

## Strongest remaining objection (for the adversary to start from, not a verdict)
