## Restatement (my own words)

Core facilities keep per-run electronic records (bookings, instrument sessions, sample counts, fund codes) for years because they are billing records. The candidate asks: if you link those records to the papers the same projects eventually published, how many runs of an assay were actually performed for each run that ends up represented in a published figure — and does that attempts-per-published-run ratio differ by assay type, by laboratory, and by whether the published finding came out in the direction the paper claims (with calibration/QC assays as a negative control)?

In short: use the facility's till roll as a process-side instrument on the file drawer, at run granularity, inside published projects.
