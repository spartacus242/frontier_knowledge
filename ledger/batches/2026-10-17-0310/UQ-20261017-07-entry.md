Domain(s): AI architecture — pretraining data layer (model-based quality filtering) x architecture/recipe evaluation methodology. Method source: design of experiments (two-factor factorial, interaction variance components, replication). Source method: presupposition inversion ("a corpus is exogenous, so a comparison on a fixed corpus measures the architecture").

Question (canonical): For ONE fixed Common Crawl source pool re-filtered at a matched keep rate by T >= 2 quality classifiers whose positive labels come from different annotator ("teacher") models — chosen capability-matched across families so teacher IDENTITY is not confounded with teacher CAPABILITY — crossed with A >= 3 pre-declared architecture/recipe arms at matched FLOPs and matched token budget, with S >= 2 seeds per cell and one evaluation suite declared before any run: (i) how large is the teacher x arm interaction variance component relative to the within-cell seed variance (ratio with CI), and (ii) how many of the A(A-1)/2 pairwise architecture contrasts reverse SIGN between teachers at a pre-registered minimum effect size?

Alternative formulations:
1. Are published architecture comparisons corpus-conditional, because model-filtered corpora are selected to suit the annotator model that filtered them?
2. If one Common Crawl pool is re-filtered by two different annotator models at the same keep rate, do the two corpora preserve the same ordering of candidate model designs?
3. (lay) When a paper says "this model design is better", is it reporting a fact about the design or a fact about whichever AI graded its training data?
(Intake version, recorded: "does the measured ranking of architectures depend on which teacher produced the quality labels — is the teacher x architecture interaction distinguishable from seed noise?" One permitted Gate 1 reformulation used, to fix the estimand, the comparator, the effect-size band and the identity-vs-capability identification condition.)

Motivating gap: Standard open corpora since 2023-24 are built by classifiers traceable to named teachers — VERIFIED by reading arXiv:2412.02595 (FineWeb-Edu classifier "trained with annotations from Llama3"; DCLM a fastText classifier on OpenHermes-2.5 + ELI5 positives; Nemotron-CC's own labels from "annotations from Mistral 8x22B-instruct and Nemotron-340B-instruct", max-ensembled with DCLM's fastText). Every architecture comparison run on such a corpus is conditional on those teachers, a conditional no recipe paper states. The same paper shows the gap ("All experiments use the same 8B transformer configuration"; its classifier comparisons "vary only the filtered data"). The teacher-comparison literature holds architecture fixed; the architecture literature holds the corpus fixed. Gate 4 correction: the gap is narrower than the record claimed — the data-centric side does cross classifier variants with a model factor and seeds (DataDecide), and the architecture side does cross dense/MoE with data-side factors (arXiv:2609.11917). The uninstantiated factor is the architecture axis crossed with teacher identity, not the whole design.

Presuppositions:
P1. Major open corpora are model-filtered with identifiable teachers. ESTABLISHED by reading arXiv:2412.02595, but OVERSTATED as written (A3): of the three, only FineWeb-Edu has a single identifiable LLM teacher of per-document labels; DCLM's "teacher" is a positive set, Nemotron-CC's a three-scorer max-ensemble.
P2. Architecture rankings are measurable precisely enough for the interaction to clear seed noise at affordable scale. CONTESTED, load-bearing, unresolved. Against: DataDecide reports run-to-run SD "as high as 2% points" at 1B and that it is "difficult to distinguish evaluation variance from true crossovers"; arXiv:2604.20549 reports 0.3-0.8% shifts from the filter's own sampling seed alone. P3. Keep rate and token budget can be matched across teacher-filtered corpora. ESTABLISHED (threshold on a score; Nemotron-CC Table 9 keep shares 8%/11%/25%; DataDecide matches at 10%).
P4. There exists architecture/recipe variation the field acts on. ESTABLISHED.
P5. Teachers' notions of quality are at least partly idiosyncratic. WEAKENED. The 10.1% keep-set intersection (Nemotron-CC Table 8, 1,152,821/11,359,655 — the ledger's own arithmetic, re-reproduced by A2 and A3) is keep-rate confounded: with 8% and 25% shares the Jaccard ceiling is 0.32 (A2), and the three pipelines also differ in rubric and classifier family, so the figure does not evidence a teacher effect at a FIXED rubric and MATCHED keep rate. A3's query 5 found no inter-teacher agreement figure at fixed rubric in either direction.
P6. Comparison is FLOP-matched, not step-matched. DECLARED.
P7. Any interaction is attributable to teacher IDENTITY rather than capability. CONTESTED; capability is multidimensional, so matching on a declared suite leaves "identity" a residual category. Deployed teachers are capability-mismatched, so the design must commission teachers.
P8. A pre-registered minimum effect size for a "sign flip" is agreeable. CONTESTED by construction (with small true gaps, signs flip for free), which is why the reformulation requires a declared band.
New, from Gate 4 (A3): matched-FLOP arms need a pre-declared per-arm per-corpus tuning protocol, or teacher x arm is confounded with tuning x corpus. WEAKENED on verification: no source quantifies a corpus-induced optimal-LR shift, and the only direct evidence runs the other way — Step Law (arXiv:2503.04715) claims "The HP scaling law demonstrates statistical invariance across linguistic and structural changes in the dataset" and "strong invariance across dense and sparse (MoE) LLMs". Melis et al. 2018 did tune per corpus by black-box optimisation; Narang et al. 2021 deliberately held hyperparameters "constant for each architectural variant" and says only that "Not tuning hyperparameters handicapped other methods". Step Law is one transformers-only preprint and does not cover recurrent or state-space arms, so this is unevidenced rather than refuted.

What would count as an answer: A pre-registered factorial — 2-3 corpora filtered from one pool by different teachers at a matched keep rate, crossed with 3-5 arms at matched FLOPs and tokens, >= 2 seeds per cell, suite fixed in advance. Deliverables: (i) teacher x arm interaction with CI against within-cell seed variance; (ii) the count and identity of pairwise architecture contrasts flipping sign at the declared band; (iii) keep-set overlap between the teacher corpora, so the interaction is readable against how different the corpora were. A null inside the declared band licenses current practice with a number attached; a non-null means an architecture claim must name its corpus's annotator and that results across differently filtered corpora are not poolable. Nothing opened across the record or three adversary logs delivers (i), (ii) or (iii) on the architecture axis.
