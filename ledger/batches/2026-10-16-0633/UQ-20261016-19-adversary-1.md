# UQ-20261016-19 — Adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my words)
Shared instrument facilities keep per-run usage logs because finance makes them bill for every run. The candidate wants to join those logs to the papers that came out of the same projects, count how many times an assay was actually run against how many runs the paper says it reports (n independent experiments), and ask (a) what that ratio looks like by assay and lab, and (b) whether it is higher when the paper's finding is positive. The record pitches this as a brand-new process-side measuring instrument for the file drawer, replacing inference from published artefacts or from self-report.

| 1 | Triviality | Could one just say "R is about 2-3 for most assays" from experience? | No measurement of run-level attempt counts linked to papers exists in any of the vocabularies searched; the number is genuinely unknown and the direction association has never been estimated. | Resolved |
| 2 | Disguise | The family "facility/administrative records joined to publications to measure non-publication, with null results as a candidate cause" is a known, mature question: astronomy does it routinely (telbib, ALMA/ESO non-publishing-programme surveys that list 'expected science not in the data' as a reason), synchrotrons report experiments-vs-publications yearly, and preclinical metascience has computed publication rates and animals-used vs animals-reported from ethics-approval records. The record must drop the "NEW INSTRUMENT / only ever artefacts or self-report" framing and cite the astronomy and animal-approval work as nearest priors on the instrument. | Partial |
| 3 | Combinatorial novelty | Metascience x facility management glued together. | The run-to-figure attribution problem (P2) and the choice of denominator are real problems that neither field has solved and the design has to confront; there is a problem at the interface. | Resolved |
| 4 | Presupposition failure | (a) "Run" is not what iLab/PPMS-class systems log for most assays: cytometers, sorters and microscopes are billed as booked/used sessions (hours), sequencing and MS as samples/lanes/injections. The denominator (paper's "n independent experiments") is biological replicates, several of which can sit inside one session or one lane; numerator and denominator are in different units, and the mapping differs by assay. (b) P3's load-bearing claim — optimisation and faults are independent of result direction — is not secure: the corpus is conditioned on publication (collider: labs that ran many times and got null mostly never enter the corpus), and legitimate practice predicts the same sign in the other direction (nulls get repeated more to convince reviewers), so a direction association does not discriminate selection from legitimate practice. (c) In biology the "null or contrary" class of published papers is small, so the outcome is near-constant. | (a) is fixable per assay by redefining the unit (sample-level records exist for sequencing/MS) but the reformulation does not do so; (b) the negative control and pre-registration do not touch the collider or the sign ambiguity; (c) is a power problem the record does not quantify. None of these is shown false-and-fatal with fetched evidence; (a) and (b) rest on general knowledge of these systems and standard selection logic. | Partial |
| 5 | Vagueness | Any distribution of R is declared informative; "direction of published result" per paper is a one-bit coding of a multi-claim paper and almost always "supports". | The feasibility-verdict exit is explicitly ruled "not an answer", and the per-assay distribution is a measurement that can come out many ways. But the outcome coding for test (iii) is underspecified enough that the test could be run and read either way. | Partial |

## Recommended verdict
Provisional — no attack is Unresolved, but Disguise, Presupposition, Vagueness, Reason-for-silence and Dissolution are all Partial. To promote: (1) rewrite the motivating gap to acknowledge administrative-record-to-publication studies (animal-ethics approvals; telescope bibliographies and non-publishing-programme surveys) as the parent family and position the candidate as the run-level, within-project refinement; (2) define the run unit per assay class from what the systems actually log (sessions vs samples vs injections); (3) either drop the claim that the direction test carries the inferential load or add a design that handles the publication collider (e.g., include unpublished project cells, or condition on submission rather than publication) and pre-specify the sign.

## Single strongest killing point
The record's "NEW INSTRUMENT" premise — that selective reporting "has only ever been measured from published artefacts or from self-report" — is false: preclinical metascience has already joined administrative approval records to publications (publication rate ~60%, with only a minority of animals used ever reported) and astronomy routinely joins facility observing records to papers and surveys non-publishing PIs with "null result" as an explicit option. The candidate survives only as a finer-grained version of that family, and its distinguishing move (the result-direction test) cannot carry the weight assigned to it because the corpus is conditioned on publication and legitimate re-replication of nulls predicts the same sign.
