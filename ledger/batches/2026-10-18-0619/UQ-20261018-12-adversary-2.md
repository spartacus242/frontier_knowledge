## Restatement (my own words)

Take a real LLM training run in a low-precision configuration — weights stored in BF16, optimizer moments in BF16 or in bitsandbytes-style block-quantized 8 bits — and watch one scalar parameter at one step. The optimizer computes a number to add to it. Two things can go wrong at the write-back: the number is so small relative to the spacing of representable BF16 values around that weight that rounding puts the weight back exactly where it was (the record calls this ANNIHILATION, defined bitwise), or the number lands but materially off from what the same step would have applied with an FP32 master weight (DISTORTION, defined as relative L2 deviation from an FP32 replay of that step). The question asks for three things: (a) how often each happens, per (configuration, parameter group, training-phase bin), across pretraining / SFT / RL at two or more scales; (b) whether the misses pile up on particular parameter groups — rare-token embedding rows, norm gains, weights in particular magnitude deciles, attention vs MLP; (c) whether the miss rate climbs as the learning rate decays, tested against a seed band rather than assumed. Behind it sits an interpretive claim: parameters that keep missing are not really being trained for part of the run, so "trained for N steps" is false of a structured subset of the model.

### Single strongest killing point

COLLAGE (ICML 2024, PMLR 235, arXiv 2405.03637) — in the candidate's own field, two years before the candidate and absent from the record — already asks the candidate's question and measures both of its limbs under its own definitions: Definition 3.3's "effective descent quality" is the deviation of the BF16-realized update from the intended one (the DISTORTION limb), and Figure 3 plots the "percentage of lost arithmetic for all model parameters" across training iterations (the ANNIHILATION limb, aggregated), while Option D's "BF16 + FP32 Optim + FP32MW" matched arms supply held-out validation perplexity at GPT 125M/1.3B/2.7B/6.7B and OpenLLaMA-7B. That single paper both removes the "nobody asked" premise independently of Prior 1 and refutes presupposition 4's claim that the matched-arm held-out-loss consequence is the candidate's "one genuinely unmeasured limb" — the limb the record identified as the candidate's last un-asked ground.
