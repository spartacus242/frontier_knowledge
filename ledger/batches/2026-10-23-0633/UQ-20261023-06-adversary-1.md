## 0. Restatement in my own words (before reading the record's framing twice)

Take a list, fixed before anyone looks at data, of finished randomised trials or registry cohorts that (a) report a consensus CGM metric as a primary or key secondary endpoint, (b) ran on a sensor model for which somebody has published a pressure-artifact detector whose hit rate and false-alarm rate are stated, and (c) still have the per-reading traces with timestamps (trials failing (c) are counted and reported as attrition, not quietly dropped). Now run that detector at an operating point chosen in advance, treat every flagged reading as missing under an imputation rule also chosen in advance, and recompute three numbers: overnight percent time under 70 mg/dL, overnight percent time under 54 mg/dL, and level-2 hypoglycaemic events per 14 days. Three questions follow. (1) By how many percentage points (and how many events) do those numbers move? (2) How much of that movement is the detector crying wolf rather than artifact removal — the bound to come from data where two sensors were worn at once, at the same operating point? (3) Does the treatment-minus-control difference, recomputed with its CI, cross the trial's own pre-specified margin — or, if there is no such margin, half the width of its primary 95% CI — or flip sign, over a band of operating points declared in advance?

In one line: how much of the overnight hypoglycaemia that CGM trials report is the sensor being leaned on, and does deleting it move the trial's answer?

## 6. What I nominate, and what kind of document it would have to be

To settle C3 (the only clause I would call a defect), the document needed is a POSTED SAP OR PROTOCOL for a completed trial that (population) enrolled insulin-using people with diabetes, (perturbs/varies) randomised two arms expected to differ in nocturnal glycaemia, and (prints) a PRE-SPECIFIED MARGIN OR DECISION THRESHOLD ON A BELOW-RANGE ENDPOINT IN ITS OWN UNITS — percentage points of time <70 or <54, or events per unit time. S2 is exactly that kind of document and it does NOT print such a margin, which is why C3 came back FALSE on it; a second and third instance of the same kind would establish whether that is general. I am NOT nominating a specific second SAP, because the only candidate URLs are four NCT numbers I could neither search for nor fetch inside budget, and naming one from its NCT number alone would be a guess dressed as a lead. S1 is the right kind of study and explicitly declines that use of its dual traces, so the object does not yet exist in anything I opened.
