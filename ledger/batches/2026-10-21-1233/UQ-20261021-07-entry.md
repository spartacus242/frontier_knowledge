## Verification of the kill at first hand (post-batch, 2026-10-21)

Three fresh-context readers: one on the two Sloley proceedings pages, one on the Shilling paper, one on an independent sweep for the distribution itself. **The verdict does not move — Rejected — but the strongest Low ground is withdrawn, the routing row is withdrawn, and four citation defects in this entry are corrected.**

**What survives.** The comparison is posed and point-answered in the candidate's own field and words. Both quoted strings are verbatim in the pages as served: from the 2019 abstract, "Operating data from four crude units supported the use of fouling factors at three times TEMA values."; from the 2018 abstract, "data from typical plants show the TEMA values are extremely low compared to experience in many units." Neither page declares a sampling frame or a service class, and neither reports any variance statistic. Shilling et al. 2009 was read in full and refutes the record's method-vocabulary claim more strongly than the entry needed: the paper does not merely rate the fouling allowance by probability, it makes the allowance contingent on that rating — "We propose to use a value for fouling resistance only if the risk level for fouling is high enough to justify it" — inside a named three-component decomposition of margin with a 1-5 probability ladder, a five-band money consequence scale and a matrix lookup to Low/Medium/High actions.

**The strongest Low ground falls on scope.** The entry graded novelty Low partly because "the distributional version is announced in prior 1's own abstract". It is not, in the candidate's regime: the announced Monte Carlo analysis is over fouling RATES and compares two MODELLING CHOICES (unit-average against a distribution), not realized against TABULATED fouling RESISTANCE over a frame of units, and the page reports none of its results. That announcement is silent in the candidate's regime rather than closing it. Both Sloley reads are also ABSTRACT ONLY behind an AIChE login wall, so whether either paper contains a distribution is UNREAD, not absent: a verdict that these papers lack one would exceed the read. The rejection therefore rests on the asked-and-point-answered comparison, which is visible in the abstracts themselves, and not on the stronger claim.

**The independent sweep found no distribution anywhere readable, and one address unresolved.** No readable source publishes a bias-and-spread, percentiles or a fitted distribution of realized against tabulated fouling resistance over a declared frame of in-service exchangers. Each candidate stops short in a nameable way: Bories & Patureaux give per-exchanger point values and a min-max range over eight named units without ever differencing them against TEMA; Zubair et al. fit a distribution to the wrong variable (time to a critical resistance) on laboratory data with assumed scatter; Awad's review carries the one large in-service dataset collapsed to category averages.
