Domain(s): AI architecture — pretraining schedule design, the annealing/cooldown data layer; secondary: LLM evaluation methodology (format sensitivity, calibration). Source method: silence mapping.

Question (canonical): For >=2 open pretraining runs with dense cooldown checkpoints and one pinned harness, decompose each benchmark's cooldown-phase score change into (i) a sharpening/format component — the part reproduced on the PRE-cooldown checkpoint by a fixed-weight output transform fitted on held-out items (temperature/entropy rescaling; length-normalised or rank-based option scoring; a label-emission format correction), no weight update — and (ii) a knowledge residual read by a pre-declared probe panel held fixed across checkpoints; report the share per benchmark with uncertainty, a manipulation check, and the re-ranking of >=3 published annealing data mixes under (ii) alone. (V2; the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation, spent. V1 asked the same without an estimator — see record.)

Alternative formulations:
- Does the cooldown teach the model anything, or mostly teach it to answer in the shape the benchmark scores?
- Is the end-of-run benchmark jump a knowledge gain or a calibration/formatting gain — and does the split change which annealing mixes look good?
- (lay) When a training run cools down and test scores jump, has the model learned more, or just learned how to take the test?

Motivating gap: The final decay is engineered everywhere and interrogated nowhere. Two mid-training surveys (arXiv:2510.23081 Sec. 8.1, "the rapid convergence in the LR decay stage is due to the first-order directional derivative diminishing"; arXiv:2510.06826 intro, "lowering the learning rate mitigates gradient variance") explain the decay-stage downstream gain by optimization and token utility alone and never raise format or calibration, while OLMo 2 (arXiv:2501.00656 Sec. 2.4) consumes cooldown-phase dev scores as its annealing-mix selection signal. The gap is real; it is not where the record thought it was (see Gate 4).

Presuppositions: P1 format-free probe family exists — holds (N1 verified; arXiv:2407.07890 Sec. 3.2). P2 knowledge/format separable in a next-token model — contested; arXiv:2609.02015 calls capability "interface-conditioned". P3 dense cooldown checkpoints for >=2 open runs — VERIFIED (adversary 3, GitHub: allenai/OLMo stage-2 checkpoints every 1000 steps, 1B/7B/13B, three ingredient seeds; Marin cooldown phases documented). P4 the jump is real under one pinned harness — unverified by anyone. P5 share reportable — holds via per-benchmark vector. P6 mixes are selected on cooldown-phase score movement — STRENGTHENED beyond the record (Marin model card; Blakeney et al. arXiv:2406.03476; Llama 3 arXiv:2407.21783; Gemma's mixture "determined partially based on downstream benchmark evaluations"). P-implicit, unlisted by the record: that the cooldown gain is either sharpening/format or newly acquired knowledge, so a small format share licenses the practice — FAILS (Gate 4, attack 4).

What would count as an answer: a pre-declared per-benchmark decomposition with estimators, uncertainty and a validated manipulation check on >=2 open runs under one harness, plus the re-ranking of >=3 published annealing mixes under the knowledge component alone. Pre-committed bands: (i) small component and no re-ranking -> practice licensed; (ii) large and re-ranks -> published ablations partly selected format compliance; (iii) large but no re-ranking -> a reporting finding without a curation consequence. Gate 4 shows band (i) does not support the inference attached to it.
