## Restatement (my own words)

Take every reservoir or lake SWOT sees whose operator (a) publishes a water-level record, (b) tells you what vertical datum that record sits in or how to convert it to one, and (c) has an operating rule that names a flood-pool, spillway-crest or freeboard elevation. For each one, subtract the operator's level — moved onto SWOT's own geoid-referenced height system using only the operator's published conversion — from SWOT's water-surface elevation. Report the spread of those differences across the population, test whether each difference holds steady or jumps/drifts over the SWOT record (checking against GNSS or similar where any is nearby), and, for the size classes where SWOT is accurate enough to tell, say what fraction of structures have a difference larger than the elevation margin in their own operating rule.

The question therefore CONDITIONS ON: (1) SWOT giving geoid-referenced WSE on reservoirs; (2) operators publishing a datum or a conversion to one; (3) operating rules naming a threshold elevation; (4) (2) and (3) co-occurring for a non-empty set; (5) the operator's OWN conversion landing in SWOT's geopotential datum, so that the subtraction is between like quantities; (6) SWOT's stated accuracy being below "the stated operating margin" in some size class; (7) an "operating margin" being a scalar the rule states. Clause (5) is the one nobody in the record tested, and it is where this log lands.
