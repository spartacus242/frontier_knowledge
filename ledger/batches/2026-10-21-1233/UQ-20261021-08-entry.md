Domain(s): Other STEM — materials engineering / failure analysis and forensic engineering (TARGET field: its literature decides novelty, its community would act). Method transplanted from forensic statistics (black-box design, variance decomposition of inconclusives) and forensic psychology (contextual bias, linear sequential unmasking). Method-owning neighbours: forensic statistics; human factors in non-destructive evaluation.

Question (canonical): Under a forensic-style black-box design — specimens failed in the laboratory by mechanisms known by construction, a mechanism label set and an explicit inconclusive option fixed in advance, examiners working independently and blind both to the answer and to one another, and each specimen randomised across context arms declared in advance as either NON-DIAGNOSTIC (which side retained the examiner) or DIAGNOSTIC-BUT-MANIPULATED-IN-VERIDICALITY (a service-history narrative that is true, false, or withheld) — what are the false-attribution rate, the inconclusive rate and the inter-examiner reproducibility of expert fracture-mode and failure-mode determination in engineering failure analysis, and by how much does each context arm shift them relative to the no-context arm?

Alternative formulations:
- (lay / historical trigger) What is the error rate of a failure analyst, measured the way the forensic pattern disciplines were compelled to measure theirs after the 2009 National Academies report?
- (sharpest single limb) Is a fracture-mode determination reproducible across examiners, and is it invariant to the narrative handed to the examiner with the specimen?
- (method field's words) For the task "assign a failure mechanism from a fixed label set", what are the discipline's failure rate, inconclusive rate and examiner/item variance components under known ground truth, and what is the measured effect of context exposure?
- V1 (intake), superseded at Gate 1: same design with "task-irrelevant context (claimed service history, identity of the commissioning party)". Reformulated because service history is a legitimate input to mechanism determination, so V1 smuggled a contested presupposition (P8) and its context limb could be answered in favour of "bias" whatever the evidence showed. V2 splits the manipulandum into a non-diagnostic component (retaining side) and a diagnostic component whose veridicality, not relevance, is varied. Estimands unchanged.

Motivating gap: Forensic pattern disciplines were pushed into measuring examiner error against constructed ground truth. Engineering failure analysis performs the same epistemic operation — an expert assigning a category from pattern features on a physical specimen — and its determinations decide insurance claims, recalls, airworthiness directives and litigation. The field's own reliability work measures DETECTION (probability of detection, false-call rate in NDE), not ATTRIBUTION. Confirmed from both sides: the method field's current methodology paper enumerates the disciplines black-box studies have been run in and names no engineering field (P3', full text); the nearest engineering neighbour measures hit/miss POD and never asks for a mechanism (P1', full text). Sharpened by P2' (abstract level), in which the target field states the premise in its own words — fractographic evaluations "are influenced by human factors, such as subjectivity, and suffer from a lack of reproducibility" — while validating a model against "the expert consensus achieved in the round robin test", i.e. the consensus is the yardstick and its own accuracy is unmeasured. That circularity is what the design would break, and it is also the candidate's closest and least-verified neighbour.

CONTESTED AND HARDER UNDER V2: forensic black-box studies withhold context, they do not fabricate it; the false-history arm has no cited precedent and is the arm most likely to be struck by a review board. - P6. Inconclusives can be analysed rather than discarded. CONTESTED AND EVIDENCED AS UNSETTLED (P3', full text: inconclusives apportioned by "the examiner variance divided by the total (examiner plus item) variance"; the method "may produce counterintuitive results in some edge cases"). - P7. An error rate, rather than a calibrated likelihood ratio, is the decision-relevant quantity. CONTESTED AND REINFORCED: P3' reports "failure rates" instead of false positive/negative error rates, so the method field is itself moving the target.
- P8 (added at Gate 1; the reason for the reformulation). Context in failure analysis separates into diagnostic and non-diagnostic components, so a bias arm is distinguishable from a legitimate-input arm. CONTESTED; the transplant's conceptual joint. If the separation fails, the diagnostic arm measures responsiveness to evidence (or credulity) rather than bias, and only the retained-side arm survives as a bias test.

What would count as an answer: A rate with a confidence interval in each design cell — false attribution, inconclusive, and an inter-examiner reproducibility statistic — plus the context effect as a difference in rates between each context arm and the no-context arm, from a pre-registered protocol in which ground truth is adjudicated independently of the examiners and the inconclusive treatment is pre-committed. A pre-registered null is an equally complete answer and is immediately usable by courts and accreditation bodies; the symmetry of usefulness between positive and null is a mark in the design's favour. A demonstration that single-mechanism ground truth cannot be constructed for the label set is a third admissible answer, reportable as a falsification of P2 (a finding about the design, not about examiners) — the discipline that stops the third branch functioning as rhetorical insurance.
