Question (canonical): In returned carbonaceous asteroid rock (Bennu and Ryugu fragments), does the internal microcrack population that depresses the rock's thermal conductivity carry the geometry and spatial statistics of volume-change (dehydration/shrinkage) cracking — near-isotropic orientation, spacing set by phyllosilicate-rich domain thickness, crack area density rising with local aqueous-alteration grade — or of impact-shock cracking (planar preferred orientations, density uncorrelated with alteration grade)?
