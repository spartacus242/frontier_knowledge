## Motivating gap

Inspection intervals and fitness-for-service decisions for pressure equipment and pipelines rest on detection performance whose empirical basis is laboratory seeded-flaw and round-robin programmes. Two of the intake's three memory claims verified, one did not:

- **MIL-HDBK-1823(A)-style POD methodology — VERIFIED from two independently opened sources.** Charles Annis's statistical-engineering.com (Annis is the handbook's author) discusses "MIL-HDBK-1823A methods" as the governing analysis; Sharma et al. 2026 (MDPI *NDT*) states that "statistically qualified probability of detection (POD), as in MIL-HDBK-1823A, is not interchangeable with AI metrics".
- **The field's belief that real POD is lower and that field data cannot yield it — VERIFIED, and more sharply stated in print than the intake assumed.** Annis's "Field Finds" page (fetched): "Using MIL-HDBK-1823A methods with Field-Finds can lead to gross errors in POD," because in field data "you know what you found, but you don't know what you missed." It rules out ordinary regression (needs the full crack population) and censored regression (needs known misses), and concludes only that field finds "require special statistical methods" — without naming any. Spencer (Sandia, 1998) opens on NDI "techniques that perform well in the laboratory but often worse in the field."
- **API 581-style RBI "consuming POD as an explicit input" — NOT VERIFIED, and the one opened source points the other way** (see the reformulation note above). This was the intake's weakest claim and it is now flagged, not asserted.
- **PISC programmes — NOT VERIFIED.** Search returned JRC repository items (EUR 21902 "How do you know?", JRC48676) consistent with a JRC-hosted NDT-reliability programme, but I opened neither. Recorded as unverified rather than carried as fact.

The structural point survives all of this: the quantity the codes need is a *field* detection probability; the field's own authority says field records cannot supply it by the standard methods; and diagnostic epidemiology and veterinary test validation solved the formally identical problem (accuracy of imperfect tests with no gold standard, from multi-test agreement across subpopulations of differing prevalence) decades ago. Thirteen queries found no instance of that machinery being pointed at inspection records.

## Presuppositions

1. **Multi-method inspection records on the same physical locations exist, are linkable, and are large enough.** CONTESTED AND BINDING — unchanged by Gate 2. The archives are owner- and vendor-held and commercially sensitive. No opened source establishes that a linkable multi-method corpus is obtainable. This is the candidate's chief feasibility risk and it is a risk about *access*, not about the estimand.
2. **Calls from different methods can be aligned to a common spatial unit** (the same weld or zone) so two methods score the same latent state. CONTESTED; plausible for girth welds and indexed nozzle/weld IDs, unevidenced for area scanning.
3. **The latent state can be binarised at a declared severity threshold.** CONTESTED and now explicitly inside the estimand: flaw size is continuous and POD is size-dependent, so the estimand is POD at declared size classes, and the size class must come from the *calls* (which are themselves error-prone) or from an external covariate. V2 fixes this by stratifying; it does not dissolve the problem.
4. **Conditional independence given the latent state is tenable or explicitly modelled.** CONTESTED and the identification crux. Conventional UT and phased-array UT share physics and will be positively correlated, which biases naive LCA toward optimistic accuracy; how many genuinely distinct physical modalities are needed is part of the question (alternative formulation 3), not a premise.
5. **At least two subpopulations with differing flaw prevalence and shared method accuracy** (the Hui–Walter requirement). CONTESTED: service class, vintage or fabrication era plausibly differ in prevalence, but the "shared accuracy across strata" half is the stronger assumption — fabrication era co-varies with weld geometry and access, which change accuracy.
6. **Inspector and vendor effects absorbable into "method" or modelled as a further level.** CONTESTED; the field's own literature treats interpreter disagreement as a first-order human-factors effect (Quality Magazine, "Human factors affecting disagreements among interpreters" — title only, not opened).
7. **Code committees and inspection owners would act on a field POD estimate.** PARTLY UNDERMINED at Gate 2 and now checkable rather than assumed: if the consuming route is a categorical effectiveness selection rather than a POD curve, the action is a re-anchoring of the categories, not an arithmetic substitution. V2 makes identifying the route part of the work.

## What would count as an answer

Identified estimates, with intervals, of method-specific POD and false-call rate at declared flaw-size classes from a pre-registered latent-class model, plus a sensitivity analysis across dependence structures showing which conclusions survive correlated methods; a comparison against the POD basis the named assessment route actually consumes (with that basis documented, not assumed); and a decision limb stating whether intervals or effectiveness-category assignments computed from field estimates differ materially from those in use. A validation arm on any subset with destructive or high-fidelity reference data (teardown, replacement sections, ILI-dig verification) is required for the estimate to be more than internally consistent. **A demonstration that the model is NOT identifiable in realistic inspection data counts as an answer**, provided it names the additional method, stratum or partial-truth sample that would identify it. Pre-committed readings: identified and materially below the lab basis → the basis is a laboratory artefact; identified and consistent → the field's folk belief is retired; not identified → a precise statement of what inspection archives would have to add, which is actionable on its own.

## Novelty — nearest prior formulations (3, with sources)

**P1. Charles Annis, "Field Finds", statistical-engineering.com** (https://statistical-engineering.com/field-finds/ — opened in full; undated methodological page by the author of MIL-HDBK-1823A). *Unit of analysis:* an individual flaw indication found in service (a "field find"), with its measured size. *Endpoint:* POD(a) — detection probability as a function of flaw size — via the handbook's â-vs-a or hit/miss regression. *Reference/comparator:* MIL-HDBK-1823A laboratory POD methodology, used as the yardstick the field data fail to meet. *Estimand:* the POD curve parameters. *What it claims:* that the standard methods cannot be used — "Using MIL-HDBK-1823A methods with Field-Finds can lead to gross errors in POD", because "you know what you found, but you don't know what you missed"; ordinary regression needs the full crack population and censored regression needs *known misses*, which field data do not supply. It says field finds "require special statistical methods" and names none.

**P2. Floyd W. Spencer, "Identifying sources of variation for reliability analysis of field inspections", SAND1998-0980C, Sandia National Laboratories, 1998** (https://www.sandia.gov/research/publications/details/identifying-sources-of-variation-for-reliability-analysis-of-field-inspecti-1998-04-01/ — abstract page opened; full text not retrieved). *Unit:* an inspection of an airframe location, within formal laboratory and field *experiments*. *Endpoint:* POD curve. *Reference:* laboratory performance of the same technique, plus two-parameter POD models as the model-form comparator. *Estimand:* POD-curve parameters under field conditions, with an argument for three- and four-parameter forms. *What it claims:* that NDI techniques performing well in the laboratory often do worse in the field, and that field POD must be modelled with extra parameters. Truth in its field arm comes from designed experiments, not from inter-method agreement; the abstract page does not state how flaw status was established, so this is recorded as partly unresolved rather than as a clean distinction.

**P3. Kuldeep Sharma, Ashok Kumar, Vineet Yadav, Sambit Dhar, Dipak K. Banerjee, "From Detection to Prediction: The NDE 4.0 Transition", *NDT* (MDPI) 2026;4(3):17, DOI 10.3390/ndt4030017** (https://www.mdpi.com/2813-477X/4/3/17 — opened). *Unit:* published sources and NDE technology generations (a structured narrative review; "No new experimental datasets were generated or analysed"). *Endpoint:* qualitative maturity assessment; no quantitative endpoint. *Reference:* statistically qualified POD per MIL-HDBK-1823A, contrasted with AI metrics (accuracy, F1), and laboratory contrasted with field human performance. *Estimand:* none — it explicitly declines to pool performance figures across non-comparable datasets and metrics. *What it claims, and why it matters here:* it discusses the laboratory-versus-field performance gap and **identifies a systematic cross-modality benchmark as an open research gap** — the field's most recent review naming the neighbourhood of this question as unfilled, while proposing a benchmark (new known-truth data) rather than an estimator over existing records.

**P4 (fourth, graded on for the MAPOD limb). Xiangyu Lei, Håkan Wirdelius, Anders Rosell, "Simulation-Based Investigation of a Probability of Detection (POD) Model Using Phased Array Ultrasonic Testing (PAUT) Technique", *Journal of Nondestructive Evaluation* 2022;41:40** (https://research.chalmers.se/publication/530257/file/530257_Fulltext.pdf — opened in full). *Unit:* a simulated PAUT inspection of a lack-of-fusion defect of known size in an AM component. *Endpoint:* hit/miss and â responses → log-normal/probit POD curve. *Reference:* a metamodel's discrete POD values, plus a modified DAC/TVG gain correction. *Estimand:* POD(a) and its model-form adequacy. ## Novelty — how this differs from each

**vs P1 (the closest, and the one that most clearly leaves the door open).** Same field, same quantity, same data class, and P1 states the candidate's motivating premise in the field's own authoritative voice. It differs on all four attributes in the way that matters: P1's unit is a *found* flaw with a measured size, so its estimand is a size-indexed POD fitted to a sample that by construction excludes misses — which is precisely why it fails. The candidate's unit is a *location* scored by ≥2 methods, its latent variable is the unobserved flaw state of that location, and misses enter as informative discordant cells rather than as missing data. P1's negative result ("regression needs known misses") is a refutation of the regression route, not of the agreement route, and P1 names no replacement. The candidate is a specific, pre-registrable answer to P1's unnamed "special statistical methods".

**vs P2.** Shares the endpoint (field POD curve) and the lab-vs-field framing, and is the field's most serious attempt at the problem. Differs on reference and estimand: P2 adds *parameters* to the POD model and obtains them from designed field experiments — i.e. it buys truth with new fieldwork — whereas the candidate adds a *latent variable* and obtains POD from agreement structure in records already written. P2 also estimates no false-call rate as a co-estimated parameter, and has no identifiability limb. Residual uncertainty: the full text is unread, so I cannot exclude that P2's field arm contains an agreement-based component; if it does, novelty drops.

**vs P3.** Differs on every attribute (review vs estimation; qualitative maturity vs POD/FCR per size class; no estimand vs a declared one). Its importance is directional rather than competitive: a 2026 review in the candidate's own journal, having surveyed the literature, names cross-modality comparison as a gap and reaches for a new benchmark. That is independent evidence both that the question's neighbourhood is open and that the field's reflex is to generate truth rather than to model it as latent.

**vs P4 and the MAPOD family.** Differs on all four attributes; its role is to explain the silence. MAPOD is the field's funded answer to "lab POD does not transfer", and it works by *manufacturing* ground truth in simulation. A method that needs no truth at all competes with MAPOD for the same problem and has no constituency built around it.

**vs the neighbouring fields swept.** (a) *Diagnostic epidemiology / veterinary validation* (Q4, Q9): owns the method, knows of no engineering application, and the transplant is not reciprocated. (b) *Software inspection* (Q11): owns the other no-truth family, capture-recapture over multiple reviewers, with a different estimand (residual defect *count*, not per-method sensitivity) and no crossing into NDT. (c) *Pipeline integrity* (Q5): has the richest multi-run archives and an actual qualification standard (API 1163), but every performance estimate is anchored on in-field verification digs — partial ground truth, which is the candidate's *validation arm*, not its estimator. (d) *Bridge management* (Q7): absorbs inspection error into transition/deterioration models rather than estimating a method-specific POD/FCR pair. (e) *Civil-marine NDT, Schoefs lineage* (Q12): already carries POD *and* false-alarm rate as a joint object (ROC), which is the candidate's parameterisation, but calibrates it against known or simulated states. (f) *Within NDE itself* (Q2, Q3): multiple inspections are used to *improve* or to *compose* POD from an assumed POD — the inverse direction.

## Significance — consequence

**If identified and field POD is materially below the basis in use:** the detection performance that inspection intervals and fitness-for-service decisions rest on is a laboratory artefact, and the error is directional — intervals are too long and remaining-life claims too generous. The correction is computable, and because P1 already concedes that field records cannot be analysed by the standard route, the finding arrives without a competing estimate to argue against. **If identified and consistent with the basis in use:** a widely held folk belief in the inspection community ("real POD is much worse") is retired with evidence rather than assertion, and the laboratory programmes are vindicated as transfer instruments — a result that would also strengthen MAPOD's standing. **If not identified:** the answer is a precise statement of what inspection archives would have to contain — one more physically distinct modality, one more prevalence-differing stratum, or a small partial-truth sample — to make field POD estimable at all. That is actionable by the parties who commission inspections, and it converts "unmeasurable" from a belief into a derived data requirement. The consequence is discounted on one count: the route into code arithmetic is now unverified (presupposition 7), so the yes-branch may land on effectiveness-category calibration rather than on interval formulae.

## Significance — audience

API's risk-based-inspection committee and the API RP 581 / API 1163 maintainers (the 4th edition, 2025, is current, so the document is live); ASME Section XI and nuclear in-service-inspection programmes, whose probabilistic-fracture-mechanics route is the likeliest place an explicit POD curve is consumed numerically (NRC/PNNL are the named publishers in that space); the NDT-reliability methods community that maintains MIL-HDBK-1823A and the ENIQ/JRC qualification tradition, which is the community that would have to accept or reject a truth-free estimator; pipeline operators and ILI vendors, who already hold the multi-run archives and already run dig-verification programmes that would supply the validation arm; insurers and regulators who price or accept interval extensions. Discount honestly: the asset owners and inspection vendors who hold the data are the parties least served by a published field POD below the certified basis, which is both an audience fact and a reason-for-silence mechanism (below).

## Significance — fecundity

Whether inspector and vendor identity, not method, carries most of the accuracy variance (a second level in the same model, and a different procurement conclusion); value-of-information for commissioning one additional *physically distinct* modality purely to buy identifiability; whether the categorical effectiveness scales in RBI are calibrated against anything measurable, and what their implied POD is; a Grubbs-type continuous analogue for depth and length *sizing* error without a reference, which is the quantity corrosion-growth models actually consume; whether POD estimated from agreement is stable across fabrication eras, which tests the Hui–Walter constant-accuracy assumption in a setting where it can be probed; and, reflexively, whether the medical no-gold-standard literature's dependence-structure diagnostics behave in a regime with very low prevalence and two near-duplicate physics, which is harsher than most clinical applications.

## Significance — anchoring

The documented laboratory-versus-field POD gap (P2 1998; P3 2026 naming cross-modality benchmarking as still open); P1's "you know what you found, but you don't know what you missed", which is the field's own statement of a missing-data problem it has not solved; MAPOD's standing as the accepted substitute (P4) and its reliance on simulated truth; API 1163-style ILI qualification and its dependence on dig verification; the identifiability limits of latent-class models under correlated errors, which is a live methodological problem in the method-owning field and would be stressed harder here than in clinical use.

## Reason for silence

Four mechanisms, in descending order of how well I can evidence them.

1. **A definitional block inside the target field, evidenced from P1 and P4.** NDE defines POD as a function of flaw *size*, so "truth" in the field's own grammar means a measured crack with a known length. An estimator whose latent variable is an unsized binary state at a location does not look like a POD estimator to an NDT reliability specialist — it looks like a category error. P1 enumerates exactly two analysis routes (ordinary and censored regression), both of which require the flaw population, and then stops; the possibility that detection performance could be recovered from *agreement* rather than from truth is not among the options considered. This is the strongest mechanism because it is visible in print rather than inferred.
2. **An incentive and ownership gap.** The multi-method archives are owner- and vendor-held (presupposition 1, contested and binding). A published field POD below the certified laboratory basis is a liability and procurement disclosure for the inspection vendor and a fitness-for-service exposure for the asset owner. The only parties with the data are the parties with the strongest reason not to compute the number, and no third party can assemble it.
3. **A disciplinary boundary with no traffic, verified in both directions.** Q4 and Q9 found the method-owning vocabulary (Hui–Walter, no-reference-standard accuracy) returning zero engineering applications, and the target field's vocabulary returning zero latent-class work. Q11 shows the same boundary for the *other* no-truth family: capture-recapture over multiple reviewers is mature in software inspection and has also never crossed. Two independent families stopped at the same border makes a boundary mechanism more credible than a single absence would.
4. **The demand was absorbed by a funded substitute.** MAPOD (P4) answers "laboratory POD does not transfer" by manufacturing truth in simulation, and P3 shows the field's 2026 reflex is still to call for a new benchmark. A method requiring no truth competes with MAPOD for the same problem, needs no new instrumentation money, and has no constituency, standard or software behind it.

Not "no one thought of it": P1 thought of the problem precisely, named its statistical character correctly, and left the method slot empty.

## Scores

**Well-posedness: 2/3.** The evidence that would settle it is statable, pre-registrable, and includes a pre-committed negative (unidentifiability with the named remedy), which is what keeps the question from being answerable-either-way. Two deductions. (a) The reference arm is not yet identified: V1 asserted a POD-curve input to RBI that the one opened code-side source does not show, and V2 had to demote identifying the consuming route to part of the question — a question whose comparator must be discovered is not fully specified. (b) The severity threshold and size-class boundaries that define the latent state are declared contested but not fixed numerically, and they must be derived from error-prone calls or an external covariate; until they are declared, two analysts could estimate different quantities and both call them field POD. Presupposition 1 (data access) is a feasibility risk, not a well-posedness defect, and is not scored here.

**Significance: 2/3.** Consequence is real and directional on all three branches, the audience is specific, live and named (API RP 581 4th ed. 2025; ASME XI/NRC; API 1163 operators and vendors), and the fecundity list contains questions that cannot be asked at all until this one is settled. Held to 2 because the arithmetic path from a field POD estimate to a changed inspection interval is exactly the claim Gate 2 could not verify: if the consuming route is a categorical effectiveness selection, the yes-branch changes a committee's anchoring rather than a formula's input, which is a softer consequence than the intake asserted. A verified POD-curve consumer (most plausibly a probabilistic-fracture-mechanics route) would move this to 3.
