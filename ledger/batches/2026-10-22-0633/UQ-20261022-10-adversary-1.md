## Restatement (my own words)

Pick a country and a date range. First, write down in advance what counts as a maker "pulling the plug" on an implant (no more programmer, no firmware or security patches, no consumables or external parts, no spare parts, no remote-monitoring service, no one to phone), then list every such event in that country and window, and for each say how many people are walking around with that implant now, with a source and an error bar. Second, for the events where those people can actually be traced, measure how often they lose therapy, get revised, get explanted or acquire a new "do not scan / do not treat" restriction after the maker left, against the same people before the maker left and against people with a comparable still-supported implant; allow the answer "no effect" for inert implants. Finally report, as a result and not a caveat, what fraction of events could not be counted and what fraction of implant classes could not be followed at all.
