## Restatement (own words)

A BPE/Unigram tokenizer is fitted on some slice of the pretraining corpus. The candidate asks: if you refit the same recipe (same algorithm, pre-tokenizer, vocabulary size) on k ≥ 5 independent random slices of the same corpus, (a) how different are the resulting tokenizers — vocabulary Jaccard, merge-order agreement, held-out per-domain fertility SD — as a function of slice size S; and (b) once each tokenizer is carried through pretraining on identical text at matched byte and FLOP budgets, is the refit-to-refit spread in BPB / downstream scores comparable to seed noise under one fixed tokenizer, or to the published algorithm- and vocabulary-size gaps? Shorter: is a tokenizer a sample statistic with a standard error, and is that SE large enough to swallow published tokenizer-comparison effects?
