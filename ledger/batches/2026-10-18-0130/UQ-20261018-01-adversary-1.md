## Restatement (my own words)

Take people who carry a chronic-disease code — COPD, hypothyroidism, coeliac disease — and look for the moment when a result that by agreed rule rules the disease out lands in the same record (post-bronchodilator FEV1/FVC >= 0.7; normal TSH >= 6 weeks off levothyroxine; negative serology plus negative biopsy on a gluten-containing diet). From that moment, how quickly, over 24 months, does the record itself say the diagnosis is gone (a resolved/inactivated/exclusion event, or failing that 180 days without the disease's drugs), compared with similar labelled people who never got such a result — and what fraction of each group is still being prescribed the disease's drugs at 12 and 24 months? Penicillin allergy + negative challenge is the pair where removal is known to happen, used as a check that the method can detect anything.

Note: the Gate 2 Low grade rests on Fortis 2016 (COPD). I treat it as a prior finding and re-ran the attacks.
