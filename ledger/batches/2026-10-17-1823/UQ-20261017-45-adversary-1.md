## Restatement (my own words)

Take every published EPD for construction products that can be scraped, sort them into bins of "same function" (same declared unit, same strength/performance class, same service life, at least cradle-to-gate A1-A3). Within a bin, declared GWP varies. The candidate asks: (i) how much of that within-bin variance is due to WHICH RULEBOOK / DATABASE / OPERATOR / VERIFIER was used, versus WHICH FACTORY made it — identified by looking at products that have been declared more than once under different rulebooks; (ii) if you forced every declaration onto one rulebook, what fraction of A-beats-B comparisons would flip, and what fraction of pass/fail calls against the Buy Clean limit in force would flip; (iii) how much of the corpus had to be thrown away because the rulebook metadata was not extractable. Headline version (the record's own alternative formulation): do EPDs measure factories or methods, and does it change who wins a Buy Clean award?
