THIS REJECTION'S OWN VULNERABILITY, recorded because nobody has measured what it turns on: (a) Leg A is an ALGEBRAIC consequence of the ICC estimator's definition — given a clone-wide variance component and a cell line that is clonal or near-clonal, sister-minus-matched-non-sister is near zero in expectation at the null by construction, which is an identity about variance components and not a bitwise or empirical finding. Its empirical premise, that the shared component in the candidate's declared cell line is clone-wide rather than division-proximate for INFECTIOUS output, is unmeasured, and the contaminating share is a number nobody has printed.
