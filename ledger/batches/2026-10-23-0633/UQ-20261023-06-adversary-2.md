## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

Take studies that already use the consensus CGM hypoglycaemia metrics as endpoints — completed randomised trials or registry cohorts where percent time below 70 mg/dL, percent time below 54 mg/dL, or the level-2 hypoglycaemic event count is a primary or key secondary outcome. Keep only those that (a) used a CGM model for which somebody has published a pressure-induced-sensor-attenuation ("compression low") detector with a stated hit-rate/false-alarm trade-off, and (b) still have the raw per-reading traces with timestamps; report how many candidate studies that filtering throws away. Now run that detector over the traces at a threshold fixed in advance, mark the flagged readings as missing, impute them by a rule also fixed in advance, and recompute the endpoints. Three numbers are wanted: how many percentage points the nocturnal <70 and <54 figures move and how much the level-2 event count per 14 days moves; how much of that movement is the detector crying wolf rather than real artifact, bounded from data where two sensors were worn at once at the same threshold; and whether the recomputed between-arm difference, with its CI, crosses the trial's own pre-specified margin (or, failing that, the half-width of its primary 95% CI) or flips sign — repeated across a band of thresholds declared up front.

In one sentence: *does deleting compression artifacts change the hypoglycaemia numbers trials are judged on, and does it change any trial's answer?* The estimand is deliberately a change in the **reported** metric under a declared rule, not the artifact's true share against real plasma glucose.

## The record's nearest-printed-quantity check, redone on a number the record does not carry

S1 prints a percentage-of-time figure the record never mentions, so it needs the numerator/denominator/overlap treatment before anyone calls the candidate's quantity unpublished.

- **NUMERATOR CLASS:** minutes of CGM signal **discarded as detector false positives** — readings the detector flagged that the three-operator consensus labels did not call faults.
- **DENOMINATOR:** 14,400 minutes, i.e. total monitoring minutes of a 10-day trace. Not minutes below a threshold, not nights, not events.
- **OVERLAP, BOTH DIRECTIONS.** What it **MISSES** that the candidate includes: every **true-positive** artifact minute (the artifact's own contribution, which is the whole object), the nocturnal restriction, the restriction to readings **below 70 / 54 mg/dL** (TBR counts minutes under a threshold, not minutes at all), level-2 event counts, and any arm contrast. What it **INCLUDES** that the candidate excludes: discarded minutes at euglycaemia and hyperglycaemia, which contribute nothing to TBR in either direction.
- **Conclusion:** a different numerator on a different denominator. S1 **bounds the detector's own wastage**; it does not measure the candidate's quantity, and the record's published-is-not-performed finding survives this closer look.
