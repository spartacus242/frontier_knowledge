## What happened to this batch

This batch was launched twice by mistake. The first launch's working directory was still empty
twenty minutes in — it was in its generate phase — and was read as a failed launch, so a second
batch was started on the same trigger. Both ran to completion, ten candidates in all, and both drew
on the same shared web-search budget. The second batch is reported separately as
[2026-10-16 19:16](2026-10-16-1916.md), entries UQ-20261016-51 … -55.

The budget is per-turn and shared by every agent, so the two batches starved each other. Across the
two, **six of fourteen adversaries executed zero of their eight required Disguise queries**, every
call refused on an exhausted pool. Four candidates were then rejected on an attack recorded
"Unresolved" that had never been run. That is not a finding about the candidates; it is a finding
about the reviewing, and it has been fixed in two places:

- `LEDGER.md` Gate 4 now distinguishes **Unverified** (the check did not run) from **Unresolved**
  (the check ran and the candidate's answer was weak). An Unverified fatal attack blocks Validated
  and caps an entry at Provisional with the unrun check as its promotion condition. It never
  produces a rejection on its own.
- `LEDGER.md` operating rules now require one batch at a time, each with its own scratchpad
  directory, and say explicitly that an empty workflow directory means a batch is generating, not
  that it has failed.

## The verification pass

Eleven fresh-context checkers were run across the two batches, one per load-bearing prior or kill,
each handed the claim and its source and not the reviewer's reasoning, plus two 12-query prior-art
sweeps for candidates whose adversaries had never been able to search. In this batch:

- **UQ-46's kill was refuted at its load-bearing figure.** The rejection rested on "substitution is
  59–65% of surviving URLs (adversary arithmetic on Carlini et al.'s stated CC-3M counts,
  arXiv:2302.10149)". That paper states no such counts. It reports about 1.04% of CC-3M images on
  expired domains and, among domains hosting more than 10 images, *one* domain serving substituted
  content under perceptual similarity (two more flagged by hash and judged benign). The confound the
  adversary pointed at is real in kind; its magnitude is unmeasured, and on the only published
  measurement it is small rather than dominant. Rejected → Provisional.
- **UQ-47's kill was upheld with its citation corrected.** arXiv:2605.22821 is "Tokenisation via
  Convex Relaxations" (Tempus, Whittington, Schmidt, Komm, Pimentel). No figure carries the caption
  the adversary gave; the real locus is Figure 3, under "Stability of Tokenisers to Randomness on
  the Training Sample", which plots average Jaccard similarity between vocabularies retrained on
  *independently sampled* subsets — the design the kill turns on. The adversary's k = 5 and "four
  sample sizes" could not be confirmed and are not carried into the entry. New: the paper does train
  language models and report bits-per-byte and CORE score, but never joins them to the stability
  figure, so the candidate's propagation limb is untouched by it.
- **UQ-48's empirical leg was confirmed, its methods leg only partly sourced.** The published UK AISI
  account of the joint US/UK institute pre-deployment evaluation describes separate but complementary
  tests, with different suites per domain (cyber: 40 public challenges against 47, of which 32
  private), only one institute's biology findings published, and access given once as a "limited
  period of pre-deployment access". No finding-level overlap is reconstructible, which is the
  presupposition the candidate's second limb needs. The capture-recapture-at-two-lists claim is
  reasoning, not a cited result, and the entry says so.
- **UQ-50's kill was confirmed verbatim** — the only decisive ground in either batch that needed no
  correction. Nemotron-CC (arXiv:2412.02595) Table 8: union 11,359,655, intersection 1,152,821
  (10.1%), FineWeb-Edu only 4,022,294, DCLM only 6,184,540; 368 of the top 1k domains shared. The
  cells sum exactly to the union, so the table is union-restricted and yields no kappa; keep rates
  are native and unequal (about 5.18M against 7.34M), so the matched-rate limb survives untouched.
- **UQ-49's Disguise was finally executed** — 12 queries in a dedicated sweep plus 6 in a neighbours
  check — and found no prior. The question's three ingredients each exist in a different literature
  and nothing joins them: NVFP4 pretraining (arXiv:2509.25149) has the right treatment and comparator
  but reports benchmark *categories*, with multilingual as an aggregate and no frequency split; "The
  Fair Language Model Paradox" (arXiv:2410.11985) has exactly the rarity-stratified apparatus but
  applies it to weight decay; the disparate-impact-of-compression lineage (arXiv:2407.03211,
  arXiv:1911.05248, arXiv:2010.03058) and DP-SGD (arXiv:1905.12101) all treat an already-trained
  network or private training, never the training trajectory.

## Reading the rate

60% is the honest number for this batch, and it is a fact about the reviewing as much as about the
candidates. One rejection was manufactured by an unrun check and a figure that does not exist in the
paper it was attributed to; one was confirmed verbatim; two stand on evidence that was re-read and
restated. Of the five candidates, two now sit at Provisional with pre-registered promotion
conditions, and UQ-49 is the strongest candidate the ledger has produced since UQ-20261016-41: its
Disguise is Resolved on executed evidence and what caps it is a fixable estimand-normalisation
defect, not a prior.
