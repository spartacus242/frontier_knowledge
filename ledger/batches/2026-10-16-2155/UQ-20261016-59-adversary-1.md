# UQ-20261016-59 — Adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)
A tree, a coral colony or a clonal plant is one "individual" made of many modules (branches, nubbins, ramets) that have accumulated different somatic mutations and, in corals, may even be fused from different zygotes. Population-genomic studies take one module per individual and compute heterozygosity, kinship, allele frequencies and Ne. The candidate asks: if you had taken a different module from the same individual, how much would those numbers move, expressed as a variance ratio (between-module-within-individual / between-individual) per estimand, with technical replicate variance removed and chimeras reported separately from mosaics, in at least one tree and one coral/clonal marine taxon. The record itself concedes the genome-wide heterozygosity limb is probably negligible and derivable from published counts, and bets the question's value on kinship-near-threshold, allele frequencies at somatically variable sites, and the coral chimerism limb.

## What the opened sources actually say (load-bearing, quoted)
- Yu et al. 2020 (Zostera marina, one genet): "All 24 ramets were sequenced at an average coverage of 81x"; three ramets at 1370x; "thousands of single nucleotide polymorphisms that segregated among ramets" (7,054 SNPs in the NJ tree); 31,777 SNPs "identically heterozygous among all 24 ramets" in the filtered set; "7 somatic polymorphisms" at microsatellites. No between-genet axis, no relatedness coefficients, no variance partition, no statement about single-ramet sampling bias.
- Devlin-Durante et al. 2016 (Acropora palmata): 3,352 samples, 5 microsatellite loci; "1387 ramets of 147 genets"; "342 unique mutational changes across the 5 microsatellite loci"; ramets assigned to genets allowing an additional/changed allele at one locus; "0.2% of samples ... classified as possible chimeras"; "sampling a colony once may cause an underestimation of mutational load due to intracolonial variation". No estimand variance, no misassignment rate.
- Oury et al. 2024 (Pocillopora, Reunion): 93 colonies x 3 nubbins (apical branch tips), target capture of 1248 UCEs + 1385 exons, "mean SNP coverage depth ... 46.3x", three nubbins "prepared and sequenced three times each" for sequencing-error estimation; "18 colonies presented IGV", "7 ... were mosaics and 11 ... were chimeras"; "the numbers of IGV and mosaic colonies were extremely sensitive to the threshold defined"; "All except 12 differences (< 0.1%) involved genotypes differing by a single allele"; "only one representative of each clonal lineage was kept for assignment tests"; no relatedness, F-statistics, allele-frequency or Ne analysis; no re-run of population analyses with different nubbins; extraction replicates omitted because sub-samples of a single nubbin "can thus present IGV".

## Recommended verdict
Provisional. Nothing Unresolved; Partials on 1, 4, 6, 7 and 8. Novelty holds at Medium (the quantitative estimand-variance form was not found under nine new vocabularies, and the on-point Oury 2024 paper was READ and does not compute it), but the record's reason-for-silence is partly falsified and the coral limb is now a re-analysis of a public dataset rather than a new study. Promotion condition: a one-page bound using published counts (Hofmeister 2020 full text; Yu 2020; Oury 2024 Zenodo data) showing the MOSAIC between-module component for kinship is not trivially below between-individual variance; if that bound shows it is, Triviality becomes Unresolved and the entry should be Rejected with routing to Oury 2024 / Devlin-Durante 2016 as the place the identity limb already lives.

## Single strongest killing point
The crossed dataset the record says cannot exist already exists and has been published without the question being asked: Oury et al. 2024 (Sci Rep, 93 Pocillopora colonies x 3 nubbins, target capture at 46x, technical replicates, chimera/mosaic classification, data on Zenodo) found that within-colony signal is dominated by chimerism (11 of 18 IGV colonies), that mosaic differences are "all except 12 ... a single allele", and that IGV counts are "extremely sensitive to the threshold defined" — so the coral limb reduces to published chimera prevalence times a kinship factor, the mosaic limb sits at the sequencing-error floor, and what remains is a weekend re-analysis, not a study.
