## Restatement in my own words

Take one genome-scale single-gene CRISPRi screen with single-cell RNA readout (the K562 Perturb-seq atlas, ~9,600 perturbations). Instead of asking the usual question — "which perturbations did something?" — turn the response matrix on its side and ask of each *measured* gene: did anything at all move it? Declare in advance which genes count as measured, a grid of minimum log-fold-change effect sizes, a multiplicity rule for the union over ~10⁴ perturbations (a gene counts as responsive only if called in ≥2 perturbations under an FDR on the whole gene × perturbation matrix), and a per-gene power curve. Then report three counts as a function of effect size — moved; not moved though we had power to see it; not moved but we never had power — and repeat in a second cell line (RPE1) to ask whether the same genes come up unmovable in both, more often than chance. Scope: reachability under CRISPRi knockdown specifically.

In one line: **how much of the transcriptome does no single-gene knockdown touch, and is the untouched part the same in two cell lines?**

### What the two opened papers actually say

**Albert FW, Bloom JS, Kruglyak L. eLife 2018;7:e35471 (read via bioRxiv 208447 full PDF).** Unit of analysis: **the measured gene's expression**, across 1,012 segregants. Endpoint: linkage to ≥1 distant locus at 5% FDR. Quoted: *"Nearly every expressed gene (98%; 5,606) had at least one such distant, trans-acting eQTL."* And: *"Only 77 genes had no detected eQTL."* 36,498 eQTL for 5,643 genes. **Per-gene power to separate "no effect" from "underpowered": absent for eQTL** — only a genome-wide power estimate; the per-gene simulations in the paper (*"For each gene, we conducted 100 simulations using the estimated ρ and sequencing coverage for the given ASE dataset"*) are for allele-specific expression, a different endpoint. The 77 non-detected genes are **not characterized** and not compared across crosses.

**Zhang et al. (Nat Commun 2023, s41467-023-41572-4) "Genome-wide promoter responses to CRISPR perturbations of regulators reveal regulatory networks in Escherichia coli."** 1,323 measured promoters × 183 TF-targeting CRISPRi sgRNAs (+5 non-targeting), three media, promoter activity by sort-seq, significance at q<0.01 with a >1.7-fold threshold. Quoted: *"936 (71% of 1323 measured promoters) variable promoters with significant activity change under at least one TFKD condition"*, and *"the other 29% of the promoters were considered as constant promoters."* Cross-condition recurrence is reported at the pair level (*"867 TF-promoter pairs appeared in all three conditions"*; 1,901/2,274/3,495 in one condition only) with the conclusion that responses are highly condition-specific. **Per-promoter power/detection-limit analysis: absent from the main text** (the paper's only detection caveat is perturbation-side: *"CRISPRi did not work well for weakly expressed TF genes, leading to false negatives due to insufficient TF repression"*). Caveat recorded honestly: Supplementary Notes were not in the fetched content, so I do not claim the power analysis is absent from the supplement — and if it is present there, the kill below is stronger, not weaker.
