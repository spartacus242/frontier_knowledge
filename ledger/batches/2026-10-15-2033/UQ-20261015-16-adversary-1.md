# UQ-20261015-16 — Gate 4 adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Take an archived robot-synthesis campaign (concretely the A-Lab's 58 attempted targets), have three or more crystallographers independently and blindly vote "target present / absent / can't tell" on every pattern at a stated minor-phase cut-off, then measure (a) how often the robot's call disagrees with the panel majority, compared against how often panel members disagree with one another, and (b) whether the confidence number the robot already computed tracks the panel's verdict well enough that the campaign could have quoted its own false-discovery rate while running.

So it is: "apply a multi-reader diagnostic-accuracy audit to one self-driving lab's archive, and calibrate the lab's deployed score against the resulting consensus label."

## Recommended verdict

**Provisional.** Nothing Unresolved, so not Rejected; but Disguise is only Partial because Gate 2 missed Hattrick-Simpers et al. 2021 (consensus multi-expert XRD phase labels with quantified variance used as an ML benchmark), which makes part (a) a known question re-sited on the A-Lab archive and falsifies the record's "disciplinary boundary" reason for silence; presupposition 1 (per-sample scores archived for all 58 targets) remains unverified. Novelty holds only for the residue (b) + campaign-level FDR, i.e. Low–Medium, not Medium. To promote: (1) add Hattrick-Simpers 2021 and the Reynolds Cup as P-neighbours and re-argue novelty against them; (2) verify from the A-Lab data release that per-sample scores exist for failed targets; (3) fix numeric acceptance bands.
