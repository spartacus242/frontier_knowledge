## 1. Restatement in my own words

Take the map s(n) = sigma(n) - n and iterate it. Average the one-step log growth, log(s(N)/N), not over *starting values* (which is what the aliquot constant lambda is) but over *iterates that are still going* — iterates whose sequence has not yet hit 1 or a cycle — and bin those iterates by how big they are (decimal length d) and by how many steps they are into their sequence (k). Question: is that survivor-conditioned average the same number as lambda, does it move with d or k, and if it moves, is the movement explained by which Guy-Selfridge driver the survivors happen to be carrying?

As it now stands after Gate 1's one permitted reformulation, the frame is pinned: **all 499 999 even seeds below 10^6, each iterated to termination at 1, to a cycle, or to a first 100-digit iterate** — i.e. Bosma 2016's computed window — with one row per iterate, labelled d, k, survived/absorbed, driver class. I test the question **as repaired**, repair included, as well as in its unpinned form.
