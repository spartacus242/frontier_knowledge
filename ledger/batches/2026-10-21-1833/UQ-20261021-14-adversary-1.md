Role: adversary (Gate 4), spawned on a Gate 2 rejection. The Low novelty grade was treated as a prior finding, not inherited; all eight attacks run on their merits. Budget used: 9 web searches (cap 9), 4 fetches (cap 4). No hearthbot tools called. No search call was refused.

## Restatement (my own words, before reading the attacks)

The open-weight masked-diffusion LLMs released in 2025 at 7–8B (LLaDA-8B, Dream-7B) report perplexities that come from an evidence lower bound, not from the model's log-likelihood. The candidate asks: by how many nats per token does that bound undershoot the true log-likelihood of the same model, and if you replaced the bound with the true number (or a two-sided estimate of it), would the diffusion-vs-autoregressive perplexity rankings in release tables change? Implicit in it: that "the true log-likelihood" of such a model is one number.
