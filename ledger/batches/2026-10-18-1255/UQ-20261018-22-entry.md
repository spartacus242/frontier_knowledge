Domain(s): Medicine — emergency/critical-care clinical research; research ethics and regulatory science. Method field: survey methodology (coverage and nonresponse error).
Question (canonical): Over a declared frame of completed FDA-regulated EFIC trials (the 105-trial EFIC census, 77 completed), with the consulted group individuated in advance as consulted individuals whose own characteristics were recorded: (a) in what fraction of trials were consultation-participant characteristics recorded and are they retrievable now; (b) among those, what is the distribution of a pre-declared distributional-overlap statistic (TVD and SMDs over age band, sex, race/ethnicity, area-based SES) between consulted and enrolled groups, plus a separate consultation-location/enrolment-catchment overlap measure; and (c) under a pre-declared rule, in what fraction are the two groups effectively disjoint?
Alternative formulations:
- Did the people consulted before an EFIC trial resemble the people the trial went on to enrol?
- Treating community consultation as a sampling exercise from the at-risk population, what is its coverage and selection bias, trial by trial?
Motivating gap: The settled claim inverted — EFIC is permissible partly because the community from which subjects are drawn is consulted first. Record established first-hand at eCFR that § 50.24(a)(7)(iii) makes the ENROLLED group's demographics a required public disclosure while nothing in § 50.24 requires any record of who was consulted, and that the 105-trial EFIC census (medRxiv 2022.08.23.22279138) abstracted neither variable. The gap claim that carried the novelty grade — "the only thing anyone has linked across the two is counts" — is false (see Gate 4).
Presuppositions: (1) consultation-participant characteristics recorded and retrievable — SETTLED AFFIRMATIVELY at Gate 4: 15,958 pages of consultation survey data on 42,448 individuals from 27 trials sit in one public FDA docket; (2) enrolled characteristics published at comparable granularity — partly false, enrolled-side race available for only 17,302 of 45,694 in that docket, and PROPPR's enrolment log carried only age, ethnicity and postal code, so area-based SES is uncomputable there; (3) CONTESTED, "representatives" = a sample vs designated spokespeople, rule sets no criteria; (4) enrolling catchment identifiable, weakest on geography; (5) CONTESTED, consultation protective in a who-dependent way — under the public-notification reading low overlap is not a defect; (6) trial-level demographic reporting not too coarse to be informative. Also presupposed by the record and refuted: that no form or publication exists where the two sides' demographics meet.
What would count as an answer: A pre-registered audit of the declared frame reporting, per trial, (a) recording/retrievability, (b) the pre-declared overlap statistics plus catchment overlap, (c) the disjointness fraction, reported under both readings of "representatives". Determinate in both directions and in the data-poor regime.
