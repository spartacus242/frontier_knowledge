## 1. Restatement (my own words, written before any attack)

Take stochastic processes whose exact per-symbol entropy rate H is known analytically — a specified 8-state discrete HMM (H via the mixed-state/Blackwell integral) and a specified PCFG in Chomsky normal form (H per terminal in closed form). Under a byte-level bijective tokenization, train ladders of models from two architecture families (a pre-LN decoder-only transformer; one non-attention gated-linear-recurrent/SSM family) at matched FLOPs on freshly drawn samples. Fit the standard parametric scaling law L(N,D) = E + A/N^alpha + B/D^beta, take the fitted E as the asymptote, and report **Delta = E − H in nats per symbol with a bootstrap interval**, declaring Delta zero if its 95% interval lies inside ±0.01. The headline question: does achievable loss bottom out *at* H, or at a strictly positive floor above it, and is that floor indexed by the architecture rather than by the data?
