Domain(s): Medicine — radiology care delivery, diagnostic quality and safety; clinical informatics; imaging protocol governance

Question (canonical): In sites whose viewer/PACS logs emit series-level display events, and under a provenance map and series taxonomy declared before any data are pulled: (i) what proportion of *diagnostically intended* series (excluding localizers/scouts, dose reports, failed/repeated acquisitions and auto-generated reformats/parameter maps) carry no display event attributable to any reader in the interpreting chain between study-available-in-PACS and report signature, counting a displayed derived series as coverage of each source series the provenance map says it encodes; and (ii) in a stratified sample of those never-displayed series, what is the per-series rate of actionable findings under a pre-specified actionability taxonomy, read by >=2 blinded readers with adjudication and the original report withheld? (Gate 1 reformulation; intake version — "what fraction of diagnostically intended archived series are never displayed to any interpreting reader before signature, and what is the blinded re-read yield in those series?" — recorded in the record, substantively identical.)

Alternative formulations:
- Does anyone look at all the images we acquire, and does it matter if they do not?
- Treating the PACS display log as the instrument, how much acquired image data is unexamined, and is the unexamined fraction diagnostically empty?

Motivating gap: The literature is dense on acquisition (protocol, dose, contrast timing) and dense on reader search behaviour (eye tracking, satisfaction of search), and silent on the join — whether an acquired series was opened at all. The logs exist in every PACS but are kept for access control and compliance, owned by IT/privacy rather than radiology research, and often purged on a short cycle; hanging protocols routinely display a subset of what was acquired. Confirmed at Gate 2: the one opened account of a department mining its own PACS audit logs (Maine Medical Center, SCAR 2003) used them for suspicious-access detection, VIP review and turnaround benchmarking, with vendor purge at 30 days.

Presuppositions: P1 series-level display events exist, are reader-attributable and timestamped against signing — CONTESTED, vendor-dependent; handled as an explicit scope restriction, not a hidden assumption (adversary 2's read of DICOM PS3.15 A.5 found no display event in the standard's audit schema, confirming P1 is viewer-proprietary, while logged instance UIDs plus NumberOfInstances make series-level reconstruction feasible where instance logging is on). P2 series can be partitioned in advance into diagnostically intended vs technical/derived — NECESSARY for non-triviality (the localizer re-read family owns the uninteresting answer). P3 non-display implies non-examination — CONTESTED; two-sided error (brief tiling counts as display; second-workstation/logged-out viewing counts as non-display); reconciliation substudy asserted but never demonstrated. P4 blinded multi-reader re-read can establish actionability — plausible, needs adjudication and report withholding. P5 report signature is the cut-off — flagged; later MDT/referrer views are a separate question (this is what separates the candidate from the "unreviewed reports after signature" family). P6 enough series are never displayed for the quantity to be interesting — CONTESTED, unbounded in either direction; limb (i) measures it and near-zero closes the question. P7 someone would act — evidenced. P8 not already published as a vendor/operational metric — WEAKLY CHECKED, NOT CLEARED in three vocabularies; vendor analytics documentation is largely non-indexed.

What would count as an answer: In >=2 sites with series-level display logs, a pre-registered estimate of the never-displayed proportion with distribution by modality, protocol, shift and reader workload; plus blinded multi-reader actionable-finding yield with an interval in a stratified sample; plus the P3 reconciliation substudy. All three outcomes act: near-zero fraction closes the question; substantial fraction with near-zero yield argues for stopping the acquisition; substantial fraction with non-trivial yield defines a prospectively detectable, series-attributable missed-finding class.

Significance — consequence: Three distinct actions, no uninformative outcome. Near-zero never-displayed fraction retires a recurring hanging-protocol worry with a number. Substantial fraction with near-zero blinded yield means scanner time, dose, contrast and archive cost are spent on data that never enters a diagnostic decision, converting protocol pruning from expert opinion into an audited exercise. Substantial fraction with non-trivial yield defines a missed-finding class that is measurable and attributable at the series level and detectable prospectively from logs, before harm — which no current QA instrument does, since discrepancy meetings and peer review act on reports, not on unopened data.
Significance — audience: (1) Protocol committees and modality leads, who already prune sequences without display-coverage evidence (opened RSNA QI project "Reducing Unnecessary Scan Acquisitions at Abdominal MRI"). (2) Radiology QA/patient-safety and medico-legal functions, for whom "acquired, archived, never opened, finding present" is a new documentable exposure (trade-press terrain confirmed by title only, not opened). (3) PACS/enterprise-imaging vendors: two issued patents and at least two shipped per-series viewed indicators show the feature side has invested but has no population evidence on whether a pre-signature gate is worth the interruption. (4) Compliance/IT custodians, for whom a positive result justifies longer retention and a series-level schema.
Significance — fecundity: Does the fraction rise with workload, shift and list length (a dose-response test of workload on coverage, independent of report error)? Is display coverage a better leading indicator of diagnostic error than turnaround time? Can AI triage be aimed at series the reader did not open — a far smaller, better-defined target than second-reading everything? Does hanging-protocol design explain between-site variance, making coverage a configuration-level rather than reader-level defect? Does a declared series taxonomy become a reusable coverage denominator for other display-log questions?
Significance — anchoring: Satisfaction of search and volumetric visual-search coverage (the within-series version of the same deficit); the unread-examination and follow-up-never-reviewed literatures (the same failure one and two levels up, P5's separate question); incidental findings on localizers/scouts (the same failure one level down, excluded by P2); dose and throughput optimization; and the DICOM/ATNA audit-trail standard, where the instrument's schema is actually decided.

Reason for silence: A custodial split across three owners, a retention wall, and no reporting hook. Verification (post-batch, two fresh-context checkers, logs UQ-20261016-65-verification.md and UQ-20261016-65-verification-2.md): both unread near-priors are still unread, the headline that looked like a demotion is not one, and the instrument presupposition is now evidenced rather than argued.
Neither of the two novelty-bearing papers could be opened. doi 10.1007/s10278-016-9881-1 and doi 10.1007/s10278-010-9337-y both failed at the DOI resolver; link.springer.com served abstract pages only, with full text paywalled. From the abstract pages: Forsberg et al. measure PACS usage as a "sequence of computer commands" per reading session — unit one command trace (one attending radiologist, one exam), endpoints command count, command classes, bigram repetitiveness and time to read, over eight weeks of logs, modelled by GLMs on radiologist, exam category and time of week — with no statement of series-level granularity and no never-viewed fraction; whether the log distinguishes series cannot be confirmed or excluded from the abstract. French & Langer is "Tracking PACS Usage with Open Source Tools", on workstation-fleet utilisation for purchasing decisions, with metrics not visible on the page and no indication of per-series display logging; its one visible line is "Lacking a vended solution for this challenge, we developed our own." So promotion condition (a) is untouched, and under Gate 4's rule the unread pair keeps attack 2 Unverified for the novelty limb, which caps this entry at Provisional without rejecting it.
The "40% of images, radiology reports requiring follow-up are never reviewed" headline that surfaced in the first pass is not about this candidate's quantity, and the second pass pinned down why. In the Radiology Business report (6 Nov 2018) of the Yousem neuroradiology dataset, "approximately 8.4 percent had neither images or imaging reports viewed in PACS or EMRs" across more than 8,200 studies, and of the 124 never-viewed studies "20 percent were classified as normal, 40 percent were minor unimportant findings," while "40 percent were classified as findings that require intervention or follow-up and less than 1 percent were critical." The 40% is therefore a severity split within 124 studies — about 50 studies, roughly 0.6% of the series the article covers — not 40% of follow-up-requiring studies. Its unit is the study or order, its endpoint any access to images or report in PACS or EMR logs, and the reader whose access is counted is on the requesting side, not the interpreting radiologist before signature. The same holds for the SIIM audit-log work reported by AuntMinnie: unit the ED exam, endpoint whether the referring physician ever viewed it ("Exam was never viewed by the referring physician: 5,236 (21%)" for CT, 5% for CR, 54% for US). Neither is the candidate's unit, reader or endpoint, so the demotion condition is not triggered — and Yousem supplies this entry with a published consequence anchor it did not have, at the study level, for the shape of limb (ii).
Promotion condition (b) comes back negative and is restated accordingly. Six further searches across vendor and enterprise-imaging material found no product documentation of a per-series display or view report: Intelerad's InteleViewer help documents how a user opens series but reports no view tracking, and Carestream's analytics dashboard material is study-level operational metrics. Study- and exam-level viewing is documented; series-level viewing is not, in anything that could be opened. That does not falsify P8, since absence over six searches is weak, but it moves the burden: the entry should now be read as presupposing an instrument whose existence is attested only by the DICOM audit schema's object-level granularity and by the scope restriction in the question itself.

Strongest remaining objection: Limb (i)'s headline fraction is a free parameter of the pre-declared provenance map, and the map choice is adversarial to limb (ii) — crediting a displayed thick reformat as coverage of an unopened thin-slice source stack writes off precisely the series where actionable yield would concentrate, so a map generous enough to be clinically defensible pushes the fraction toward zero and closes the question by convention, while a strict map inflates it with informationally redundant series; nothing in the record or either adversary log bounds that sensitivity in either direction, and all three pre-named actions are keyed to a threshold on a number that moves with the convention.
