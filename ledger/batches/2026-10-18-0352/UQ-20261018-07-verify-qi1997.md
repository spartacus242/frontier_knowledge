2. Sample: FOUND in abstract as "39 lithium reagents from several manufacturers" (fragment 1, verbatim per summariser). The word "bottles" does NOT appear in what I read; unit is "reagents"/"samples" (abstract also says "two of the 39 samples"). Kinds of reagent (compounds) NOT stated in the abstract; body text not read. Seven of 39 "artificially depleted in 6Li".
3. Range: FOUND in abstract: "δ7Li, reported relative to L-SVEC lithium carbonate, ranged from −11 to +3013‰" (fragment 4). So -11 to +3013 per mil is confirmed in the abstract, per-sample (per reagent) values. The four-digit figure exists as stated. Body-text table not checked.
4. Atomic weight: FOUND as a range of measured values, not a derived standard interval: "The atomic weight of lithium in analyzed reagents ranged from 6.939 to 6.996". Also: two of 39 samples exceeded 6.99, contradicting a 1993 IUPAC statement (commercial Li atomic weights between 6.94 and 6.99) ("This statement has been found to be incorrect."). Method of calculating atomic weights from the measurements: NOT READ (body).
