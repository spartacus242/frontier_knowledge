## Restatement (my own words)

When an LLM pretraining recipe says "clip the global gradient norm at 1.0", how often does the clip actually fire over the run, and does that same number impose the same constraint (a) at different model sizes along a scaling ladder and (b) under different training stacks that reduce the norm over different parameter groupings? "Same constraint" is to be judged by two endpoints at matched checkpoints: the fraction of steps on which the clip coefficient is < 1, and the deviation (relative L2, cosine) of the update actually applied from the update that would have been applied with clipping off. If not the same, what per-scale, per-grouping threshold would reproduce a reference run's two endpoints?
