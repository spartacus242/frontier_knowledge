Question (canonical): For a declared window (index case 2010–2025), construct the production-weighted δ7Li of the commercial lithium pool year by year from (a) feedstock-resolved δ7Li domains and (b) measured per-process Δ7Li for the route each feedstock takes to battery- or reagent-grade product, weighted by public annual production by deposit type and by the recycled share, with uncertainty propagated: (i) what is the year-on-year change Δδ7Li_pool and its interval, and (ii) does substituting the year-appropriate pool value for the anthropogenic endmember of a NAMED published apportionment (index case: effluent endmember 14.5 ± 4.3‰, 1σ, n = 6, Choi et al. 2019) move that study's inferred anthropogenic fraction outside its stated interval?
(V1 as intake, and the Gate 1 reformulation that produced the above: batches/2026-10-18-0352/UQ-20261018-07-record.md)

Alternative formulations:
- Is the anthropogenic lithium isotope signature used as a tracer a constant of the anthropogenic pool, or a moving target set by the supply chain that produced it?
- Do dated commercial lithium products, and datable archives downstream of a steady source, record a secular trend in δ7Li analogous to the Suess effect for carbon?

Motivating gap:
Presupposition inversion on the claim that δ7Li discriminates anthropogenic from natural lithium because the anthropogenic endmember is distinct and stable. IUPAC/CIAAW withdrew a single standard atomic weight for lithium because commercial lithium is isotopically fractionated by processing (interval 6.938–6.997; ciaaw.org/lithium.htm, doi 10.1515/pac-2017-1002); Qi et al. 1997 found 7 of 39 shelf reagents outside natural variation (to +3013‰); Desaulty et al. 2022 showed ordinary battery-chain lithium carries feedstock- and process-resolved δ7Li differences of 5–25‰ against ±0.4‰ (2σ) precision. Nobody has indexed that pool by time or carried it into an apportionment.

Presuppositions:
1. δ7Li of commercial products varies by feedstock/process above analytical precision — ESTABLISHED (Desaulty 2022; corroborated independently by Honra et al., Goldschmidt 2025/30726, Δ7Li(SC–Li2CO3) +2.4 to +4.5‰).
2. The product mix changed materially over the window — ESTABLISHED on tonnage; sourcing is economic, so volatile rather than trending.
3. Dated material exists to measure drift directly — CONTESTED; no archive found. V2 avoids needing it.
4. Published apportionments assume a fixed borrowed anthropogenic endmember — FALSIFIED for the index case (Choi et al. 2019 measured its own effluent endmember in the same July 2015 campaign) and unconfirmed anywhere else: two independent citation-direction sweeps found zero studies that borrow a literature anthropogenic δ7Li endmember.
5. Environmental Li derives predominantly from the commercial pool — PLAUSIBLE, UNVERIFIED.
6. Fractionation during use, treatment and transport is small or correctable — CONTESTED; nothing measured downstream of the consumer.
7. Public production statistics can be partitioned by deposit type and recycled share annually with usable uncertainty — PLAUSIBLE, UNVERIFIED; also ambiguous between mine-mouth and refinery-output accounting.
(Full list and gradings: record.)

What would count as an answer:
A production-weighted δ7Li series for the commercial pool with a propagated interval — from route-resolved δ7Li plus annual production by deposit type and recycled share, or measured on manufacture-dated products and a datable downstream archive — plus recomputation of at least one named published apportionment under the year-appropriate endmember, showing whether its anthropogenic fraction moves outside its stated interval. Three outcomes pre-declared: drift exceeds existing endmember uncertainty; null (drift smaller, tracer robust); or drift large on the production side but irrelevant downstream because studies calibrate locally, re-aiming the finding at provenance-forensics reference libraries.

Significance — consequence:
If drift exceeds existing endmember uncertainty, borrowed-endmember, cross-year and retrospective-archive δ7Li apportionments become year-sensitive, provenance reference libraries acquire a vintage, and the drift becomes a dating handle (a lithium Suess effect). If smaller, borrowed endmembers are licensed and an anomaly the IUPAC withdrawal makes look alarming closes with a number. Deflated by the audit result: the index case measured its own endmember, so a yes retracts nothing; consequence is confined to borrowed, cross-year, retrospective and forensic uses, a population not yet shown to be non-empty.
