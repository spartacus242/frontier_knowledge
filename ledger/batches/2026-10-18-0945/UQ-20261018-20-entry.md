Domain(s): Biology — cell biology (regulated cell death, tissue homeostasis; non-clinical). Method-owning neighbours: quantitative histology / tissue kinetics, quantitative image cytometry, whole-body turnover accounting.

Question (canonical): For one declared tissue in a declared species, strain, sex, age and housing condition, assayed in a declared physiological state with a pre-declared in situ marker panel whose per-modality sensitivity and specificity have been measured in that same tissue against induced positive controls: what is the per-modality prevalence of marker-positive dying cells per unit tissue (with intervals, an explicit multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual), and — where a modality's corpse-persistence time is independently measured or bounded in that tissue — the implied per-modality death incidence per unit tissue per day, reconciled against an independently measured total turnover? (V2, the graded version; V1 as intaken: what share of in vivo physiological cell death is apoptotic and what share proceeds by each non-apoptotic regulated modality.)

Alternative formulations:
- The field has defined a dozen ways a cell can die; which ones actually kill cells in a normal body, and in what proportions?
- For a declared tissue and a declared modality-marker panel, what is the per-modality incidence of cell death per unit tissue per day once each modality's corpse-clearance time is accounted for?
- (lay) Of the cells a body loses per day, how many die by each named death programme, and on what measurement does that attribution rest?

Motivating gap: The regulated-cell-death literature is dense with how-answers and thin on how-much for declared solid tissues. The authoritative whole-body accounting (Sender & Milo 2021, read: 0.33 +/- 0.02 x 10^12 cells/day, 80 +/- 20 g/day) assigns no death modality anywhere, splitting the total only by disposal route in Methods; the field's own in vivo methods introduction (Newbold et al. 2014, read and independently re-verified verbatim by adversary 3) attributes "billions of cells per day" to apoptosis while conceding that healthy-tissue counts reflect clearance efficiency rather than death rate. The partition is the denominator of every claim that a modality matters physiologically and of every modality-knockout phenotype read in healthy tissue.

Presuppositions: (1) modalities mutually exclusive per dying cell — CONTESTED, handled by construction (declared, possibly overlapping marker partition; multi-positive fraction and unclassified residual reported); (2) modality-specific markers specific enough to classify a single dying cell in situ — CONTESTED, the binding feasibility constraint; residual worry that calibration on induced death may not transfer to spontaneous death; (3) per-modality corpse-clearance times estimable, so prevalence converts to incidence — CONTESTED, incidence made conditional; (4) 'physiological' declarable by stated criteria — ESTABLISHED as a convention; (5) total tissue death incidence independently estimable — ESTABLISHED, wide intervals; (6) partition stable rather than diurnal/dietary/age-dependent — FLAGGED, changes the estimand's form not its answerability; (7) no modality systematically missed because corpses clear faster than markers persist — CONTESTED, boundable only; (8) ADDED AT GATE 4 (adversary 1), not in the Gate 0-3 record: that physiological loss presents as marker-positive dying cells at all — modalities that leave no corpse (removal of a living cell by phagocytosis) cannot be scored by any in situ panel and would not land in a residual defined over detected dying cells; the reconciliation deficit against total turnover is the only place such deaths appear, and the entry must say so.

What would count as an answer: per-modality marker-positive prevalence per unit tissue with intervals, a reported multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual, for a declared tissue and a pre-declared panel with in-tissue sensitivity/specificity; plus, where clearance/persistence times are measured or bounded, conversion to per-modality incidence per day reconciled against an independent turnover total. Prevalence alone, with the clearance confound bounded and stated, is a partial but publishable answer and must not be inflated into incidence. A null in which no panel reaches classifying specificity in situ is also an answer, about the field's measurement capability.

Significance — consequence: An apoptosis-majority result turns an asserted premise (P3) into a measurement with an interval and positions non-apoptotic modalities as reserve programmes whose healthy-tissue knockout phenotypes need another explanation. A substantial non-apoptotic share gives every such phenotype a direct reading it now lacks, moves the homeostatic/pathological line the field draws around necroptosis and ferroptosis, and gives on-target safety reasoning for RIPK1/MLKL/GPX4-directed drugs a baseline physiological rate. A null in which no panel reaches classifying specificity is consequential in itself: current claims about which modalities matter physiologically would then be ungroundable in counted deaths.
Significance — audience: the regulated-cell-death community and NCCD (prevalence endpoint alongside operational definitions); the efferocytosis/clearance community, for whom per-modality persistence times are the missing conversion factor; whole-body quantitative physiology (P1 fixes the total and assigns no modality); quantitative-pathology and spatial-omics method developers, who own panel calibration and point it at expression; cfDNA turnover accounting (adversary 2, eLife 89321), a second community working the same denominator without a modality index. Counter-indicator, sourced: the flagship therapeutic review on non-apoptotic death (Hadian & Stockwell, NRDD 2023) argues its whole case with no homeostatic share and no statement that one is missing, which is evidence the preclinical-safety audience does not currently treat the baseline as load-bearing.
Significance — fecundity: per-modality corpse-persistence times in vivo; whether the partition differs between tissues of similar turnover; diurnal, dietary and age dependence; whether non-apoptotic physiological deaths are immunologically silent; which clearance route takes which modality's corpses; compensatory substitution within the partition in knockout animals; a death-modality signature matrix for existing deconvolution machinery.
Significance — anchoring: the standing "is necroptosis/ferroptosis physiological or only pathological?" dispute; the modality-free accounting at P1; the old tissue-kinetics problem of what an apoptotic index measures (prevalence = incidence x persistence), generalised from one modality to a panel; whether markers calibrated on induced death transfer to spontaneous death.

Reason for silence: A disciplinary-boundary and incentive gap with a measurement obstruction on top, sharpened at Gate 4. The field's unit of credit is a pathway demonstrated by loss of function, so prevalence in a healthy animal is nobody's endpoint (adversary 1's BAX/BAK sweep: the knockout literature asks whether a modality is required, never what share it carries; P3 shows the in vivo method literature organised around intervention contrasts). Ownership is split: the group holding the total assigns no modality, the group holding modality assays produces expression maps. The sharper form, supplied by adversary 1 and accepted here: when the how-much question IS asked (P5), it is answered by the lifespan/turnover route that never looks at a corpse, precisely because efferocytosis clears corpses fast and the per-modality persistence times needed to divide prevalence by have not been assembled. That explains silence about the corpse-counting method, not about the topic — a weaker and more honest mechanism than the record claimed. Adversary 2 adds a pre-emption-by-definition limb (apoptosis was introduced in 1972 as the tissue-kinetic mechanism OF physiological deletion), which is plausible but unsourced here: the 1972 paper could not be opened.
