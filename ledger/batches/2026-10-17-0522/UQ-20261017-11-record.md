Question (canonical, Gate 0 as intaken):
In reference genome assemblies produced by the standard Earth BioGenome-era pipeline, what fraction of genuine recent gene duplicates is removed by the haplotypic-duplication purging step (purge_dups / purge_haplotigs plus manual curation), and by how much does that removal shift published estimates of gene-family expansion and contraction rates?

Question (canonical, Gate 1 reformulation — ONE reformulation used, for well-posedness only; both versions retained):
For assemblies whose reads and pipeline version are archived so the pipeline can be re-run with the purge module as the only varied step, and for an individual with an orthogonal per-locus copy-number instrument (trio-phased or duplex-validated haplotypes, or an independent high-quality assembly of the same individual): (a) within a pre-declared paralogue identity band (e.g. 99.0-99.8% CDS identity, declared before any data are pulled, with the band's instrument-resolvable floor stated), what is the probability that a gene copy judged genuine by that instrument is absent from the post-purge assembly but present pre-purge; and (b) when the purged sequence is restored to the annotation of a pre-specified set of published CAFE-style analyses, how far do the estimated gene-family gain/loss rates and the youngest bin of the paralogue identity/Ks distribution move, relative both to the published point estimate with its own stated interval AND to the error-model-corrected rate those analyses report or could report (CAFE 3/5 epsilon)?

Alternative formulations:
- When an assembly pipeline is tuned to remove false duplications, how many real young duplicate genes go with them, and does the resulting gene-content bias propagate into comparative-genomic rate estimates?
- Is the apparent scarcity of very young, near-identical gene duplicates in reference assemblies a biological fact or a residue of duplicate-purging?

Motivating gap (as intaken, verified against the record this agent opened):
Ko et al., "Widespread false gene gains caused by duplication errors in genome assemblies", Genome Biology 23:205 (27 Sep 2022, 10.1186/s13059-022-02764-1) quantifies the GAIN direction gene by gene (4-16% of sequence falsely duplicated pre-VGP, 4-24% of coding genes affected, falling to 0.3-2.3% in VGP assemblies; 1,175 -> 176 affected zebra-finch genes after the v1.7 pipeline change). Its companion, Kim et al., Genome Biology 23:204 (same day, 10.1186/s13059-022-02765-0), quantifies LOSSES (26-60% of genes in prior assemblies carry >=1 of eight false-loss types) but attributes them to GC-rich and repeat-rich sequence, names no purging tool, and reports the one case where haplotype duplication caused a loss (46 unannotated genes in a 2.7 Mb zebra-finch region) as a one-off. Both were read at link.springer.com on intake (first call each); neither attributes false gene loss to the purging step and neither propagates either error into gene-family size or rate estimates. The asymmetry is reinforced by what this agent opened: the purge_dups paper itself (Guan et al. 2020) validates only in the gain direction and criticises HaploMerger/Redundans for "over-purging repeats" without ever testing purge_dups for it; the hifiasm paper's discussion concedes purge_dups "might be not reliable on segmental duplications or repeats, leading to more duplications left or overpurged repetitive regions" with no numbers; and hifiasm's own FAQ warns that a larger --purge-max "makes primary assembly more contiguous but may collapse repeats or segmental duplications". The over-retention error is quantified gene by gene; the over-removal error is voiced in tool scope statements and left unmeasured.
NEW in Gate 2 and material to the gap claim: the complement of limb (a) IS routinely published in a weakened form — paftools asmgene "multi-copy genes retained / missed" (see priors 1 and 2), where 23-33% of GRCh38 near-identical multi-copy genes are not retained as multi-copy in human HiFi primary assemblies. So "nobody has measured the loss direction" is false as stated; what is unmeasured is the ATTRIBUTION of that loss to the purge step and its propagation into rates. The candidate's gap statement must be narrowed accordingly.

Presuppositions:
1. A ground truth for "genuine young duplicate" is obtainable independently of the purged assembly (trio-phased or duplex long reads, FISH/ddPCR copy number, or an independent high-quality second assembly of the same individual). [CONTESTED IN DEGREE, and this is now the load-bearing weakness: the identity band where purging and biology are confounded (>=99.9%) is the same band where trio phasing and duplex reads also collapse copies. The Gate 1 reformulation handles it only by declaring an instrument-resolvable floor, which concedes that the most interesting sub-band may be unanswerable.]
2. Purging decisions are recoverable: the purged sequence or pre-purge assembly is archived, or reproducible from archived reads at the recorded tool version. [PARTLY CONTESTED — most projects deposit only the final assembly; frame must be restricted to assemblies with archived reads and pinned versions. Supported in part: hifiasm/HiCanu runs in the papers opened record purge settings and manually adjusted purge_dups cutoffs, so re-running is feasible for at least the human and VGP-flagship cases.]
3. Published gene-family expansion/contraction rates (CAFE-style) are computed from these reference annotations, so a gene-content bias propagates into them. [ESTABLISHED, and now evidenced in magnitude: Han et al. 2013 show lambda falls to ~25-50% of the uncorrected value once a family-size error term is admitted.]
4. The bias is not already absorbed or cancelled by annotation-side filters or by rate-estimation-side error models. [CONTESTED, and WEAKENED by Gate 2: CAFE 3's epsilon error model is precisely an absorbing device and is standard in CAFE 3/5 practice. A directional young-duplicate loss is partly absorbable into a per-species epsilon, so limb (b) must be posed against error-modelled rates, not only against naive ones. The reformulation does this; the original formulation did not.]
5. Young duplicates occur at a non-trivial rate in the taxa in frame. [Established for vertebrates and plants; to be stated per clade.]
6. Purging-induced loss is not confined to true artefacts — the real-young-duplicate and haplotypic-duplicate populations overlap in the statistic the tool thresholds on (read depth near half the haploid mode plus high sequence identity). [CONTESTED; this is the mechanism the question presumes. Documentary support found, no measurement: hifiasm discussion ("overpurged repetitive regions"), hifiasm FAQ (--purge-max "may collapse repeats or segmental duplications"), and purge_dups' own -l0-style advice to disable purging for inbred/homozygous genomes, which concedes the discriminant is coverage-shape-dependent rather than artefact-specific.]

What would count as an answer:
Two numbers with uncertainties.
(a) A per-assembly over-removal rate: genuine duplicate gene copies removed by the purge step as a fraction of genuine duplicates in the pre-declared identity band, on a frame of assemblies with both a pre-purge counterfactual (archived or reproducible at the pinned version) and an orthogonal copy-number instrument for the same individual. The estimand is causal-with-respect-to-the-purge-step: everything else in the pipeline held fixed. Reporting it alongside the all-causes asmgene collapse rate for the same assemblies is required, so the purge-attributable share is separated from assembler collapse and from true individual-vs-reference CNV.
(b) A re-estimate of a pre-specified set of published gene-family expansion/contraction rates and paralogue-age distributions with purged sequence restored, reported as the change relative to (i) the published point estimate, (ii) that estimate's own stated interval, and (iii) the error-model-corrected estimate.
A null is usable: over-removal bounded at <= x% in the declared band, and rate change inside the published interval and inside the epsilon-corrected interval, would license the current pipeline default. A clade-structured result (loss scaling with heterozygosity) would be a third usable outcome and would make the bias a confound for between-taxon comparisons rather than a uniform offset.

Its comparator is GRCh38's own annotation on a DIFFERENT individual, so the 23-33% pools three causes the candidate's design is built to separate — assembler-stage collapse, purge-stage removal, and real copy-number difference between the sequenced individual and the reference — and it is not a measurement of any one of them. The candidate's comparator is the same assembly's own pre-purge state from the same reads at the pinned version, with an orthogonal per-locus instrument on the same individual, which makes the purge step the only varied factor and the estimand purge-attributable. What the priors DO establish is that the quantity is in the 10-35% range before attribution, which raises rather than lowers the stakes of the attribution question but also means the candidate cannot claim the loss direction is unmeasured.
vs PRIOR 3: Han et al. answer the generic form of limb (b) — gene-content error inflates lambda, by a factor of roughly 2-4 at epsilon 0.1-0.4 — and ship the correcting machinery now standard in CAFE 3/5. Significance — consequence:
If the over-removal rate in the declared band is material and purge-attributable, then the default EBP/VGP pipeline imposes a directional, age-structured gene-content bias — depleting exactly the youngest, most near-identical duplicates — on the assemblies that the whole comparative-genomics stack treats as ground truth. Three things change: the pipeline needs a restore-or-flag step (purged sequence deposited as a track, not discarded) rather than a tuning change; the youngest bin of published paralogue identity/Ks distributions, which is the evidence base for duplicate birth rates and the early fate of new genes, becomes a measurement artefact of known sign rather than a biological datum; and because purge aggressiveness tracks heterozygosity and coverage-peak shape (hence the -l0 advice for inbred genomes), the bias is plausibly clade- and project-structured, making it a confound for BETWEEN-taxon rate comparisons rather than a common offset that cancels. If the answer is null — bounded over-removal, rate movement inside both the published and the epsilon-corrected intervals — the current default is licensed on evidence rather than on the absence of an audit, and the scarcity of very young duplicates in reference assemblies can be read biologically. Either outcome also calibrates how much of the published 23-33% asmgene collapse is the purge step's doing, which nobody currently knows.

Significance — audience:
(1) EBP/VGP assembly and curation teams (Sanger Tree of Life pipeline, the Rhie/Formenti/Jarvis curation line) and the maintainers of purge_dups, purge_haplotigs and hifiasm's purge module: they own a default-on step and currently have no metric that penalises over-removal; they would act by archiving the pre-purge assembly or the purged sequence, by adding a purge-direction QC statistic alongside Merqury's false-duplication statistic, or by changing the default for high-heterozygosity genomes. (2) Gene-family evolution and CAFE users: they would act by stratifying or error-modelling by assembly provenance and purge status, and by treating the youngest identity bin as censored. (3) Annotation providers (Ensembl, NCBI RefSeq): a purged-duplicate track changes what is annotatable. (4) Segmental-duplication, CNV and pharmacogene communities (CYP2D6, amylase, immunoglobulin-adjacent arrays), where a collapsed young duplicate is a clinically consequential absence. The first audience is concretely real: two of the papers opened here voice the over-purging worry in their own discussions and decline to quantify it, which is an audience asking the question without posing it.

Reason for silence:
A metric-and-incentive asymmetry sitting on a disciplinary boundary, plus an archiving gap.
(i) Every QC instrument the assembly community owns penalises the GAIN direction and is structurally blind to the loss direction: BUSCO's duplicate rate, Merqury-style false-duplication k-mer statistics, and reference-annotation mapping all take single-copy expectation as the reference, so a removed genuine duplicate reads as an improvement in the very numbers used to certify an assembly. There is no statistic whose value worsens when a real young duplicate is purged — so the error has no owner inside assembly QC.
(ii) The people who would notice consume the assembly as given: gene-family and paralogue-age analysts attribute missing young duplicates to biology, to annotation, or to a generic error term (Han et al.'s epsilon), which absorbs the signal into a nuisance parameter and removes the incentive to trace it to a pipeline step. The error-model literature is thus not just a prior but part of the silencing mechanism.
(iii) The counterfactual is usually not archivable: pre-purge assemblies are generally not deposited, purge cutoffs were often "manually adjusted" (as the hifiasm Hi-C supplement records), and curation decisions are not machine-readable, so until read archives plus pinned tool versions became the norm nobody outside the producing consortium could have computed the quantity at all. That makes this a new-instrument/new-provenance question rather than an oversight.
