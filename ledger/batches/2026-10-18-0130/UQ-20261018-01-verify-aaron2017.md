## Findings
1. Exists, but title differs from the one given. Real title: "Reevaluation of Diagnosis in Adults With Physician-Diagnosed Asthma". First author Shawn D. Aaron. JAMA 2017;317(3):269-279, DOI 10.1001/jama.2016.19627. Given title "Reevaluation of Diagnosis of Asthma in Adults" is a paraphrase. (The PDF running header reads "Reevaluation of Asthma Diagnosis in Adults With Previously Diagnosed Asthma".)
2. FOUND. Current asthma ruled out in 203 of 613 (33.1%; 95% CI 29.4-36.8). Denominator 613 = participants who completed the study and could be conclusively evaluated (of 701 enrolled; 64 withdrew early, 24 could not have bronchial challenge). Not 701.
3. By the study team as part of the research protocol: spirometry, home peak flow, serial methacholine challenges, medication tapering over 4 visits, study pulmonologist assessment. Community workup was only reconstructed from physician records.
4. NOT FOUND for routine care. Follow-up was research-based (challenges at 6 and 12 months, 3-monthly calls). Medication-related: 22 of 203 had positive challenge at 6/12 months; 16 asymptomatic and stayed off medication, 6 resumed. 181/613 (29.5%; 25.9-33.1) still no asthma at 12 months. Summariser reports Discussion saying ">90%" of ruled-out stayed off medication for a year; 181/203 is ~89%, so that wording is unverified/inconsistent.
5. NOT FOUND as stated. Limitations mention 24% of community physicians did not return records (so appropriateness of initial workup unknown) and that spirometry was often done after treatment began. Nothing found on routine care behaviour when a disconfirming test is already on file. Read: full text via summariser, so absence is probable not certain.
