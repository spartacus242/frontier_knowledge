# UQ-20261015-10 — Adversary 1 log

## Restatement (my words, before reading the generator's defence)

Take the open knockout-validation datasets for off-the-shelf research antibodies (YCharOS: ~1,200 antibodies, ~120 human proteins). For each use separately (western blot, immunoprecipitation, immunofluorescence), ask: how much of "did this antibody work?" is about **which protein it was aimed at**, rather than about the antibody itself (recombinant vs. polyclonal, which company sold it, which animal it came from, which bit of the protein was injected) or about the assay setting (how much of the protein is in the cell line used, how well studied the protein is)? Express that as a between-target variance share (ICC) with a confidence interval. Then ask whether six pre-named protein properties — transmembrane segment count, main subcellular compartment, predicted-disorder fraction, closest-paralog identity, PTM-site density, length — actually account for that share.

In plainer terms: **is "some proteins are just hard to make antibodies against" a real, measurable thing once you hold the reagent constant, and if so, what about the protein makes it hard?**

## Single strongest killing point

The novelty claim has never been tested where it is most likely to fail, and still has not been. The record's own Gate 2 fell one query short of the ledger minimum and covered five vocabularies, none of them the four it itself identifies as the probable hiding places for a disguise — MS proteomics ("protein detectability" / HPP "missing proteins"), immunology (self-tolerance and conservation as target-intrinsic causes of antibody-raising failure, a framing with a standing answer that long predates the 2009 GPCR version), de novo binder design ("target difficulty"), and HPA enhanced validation by protein class. My verification fetch then removed the main defence for *why* nobody asked: the closest prior (Waury 2025) already held ~1,000 antibody-outcome pairs with 35.72% of proteins carrying two or more antibodies, and states that clustering on the page while declining to model it. So the data needed for a target variance component pre-existed YCharOS, which means "new data" cannot explain the silence and the silence may simply be apparent — the signature of a question asked under another name, in a vocabulary nobody has yet searched.
