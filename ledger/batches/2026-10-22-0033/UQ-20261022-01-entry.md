Question (canonical): Over a frame of published animal respirometry studies declared before any data pull that report both O2 consumption and CO2 production (or an RQ/RER computed from them) for the same individuals or same declared group, with the unit fixed in advance as one reported value-pair at the finest grain the paper prints and the feasibility window fixed in advance at RQ 0.70-1.00 with pre-declared arms at the clinical 0.67-1.30 and at taxon/state exemptions (anaerobiosis, net lipogenesis, discontinuous gas exchange with CO2 storage, aquatic carbonate buffering, non-steady state): (i) what is the infeasible share, with a CI, by taxon, respirometry mode and decade, against a dual-gas denominator that is itself uncounted; (ii) per respirometry mode, derived from that mode's own computation, which error classes an infeasible RQ can and cannot identify, and only then what bound if any it places on the reported mass-specific metabolic rate; and (iii) under pre-declared exclusion and correction rules, does the refitted interspecific allometric exponent move beyond its published confidence interval? [Operative form is V2 = R1, the single permitted Gate 1 reformulation; V1 and the two changes R1 makes are in the record.]
Alternative formulations: Do the published metabolic rates that comparative physiology's scaling datasets rest on fail their own internal arithmetic check, and by how much? · Treating simultaneously reported VO2 and VCO2 as a redundant pair, what is the internal-inconsistency rate of the comparative respirometry literature, and what error floor does it place on interspecific metabolic-rate comparison?

Motivating gap: RQ is bounded by substrate stoichiometry (~0.70 fat to 1.00 carbohydrate), so a printed pair outside the window is a failed redundancy check. Comparative databases and the 2/3-vs-3/4 exponent dispute pool exactly these primary measurements, and no audit found computes the corpus's infeasible-RQ share or propagates it into pooled values. Carried forward from Gate 1: the intake's mechanism was inverted. In flow-through mode both rates are linear in STP-corrected flow, so a multiplicative flow error cancels in the ratio to first order; the check is blind to the dominant shared term and sensitive to per-channel span/zero and water-vapour asymmetry. That derivation is the generator's own algebra, unsourced, and all three adversaries declined to source it in either direction.

Presuppositions: (1) a non-trivial number of papers print both channels for the same animals — CONTESTED, the denominator is part of the answer (PRIOR 1's 202-paper aquatic frame never mentions VCO2/RQ); (2) aerobic catabolism confines steady-state RQ to ~0.70-1.00 — established for the three substrate classes, contested in the arms' taxa/states, and the institutional window is wider (AARC 0.67-1.3); (3) an infeasible RQ in the pre-declared strata indicates measurement error — CONTESTED, and the single most damaged presupposition after Gate 4 (see adversary 1, S1); (4) an RQ deviation bounds the VO2 error — PARTLY FALSE AS STATED, conceded inverted for shared terms, limb (ii) rewritten to ask identifiability first; (5) mode/calibration/humidity classifiable from published method text — PARTLY CONTESTED, and the only presupposition with direct quantitative evidence, pointing against the candidate (PRIOR 1 reporting prevalences 71%/12%/48%/15%/19%, ">30%" silent on background respiration); (6) the interspecific fit is sensitive to a minority of bad points — CONTESTED; the bias path runs through mass-dependence of the error, predicted and untested.

What would count as an answer: a pre-registered audit with frame, window, unit and arms fixed before extraction, reporting (i) the infeasible share with a CI by taxon, mode and decade, with the dual-gas denominator and the unclassifiable share reported separately; (ii) a per-mode derivation of which error classes the check identifies, then any bound it places, carrying sources' own uncertainties; (iii) exponents refitted under pre-declared rules against the published interval. A near-zero share with exponents unmoved answers it negatively and is usable: an explicit measurement-error floor and a licence to pool, which nobody can currently issue.
