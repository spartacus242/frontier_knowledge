## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

Two joined questions about what is inside the tarball of an "open" model release.
(a) Counting question: of the model releases people actually download, how many ship the optimizer's internal bookkeeping — AdamW's running average of gradients (m) and of squared gradients (v), plus the step counter — in a form that lines up with the released weights, and at what step spacing / format / licence?
(b) Measuring question: hold a checkpoint fixed, run one attack pipeline twice — once given (weights, m, v), once given weights only, with everything else (attack family, compute, shadow models, query budget) identical — and report the signed change in attack success (AUC for membership / source-presence, extraction rate at fixed FPR for canaries), including how that change decays with how many steps k before the checkpoint the target document was seen. The sharp prior is that any effect lives in m (≈10-step memory at beta1=0.9) and dies in v (≈1000-step memory, squared-and-averaged over 1e9–1e10 tokens).
So: is the resumability artifact a disclosure channel on top of the weights, and does anyone ship it?

## Recommended verdict

**Provisional** — nothing Unresolved, so no Gate-4 rejection; but novelty is Medium with two close neighbours, one of which (NP2, "Gradient Inversion Attacks Beyond SGD") neither the Gate 2 record nor I could read, and the census presupposition P1 is unconfirmed at object level in every case checked. Promotion to Validated requires exactly two things: (i) read NP2's full text by a publisher/PDF route and confirm it contains no released-checkpoint stored-moment experiment; (ii) confirm at object level one downloadable, weight-aligned optimizer-state artifact for a major release (e.g. an HTTP HEAD on an OLMo `step*-unsharded/optim.pt` from an unblocked route), since the two instances actually inspectable here — Amber and `bloom-560m-optimizer-states` — are a declared negative and a repo whose own maintainer could not locate the states.

## Single strongest killing point

Every release anyone has actually inspected for this artifact comes back negative or unresolvable: LLM360/Amber states outright that optimizer states were not saved, the Hugging Face repo named `bigscience/bloom-560m-optimizer-states` is reported by its user to contain no `optim_states` file with the maintainer answering only "I think they are in the mp_rank file", and OLMo's `optim.pt` is construction-level inference plus a proxy-blocked URL. Limb (b)(i) therefore rests on a premise no opened source confirms, and if the census is near-zero the candidate collapses into the counterfactual re-posing its own record names as the failure condition.
