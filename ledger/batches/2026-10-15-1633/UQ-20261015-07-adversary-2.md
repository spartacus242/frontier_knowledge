# UQ-20261015-07: Gate 4 adversary #2 log

## Restatement (my own words)
Some protein crystals have been measured at both about 100 K and room temperature, keeping the lattice the same. In those pairs, a minority of residues settle into a different dominant conformation at the two temperatures. When AlphaFold-type networks predict those residues, do they land on the frozen state or the warm state, and how often? Is any tilt toward the frozen state larger than what a plain "pick the most common rotamer" rule would give? Does the tilt survive for proteins whose structures the networks could not have memorised? Underneath this sits a label-shift question: is the PDB's cryo bias baked into the learned predictor?
