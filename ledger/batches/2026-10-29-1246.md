## The five questions, in one line each

- **UQ-20261029-06** — Provisional. Training networks on synthetic data whose input covariance eigenvalues follow a power law, and estimating the weight-spectrum tail exponent by a Hill estimator over an enumerated grid, what is the dimension of the resulting exponent surface — does it collapse onto the data exponent alone, onto a temperature-like combination of learning rate and batch size alone, onto one product of the two, or does it need both irreducibly?
- **UQ-20261029-07** — For data from a process whose entropy rate is known exactly by construction, does the loss a transformer family can achieve converge to that entropy rate as parameters and tokens grow, or to a strictly positive floor — and is the excess a property of the architecture rather than of the data? *Rejected at Gate 4, late and separately: the question is asked, in four other vocabularies, and the one published fit of the candidate's own estimator on the candidate's own data family returns impossible values.*
- **UQ-20261029-08** — Provisional. For a managed-runtime program traced to exact object death times on one declared clock, does an object's death hazard depend only on its age, or do the identifiable curvatures of the death-time and allocation-epoch axes differ from zero?
- **UQ-20261029-09** — For shipped sub-quadratic language models whose recurrent state is a fixed-size tensor, how many bits about the processed prefix are recoverable from that state, and what fraction of its nominal capacity does the model realize? *Rejected at Gate 4: the fraction is pinned below 4e-4 by arithmetic on the model's own shipped declarations, so the question's discriminating range is empty before any measurement.*
- **UQ-20261029-10** — Is a JIT-compiled program's failure to reach a steady state caused by the profile-guided recompilation feedback loop — does an execution whose compilation decisions are frozen after a declared point converge while the same pair with the loop live does not? *Rejected at Gate 4, late and separately and unanimously: this is the anchor paper's own Hypothesis H3, its JIT disjunct separated and examined alone and declared unsettled by its authors.*

## What the verification pass and the late Gate 4 found

**The kill on UQ-07 came out of the entry's own unopened residue.** Its Novelty-confidence field named "four own-search returns left unopened, chiefly arXiv:2402.04161 and arXiv:2502.10178". Reader 2 opened both, and 2402.04161 — Makkuva et al., "Attention with Markov" — is the kill: it prints the global minimum as the chain's entropy rate, shows the test loss converging to it, and finds the plateau only under weight tying, removed by depth. That answers the candidate's convergence limb in the *opposite* direction from the paper the first pass rejected it on. Three further groups hold the question in their own vocabularies, and reader 1, searching independently, reached a paper that measures the candidate's exact finite-scale quantity over 2100 exact-entropy sources for an LSTM against a transformer. **The lesson is cheap and general: an entry's own named unopened Disguise residue is the first thing a late Gate 4 should open, not the last.**

**A published fit of the candidate's own estimator returning impossible values is a presupposition kill.** Pandey (arXiv:2405.16684) fits exactly UQ-07's estimator on exactly its PCFG family, over four orders of magnitude in parameters and three in tokens, and prints irreducible losses of -0.734, -0.262, -1.446, -1693.171 and 1.174. A cross-entropy floor cannot be negative. That moves the identifiability clause from CONTESTED, where the entry had it, to adversely instanced in print.

**And on UQ-10 the judge took a reading from its own verification pass and both cold readers reversed it.** The first pass rejected this candidate because Barrett et al. §5.2 *asks* its causal question and leaves it open. A checker then offered the contrary reading — that Hypothesis H3 is too broad a disjunction over JIT compilation and garbage collection to occupy a question about the recompilation loop alone — and labelled it in terms, "That equivalence step is the judge's to weigh". The judge weighed it, took it, and treated the ground as fallen, which is the only reason a late gate ran. Both cold readers then fetched the authors' own LaTeX and restored the first pass's reading with better evidence than it had: §1.1 **separates the JIT disjunct and examines it alone** ("separate out the time taken to perform Garbage Collection (GC) and JIT compilation"), Figure 7 is devoted to it, and §5.2 declares H3 unsettled "at least on HotSpot and PyPy" — a scope qualifier the first pass had dropped. They read it with grep and sed rather than a summariser, at full text and section level respectively. **A broader open hypothesis whose disjunct was separately examined does make a sharper interventional restatement "asked"** — and a verifier's interpretive step, handed back as the judge's to weigh, is not a finding. One of the judge's supporting claims did not survive either: §9.1.1's counter-threshold sentence is the paper's rationale for choosing 2000 in-process iterations, not a hypothesis about non-warmup, as two of the three readers of that file say against the third.

**Two findings that would have damaged UQ-10's design even had the kill failed.** In a speculative JIT a freeze cannot hold the executed code fixed — deoptimization continues and leaves the method interpreted, the regime an OpenJDK bug describes as a "deoptimization storm" — so the entry's reading rule, that no drop locates the cause outside the compiler, is wrong in that direction. And no current release of any VM in the declared matrix has been shown to install a recorded per-method plan and then stop deciding: the only confirmed instrument is a 2007 build outside the frame, so the reformulation's generating rule has no confirmed member.

**UQ-09's kill was re-derived from primary declarations by two independent readers and came back stronger.** The config conflict that could have doubled its denominator is resolved three ways and adversary 2's alternative branch excluded; the entry's own pre-declared falsification condition fired and closed against the repair rather than for it, on two confirmed predicates and one partial. No late Gate 4 is owed there: the ground was tested at first hand against its own sources and held.

**UQ-08's promotion got harder, not easier.** The promotion condition asking for one primary identification source is discharged — by a substitute primary, since both sources it named are stopped reads at one route each — and the discharging result turns out to be proved for *equal unit intervals*, which converts this question's undeclared cell width from a resolution matter into an identifiability matter. That is the capping objection the verdict already stood on, now confirmed at full text where the first pass had it only at abstract level.

The documents are primary and the agents followed them; these are recorded because a brief that misreads its own evidence will misread it again.

The consequential ones:

The first-pass entry says the opposite in terms — "P1 Section 5.2 **asks the candidate's causal question** and leaves it open, which is the rejection ground" — and it rested on Barrett and Antoch jointly. ## What this batch adds to the method

**The late-Gate-4 trigger is broader than this project's own memory of it, and this batch is why that matters.** LEDGER.md's clause fires when a Gate 2 ground is withdrawn "on verification, **or because a prior turns out not to do what the limb needed**". The second half is the half that fired here: UQ-07's prior was real, published and correctly cited, and it simply did not answer the question in the regime the limb needed; UQ-10's prior did not pose the question at all, only disclaim external validity. Neither is a retrieval failure or a misquotation, which are the failure modes the catalogue is full of.

**A late Gate 4 can confirm Rejected at a different gate, and that is not a null result.** Both of these entries stay Rejected and both now carry a different gate, a different ground, priors neither had cited, and — for UQ-10 — a routed row stated on a different paper's terms. An entry whose verdict did not move can still have had most of its face rewritten, and a batch report that leads with "no verdict moved" buries that.

**A broader open hypothesis whose disjunct was separately examined DOES make a sharper interventional question "asked" — and a verifier's interpretive step is not a finding.** This is the most transferable thing in the batch, and it is a correction to the judge rather than to an adversary. Barrett et al.'s H3 is a disjunction: non-warmup executions are largely due to JIT compilation *or* GC. A verification checker read that disjunction as too broad to occupy a question about the recompilation loop specifically, said in terms that the equivalence step was the judge's to weigh, and the judge took it — which is what sent a standing rejection into a late gate. The authors had in fact separated the JIT disjunct, examined it alone with a figure devoted to it, and declared themselves unable to settle it. **The test is not whether the prior's *sentence* is narrower than the candidate's; it is whether the prior's authors took the candidate's limb apart and worked on it.** Recorded in team memory as `ledger-asked-at-another-grain`, with two companion rules: taking an interpretive step a note hands back needs its own trip to the primary, and public discourse counts when the Mission says it does, at its real strength — one reader counted the mailing-list threads load-bearing and the other's verdict was "Neither is needed for the kill; both point the same way".

**An entry's own named unopened Disguise residue is the first thing a late Gate 4 should open.** UQ-07 named two titles it had not fetched. Both were opened by one cold reader; one became the kill and the other corroborated it. Nothing in the first pass's budget had gone to them, and nothing in the verification briefs pointed at them, because the briefs were built from the entry's *load-bearing* citations and these were by definition not load-bearing yet.

**A published fit of the candidate's own estimator, on the candidate's own data family, returning impossible values is a presupposition kill rather than a doubt.** Not "the extrapolation may be unstable" but "here are five fitted floors, four of them negative, for a quantity that cannot be negative". The general form: when a question's identifiability clause names an estimator, look for a published run of that estimator on that family before grading the clause CONTESTED.

**And a routed row's source need not be the one the rejection used.** Both rows going out from this batch are stated on papers the entries had not cited for that purpose: UQ-07's on the open direction its killer poses in its own words, UQ-10's on the anchor paper's own hypothesis with the authors' own verdict and scope qualifier attached. The row that the first pass would have written for UQ-07 — resting on a different paper's assumption-plus-caution — was withdrawn on verification, so the routing survived while its source changed underneath it.
