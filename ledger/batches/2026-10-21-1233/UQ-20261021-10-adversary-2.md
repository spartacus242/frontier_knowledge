## Restatement in my own words

Somebody should go to the gate of real battery-recycling plants in a named region over a named stretch of time, and, using a sampling method declared up front and reported with its own error budget in the way minerals-processing sampling theory demands, actually weigh out what fraction of the arriving mass is which cathode chemistry (with the elemental assay of the resulting black mass reported as a second, separate layer, plus non-battery junk; format only as a stratifier; state of health only on the packs that arrive whole, with the unmeasurable remainder's mass share stated). Separately, somebody should take a pre-declared list of published recycling-process and LCA papers, read off what feedstock composition each one *assumed*, report how many of them do not say, and then subtract: how far off were the assumptions, in which direction, and what happens to those papers' own recovery-efficiency and recycled-content numbers if you re-run their own models on the composition that actually arrives.

Three things are therefore being asked at once: a measurement with a variance budget (a), a coding exercise over a literature frame (b), and a subtraction between the two with a re-run (c). My attacks below target the subtraction hardest, because it is the leg the record itself says it would stand on.
