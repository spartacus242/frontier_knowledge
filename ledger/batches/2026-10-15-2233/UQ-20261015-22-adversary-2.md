## Restatement (my words, before attacking)

Strip the apparatus and the candidate asks two things about the "physiology" numbers baked into PBPK simulators (liver microsomal/cytosolic protein per gram, hepatocyte count per gram, organ volumes, organ blood-flow fractions, the tissue lipid/water/phospholipid/protein fractions that drive Rodgers–Rowland and Poulin partitioning):

1. **Who was actually measured?** For each number and for each uncertainty range attached to it, how many genuinely separate human studies (different donors, different assay, different lab) stand behind it, how many bodies in total, from what years, what ages/sexes/ancestries, and obtained how (autopsy vs surgical resection vs rejected transplant liver) — and crucially, which pairs of numbers come from *the same bodies*, so that treating them as independent inputs is a mistake.
2. **Does it matter to a regulatory call?** Take five published PBPK analyses that fed a regulatory decision and are re-runnable in an open platform; re-run the Monte Carlo twice — once with vendor defaults and independence, once with the uncertainty-on-the-mean implied by the real independent-source count plus the shared-donor correlations — and see whether the answer crosses a line fixed in advance (predicted AUC ratio leaving 80–125% / the ICH M12 no-effect boundary; paediatric predicted exposure leaving the adult matching band).

Note on the correction as defined: it deliberately touches only the *confidence in the mean* and the *correlation*, and leaves population (between-subject) variability at platform defaults. That choice is load-bearing and I attack it below.

Scope is restricted up front to openly documented platforms (PK-Sim / OSP and published parameter tables / EMA-FDA annexes). Four verdicts are pre-committed, including "untraceable".

## Single strongest killing point

The claimed reason for silence — a disciplinary boundary keeping pedigree analysis and correlated-input propagation outside PBPK — is false: participant double-counting is a named concern in medicine's own evidence-synthesis literature, citation-chain provenance audits of decision-bearing reference physiology have been executed inside clinical medicine (eGFR, spirometry), regulatory PBK guidance already templates parameter-source documentation (OECD GD 331), and P3 performed this candidate's own limb (a) inside PBPK for one parameter; so what is unasked is a scope increment under an unrewarded labour cost, which the pipeline does not accept as a mechanism.

Runner-up (would be the killing point if attack 5 were graded harder): the pre-registered correction rescales uncertainty-on-the-mean and adds correlation while leaving population variability at defaults, so it cannot move a point estimate, and the output statistic/quantile that must cross each boundary is never fixed — in the closest executed analogue (Value in Health 2025) imposing correlation left the ICER "comparable" and changed only decision certainty, which makes limb (b) structurally biased toward the "thin but robust" verdict for reasons of construction rather than of evidence.
