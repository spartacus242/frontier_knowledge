## Restatement (my words)

The Epoch Capabilities Index fits a one-dimensional latent-ability model to a sparse grid of (model, benchmark) scores and reads a capability-vs-time slope off it. The grid has holes: a benchmark exists only after it is created (hard for the models of its day), new models stop being scored on old benchmarks once those are saturated, and developers publish the scores they like. The candidate asks: do the holes depend on the very capability the index is measuring (after conditioning on things like release date, developer, benchmark age), and if they do, does modelling that dependence — with a selection model and a pattern-mixture model, plus worst-case bounds — move the fitted slope (the doubling time) up or down, and by more than the index's own error bar?
