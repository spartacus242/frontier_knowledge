Domain(s): Biology — experimental laboratory methods; metascience of protocol standardisation. Research-biology protocols only; clinical laboratory/diagnostic standards excluded.

Question (canonical): Over a frame of research-biology protocol parameters enumerated BEFORE any tracing by a pre-registered rule (frequency of exact numeric-plus-unit strings in a dated protocols.io + Methods-section corpus, with physically or instrumentally forced parameters flagged in advance), and traced under a written, dual-coded rubric to exactly one of four states — (i) primary optimisation found, conditions still current; (ii) primary optimisation found, conditions superseded; (iii) traced to a non-evidential source (vendor document, textbook, earlier protocol); (iv) chain unresolvable — (a) what are those four rates, with intervals, a coder-agreement statistic, and forced parameters reported separately; and (b) for a random subsample in state (ii), does re-running the original optimisation under current conditions move the optimum beyond a pre-declared margin?
(V1 as intaken, one permitted Gate 1 reformulation: "For the canonical default parameters of experimental biology protocols, what fraction can be traced to a primary determination that actually optimised a measurable endpoint, and among those, what fraction were optimised under conditions no longer in use?" V1 left denominator, coding rule and limb (b) implicit.)

Alternative formulations:
- A1: Where do biology's default numbers come from, and how many were ever optimised at all rather than inherited?
- A2: Treating each ubiquitous protocol constant as a claim, what is the citation-chain depth to its primary evidence, and what share of chains terminate in no evidence? — WEAKEST; P1 occupies most of A2's territory. If the question is reduced to A2 it must be re-graded Low.

Motivating gap: Silence map. Protocol repositories, vendor manuals and Methods sections specify thousands of exact parameters (37 C / 5% CO2, 10% FBS, 4% PFA for 10 min, 1:1000 primary antibody, 12/12 h light, 20-24 C vivarium) and for almost none is there a literature asking what the primary determination was or whether it still holds. Dense in "how", empty of "why". Class demonstrably non-empty: ambient-O2 gas phase (physioxia; Alva et al. 2022) and sub-thermoneutral vivarium temperature were each re-examined one at a time by individual curiosity, never prompted by a survey; the population of defaults has never been enumerated, so nobody can say which default to re-examine next.

Presuppositions: (1) a frame of canonical defaults is enumerable before tracing — DEFENSIBLE, and the rule is itself the first registrable deliverable (wet-lab-protocol NLP, WNUT-2020, makes it constructible). (2) tracing is feasible enough for rates to be estimable — CONTESTED; "chain unresolvable" is its own reported category, never pooled with "no evidence exists". (3) "optimised a measurable endpoint" is reliably codable — CONTESTED; needs rubric, dual coding, agreement statistic. (4) an untraceable or superseded default may be wrong — CONTESTED and EXPLICITLY DISCLAIMED; the audit measures evidential status only. (5) someone would act on a prioritised list — WEAKEST and undefended; PRO-MaP 2024 (opened, keyword-verified by adversary 2) asks for nothing about provenance. (6) parameters are conventional rather than instrument/chemistry-forced — CONTESTED PER PARAMETER; 5% CO2 is forced by the medium's bicarbonate, itself a default, so forcing relocates rather than dissolves conventionality.

What would count as an answer: Two limbs; not well-posed without the second. (A) N defaults enumerated by the pre-registered rule, each coded to one of the four states under the written protocol, dual-coded with an agreement statistic, reported as rates with intervals, forced parameters separate, state (iv) never pooled with "no evidence". (B) For a random subsample of state (ii), re-run the original optimisation under current conditions and report whether the optimum moved beyond the pre-declared margin. "Evidenced" = most trace to current-condition optimisations AND re-determinations reproduce the optima within margin; "not evidenced" = a ranked re-determination worklist. Limb (A) alone answers a bibliometric question, not this one.
