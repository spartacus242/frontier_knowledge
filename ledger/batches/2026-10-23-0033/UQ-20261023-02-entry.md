Question (canonical): For >= 3 nominally constant-condition (DD or LL) recording chambers in >= 2 facilities, instrumented with passive multi-channel logs (temperature, vibration, sound, CO2, humidity, door/access events) at <= 1 min resolution over >= 2 complete experiments: (a) what is the amplitude and phase of the 24-hour component of each channel, with CIs, and does the temperature channel's amplitude reach or exceed the documented thermal entrainment threshold for the preparation under test; and (b) in a prospective chamber-crossed design running one fixed genotype or preparation across those same instrumented chambers, with chamber assignment randomised or counterbalanced, how much of the observed between-chamber variance in free-running period (tau) is attributable to the chambers' measured 24-hour amplitude, estimated as a chamber-level variance component alongside an imposed-cue-versus-sham arm at the measured facility amplitude testing for period change, phase-angle locking and after-effects?
Reformulation note: this is V2, the one reformulation permitted at Gate 1. V1 asked whether the recording environment carries a 24-hour component large enough to weakly entrain or mask the oscillator and how much of the between-chamber and between-laboratory spread in reported tau it accounts for; it failed on an estimand defined over a covariate published tau reports do not print, and on a per-channel threshold clause with no truth condition for vibration, sound and CO2. V2 measures the environment directly instead of inferring it from published reports and confines the threshold comparison to temperature. Both versions are in the record.

Alternative formulations:
- Is "constant darkness" constant — what residual 24-hour cues do circadian chambers actually deliver, and do they move the measured period?
- How much of a reported free-running period is the building's day rather than the organism's clock?

Motivating gap: Source method, presupposition inversion. Tau measured in constant conditions is treated as an endogenous property of the genotype or preparation; the untested presupposition is that the nominally constant environment carries no 24-hour periodic component of entraining or masking amplitude. Small-amplitude temperature cycles are an established resetting cue for peripheral and cell-autonomous clocks, and methods guidance names residual light leakage and scheduled noise as hazards, but nothing opened across the record and three adversary sweeps logs the 24-hour-periodic content of a recording chamber at facility amplitudes and tests its effect on tau.

Presuppositions:
1. A 24-hour component exists in facility environments at all — MEASURAND; limb (a) reports it with CIs and a stated null. Exemption declared by the record and applied by all three adversaries; it cannot ground a presupposition-failure kill.
2. The amplitude needed for weak entrainment or masking is known or bracketable — established for imposed temperature cycles in several preparations, not established for vibration, sound or CO2. V2 confines the threshold claim to temperature.
3. Tau is published with enough precision to detect a shift of the expected size (the intake asserts 0.01-0.1 h) — FLAGGED, unsourced in this record and left Unverified by all three adversaries.
4. Tau for one fixed genotype does vary between chambers beyond stated precision — MEASURAND under V2 (the variance component may return zero); if tau is reproducible within measurement error, limb (b) is degenerate and limb (a) survives intact.
5. Chambers can be logged on all six channels at <= 1 min without the loggers themselves delivering a cue — feasibility part-sourced (sub-minute in-cage gas logging; room-level sound/vibration/light instrumentation), door/access-event logging unsourced.
6. Entrainment and masking are separable under the declared phase-angle, after-effect and release tests — FLAGGED, not established for the non-thermal channels.
7. Repair-introduced: the sham arm must impose a cue at whatever amplitude limb (a) measured, which presupposes control resolution finer than the measured residual, and cannot be specified until limb (a) returns. Unverified; the record did not write this clause down.
8. Period is the measurand; amplitude and phase effects are out of scope.

What would count as an answer: (i) per-channel 24-hour amplitude and phase with CIs for each of >= 3 chambers in >= 2 facilities over >= 2 complete experiments, with the temperature amplitude placed against the preparation's documented threshold; (ii) the imposed-cue-versus-sham arm's effect on tau, phase angle and after-effects, reported against the measurement precision of the tau estimator actually used; (iii) a chamber-level variance component for tau with its CI, with measured 24-hour amplitude as the candidate predictor. Negative answer: measured 24-hour components below the preparation's documented threshold and an imposed arm shifting tau by less than estimator precision — which licenses explicitly what the field now inherits tacitly.

Novelty confidence: Medium

Significance — consequence: If yes (a 24-hour component at or above the preparation's thermal threshold and a tau shift exceeding estimator precision), tau stops being a property of the genotype or preparation and becomes a chamber-conditional measurement; the immediate casualties are mutant-versus-wild-type, strain and aging tau differences whose arms sat in different chambers or facilities, and the remedy is cheap — log and publish each chamber's per-channel 24-hour amplitude, randomise chamber against genotype. If no, a hazard that methods guidance names without quantifying is retired with bounds and an inherited presupposition becomes a licensed one. Both branches change practice.
Significance — audience: Circadian laboratories publishing tau for mutants and comparing across facilities; recording-cabinet manufacturers, whose specifications cover set point and uniformity rather than 24-hour spectral content; laboratory-animal facility engineers and the behavioural physiologists who already publish in this measurement class (Barabas 2022 in JAALAS, Milligan 1993 in Physiol Behav) and would need only a time-of-day axis added to data they are positioned to collect; reporting-checklist bodies, including the plant-chamber community's environmental-parameter guidelines (named, not opened). Two adversaries graded significance inflation Resolved on exactly this evidence.
Significance — fecundity: Whether published strain and mutant tau differences measured in different facilities survive chamber adjustment; whether documented after-effects partly record the prior chamber rather than the prior light cycle; whether cell-autonomous period measurements in CO2 incubators carry the same contamination through a channel with no documented threshold at all; whether between-individual tau variance has a within-rack position component.
Significance — anchoring: The environmental-standardization and heterogenization debate on between-laboratory variance in mouse phenotypes, which owns the method and has not been pointed at tau (those papers returned and not opened, so this anchor is named rather than evidenced); the controlled-environment plant-science "chamber effect" literature, where limb (b)'s estimator is already a named quantity for plant traits; the historical exogenous-timing controversy (Brown), the same presupposition at a different amplitude scale and a different source; the established small-amplitude thermal resetting of peripheral and cell-autonomous clocks, which supplies limb (a)'s comparator.

Reason for silence: A disciplinary boundary with an instrument gap and an incentive asymmetry. The 24-hour spectral content of a cabinet is an influence quantity owned by facility engineering and metrology, where the measurand is a level against an exposure limit rather than an amplitude at a frequency; the field that owns tau treats the chamber as a calibrated utility. Aschoff 1965 shows the historical response to the access-event channel was to randomise it — a mitigation believed sufficient, which makes the question look answered. The incentive runs the wrong way: a positive result impeaches the asker's own published tau and funds no new biology. Minute-resolution multi-channel passive logging inside a sealed cabinet is recent.
