**Provisional detail.** UQ-20261016-12 asks how often the behaviour-determining non-weight artifacts of a popular open-weight model are changed after the weights stop changing, and whether those config-only commits alone, with weights byte-identical and the harness fixed, move benchmark scores by more than the margins papers routinely treat as evidence. Neither adversary left an attack Unresolved, so it is not Rejected. Two things cap it. First, novelty: arXiv:2609.08765, which Gate 2 missed, already publishes the fixed-weights formatting-and-stop-config effect in the same field, so only the *prevalence census* — how often this happens across repos — is plausibly un-asked.
