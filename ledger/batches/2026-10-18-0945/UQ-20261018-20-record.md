Source method: silence mapping

Question (canonical, V1 as intaken):
In a healthy adult mammalian tissue, how are the cell deaths that actually occur partitioned among the molecularly defined death modalities — what share of in vivo physiological cell death is apoptotic, and what share proceeds by each non-apoptotic regulated modality?

Question (canonical, V2 — the one reformulation permitted at Gate 1, for well-posedness only; V2 is the version graded):
For one declared tissue in a declared species, strain, sex, age and housing condition, assayed in a declared physiological state, and using a pre-declared in situ marker panel whose per-modality sensitivity and specificity have been measured in that same tissue against induced positive controls: what is the per-modality prevalence of marker-positive dying cells per unit tissue (with intervals, an explicit multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual), and — wherever a modality's corpse-persistence/clearance time is independently measured or bounded in that tissue — the implied per-modality death incidence per unit tissue per day, with the summed incidence reconciled against an independently measured total turnover for that tissue?
(V1 retained as the plain-language statement; V2 is V1 with the estimand, the partition's residual, and the prevalence-vs-incidence distinction made explicit. V2 changes nothing about what is being asked, only what would count as having answered it.)

Alternative formulations:
- The field has defined a dozen ways a cell can die; which ones actually kill cells in a normal body, and in what proportions?
- For a declared tissue and a declared panel of modality-specific in situ markers, what is the per-modality incidence of cell death per unit tissue per day once each modality's corpse-clearance time is accounted for?
- (lay) Of the roughly 330 billion cells a human body loses per day, how many die by each of the named death programmes, and on what measurement does that attribution rest?

Motivating gap:
The regulated-cell-death literature is dense with how-answers and silent on how-much. Modality definitions are maintained by a standing nomenclature committee (NCCD), and method papers exist specifically to tell the modalities apart — e.g. "Discriminating between apoptosis, necrosis, necroptosis and ferroptosis by microscopy and flow cytometry", Current Protocols, doi 10.1002/cpz1.951 (located this pass by title and DOI only; COULD NOT READ — no fetch budget was spent on it, so nothing is inferred from it in either direction). Yet the partition is the denominator of every claim that a modality is physiologically important and of every inference drawn from a modality-knockout phenotype in healthy tissue. Two findings this pass sharpen the gap and are read, not assumed:
(i) the authoritative whole-body turnover accounting (Sender & Milo, Nat Med 2021) fixes the total at ~0.33x10^12 cells/day and ~80 +/- 20 g/day but assigns no modality: apoptosis appears once, in Methods, as the clearance route for "about half" of the daily total, necroptosis/ferroptosis/pyroptosis not at all, and the authors state that a quantitative analysis of the death and removal processes is still missing;
(ii) the field's own in vivo methods introduction (Newbold et al., Cold Spring Harb Protoc 2014, pdb.top070417) states that the body loses "billions of cells per day" to apoptosis and that apoptotic cells in healthy tissue are "surprisingly low" because phagocytes clear them efficiently — i.e. the whole of physiological turnover is attributed to apoptosis in the same breath as the admission that the healthy-tissue count is governed by clearance rather than by death rate. That is the assumption the question asks to measure.
Two credible silence mechanisms, not oversight: the field's unit of work is the pathway interrogated by loss of function, usually in vitro, so prevalence is nobody's endpoint; and corpses are cleared fast, so any static count measures prevalence x persistence time, which means an incidence estimate needs per-modality clearance kinetics the field has not assembled.

Presuppositions:
1. The modalities are mutually exclusive at the level of one dying cell. CONTESTED and serious — pathways interconvert and share effectors, a single cell can engage more than one, and the committee's definitions are partly operational. V2 answers this by posing the question over a declared, possibly overlapping marker-defined partition with a reported multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual, rather than over a presumed exhaustive disjoint set.
2. Modality-specific markers detectable in fixed tissue exist and are specific enough to classify a single dying cell in situ for every modality in the panel. CONTESTED and the binding feasibility constraint. V2 makes the panel declared and its per-modality sensitivity/specificity measured in that tissue. RESIDUAL WORRY, flagged and not dissolved: the calibration uses induced positive controls, and an induced death may present differently from a spontaneous one, so the measured specificity is itself an extrapolation.
3. Each modality's corpse-clearance time is estimable, so a static marker count converts to an incidence. CONTESTED — clearance kinetics differ by modality and tissue and are measured in few systems. V2 makes incidence conditional on clearance times being measured or bounded, and prevalence the primary, always-identifiable estimand. Independent support for the structure of this worry, read this pass: the Frontiers in Cell Death 2025 review notes of a prolonged (>24 h) germ-cell death that "their persistence within the tissue makes them more readily detectable", i.e. the field recognises prevalence = rate x persistence when it happens to matter locally.
4. 'Physiological' is definable: a tissue state declared healthy by stated criteria, excluding injury, infection and disease models. ESTABLISHED as a declarable convention, not a natural kind.
5. Total death incidence in the tissue is independently estimable, so per-modality shares can be checked against a total. ESTABLISHED at whole-body and many tissue levels (Sender & Milo 2021, read), though their per-tissue intervals are wide.
6. The partition is a stable property of the tissue rather than of the hour. FLAGGED — diurnal, dietary and age variation would make the estimand a distribution over time rather than a number; that changes its form, not its answerability.
7. Dying cells are not systematically missed because a modality's corpses are cleared faster than the marker persists. CONTESTED — the same confound as (3) seen from the detection side; it sets which modalities the design can see at all, and it can only be bounded, not eliminated, by a static design.

What would count as an answer:
For a declared tissue in a declared species, strain, sex, age and housing condition, with a pre-declared marker panel whose per-modality sensitivity and specificity have been measured in that tissue against induced positive controls: the reported per-modality marker-positive prevalence per unit tissue with intervals, a reported multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual; and, where modality-specific clearance/persistence times are measured or bounded in that tissue, the conversion to per-modality incidence per day with shares reconciled against an independently measured total turnover. Prevalence alone, with the clearance confound stated and bounded, is a partial but publishable answer and must be reported as such rather than inflated into incidence. A result that apoptosis accounts for the large majority would turn a standing assumption into a measurement; a substantial non-apoptotic share would change how modality-knockout phenotypes in healthy tissue are read and which modalities the field treats as homeostatic rather than pathological. A null in which no panel achieves classifying specificity in situ is also an answer, and a reportable one about the field's measurement capability.

Significance — consequence:
If apoptosis accounts for the large majority: a load-bearing assumption that is currently asserted in method introductions (P3's "billions of cells per day" to apoptosis) and left blank in the authoritative turnover accounting (P1) becomes a measurement with an interval, and the non-apoptotic modalities are positioned as reserve or pathology-triggered programmes whose knockout phenotypes in healthy tissue must then be explained by something other than blocked routine killing. If a substantial non-apoptotic share exists: every modality-knockout phenotype in healthy tissue acquires a plausible direct reading it currently lacks, the homeostatic/pathological line the field draws around necroptosis and ferroptosis moves, and on-target safety reasoning for RIPK1/MLKL/GPX4-directed drugs gains a baseline physiological rate it does not now have. If no panel reaches classifying specificity in situ: that is itself consequential, because it means current claims about which modalities matter physiologically are not and cannot presently be grounded in counted deaths.
Significance — audience:
(a) The regulated-cell-death community and its nomenclature committee (NCCD; Cell Death & Differentiation / Cell Death Discovery), who would gain a prevalence endpoint alongside their operational definitions and whose "physiological relevance" claims currently rest on knockout phenotypes alone; P2's authors are already asking the adjacent mapping question and would act on a counting protocol. (b) The efferocytosis/clearance community (Ravichandran-type reviews, Immunol Rev 2023), for whom per-modality clearance kinetics are the missing conversion factor and a direct research product. (c) Whole-body quantitative physiology (P1's authors state the missing analysis in print). (d) Preclinical safety and target-validation groups for cell-death-pathway drugs, who need to know what the inhibited pathway does in a healthy animal per day. (e) Quantitative-pathology and spatial-omics method developers, who own the panel-calibration machinery (P2's atlas, multiplex IHC) and currently point it at expression rather than events.
Significance — fecundity:
Per-modality corpse-persistence times in vivo (needed for the conversion, measured almost nowhere); whether the partition differs between tissues of similar turnover; diurnal, dietary and age dependence of the partition; whether non-apoptotic physiological deaths are immunologically silent in vivo as the apoptosis-is-tolerogenic framework predicts; which clearance route takes which modality's corpses; whether modality-knockout animals show compensatory substitution within the partition; and a death-modality signature matrix for the deconvolution machinery query 9 showed exists and has never been aimed here.
Significance — anchoring:
The standing "is necroptosis/ferroptosis physiological or only pathological?" dispute; the explicit accounting hole P1 names (330 billion deaths per day with no modality attribution and the death/removal analysis "missing"); the old tissue-kinetics problem of what an apoptotic index measures (prevalence x persistence), which the candidate generalises from one modality to a panel; and the methodological question of whether modality markers calibrated on induced death transfer to spontaneous death.

Reason for silence (candidate mechanism, argued):
A disciplinary-boundary plus incentive-structure gap, with a measurement obstruction on top. The field's unit of credit is a pathway demonstrated by loss of function, usually in vitro or in a disease model, so prevalence in a healthy animal is nobody's endpoint and no journal section asks for it: P3 shows the in vivo method literature organising itself around intervention contrasts, where a healthy baseline is a reference level and never an estimand. The group that owns the total (P1, systems physiology) does not own modality assays; the group that owns modality assays (cell death biology) does not own turnover accounting, and its own in-vivo-mapping instinct, when it fires, produces an expression atlas (P2) rather than a death census, because expression is what fixed tissue gives up easily. The obstruction that keeps the boundary in place is efferocytosis: corpses vanish in minutes, so a static count measures prevalence x persistence, and the per-modality persistence times needed to divide it out have not been assembled — which makes the question look unanswerable rather than unasked, and so it is never written down. That is a mechanism, not "no one thought of it": the same field states the clearance confound plainly (P3: healthy-tissue counts reflect "how efficiently phagocytes clear them") and then proceeds to attribute the whole daily total to apoptosis anyway.
