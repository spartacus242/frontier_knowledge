## Restatement in my own words

Take a set of alloy or compound systems for which two or more different groups have each published a complete CALPHAD Gibbs-energy parameter set. Build one pooled table of digitised experimental points for each system, every point tagged with the paper it came from. For each published parameter set compute (a) its residual against the whole pool under one declared metric and (b) a "leave-one-study-out predictive residual" under the same metric. Rank the competing parameter sets both ways. Report the Kendall tau between the two rankings, the distribution of (b) minus (a), and how many systems change their winner. The entry's own repair (R1) added the recomputed apparent residual because published assessments print no fit statistic.

## Attack 4 — conditioning clauses, numbered, tested literally

C1. Systems with ≥2 independently published assessments exist. TRUE (sourced): F1, "The Cu-Mg system has previously been modeled by several times [27,28], most recently by Zhou et al. [29][30]." Full-parameter RETRIEVABILITY of those earlier Cu-Mg sets: UNVERIFIED (I opened none of refs 27-30).
C2. A pooled experimental corpus with per-datum source-study attribution can be digitised for each frame member. UNVERIFIED by me; the record checked one instance of the document kind (arXiv 2601.11899) and found inclusion marked but values not tabulated. Nothing I opened contradicts it.
C3. "Leave-one-study-out predictive residual" is a defined quantity for a PUBLISHED assessment, i.e. a FIXED parameter set. THIS IS THE LOAD-BEARING CLAUSE AND IT IS NOT TRUE AS WORDED. MY OWN REASONING ABOUT THE CLAIM'S LOGIC, NOT A SOURCE FINDING: with parameters fixed, removing study j from the evaluation corpus changes nothing about the model's prediction for study j, so the "held-out" residual on study j is identical to that study's share of the apparent residual; summed over folds, predictive residual equals apparent residual (up to fold weighting), the gap is identically zero and tau is identically 1 by construction, for every system, regardless of the data. That is not a finding the measurand exemption protects — P6 covers an EMPIRICAL agreement, not an algebraic identity. For the quantity to be non-trivial each assessment must be REFITTED with study j removed, which R1 nowhere says; P2 and P3 discuss only recoverability of inclusion and of fit statistics, never of the optimisation. A refit presupposes that the published optimisation is reproducible: free-parameter set, fixed parameters, per-dataset weights, objective function and software behaviour. Sourced part of this leg: in the two instances of the assessment document kind now opened across this entry — arXiv 2601.11899 (record, read in full: no weights, no fit statistic, PanOptimizer) and arXiv 1902.01269 (F1: default sigmas printed for thermochemical and activity data, NO sigma printed for phase-equilibria data) — the per-dataset weights needed to refit are not fully printed. Two instances is below the two-to-three the ledger's document-kind rule asks for an established claim, and one of the two is carried from the record, so the reproducibility leg is UNVERIFIED-leaning-CONTESTED, not FALSE. The alternative reading — treat the studies each assessor EXCLUDED as that assessment's natural out-of-sample set — is not "leave-one-study-out" at all, and it is confounded by the record's own evidence that exclusion is a judgement of unreliability ("we exclude these data [4] from further analysis in this study"), so a residual against excluded studies penalises an assessor for correctly rejecting bad data. Under either reading R1's central operation is undeclared, and under the refit reading the ranked objects stop being the published assessments and become the re-scorer's re-optimisations of their model FORMS, which changes the question. Grade for C3: CONTESTED, with the load-bearing step my own inference and the sourced step resting on two document instances. The search that would ground the inference leg — a paper stating in terms that CALPHAD optimisations cannot be rerun from the published paper because weights and free-parameter choices are not reported — was my Q2, and the one paper opened from it (F1) does not say that, so the leg stays unsourced.
C4. One common metric can be fixed across property types without the weighting deciding the ordering. Converted by R1 into a protocol item (weighting-sensitivity panel); treated as a MEASURAND, not a presupposition. No test needed.
C5. Ranking between competing descriptions is a decision the field makes. TRUE at the level of parameterisations within one group (F2 prints posterior weights ranking four Hf-Si models in Section 4; F1 selects parameters by AICc); UNVERIFIED at the level of curators choosing between independently published assessments (Thermo-Calc methodology page still unopened; Prince F4 shows evaluators resolving conflicting DATA by consistency checks and new experiments, with no residual criterion).
