## Restatement (my own words)

Take every pair of published kinetic measurements that are on the same enzyme (same organism, same sequence span, same mutational state) and the same substrate, under matched temperature / pH / buffer / co-substrate saturation, where the only difference is the construct's tag status — untagged vs tagged, or tag on the N- vs the C-terminus. Question (a): averaged over such pairs in a mixed model carrying tag-type, terminus and lab terms, does log10 kcat shift away from zero, does log10 KM, and does the shift point the same way across tag classes? Question (b): if you re-extract those labels from the source papers and refit one existing turnover-number predictor (DLKcat / TurNuP / CatPred / RealKcat) with versus without tag/terminus strata, does held-out error or the top-k ranking move more than resampling noise would move it? In short: is the affinity tag a *bias* in the kinetics corpus or just *noise*, and do tag-blind ML predictors care?
