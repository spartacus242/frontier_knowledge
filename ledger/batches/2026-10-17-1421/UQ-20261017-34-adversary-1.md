## Restatement (my words)

Take a cohort of isogenic, co-housed animals. Count the cells in every part of one organ in every animal. Across animals, are the parts' counts negatively correlated so that the whole varies less than it would if the parts were independent (a budgeted total), uncorrelated (additive, independent parts), or positively correlated (a shared growth signal) — after subtracting technical counting error, at a declared partition granularity, replicated in a second line?
