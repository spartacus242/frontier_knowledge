## Gate 0 — Intake

Question (canonical, V1 as submitted):
In nanopore adaptive ("Read Until") sequencing, where the instrument ejects reads mid-translocation on the basis of a reference-mapping decision taken on each read's first few hundred to few thousand bases, what bias does that data-dependent selection induce in estimands computed from the retained reads — allele balance and heterozygote recovery at target loci, structural-variant and large-insertion recall, repeat-length distributions, and per-site methylation fraction — and is the bias correctable from the instrument's own rejection log?

Question (canonical, V2 — THE ONE REFORMULATION PERMITTED AT GATE 1; this is the version graded):
In nanopore adaptive ("Read Until") sequencing, where the instrument ejects reads mid-translocation on a REFERENCE-DEPENDENT decision taken on each read's first few hundred to few thousand bases, what signed bias does that data-dependent selection induce in estimands computed from the retained reads — reference-allele fraction and heterozygote recovery at target loci, insertion/SV recall by size class, repeat-length distribution, per-site methylation fraction — and is the bias correctable from the run's OWN RETAINED OUTPUT, i.e. the ejected reads' own first-chunk prefixes joined by read_id to the logged sequence/unblock action, with the decision rule replayed over them to reconstruct a selection propensity?

Why V1 was reformulated (recorded per Gate 1): V1's correction limb rests on "the instrument's own rejection log". I opened three actual instances of that document kind (below) and the premise is FALSE as stated: the shipped logs record WHICH read was ejected and nothing about WHY. V2 relocates the propensity information to where the opened instances show it actually sits — the ejected read's own retained prefix sequence plus a replay of the decision rule — and keeps the log only as the action indicator. V1 is retained above because its failure is itself a finding.

WHAT THE REPAIR ITSELF NOW PRESUPPOSES (tested as hard as the clause it replaced):
- R1. The ejected reads' truncated prefixes are written to the run output and joinable to the action record by read_id. SUPPORTED on two opened sources: the MinKNOW sequencing_summary spec states "The sequencing summary is a TSV file, containing one row per read" and its `end_reason` regex admits `data_service_unblock_mux_change`; and Munro et al. (Genome Research, read in full) state in Methods that "reads were divided into those actively sequenced and those unblocked using the unblocked read IDs file" and go on to estimate CNV from the rejected reads — so ejected prefixes are present, retrievable and already analysed in practice.
- R2. The decision rule and the exact reference index used during the run are pinnable for replay. NOT VERIFIED — no source opened. FLAGGED; this is now the weakest limb of V2.
- R3. The join is clean. CONTRADICTED IN PART by the AS decisions spec's own caveat: "There may be duplicated read IDs in this file due to the asynchronous implementation of the script." FLAGGED; any correction limb must state how duplicates are handled.

Alternative formulations:
1. Does a sequencer that chooses which molecules to finish reading hand you a biased sample of the genome, and by how much?
2. Adaptive sampling as informative censoring: what is the estimand bias, and can a replay of the decision rule over the ejected prefixes serve as a propensity record that undoes it?

Motivating gap:
Adaptive sampling is a genuinely new instrumental mechanism — the instrument makes a data-dependent decision about which molecules to finish — and the three tool papers I read in full evaluate it by on-target yield, coverage, read-classification accuracy and expected information gain. The selection rule is reference-dependent, so the molecules most likely to fail the first-chunk decision are those carrying divergent haplotypes, large insertions or breakpoint-spanning structure: the classic shape of informative selection, where the retained sample is biased for the very features the experiment is run to find. BOSS-RUNS names the mechanism in its Discussion and declines to quantify it; nobody I opened measures the induced estimand bias.

## Gate 1 — Presuppositions and answer standard

Presuppositions (each established on something opened, or flagged):
- P1. The rejection decision is REFERENCE-dependent at the shipped operating point. ESTABLISHED across three implementations read in full, but the candidate's own word "mapping" is TOO NARROW and is corrected here: readfish/BOSS-RUNS use minimap2 mapping ("the reference genome is used to determine the origin and orientation of nascent sequencing reads", BOSS-RUNS Discussion; unmapped reads rejected by configuration, "reject reads from this sector if they did not map", Methods), whereas ReadBouncer is reference-dependent WITHOUT mapping: "Readbouncer uses IBFs to classify basecalled DNA fragments for ejection" and "We neither use signal nor sequence space mapping algorithms for read classification". So the premise holds as REFERENCE-dependence; it does not hold as mapping-dependence. The intake's flag for basecall-free SIGNAL-matching implementations is NOT discharged — ReadBouncer is basecall-DEPENDENT and signal-matching tools (e.g. RUBRIC-style, SquiggleNet-style) were not opened. FLAG RETAINED for signal-space implementations only.
- P2. Rejection decisions are logged per read with fields sufficient to reconstruct a selection propensity. **FALSE AS STATED — this is the document-kind check, and it fired.** Three instances opened (see Gate 2 document-kind section). All three carry the ACTION and not the DECISION COVARIATES. V1's correction limb dies here; V2 replaces it.
- P3. An ejected molecule's remaining sequence is never read. ESTABLISHED by mechanism (voltage reversal expels the molecule); not independently contested by any source opened.
- P4. A matched control is constructible. FLAGGED, unchanged. A same-library/same-chemistry control run doubles flowcell cost; an in-silico replay over a non-adaptive run reproduces the DECISION but not the pore-occupancy consequences (freed pore time is the mechanism by which adaptive sampling redistributes coverage, and BOSS-RUNS' own benchmark gain comes from exactly that). So replay is a decision control, not an outcome control, and the answer standard must use a physical control for the outcome limb.
- P5. The four named estimands are defined on the retained read set the way downstream analyses assume. FLAGGED; each needs its denominator declared in advance (reference-allele fraction: reads overlapping the site after the analysis's own filters; insertion/SV recall: truth-set events by size class; repeat length: per-allele spanning reads only; methylation fraction: called CpGs per site at a declared coverage floor).
- P6. Any induced bias is large enough to matter at the depths adaptive sampling is actually used for. This is the OBJECT OF MEASUREMENT, not an assumption. Declared as such (measurand exemption DECLARED, not merely considered: reporting that a bias magnitude is unknown is the thing the question asks about, and must not be scored as a false premise).
- P7. Divergence from the decision reference is measurable per locus, so bias can be stratified on it. FLAGGED; requires an independent truth set declared in advance (the answer standard supplies one).

What would count as an answer:
For >=2 samples with independent high-confidence truth sets (a deep non-adaptive run of the same library plus one orthogonal assay), the adaptive run and its matched physical control, reporting PER ESTIMAND a signed bias with CI — reference-allele fraction at heterozygous target sites, insertion and SV recall by size class, repeat-length distribution shift, per-site methylation difference — each stratified by divergence from the decision reference and by first-chunk mappability; plus a test of whether inverse-propensity weighting built from a pinned replay of the decision rule over the ejected prefixes removes the bias; plus an explicit statement of which estimands remain uncorrectable because the ejected molecules carry information no prefix and no log can recover (the obvious candidate: an insertion whose breakpoint lies beyond the first chunk).
A negative answer is equally admissible and equally publishable: all biases fall within depth-matched sampling noise, so no correction is needed.

Is it a question, not a thesis? Yes — the negative answer is pre-specified and is a real outcome, and the direction of the one adjacent number that exists in the literature (BOSS-RUNS sensitivity 0.79 adaptive vs 0.68 control) runs AGAINST the generator's intuition.

## Gate 3 — Significance

Consequence (argued): If the bias is material, then every non-clinical adaptive-sampling result computed on the retained reads is suspect in a direction nobody currently reports — conservation-genomics heterozygosity and inbreeding estimates, plant SV panels, environmental-microbial strain resolution — and the remedy is not a software patch but either a matched control run (doubling flowcell cost) or a propensity reconstruction that the shipped three-column log cannot supply. If the bias is immaterial at the depths in use, that is equally consequential and cheaper to act on: it retires a standing methodological worry, licenses adaptive sampling for exactly the estimands people are nervous about, and saves the control runs that cautious groups currently pay for. Both branches change practice, which is the test.

Audience (specific, and would act): (a) non-clinical targeted long-read users — conservation and population genomicists running panels on non-model species, where the decision reference is BY CONSTRUCTION divergent from the sample and so the mechanism is at its strongest; (b) the readfish and BOSS-RUNS developers, who would gain a bias-aware objective rather than an information-gain objective computed under a reference they already know is wrong; (c) the MinKNOW output-specification owners — and this is the most concrete action in the list, because the AS Decisions CSV is at **format version 0.1 with three columns**, so a demonstrated bias plus a demonstrated correction identifies the exact missing fields (decision target, matched reference position, bases-before-decision, chunk index) and the fix is a spec change, not research.

Fecundity: it makes askable (i) bias-aware or reference-free decision rules, and decisions taken against a pangenome graph rather than a linear reference; (ii) which estimands are IRRECOVERABLE in principle, e.g. an insertion whose breakpoint lies past the first chunk, which is a genuine information-theoretic question about where the ejection boundary sits relative to the feature; (iii) the same analysis for every other instrument that makes a data-dependent decision to stop acquiring — data-dependent acquisition in mass spectrometry is structurally the identical problem, selection on a first-pass scan, and is evaluated with the identical throughput-only metrics; (iv) whether the rejected-read background that Munro et al. already use for CNV can be promoted into a formal control arm.

Anchoring: reference bias in genomics (the pangenome literature's founding complaint, here re-sited from the ALIGNMENT step to the ACQUISITION step, which is new because acquisition is irreversible and alignment is not); informative sampling and MNAR in survey statistics; estimation after adaptive data collection in the adaptive-experiment literature my query 10 surfaced; and DDA selection bias in proteomics.

**Significance: 3/3.** Argued above on all four heads, with one named action (the v0.1 spec) that is concrete and currently blocked only by the absence of the measurement.
**Well-posedness: 3/3.** The answer standard is fully operational and pre-specifies the negative; every presupposition is now either established on something opened (P1 as reference-dependence, P3, R1) or explicitly flagged (P4, P5, P7, R2, R3, and the retained signal-space flag on P1); the one presupposition that FAILED (P2) was found to fail by opening three instances of the document kind and was repaired by the single reformulation Gate 1 allows, with the repair's own three presuppositions written out and tested — R1 supported on two sources, R2 flagged as unverified, R3 partly contradicted by the spec's own duplicate-read-ID caveat.

Reason for silence (candidate mechanism — new instrument plus a disciplinary boundary, with an incentive gap on top):
The mechanism is three years old and its evaluation vocabulary was fixed by the engineers who built it: on-target fraction, enrichment factor, yield, coverage uniformity, classification precision. All three papers I read in full score the method on the decision's correctness or on throughput, never on what the decision does to a downstream estimate — ReadBouncer stops at the read, Munro et al. stop at coverage and CNV, BOSS-RUNS stops at information gain. The machinery that would frame it as informative selection lives in survey statistics and missing-data theory, which has no contact surface with ONT tool development; my query 10 confirms that field has the correction apparatus and has never pointed it at a sequencer. Two further reinforcements: the measurement needs a matched deep non-adaptive control of the SAME library, which doubles flowcell cost for a result that, if positive, reduces the apparent value of a feature both the vendor and the tool authors are actively promoting; and BOSS-RUNS' Discussion supplies the field a ready-made reason not to look — the hedged, uncited, unquantified expectation that "barring major structural rearrangements, moderate amounts of divergence will not have a substantial impact". A plausible-sounding expectation in a flagship paper is an efficient silencer.
