Question (canonical): For one production serving engine and one open target/draft pair at fixed weights, precision, temperature and engine version, with the engine-level speculative-decoding switch as the treatment and matching defined at the request level (the target pass's tensor shape conceded as part of the treatment): what is the distributional distance D(spec-on, spec-off) on a fixed prompt set against a permutation null, what is the nuisance baseline D(spec-off, spec-off') under varied request composition, what is the excess (i)−(ii) with a CI, and does that excess exceed a pre-declared yardstick (one quantization step on the same instrument; the smallest benchmark difference the field publishes as a result)?
Alternative formulations:
1. Does turning speculative decoding on change what a served model actually samples, over and above the change caused by request/batch composition alone?
2. What is the measured size of the gap between "algorithmically lossless" and "lossless as served" for speculative decoding, as a two-sample distributional distance with a permutation null?

A null with a tight interval answers it as decisively as a positive. If large, one-at-a-time ablation names the responsible implementation choice.

Verification (post-batch, fresh-context checkers reading the cited priors at first hand, then a late Gate 4 of two literature surveys): five checks and two surveys ran. The Gate 2 and Gate 4 novelty ground FALLS, and a stronger objection replaces it, so the entry stays Rejected on new grounds.
- P1 (arXiv:2607.17283, Chordiya) — numbers CONFIRMED, status as near-duplicate REFUTED. Its figures are right: a two-sample chi-squared of 162.5 on 200 degrees of freedom, p = 0.976, over 201 frequency bins binned so every expected count is at least 5, on 9,209 speculative against 9,214 baseline tokens from 100 prompts of 100 tokens at temperature 1.0. But FIVE features that would make it this candidate's study are absent: no deviation magnitude with a confidence interval or standard error; no power analysis or minimum detectable effect; no permutation or bootstrap null; NO baseline-versus-baseline arm, so it never compares two non-speculative runs with each other; and no calibration against effect sizes the field treats as findings. Its own limitations say "All results are single-stream, batch-size-one, on one consumer machine;" and "The sampled-leg equivalence test has the statistical resolution of about 9,200 tokens per method". Its engine is a from-scratch implementation on an Apple-silicon laptop, and the sentence concluding identity is a THEORETICAL claim about the marginal process. It also discounts its own KL figure: "the reported smoothed symmetric KL (0.471) is dominated by add-0.5 smoothing". So it is a non-rejection at a resolution it does not state, not an answer.
- P2 (arXiv:2510.22876, "Correctness Forensics for Batch Speculative Decoding: Diagnosing the Ragged Tensor Problem") — CONFIRMED, and it carries a datum that reframes the whole question. Table 1 gives same-engine spec-on against spec-off exact-match rates: vLLM+Spec 96.9 (Vicuna), 65.6 (Qwen3), 72.7 (GLM4); SGLang+EAGLE 69.8 and 47.7; SGLang+EAGLE+Deterministic 85.0 and 50.6. It ALSO carries a non-speculative batched control, Non-Spec-Batch at batch 4: 53.8 on Vicuna, 92.9 on Qwen3, 93.3 on GLM4. On Vicuna the NON-speculative batch-composition control (53.8) therefore diverges far more than the speculative contrast (96.9) — the nuisance is larger than the treatment. Every figure is a single-run point estimate with no test, interval or repeated-run variance, while the paper's own account of residual divergence is floating-point non-determinism, so no noise floor is established; and the only engine-against-itself comparison under varied batch size is a THROUGHPUT figure, not an agreement one.
- vLLM RFC 54506 — CONFIRMED verbatim and open. Title: "[RFC]: Batch invariance for speculative decoding needs to cover the forward pass (M=1 vs M=k+1)". It states "nothing in the current stack guarantees those two produce identical bits" and "At temperature 0, enabling speculative decoding can change the generated sequence relative to plain decoding.", and asks that "the target model's verify pass over rows `[t..t+k]` must reproduce, bit for bit, what plain `M=1` decode would produce". No maintainer has ruled; the only two responders are non-maintainers, so it evidences a believed gap under discussion, not a conceded one. Its own control experiment is the most useful thing in it: two launches of the NON-speculative engine differed by up to 5.25 nats in logits while producing identical 64-token greedy output. - The two purpose-built instruments both exclude this axis, CONFIRMED. arXiv:2410.20247 (Model Equality Testing) uses a Hamming string kernel with a median 77.4% power at about 10 samples per prompt over 25 prompts, and evaluates quantization (nf4, int8, fp16 against fp32), watermarking, finetuning, substitution of 13 other models and 31 real API endpoints; speculative decoding, batch size, concurrency, engine version and kernel choice are absent from 98% of its text as read. arXiv:2511.20621 (DiFR) states "We have not evaluated our approach on other sampling procedures such as speculative decoding" and "We do not evaluate speculative decoding scenarios," citing lack of vLLM 0.10.0 support, with Appendix F a sketch carrying no experiments.

- Late Gate 4, survey B (presupposition and significance) — UNRESOLVED ON ATTACK 4, and this is now the rejection. The candidate's estimand is the excess of speculation-on deviation over a composition-matched speculation-off baseline. That baseline cannot be held fixed: enabling speculation submits k+1 query rows per request where decoding submits one, which changes the kernel path and reduction order even for a request alone in its batch, and the accepted-token count varies per request, so trajectories, context lengths and co-residency drift apart across the two arms in a data-dependent way. Composition is therefore a POST-TREATMENT variable on the path speculation to shape to output, and matching on it is matching on a mediator. In exact arithmetic the algorithmic effect is zero by the sampling proof, so every observed deviation is a numerics-through-shape effect and the direct algorithmic component is not identified in a production engine at all. The same survey establishes two further things. The literature does distinguish algorithmic from served losslessness, so the candidate's framing is fair: P1 separates bit-exact greedy comparison as "a backend property" and ran its greedy leg on an fp32 CPU backend because bit-exactness on its quantized backend was unattainable. And the significance branch weakens: where the nuisance exceeds the treatment, the result is not a null about speculation but a statement about the instrument's resolution, because a null needs power and an equivalence margin and the candidate declares neither.

Strongest remaining objection: the estimand is not identified — attack 4, Unresolved at the late Gate 4, and this is the rejection. "Excess deviation net of a composition-matched non-speculative baseline" requires batch composition to be holdable fixed across the two arms, and it is not: speculation submits k+1 query rows per request where decoding submits one, changing the kernel path and reduction order even for a request alone in its batch, and the per-request accepted-token count varies, so trajectories, context lengths and co-residency diverge in a data-dependent way. Composition is a post-treatment mediator on the path from speculation to output, so matching on it is matching on a mediator and the residual is not an effect of speculation. The 2510.22876 figures make the same point from the data: its non-speculative batch control agrees with itself on 53.8% of Vicuna outputs while its speculative contrast agrees on 96.9%, so the nuisance is larger than the treatment and no amount of subtraction recovers a clean quantity.
