# UQ-20261017-06 — Adversary 1 log (Gate 4)

## Restatement (my own words)
Take one sparse-MoE pretraining recipe and run it several times changing only the seed (init + data order). Line up the experts across runs as well as a pre-declared rule allows, then ask: at the point where OLMoE says routing has already frozen (~1% of training), do the runs send the same tokens to "the same" experts more than a permutation null and an unrelated-run null would produce — i.e. is the early-frozen routing a property of the recipe or a per-run accident? And does the early routing state carry information about how good the run ends up (share of between-seed terminal-quality variance explained)? Everything is conditioned on first reproducing OLMoE's saturation curve at the chosen scale and on an aux-loss-weight sweep.
