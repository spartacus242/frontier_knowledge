## (2) The entropy rate — PRINTED in closed form; same object as the candidate's H

- §2.1 Example 2.2 (html v2): the switching process of Figure 1 on {0,1} with p = q = 1 − δ; printed: "lim (m→∞) (1/m) H(P) = H_Ber(δ)" with "H_Ber(δ) = δ log(1/δ) + (1−δ) log(1/(1−δ))" (exact fragment), plus "so H(π)=H_Ber(1/2)=log(2)" for the uniform stationary distribution, and the statement that the ratio m·H(π)/H(P) grows without bound as δ → 0 (summarised, not verbatim). The phrase "entropy rate" does NOT occur on the served v2 page (two passes, Q6 and S5(iv), both NOT FOUND over the 62,826-character page); the paper writes the limit without naming it. The camera-ready DOES name it: Appendix A.1, "for which we can define the “entropy rate”", and its Appendix A.2 Example A.1 prints "lim_{m→∞} (1/m) H(P) = H_Ber(δ) = δ log(1/δ) + (1 − δ) log(1/(1 − δ))" (exact fragment, spacing normalised by the summariser).
- §4.3 (html v2, "A sequential variant of BPE"): "which uses the fact that H(P)=mδlog(1/δ)+m(1−δ)log(1/(1−δ))" (exact fragment; LaTeX simplified by the summariser). The camera-ready's counterpart sits in §3 at eq. (2) and is labelled an approximation: "the approximation in (i) uses the fact that as m grows large, 1" [cut]. So the finite-m formula H(P) = m·H_Ber(δ) is the paper's large-m approximation (my own check: for the order-2 switching chain, H(X_1..X_m) = H(X_1,X_2) + (m−2)·H_Ber(δ), so m·H_Ber(δ) is exact only asymptotically), while the per-symbol limit is exact.
- Rate or single-step conditional entropy? The object in Example 2.2 is lim_{m→∞} (1/m)H(P): the asymptotic per-symbol entropy, i.e. the entropy RATE. For this stationary order-2 chain it coincides with the single-step conditional entropy H(X_n | X_{n−2}) = H_Ber(δ), but what the paper prints is the limit. The candidate's H (per-symbol entropy rate of a fixed source) is the SAME KIND of quantity. The entry's "(section 2.1 Example 2.2, and section 4.3)" is correct for the v2 numbering; in the camera-ready the same statements are at Appendix A.2 (Example A.1) and §3 eq. (2).
- Value, MY OWN ARITHMETIC from the printed closed form and the stated p = q = 0.8 (δ = 0.2), base e as the paper declares: 0.2·ln 5 = 0.2 × 1.6094379 = 0.3218876; 0.8·ln 1.25 = 0.8 × 0.2231436 = 0.1785148; H_Ber(0.2) = 0.5004024 nats/char (= 0.7219 bits/char). Best i.i.d. (unigram) loss: H(π) = ln 2 = 0.6931472 nats/char. Difference 0.1927448 nats/char (= 0.2781 bits/char); ratio 1.385. This reproduces the record's 0.193 nats/char, which the record correctly labels as its own arithmetic. The paper prints the two closed forms and the δ → 0 divergence of the ratio; it does not print the difference at δ = 0.2.
