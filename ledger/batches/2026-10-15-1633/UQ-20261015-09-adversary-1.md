# UQ-20261015-09 — Adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my own words)

Take the Lean projects whose authors wrote a human blueprint (a LaTeX document where each lemma and its proof is tied to specific Lean declarations). For each lemma-node, divide the gzip size of the Lean code attributed to it by the gzip size of its blueprint text. Then ask: within one project, are nodes whose formal ingredients were mostly already in mathlib (at the commit where the node was first proved) cheaper in this ratio than nodes whose ingredients were mostly missing, after adjusting for how deep the node sits in the proof DAG, its subfield, and whether it is Lean 3 or Lean 4? Secondary: is there any leftover association with how big mathlib was in total?
