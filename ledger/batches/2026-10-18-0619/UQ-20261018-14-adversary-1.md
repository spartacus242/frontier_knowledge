## Restatement (my own words)

Take the modded-nanogpt leaderboard: a dated list of code versions, each claiming to train a small GPT to validation loss 3.28 on FineWeb faster than the previous one on 8×H100. Re-run every version several times on more than one node, once in its original software stack and once in a single modern stack. For each pair of consecutive records, ask whether the two re-measured time distributions are statistically distinguishable. Add up (in log space) how much of the total headline speedup comes from the distinguishable steps. Separately, re-apply each record's change at a bigger model/token budget and on the repo's second target, and report how much of the speedup persists. Also count how many records no longer run and why. In short: how much of the leaderboard's fall is measurement artefact or toolchain drift versus method that generalises?
