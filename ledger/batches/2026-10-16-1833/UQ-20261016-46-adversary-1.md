## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Training sets such as LAION and DataComp are shipped as lists of URLs, not as bytes. Links die. Two sub-questions: (i) are the links that die systematically different from the ones that live, on fields the list itself records (domain, language, caption length, licence, aesthetic/NSFW score, shard), and how well do those fields predict survival? (ii) if you train one model on what still downloads today and another on an equally large random slice of the original (which requires an archive of the original bytes), do their benchmark scores differ by more than seed noise?
