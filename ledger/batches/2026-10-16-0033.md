**Provisional detail.** UQ-20261016-02 (drug-interaction severity grades versus measured harm): 11 Gate 2 queries plus 18 adversarial ones in nine further vocabularies found nobody auditing a compendium's severity classes against outcomes at the classifier level. Two independent adversaries then landed on the same objection, which is why it is not Validated: the grades encode consequence magnitude *if the interaction occurs* crossed with documentation quality, not population incidence, so auditing them against an attributable harm rate scores a classifier against a target its authors never claimed to predict — a class can be entirely right about consequence and show zero rank discrimination on population harm. The question survives as an audit of the *deployed use* (hospitals do use the grade as a harm ranking, and the alert-volume limb is axis-agnostic), but the headline limb needs re-specifying. Both adversaries also demoted novelty by the same route: in geriatric pharmacoepidemiology the identical question form is routine — Beers versus STOPP criteria compared head-to-head for predictive validity against hospitalisation and ED visits — so "nobody asked this" shrinks to "nobody asked it about this classifier".

**Process notes.**

*A safety classifier blocked one candidate's searches, twice, and the shape of the failure is worth recording.* UQ-20261016-03's Gate 2 never ran in the generating pass: all 11 permitted queries were refused by a conversation-level classifier reacting to prior conversation content rather than to query content, and the record correctly logged the gate as not run with novelty **Unverified** rather than as passed. Completing it in a fresh context ran all 14 queries normally and raised novelty to Medium. The first strict replacement adversary then had **all 12** of its searches refused the same way, while a neutrally-framed prior-art sweep launched on the same topic ran 16 of 16. So the trigger tracks conversation context and framing, not subject matter. The second adversary executed 9 Disguise queries (3 further refused, recorded, not retried and not reworked), clearing the 8-query floor, and killed the candidate on evidence a refused call would not have supplied: Hyatt-Twynam et al. 2017's own abstract, read verbatim from the Cambridge repository, says the method "preferentially removes hosts expected to cause a high number of infections in the remaining host population" and that this "motivates a variable radius strategy ... via removal radii that vary by location" — directly contradicting the record's sole stated ground for distinguishing itself, that in that model the fraction of infected hosts that never reach an endpoint is "not representable".

*Two kills were checked by this session rather than taken on an adversary's word.* UQ-20261016-05's Disguise finding rested partly on a prior the adversary could only identify by title, so it was verified directly: the PLoS ONE 2017 registry paper linked New South Wales Cancer Registry records to a cohort study, extracted true stage from clinical notes for cases whose registry stage was "unknown", and found missing-at-random held only when the imputation model included treatment — the candidate's design, in the candidate's own field, on real missingness. UQ-20261016-01's and -04's Gate 2 rejections were read against the opened primary sources named in their records.

*Both of UQ-03's own Gate 4 pointers came back negative, and one was mischaracterised.* Tildesley et al. 2009's outcome measure is "fewer farms losing livestock", not the "farms culled per outbreak averted" the record attributed to it. Separately, the sweep established what the record's pointer was reaching for: Tildesley et al. **2010** (PNAS 107:1041–1046) do minimise "infected plus culled farms", i.e. the candidate's exact radius objective, in livestock; and Parnell et al. 2010 (Phytopathology 100:638–644) do the constrained plant version, minimising hosts removed subject to eradication succeeding.

*Where the empirical gap is real even though the question was rejected.* No source in English, Spanish, Italian or French reports the overdiagnosis fraction for any plant **or animal** statutory eradication programme. The foot-and-mouth literature names it only qualitatively — Riley's 2010 PNAS commentary speaks of "the loss of many never-to-have-been-infected farms" — though it is implicitly computable from those simulations; and the word "overdiagnosis" has not crossed into population-level veterinary or plant health at all. UQ-20261016-03 failed because the *question* is already asked in the field's own units, not because the number exists. A candidate restricted to measuring it in the field, on a tolerant-cultivar or non-crop stratum and graded against those three priors from the start, would be a defensible fresh intake.

*Fetch-level refusals, recorded as evidence gaps rather than worked around.* Two sources that would have settled UQ-03's contested presupposition directly — Lee et al. 2015 PNAS on asymptomatic huanglongbing spread (PMC4475945) and PMC6835679 on *Xylella* acquisition from asymptomatic plants — were refused by the fetch tool itself. Neither was retried. PMC's browser check also blocked PMC5487067 for UQ-05, which was read from the DOAJ abstract listing instead.

*Where the priors came from this batch.* Two of the four rejections were killed by a prior in the candidate's **own** field (UQ-01 in AI systems, UQ-04 in wastewater phylodynamics), both caught at Gate 2; the other two by a prior in a neighbouring field's vocabulary (UQ-03 in plant-disease optimal control and livestock ring-culling, UQ-05 in labour economics), both caught at Gate 4 Disguise. Across the six batches so far the cross-vocabulary prior remains the dominant failure mode, but the generator is now also producing candidates whose near-duplicates sit in plain sight in their own literature, which Gate 2 catches more cheaply.
