## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

When the same national metrology institute (NMI) takes part in one international key comparison after another of the same quantity, does its signed offset from the reference value come back with the same sign and size next time (lag-1 autocorrelation), and does an institute that is off in one quantity tend to be off in others (cross-measurand correlation)? What fraction of the standardised offsets is a stable "institute fingerprint" — and, under a propagation rule the asker fixes beforehand, how much larger should the uncertainty a downstream calibration customer inherits be than if each comparison were an unrelated snapshot? The candidate's motivation is an "inverted presupposition": that the CIPM MRA treats each comparison outcome as an independent draw and therefore carries no persistent participant-level term into what a user inherits.

## Own arithmetic (labelled)

Chain length per (institute, measurand) for CC-level key comparisons: MRA signed 1999; CCRI default validity 10 y and CC repeat cycles are set per CC (MRA Review Rec 1a), so an institute repeating on a ~10-year cycle has at most ~3 results per measurand by 2026 (27 years / ~10). This is MY arithmetic from the stated 10-year default; actual repeat cycles vary by CC (some KCs have never been repeated; BIPM ongoing bilateral comparisons and the SIR admit more frequent participation, but there the comparator is the BIPM's own standard, not a participant-derived KCRV). Consequence: within-chain lag-1 autocorrelation (limb ii) is not estimable per chain; only a pooled cross-institute correlation between iterations is, which is the quantity a repeat comparison's "compared with previous" paragraph would report incidentally.

## Recommended verdict

REJECTED — Unresolved on attack 4 (Presupposition failure): the question inverts a presupposition the field does not hold. Primary sources opened (CCRI/12-05; IAWG-GD-01 of 25 Sept 2019; CIPM MRA Review Recommendations of 23 Aug 2016) show the arrangement presupposes persistence of an NMI's demonstrated performance for ~10 years conditional on no change in staff, equipment or methods, supersedes earlier results with the most recent, pools an institute's record across comparisons in CMC review, and extends KC evidence across measurands — so "each comparison outcome treated as an independent draw" (R1 limb iv's comparator) describes no actual treatment, the inherited uncertainty is the NMI's own claimed uncertainty rather than anything derived from a DoE sequence, and the candidate's consequence map has its signs reversed.

Routing: not rejected as already-asked in its full estimand form (Disguise is Partial, not Unresolved). Nearest in-field prior formulation to log, missed by the record: the CCQM IAWG Record of Participation (IAWG-GD-01) — unit = institute across comparisons over 10 years; endpoint = standardised deviations; comparator = KCRV; estimand = fraction of points within +/-1 (pass/fail), not autocorrelation, variance share, cross-measurand correlation or inflation. Promotion condition if re-posed at a later date (would be a new candidate, not this one): frame the study as a test of the field's actual presupposition (persistence-until-change, CCRI/12-05), with the configuration of staff/equipment/method as the unit, and drop limb (iv).
