Motivating gap: a large, independently operated population of GNSS-disciplined clocks now logs time error or holdover state continuously, while GNSS interference has itself become continuously mapped and externally datable — so exposure can be defined without using the receivers' own output. The pairing is the new instrument. Receiver vulnerability under interference is currently studied as a TEST CONDITION (P3 measures one "receiver under test" in a simulated obscuration scene), and a figure measured under a test condition is not the operating value.

What would count as an answer: per epoch and per model or oscillator-class stratum, the empirical distribution of maximum |time error| delivered (median and upper quantiles with contributing-unit counts), the exceedance fraction against each enumerated limit with a Clopper-Pearson interval, a decomposition by oscillator class and multi-constellation capability, a statement of the exposure definition's independence from the outcome, and the share of the nominal denominator lost to missing logs.
