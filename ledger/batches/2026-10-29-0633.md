## What the verification pass found

**One refutation, and it changed the batch's output.** Check 02-e refuted UQ-02's routing recommendation on both of the sentences it rested on. Prior 1 does not show the narrowed V2 "posed in print" — it poses and *performs* a direct computation and never mentions experiment. And the glass-science literature does *fit* the Tolman parameter to experimental nucleation data, so the entry's "reports gamma_inf for assumed delta rather than a determined delta" is wrong for everything after 2006 — "fit" rather than "determine", because no reader could establish whether the data pin the parameter separately or only in combination with the interfacial tension. Independently of those two errors the row was barred by rule: answered and disguised-known rejections are not routed, and the drafted problem statement was broader than the question that had been graded, in exactly the form the glass fits already answer. One routed row goes out from this batch, not two.

**A kill that held under three readers.** UQ-04 died on a single adversary's attack 4: the candidate's null is a ratio of a measured displacement to a computed one, so it is an absolute comparison, and the only published ptychographic Debye-Waller value ever checked against an independent standard runs about six times low. Three checkers read that paper and the number held every time — though what is independent there is the *readers*, not the routes: all three fetched the same URL through the same summariser, one quotation came twice off one cached page, and the published version was served to nobody. Only one checker's retrieval path was independent, having reached the paper from the subject alone.

What narrowed was the *transfer*. The comparison is an in-plane ptychographic value against an isotropic powder one; the paper names three causes, of which the missing out-of-plane vibrations are tied to hBN's layered structure while the layer-averaging factor is stated as a slice-thickness relation with no reference to coupling, so they are two mechanisms the paper keeps separate; and its own 3-D covalent *simulated* case underestimates by about 25 per cent. That puts the general-technique bias near 1.3x — a checker's labelled inference, not a demonstrated figure, and drawn from a correlation-magnitude comparison against a same-projection simulation rather than a B-factor against a standard. The rest of the 6x is unapportioned, so the entry's own falsification condition got neither a yes nor a no.

The pass then re-grounded the kill on a second instance pointing the other way: Zhang et al.'s data yield measured-to-computed ratios of about 1.1-1.5 in the squared displacement in a **defect-free** lattice. A ratio differing from unity with no defect present is what an absolute null forbids. Two caveats travel with it and belong on the face of the finding: the paper prints no such ratio, both figures being the checker's own arithmetic over printed pairs; and its host is twisted bilayer WSe2, the same 2-D van der Waals class as the first instance, so it re-grounds the absolute-scale point and says nothing about transfer to a 3-D host — the very axis the kill was narrowed on. The genuinely 3-D evidence came from a different check and runs *against* the kill.

**A promotion condition shown ill-formed.** One of the three stated grounds for UQ-05's well-posedness cap does not survive a first-hand read: the two snow-crystal transition enumerations the entry set against each other describe the *centres* and the *boundaries* of the same four-regime diagram, so they are not a mismatch, and the promotion condition asking for a "stated choice between" them asked for a choice between non-comparable things. What is genuinely open in its place is narrower and real — where the first boundary lies, and a citation for the three-member convention, which exists nowhere in this batch.

**And promotion made harder.** A review sentence reporting a *measured* non-zero isotope shift of about four degrees, cited to a primary nobody has reached, answers one of UQ-05's own alternative formulations at review level and contradicts its claim that no habit diagram for a second isotopologue had surfaced. Canonical V2 survives, because the sentence prints no per-transition value — but reaching that primary is now a condition of promotion, and it is one fetch away. Worth stating plainly: an Unresolved attack 2 would be a rejection under the Gate 4 rule, and that is a possible outcome of reaching it. UQ-05 is Provisional in part because a document was not opened.

**Two priors that proved closer to their candidate than the entry said.** UQ-02's Prior 2 is a peer-reviewed 2020 paper with a served DOI, not the undated preprint the entry called it — the DOI being served on the arXiv abstract page and absent only from the PDF route the first reader used — and its clusters span 2.6-10.5 nm, *overlapping* the candidate's size range rather than sitting below its floor. Both errors made the kill look weaker than it is. The same check confirmed the prior's printed value, its sign, its location and the candidate's own functional form, while declining to assess the extrapolation behind it, having not seen the figure.

And a Tolman parameter fitted to real-material data turns out to be in print for five silicate glasses — all five in one 2019 table, whose unit for the column is itself a checker's inference from a consistency check rather than a read header, and whose value for the one glass a second paper also reports does not reconcile with it. That is the stronger disguised-known ground, and it is stronger than the entry's own account of its kill.

**One document opened for the first time by anyone.** Michikami et al. 2023, the shock-experiment paper that might have carried UQ-01's estimand, was read at full text and prints none of the three quantities. The absence is of the *quantity*, not of the vocabulary: "anisotrop" occurs once in its Introduction, for the failure-stress anisotropy of a different meteorite, and "density" twice for the targets' bulk densities. The finding also rests on three convergent summariser-level reads of one page rather than on the two independent term sweeps the ledger asks for before recording an absence, the budget having ended first.

## Where the judge's briefs were wrong

Each of the five amending agents was asked to report, specifically, every place my ruling was wrong or overstated against the verification note it compressed. All five reported defects, and the count is higher than the mathematics batch's. The notes are primary and the agents followed them; these are recorded because a brief that misreads its own evidence will misread it again.

The consequential ones:

- **I called the second instance re-grounding UQ-04's kill a 3-D host. It is twisted bilayer WSe2** — the same 2-D van der Waals class as the first instance. That is not a labelling slip: the whole transfer question turns on 2-D against 3-D, so a 3-D second instance would have borne on transfer and this one does not. The genuinely 3-D evidence came from a different check and runs against the kill.
- **I said that paper "prints" ratios. It prints none of them** — both the raw and the squared figures are the checker's own arithmetic over printed pairs, and the checker labelled them so.
- **I said the kill's source was opened on three independent routes.** All three fetched the same URL through the same summariser, one quotation came twice off a single cached page, and the published version was served to no one. The readers were independent; one retrieval path was.
- **I wrote that a constant bias "cancels" in the temperature limb.** It rescales both the ratio and its temperature derivative; what it cannot do is manufacture a temperature dependence where none exists — which is exactly why that adversary had to argue the bias is itself temperature-dependent, and could not source it.
- **I described all four refused checks as the readers for one promotion condition.** Two were; the third was to test the one objection that could have turned UQ-03 from Provisional to Rejected, and the fourth to read a prior three readers had left at abstract level. So the refusals cost more than promotion — they left the only potentially fatal objection untested. UQ-03's survival rests in part on a check that never ran, and its entry now says so. The agent flagged that it could not corroborate my description from anything on disk; it was right to, and I corrected the entry from the commissioning script rather than from memory.
- **I asserted that the glass literature "determines" the Tolman parameter.** It fits it; whether the data pin it separately or only in combination with the interfacial tension is open, and no reader could tell, because the defining equation was served with its fractions flattened at every route tried — so even the sign convention is unreadable.
- **I attributed five glass magnitudes to two papers.** All five are in one 2019 table; the second paper carries one glass, and its value for that glass does not reconcile with the table's.
- **I cited the no-late-Gate-4 rule loosely.** Its actual trigger is a Gate 2 rejection that never had an adversary. UQ-04 was rejected at Gate 4 with two adversary logs, so the rule does not reach it in form; the conclusion holds the more easily, and the entry now says it that way instead of mis-citing.
- **I would have shipped a term-absence claim on UQ-01.** "Prints no anisotropy parameter" is true of the quantity and false of the word, which appears in Michikami's Introduction for a different meteorite's failure-stress anisotropy. The agent caught it before it was written.

Several agents also recorded amendments the ruling owed and did not list — four findings from a check the ruling skipped entirely, a location error in UQ-01's own attack-1 row, a query count restated in three places and wrong in all three, and an internally inconsistent count of Unverified clauses in UQ-05's status line. All were made.

The pattern across two batches is now consistent enough to act on: the judge's compression of a verification note is the least reliable document in the chain, and an agent told to read the note and report back against the brief catches more than it costs.

## What this batch adds to the method

**A refusal is a third failure mode, and it needed its own name.** The ledger already distinguished a check that ran and could not read its document (Unverified) from a check that ran and found its claim false (Unresolved, which can reject). Checks 03-a through 03-d are neither: they never reached a document. The distinguishing fact about them is that all four belonged to one entry while the other 21 checks ran on the same shared preamble — so this safeguard tracked the entry's own subject and sources, not the prompt shape the earlier cases pointed at. That is the first per-entry instance on record, and it is now in team memory under `search-classifier-refusals` beside the three prompt-shaped ones.

**Ask what kind of average a comparator is.** UQ-04's whole kill turned on a 6x gap between two numbers — and one of them is an in-plane measurement while the other is an isotropic powder value, in a material whose out-of-plane vibrations only the comparator contains. A deficit between two different kinds of average is not yet a deficit of the instrument. This is the same family of defect as asking what one row of a cited table *is*, which the mathematics batch ran into a day earlier, and it is now recorded with it.

**A narrowed ground is usually re-groundable, often on a document the batch already had open for something else** — but the second instance needs its own regime and read level graded before anyone leans on it. UQ-04's came from Zhang et al., a paper this batch's readers had already read for a different purpose, and it turned out to share the first instance's 2-D class and to print none of the numbers attributed to it. The reading rules for a PARTIAL on a fatal ground — what a narrowing licenses, what it does not, how to separate the limb a kill reaches from the limb it does not, and how to count what is actually independent about "three independent reads" — are now in team memory as `ledger-narrowed-grounds`.

**And a routing recommendation is a claim about two literatures, so it gets its own check.** UQ-02's row would have gone out on two false sentences. It was also barred by a rule nobody had to read a paper to apply: a disguised-known rejection is not routable at all, whatever its narrowed reformulation looks like. Both now sit in `ledger-routing-test`, with the matching rule that a routed problem must never be stated more broadly than the question that was actually graded — the broad form is what the adjacent literature already answers.
