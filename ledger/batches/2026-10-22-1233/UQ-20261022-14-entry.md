Question (canonical): Over a declared frame of SWOT-observed reservoirs and lakes whose operator publishes both a water-level record and a documented datum (a named vertical datum, or a published conversion to one), and whose operating rule states a flood-pool, spillway-crest or freeboard threshold: (i) what is the distribution across the frame of the per-water-body residual r = (SWOT geoid-referenced water-surface elevation) minus (operator-reported level transformed to the same geopotential datum by the operator's OWN stated conversion), with its uncertainty; (ii) is r stationary over the mission record, tested for steps and trend against an independent local vertical-motion constraint where one exists; and (iii) within each water-body size class for which SWOT's stated WSE accuracy is smaller than the stated operating margin, for what share of the frame does |r| exceed that margin?

Motivating gap: SWOT-vs-gauge validation removes a per-water-body offset in its methods section before computing any metric and declines to investigate it (Koehn & Nielsen 2026, egusphere-2025-6366: "we remove the median offset between SWOT and the gauge on a per-lake-basis, before reporting any metric"; "investigating the origin of the removed offset lies beyond the scope of this study"). The offsets are printed but as nuisance residue, undecomposed, over frames chosen for altimeter validation, with no stationarity test and no comparison against any operating-rule threshold.
