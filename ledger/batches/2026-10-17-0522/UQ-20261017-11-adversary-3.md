## 1. Restatement (my own words, not the generator's)

Diploid-genome assembly pipelines include a "purge" step (purge_dups / purge_haplotigs, or hifiasm's built-in -l purge) that deletes contigs judged to be the second allele of a heterozygous locus. The candidate asks: when that step deletes sequence, how often is the deleted sequence not an allele but a real, recently born paralogue (a second gene copy that genuinely exists in the sequenced individual); and if those real copies are put back, do published gene-family birth/death rates (CAFE-style) and the youngest bin of paralogue-age distributions move outside their stated and error-model-corrected intervals? Underneath sits a mechanism claim: that real young paralogues and allelic haplotigs are indistinguishable to the tool because both show roughly half-depth coverage plus near-identical sequence.
