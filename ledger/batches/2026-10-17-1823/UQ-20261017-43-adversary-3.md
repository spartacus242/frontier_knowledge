## Restatement (my own words)

Take archived in-service NDT results in which the same weld or location was examined by two or more physically different methods (RT, conventional UT, phased-array UT, eddy current) and nobody ever cut the weld open to learn the truth. Treat "is there a recordable flaw at this location, above a declared size threshold?" as a hidden binary variable. Apply the Hui–Walter / latent-class machinery of diagnostic epidemiology — which recovers each test's sensitivity and specificity from the agreement/disagreement pattern across tests on populations of differing prevalence — to estimate each NDT method's POD (sensitivity) and false-call rate (1 − specificity), separately for pre-declared flaw-size classes and strata and under a pre-declared conditional-dependence structure. Ask (a) whether that model is identifiable at all with the methods and strata real archives contain, and (b) where it is, how the field numbers compare with whatever POD basis the relevant assessment route (RBI, PFM) actually consumes, that route to be identified as part of the work.

The record itself has already scaled the claim down to "an un-asked method-and-identifiability question aimed at an acknowledged open quantity" (field POD from field records is conceded asked-but-open). My job is to test whether even that reduced claim survives.

## Recommended verdict

**Rejected** — Unresolved on attack 4 (presupposition failure: the pre-declared flaw-size-class stratification that V2 relies on cannot be built from inspection records because undetected locations are unsized, which empties the all-negative cell of every size class and leaves the false-call rate per class without a denominator; the covariate fallback makes the field-vs-lab comparison confounded with the unknown field flaw-size spectrum, so the pre-committed "below the lab basis → laboratory artefact" reading does not follow; and the motivating premise that the field has no field-record POD method is contradicted by Brewer 1995, FAA/Volpe, opened). Attacks 3 and 7 are also Unresolved; attack 2 is Partial, with two of the record's load-bearing novelty claims ("zero engineering applications"; "boundary with no traffic, verified in both directions") falsified by van Wieringen & de Mast 2008 (opened).

Routing: the general question belongs to industrial statistics — measurement system analysis for binary data without reference values (*Technometrics* 2008; Waterloo BMS theses) — and to aerospace field-POD estimation from service records (FAA/Volpe 1995). A re-submission would need to be a different question: identifiability of a *polytomous* latent-class model (no flaw / declared size classes) with called sizes as ordinal indicators over physically distinct NDT methods, with the lab comparator dropped or made commensurable by an explicitly assumed size spectrum; that is a pointer, not an attempt.
