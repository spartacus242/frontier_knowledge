Domain(s): AI — large-scale training systems and training-data provenance (AI architecture); neighbours: distributed-systems recovery semantics, data attribution/memorization auditing. Source method: silence mapping.

Question (canonical): (V1, assessed) For training runs whose restart history and data pipeline are auditable (open runs, or a deliberately instrumented replication under shipped recovery semantics), taking the run's *claimed* training sequence as an artifact (a manifest with an order, or, where order is only procedural, the sequence (seed, loader version, world size) would have produced absent faults): (a) what is the divergence between claimed and realized token sequence induced by dataloader-state recovery at each restart and in total, reported both as a multiset divergence (tokens/documents never consumed; consumed more times than claimed, as a fraction of nominal) and as an ordering divergence (displacement from claimed position); and (b) under a pre-registered test with a stated detection floor, does per-document memorization or trajectory-specific influence differ for documents adjacent to a restart boundary relative to matched documents away from any boundary?

Alternative formulations:
- (V0, intake, superseded at Gate 1) In large-scale runs with documented restarts, what fraction of the nominal token sequence is actually skipped or re-consumed by dataloader-state recovery, and is the deviation detectable in the trained model as a dated discontinuity in memorization or influence?
- (lay) When a big training run crashes and resumes, how much data gets quietly dropped or seen twice, and does it leave a mark on the model?
- (neighbour vocabulary) What are the delivery semantics (exactly-once / at-least-once / at-most-once) of a production training dataloader across failure recovery, and what is that recovery's coverage measured on the model rather than on the run?
One Gate 1 reformulation used (V0 to V1): fixed the claimed-sequence artifact, split the single "fraction" into multiset + ordering divergence, and required a pre-registered detection floor so a null is bounded. Full note in batches/2026-10-16-1033/UQ-20261016-30-record.md.

Motivating gap: The fault-tolerance how-literature (restartable/deterministic dataloaders, distributed checkpointing, elastic training) is dense and denominates success in goodput; public chronicles report interruption counts and effective training time (OPT-175B arXiv:2205.01068; Llama 3's 466 interruptions, arXiv:2407.21783; 504-GPU operational analysis arXiv:2605.09370, "median retry interval of 11 minutes") but no source was found reporting tokens or documents skipped or re-consumed per restart.
