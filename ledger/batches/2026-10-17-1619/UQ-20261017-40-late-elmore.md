- Closest, abstract only: Wu MZ, McInnes MDF, Macdonald DB, Kielar AZ, Duigenan S. CT in Adults: Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis of Interpretation Discrepancy Rates. Radiology 2014;270(3):717-35 (online 2013-11-13). 58 studies, 388,123 CTs. Overall discrepancy 7.7% (5.6-10.3); major 2.4% (1.7-3.2). Major discrepancy where the reference radiologist was NOT blinded to the initial report: 2.0% (1.4-2.7; 43 studies); where blinded: 12.1% (4.4-29.4; 5 studies); Q=10.65, P=.001. Design is a between-study meta-analytic comparison; the "reference" is a second radiologist's read, not a reference standard independent of the index read, so it shows the effect of knowing the original but not against an independent truth. Heterogeneity high; authors call for more research on blinding.
