Source method: presupposition inversion (the settled claim inverted: "a pretraining corpus is exogenous, so a comparison run on a fixed corpus measures the architecture").

Question (canonical — Gate 1 reformulation, the version to carry forward):
For ONE fixed Common Crawl source pool re-filtered at a matched keep rate by T >= 2 quality classifiers whose positive labels come from different annotator ("teacher") models — chosen capability-matched across families so that teacher IDENTITY is not confounded with teacher CAPABILITY — crossed with A >= 3 pre-declared architecture/recipe arms at matched FLOPs and matched token budget, with S >= 2 seeds per cell and one evaluation suite declared before any run: (i) how large is the teacher x arm interaction variance component relative to the within-cell seed variance (ratio with CI), and (ii) how many of the A(A-1)/2 pairwise architecture contrasts reverse SIGN between teachers at a pre-registered minimum effect size?

Question (intake version, recorded as required):
Holding the raw source pool, keep rate and token budget fixed, does the measured ranking of pretraining architectures and recipes depend on which teacher model produced the quality labels used to filter the corpus — that is, is the teacher x architecture interaction in downstream performance distinguishable from seed noise?

Why the reformulation was needed (one permitted Gate 1 reformulation, used): the intake version is answerable-either-way on three counts — "ranking depends on" names no criterion or magnitude; "distinguishable from seed noise" names no effect size and no noise estimator; and "which teacher model" does not separate teacher identity from teacher capability, which the available real-world teachers confound (Nemotron-CC's own annotators are Mistral 8x22B-instruct and Nemotron-340B-instruct — not capability-matched). The reformulation fixes the estimand (interaction variance component + sign-flip count), the comparator (within-cell seed variance), the design (matched keep rate, matched FLOPs and tokens, S >= 2 seeds, suite declared in advance) and the identification condition (capability-matched teachers from different families).

Alternative formulations:
1. Are published architecture comparisons corpus-conditional, because model-filtered corpora are selected to suit the annotator model that filtered them?
2. If one Common Crawl pool is re-filtered by two different annotator models at the same keep rate, do the two corpora preserve the same ordering of candidate model designs?
3. (lay) When a paper says "this model design is better", is it reporting a fact about the design or a fact about whichever AI graded its training data?

Presuppositions:
P1. Major open corpora are model-filtered with identifiable teacher models. ESTABLISHED (upgraded from intake's FROM MEMORY) by reading arXiv:2412.02595, lines quoted above. Caveat carried forward: for Nemotron-CC the operative "teacher" is a max-ensemble of three scorers, not one model, so that corpus is not a single-teacher artifact and cannot serve as an arm without re-running its pipeline per teacher.
P2. Architecture/recipe rankings are measurable precisely enough for an interaction to be detectable above seed noise at affordable scale. CONTESTED and load-bearing; not resolved here. One opened datum sharpens it in both directions: arXiv:2604.20549 reports that "downstream aggregate accuracy remains sensitive to the classifier's initial sampling seed (shifting by 0.3% in Arabic and 0.8% in French)" — nuisance variance from the FILTER's own randomness alone is already the size of many published architecture gaps, which is why the question matters and why it needs per-cell replicates. Unread lead bearing on this: arXiv:2608.02705 "Can Training Logs Make Model Comparisons More Precise?" (TITLE ONLY, not opened).
P3. Keep rate and token budget can be matched across teacher-filtered corpora. ESTABLISHED (keep rate is a threshold on a score; Nemotron-CC Table 9 reports 8%/11%/25% keep shares, so matching is a choice, not a constraint).
P4. There exists architecture/recipe variation whose ranking the field acts on. ESTABLISHED (dense vs MoE, attention variants, depth/width, positional schemes, optimizer/LR rules).
P5. Teachers' notions of quality are at least partly idiosyncratic rather than converging. SUPPORTED, from the ledger's own prior reading of Nemotron-CC Table 8 (union 11,359,655 vs intersection 1,152,821 = 10.1% overlap) and from the fact that quantified disagreement is that paper's stated justification for ensembling; also consistent with 2510.00866's Appendix D heading. If two teachers' keep sets nearly coincided the interaction would be unmeasurable by construction; they do not.
P6. Comparison is FLOP-matched rather than step-matched. DECLARED, not contested.
P7. The interaction, if present, is attributable to teacher IDENTITY rather than to capability level alone. CONTESTED and now sharper: the real deployed teachers are capability-mismatched (Mistral 8x22B-instruct vs Nemotron-340B-instruct; Llama3 vs a fastText bag-of-ngrams), so an off-the-shelf arm pair cannot separate the two and the design must commission capability-matched teachers from different families. This is a cost, not an impossibility.
P8 (new, from the reformulation). A pre-registered minimum effect size for a "sign flip" is agreeable. CONTESTED — with arbitrarily small true gaps, signs flip for free; the limb is only meaningful against a declared band, which is why the reformulation requires one.

What would count as an answer:
A pre-registered factorial experiment: 2-3 corpora filtered from ONE source pool by different teacher models at a matched keep rate, crossed with 3-5 architecture/recipe arms at matched FLOPs and tokens, >= 2 seeds per cell, one evaluation suite fixed in advance. Deliverables: (i) the estimated teacher x arm interaction with CIs, reported against the within-cell seed variance; (ii) the count and identity of pairwise architecture contrasts whose SIGN flips between teachers at the declared effect size; (iii) the keep-set overlap between the teacher corpora, so the interaction can be read against how different the corpora actually were.
A null — interaction inside seed noise at the pre-set effect size — licenses current practice and is as publishable as a positive. A non-null means an architecture claim must name the annotator model of its corpus as part of the claim, and meta-analyses across differently filtered corpora are not poolable.

Significance — consequence:
If YES (interaction outside seed noise, with sign flips): the sentence "architecture X beats Y at this scale" ceases to be well formed without naming the annotator model of the corpus it was measured on, and the standard move of using FineWeb-Edu or DCLM-baseline as a neutral control becomes a hidden conditioning variable. Concretely: recipe papers must report the teacher as part of the claim; meta-analyses and leaderboards that pool architecture results across differently filtered corpora are not poolable; and labs that pick an architecture by small-scale ablation on one filtered corpus have been selecting partly for fit to an annotator rather than for the design. It also inverts a cheap-research assumption: a corpus is not a fixed measuring stick but an instrument with its own calibration.
If NO (interaction inside a pre-set band): current practice is licensed, with a number attached — the first published noise-floor statement for corpus-conditionality — which lets reviewers stop asking for corpus-robustness checks and lets the field keep comparing across FineWeb-Edu, DCLM and Nemotron-CC results. A null is as useful as a positive here, which is what makes it a question rather than an agenda.

Significance — audience:
(a) Pretraining teams who choose architectures and recipes from small-scale ablations on one filtered corpus (the DCLM / FineWeb / Nemotron / OLMo / Marin community) — they would either stratify ablations by teacher or report the conditioning. (b) Corpus builders (HuggingFace, AI2, NVIDIA) — a positive makes teacher provenance a first-class dataset-card field and makes a teacher-invariant construction (intersection, or the max-ensemble Nemotron-CC already uses for a different reason) a robustness device rather than a recall trick. (c) Designers of data-centric benchmarks, whose premise is that fixing the model makes data comparable — a positive says the two factors are not separable in the direction they assumed. (d) Reviewers and area chairs who currently accept a single-corpus ablation as evidence about an architecture.

Significance — fecundity:
Is the interaction predictable from keep-set overlap, which would let a cheap set-overlap statistic (the 10.1% figure's descendants) stand in for a factorial? Does it shrink with scale, i.e. is this a small-ablation pathology or a frontier one? Is there co-design — an optimal teacher for a given architecture class, which would make filter and architecture joint design variables rather than independent ones? Can a teacher-invariant corpus be constructed and does training on the intersection cost performance? Does the same interaction appear with post-training data and reward models, where the teacher is even more explicit? And methodologically: what is the minimum per-cell replication that makes an interaction of the observed size detectable, which is the direct descendant of UQ-20261016-06/-49.

Significance — anchoring:

Goyal et al.'s compute-conditional curation result, which this would extend to a factor the field treats as exogenous. The data-centric-benchmark premise of DataComp-LM. The published rank-instability genre (arXiv:2507.01633, SafetyRepro, RankShift), which has mapped evaluation-side instability and not training-data-side instability. Reason for silence:
A disciplinary boundary manufactured by two complementary ceteris-paribus conventions, reinforced by multiplicative cost and by the factor's youth. The data-centric side (DataComp/DCLM, Nemotron-CC, FineWeb) fixes the model to make corpora comparable — "All experiments use the same 8B transformer configuration" is the protocol, not an oversight; the architecture/recipe side fixes the corpus to make models comparable. Each community's methodological virtue makes the other's factor a constant, so the crossed cell is nobody's experiment and no venue owns the design. Cost compounds it: each side's publishable unit is T runs or A runs, while the interaction needs T x A x S runs — an order-of-magnitude jump for a result whose best case is a caveat on other people's papers. And the factor is barely two years old: model-based quality filtering with a nameable teacher dates from roughly 2023-24 (FineWeb-Edu's Llama3 annotations, DCLM's fastText, Nemotron-CC's Mistral/Nemotron annotators), so before then there was no teacher to cross anything with. A weaker incentive strand, named but not load-bearing: the labs best placed to run this are the ones whose corpora and whose prior ablations a positive result would devalue.
