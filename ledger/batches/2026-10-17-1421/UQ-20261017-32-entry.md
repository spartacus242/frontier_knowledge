Alternative formulations: How many independent measurements actually lie behind a comparative dataset of N species, and does the effective sample size change the allometric exponent? · Is comparative biology's degrees-of-freedom count inflated by citation chains converging on one original measurement, and does correcting for that move the exponents the field argues about?
Motivating gap: Comparative fits over species-level compilations treat one row per species as one observation and correct non-independence for phylogeny alone. The untested presupposition is measurement independence: that rows descend from distinct primary determinations rather than from shared handbook or secondary-compilation ancestry. CORRECTION carried from Gate 2: the intake claim that nobody has looked is partly false — Genoud, Isler & Martin (2018) traced 1739 BMR entries for 817 species to 946 original publications (95% read, 57 entries unresolvable) and reported exponent movement across data selections; Farrell-Gray & Gotelli (2005) named shared data as a second, non-phylogenetic non-independence inside the 2/3-vs-3/4 dispute. The residue claimed as un-asked was only the estimand: cluster structure -> design effect -> provenance-clustered refit.
Presuppositions: (1) enough per-record source metadata for primary-source resolution at scale — CONTESTED, partly relieved by Genoud's 3.3% unresolvable; (2) "primary determination" is definable as a reported measurement on specified individuals — CONTESTED, and the gate this entry failed at; (3) shared sources induce correlated error — established as statistics, magnitude unknown here; (4) published exponents are consequential — established; (5) phylogenetic correction does not already absorb the clustering — CONTESTED, limb (iv) measures it; (6) published intervals are narrow enough for a clustered refit to cross a decisive threshold — SUPPORTED for Genoud's SELECT fit only (0.729 +/- 0.010 S.E.; a design effect of 2 readmits 0.75 — judge's and adversaries' arithmetic on his S.E., which he reports instead of CIs).

Presupposition harmless = effective N within a pre-declared fraction of nominal N with exponents moving less than their published CIs; presupposition fails = exponents moving outside them, or intervals widening past the margins that separate competing scaling predictions.
