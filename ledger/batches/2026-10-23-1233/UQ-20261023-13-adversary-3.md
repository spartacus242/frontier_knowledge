## 1. Restatement in my own words

Take alloy or compound systems for which at least two research groups have independently published complete CALPHAD Gibbs-energy parameter sets. Pool every experimental datum those groups used into one digitised corpus, each datum tagged with the study that produced it. For each system, rank the competing parameter sets by how well they reproduce the pooled corpus under one declared metric (the "apparent" residual, recomputed because the papers print no fit statistic), then rank them by a "leave-one-study-out predictive residual" under the same metric, and report (a) Kendall's tau between the two rankings across the frame, (b) the distribution of predictive-minus-apparent gaps, (c) how often and where the top-ranked assessment changes, with a weighting-sensitivity panel. A null result (tau near 1, small gap) is declared to be an answer.

What the question CONDITIONS ON, read literally (tested in section 5): that such a frame exists with retrievable parameters; that each assessment's fitted corpus is recoverable per study; that "leave-one-study-out predictive evaluation" is a defined operation on a PUBLISHED, FIXED parameter set given only the attributed corpus; that the pooled corpus is an in-sample ("apparent") set for every assessment; that one metric can be fixed across property types; that curators rank assessments.
