## Restatement (my own words, before reading the generator's framing again)

Take a piece of ordinary non-clonal tissue. Sequence individual DNA molecules on a platform that reads both the base sequence and the methyl marks on that same molecule. Fish out the rare molecules that carry a somatic point mutation. For each one, line up the mutation-free molecules covering the same position in the same sample on the same strand, and ask a single question: do the mutation-carrying molecules carry more or less 5mC on *nearby* CpGs — with the mutated base itself and any CpG the mutation created or destroyed thrown out — than their matched mutation-free neighbours? Report it as a matched odds ratio, split by mutation type, with a mutation class where no effect is expected as a control and a stated model of how often per-read methylation calls are wrong. A clear non-unity OR says the methylation–mutation relationship is a property of the individual molecule; a tight interval around 1 says the familiar regional correlation is regional, i.e. carried by something other than the mark on the molecule that mutated.

Two things the restatement makes obvious, which I then tested: the question is about the molecule's *current* neighbourhood, not about the base that mutated; and "the same molecule" has to be defined by the assay, not by intuition.

### Spot-check of the record's cited priors

- **P1 real and accurately described, with one correction and one new fact.** Title confirmed as "...copy number *alterations*..." (the record writes "copy number variation"; cosmetic). - **P2 real and accurately described.** Kusmartsev, Drożdż, Schuster-Böckler, Warnecke, *Genetics* 214(4):809–823, 2020, DOI 10.1534/genetics.120.303028. Methylation assigned from aggregated read ratios (≥0.7, ≥10 reads); no read- or molecule-level analysis. The record's characterisation holds; I could find no sentence in which the authors themselves concede the molecule-vs-region limitation, so the record's framing of P2's weakness is the record's inference, not P2's admission.

## Nothing Unresolved; what that does and does not buy

No attack came out Unresolved, so nothing here forces a Rejection, and I decline to manufacture one: the single commonest error on this ledger would have been to read P1's title and conclude the candidate was already done. P1 is aimed at a different object — the created/destroyed CpG at the mutated base — and the flanking-CpG estimand V2 is built on is verifiably **absent** from it. Gate 2's Low grade is nonetheless correct *for the question as intaken*: V1 is answered, in print, by P1. What survives is V2, which my nine fresh queries did not find anywhere, and which would grade Medium.

Validated is unavailable on two independent grounds: the ledger requires Well-posedness 3/3 (the record scores 2/3, and my attacks 4 and 5 land as Partial on exactly the two limbs it concedes), and novelty ≥ Medium holds only for the narrowed V2, not for the question as intaken.

## Recommended verdict

**Provisional** — no Unresolved attack, but novelty holds only for V2 and well-posedness is 2/3; promotion requires a measured per-read 5mC error rate on a conversion-free platform, a fixed window with an equivalence margin, and a power calculation from published per-molecule yields.

## Single strongest killing point

On the one platform shown to deliver per-molecule mutation-plus-methylation co-measurement, bisulfite conversion averaged **77% (IQR 75–79%)**, deliberately chosen because >98% conversion "lost about half the template molecules" — so roughly a quarter of unmethylated cytosines read as methylated (my arithmetic from their stated efficiency, ~21–25% across their IQR), and because conversion failure is a per-molecule chemical event the error is correlated across the whole window. The record's fix for presupposition 1 — move the estimand from a single CpG to window-level density — therefore does not fix it, and the same trade-off hits the undemonstrated feasibility limb at the same time. The escape to conversion-free native modification calling is the candidate's best answer and is exactly the limb neither Gate 2 nor I could verify, because HiDEF-seq could not be read.
