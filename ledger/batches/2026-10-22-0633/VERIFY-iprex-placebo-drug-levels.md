## CLAIM

    specimens were assayed). An earlier agent reported the count is referred to Fig. S5, which it

(e) What Fig. S5 and its caption actually show, including any placebo-arm denominator.
(f) What assay was used for drug levels, and whether the paper prints its limit of detection or
    quantification.

The legend names **no denominators at all** and carries **no placebo footnote**. Its own scope

So: the paper **MEASURES** the placebo specimens (assays were run — the sentence asserts a result

**Correction to the earlier agent's report.** The earlier agent wrote that the main text "refers
the count to Fig. S5". That is not what the main text does. Fig. S5 is cited **once**, in the
Methods, and it is cited for the **matching scheme**, not for a placebo count:

> "Subjects with HIV infection were matched with two control subjects, one from each study group

The main text therefore does not defer a placebo denominator to Fig. S5; it simply **omits** a

### (c) Sampling frame — the Methods, quoted

The entire Methods subsection on drug levels, as served, every sentence in order
(reconstruction from 6 consecutive segments; junctions verified at "study group | who were",
"mononuclear cells | were tested", "metabolites | of FTC"):

> "**Subgroup Analysis of Drug Levels**
> A prespecified subgroup analysis was performed to investigate whether drug levels correlated
> with protective effect. Subjects with HIV infection were matched with two control subjects, one
> from each study group who were selected from among seronegative subjects, according to study
> site (Fig. S5 in the Supplementary Appendix). Plasma was tested for the presence of FTC and
> tenofovir (TFV), and peripheral-blood mononuclear cells were tested for FTC triphosphate
> (FTC-TP) and TFV diphosphate (TFV-DP), which are the active intracellular metabolites of FTC and
> TFV, respectively, with the use of validated liquid chromatography and tandem mass spectrometry
> assays."

That is the whole subsection. It is **(ii): a prespecified seroconversion-matched case–control
subgroup**, matched on study site, 1 case to 2 controls with one control drawn from each arm. It
is emphatically **not** "all placebo participants at scheduled visits". The subsection states no
statistical method and does not state which visit's specimen was tested; the only visit anchoring
in the paper is the Results sentence about cases ("median time between the tested specimen date
and the last uninfected visit was 35 days").

So the placebo zero is a **subgroup finding on a case-anchored, site-matched sample**, not a
trial-wide prevalence of drug exposure in the placebo arm.

### (d) Does the paper re-estimate efficacy for placebo-arm exposure?

**No.**

What the paper does re-estimate is a **within-arm** contrast, among FTC–TDF subjects only
(reconstruction from 5 consecutive segments; junctions verified at "with | those without",
"12.9 ... P<0.001), | corresponding", "reduction was 95% | (95% CI, 70 to 99"):

> "In the FTC–TDF group, among subjects with a detectable study-drug level, as compared with those
> without a detectable level, the odds of HIV infection were lower by a factor of 12.9 (95% CI,
> 1.7 to 99.3; P<0.001), corresponding to a relative reduction in HIV risk of 92% (95% CI, 40 to
> 99; P<0.001). After adjustment for reported unprotected receptive anal intercourse, the relative
> risk reduction was 95% (95% CI, 70 to 99; P<0.001)."

The comparator there is **non-detectable FTC–TDF subjects**, not the placebo group. The headline
estimate — from the Abstract, "100 became infected during follow-up (36 in the FTC–TDF group and
64 in the placebo group)" … "indicating a 44% reduction in the incidence of HIV (95% confidence
interval, 15 to 63; P=0.005)" — is **never re-estimated, re-weighted or sensitivity-analysed for
placebo-arm drug exposure**.

Nor does the paper discuss the scenario at all. On a whole-document check for placebo-group
participants obtaining antiretrovirals or PrEP outside the trial: **no such sentence exists**. The
nearest text concerns in-trial post-exposure prophylaxis — "so that they could receive
postexposure prophylaxis for HIV (P=0.28)" — which is a different thing.

### (f) Assay and limits

Assay, as quoted in full in (c): plasma assayed for **FTC and tenofovir (TFV)**; peripheral-blood
mononuclear cells assayed for **FTC triphosphate (FTC-TP) and TFV diphosphate (TFV-DP)**, "with
the use of validated liquid chromatography and tandem mass spectrometry assays." No assay vendor,
platform or validation citation is printed in the main-text subsection.

**Limit of detection / quantification: the main text prints none.** No LOD, no LLOQ, no units, no
threshold, for any of the four analytes. The **only** analytical limit printed anywhere in the
paper is for a different assay — HIV RNA, 40 copies per millilitre. The paper **DEFERS** the assay
characteristics to the appendix, and the one assay property it does state is a persistence window,
not a threshold (reconstruction from 2 consecutive segments; junction verified at
"14 days or more | after the last dose"):

> "The intracellular assay that was used in this study is expected to detect TFV-DP for 14 days or
> more after the last dose of TDF is taken (see Methods in the Supplementary Appendix)."

Because the drug-level result is reported as a **dichotomy** ("detected" / "no drug was
detected"), and no detection threshold is printed, the placebo zero has **no stated analytical
threshold** in the main text. Per instruction (8) I cannot re-derive it at its own author's
threshold, because its own author did not state one here.

Discussion limitations the paper does state about the drug-level analysis:

> "Although reported pill use was high, drug exposure that was measured objectively was
> substantially lower."
> "The testing of a larger number of specimens, from more subjects at more times, is needed to
> better define the minimum protective drug concentration."
> "Protective drug levels may differ according to the type of exposure (rectal vs. penile)."

The second of these is the paper itself saying its drug-level sample was too small and too sparsely
timed — relevant to (b), and notably it is phrased about defining a protective concentration, not
about the placebo arm.

## REGIME

**The trial's primary regime.** Population: HIV-seronegative men and transgender women who have
sex with men, at 11 sites in six countries (Peru, Ecuador, Brazil, South Africa, Thailand, USA),
at high risk of HIV acquisition. Unit of analysis: the randomized participant. Endpoint: emergent
HIV-1 infection during follow-up. Comparator: daily oral FTC–TDF versus matching placebo,
double-blind. Estimand: relative reduction in HIV incidence, modified intention-to-treat —
44% (95% CI, 15 to 63; P=0.005) on 36 vs 64 infections.

**The drug-level subgroup's regime, which is narrower in every dimension.** Population: not the
randomized cohort, but a **prespecified matched case–control subgroup** — each HIV-infected
subject matched to two seronegative controls, one per arm, matched on study site. Unit of
analysis: the **specimen**, one per subject, case-anchored (in cases, a median of 35 days, IQR 28
to 56, before the last uninfected visit). Instrument: validated LC-MS/MS on plasma (FTC, TFV) and
PBMC (FTC-TP, TFV-DP). Threshold: **not stated** — the readout is a bare detected/not-detected
dichotomy. Category: presence of at least one study-drug component. Comparator for the conditional
estimate: **detectable versus non-detectable within the FTC–TDF arm**. Estimand there: an odds
ratio for HIV infection, 12.9 (95% CI, 1.7 to 99.3), re-expressed as a 92% relative risk reduction
(95% CI, 40 to 99), 95% (95% CI, 70 to 99) after adjustment for reported unprotected receptive
anal intercourse.

**Does the claim handed to me sit inside that regime?** Partly, and the part that does not is the
part that matters.

- Sub-claim (a) sits inside it: the string exists exactly as quoted.
- Sub-claim (c)'s second option is the correct one, and that answer **relocates** the finding: a
  zero established in a site-matched, case-anchored case–control subgroup is **silent** about
  trial-wide placebo-arm drug exposure. Using it as "no placebo participant in iPrEx had drug in
  them" is the regime error — a subgroup prevalence cited as a cohort prevalence.
- Sub-claim (b) fails inside the regime too: the subgroup's own placebo N is not printed, so even
  the subgroup-level rate is unquantified in the main text.
- Sub-claim (d) is a **comparator** mismatch to watch for downstream: the 92%/95% figures are
  within-arm (detectable vs non-detectable FTC–TDF). They are frequently retold as "efficacy with
  good adherence versus placebo", which is a different contrast and one the paper did not run. The
  retelling leans on the placebo zero as an unstated assumption — which is precisely the
  undenominated number.
- Sub-claim (f): no threshold is stated, so "no drug detected" carries no stated uncertainty and no
  stated sensitivity. The assay's 14-day-or-more persistence window is about how long drug stays
  detectable after a dose, not about how little drug the assay can see.

## DEFECTS FOUND

1. **Undenominated zero (the central defect).** "No drug was detected in any plasma or cell
   specimens from subjects in the placebo group" is a zero numerator with **no denominator
   anywhere in the main text** — no participant count, no specimen count, no figure panel. The
   paper MEASURES the placebo specimens and does not PRINT the count. Published is not performed
   in reverse here: the analysis was performed and the number was not published.

2. **The earlier agent's deferral claim is wrong on its face.** The main text does **not** refer
   the placebo count to Fig. S5. Fig. S5 is cited once, in Methods, for the **matching scheme**.
   The main text omits the placebo denominator rather than deferring it. This matters because
   "deferred to the appendix" and "omitted" lead to different ledger conclusions.

3. **Regime slip from subgroup to trial.** The sampling frame is a prespecified
   seroconversion-matched case–control subgroup, site-matched, 1:2, with case-anchored specimens —
   not all placebo participants at scheduled visits. Any use of the zero as a trial-wide placebo
   prevalence is outside the regime in which it was established.

4. **Comparator mismatch in the conditional estimate.** The 92% (95% CI, 40 to 99) and 95% (95%
   CI, 70 to 99) reductions are **detectable vs non-detectable within FTC–TDF**, not vs placebo.
   The paper does **not** re-estimate the headline 44% (95% CI, 15 to 63) for placebo-arm
   exposure, and runs no sensitivity analysis on it.

5. **No stated analytical threshold.** The paper prints no LOD and no LLOQ for FTC, TFV, FTC-TP or
   TFV-DP, in any units. The only limit printed in the paper is HIV RNA's 40 copies/mL — a
   different assay. A "not detected" dichotomy without a printed threshold cannot be re-derived at
   its own author's threshold.

6. **Internal numeric inconsistency in the source, reported as served and not corrected.** The
   Results text reads "22 of 43 seronegative control subjects (51%)" while the Figure 4 panel
   label reads "22/42 Detectable". Both as served from the kimirina mirror. I flag the
   discrepancy; I do not pick a value. (51% is consistent with 22/43; 22/42 would be 52%.)

7. **Figure 4 title/body mismatch in the source, as served.** The legend is titled "Levels of
   Study-Drug Components in **Serum** …" while the legend body and the Methods and Results all
   describe **plasma** and peripheral-blood mononuclear cells. Reported as served.

8. **Citation not verified from front matter.** The mirror is the online-first version and prints
   "N Engl J Med 2010" with no volume and no pages. The ledger's "363:2587–2599" was taken from a
   short form, not from front matter I read. Not asserted wrong — **unverified at this route**.

9. **A non-defect, stated so it is not mistaken for one.** The paper's silence on placebo-arm
   participants sourcing antiretrovirals outside the trial is silence, not a prohibition and not a
   denial. Equally, the Discussion's note that "the testing of a larger number of specimens, from
   more subjects at more times, is needed" is the authors flagging insufficiency for defining a
   **minimum protective concentration** — a different thing from flagging the missing placebo
   denominator. That sentence must not be cited as the authors acknowledging the gap checked here.

## BEARING ON THE LEDGER ENTRY

**The source is real, open at the mirror, and the quoted string is genuine.** Sub-claim (a) is
confirmed verbatim, with the flanking sentences reproduced, and the quotation is not a
grammar-at-the-cut misquotation — it is a whole sentence.

**The gap the ledger entry was sent to check is real and is of the "published is not performed"
family, inverted:** the assay *was* performed on placebo specimens — the sentence reports its
result — but the **denominator was never printed**. On the test the instructions set for a
quantity question, I cannot name a number or a table, figure or sentence that holds it, because
none exists in the main text. What the main text does is **ASSERT** a zero. So the ledger may say,
with the main text alone behind it: iPrEx states a zero placebo-arm drug-detection result and
attaches no N to it.

**Two of the ledger's framings need tightening before the entry is sound.**

1. The entry should not say the count is "deferred to Fig. S5". It is **omitted**; Fig. S5 is cited
   for the matching scheme. Writing "deferred" implies the number exists one hop away, which this
   read does not support.
2. The entry should not treat the zero as a trial-wide placebo-arm figure. Its regime is a
   prespecified, site-matched, case-anchored **case–control subgroup**. This is the scope point
   that decides the entry: as a subgroup finding the zero is modest and the missing N is a
   reporting gap; as a trial-wide prevalence it would be a much stronger claim, and the paper does
   not make it.

**The strongest downstream consequence is sub-claim (d), and it is a clean "no".** The paper never
re-estimates its 44% (95% CI, 15 to 63) for placebo-arm exposure, and never discusses placebo

**within-FTC–TDF** contrast. When those figures are retold as "efficacy among the adherent versus
placebo", the retelling silently assumes the undenominated placebo zero. That chain — unprinted
denominator → unexamined assumption → widely cited conditional efficacy — is the substance of the
entry, and it survives this check.
