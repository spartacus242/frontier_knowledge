## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

A robot lab ran a batch of attempted inorganic syntheses, decided by itself from each X-ray powder pattern whether the intended compound had been made, and kept both the raw patterns and the per-sample number its matching software produced. Question: (a) if you hand those same patterns — all of them, including the attempts the robot called failures — to three or more crystallographers who each tick *target present / absent / indeterminate* against a stated minor-phase weight-fraction cut-off, without knowing what the robot said, what the paper claimed, or what the other readers said, how often does the robot's call differ from the readers' majority call, and how does that difference compare with how often the readers differ from each other; and (b) is the robot's own match number a good enough predictor of that majority verdict — right slope, right Brier score, right AUC — that a campaign still in progress could compute its own false-discovery rate live, from data it is already collecting, without anyone re-reading anything?

In short: treat the loop as a diagnostic test, make a blinded human panel the (knowingly imperfect) reference standard, and ask for an error rate with an error bar plus a calibration curve.

## Pages opened (4 fetches, cap 4)

- **https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.12113** — *Toward Trustworthy Autonomous Science: A Two-Year Community Roadmap* (2026). Cites the Szymanski correction, says it "walked back its central novelty claims" and that a compound "leaked from training data"; notes a case where "the automated structural refinement was below the quality a human expert would accept". Recommends that "a high-consequence action should additionally be checked against independent measurements that the agent does not control" and that "instrument-level outputs should carry the provenance needed to audit downstream claims". **Contains no blinded multi-expert re-read, no inter-expert kappa, no calibration metrics (no reliability diagram/Brier), no FDR estimation, and reports no measured agreement or error rate of its own.** Decisive both ways: it does not pre-empt the candidate, and it is strong new evidence of a real audience reaching for this concern in non-metrological vocabulary.
- **https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12872444** — P1 **verified exactly**: Author Correction, *Nature* 2026 Jan 19; **650(8100):E1**, doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09992-y, PMID 41554984, correcting *Nature* 624:86. Wording: authors "have confirmed that the prediction platform came to the correct conclusion in 36 of its 40 reported successes, with 4 compounds being inconclusive"; re-analysis was **manual**, **blinding not stated**, **analyst count not stated**, **failed targets not re-analysed**, **no agreement statistic, no CI, nothing on the platform's internal score**. Every structural deficit the record attributes to P1 is confirmed from the source.
- **https://journals.iucr.org/j/issues/2001/04/00/hw0085/** — IUCr CPD quantitative-phase-analysis round robin (sample 1 suite). Confirms the record's unopened-lead characterisation and more: participants analysed corundum/fluorite/zincite mixtures, **many of them the identical CPD-supplied dataset** (59 analyses of the supplied data for sample 1g vs 123 of own data). **No qualitative phase identification is reported: no false-identification rate, no missed-phase count, no kappa or percent-agreement statistic, no comparison against automated analysis.** But it does supply a damaging adjacent number: on *identical* data, a phase weighed at 31.37 wt% was returned with mean 31.55, SD 3.30 and **range 22.40–48.60 wt%**, and some returns reported **0.00 wt% for a phase present at ~1.15 wt%**. So analyst-to-analyst scatter on identical patterns is already known to be large, and minor-phase misses already documented — qualitatively, though not as an identification rate.
- **https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27883** — *False-science induction in autonomous scientific discovery*. About coherent object/measurement mis-pairing corrupting neural surrogates in closed loops (GFP-fitness and band-gap loops), concluding "error coherence, rather than raw error frequency" drives budget misallocation; proposes quarantines and feedback-conflict triage. **No measured FDR for a real campaign, no blinded expert re-read, no inter-expert agreement, no calibration against expert labels, no A-Lab discussion.** The record's flagged hole is closed in the record's favour.
