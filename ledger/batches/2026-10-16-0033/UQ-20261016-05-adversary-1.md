## Restatement (my own words)

Take real clinical datasets that were analysed with multiple imputation while some values were genuinely missing, and in which those missing values were afterwards filled in from an outside source (linkage, chart pull, registry update). Check two things: (1) how often the recovered value lands inside the 95% interval MI had drawn for it, and (2) how far the MI-based estimate of the quantity the study cared about sits from the estimate you get once the recovered values are in — with complete-case and a pre-specified MNAR model alongside. The record's thesis is that this audit has never been run in medicine because the field misread "MAR is not identifiable from observed data" as "MAR cannot be tested".
