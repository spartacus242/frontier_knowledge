# UQ-20261016-33 — Adversary 1 log

## Restatement (my own words)
Take one hospital ward or OR suite served by an automated dispensing cabinet and one drug class (say injectable fentanyl). Each month, add up what came in and subtract what the records say went out (charted administrations, witnessed-waste attestations, returns, closing stock). Treat the leftover as a "missing material" statistic with an honest error budget in which every term is either a measured variance or, where the record is only a human attestation, a bound. Then ask: how much sustained monthly theft would a reviewer, a vendor alarm, or a CUSUM-type sequential test catch 95% of the time while false-alarming only once a year per ward — and is that threshold above or below the monthly rates at which convicted diverters were actually stealing?
