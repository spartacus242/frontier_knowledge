# Adversary #2 — UQ-20261016-48

## Restatement (my own words, before engaging the record's framing)

Take an occasion where the same frozen model checkpoint was handed to two or more separate
organisations to attack, under access and time budgets we can document. Two questions.

First, a bookkeeping question that nobody has bookkept: write down a rule, in advance, for when
team A's finding and team B's finding count as *the same kind of failure*; have blinded raters
apply it; report how often the teams landed on the same class, pairwise and jointly; report how
reliable the raters were on the matching itself; and redo it with a coarser and a finer rule to
show the number is not an artefact of where you drew the line.

Second, a much stronger question: feed that overlap into the machinery ecologists and software
inspectors use to estimate what a sample missed — how many failure classes did the exercise not
find? With two honest concessions built in: the target is not "all failure modes" but "the classes
reachable at this much effort with these tools", and the estimate only counts if a pre-declared
vacuity check passes (the upper confidence bound is finite, the interval excludes "arbitrarily
many"), fitted in at least two estimator families, one allowing some failures to be much harder to
find than others, one allowing the teams' hit rates to be correlated.

So: a rediscovery rate for red teams, and a missing-mass estimate built on it that is honest about
why it should not work.

### F3 — spot-check of the record's nearest prior (N1)

**The prior is real and the record describes it accurately.** "How Vulnerable Are AI Agents to
Indirect Prompt Injections? Insights from a Large-Scale Public Competition", Dziemian, Lin, Fu,
Nowak et al. (Gray Swan AI), arXiv:2603.15714v1, 16 Mar 2026. On the four things I tested:

- No cross-participant rediscovery/duplicate/overlap statistic. Dedup is exact-match only:
  "we removed duplicate attacks identified via MD5 hashing for the same model and behavior pair."
- No estimate of total or remaining distinct attacks; no capture-recapture, Chao1, species
  richness, missing mass or discovery-curve extrapolation.
- No inter-rater reliability, and the near-duplicate rule is an unvalidated threshold: attacks are
  linked at "cosine similarity greater than 0.8", and the paper does not validate that threshold
  as a test of whether two attacks are the same.
- On saturation: "Break counts increase approximately linearly for all models"; the curves
  "confirm that even the more robust models remain susceptible under sustained adversarial
  pressure."
- The record's list-dependence corroboration checks out verbatim: "participants may have converged
  on similar attack template due to shared familiarity with known techniques."

So the generator did not overstate its own nearest neighbour, and N1 does not pre-empt either limb.
**Scope note on the linear break counts:** I considered using this against limb (b)'s non-vacuity
and will not. Break counts are *successful attack events*, not *distinct failure classes*; a linear
event curve does not entail a non-saturating class-accumulation curve, and I have no primary source
open on the relation between the two. I record it as an unresolved risk to limb (b), not as a kill.

### F4 — the finding that does the damage

`C05_Safety_Evals_Course/03_redteam_methodology/03_讲解.html`, a module (marked "MODULE 03 / 8") of
a frontier-AI safety-evaluations course, in Chinese. **This is in the candidate's own field and it
poses and computes the candidate's limb (b).** Quoting it:

- It asks the missing-mass question explicitly and says eyeballing is not good enough: "怎样把
  '还剩多少'算出来而非靠眼睛" — *how do we compute "how many are left" rather than going by eye?*
- Discovery curve over cumulative probes vs. cumulative distinct weaknesses ("横轴是累积探索次数，
  纵轴是累积不同弱点数"), with the warning that a long tail can make it flatten falsely
  ("不能只看眼睛").
- **Pooled rarefaction across several red-teamers** — merge their findings and subsample — used to
  compare "三人合并" (three people pooled) against "单人三倍努力" (one person at triple effort),
  because different people "覆盖不同区域". *That is the record's own named decision — commission an
  Nth team or extend an existing team's budget — set up as a computation.*
- Chao1 as a lower bound on the total: `Ŝ_Chao1 = S_obs + f1²/(2f2)`, bias-corrected form for
  f2 = 0, "Chao1 是下界估计", with the singleton intuition "singletons 越多……说明长尾里藏着越多还
  没撞见的——剩余越大".
- A stopping rule requiring both K consecutive rounds with no new findings **and** a small estimated
  remainder ("连续 K 轮探索无新发现" + "估计剩余量足够小").
- §8.2 **names the two violations the record treats as the novel interface problem**: "Chao1 等
  估计量假设'采样独立、频率结构稳定'", broken when "红队成员之间互相启发（非独立采样）" (red-teamers
  inspire each other — non-independent sampling = the record's P6) and when "模型在红队过程中被持续
  修复（总体在变动）" (the model is patched during the campaign, population in flux = the record's P4).
- Heterogeneous catchability is in the setup rather than named: frequencies are "幂律 / 重尾" and
  "长尾低频弱点极难撞见"; the companion notebook simulates `S_TRUE = 50` weaknesses under that
  distribution and prints "估计剩余未发现" against the true remainder.
- It teaches inter-rater reliability — **but only for severity**: "在把 severity 用于排序前，必须先
  量化评分者间一致性（inter-rater reliability）", weighted Cohen's κ. For individuation it offers
  only "去重" (deduplicate) as a triage step, **with no criterion**.
- Cites Perez 2022, Ganguli 2022, Mazeika 2024 (HarmBench). **No citation at all for the Chao1
  application** — consistent with the course author importing it directly, i.e. consistent with no
  published anchor existing.

**Provenance caveat, stated plainly.** No author, no date, no version in the content; latest cited
paper is 2024; I cannot determine whether it is human- or machine-written, and the repository is
unvetted. I read it in full via raw.githubusercontent.com but could not corroborate it. A verifier
who judges that unvetted teaching material is not "the literature or public discourse" should
downgrade my attack-7 kill from Unresolved to Partial; I note that explicitly rather than hide it.

## Unexecuted Disguise leads — for whoever reruns Gate 4 with a search budget

- **L1 (highest priority) HCI, the evaluator effect.** Hertzum & Jacobsen, "The Evaluator Effect: A
  Chilling Fact About Usability Evaluation Methods", IJHCI (citation seen in a GitHub reference list,
  G2; **paper not opened**); Nielsen & Landauer's problem-discovery model and the estimate of the
  total number of problems; "problem matching" reliability; Woolrych & Cockton and Hornbæk & Frøkjær
  on heterogeneous detectability breaking the estimate. This is the candidate's whole structure —
  independent evaluators, one artifact, pairwise overlap, total-problem estimate, matching
  reliability as the weak link — one field over and decades old. If it has been carried into LLM
  evaluation, the candidate is dead; if not, the candidate is a transplant from a field whose own
  verdict on the method may already settle limb (b).
- **L2 Software inspection with teams, not inspectors: "N-fold inspection"** (Martin & Tsai lineage).
  A GitHub method-audit log (G1/G10, `renchris/claude-infrastructure`
  `docs/research/upfront-method-audit-2026-10-04/evidence/lens-prior-art.md`) summarises it as
  "Independent teams find different faults" with per-N detection rates attributed to Kantorowitz,
  Guttman & Arzi (35.1% at N=1 rising to 77.8% at N=9). **I did not open that source and rely on
  nothing from it** — but Gate 2 searched inspection-with-capture-recapture, not N-fold inspection,
  and N-fold is the independent-*teams* vocabulary.
- **L3 Fuzzing / testing as species discovery.** Böhme's STADS line and residual-risk /
  discovery-probability estimation, plus bug-deduplication-by-stack-hash as a de facto equivalence
  relation. The closest mature treatment of a *generator-conditioned, non-closed* discovery
  population — i.e. the exact difficulty the record claims is new. Both my attempts to reach it
  were blocked.
- **L4 Qualitative research:** thematic saturation estimation, inter-coder reliability on code
  matching, "information power".
- **L5 The "fraction already computed" limb in platform vocabulary:** bug-bounty duplicate rates
  published by platforms.
- **L6 Non-English and grey pedagogy** — the one on-point hit I got came from Chinese-language
  course material, so this vein is productive and barely scratched.
