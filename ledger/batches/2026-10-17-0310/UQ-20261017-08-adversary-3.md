## Restatement (my own words)

(a) Census: among the most-downloaded base-model repositories on the Hugging Face hub plus every self-declared fully-open family, what fraction actually publishes resumable Adam/AdamW moment tensors (m, v), at what step granularity, in what format, under what licence, and with coordinates aligned to the released weights?
(b) Measurement: when a release ships a checkpoint's Adam moments next to its weights, how much more does an attacker learn about the data seen in the last few-to-few-thousand steps before that checkpoint — membership of a given document, presence of a data source, recoverable canary text — than the identical attack (same family, compute, shadow models, query budget) learns from the weights alone, and how fast does that excess decay with the number of steps k since the data was seen?

The core intellectual claim is that stored Adam moments are a disclosure channel distinct from the weights, in a regime (un-noised EMA over ~2M-token batches, white-box, one checkpoint) that neither the FL gradient-inversion literature nor the release-engineering literature covers.
