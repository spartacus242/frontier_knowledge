## Restatement (my own words)

Hold one open-weight checkpoint and one multiple-choice / exact-match benchmark fixed, with the answer-extraction rule pinned, and run it at batch size 1 offline under four kinds of change the field treats as not changing the model: different quantization recipes at the same bit width, a release-dtype cast (bf16 vs fp16), different attention-kernel implementations (eager / SDPA / FlashAttention), and different decode seeds at T>0. Question: are the questions whose correctness flips the SAME questions across these different kinds of change — a common "fragile stratum" — once each question is allowed its own flip rate estimated from replicates within a kind; does that sharing survive conditioning on how close the top-two logits were; and how much of the headline score sits on those questions? In R1 the estimand is sharpened to the between-TYPE variance component of per-item flip propensity in a two-level model, with the within-TYPE component as the null's own calibration.

In plain terms: is numeric fragility a property of the question or of the perturbation, and is it just "small logit margin" wearing a new name.

## Novelty, independently re-checked

The three priors are real; A and B are accurately characterised at the abstract level, including the 95.7% figure (the 0.85 config-lucky fraction is full-text-only and unconfirmed by me). Both, however, are aimed at adjacent objects against the candidate's unit+axis pair: A's axis is harness configuration with "the weights... stay fixed", B's unit is the tool-call/safety decision, not the MCQ item. The Gate 2 Low grade therefore holds for limbs 2 (share-of-score, published for the harness-config family) and 3 (margin predicts flips, published for the quantization family), and it does NOT reach limb 1, the cross-type concordance estimand. The paper best placed to reach limb 1 — 2609.04748, which Gate 2 could not read — turns out to be episode-level with no concordance statistic, so clearing it strengthened rather than weakened the candidate's residual. Nine fresh vocabularies found nothing on the object. Composite novelty: **Low** as graded (two of three limbs pre-empted); residual-limb novelty: Medium.
