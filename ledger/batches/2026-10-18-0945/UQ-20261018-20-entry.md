Domain(s): Biology — cell biology (regulated cell death, tissue homeostasis; non-clinical). Method-owning neighbours: quantitative histology / tissue kinetics, quantitative image cytometry, whole-body turnover accounting.

Question (canonical): For one declared tissue in a declared species, strain, sex, age and housing condition, assayed in a declared physiological state with a pre-declared in situ marker panel whose per-modality sensitivity and specificity have been measured in that same tissue against induced positive controls: what is the per-modality prevalence of marker-positive dying cells per unit tissue (with intervals, an explicit multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual), and — where a modality's corpse-persistence time is independently measured or bounded in that tissue — the implied per-modality death incidence per unit tissue per day, reconciled against an independently measured total turnover? (V2, the graded version; V1 as intaken: what share of in vivo physiological cell death is apoptotic and what share proceeds by each non-apoptotic regulated modality.)

Alternative formulations:
- The field has defined a dozen ways a cell can die; which ones actually kill cells in a normal body, and in what proportions?
- For a declared tissue and a declared modality-marker panel, what is the per-modality incidence of cell death per unit tissue per day once each modality's corpse-clearance time is accounted for?
- (lay) Of the cells a body loses per day, how many die by each named death programme, and on what measurement does that attribution rest?

Motivating gap: The regulated-cell-death literature is dense with how-answers and thin on how-much for declared solid tissues. The authoritative whole-body accounting (Sender & Milo 2021, read: 0.33 +/- 0.02 x 10^12 cells/day, 80 +/- 20 g/day) splits the total only by disposal route, in one Methods sentence (CORRECTED ON VERIFICATION: "assigns no death modality anywhere", as this entry first put it, is wrong at the sentence level — the sentence names apoptosis and phagocytosis, and a flat no would be false; what holds is the narrower claim that the paper's ACCOUNTING is modality-blind, since Figs. 1b and 2b, the two panels that are the turnover accounting, are split by cell type and tissue and by nothing else. The sentence reads "About half of cells replaced daily go through apoptosis and phagocytosis by tissue macrophages", with "the other half" being gastrointestinal and skin cells "which are shed"; it sits in the last of nine Methods subsections, on power consumption, among costs left OUT of the energy estimate, carries no citation, and is followed by the authors' assumption that these processes "do not have a dramatic effect on the overall calculation". A request to transcribe that paragraph raw was refused by the summariser, so whether it is an assumption or a claim is summariser-characterised and is the weakest link in this reading); the field's own in vivo methods introduction (Newbold et al. 2014, read; re-verified un-primed by adversary 3 and again by a third independent reader on post-batch verification) attributes "billions of cells per day" to apoptosis. CORRECTED ON VERIFICATION: the verbatim string is the document's, and its construction is "the loss of billions of cells per day by apoptosis in the human body" — whole organism, per day, an order of magnitude with no number, range or uncertainty attached, and whether it is measured, calculated or cited cannot be determined, because it is a bare background premise in the abstract's third sentence and the body is paywalled. But the clause built around it was this entry's gloss and not the document's claim. The document writes "the number of apoptotic cells found in healthy tissue is surprisingly low and reflects the efficiency of this process", where "this process" refers back to the phagocyte recruitment and clearance of its preceding sentence. So the referent is clearance, but "clearance efficiency" is not the document's phrase; "rather than the death rate" appears nowhere and is an inference from the concessive "Despite"; and "conceding" is this entry's framing — the document presents the point as unremarkable background and immediately pivots to using it to explain why apoptotic cells become visible in treated tumours. What the document does do, and what matters here, is set a whole-body per-day RATE against a per-tissue COUNT without reconciling them numerically, which is this entry's own prevalence-versus-incidence problem appearing unremarked in the field's own methods introduction. The partition is the denominator of every claim that a modality matters physiologically and of every modality-knockout phenotype read in healthy tissue.

Presuppositions: (1) modalities mutually exclusive per dying cell — CONTESTED, handled by construction (declared, possibly overlapping marker partition; multi-positive fraction and unclassified residual reported); (2) modality-specific markers specific enough to classify a single dying cell in situ — CONTESTED, the binding feasibility constraint; residual worry that calibration on induced death may not transfer to spontaneous death; (3) per-modality corpse-clearance times estimable, so prevalence converts to incidence — CONTESTED, incidence made conditional; (4) 'physiological' declarable by stated criteria — ESTABLISHED as a convention; (5) total tissue death incidence independently estimable — ESTABLISHED, wide intervals; (6) partition stable rather than diurnal/dietary/age-dependent — FLAGGED, changes the estimand's form not its answerability; (7) no modality systematically missed because corpses clear faster than markers persist — CONTESTED, boundable only; (8) ADDED AT GATE 4 (adversary 1), not in the Gate 0-3 record: that physiological loss presents as marker-positive dying cells at all — modalities that leave no corpse (removal of a living cell by phagocytosis) cannot be scored by any in situ panel and would not land in a residual defined over detected dying cells; the reconciliation deficit against total turnover is the only place such deaths appear, and the entry must say so.

What would count as an answer: per-modality marker-positive prevalence per unit tissue with intervals, a reported multi-positive fraction and an explicit unclassified residual, for a declared tissue and a pre-declared panel with in-tissue sensitivity/specificity; plus, where clearance/persistence times are measured or bounded, conversion to per-modality incidence per day reconciled against an independent turnover total. Prevalence alone, with the clearance confound bounded and stated, is a partial but publishable answer and must not be inflated into incidence. A null in which no panel reaches classifying specificity in situ is also an answer, about the field's measurement capability.
