Question (canonical): For the open-weight masked-diffusion language models now released at 7–8B scale, how loose is the variational bound their reported perplexities are computed from — what is the gap, in nats per token, between the published bound and an unbiased or sandwiched estimate of the model's actual log-likelihood — and does closing that gap change the diffusion-versus-autoregressive comparisons those perplexities are used to support?
