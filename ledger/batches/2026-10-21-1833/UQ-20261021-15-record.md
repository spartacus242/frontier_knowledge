## Gate 0 — Intake

Question (canonical, V0 as intaken): For retired commercial model snapshots whose per-item outputs survive in public research artifacts, how far does the vendor-designated successor stand from the model it replaced on those same items, and what share of the published conclusions drawn from the retired snapshot reverse when re-derived on the successor?

Alternative formulations:
- When a vendor retires a model and points you at its replacement, is the replacement a substitute for the purposes of the papers that used the original?
- At what rate does carrying a result forward from a retired model snapshot to its designated successor fail?

Motivating gap (source method: silence mapping): Retirement is dense with how-answers — deprecation calendars, migration guides, successor tables, "use this model instead" notices — and the field has begun to count the exposure, but the intake's claim was that nobody asks whether the prescribed migration preserves the finding. The census limb is DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED from this candidate: arXiv:2609.04699, "Model Retirement Creates Reproducibility Risk in Biomedical AI Publications" (READ LEVEL per intake: abstract read in full from the arXiv abs page; not re-opened in this record, so it grounds only the intake's own quoted figures). REGIME of that census: population = PubMed original research articles 2022–March 2026 applying a specific LLM to a biomedical task, 50 most-used models; unit = the paper-model mention (8,931 mentions / 5,242 publications); instrument = vendor lifecycle data joined to agent-extracted model names; endpoint = exposed share (42%) and median publication-to-retirement interval (538 days); comparator = none, descriptive; estimand = exposure, not behaviour. It measures no model behaviour, no distance to a successor, and re-derives no published conclusion.

## Gate 1 — Well-posedness

One reformulation used, for well-posedness only. Both versions recorded.

V0: the intake canonical above. Defect: it names neither the comparator against which "how far" is measured (predecessor's own run-to-run band versus zero) nor the unit on which the share is taken, so limb (b) could be satisfied by an uninterpretable number.

R1 (adopted, the version graded): Over an enumerated frame of published studies that applied a since-retired commercial model snapshot and released per-item outputs plus a recoverable configuration: (a) what is the per-item agreement between the archived predecessor outputs and freshly collected outputs from the vendor-designated successor named in dated vendor documentation, under the recorded configuration, stratified by task class, with a declared distance per output type (exact match; graded; free text under a named scorer whose own reliability is reported), read against the predecessor's own archived run-to-run band where two archived runs exist; and (b) under a pre-declared rule mapping each paper's own stated claim and its own threshold onto a re-derivable decision on the same items, with inter-rater reliability on the mapping, what share of those studies' conclusions reverse, with a confidence interval — the excluded share of the frame and the exclusion reasons being part of the answer?

What would count as an answer: (i) an enumerated frame of retired-snapshot studies holding archived per-item outputs and a recoverable configuration, with excluded share and exclusion reasons; (ii) per-item predecessor-versus-successor agreement under the recorded configuration, stratified by task class, with CIs and a declared distance per output type; (iii) a conclusion-level re-derivation under the pre-declared claim-mapping rule with IRR, reporting the reversal rate with a CI; (iv) the negative control of predecessor-versus-predecessor repeat runs, so successor distance is read against the predecessor's own band. A clean negative counts: successors agreeing inside that band would license "rerun on the successor" as a valid reproduction.
NOT answers: predecessor and successor aggregate scores from vendor release notes; a census of papers exposed to retirement; drift studies within a single snapshot's lifetime; a single case study without an enumerated frame.
It is a question, not a thesis: both the agreement direction and the reversal rate have admissible null outcomes that the design would report.

## Gate 3 — Significance

Consequence: If the answer is that successors sit inside the predecessor's own run-to-run band on these items, then "rerun it on the replacement" is licensed as a reproduction, the exposure that arXiv:2609.04699 counts at 42% of biomedical paper-model mentions is largely benign, and reproducibility policy can keep treating a successor run as evidence about the original claim. If the answer is that they do not, then a specific, countable share of the published record became unverifiable on the retirement date, every audit that re-examined a withdrawn deployment on its replacement has to be re-read, and the remedy shifts from migration guidance to output archiving or weight escrow. The two outcomes point at different policies, which is what makes it a question rather than a thesis.
Audience: journal and conference reproducibility policy bodies — the ACL reproducibility track already ships reporting standards (ReproEvalCard, ACL 2026 short 22, TITLE ONLY here), and a measured reversal rate is the input such a standard currently lacks when it decides whether a closed-snapshot result may be cited as reproduced; regulatory and internal auditors who must re-examine a deployed system after its snapshot is withdrawn and today have no basis for treating the successor's behaviour as evidence about the predecessor's; and the small number of groups maintaining artifact-review criteria, for whom "released per-item outputs" would become a load-bearing requirement rather than a courtesy. ARGUED, not asserted: the audience's current default is visible in print — arXiv:2510.25506 Sec. 5.1 frames deprecated-model usage as a reason to avoid commercial models, i.e. the field is already legislating on this without the measurement.
Fecundity: once a predecessor-successor distance is measured against a run-to-run band, these become askable — whether distance predicts reversal (so a cheap item-level check could substitute for re-derivation); whether vendor-published aggregate deltas in release notes predict the measured per-item distance at all, which would make release notes usable or not as migration evidence; whether distance is larger across a model-family boundary than within one; what minimum archived-output schema makes a result migration-checkable, which is a concrete artifact-policy question; and whether an archived-output escrow is cheaper than the reversal risk it averts.
Anchoring: the closed-model reproducibility complaint (AI Snake Oil on OpenAI's policies; Michaelov et al. on closed LLMs and science — both TITLE ONLY here); the model-retirement exposure census (arXiv:2609.04699) and the obsolescence-timing census (arXiv:2604.07530); the annotator-substitution instability results (arXiv:2602.11898, NBER w35110); and, as the method-owning neighbours whose vocabulary this question has never been posed in, behavioural breaking changes in API evolution (FSE 2024), significant-properties verification of migrated digital objects (Guttenbrunner), bridging and comparability studies for a replaced assay, and slice-wise non-regression certification of a model upgrade (arXiv:2609.13714, TITLE ONLY).
Significance score: 2/3. Real audience with a visible default to overturn, and genuinely divergent consequences; held off 3 because P5 is live — the field's reflex remedy (open weights, output archiving) is being adopted without this measurement, so a reviewer can hold the answer to be policy-irrelevant, and because arXiv:2510.25506's 5-of-69 executability suggests the frame may be too thin to carry a rate with a usable CI.

## Reason for silence

A disciplinary-boundary and instrument mechanism, with an incentive gap on top. The measurement needs three things that no single community holds: dated vendor lifecycle text (platform engineering), paper replication artifacts with per-item outputs (metascience and repositories), and funded live API access (a lab that by construction no longer has the predecessor). The predecessor is unqueryable the moment the question becomes urgent, so the only admissible instrument is the archived per-item output — and metascience treats released outputs as provenance or as supplementary material, not as a measurement device standing in for a withdrawn instrument, which is exactly the move archival science (significant properties of a migrated object) and clinical chemistry (bridging a replaced assay) made long ago in their own domains and which has not crossed over. The incentive gap closes the loop: the people best placed to run it are the original authors, who have the least reason to publish that their conclusion did not carry forward, while the vendors who publish the successor tables have no reason to fund a measurement of how far the successor stands from what it replaced. "No one thought of it" is not the mechanism and is not available here anyway — Gate 2 found the thought in print twice; what is missing is a party with the access, the instrument and a reason to spend.
