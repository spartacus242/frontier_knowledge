# UQ-20261016-03 — Adversary #1 log (Gate 4)

## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Of the olive trees in Apulia cut down because they themselves tested positive for *Xylella fastidiosa*, what share would — had they been left standing for about ten years — never have either (E1) handed the bacterium to a spittlebug or (E2) crossed a stated damage threshold? Treat that share as a parameter in an explicit loss function (trees lost vs. onward infections averted, with the public-good term written out) and ask which felling radius the loss function picks. Separately: of the *neighbours* felled by the radius rule, how many were uninfected or likewise harmless. The record insists the first quantity (true positives that would never have mattered) is the novel one and the second is already known.
