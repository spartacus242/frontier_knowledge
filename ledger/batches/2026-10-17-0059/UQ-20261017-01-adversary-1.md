# UQ-20261017-01 — Adversary 1 log (Gate 4)

## Restatement (my own words, not the generator's)
Take every archived gas-adsorption isotherm (NIST/ARPA-E ISODB or a journal SI file) that can be matched to the paper it came from, where that paper also prints a BET surface area computed from that very isotherm. Recompute the BET area from the archived curve with one fixed, pre-registered Rouquerol-criteria program (BETSI or equivalent, with tie-breaking and "no admissible window" rules fixed in advance). Call the signed relative gap Δ = (authors' number − program's number)/program's number. Two sub-questions: (a) is the average Δ positive, i.e. do authors' numbers run high relative to the mechanical reference; and (b) is Δ larger for samples whose RECOMPUTED area sits close to the highest area then on record for that class of material, after controlling for material class, year and isotherm quality? In plain terms: when a group's sample is within reach of a record, do they pick a BET fit window that nudges the number up?

No surface-area or BET key. isotherm_operations.py: "for key in copy.copy(isotherm): if key not in canonical_keys: del isotherm[key]" — non-canonical keys are stripped on ingest, so a reported BET area CANNOT be stored in an ISODB isotherm record. Searching the whole NIST-ISODB organisation for "surface area"/BET returns only paper titles in Bibliography/*.json (e.g. 10.1021ja512973b "Ultrahigh Surface Area Zirconium MOFs...", 10.1002adma.202201502).
E2 (digitisation). ISODB isotherm JSONs carry "articleSource": "Figure 4" / "Table 4" / "UNKNOWN" and a boolean "tabular_data" ("Convert the tabular_data boolean variable to integer"); sample records show "digitizer": "UNKNOWN", "date": "1000-01-01". I.e., a substantial, unquantified share of archived isotherms are digitised from published figures, not authors' raw point lists. (Counts by tabular_data could not be obtained; punctuation-qualified searches returned 0.)
E3 (Ambroz et al. 2018 Small Methods, opened). Review compiling experimental literature BET vs simulated/geometric areas for ~13 MOFs; uses authors' REPORTED values, does not recompute. Quotes: "the standard pressure range (0.05 < P/P0 < 0.30) should not be used for MOFs with pore sizes below 7 Å since the obtained BET surface areas can be incorrect"; "This can lead to obtaining higher or overestimated surface areas"; "High disagreement (≈33%) can be seen for NU-110 despite fulfilling the four consistency criteria"; for the NU-110x series "a tolerance (around 10%) was needed"; for NU-1103 (largest area in its series) the review attributes the excess over geometric to pore filling before monolayer completion ("the monolayer loading is overestimated resulting in excessive surface area"). No incentive/record covariate; not the candidate's question, but it bears on attacks 1, 4 and 8.
E4 (Osterrieth, chemRxiv abstract only). "wide range of values for each isotherm"; concern about "the reliability of reported BET areas"; no sign, no incentive language visible at abstract level.
