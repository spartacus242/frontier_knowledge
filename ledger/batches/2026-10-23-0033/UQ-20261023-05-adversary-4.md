## 1. Restatement in my own words

Take the published phylogenetics literature. Pair up studies that share enough taxa (after a declared
name-reconciliation step). Inside each pair, look only at clades that one paper printed with a support
label of at least 95% (bootstrap, UFBoot, or Bayesian posterior probability). Ask: how often is such a
clade logically incompatible — on the shared taxon set, under a declared bipartition-conflict test — with a
clade the *other* paper also printed at ≥95%? Report that rate separately per support type, stratified by
how much data the two studies share and by era. Then do two further things: (a) call that rate a lower
bound on the joint error of the two ≥95% labels, since two incompatible resolutions cannot both be right;
and (b) compare it with a per-method "nominal" benchmark declared in advance — i.e. ask whether a 95%
label behaves like a 95% claim.

So: a reliability/calibration audit of the field's own printed confidence labels, using one published study
as the check on another and needing no known truth. Limb 1 is descriptive (a rate). Limb 2 is normative
(the rate means the labels are miscalibrated by this much).

### C6 in full, and why it is a presupposition and not a measurand

The record lists this as presupposition 5 and grades it **"Arithmetic, not an assumption."** It is not
arithmetic. It is an assumption that the two ≥95% labels in a pair are claims about the *same* true tree.
V2's frame conditions on **shared taxa only** — it nowhere requires that the two studies estimate the same
genealogical object — and its declared stratifiers are **support type, data overlap and era**. Target object
is not among them. So the clause is assumed, never reported, and the measurand exemption does not reach it.

Over the cytoplasmic-versus-nuclear subset of that frame, the clause is false, on a document read in full:

> **Larson et al. (EcoEvoRxiv), full text** — verbatim, scoped by its own opener: *"Given that there is
> discordance between a cytoplasmic locus and the species tree"*, **"the observed discordance is due to
> error, ILS, and/or genetic exchange among lineages"**.
> **REGIME:** population = lineages with both cytoplasmic and nuclear data in published practice; unit of
> analysis = a well-supported branch of a cytDNA tree against a species tree treated as a *distinct object*;
> operating condition = empirical cytonuclear comparison; instrument = "well-supported branches" (the word
> "bootstrap" does not occur in the document); threshold = none stated; category = cause attribution of
> discordance. The candidate's frame **admits** this regime, because it conditions only on shared taxa.
> The clause my attack sits in **asserts** a three-way disjunction in which error is one disjunct among
> three — so discordance does **not** entail that either label is wrong. (Its neighbour sentence cuts the
> other way and I record it: *"Even with whole cytoplasmic genomes, one should not ignore gene tree
> inference error as a possible source of discordance"* — error is never excluded either. Both facts point
> the same way for C6: the cause is **not determined** by the discordance, so the discordance bounds nothing.)

Corroborated by a second full-text source, used strictly inside its own regime:

> **Lanfear & Hahn 2024, MBE 41(11):msae214, DOI 10.1093/molbev/msae214** (fetch 3, full text), Introduction:
> **"individual loci do not have to share the same topology with either the species tree or with each other"**;
> **"The biological processes that drive this variation include incomplete lineage sorting (ILS),
> introgression, and horizontal gene transfer"**; **"Duplication and loss can also generate gene trees that
> differ from species trees"**. The clause **asserts** that topological difference between trees is a normal
> property of distinct genealogical objects, not an error.
> **REGIME:** population = loci within a *single* large phylogenomic dataset (bird worked example reanalyses
> gene trees from Stiller et al. 2024); unit = a gene tree / branch inside one dataset; operating condition =
> ILS/introgression present; instrument = gene and site concordance factors contrasted with BP/PP; threshold

> **SCOPE DISCIPLINE, stated rather than skipped:** this paper's unit is a locus inside one dataset, **not a
> published study**. So I use it **only** for the claim that distinct genealogical objects legitimately carry
> distinct true topologies, and I **do not** transfer its *"many—sometimes most—gene trees in a large
> phylogenomic dataset will not match the species tree"* into any claim about the share of *published study
> pairs* that target different objects. That share is **not measured by me and not measured by this paper.**

**The consequence, labelled as my own arithmetic.** Let *T* = "the two studies of the pair target the same
true tree". Then
`P(contradiction) = P(contradiction & T) + P(contradiction & not-T)`.
Only the first term lower-bounds joint label error; the second is real biology plus target mismatch. So the
rate V2 defines bounds joint label error **from above within the mixture**, not from below — the inequality
V2 asserts runs in the wrong direction over its own declared frame. The bias adds to the upward bias the
record already concedes in presupposition 6 (contested clades attract re-study), so both known biases push
the same way and V2 supplies no stratum that separates them.

**Best response available to the candidate, and why it fails now.** Add a target-object stratifier
(organellar / single-locus / concatenated-nuclear / coalescent species tree). That is plausible — but it is
**unevidenced** (the record opened nothing bearing on whether data type is recoverable at corpus scale, and
its one document-kind check found support *type* machine-declared in **0 of 5** studies, which is adjacent
evidence that metadata of exactly this kind is absent), and per rule (iv) **the single permitted
reformulation is spent**, so the question as it now stands cannot acquire the stratifier. Reading "error of
the label" instead as "error of the claim the paper advances about the organisms" is the other escape, and
Larson is evidence against it: the field's own treatment of cytonuclear discordance is that the discordance
does not establish that anyone was wrong.

## 4. The leg of my kill that INVERTED on my own last fetch — recorded, not buried

I intended a **second, independent leg**: that the ≥95% label has no truth-referring nominal target at all,
so C5 fails and limb 2 has no referent. Three full-text sources appeared to support it — Felsenstein 1985
p. 786 (established fact 1): the interval contains **"not the true phylogeny, but the phylogeny that would be
estimated on repeated sampling of many characters from the underlying pool of characters"**; Thomson & Brown
2022 (fetch 1, p. 917): **"BPs and marginal PPs of bipartitions are both estimated as frequencies with which
a bipartition appears in a set of trees."**; Lanfear & Hahn 2024 (fetch 3): **"both are measures of sampling
variance"** and **"estimates of our confidence that a branch exists, given some assumptions about the data
and the models being used"**.

**My last fetch defeated it.** Simon 2020 (fetch 4), a Systematic Biology historical essay on exactly this
question, relays four *stated* meanings for the bootstrap and one for PP, two of which are explicitly
truth-referring:

- Hillis and Bull, as relayed: **"the probability that a specified group is contained in the true tree."**
- Felsenstein and Kishino, as relayed: **"1 - BP is a P value for the test of the null hypothesis that a split is not present."**
- Swofford et al., as relayed: **"the frequency in which a group appears in replicate trees."**
- Holmes, as relayed: **"a measure of the robustness of the results of a phylogenetic analysis to small changes in the data."**
- Posterior probability: **"the probability that a particular node is found in the true tree given the data, the model, and the priors"**

**REGIME:** population = the published methodological literature on branch support, 1985–2020; unit = a
published method or interpretation of support; operating condition = narrative history; comparator = none;
estimand = none — **this is a historical essay and measures nothing**, and these are Simon's relays of other
authors' definitions, not Simon's own findings (the review-versus-study defect, flagged).

V2 needs only **one** benchmark *declared in advance*, per method. Hillis & Bull's reading supplies a
truth-referring one for the bootstrap and the PP definition supplies one for Bayesian support. So C5 is
**CONTESTED, not false**, and V2's "declared in advance" device absorbs the contestedness. **My leg 1 does
not survive, and I do not grade on it.** This also means established facts (1) and (2) — Felsenstein denying
and then using the true-tree target within one page — are a real internal contest, but one the question's
pre-declaration handles.

**The leg my verdict stands on if the other falls: C6 (target-object mismatch), §3.** It is independent of
C5: it bites *hardest* under the truth-referring reading that rescues C5, because once BP = P(group in the
true tree), the question becomes *whose* true tree, and a mitochondrial study and a nuclear study have two
different ones.

## 7. Recommended verdict

**REJECTED** — Unresolved on attack 4 (presupposition failure), with Partial on attacks 5 and 8; attacks 1, 2, 3, 6, 7 Resolved and novelty intact at Medium.

**Single strongest killing point.** V2 declares its frame on **shared taxa only** and stratifies by support
type, data overlap and era — never by the genealogical object the two studies estimate — yet its second limb
asserts that the mutual-contradiction rate **lower-bounds the joint error of the two ≥95% labels**, a clause
the record grades "Arithmetic, not an assumption". It is an assumption, and it is false over the frame as
declared: Larson et al. (full text) states that, given discordance between a cytoplasmic locus and the

same topology with either the species tree or with each other"**. So the declared statistic is a mixture of
label error and real biology that bounds joint label error from *above*, not below (my own arithmetic), with
its magnitude unmeasured and — the reformulation being spent — no stratum available to separate it. The kill
bites hardest under exactly the truth-referring reading of a support value that rescues the question's other
contested clause.

**Nomination, justified by document kind rather than by title.** What would settle this: a document whose
**population** is *pairs of published phylogenetic studies sharing taxa*, whose **unit** is a study pair, that
**varies** the genealogical object each study targets (organellar / single-locus / concatenated-nuclear /
coalescent species tree), and that **prints** the share of high-support conflicts attributable to target
mismatch rather than to error. **I know of no source of that kind, and I am not nominating one.** The three
