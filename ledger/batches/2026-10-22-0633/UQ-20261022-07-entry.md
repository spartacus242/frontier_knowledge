Question (canonical): For a frame of completed placebo-controlled trials declared before any specimen is pulled (study class licensed or obtainable outside the trial's own prescribing channel during the enrolment window; scheduled-visit control-arm biospecimens retained; release permissible under the governing consent): what is the proportion of control-arm participant-visits at which the study class is detected by one pre-declared validated assay whose limit of detection and false-positive rate are reported and whose detection window is stated against the visit interval; what is that positivity's concordance with the same participant's protocol-recorded concomitant medication; and what is the signed difference between the trial's reported primary estimate and the estimate under one contamination-adjusted estimand declared before any assay result is seen — reported with the share of the declared frame whose governance refused release, so the denominator is auditable?
Reformulation note: this is V2, the one reformulation permitted at Gate 1, scored as canonical. V1 (intake) asked the same object less tightly: "In randomised placebo-controlled trials of a drug class that is simultaneously obtainable outside the trial, what fraction of placebo-arm participants has the active class detectable in their own stored trial biospecimens at scheduled visits, and by how much does that measured off-protocol exposure attenuate the trial's estimated treatment effect?" V2 changes specification only: visit-level denominator, assay error characterisation, estimand pre-declaration, governance-refusal share as a reported quantity.

Alternative formulations:
- How many people in the placebo group are actually taking the real drug — measured in their blood rather than asked on a form — and what does that do to the trial's answer?
- Treating off-protocol exposure as a measurable contamination of the control condition, what is its assayed prevalence per visit and what is the effect estimate under one pre-declared contamination-adjusted estimand?

Motivating gap: Presupposition inversion. Trials verify adherence in the active arm biochemically and ascertain control-arm exposure by self-report on a concomitant-medication CRF — inverting the burden of proof exactly where the comparison lives. Rytgaard et al. (arXiv:2605.05859, 8 May 2026), building time-varying concomitant-medication estimands on LEADER, renders control-arm access to the study class as nil rather than as a quantity (clipped fragment; whether that is a modelling assumption or a descriptive claim is UNVERIFIED). Two sponsor protocols opened at Gate 4 (SOUL NCT03914326, STEP-HFpEF NCT04788511, tirzepatide NCT04311411) show the asymmetry in the documents themselves: drug-concentration sampling sits in a pharmacokinetics section while outside use of the class is prohibited by instruction and ascertained by eCRF review. The intake's blanket negative ("no study has assayed control-arm samples for the study class") was withdrawn at Gate 2 (iPrEx) and is further falsified at Gate 4 (ASCEND, below).

Significance — consequence: If assayed prevalence exceeds the assay's false-positive rate materially, placebo-controlled trials of a widely obtainable class carry a bias of known sign (toward the null) that is currently neither measured by a detection assay nor declared, and the estimand framework has no category for an intercurrent event ascertained by assay. If it is indistinguishable from the false-positive rate, the worry is retired on evidence and CRF ascertainment is licensed on measurement rather than assumption. The governance-refusal share measures, auditably, how much of the trial enterprise's own stored evidence is unavailable for methodological self-examination. Trimmed by Gate 4: the one printed magnitude in a maximally obtainable class is small (7% overall, 2-4% among placebo-adherent), which points toward immaterial, and the "neither measured nor declared" wording is falsified — ASCEND measured and declared it in a methods journal. No adversary converted those percentages into an attenuation, correctly declining to do its own arithmetic on a surrogate with no stated uncertainty, so the "signed, bounded correction" remains a promise rather than a demonstration.
Significance — audience: (i) the estimand community, which demonstrably builds time-varying interventions on control-arm concomitant medication in this exact trial family; (ii) regulators reading estimands under ICH E9(R1), where an assay-ascertained intercurrent event has no slot and the bias runs against the sponsor; (iii) biobank governance committees and IRBs, now documented rather than hypothetical — the opened protocols show a real optional-consent biobank object with a named scope; (iv) meta-analysts and HTA bodies pooling estimates from the availability window; (v) bioanalytical laboratories of the HPTN type, independently re-surfaced by an adversary in a vocabulary the record did not use.
