## What the verification pass changed

Five fresh-context checkers, one per load-bearing kill, each handed the claim and its source but not the reviewer's reasoning (74 queries, 7 refused or access-failed). The batch returned **five rejections — a 100% rate**, and three of the five kills did not survive.

- **UQ-26's kill holds, and its decisive leg is confirmed to the decimal.** Clare et al. 2022 — the paper the candidate's motivating gap says left distance unquantified — ran read count against distance from the enclosure as a named model (LR = 2.51, df = 1, p = 0.113) over 0–276 m with 27 in-enclosure points excluded. Three further sources answer the same limb at widening scope (Kroos et al. 2026 to 1000 m; Stewart et al. 2026 to 655 m with a modelled catchment and a "<200 m" conclusion; the UK national survey with an 18.6 km median estimate). But the record's figures needed four corrections: 110 is the planned not the analysed sample count (88 + 20 = 108), "0 m" is an in-enclosure proof-of-concept arm rather than a distance class, "27 verified animals" is 27 animals reported present with no verification method stated, and Kroos fits **no** detection-probability-versus-distance function at all — three levels, coefficients and observed frequencies only. The "32% non-resident" figure is this ledger's own arithmetic (8/25), not Clare et al.'s wording. And the second limb — what share of a taxon list came from outside the unit — is **posed but unanswered**, so "both limbs are executed" was wrong and the rejection is re-grounded.
- **UQ-27's novelty kill fails on three independent grounds.** The kill rested on a Czech registry batch analysis found by the second adversary. It exists and is cited accurately, but its endpoint is three-month all-cause mortality — titled by its own authors a safety signal and cross-validated against adverse-reaction rates — with no infection endpoint, no comparator and no VE estimate anywhere in it; it reports per-batch rates and a cluster analysis with no random effect, no ICC and no variance decomposition; and it is not peer-reviewed, with an impossible date, R² = 1 on a one-batch cluster and a post-hoc exclusion. A safety-axis analysis is not prior art for a protection-axis question. What the checkers **did** confirm is the adversaries' scale objection: log(1−VE) is the canonical scale and the discrepancy factor is exactly VE/(1−VE) — 9.0 at VE 90%, 19.0 at VE 95% — so an effect size written on the wrong scale is off by an order of magnitude.
- **UQ-28's "already answered" is refuted in detail.** The ARIC/Parrinello numbers check out exactly (CKD prevalence 21.7% → 1.3% at visit 1), but Poh/de Lusignan is a mixture-of-regressions consistency check between reported and self-recomputed eGFR across 127 primary-care practices, with no change-log validation, and Parrinello has no detection step at all — its breakpoints are known visit dates and its correction comes from re-assaying frozen samples by Deming regression. The co-catchment-laboratories-as-mutual-reference-series design was found nowhere in clinical chemistry; the adjacent peer-group literature treats patient-population differences as noise to suppress, never as a shared signal to exploit.
- **UQ-29 is not asked-and-answered, but it is asked-and-deferred.** No paper computes the candidate's joint distribution, and Bai et al. 2024 is materially misdescribed in the record: it is a vendor preprint over client submissions, its true map-versus-material arm is 49 Addgene AAV plasmids, its tiering covers design errors rather than sequencing discrepancies, and it never mentions citing publications. What carries the rejection instead is McGuffie & Barrick 2024, which poses the publication join in its Discussion and books it as "years of expert curation and laboratory experiments by a community of scientists". Neither paper is map-versus-material at catalogue scale, which is a gap in the candidate's own premise.
- **UQ-30's Triviality kill fails on the leg that does the work.** Two legs survive — the question is posed routinely in public issue trackers, and the cited Megatron-LM issues do contain exact duplicate and drop counts obtained on a CPU. But the kill's claim is that the quantity is *derivable by reading the sampler code*, and issue #7646 says its numbers "are not derived analytically": they came from running a script and tallying drawn IDs with `Counter`. The generalisation fails too — HuggingFace's shuffle buffer is absent from the checkpoint by design, accelerate draws its permutation before saving the RNG, DeepSpeed's curriculum sampler checkpoints the wrong generator, and torchtitan asserts the world size is unchanged. The accurate classification is tractable-but-unmeasured, not trivial.

## Gate 4 ran late on UQ-28, because a Gate 2 kill means no adversary ever ran

This is the batch's structural finding, and it is now in `LEDGER.md`. The batch script spawns adversaries only for candidates that reach Gate 4, so a candidate killed at Gate 2 has an empty eight-row log. When verification then withdraws the Gate 2 ground, re-judging the record would make a survivor out of a candidate nothing has ever attacked. So UQ-28 was handed to one fresh-context adversary at the strict standard, on the Gate 0–3 record alone: 15 searches against a cap of 14 (the overrun recorded by the adversary itself), 14 of them in vocabularies Gate 2 had not tried, 6 fetches with 2 technical failures, no refusals.

It returned **Resolved, Partial, Resolved, Partial, Partial, Partial, Resolved, Partial** — nothing fatal — and did three things the batch had not.

It derived a presupposition nobody had listed, now **P9**: a pairwise-reference design can only see breaks that are *not* shared across the frame, because a simultaneous common shift cancels identically in every differenced series. In laboratory medicine the largest breaks are precisely the shared ones — manufacturers, traceability programmes and guideline committees change a whole country's laboratories at once, whereas weather stations are re-sited one at a time. That inverts the condition that makes the climatological transplant work, and it biases the headline deliverable in the most dangerous direction: limb (c) will under-attribute trend magnitude to breakpoints, manufacturing the record's own "trends unchanged, raw series licensed" outcome for the wrong reason. It graded this Partial rather than fatal only because it could not source the synchronised-versus-idiosyncratic split, and said plainly that a reviewer with that number could make it a kill.

It also corrected the record twice from sources it opened — UK Biobank *does* ship corrected biochemistry values with a per-result "Assay correction level" flag across 30 fields, so the audience claim was wrong as written; and the record's finding that neighbour-referenced homogenization has never been transplanted out of climatology is an artefact of searching in climatology's words, the same design being a mature named problem family in signal processing ("blind drift calibration of sensor networks"). And it declined to kill on detection power after checking the regime itself: Lindau & Venema 2018 report that breakpoint search "fails under typical conditions" below SNR 1 and that monthly temperature sits at about 0.5, so climatology's own flagship case is the marginal one and the clinical regime is plausibly better powered.

## The rejection rate and what it is allowed to mean

The batch returned 100% and finished at 40%, entirely because three kills dissolved. `LEDGER.md`'s operating rules say to treat a low rate as a warning that the adversaries were too gentle, never as a result — so none of the three survivors was allowed to stand on the judge's reconstruction.

- **UQ-28** had never been attacked at all, so it got the full eight attacks (above).
- **UQ-27 and UQ-30** each went to a **third** fresh-context adversary, given the Gate 0–3 record alone and held to the strict standard: an adversary's own technical claims must be cited to primary sources it opened, and a kill it cannot source is Partial at best.

Both survived, and both were sharpened rather than confirmed. **UQ-27's third adversary** produced the entry's new strongest objection: a five-fold cut in yellow fever vaccine dose moved day-28 seroconversion by −0.90% to +1.82% across four manufacturers, in batches chosen near minimum release potency that still assayed at "6-43 times the minimum specification established by WHO" — so the potency channel for between-lot variation in protection is bounded in a page. Its own arithmetic puts the proposed design's best one-sided limit at about τ = 0.03 on the log(1−VE) scale, roughly 0.3 VE points at VE 90%, which no lot-release body could act on because no effectiveness assay exists to add. It declined to make that fatal for three stated reasons: no public distribution of commercial lot-release potency results exists to complete the bound, the mRNA dose slope is not sign-determined once the published confidence limits are carried through, and the non-potency channels have no outcome mapping at all. It also found the nearest prior anyone has opened for this question — Bercu et al. 2019 on patient-centric specifications, which states the parent gap verbatim ("The 'process experience based' approach does not answer the question whether the predicted variability will impact product safety/efficacy") while building its limits from toxicology rather than outcomes. Gate 3 drops from 3/3 to 2/3 on that evidence.

**UQ-30's third adversary** found that OPEN-1B — a paper the record cites and both batch adversaries described as making no mention of restarts — in fact validates each of seven restarts "using hash comparisons on the steps which were repeated against the prior segment", on exactly the auditable-run class the question scopes to. That is a partial performance of the candidate's own limb (a), and it exposes the ambiguity the entry now leads with: "realized token sequence" does not distinguish the tokens a loader *delivered* from the tokens that *shaped the released weights*, and under rollback those differ by precisely the discarded branch of the optimisation. Well-posedness falls to 2/3. It also struck the EU AI Act audience on the Commission's own words (the training-data summary is "generally comprehensive in its scope instead of technically detailed", with no "work-by-work assessment"), and added a 2024 in-field paper all eleven Gate 2 queries missed, arXiv:2406.05546, whose existence corroborates the silence claim rather than refuting it: even the paper named for data consistency under failure counts gradients rather than sample IDs.

Twenty-four attacks across three adversaries on each survivor, not one Unresolved. The 40% stands.
