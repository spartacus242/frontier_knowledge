## Restatement in my own words

Take a frozen copy of ProteinGym's substitution benchmark. For every assay in it that actually shipped more than one independent measurement of the same variant, measure how well the assay agrees with itself (a test-retest reliability coefficient, with error bars), and report up front how many of the 217 assays even allow that. Then use each assay's self-agreement to ask a second thing: when a predictor scores, say, 0.45 Spearman against that assay, how much of the missing 0.55 is the model being wrong versus the assay being noisy? Finally, redo the published leaderboard with every predictor's correlation corrected upward by its assay's unreliability and ask whether the order of models changes, measured as Kendall tau against the published order plus a count of pairwise flips in the top-k.

In one line: is the protein variant-effect leaderboard partly a leaderboard of which assays were measured carefully, and nobody has computed the correction even though the benchmark ships the raw replicate layer it would need.
