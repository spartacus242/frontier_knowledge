## Gate 0 — Intake

Source method: presupposition inversion.

Question (canonical, V1 as received): In nominally constant-condition (DD or LL) circadian experiments, does the recording environment itself carry a measurable 24-hour periodic component — in temperature, vibration, sound, CO2, humidity or human-access events — large enough to weakly entrain or mask the oscillator, and how much of the between-chamber and between-laboratory spread in reported free-running period for one genotype or preparation does that component account for?

Alternative formulations:
- Is "constant darkness" constant — what residual 24-hour cues do circadian chambers actually deliver, and do they move the measured period?
- How much of a reported free-running period is the building's day rather than the organism's clock?

Motivating gap: The settled claim is that tau measured in constant conditions is an endogenous property of the genotype or preparation; its untested presupposition is that the nominally constant environment contains no 24-hour periodic component of entraining or masking amplitude. Small-amplitude temperature cycles are an established resetting cue for cell-autonomous and peripheral clocks, and method guidance warns about incidental cues, but no source opened in this record LOGS the 24-hour-periodic environmental content of recording chambers at the amplitudes real facilities deliver and tests its effect on tau.

Presuppositions (as received, with this gate's disposition):
1. Facility environments contain a 24-hour periodic component at all — CONTESTED and unmeasured here. **MEASURAND EXEMPTION DECLARED, not merely considered:** this is the question's object, not a smuggled assumption. Limb (a) exists to measure it, and an attack that calls it a false premise attacks the measurement the question proposes. It cannot ground a presupposition-failure kill.
2. The amplitude needed for weak entrainment or masking is known or can be bracketed — established for temperature in several preparations, NOT established for vibration, sound or CO2 — FLAGGED. V2 (below) confines the threshold claim to temperature and demotes the other channels to amplitude reporting without a threshold claim.
3. Reported tau is published with enough precision that a shift of the expected size would be detectable (typically 0.01-0.1 h) — FLAGGED. **Not verified by anything opened here.** The one methods source opened (Prior C) gives the two standard estimators (onset regression; dominant periodogram peak) over 1-3 weeks of DD and prints NO precision figure, so the 0.01-0.1 h bracket is carried forward as the intake's assertion, unsourced in this record.
4. Reported tau for one fixed genotype or preparation does vary between chambers and laboratories beyond stated precision — FLAGGED, needs its own check, NOT established here (query 2 and query 8 returned no between-laboratory tau variance decomposition that I opened). If tau is reproducible within measurement error, limb (b) is degenerate while limb (a) survives intact.
5. Chambers can be instrumented without the loggers themselves introducing a cue — plausible (passive loggers), declared.
6. Entrainment and masking are distinguishable under the declared design (phase-angle, after-effect and release tests) — FLAGGED. For the non-thermal channels this is not established, and a design that cannot separate them answers a different question.
7. Period is the right measurand; amplitude and phase effects are a separate question, declared out of scope.

## Gate 3 — Significance

**Consequence.** If YES (a measurable 24-hour component at or above the preparation's thermal threshold, and a tau shift exceeding the estimator's precision): tau stops being a pure property of the genotype or preparation and becomes a chamber-conditional measurement. The immediate casualties are comparisons whose two arms sat in different chambers or different facilities — mutant-versus-wild-type tau differences of the order of the shift, and the aging and strain tau differences that query 2 returned — and the remedy is concrete and cheap: log and publish each chamber's 24-hour amplitude per channel, and randomise chamber against genotype. If NO: a hazard that methods guidance currently names without quantifying (Prior C, verbatim above) is retired with bounds, and the field's inherited presupposition becomes a licensed one. Both branches change practice, which is why the null here is not a dead end.

**Audience, named and specific.** Circadian laboratories that publish tau for mutants and knockouts and compare across facilities; manufacturers of recording cabinets and light-tight chambers, whose specifications today cover set point and uniformity rather than 24-hour spectral content; laboratory-animal facility engineers, whose HVAC schedules (query 3) are the suspected source and who do not currently know they are an experimental variable; reporting-checklist bodies, where the plant-chamber community already maintains "Guidelines for Measuring and Reporting Environmental Parameters" (controlledenvironments.org, returned at query 9, **NOT opened** — named as a plausible audience, not as a source supporting anything).

**Fecundity.** Opens: whether published tau differences between strains measured in different facilities survive chamber adjustment; whether documented after-effects partly record the prior chamber's environment rather than the prior light cycle; whether cell-autonomous period measurements in CO2 incubators carry the same contamination through a channel (CO2, door openings) with no documented threshold at all; whether the between-individual tau variance the Halberg page notes has a within-rack position component (airflow and thermal gradient by shelf).

**Anchoring.** Touches the environmental-standardization / heterogenization debate on between-laboratory variance in mouse phenotypes (query 10; those papers NOT opened, so this anchor is named, not evidenced), which owns the method but has not been pointed at tau. Touches the historical exogenous-timing controversy (query 4): Brown's claim was the same presupposition at a different amplitude scale and a different source — geophysical fields rather than the building — and a facility-generated answer would recast that debate's framing rather than revive its content. Touches the established small-amplitude temperature resetting of peripheral and cell-autonomous clocks (query 5), which supplies the threshold limb (a) tests against.

## Reason for silence

A disciplinary boundary with an instrument gap and an incentive asymmetry on top; "no one thought of it" is explicitly NOT the mechanism, because the Halberg page poses limb (a) and Prior C names it. What is missing is the measurement, and the measurement falls in a gap: the 24-hour spectral content of a room or cabinet is a facility-engineering and metrological quantity — an influence quantity, in metrology's words — owned by people who never estimate tau, while tau is owned by people who treat the chamber as a controlled utility. Query 9 shows this directly: chamber mapping and environmental-parameter reporting are mature methods, but they were returned from pharmaceutical validation, plant tissue culture and a cage-vendor SOP, not from chronobiology. This is the same mechanism the ledger's UQ-20261017-55 found for culture temperature, where standards bodies file temperature under calibration and monitoring rather than as a specified variable. The incentive runs the wrong way as well: a positive result impeaches the asker's own published tau values and their field's, and the work funds nothing new — it is loggers plus a chamber-crossed design with no novel biology at the end. And the instrument side was genuinely awkward when the field's tau canon was built: cheap multi-channel passive logging of temperature, vibration, sound, CO2 and door events at minute resolution inside a sealed cabinet is recent.
