## The rejection that reversed

**UQ-20261023-03** (does a repeated fixed-amplitude hypo-osmotic downshock protocol put a stress-life curve, an endurance limit and a Palmgren-Miner damage sum on the bacterial sacculus) was rejected at Gate 4 on attack 4, Unresolved from adversary 3, with attack 8 Unresolved independently but non-fatal alone. The entry pre-declared its own multi-leg discipline: "the verdict stands on C5. If C5 fell, C3/C2 survives … and would hold attack 4 at or near Unresolved on its own … if C5 and C3/C2 both fell the verdict would soften to Provisional rather than reverse."

**C5 fell at two of its three joints.** Joint 1, the geometry, survives and was confirmed at two performed protocols: a two-level fixed-amplitude square wave does deliver equal-osmolarity excursions in both signs, and both authors name both limbs as shocks. Joint 2, the modal claim that such a protocol "cannot have one sign", is true only in the unbounded-repetition limit, which the claim does not say — and the checker marked its own argument as exactly that, "MY OWN REASONING ABOUT THE CLAIM'S LOGIC, NOT A SOURCE FINDING", with the search that could have grounded it never run. Joint 3, that damage per cycle cannot be attributed to the downshock limb, is unsupported and **contradicted in direction** by the only turgor source that could be opened: on its statements the hyperosmotic limb gives "a decreased turgor pressure", "an instantaneous drop in turgor pressure" and at the extreme "the turgor pressure is zero", while the hypo-osmotic limb is where "the dramatic water influx stretches the cell wall". The upshock UNLOADS the wall and the downshock LOADS it, so C5 silently upgraded equal osmolarity magnitude to equal mechanics. That is a modelling paper's prediction with no tension or work printed in any units, and it was a substitute for the envelope-mechanics papers nobody could open — so it refutes the joint without founding its converse.

**And the fallback's first clause was not in the entry at all.** C3/C2 rested partly on an internal contradiction between V2's "each below the single-shock lytic threshold" and the answer criterion's ">= 4 amplitudes spanning the single-shock lytic threshold". The contradiction is real in the RECORD and absent from the ENTRY: the entry's own criterion had already deleted "spanning the single-shock lytic threshold". The entry quietly repaired its criterion and then, thirty lines later, cited the unrepaired version as a live defect, with the quoted string attributed to a criterion in which it does not appear. A repairable drafting slip the entry itself calls "repairable by stipulation" cannot alone hold a presupposition-failure attack at Unresolved.

So Gate 4 was incomplete and was re-run from scratch: two fresh-context adversaries on the Gate 0-3 record alone, prior grade declared void and not described to them, handed the twelve facts verification had established — **including clause (ii) of C3/C2 verbatim**, the printed single-cell numbers showing cycle-1 outcome is a per-cell probability at fixed amplitude rather than a threshold in amplitude — all eight attacks at the strict standard, 10 and 9 Disguise queries. **Both returned Provisional. Attack 4 came back Partial from each, on two different clauses, and neither returned Unresolved on anything.**

- **New ground one, the load-history leg.** The question manipulates the external osmotic step, but the wall is loaded by the internal-minus-external difference, and internal osmolyte content is history-dependent: solutes are exported through tension-gated channels on every down-step and must be actively re-accumulated, which the one full text opened describes only for upshocks and never shows to be complete within a fixed interval. So "repeated downshocks of fixed amplitude" is not demonstrated to deliver a constant mechanical amplitude across cycles, and limb (a)'s S-N abscissa is a nominal rather than a measured load. The adversary stated its own limit: the leg is sourced only to the ABSENCE of a printed recovery-completeness figure, and no opened source prints a wall tension or wall work in any units for either limb.

(eLife 88750) is closer than any prior the record names — walled single cells in a microfluidic fixed-amplitude periodic osmotic square wave, with per-cell lysis attributed to the hypo-osmotic limb and a death rate of 2.4e-3 min^-1 printed — and its text **already answers the candidate's core limb qualitatively and negatively at one period**: "the dynamics of cell death remained constant over multiple periods of osmotic fluctuation (Figure S6f)". ## The rejection that held, on a ground nobody had raised

**UQ-20261023-05** (what share of between-study high-support topological contradictions in the published phylogenetic record is attributable to label error) was rejected at Gate 4 on attack 4, Unresolved from adversary 3, whose ground was that the error reading (Kumar et al. 2012) and the non-error reading (Larson et al.) "agree only in the deep-divergence regime". **That ground is withdrawn, not downgraded.** It is not a finding about either document: Kumar does not scope to depth, and Larson expressly says depth is not the variable. The leg beneath it failed first, on the quotations:

- the load-bearing element, "well-supported differing branches treated as the reliable signal of real discordance", is the **inverse** of Larson's sentence, which gives only a necessary condition, and the sufficiency reading is blocked next door ("one should not ignore gene tree inference error as a possible source of discordance");
- "due to error, ILS, or introgression" is not verbatim — the text reads "error, ILS, and/or genetic exchange among lineages", a class narrowed to one member, under an opener scoping it to the case where discordance is already established;
- "essentially just one random draw" is not verbatim — "may essentially be one random draw", with "just" inserted and the hedge deleted. **The grammar holds on both sides of that cut, which is precisely the failure mode: what changed was the modality.**

So Gate 4 was incomplete and was re-run from scratch, same form: two cold adversaries, Gate 0-3 record alone, prior grade void, established facts supplied, all eight attacks, 9 and 10 Disguise queries. **One returned attack 4 Unresolved; the other returned Partial. One Unresolved on attack 4 from any reader is fatal, so the verdict stands on the new ground alone.**

**The new ground is an inequality running the wrong way.** V2's clause that mutual contradiction lower-bounds the joint error of two >= 95% labels — which the record grades "Arithmetic, not an assumption" — assumes both labels refer to one true tree, and that is false over the frame V2 itself declares: V2 conditions on shared taxa and stratifies only by support type, data overlap and era, never by the genealogical object each study estimates, so the frame admits cytoplasmic-versus-nuclear and single-locus-versus-species-tree pairs. Larson et al. print that given such discordance "the observed discordance is due to error, ILS, and/or genetic exchange among lineages", error one disjunct of three; Lanfear & Hahn 2024, MBE 41(11):msae214 print that "individual loci do not have to share the same topology with either the species tree or with each other". Since P(contradiction) = P(contradiction & same target) + P(contradiction & different target), the declared statistic is a mixture bounding joint label error from ABOVE. It is assumed rather than reported, so the measurand exemption does not reach it.

**That kill's own two weaknesses are in the entry, both stated by the adversary itself.** Its existence claim is unenumerated — "I opened no instance of a qualifying study pair and did not measure the contaminating share, so a verifier holding that the existence claim needs an enumerated pair should downgrade this to Partial" — which is this ledger's own signature defect, now carrying a verdict. And its intended second leg **inverted on its own last fetch**: the claim that a support label has no truth-referring nominal target collapsed when Simon 2020 turned out to relay two truth-referring bootstrap definitions that V2's "declared in advance" device can take up. The adversary declined to grade on it and said so.

The second cold reader's Partial is a separate live objection, and it points the opposite way: the question's closing limb, comparison with "a per-method nominal benchmark declared in advance", has no referent for the nonparametric bootstrap, since Felsenstein 1985 p.786 targets "not the true phylogeny, but the phylogeny that would be estimated on repeated sampling of many characters from the underlying pool of characters" and Kumar et al. print that "the bootstrap support value has no simple frequentist interpretation as a probability". **So the two live objections are in partial tension about what a support value targets** — one bites hardest under the truth-referring reading, the other because the reading is not available — and the fatal one is fatal because of the grading rule, not because they agree.
