Question (canonical, Gate 0 as intaken):
Using a deployed model's thinking-budget dial as an exogenous manipulation on a fixed item set, how much of the reported accuracy-versus-reasoning-token relationship is a within-item causal dose-response and how much is between-item difficulty selection — and is a model capped at N reasoning tokens behaviourally equivalent to the same model naturally stopping at N?

Question (Gate 1 reformulation — ONE used, for well-posedness only; both versions recorded):
The canonical form conjoins two estimands under one "how much … and is …", which leaves the second limb without a stated contrast. Reformulated as two named estimands on one design:
(a) DECOMPOSITION: on a pinned item set, let b_w be the within-item slope of correctness on log reasoning tokens under randomly assigned forced budgets and b_p the pooled (observational) slope on naturally emitted length over the same items; report the signed selection share (b_p − b_w)/b_p per difficulty stratum and per truncation convention.
(b) EQUIVALENCE: on the subset of items where the model naturally emits approximately N tokens, test correctness-distribution equivalence between forced-at-N and natural-at-N responses, at a pre-declared equivalence margin.
Both versions retained; (a)/(b) is the version graded below.
