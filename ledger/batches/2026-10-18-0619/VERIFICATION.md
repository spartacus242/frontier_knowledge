## UQ-20261018-12 — parameters frozen by low-precision update arithmetic

Five readers: Prior 1 by identifier, Prior 3 by identifier, COLLAGE by identifier, AuroraRL by identifier, and one cold by subject.

**Holds.** Prior 1 (arXiv:2602.03839, Miahi and Belilovsky) measures the candidate's headline estimand as described: Definition A.2 Update Sparsity is "the fraction of parameters that remain bitwise identical between step t and t + k after casting to D", Definition A.3 is update absorption bf16(w+dw) = bf16(w), the measured mean per-step sparsity is "approximately 99%" over 400 steps with step SD 0.2-0.4% across five models, the critical magnitude 7.68e-4 at a learning rate of 3e-6 reproduces the adversary's arithmetic, and Table 2 reports 94.8-97.6% of weights above it. COLLAGE's Figure 3 left caption is essentially verbatim as cited: "Imprecision percentage (%) measured as the percentage of lost arithmetic for all model parameters, i.e., not updated, vs iterations for BF16."

**Falls.** The Gate 2 finding says a second paper "reports the bitwise-unchanged fraction broken down BY MODULE GROUP and by source-weight magnitude decile (arXiv 2606.13657)". Read at first hand in both the v2 and v3 HTML renderings: the word "decile" is **absent from both, and no magnitude-decile breakdown of the unchanged fraction exists**. The module-group table reports relative DELTA NORM, not a zero fraction. Per-layer sparsity appears only in a figure with no numbers. The nearest magnitude analysis is a bottom-10%-source-magnitude coverage metric that overlaps a bottom-magnitude mask with the visible-update mask. Its unit is the END-CHECKPOINT delta rather than a per-step update, its setting is post-training rather than pretraining, and its stated direction is the OPPOSITE of the candidate's hypothesis: updates "preferentially occupy coordinates where the source weight was small in magnitude", so small weights are more likely to be updated. The "3.06-86.50%" exact-zero range attributed to it is supported by nothing readable: v3's exact-zero column runs 3.06 to 84.65 over 12 rows, v2's is threshold-based at 1e-5 tolerance and runs 3.06 to 89.50, and the figures 86.50 and 64.13 appear in neither.

**Falls.** COLLAGE was the Gate 4 Disguise finding's strongest leg — "already asks both limbs under its own definitions ... and runs the BF16-vs-FP32-master-weight matched arms". Read at first hand: Definition 3.3 Effective Descent Quality is an INNER PRODUCT, the paper's own words being that EDQ "is projection of the effective update along the desired update" — a projection, not a realized-minus-intended deviation. Its imprecision percentage is POOLED "for all model parameters" per iteration, with a breakdown by parameter group NOT FOUND. Nothing links imprecision to a frozen parameter group or to learning-rate decay. And its arm A against arm D differs in more than master weights, because D also carries FP32 optimizer states, so the matched arm is confounded.

**Corrections on decoration.** AuroraRL's rho is the share of elements that CHANGE, not the unchanged share, pooled element-wise; the "0.93-2.56%" range is stated nowhere in the paper and stitches a Table 4 algorithm cell (RLOO on Qwen3-8B) to a Figure 3 model cell (Llama3-8B). Prior 1 is RL fine-tuning only, with no pretraining measurement, no parameter-group breakdown, and no matched-arm held-out consequence (its Figure 8 plots sparsity beside validation pass@1 for one model but runs no BF16-master comparator). The nearest real prior for the stratified limb is one the batch never named: Zamirai et al., arXiv:2010.06192, which reports the percentage of non-zero updates cancelled by nearest rounding per iteration for a DLRM embedding layer against an MLP layer, exceeding 80% late in a run with a decaying learning rate — its Appendix D.3 is COULD NOT READ, two ar5iv attempts having truncated. Seven searches in the method-owning vocabularies found no work stratifying the unchanged fraction by rare-token rows, normalization gains or small weights, and no matched-arm held-out consequence for the unchanged fraction itself.

## UQ-20261018-15 — cooldown gains: format sharpening against knowledge

Four readers: the kill paper by identifier, Guo et al. by identifier, the OLMo cooldown mix, and one cold by subject.

**The ground substantially falls.** The kill is arXiv:2407.07890, cited in the entry as "Dominguez-Olmedo, Hardt & Mendler-Dunner". The printed author line on the paper's own first page is **Ricardo Dominguez-Olmedo, Florian E. Dorner, Moritz Hardt**: there is no Mendler-Dunner among the authors at all, Dorner is omitted, and Hardt is last rather than second. The ICLR 2025 virtual page lists the same three.

What the paper does: Appendix B splits the effect of model recency on accuracy into direct and indirect effects through T, where **T is training on the test task in general, not multiple-choice-format ability**. Its unit is 56 released base models of 70M to 70B parameters, split by release before or after November 2023 and controlled for pretraining compute. A within-pretraining-run checkpoint comparison is NOT FOUND, and learning-rate cooldown or annealing is NOT FOUND anywhere in the roughly 100,000 characters read — the only intermediate checkpoints in the paper are checkpoints of fine-tuning runs. Its instrument is prompt reformulation plus training: it reformulates ARC and HellaSwag as MMLU-style multiple-choice questions and adjusts by fine-tuning all models on the MMLU auxiliary training set, with **no temperature or entropy rescaling, no length-normalised or rank-based scoring, and no label-emission correction** — so it is not the candidate's fixed-weight output transform.

A mediated **SHARE is published nowhere** in what was read: only effects in accuracy units with standard errors (indirect 0.071 for MMLU and 0.168 for GSM8K, in Table 3 rather than the Table 2 the entry cites, with SEs 0.018 and 0.032), no proportion or percentage mediated, and no total effect in the same table from which a reader could form one. And the "more than 17 accuracy points in GSM8K" figure **does not exist**: the paper says 19 accuracy points, in both Sec. 2.2 and the Introduction. The verbatim Sec. 3.2 string about MMLU conflating knowledge-testing with the ability to answer multiple-choice questions is confirmed, as is "over 7 accuracy points in MMLU".

**A nearer prior, properly identified.** The pass confirms Bunn, Wiegreffe and Bogin, "Fine-Tune on the Format" (GEM2 2025, pp. 511-521), as the closest published work on the format limb: ONE OLMo-1B run, 1T tokens, 10 checkpoints every 40k steps, with a final checkpoint whose zero-shot label-based accuracy is near chance (SciQ 24.6, ARC 24.7) against sequence-based scoring at SciQ 58.6 and ARC 46.1, and 95.1 after auxiliary fine-tuning. It carries no cooldown analysis, no numeric decomposition, and its format separation is a TRAINED step rather than a fixed-weight transform; it is explicit that length normalisation "does not consistently improve performance".

**Guo's invariance is narrower than the entry uses it.** Both quoted sentences are verbatim in Sec. 4.2. But the claim covers only the argmax over a single input's K-class logit vector. Greedy decoding of a multi-token completion is not stated; the paper never mentions decoding, sequences or generation. **Summed or length-normalised log-probabilities over multi-token options are not covered at all** — dividing logits by T changes each token's log-softmax non-uniformly, so sequence ranking can change — and sampling is not covered. The paper also does not claim that vector or matrix scaling preserve the argmax.

**The cooldown-mix premise is sourced but not where the record says.** The GSM8K train split is literally in OLMo 2's stage-2 mix (2.74M tokens, 17.6K documents) alongside synthetic GSM-style data, FLAN is present at 16.6-16.7% of the 50B and 100B mixes, and mix selection ran on downstream benchmarks — 19 microanneals of about 130B tokens evaluated mainly on MMLU and a 200-question GSM8K dev subset. All of that is in the paper and the dataset card; the YAML configs the record cites are robots-blocked and COULD NOT BE READ. Two corrections: a checkpoint cadence of "every 1000 steps" is NOT FOUND in the paper, either model card or the repository's Checkpoints.md, and the record's "three ingredient seeds at 1B, 7B and 13B" is not supported as stated — the 7B does have three souped 50B anneals, but the 0425 1B keeps only ONE, unmerged, with the others described as exploratory. The Marin side of the claim is entirely COULD NOT READ. OLMo 2's own anneal gain has a text-against-table discrepancy worth recording: the text says 10.6 points at 7B where Table 9 gives 53.0 to 62.9, a difference of 9.9, and 10.3 at 13B where the table gives 9.4.
