# UQ-20261016-48 — Gate 4 adversary #3 (hard rerun), 2026-10-16

## Restatement (own words)
When two or more organisationally separate teams test the same frozen model with comparable access and effort, (a) how often do they independently surface the same failure mode — "same" fixed by a pre-registered classification whose reliability is itself measured — and (b) can that overlap, fed to capture-recapture / multiple-systems estimators that allow unequal detectability and correlated teams, give a finite, non-trivial estimate of the failure modes nobody found at that effort? Novelty rests on (i) nobody having computed the overlap for AI evaluations and (ii) the transplant problem (elicitation-created, non-closed population; no matching relation) being genuinely open.

E4 (HOSTED-PRIMARY, Petersson et al. 2004 text): "(2001) conclude that Mt-Ch is the best estimator for two reviewers. E8 (MY OWN DERIVATION, degrees-of-freedom count): with k=2 lists the incomplete 2x2 table has three observed cells (both, A-only, B-only) and one unobserved; a log-linear model with an interaction term has four parameters, so the list-dependence term is not estimable from the data and must be assumed (independence) or imported — consistent with the Chao line in E5. Under independence the Chapman/Petersen estimator has a finite point estimate and finite CI whenever the both-found cell is non-empty, so R1's vacuity test ("finite upper confidence bound") is passed by construction at k=2, not by evidence.
