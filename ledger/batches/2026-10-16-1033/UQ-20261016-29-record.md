Domain(s): Biology — research-reagent integrity, molecular cloning, repository quality control. Neighbours: metascience/reproducibility (literature-contamination counting), cell-line authentication (ICLAC), software supply chain (reproducible builds, SBOM drift), metrology (reference materials), microbial culture collections.

Question (canonical) [V1, adopted at Gate 1]: In a plasmid repository that holds, per plasmid, (a) a depositor-supplied map and (b) its own full-construct sequencing of the distributed material, and (c) a per-plasmid list of citing publications: over the sequenced catalogue, what is the joint distribution of (discrepancy class: ORF-altering / regulatory element / backbone / selection marker / silent) x (number of distinct citing publications whose methods describe the construct by map rather than by catalogue number), with CIs; and in a pre-specified subsample, does orthogonal re-sequencing plus a function assay of the discrepant and as-mapped constructs confirm the predicted consequence and establish whether the discrepancy predates or postdates the depositor's publication?
Question (V0, intake, superseded): Among research plasmids whose repository full-construct sequencing differs from the depositor's own published map, what is the joint distribution of discrepancy class by functional consequence and number of citing publications — that is, how many published results were built on a construct other than the one their methods describe?
Reformulated once at Gate 1 (V0 -> V1), for well-posedness only. Changes: (i) names the three data objects the estimand needs and so makes the comparison procedure stateable; (ii) demotes the bare discrepancy *rate* from the estimand, because it is published (Bai et al. 2024, below) — the primary estimand is the exposure-weighted class distribution; (iii) restricts the exposure denominator to publications that describe the construct by map/sequence rather than by catalogue number, which is what makes "a construct other than the one their methods describe" well-defined at all (see P8); (iv) makes the direction-of-error question an explicit experimental arm rather than an aspiration.

Alternative formulations:
- (lay) When the plasmid in the freezer isn't quite the plasmid in the paper, how often does the difference matter, and whose results rest on it?
- (technical) Using repository full-construct sequencing as ground truth against depositor-supplied maps, estimate the joint distribution over (discrepancy class, predicted functional consequence, citing-publication count) across a repository's catalogue.
- (cross-field framing) What is the plasmid-catalogue equivalent of the "ghosts of HeLa" count: how much of the literature rests on deposited material that differs from its own description, and in what way?

Motivating gap (as intaken, and as Gate 2 revised it): The intake gap was that repository full-construct sequencing newly makes map-versus-reality measurable at catalogue scale while the same repositories count per-plasmid citing publications, making downstream exposure countable. Gate 2 falsifies the first half and confirms only the second. The map-versus-material comparison has been run and published, including an Addgene arm using depositor-supplied maps against Addgene's own sequencing, with a functional-significance tiering and an exposure *proxy* (request counts), by Bai et al. 2024. Catalogue-scale discrepancy classification has been run over 51,384 fully sequenced Addgene plasmids by McGuffie & Barrick 2024, against canonical part databases rather than depositor maps, and that paper explicitly identifies the publication-tracing step as the work it did not do. What Gate 2 could not find in anything opened: the citing-publication axis joined to discrepancy class, and any resolution of the direction of error. The candidate's own GATE 2 FLAG was therefore right about where the weight sits and wrong to expect the rate limb to be merely "possibly" published.

Presuppositions:
- P1 Repository full-construct sequences are accurate enough to be ground truth. CONTESTED at repeats/low-complexity; retained from intake. Partly supported in practice by Bai et al., who treat independent sequencing as the arbiter against sender maps, but they do not quantify their own error rate; a subsample needs orthogonal confirmation.
- P2 The deposited physical material is the material the depositor used in the published work. CONTESTED, and intake correctly made the direction part of the question. Bai et al. bear directly on it: they propose that a common 11-bp AAV 5' ITR deletion arose independently in several labs and spread, and that some researchers may have adopted the mutated sequence as their reference — i.e. the direction can run either way and can also be circular. Not resolved anywhere opened.
- P3 Depositor maps are retrievable in machine-comparable form for enough of the catalogue. PARTLY SUPPORTED, UNVERIFIED IN DETAIL: Addgene plasmid pages are served as "Sequences (2)/(3)/(4)", which is consistent with multiple sequence records (depositor-full, Addgene-full) per plasmid; inferred from search-result titles only, no sequences page was opened. Bai et al.'s Addgene arm demonstrates the comparison is at least feasible per plasmid.
- P4 Per-plasmid citing-publication lists are reasonably complete. CONTESTED (self-reported + text-mined). The lists exist and the repository documents its counting method (Addgene help article "How do you calculate articles citing a plasmid", title only, not opened).
- P5 Functional consequence can be coarsely stratified from sequence class (ORF-altering / regulatory / backbone / marker / silent). DEFENSIBLE; note a published precedent uses a consequence tiering rather than a sequence-class tiering (Bai et al. Table 1: "Destroy intended function" / "Compromise intended function" / "Context dependent"), so the candidate's stratification is a variant of an existing scheme, not a new one.
- P6 Discrepancies are not concentrated entirely in unused plasmids. Now partly ESTABLISHED in the negative direction for one class: Bai et al. report 34/49 (69%) of highly requested ("blue flame", >100 requests) Addgene AAV plasmids with a mutated 5' ITR, so discrepancies reach heavily used material.
- P7 Repository catalogue composition is informative about wider lab practice, or the finding is scoped to deposited material. CONTESTED; Bai et al.'s non-repository arm (plasmids submitted to a vendor by labs worldwide) is the wider-practice evidence and gives ~15% design errors and 35% (91/259) sequence variations in directly sequenced plasmids.
- P8 NEW, added at Gate 1, load-bearing: published methods sections describe the construct in a way that can disagree with the repository's sequence. Where a paper cites only "Addgene #X", the described construct *is* whatever the repository distributes, so no mismatch between paper and material exists to count, and the exposure denominator collapses toward the subset of papers that state a map, sequence or element composition. Unquantified anywhere opened; this is the weakest link in the candidate's framing.
- P9 NEW: the depositor's map is a *published* map. Intake's V0 says "published map"; in practice maps are supplied to the repository and are usually not in the paper, which is why V1 says depositor-supplied.

What would count as an answer: (i) The joint table over the sequenced catalogue: per discrepancy class, the count of plasmids and the number of distinct citing publications in the exposure denominator of P8, with CIs, plus the class-by-consequence cross-tab; (ii) pre-committed bands so a negative is real — NEGLIGIBLE (mass silent/marker-only, ORF-altering exposure CI upper bound below a pre-set publication count), MARKER-ONLY, SUBSTANTIAL ORF EXPOSURE; (iii) a validated subsample in which discrepant and as-mapped constructs are both assayed, confirming or refuting the sequence-class-to-consequence mapping; (iv) a direction-of-error arm: the repository's archived original deposit and an independently obtained aliquot from the depositor's own lab, re-sequenced orthogonally, so that pre- versus post-publication origin is assigned rather than assumed. Without (iii) and (iv) the result reduces to a rate-plus-annotation exercise of the kind already published.

Significance — consequence: If ORF-altering discrepancies carry substantial citing-publication exposure, the acting parties have a template ready: the Horbach & Halffman notification system, applied per plasmid rather than per cell line, plus prominent construct-versus-map diffs on repository pages and a materials-verification clause in journal policy. If the mass is silent or marker-only with negligible exposure, current partial-confirmation practice is justified and the finding closes a live worry that Bai et al.'s press coverage opened. The marginal consequence is reduced by the fact that (A) already produced alarming headline rates and public recommendations without the exposure axis; the exposure axis changes *how many papers get flagged*, not whether repositories are being asked to tighten QC.
Significance — audience: plasmid repositories (Addgene and equivalents) — whether to publish per-plasmid diffs and to notify citing authors; journals and publishers — materials-availability and verification policy, and whether to attach expressions of concern or notices; the AAV vector community specifically, where (A) found ITR mutation in a majority of highly requested constructs; reusing labs deciding whether to re-sequence before use; ICLAC-style standing bodies as the institutional model for notification; commercial full-plasmid sequencing providers, whose market case this quantifies (a non-trivial conflict of interest, since (A)'s authors are at such a provider).
Significance — fecundity: does exposure concentrate in a few heavily cited constructs (a power law of risk) or spread thinly; do discrepancies in a plasmid predict discrepancies in results that used it (testable against replication corpora); does the 11-bp ITR deletion's apparent independent recurrence mean selection rather than error, making some "discrepancies" adaptive; can repository diff data be used to date when a construct's lineage diverged; what does the equivalent audit show for cell lines, antibodies, mouse lines and recombinant proteins once each has a full-sequence ground truth.
Significance — anchoring: (A)'s unresolved direction-of-error puzzle; (B)'s explicitly deferred publication-tracing step; the cell-line literature-contamination estimate and its unimplemented notification proposal; the wrongly-identified-nucleotide-sequence reagent literature (Byrne/Labbe-type work surfaced at query 7) and the aptamer sequence-alteration review, both of which trace reagent-description errors through the literature in neighbouring reagent classes.

Reason for silence: Mostly NOT silent — the core question was asked and answered in 2024, which is itself the Gate 2 finding. For the residual exposure limb, the candidate mechanisms are: (1) institutional-function boundary inside the only party able to do the join — the repository's full-construct sequencing lives in QC while the per-plasmid citing-article list is built and promoted as a depositor incentive ("Addgene depositors get more citations"), so the two datasets serve different functions of the same organisation; (2) an incentive/reputational gap on top of that boundary: publishing construct-versus-map diffs with citation exposure attached names depositors and devalues catalogue entries, and outsiders can obtain sequences and citation lists but not necessarily depositor maps in bulk machine-comparable form, since the authoritative full sequence is what the page foregrounds (mechanism 2 is INFERRED, unevidenced — no refusal, policy or statement was located); (3) plain labour, which is the only evidenced mechanism: (B) says the publication tracing would require analysing hundreds or thousands of publications; (4) genuine conceptual obstacle, not mere silence: P8 — once a paper cites a catalogue number instead of a map, there is no paper-versus-material mismatch to count, so the exposure denominator is ill-defined in exactly the cases where reuse is best documented. (4) is the strongest candidate and is not a "no one thought of it" mechanism.
