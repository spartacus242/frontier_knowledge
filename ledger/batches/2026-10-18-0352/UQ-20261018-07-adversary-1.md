# UQ-20261018-07 — Adversary 1 log

## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

Has the average lithium-isotope ratio (δ7Li) of the lithium that industry actually sells each year moved over 2010–2025, because the feedstock mix (salar brine vs spodumene vs recycled scrap) changed and because refining fractionates the isotopes? And if you take that year's weighted average and plug it in as the "human-sourced lithium" value in a published study that computed how much of a river's lithium is anthropogenic (named index case: Choi et al. 2019, effluent endmember 14.5 ± 4.3‰), does that study's anthropogenic fraction move outside its own error bars?

Two limbs: (i) build a production-weighted δ7Li time series for commercial lithium with an interval; (ii) propagate it into a named apportionment.
