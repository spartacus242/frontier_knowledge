## Three kills, and the prior was the whole argument in all three

Every first-pass rejection in this batch rested on a single published prior said to share the candidate's estimand. Verification opened all three.

- **UQ-31** (is the ambient "soup" a quantitative record of the cells dissociation destroyed?) — kill FALLS. Janssen et al. (Genome Biology 24:140) was said to perform the candidate's central operation. Its Methods say "the endogenous single cell allele counts from the respective replicate were used as reference (qcthreshold = 0.6)": the reference is the run's own recovered cells, which structurally cannot contain a type the run failed to recover. The "differential destruction" reading is one hedged side remark — "suggesting that this cell type is more vulnerable to dissociation procedure than other cell types" — about a slight proximal-tubule enrichment, while the paper's snRNA-seq arm shows "a clear shift in cell type composition with a decreased PT fraction", in the opposite direction.
- **UQ-34** (is an organ's absolute cell number regulated as a total?) — kill FALLS. Herculano-Houzel et al. 2015 was said to share the candidate's unit, endpoint, comparator and estimand. It computes neither the variance of a total against the sum of its parts' variances nor a CV of whole against parts: Table 1's caption reads "Variation refers to the ratio between maximum and minimum values for each parameter", so the 1.63 the entry carried is a max/min fold ratio. Its cohort is "19 male mice of the Swiss variety, a non-isogenic strain" — not the genetically identical individuals the question is about. And its conclusion carries an exception the quotation had dropped: "(except between the rest of brain and the spinal cord)", which is the one negative neuron-count-against-neuron-count correlation it prints (rho −0.4820, p 0.0060).
- **UQ-32** (do trait databases share primary sources densely enough to inflate allometric exponents?) — kill CONFIRMED and strengthened. Genoud et al. 2018 state that of each analysis data set "In each only a single entry from the BMR database was used for any species included". One entry per species means two rows cannot share a measurement on specified individuals, so the headline quantity is degenerate at the unit the question freezes. The kill is now quoted rather than inferred.

**The sharp observation**: in both cases where the kill failed, the entry's OWN record described the prior correctly and the adversary contradicted it. UQ-31's record says of Janssen "reference = the run's own recovered cells"; the Gate 4 kill asserted a cell-type reference. The record had the refutation in it. When an adversary's kill contradicts the record it was handed, that disagreement is the place to check the source — not a sign that the record was sloppy.

## The late Gate 4, and what now caps the two revived entries

Both revived entries got a full eight-attack re-run against the Gate 0–3 record, in the literature-survey form (a disclosed departure from LEDGER.md's adversary form: cold context and two independent readers kept, the adversary's own framing given up). Neither came back unencumbered.

- **UQ-31** → Provisional, capped by a new significance objection. Two deployed tools already answer the question in the NO direction as a modelling assumption: SoupX's soup is "approximately uniform sampling of the cells in the sequencing batch" with background-to-cell expression correlations of 0.71–0.96 (median 0.86), and CellBender's ambient profile is "a weighted average of total expression". The one published counter-signal is CellBender's own aside that PBMC "ambient counts might be enriched with granulocyte lysates" although granulocytes are absent from the clusters. And the estimand is not yet identified: per-cell-type mRNA content is published for no solid tissue in anything opened, so the YES and NO arms stay confounded with RNA content per cell.
- **UQ-34** → Provisional, capped by a borrowed instrument and absent data. Schluter's variance ratio is standard in community ecology with a published null of 1 and a chi-square test (codyn: "A variance ratio = 1 indicates that species do not covary"), so the contribution is an application, not a method. And the computation cannot be done from any published table: Elkind et al. declare "Cell count (N) is the product V×D; therefore, it was not considered an independent variable" and publish per-region medians and densities with per-mouse volume and weight only; Herculano-Houzel's table censors every non-significant pair; Johansen et al. publish per-donor fractions. The question needs a new cohort or an unreleased count matrix. Then it must separate a biological variance from a technical one that was the LARGER of the two in the only isogenic dataset publishing both (Williams et al. 1996: technical CV 5.70% ± 0.1% against a corrected environmental CV of 4.60% ± 0.4%), with no subtraction formula verifiable in anything opened.

## What to carry forward

1. **When an adversary's kill contradicts the record it was handed, open the source.** Two of three kills here were refuted by text the record already quoted correctly.
2. **A prior that defines or bounds the candidate's quantity without computing it is not an answer**, and neither is a prior that computes a different statistic on a different population. UQ-34's near-duplicate failed on three of the four attributes it was said to share.
3. **Grade the read level of the thing the kill actually rests on.** DIEM's and Janssen's load-bearing content is in supplements that four separate passes could not open.
4. **A verification pass can make promotion harder.** UQ-33's null band and UQ-35's missing rate both tightened conditions rather than loosening them, and saying so is the point of the pass.
5. **Equalise found the rejections again.** The phase fired on UQ-31, UQ-32 and UQ-34 — all three of the batch's rejections, and the fifth consecutive batch in which every candidate it reached was a one-adversary rejection.
6. **Two generated index counts were wrong** (129 and 139 against a committed 120). Both are corrected in the entries; a generated count of the ledger's own size should be checked against `INDEX.md` before it is committed.
