Verification (post-batch, fresh-context checker, log UQ-20261017-02-verification.md): the kill survives, but not on the legs it was argued from, and one of its three cited documents carries none of the content attributed to it.
Checked leg by leg against the three documents adversary 3 opened. (a) Persistence-until-change: CONFIRMED in CCRI/12-05 only — "This presumes that there have been no significant changes in staff, equipment or measurement methods" — and a change triggers a new comparison. (b) Supersession: CONFIRMED in CCRI/12-05 only — an NMI "may have one result in each comparison, the most recent replacing any earlier participation". (c) Pooling of an institute's deviations across comparisons in CMC review: CCRI/12-05 **forbids** it — "If an NMI has more than one comparison supporting its capability, only one of these should be quoted", and "there is no averaging of results over several qualities" — so the adversary's attribution is wrong for that document. It is confirmed only in CCQM IAWG-GD-01, and there as a participation record rather than an aggregation of deviations: "the NMI/DI should participate in at least three studies" in ten years, at least ten points in the Record of Participation, 90% of points within -1 to +1. (d) Cross-measurand transfer: CONFIRMED in both, by fixed rule — named conversion cases and the Measurement Methods Matrix in CCRI/12-05, analyte-group and matrix-challenge cells of the Core Capability table in IAWG-GD-01. And the third document, the 2016 Recommendations from the Working Group on the CIPM MRA Review, addresses none of (a) to (d): the words supersede, replace, valid and validity do not occur in it, and its nearest content is a Key Point about "comprehensive in-house procedures for monitoring the stability of standards". Citing it for this kill was an error and it is struck.
The finding that matters is one neither the record nor the adversary stated. **No document gives any statistical model of persistence.** Nothing quantifies how well a result persists, how an older result loses weight, or how much uncertainty an extrapolation to another measurand adds; in CCRI/12-05 and IAWG-GD-01 validity and extrapolation are set by fixed time windows and fixed rules (ten years by default, fifteen or twenty for retention or removal from the KCDB). So the candidate's limbs (i) to (iii) — the variance share, the lag-1 autocorrelation and the cross-measurand correlation — are genuinely unmeasured, and the ledger records that rather than letting the rejection imply otherwise.
What the rejection now rests on is limb (iv), and on a sharper presupposition failure than the one argued. The limb asks what "the current per-comparison treatment of degrees of equivalence leaves out of the uncertainty a calibration user inherits" — and the documents show the degrees of equivalence do not feed that uncertainty at all. What a user inherits is the institute's own submitted CMC claim, validated against fixed participation rules. There is therefore no quantity being left out by a per-comparison treatment, because no per-comparison treatment produces the inherited uncertainty. Limb (iv) carries the entry's significance, so attack 4 stays fatal; but the entry's earlier ground, that the field "demonstrably does not hold" the independence presupposition and that the question is therefore already asked in policy, is narrowed to the part that verified, and the "asked and answered in policy" characterisation is struck — a presupposed persistence that nobody has measured is not an answered question.

That is a different candidate and is not graded here.
