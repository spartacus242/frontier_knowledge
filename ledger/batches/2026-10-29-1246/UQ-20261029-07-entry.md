Question (canonical): For data drawn from a generating process whose entropy rate H is known exactly by construction, does the loss achievable by a transformer family converge to H as parameters and tokens grow, or to a strictly positive floor H + Delta, and is Delta a property of the architecture rather than of the data? Alternative formulations: (1) Is the "irreducible loss" term of a neural scaling law the entropy of the data, or the sum of the data's entropy and an architecture-specific approximation floor? — judged on its own words this fails the object test (its blank is the interpretation of a published fit) and is not the headline wording, as the record itself concedes. (2) What is the asymptotic excess loss Delta = lim(achieved loss) − H on a process of exactly computable entropy rate, and how does Delta differ across architecture families at matched compute?
