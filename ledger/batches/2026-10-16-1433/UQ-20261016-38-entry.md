THE KILL SURVIVES, and it is the only one in this batch that survives whole: all four attributes confirmed, the decisive phrase found verbatim, and both absence findings confirmed. That is worth recording in a ledger where verification has withdrawn a kill in every recent batch.
(i) CONFIRMED on all four attributes, and on the two checks that make the Disguise verdict fatal rather than suggestive. The paper does compare the CONTENT of the two strata, not only the volume lost — topic and format composition, token loss, benchmark scores and verbatim-memorization rates are all computed across compliant and non-compliant corpora. And it contains NO missing-data machinery of any kind: no MAR, no MNAR, no ignorability, no selection model, no inverse-probability or propensity reweighting, and no attempt to correct or reweight the retained corpus to stand in for the pre-exclusion one. The record's reading was right on both, which is why deliverable (ii) survives as the candidate's only untouched part while the headline does not.
(ii) THE SIGN OF THE HEADLINE PAIR MUST BE STATED. 42.5 is the NON-COMPLIANT average and 42.8 the COMPLIANT one (Table 2): the compliant model scores HIGHER by 0.3. Written as "42.5 vs 42.8" without naming which is which, it invites the reading that compliance cost 0.3 points, when the sign is the opposite.
(iii) AND THE PAIR CARRIES NO UNCERTAINTY. Tables 2 and 4 report point values with no seeds, standard deviations or confidence intervals, so there is nothing to carry through and the 0.3 gap cannot be distinguished from run-to-run noise at 1.5B parameters and 100B tokens. The defensible statement is "no detectable difference", not "a measured near-zero effect" — a distinction that matters, because the candidate's own nominated answer was that the effect is small, and this paper cannot bound how small.
(iv) Three numbers need qualifying. The 8% token loss cannot be independently recomputed: the paper gives no absolute token counts for the two corpora, the only absolute figure being "the compliant dataset (4.5T tokens)" with no non-compliant counterpart. It must not be conflated with the separate "approximate 4% reduction in total tokens" for the news-removed variant. And the LCCS movement 23.11 -> 21.27 is in a unit the paper never states, over a computation it never describes; the non-integral values imply a mean over the 12,800 articles, which the paper does not label as a mean.
(v) The "minimal" phrase is real and hedged inside its own sentence, which goes on to name a decrease in News Articles and in Science & Tech and an increase in Personal Blogs. Quoting the fragment alone overstates the paper's own claim.
(vi) The comparator set is LARGER than the record states, and the enlargement is where the one positive finding lives. Besides the unfiltered/filtered contrast and the subtractive "-News" row, Table 3 carries three ADDITIVE variants — "Compliant + News", "Compliant + Med", "Compliant + MathSci" — and the medical signal comes from that continual-pretraining arm, not from the from-scratch arm. Relatedly, the estimand exists in two forms the record collapses into one: M1, exact, training from scratch, and M2, an approximation via continual pretraining. The endpoint list also omits Table 1, the percentage of documents with a 50-gram match to nytimes.com, medicalxpress.com and stackexchange.com, which is a fourth cross-strata statistic.
(vii) "Only medical" is partly an artefact of measurement. Appendix A states the authors "are unable to reliably evaluate domain-specific knowledge in Math & Science", so the absence of a signal there is not evidence of its absence.
(viii) Two scope claims are downgraded. "Confined to education-filtered FineWeb-Edu" is unverified for the continual-pretraining arm, which the paper never states is FineWeb-Edu only. And "uses only the most recent robots.txt" is right for the stratification but not for the paper: Figure 1 does use historical robots.txt via the Internet Archive. The consequence the record drew is nonetheless sharpened — because stratification uses today's robots.txt, a document crawled lawfully years ago counts as non-compliant if its domain blocks now, so the strata are a "what if we re-crawled today" counterfactual rather than a record of what any crawler actually took.
(ix) VENUE RESOLVED, and the record's caution was warranted. It is an ICML 2025 WORKSHOP poster (the DataWorld workshop) per icml.cc, not a main-conference paper, and no venue statement appears on the arXiv page, in the paper's header, or in any DOI record. Citing it as "ICML 2025" unqualified would be mis-attribution.
(x) One arithmetic discrepancy found and left open: Table 2's "-News" row prints ten values summing to 427.5, a mean of 42.75, against a printed 42.6 — an unreconciled 0.15. It does not touch the 42.5/42.8 pair the verdict uses.
(xi) Fidelity caveat: raw HTTP to arxiv.org was refused by the egress policy, so the full text was reachable only through a fetch tool that refuses quotations beyond about 125 characters. Quotes are short fragments obtained through a mediating model rather than a local grep, and the absence findings in (i) are tool-reported searches rather than verified greps — strong, but not the same thing.
