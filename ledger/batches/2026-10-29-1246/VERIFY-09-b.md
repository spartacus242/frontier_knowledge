## Regime of the source

Population: untrained dynamical systems — a driven logistic map; an echo state network "with N 5 50 variables" (= 50; Figure 1 caption "an ESN with 50 nodes"); a Gray–Scott reaction–diffusion system where "The concentration of A is observed at N discrete positions (25 in our experiments)". Unit: one system's observed state trajectory recorded "for a duration T 5 10^6 time steps" (= 1e6). Operating condition: inputs "independent and identically drawn from some probability distribution p(u)", with "p(u) ... the uniform distribution over the interval [–1, 1]"; gain r and input scaling varied; noise-free except Fig. 4, where noise is a second unobserved i.i.d. input v at a stated SNR in dB. Instrument: a linear estimator — "Consider a linear estimator constructed from the observed data" — least-squares over weights W on the N observed variables, target functions = "finite products of normalized Legendre polynomials" of past inputs. Threshold: estimated capacities below θ set to zero (θ = 1.7e-4 ESN, 1.1e-4 RD, 2.2e-5 logistic map — summariser paraphrase of the printed values). Category: a dimensionless count — total capacity "interpreted as the total number of linearly independent functions of its stimuli the system can compute".

Is the candidate INSIDE this regime? No. Trained LMs, not untrained reservoirs; discrete tokens from a 65,024-symbol vocabulary, not continuous uniform scalars; N ≈ 1.05e7 carried elements, not 25–50; decoder family D1–D3 of unstated linearity, where IPC requires a linear readout; a bits numerator, where IPC counts functions. The paper declares its framework extensible — "The distribution p(u) can be chosen according to what is most relevant" and "Most of the theory should also hold for the non-i.i.d. case" — but does not apply it to discrete-alphabet inputs or to any trained network.

## (3) Does the normaliser depend on the input's entropy or on the system's state?

In the paper's words, the bound N is "independently of h, of the set YL, or of its size |YL| = L" — independent of the input-history length h, of which orthonormal functions are used and how many. The input distribution is a modelling choice ("The distribution p(u) can be chosen according to what is most relevant"; "Most of the theory should also hold for the non-i.i.d. case"), and the Discussion makes the normalised total a property of the system alone ("the same total normalized capacity to process information ... independent of their physical realization"). The word "entropy" does not occur (two dedicated sweeps, one extraction), so the paper never frames the input's information content at all; what it establishes is that the ceiling N is set by the SYSTEM (its observed state dimension), not by the stimulus.

So the factual predicate the entry stakes its vulnerability on — a nominal capacity equal to the number of state variables with no precision multiplier, with realized totals reported against it — IS what this paper does. The realized/nominal fraction here is C_TOT/N; Theorem 7 says it tends to 1 for any noiseless fading-memory system with linearly independent variables as T → ∞ and the basis is completed.

MY OWN REASONING, marked as such (not in the paper): the IPC total is also bounded by the number of orthonormal functions that exist on the input space — for a prefix of L tokens i.i.d. uniform over |V| symbols that space has dimension |V|^L. For the candidate's Falcon-Mamba-7B frame (N from the entry's own config arithmetic, re-run: per layer 8192×16 + 8192×4 = 163,840 elements; ×64 layers = 10,485,760 = 1.0486e7; ×16 bits = 1.678e8 bits, matching the entry) and |V| = 65,024 (log10 = 4.8131): |V|^1 = 65,024 < N (cap binds only at L = 1, outside the ladder); |V|^2 = 4.23e9 > N; |V|^16 = 10^77.0; |V|^4096 = 10^19714. Hence min(N, |V|^L) = N at every declared rung L ∈ {16, 64, 256, 1024, 4096}: an IPC-TYPE realized fraction is not pinned by the prefix ladder. What would have grounded this beyond my arithmetic: a source applying IPC to finite-alphabet inputs; none was opened.

## Checking the entry's and brief's statements against the record

- "total capacity is normalised to the NUMBER OF STATE VARIABLES" — CONFIRMED (Theorem 4; N = number of linearly independent observed variables).
- "with no precision multiplier" — CONFIRMED (two dedicated term sweeps, one extraction; noise modelled as an input, not as precision).
- "realized fractions near saturation are routinely reported" — PARTIAL. In-paper: "For very small input scaling, the capacity CTOT remains close to N" "up to the bifurcation point of the undriven system and then drops off rapidly" (section "Total capacity", re Fig. 1); Fig. 4 plots "The fraction of the total capacity which is usable" against SNR. But read D's dedicated check: "The paper reports no specific numerical value of C_TOT for any system" — the fraction is PLOTTED, not printed as a number (the same defect this ledger charged against arXiv:2504.19561). "Routinely" is a field-level universal that one paper cannot confirm.
- "the ceiling is an artefact of the x-precision multiplier rather than of the question" — DOES NOT FOLLOW, by my arithmetic (mine, from the entry's own inputs). The bits ceiling re-run: log2(65024) = 15.989; L=16 → 255.8 bits / 1.678e8 = 1.52e-6 (entry 1.5e-6, agrees); L=4096 → 65,490 bits / 1.678e8 = 3.90e-4 (entry 3.9e-4, agrees). Deleting the ×16 precision multiplier and normalising bits by N = 1.0486e7 variables gives ceilings of 2.44e-5 (L=16) and 6.25e-3 (L=4096) bits per state variable; a ceiling of 1 would need L = 1.0486e7/15.989 = 6.56e5 tokens (vs 1.05e7 with the multiplier). The precision multiplier accounts for a factor of 16 of the slack; the remaining ~160× at L=4096 is the bits numerator against the ladder. IPC escapes the cap not by dropping ×b but because its NUMERATOR is a different kind of quantity (a count of linearly reconstructable orthonormal functions, bounded by |V|^L, a dimension) rather than bits (bounded by log2 of that dimension). Comparing the two ceilings (1 vs 3.9e-4) is therefore a comparison across kinds and is not a deficit of the same thing.
- Adversary 1's recollection "the reservoir literature's own normaliser is the number of state variables, not x precision" — CONFIRMED for this primary.
- Entry's route note "Try a UGent biblio or ULB difusion mirror" — CORRECT: the ULB DI-fusion PDF served to WebFetch; the UGent handle exists (not opened).
- Brief wording "independent of how much entropy the input stream carries" — the paper never speaks of entropy; its independence is from h, Y_L and |Y_L|, with p(u) a free choice. A terminology mismatch, not an error of substance.
- Brief: "Nobody in the batch opened it" — consistent with all three logs (record: not returned; A1: budget; A2: declined by choice).

## Bearing (no verdict recommended)

For the judge: the entry's pre-declared condition is met on its factual predicates — the method-owning primary normalises total capacity by the number of state variables, has no precision term, proves C_TOT ≤ N with equality under fading memory, and reports (qualitatively, by plot) totals close to N. On the entry's own terms that moves the kill from "dissolution" toward "repairable normaliser defect". Two things cut the other way and are the paper's own words plus my arithmetic: (a) the repair is not the deletion of ×precision — with a bits numerator the ladder-pinned ceiling survives at ≤ 6.25e-3 per variable; the repair that removes the cap is adopting the IPC's function-count numerator, i.e. a different quantity from the one the canonical question asks in bits; (b) under that repaired quantity, Theorem 7 makes C_TOT/N tend to 1 for ANY noiseless fading-memory system with linearly independent variables — "all dynamical systems, provided they obey the condition of having a fading memory ... have in principle the same total normalized capacity" — so the realized fraction would be pinned near 1 by theorem rather than near 0 by the ladder, and what the IPC literature actually reads off is the allocation (memory vs non-linearity, Fig. 2–3 regime; usable fraction under noise, Fig. 4), not the total. Also (mine): the paper computes IPC by least squares on N ≤ 50 variables with T = 1e6 samples and thresholds ~1e-4; it offers no scaling to N ≈ 1e7. On novelty: within two sweeps and one opened IPC paper, no application of IPC to a shipped transformer-alternative LM was found; the negative is sweep-bounded and a citation-index enumeration was not run.
