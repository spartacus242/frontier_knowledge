Verification (post-batch, two fresh-context checkers, logs UQ-20261016-63-verification.md and UQ-20261016-63-verification-2.md): the first of the two Gate 2 grounds is WITHDRAWN, the second is CONFIRMED and sharpened, and the rejection stands on the second alone.
Ground (i) falls on unit. Prior A is Zavalis EA, Pezzullo AM, Ioannidis JPA, "Instability of Global Burden of Disease Estimates of Deaths and Disability-Adjusted Life-Years From Major Risk Factors: A Meta-Epidemiological Analysis", JAMA Health Forum 2026;7(3):e260108 — and it is not a country-year analysis. Its unit is risk factor crossed with year, reported mostly as GLOBAL totals for 66 harmonised risk factors across eight GBD iterations (2010, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023), with sex and World Bank region strata only for dietary risks and low physical activity. Its endpoint is absolute annual deaths and DALYs attributable to a risk factor; its estimand is descriptive dispersion across releases (range-to-mean ratio, coefficient of variation, share of point estimates outside the previous iteration's 95% uncertainty interval), reported as median R:M 0.8 for deaths and 0.7 for DALYs, same-year original-versus-revised median R:M 0.4, CV above 0.2 for 336 of 675 death estimates, and GBD 2021 points outside GBD 2019's interval for 70-96% of several dietary risks. It does NOT test whether a revision is predictable from the prior release's value, and it does NOT re-run any published analysis on a later vintage — its own limitation says the analyses "were limited to published GBD estimates rather than primary data or replication of underlying models". So it does not answer limb (a) on the candidate's unit (country-year coverage and mortality indicators), and it does not touch limbs (b) or (c) at all. The entry's earlier claim that Prior A asks and answers limb (a) "in the candidate's own field, in its own words", and that it falsifies the motivating gap as written, is struck. What survives is weaker and still relevant: release-to-release instability in modelled global-health estimates is a live published concern with headline numbers attached, so the gap is not virgin territory even though the candidate's quantity is unmeasured.
Ground (ii) is confirmed, and one leg of it is closer to the candidate's object than the record claimed. Wolff H, Chong H, Auffhammer M, "Classification, Detection and Consequences of Data Error: Evidence from the Human Development Index", Economic Journal 2011;121(553):843-870 (read in the NBER w16572 working-paper version): unit country(-year) HDI components and index; endpoint HDI value, rank and development category; comparator the originally published vintage against the revised one; estimand the size of the revision and its consequences, with "up to 34% of countries are misclassified" and prior-study coefficients shifting by up to 100%. Its components include life expectancy from the "Population Division of the UN Department of Economic and Social Affairs" — so not national accounts and education only, and the record's reading that the components exclude any UN-produced demographic series is wrong — with Table 6 giving country-level standard deviations of that component's revisions and the text reporting, verbatim, "In contrast, life expectancy is revised much less." It also re-runs a published analysis on a later vintage (Noorbakhsh 2006's convergence regression, Table 8), and reports that "key parameters... vary by up to 100%". Two limits on how far it reaches: it is not an efficiency or serial-correlation test, so limb (b) has no precedent even here, and it includes no WHO or UNICEF series, so the candidate's primary indicator is not in its corpus. Goes C, I4R Discussion Paper 23 (2023): 459 International Organization articles 2005-2020, 173 using cross-country macro data, three published studies re-run across WDI, PWT and Maddison vintages (de Soysa & Neumayer 2005; Vreeland 2008; Goldstein, Rivers & Tomz 2007); unit country-year or dyad-year, endpoint the key coefficient's sign and significance, estimand vintage sensitivity of a regression coefficient. So each of the candidate's three limbs is an executed design pointed at a different corpus, and one precedent has already pointed it at a UN-modelled health series.
The object itself remains unmeasured: four further searches here, on top of the record's twenty, found no revision, serial-correlation or efficiency analysis of WUENIC, UN IGME or MMEIG release vintages — only methods-description papers and agency documentation. That is why this entry's rejection is a rule application, not a demonstration that the question has been answered, and it is recorded as such below.
