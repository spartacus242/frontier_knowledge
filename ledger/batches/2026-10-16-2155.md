## What verification found

- **UQ-56: the kill's source is real and says what was claimed, on a weaker footing than the batch
  implied.** DOI 10.18699/vjgb-25-26 is Lukhtanov & Pazhenkova, "Cytogenetics of insects in the era
  of chromosome-level genome assemblies" (Vavilov J Genet Breed 29(2):230-237, 2025), and the quotes
  check out verbatim: assemblies for "452 species", microscopy counts for "more than half of them",
  and "complete agreement in the haploid chromosome number calculations". But it is a narrative
  review: the comparison is one summary sentence, the compared N is never given exactly, and there is
  no per-species table, no listed exception, no statistic and no model. It settles the candidate's
  premise — in the clade with the densest data the instruments agree and the second limb has no
  signal to propagate — so the rejection stands on a corpus extension plus a conditional sensitivity
  analysis. It does not supply the measured rate the kill implied, and the entry says so.
- **UQ-57: the quote is real, the estimand is not the candidate's.** de Visser & Lenski 2002 does say
  "the ANOVA tests all forms of non-transitivity", with the interaction term under 2% of variation at
  P > 0.5. But the design is one population (Ara-1) on a fixed 21 × 5 grid of time-point samples
  against marked clones — 315 competitions, not a round robin — and the estimand is an interaction
  variance share, not a rate of intransitive triples against an assay-error null. No triple counts
  appear anywhere in it. The rejection holds because the *question* was asked and answered negatively
  inside the LTEE's own series and re-checked against 40,000-generation reference clones by the very
  power-law paper the candidate targets; what the candidate adds is estimator machinery. The
  triple-level rate is genuinely unmeasured and is recorded for a future candidate's own Gate 0.
- **UQ-58: the cap is confirmed from documents rather than argument.** Of the operational documents
  opened, none mentions RIN or RQN at all. The Sanger Tree of Life core-lab paper gates on total
  yield (over 100 ng) via Qubit and Nanodrop, retrying failures with another individual or tissue;
  the manual TRIzol and triage protocols contain no RIN, RQN or 28S text. So the primary limb has no
  documented decision channel in the programmes the question names — in these documents the integrity
  score is not the gate, yield is. That points at the limb worth keeping: the equivalence mapping
  between RIN values in break-bearing and break-free taxa needs no decision channel, and nothing
  opened measures it. Four documents remain unread (the full EBP standards report, the Sanger "RNA
  Decision Making Tree" appendix, DToL manifests, generic core-facility thresholds) and are listed as
  promotion conditions rather than as checks that passed.
- **UQ-59: the arithmetic holds, two decorations around it do not.** "All 17 confirmed SNVs were
  heterozygous" and "We identified 1,832,554 heterozygous sites common to both samples" are both in
  Schmid-Siegert et al. (bioRxiv 149203; Nature Plants 3:926-929, 2017) — one paper, one pipeline, so
  the division is legitimate and the between-branch shift is of order 1e-5. Struck: the "17–113"
  range, since 113 appears in no source opened (the only other oak figure found is 46 across three
  branches, reported second-hand from Plomion et al. 2018); and "four orders below the technical
  false-positive load", since the paper states no false-positive load, only an F-score above 95%.
  The entry also now records that the inference about heterozygosity, kinship and Ne is this ledger's
  own, drawn from the paper's two numbers, not the paper's conclusion.
- **UQ-60: confirmed on both halves.** Kuntz & Eisen 2014 scored 36 landmarks per embryo at seven
  temperatures: total time "varies over two fold" while "the relative timing of events from
  cellularization through hatching is constant", in "the same temporal order", across ten further
  species. The 2015 companion reports that oxygen does not rescale uniformly and that "the order of
  several developmental landmarks is inverted at different oxygen levels" — head involution against
  heart-shaped midgut, with the inversion point moving from about 29% O2 at 27.5 °C to about 19% at
  17.5 °C, over 800+ embryos in 18 conditions. Qualifications recorded: the evidence is morphological
  rather than transcriptomic, the oxygen strings are single-sourced through a page summariser, and
  RAPToR reports R² rather than a staging error in time units, so the record's phrasing there was
  imprecise.
