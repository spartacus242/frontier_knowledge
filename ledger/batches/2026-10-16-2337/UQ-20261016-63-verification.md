## Findings
Q1. Measures instability of GBD estimates of absolute annual deaths and DALYs attributable to 66 risk factors, 8 iterations (GBD 2010, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023). Unit is risk factor x year, mostly GLOBAL totals; sex and World Bank region breakdown only for dietary risks and low physical activity. NOT a country-year analysis ("Country" only in passing). So the "same country-year across eight releases" claim is wrong on unit: same risk-factor-year (global), eight iterations right. Headline: median R:M 0.8 (deaths), 0.7 (DALYs) across iterations; same-year original vs revised median R:M 0.4; CV >0.2 for 336/675 death estimates (50%); GBD 2021 point estimates outside GBD 2019 95% UI for 70-96% of several dietary risks (SSB 96% per preprint).
Q2. No test of predictability/forecastability of revision from prior release value (summariser: neither examined; descriptive).
Q3. No re-run of published analyses to see if conclusions change. Only compares published GBD estimates; own limitation says no replication.
Q4. Unit: risk-factor-year absolute deaths/DALYs (global; some sex/region strata). Endpoint: deaths and DALYs attributable to risk factors. Comparator: same estimate across other GBD iterations (range across 8; original vs latest revision for same year 1990-2021; point estimate vs previous iteration's 95% UI). Estimand: descriptive dispersion across releases (R:M, CV, share of point estimates outside prior UI); not bias, not causal.
