## Findings
1. Exists. Two versions: Protocol published 20 May 2016 (Baak, Cantineau, Farquhar, Brison) and completed Review (pub2) published 17 Sep 2019, CDSR 2019 Issue 9, Art. No. CD012192; authors Nora A Baak, Astrid EP Cantineau, Cindy Farquhar, Daniel R Brison. Search last run 6 March 2019. The DOI as given (no .pub2) resolves to the protocol version; I read the review.
2. Unit of randomisation: mixed across included trials. Fawzy 2018 per woman; Hong 2014 and De Munck 2019 randomised oocytes/embryos (paired design); De Munck 2019 rated high risk of bias for this. Primary outcomes (per summariser): live birth and miscarriage, per woman. Review-level unit of analysis = woman. Protocol's stated unit not read.
3. Comparison: 37 C vs lower temperatures: 36.5 (Fawzy), 36.0 (Hong), 36.6 vs 37.1 (De Munck). Eligibility minimum difference "≥ 0.5 degrees Celsius" between incubators (single short summariser quote). Plain-language summary says 37.0 or 37.1 vs 36.0-36.6; 3 RCTs, 563 women.
4. Effect measure: odds ratios, Mantel-Haenszel fixed-effect, 95% CI. Population: ITT as far as possible (summariser).
5. Reports results (review): miscarriage OR 0.90 (0.52-1.55), 1 study, n=412; clinical pregnancy OR 1.08 (0.73-1.60); ongoing pregnancy OR 1.10 (0.75-1.62); multiple pregnancy OR 0.80 (0.31-2.07). Live birth not analysable (Hong 2014: 12 singleton births among 36 women, P=0.38, not interpretable because randomised by egg with paired transfers). All GRADE very low. Adverse events mostly not reported. Conclusion: no evidence lower temp improves live birth or any outcome. Protocol-only? No: the review exists.
6. Cell-level physiological fidelity: not an outcome or framing question. Background only, per summariser: temperature "a few degrees above core temperature in cattle has been reported to be detrimental to cells" (fragment as returned by summariser, not verified verbatim), plus discussion of human oocyte spindle sensitivity to high temperature, and (plain-language summary) rationale that the female reproductive tract may be slightly cooler than core 37 C. Outcomes are clinical (live birth, miscarriage, pregnancy). Not read: protocol background, full-text discussion.
