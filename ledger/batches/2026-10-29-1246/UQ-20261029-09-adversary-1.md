## Restatement (my own words)

Take a deployed language model whose whole memory of what it has read so far is a tensor of fixed shape (a Mamba-2-type model, Falcon-Mamba, or the recurrent part of a hybrid). Feed it a prefix of known entropy, stop, read out that tensor, and ask two things: (i) how many bits about the prefix can a trained read-out recover from the tensor, as a function of prefix length; and (ii) what is that number divided by the tensor's element count times its float bit-width — i.e. what share of the "bits the tensor could hold" does the trained model actually put prefix information into. The record's reformulation fixes the prefix family (i.i.d. uniform over the vocabulary, L ∈ {16, 64, 256, 1024, 4096}), the decoder family (linear / MLP / state-initialised autoregressive), the state boundary (`ssm_state` + `conv_state` per layer; hybrid attention KV ablated or counted) and the numerator (L·log2|V| minus the decoder's summed per-position cross-entropy, in bits).

  - (my reading of the record, line 40) the numerator is "L·log2|V| minus the decoder's per-position cross-entropy summed over positions, in bits". Cross-entropy of a discrete predictive distribution is non-negative (−log p ≥ 0 for p ≤ 1; MY OWN ELEMENTARY REASONING, no source needed beyond the definition), so **recovered bits ≤ L·log2|V|** for every decoder, however good.
  - (GHOST, F4, printed) per-layer state memory for the main Mamba2 model: "2.1 MB per layer in float 32" (Appendix A); layer count: "48 layers with 128 states each" (Figure 1 caption). MY INFERENCE: these describe Mamba2-1.3B, the paper's main experimental model (§5 says most experiments use Mamba2-1.3B); 2.1 MB = 4096 channels × 128 states × 4 bytes, consistent with d_inner=4096. If the figure belonged to a different member of the family the conclusion below changes by at most a factor of ~4 in either direction and does not reverse.
  - MY ARITHMETIC: nominal bits (fp32, as GHOST states the precision) = 48 × 2.1 MB = 100.8 MB = 8.06 × 10^8 bits; in bf16 (the record's likely stored dtype) 4.03 × 10^8 bits. `conv_state` adds a few percent and only raises the denominator.
  - MY ARITHMETIC: at the ladder's largest L = 4096, with any vocabulary |V| ≤ 2^17 = 131,072 (the GPT-NeoX tokenizer Mamba-2 uses is ~50k by my recollection — UNVERIFIED, but the bound below is stated for every |V| up to 2^17 and barely moves at 2^20), the numerator is ≤ 4096 × 17 = 69,632 bits.
  - Therefore **the headline fraction at L = 4096 is ≤ 69,632 / 4.03 × 10^8 ≈ 1.7 × 10^-4 (bf16) or ≤ 8.6 × 10^-5 (fp32), whatever the state does.** At L = 16 it is ≤ 7 × 10^-7. For the fraction to reach 0.5 the prefix would need L ≥ 0.5 × 4.03 × 10^8 / 17 ≈ 1.2 × 10^7 tokens — three orders of magnitude beyond the ladder, and beyond any training length the record's Prior 2 reports for these models (I did not open Prior 2; this last clause is the record's report, not mine). For Falcon-Mamba-7B (N = 16 per F3; d_inner and layer count UNVERIFIED — my recollection is 8192 × 64 layers, which would give ~1.7 × 10^8 bf16 bits for `ssm_state` alone) the ceiling at L = 4096 would be ≈ 4 × 10^-4; even at the smallest shipped Mamba2 (130M; dims UNVERIFIED by me) it would be ≲ 10^-3. The two huggingface.co config files that would have replaced these recollections were refused to curl (403, tool-scoped) and I had no WebFetch left; the GHOST-based figure above is the sourced leg and suffices.
  - Consequence (MY REASONING ABOUT THE CLAIM'S LOGIC): on the declared frame the "fraction of nominal capacity realized" is a number about the prefix ladder, not about the model's state code. A perfect decoder and a useless one differ by at most a factor of 10^4 *below* 10^-4 — both outcomes are "a tiny fraction", so the record's dichotomy (small fraction ⇒ badly coded state, state growth is the wrong lever; near 1 ⇒ information-theoretic wall) cannot be reached by any observation the frame permits. The denominator is also ratio-invariant across L within a model, so the "fraction" curve is the recovered-bits curve rescaled by a per-model constant: the second limb collapses into the first, and the first is Hossain et al.'s instrument (record Prior 1) reported in bits instead of ROUGE.
  - What would rescue C5: a frame with L of order nominal/log2|V| (≈ 10^7 tokens for a 1.3B model, far more at 7B), or a denominator that is not numel × float width. The record's single permitted reformulation is spent and introduced the ladder, so this cannot be repaired without a new candidate.
