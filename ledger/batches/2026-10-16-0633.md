## Verification pass, and what it changed

Every verdict's load-bearing prior or kill was re-tested by a fresh-context checker that had the claim and the source but not the reviewer's reasoning (5 checkers, 82 queries, 1 safety refusal), and the signal-processing claims behind one rejection were re-derived from primary sources with numerical checks. Four of the five entries needed correction, and one verdict changed.

- **UQ-18 Rejected → Provisional.** Adversary #1 killed it on the claim that a loop sampling every T cannot oscillate at period T ("minimum limit-cycle period 2T"), so the pre-registered band at 1/T measures only the actuation step-train comb. All three legs fail: Gawthrop (arXiv:1705.08228 §3.3) derives a limit cycle of period *exactly* one intermittent interval; the Nyquist-style 2T bound is a property of the observation record, not of the loop; and the zero-order-hold envelope has its exact *nulls* at f = k/T, so a stationary staircase carries a spectral minimum at period T, not a peak (verified numerically, comb/background 0.099 at k = 1). The surviving half of the objection — that a Fourier-phase-randomised surrogate preserves the periodogram exactly (Theiler et al. 1992 §§2.2.3, 2.4.1; verified to 4.5e-13) — is real but reaches only one of the four statistic/null pairings: the block-shuffled surrogate does not preserve the spectrum, and magnitude-squared coherence is normalised by both auto-spectra, so independently phase-randomised surrogates are in fact the field's *recommended* coherence floor (Prichard & Theiler 1994 Eq. 4; Faes et al., IEEE TBME 2004;51(7):1156–1166; measured, data MSC 0.985 vs surrogate mean 0.040). Adversary #2, run fresh with none of this, found the same defect at source and also graded it Partial. One real finding did survive in a new form and is logged as a confound: under a *drifting* held level — steady weaning — the hold error is a sawtooth putting lines at exactly k/T (comb/background 6.8e9), so 1/T cannot serve as a built-in negative control without detrending.
- **UQ-17's triviality ground withdrawn; rejection re-grounded on attack 4 alone.** The entry reported that the heparin International Standard chain had been checked retrospectively and that its generations "recover their labelled potencies to within +0% to +11.5%". The Gray 2017 deck contains one slide with three rows (1st, 2nd and 3rd IS against the 5th IS in 2008) — 3 of 6 generations, no 4th IS row, no extractable values on the 6th IS slide. The "+0% to +11.5%" figure is not in the document: it is arithmetic on the point estimates, which reproduces exactly, but carried through the document's own 95% confidence limits the envelope is **−7.5% to +20.8%**, wider than the 10% relevance threshold the question names. The document attributes the 130→173 IU/mg change to nothing; the source-material reading is an inference from a species column. What *is* confirmed, and what kills the motivating anomaly, is that each standard measures close to its own label (130-labelled materials measure 135–145, the 173-labelled 173–178). But the two things the candidate actually asked for — a chain-propagated uncertainty budget, and a comparison to a pharmacopoeial or clinical threshold — appear nowhere in that source, so the question is not answered and not trivial on its strength.
- **UQ-20 novelty cut from Medium to Low on limb (a).** The Disguise caveat closed against the candidate: neither unopened item was a prior (DNA Scanner is a vendor price aggregator with no classifier; Sc2.0 quantifies what it changed, never what could not be made), but a closer prior both gates missed does exist — Halper et al. 2020 ran a vendor-trained classifier over the whole *E. coli* MG1655 genome (~15% of 2.5 kb fragments, 15.5% of CDSs not readily synthesizable) and 4,474 plasmids (34.4% readily synthesizable), with SCP4ssd (13%) and Zheng et al. 2023 reproducing and extending it.
- **UQ-19's falsification confirmed and strengthened.** Linking pre-publication process records to publications to measure non-reporting is an established tradition in three families (animal-ethics protocol cohorts; observatory time-allocation records; protocol/registry/grant comparisons). The sharpest counterexample, which the entry did not cite, is Chan et al. 2004: ethics-committee protocols rather than published artefacts, *and* self-report failing — 86% of trialists denied unreported outcomes their own protocols proved existed.
- **UQ-16's novelty arm executed at last and confirmed.** Its Gate 2 had never reached a bibliographic database in three sessions. Wright & Auwaerter is conjecture with no era data on fever height. The closest thing found, Gurven et al.
