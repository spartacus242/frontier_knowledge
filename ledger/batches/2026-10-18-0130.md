The rule that follows is narrow enough to apply mechanically: **brief a verification reader

back as "does not exist" is how a verification pass invents a defect.

## UQ-02 — Provisional: how much of a labour curve's shape is measurement error?

Validated stays barred independently by Gate 5's "Medium novelty with a close neighbour"
clause, so the corrections below demote the supporting record without moving the verdict.

Beyond the three void briefs discussed above, the re-check against the real identifiers found
three things:

- **Two of the three attributed quotations are paraphrases presented as quotations.** Phelps
  actually reads "an important consideration when evaluating dysfunctional labor", not
  "relevant when evaluating dysfunctional labor"; Hanidu actually reads "can contribute to
  variability in clinical decisions within obstetrics", not "can contribute to variable

- **Hanidu's 147 pairs are not same-time-point, and this is a real demotion.** The Methods

**The most useful thing the pass found is the pattern across all three voicings: none of the
three papers measures digital-examination error against a labouring woman's true dilatation.**
Phelps used PVC pipes, Roper used printed circles, Hanidu used ultrasound as the comparator.
That is why the question survives at all, and it is now stated that way in the entry.

## UQ-03 — Rejected: ground versus decoration, and the ground held

The question asks whether a within-operator recency–outcome association is real,

That is a **decoration, not the ground** — the Gate 2 Low rests on P1 being a near-duplicate
read in full, which held — so under the ground-versus-decoration rule the objection was
rewritten and **no late Gate 4 was owed**. The entry says which leg fell and what carries the
grade without it.

A prior nobody had read, bearing directly on the interval limb: **Hockenberry, Lien & Chou
2008** (J Human Capital 2(3):303-335, doi 10.1086/593064) estimates 1-month mortality for

## What to carry forward

1. **Brief a verification reader with the full citation the entry carries, never a label from
   a summary, and never ask a narrower question than the entry claims.** Three briefs broke
   this rule in one pass and manufactured a false unlocatable-citation finding plus two false
   title defects.
2. **Never let a reader put the user's email — or any personal identifier — into an API query
   string.** Unpaywall's courtesy parameter is not a reason. Skip the API.
3. **A paraphrase presented as a verbatim quotation is a defect class of its own**, and it is
   invisible unless each quoted string is checked alone. Two of three attributed quotations in
   UQ-02 were paraphrases; the other two quotations in the same entry were verbatim.
4. **A prior can sit at the wrong level of aggregation.** Van Gestel's exposures are per
   hospital with hospital fixed effects, which makes it centre-level and not the
   operator-level swap it was read as.
5. **A paper can misquote its own first author's earlier numbers.** WP 20017's "11–14%" is
   not in Hockenberry 2008, whose coefficients imply about 17%.
6. **"A figure is the paper's own and correctly quoted" does not make it the right
   denominator.** Yasuda's 3,410 PIVCs are real; the noradrenaline analysis runs on 91 of
   them.
7. **A verdict-unchanged pass is not a wasted pass.** Nothing moved here, and the pass still
   withdrew one false decoration, demoted one presupposition, cut a three-fold falsification
   to one instance, discharged a demotion condition and a promotion item, and found two priors
   and one unread paper nobody had.
