4. Established concept, novel application. Concept is B17C / HEC-SSP; paper quotes B17C "the perception thresholds do not depend on the actual peak discharges that have occurred" and Conclusion "perception thresholds are primarily intended for unobserved historic flood representation." Intro: "introduces the concept of Perception threshold that can be used for missing data representation." Using them for gaps inside the systematic record is the authors' proposal. Methods: a threshold "is set to indicate that missing data is smaller than some flow value".
5. Does NOT model a probability of missingness as a function of magnitude. Gaps are deterministic, chosen by design (A/B/C pick min/max/average-peak years), so gap selection is magnitude-dependent by scenario, but missing peaks are then represented as interval/threshold data (bounded by lower bound, upper infinity). Thresholds set from the observed record, not from a missingness model. (Summariser inference for "no probability model": no passage found; not a full-text grep guarantee.)
