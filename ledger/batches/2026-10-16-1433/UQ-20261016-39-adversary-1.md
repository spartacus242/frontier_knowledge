# UQ-20261016-39 — Adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my own words)

Take one dynamic total-body PET scan. Fit each organ's compartmental model on its own, as everybody does. Multiply each fitted organ curve by the organ's volume, add them up, add the activity that has left the body or the field of view, add a pre-declared ceiling for tissue you could not segment, and compare the sum with the decay-corrected activity you injected. (a) Does the books balance within the propagated error? (b) If not, redo all the fits at once with the balance forced to hold, and say which organ's K1, Ki, Vt and absorbed dose changed, which way, and by how much.

Two things I will keep separate that the record runs together: (i) whether the IMAGE accounts for the injected activity, and (ii) whether the KINETIC PARAMETERS are biased. The record's estimand is built from fitted curves, so the question is only interesting if R(t) actually depends on thetahat.

## Recommended verdict

Provisional at best. No attack is Unresolved by the ledger's own standard (my kills on 1, 4 and 8 rest on a derivation from the record's formula and on title-level literature that the budget did not let me open), but 1, 2 and 4 are each Partial for substantive reasons, the record's Gate 2 claim that no coupled whole-body-constrained estimator exists is contradicted by an opened source (Kamp et al. 2023), and the record's own live novelty risk (PMC10881693) is still unopened after four routes.

Single strongest killing point: R(t), as defined in V2, is built from fitted curves that reproduce the measured organ curves, so it is to first order independent of the kinetic parameters it is supposed to audit; the question is a total-body quantification-accuracy question (percent injected dose recovered, already a biodistribution/dosimetry quantity) wearing kinetic-modelling clothes, and limb (b)'s "which organ moves" is determined by volume-weighted fit weights rather than by which organ's model is wrong.

What would promote it: open PMC10881693 and the uEXPLORER %ID biodistribution papers (query 1) and show none reports total image activity against injected activity; and restate V2 so that limb (b) separates volume/calibration scale factors from kinetic rate constants in the constrained refit (otherwise the refit cannot attribute a misclosure to kinetics).
