Domain(s): Medicine — patient safety, hospital pharmacy and medication systems (target field, whose literature decides novelty). Method-owning neighbours swept: reliability engineering / PRA (CCF beta, MGL, alpha factors), LOPA independent-protection-layer criterion, human reliability analysis (THERP dependence assessment), plus the nursing double-check literature.
Question (canonical): For each of a pre-declared set of medication-error classes, restricted per class to orders whose barrier-capability set contains at least two barriers and whose per-barrier electronic outcome is joinable to the order record (CPOE alert fired/overridden, pharmacist verification action, BCMA scan outcome): (a) estimate P(barrier k+1 fails to stop the error | barrier k failed) against the same cohort's marginal P(barrier k+1 fails), with intervals; (b) express the coupling as a common-cause-failure beta factor (or alpha-factor set) with an interval; (c) report the ratio of the realised all-barrier passage probability to the prediction from that same cohort's own marginals; (d) bound the unobserved "passed every barrier and was never detected" stratum with assumptions declared; and (e) record whether any of the sampled institution's own published barrier/FMEA/business-case analyses states a product of barrier failure probabilities. (V2, the one Gate 1 reformulation; V1 in the record.)
Alternative formulations:
- Are a hospital's successive medication checks statistically independent, and if not, how much does adding another check really reduce the chance an error reaches the patient?
- Estimate the common-cause coupling between CPOE decision support, pharmacy verification and barcode administration for matched error classes, and the error in independence-based barrier calculations that follows.
Motivating gap: Medicine imported defence-in-depth as a qualitative metaphor; each barrier's marginal catch rate is measured and reported (by a different service to a different committee), while the conditional between consecutive barriers belongs to no one's reporting. The method vocabulary that would parameterise it (beta factor, independent protection layer, dependence assessment, CCF) returned zero healthcare applications across four queries, while the identical critique of multiplying protection-layer failure probabilities is in print in dam safety, process safety and autonomous systems.
Presuppositions:
1. Each barrier's per-order outcome is electronically recorded and joinable to the same order — CPOE/BCMA established in principle (Galanter 2013, per-alert records at order granularity); pharmacist verification *content* granularity CONTESTED and the leading feasibility risk, unevidenced either way after two adversaries.
2. A ground-truth error label assignable independently of the barriers — CONTESTED and central; V2 limb (d) is a response, not a solution.
3. At least two barriers nominally capable of catching each class — CONTESTED per class; White 2010 measured 0/30 detection of seeded clinical-decision errors, i.e. structural blindness to a whole class. V2's capability-set restriction exists to stop that counting as dependence.
4. Medicine's barrier reasoning implicitly multiplies barrier failure probabilities — was unevidenced at Gate 2; now EVIDENCED in a primary source by adversary 1 (CJHP 2003, below), but only as task-error x one check, not a product across barriers. Cross-barrier product still imputed.
5. Event counts suffice for the needed precision — CONTESTED; power statement required before any pull (Galanter: 32 intercepted wrong-patient errors in 127,320 alerts).
6. Barriers act in a fixed order — CONTESTED; verification and administration can interleave.
7. (Raised by adversary 2, previously unflagged) The within-cohort marginal in limb (a) is observable — it is not, in a filter cascade; see Strongest remaining objection.
What would count as an answer: Per class — (a) the conditional vs the marginal with intervals; (b) a beta or alpha-factor set with an interval; (c) the realised/independence ratio, null 1, two-sided; (d) a declared bound over the undetected stratum; (e) the stated-product audit. A beta indistinguishable from zero, reported with a stated minimum detectable coupling, answers the question negatively and is substantive.

Novelty confidence: Medium
Significance — consequence: If beta is materially above zero, every "add another check" decision is overvalued by a computable factor and the remedy inverts — decorrelate the three existing barriers (different modality, information source, operator, time) rather than add a fourth; that is a different procurement and a different accreditation requirement. Mechanisms that would produce coupling are named and partly measured: automation complacency and confirmation bias (2019 review), Lyell 2017's experimental CDS→prescriber effect, and White's 0/30 structural blindness. If beta is indistinguishable from zero at a stated minimum detectable coupling, that is the first quantitative defence of medication defence-in-depth.
Significance — audience: ISMP and ISMP Canada, which advise against sole reliance on independent double checks and would gain the quantity their guidance asserts qualitatively; P&T and medication-safety committees pricing a proposed extra check; health-IT vendors and CDS governance configuring alert/verification/scan workflows; HFMEA practitioners, whose ordinal detectability scores could be replaced by measured conditional detection; accreditors setting double-check and scanning requirements. Shown to be stuck rather than assumed: Koyama finds insufficient evidence for the marginal effect (abstract, first-hand); Westbrook's observational study reports no reduction and leaves independent double-checking undetermined because checks were "rarely done independently"; adversary searches found live decisions in both directions, including a children's-hospital move to single checks. Caveat: the opened evidence concerns the human double-check; the electronic three-barrier chain's audience is argued, not shown.
Significance — fecundity: Makes askable — which barrier pairs are decorrelated by modality, and whether modality diversity rather than barrier count is the design variable; whether automation complacency is estimable as a named alpha-factor cause; whether HFMEA detectability should be re-specified as a measured conditional; whether the same beta holds for the other healthcare barrier chains sharing the architecture (transfusion bedside check, surgical count, specimen labelling, radiotherapy chart check — adversary 2 confirmed the method vocabulary is absent from transfusion and radiotherapy too); whether accreditation should require evidence of a required check's independence rather than its existence.
Significance — anchoring: The unresolved double-checking effectiveness question; the Swiss-cheese model's holes (2005 BMC Health Serv Res critique, never made quantitative); alert-override and alert-fatigue research, which measures overrides but never the downstream conditional; HFMEA ordinal detectability; the LOPA IPL independence criterion, unapplied in healthcare; and in this ledger UQ-20261016-62's own observation that CCF in a redundant confirmatory channel is mature in reliability engineering and unapplied in medicine.
Reason for silence: Primary (stands, argued not evidenced) — a disciplinary and committee boundary with no owner of the joined record: CPOE alerting is reported by informatics/CDS governance, verification by pharmacy, scanning by nursing informatics, each with a complete answer to its own marginal question; nothing found joins the three outcomes on one order, which is the join no single service's indicator set requires. Secondary (STRUCK, refuted by both adversaries) — "medicine lacks the vocabulary": the field had the words in 2003 ("not all double checks are truly independent"), and conditional dependence between imperfect detectors without a gold standard is a developed medical-statistics genre; what is missing is the application, not the language. Replacement secondary mechanisms, both credible and neither "no one thought of it": a new-data mechanism (the joined per-order record across all three barriers exists only at sites that have all three electronically, a recent condition), and a cheaper-substitute mechanism (comparing outcomes with and without the extra check answers the field's decision question adequately, so the harder coupling question never had to be posed). The identification obstacle (the passed-everything stratum is unobserved by construction) is recorded as why answering is hard, not as why nobody asked.
