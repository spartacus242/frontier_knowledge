## Restatement (my own words, before engaging the record's framing)

Cell culture puts almost everything at 37 °C, but the tissues cells come from are not all at 37 °C —
skin and testis sit cooler, muscle and gut can sit warmer, and the physiological span is roughly
32-40 °C. Cultured cells are also known to drift away from the phenotype they had in the body. The
question asks how much of that drift is caused by the thermostat rather than by anything else: take
at least three cell types from tissues that are definitely not 37 °C (some cooler, some warmer), grow
each at its own tissue temperature and at 37 °C, cross that with physiological versus room-air oxygen,
hold proliferation rate matched so you are not just measuring "cells divided slower", and measure how
far each condition sits from a freshly isolated in vivo profile under a metric fixed in advance. The
deliverable is a per-cell-type number — the share of the in-vitro-to-in-vivo gap that temperature
correction closes — placed beside the share oxygen correction closes in the same experiment, plus
whether the two interact. The underlying claim being tested is that temperature is a second
mis-specified default of the same kind as the oxygen one, and nobody has checked.

## Single strongest killing point

The candidate's motivating premise is contradicted by its own most load-bearing source. Everything in
the record — the silence map, the novelty grade, the significance claim that "the published phenotype
of every cell type from a cool or warm tissue acquires a named, estimable bias term" — leans on Klein
et al. 2022. I opened it, and it carries the only corpus measurement in play: "incubator temperatures
were only reported in 42% of papers between 2014 and 2019", with "a number of studies used different
culture temperatures." You cannot attach a temperature bias term to a literature that mostly does not
state its temperature, and the premise of a near-universal 37 °C baseline is not merely unverified (as
the record concedes) but is pointed against by the one source the record relies on most. The attack
stops short of a kill because non-reporting is not the same as deviation and the same paper calls 37 °C
the implicit assumption — but it moves presupposition 2 from "unmeasured" to "contraindicated", and
that is where this candidate is weakest.
