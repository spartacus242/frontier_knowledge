## 1. Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

When an ONT flowcell running Read Until kicks a molecule out of the pore after looking at only its first few hundred bases and deciding, against a reference, that the molecule is not wanted, do the molecules that survive give a skewed picture of the genome — fewer non-reference alleles at heterozygous sites, missed large insertions, shifted repeat lengths, distorted methylation levels — relative to a run of the same library with the feature switched off? And if so, can the skew be undone using nothing but the run's own output: the stubs of the ejected reads, joined to the sequence/unblock record, with the decision rule re-run over the stubs to estimate each read's chance of ejection and build inverse-probability weights?

The version graded is V2 (the one permitted reformulation), repair included: R1 (ejected prefixes retained and joinable), R2 (rule and reference index pinnable for replay), R3 (join is clean).
