## Restatement (my words, before attacking)

When a hospital swaps EHR vendors, only part of the old record is loaded into the new system's database; the rest is parked in a read-only legacy viewer or a scanned-document store. The candidate asks three things: (i) by data type — labs, notes, meds, orders, imaging metadata, waveforms, scanned documents, device feeds — what share of what existed in the old system on go-live day is actually queryable from the new system's research warehouse; (ii) whether a before/after density plot with go-live as the break, plus a class expected to be untouched and a class known to have been dropped, confirms the figures the conversion spec implies; (iii) for one published EHR study and one deployed risk model whose look-back crosses the switch, how much feature availability and calibration change. The implied deliverable is a "migration-provenance statement" as a reporting item and a model-gating rule at go-live dates.
