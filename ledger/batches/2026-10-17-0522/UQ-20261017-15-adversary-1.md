## Restatement (my words, before reading the generator's framing twice)

Every method used to build interactome maps can only register a pair if enough of the pair is actually in complex when the assay looks. "Enough" is set by the assay's own limit of detection and by the concentration at which the assay operates; "how much is in complex" is set by the pair's affinity and the partners' concentrations. So each method owns a region of the (affinity, concentration) plane it can reach, and the plane has a region no method reaches. The candidate asks: draw those regions in the cell's own units for the five method classes in current use, take their union, measure the complement, say what share of interactions sits in the complement under two stated denominators, and then re-express at least one published "we have mapped X% of the interactome" figure as coverage of the reachable region rather than of the interactome. The sting is that the field measures its own sensitivity by running assays against reference positives that were themselves assembled from previously detected interactions, so that sensitivity figure is measured from inside the window and cannot see its edge.

## Two findings that move several attacks

**A. A prior the record missed, and what it does and does not do.** Huang, Jedynak & Bader 2007 (PLoS Comput Biol, read) decomposes two-hybrid false negatives into a *systematic* term and a *sampling* term: "Prey constructs are either functional (with probability psyst) or systematically lost (with probability 1 - psyst)", and the abstract attributes "a 55% to 85% false-negative rate" to "proteins that appear to be systematically lost from the assays", against overall false-negative rates of "75% for worm to 90% for fly" and "about 95% remaining to be discovered." So the field already has, in its own journals, a quantitative split between systematic undetectability and undersampling. **But** its systematic term is construct quality — "Some constructs may not be functional at all due to improper folding or incompatibility with the two-hybrid system" — with no affinity, Kd, abundance or concentration content, no detectability threshold, and no region of any plane. Decisively for novelty, its Discussion defers exactly the candidate's physics to future work, citing "varying effective nuclear concentrations and binding constants" as the way to model unequal capture probabilities. This trims the record's significance framing and damages its silence mechanism while leaving the candidate's own estimand unoccupied.

**B. My intended strongest kill inverted on verification.** I expected to show that Y2H output is a transcriptionally amplified, kinetically gated signal that does not track affinity, which would make v2's detectability criterion (equilibrium complex fraction > LOD) untranslatable into a Kd boundary and sink the presupposition. The one primary calibration study says close to the opposite: Estojak et al. report that "the strength of interaction as predicted by the two-hybrid approach generally correlates with that determined in vitro", "permitting discrimination of high-, intermediate-, and low-affinity interactions". The genuine caveats are weaker than my claim and are already in v2's design: "there was no single reporter for which the amount of gene expression linearly reflected affinity measured in vitro", and "some interactions were subject to directionality, and their apparent strength depended on the reporter used". Scope note: this is a small-N 1995 LexA-based study, not HT-Y2H at screening scale, and it reports no Kd threshold — so it supports rank-order recoverability and interval-valued boundaries, not a sharp boundary. I record the inversion rather than restating the attack.

**My own arithmetic, labelled as mine.** For a single-site bimolecular equilibrium A+B <-> AB, bound fraction of A is [B]free/(Kd+[B]free); requiring that to exceed a detection floor f gives [B] > Kd*f/(1-f) — a straight diagonal in log-log (Kd, concentration). This is my derivation from the standard isotherm, not a quoted result, and it is what makes attack 1 bite on limb (a) only. Note it is also, qualitatively, the border Hein et al. already read off their plot as "the stoichiometry limit imposed by the relative cellular protein abundances (diagonal)" per the record's P1 quotation.
