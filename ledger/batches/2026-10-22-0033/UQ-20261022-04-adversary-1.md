## Restatement in my own words (before reading the record's framing back)

Take one unstressed cell. Every finished protein molecule in it is a copy of a coding sequence, and some copies have the wrong amino acid at some position because the ribosome mis-decoded. The known number, ~1 wrong residue per 1,000 made, is a *ratio over all residues in a population of molecules*: it tells you the total amount of wrongness in the pot, not how it is parcelled out among the molecules. The candidate asks for the parcelling: count the errors on each individual full-length chain, one molecule at a time, and report the histogram of those counts — how many molecules have 0, 1, 2, 3 errors. Then ask whether that histogram is the one you would get if each site erred on its own, independently of the others, at that site's own rate (the Poisson-binomial over each molecule's own sites — not a single-lambda Poisson), or whether instead the errors pile up on a minority of molecules. One headline number is demanded: the fraction of molecules carrying two or more errors. Two fences are put round it: the condition must be unstressed (not antibiotic-stressed), and the counting window must be longer than one tryptic peptide, out to the whole chain.

So: not "how often does the ribosome err" (known), and not "can two errors land in one peptide" (known, under drug) — but "is a wrong protein usually slightly wrong, or is wrongness concentrated", measured rather than assumed.

### The instrument evidence, with regimes

**S1. Infinite re-reading (bioRxiv 2021.07.13.452225). READ LEVEL: full text.**
**REGIME:** population = *one* 26-amino-acid **synthetic** peptide conjugated to an 80-nt DNA strand, in three designed variants (D/G/W) differing at a single site four residues from the C-terminus; unit of analysis = one peptide molecule re-read many times; operating condition = in vitro nanopore with helicase-driven re-reading; instrument = nanopore ionic-current reading; threshold = correct assignment among **three known** variants; category = variant-discrimination accuracy, not de novo sequence.
**NUMBERS, where printed:** single-read "we can identify the correct variant with, on average, 87% accuracy" (main text / Fig. 2C); "undetectably low error rate (< 1 in 10^6) when using more than ~30 re-reads of an individual peptide" (main text, re-reading section; Fig. 3C caption). Read length "approximately 25-30 amino acids" (Discussion). All-20 discrimination **not demonstrated**: de novo work "will require taking reads of a broad library of peptides" to assess performance "across all 20 amino acids" (Discussion).

**S2. Multi-pass long-strand reading (bioRxiv 2023.10.19.563182), via preLights. READ LEVEL: secondary.**
**REGIME:** population = **59-amino-acid synthetic** constructs "that contain repeating sequence blocks" with a single designed mutation at a known central position in each block (plus four folded domains, lengths not given); unit = one strand; condition = in vitro; instrument = multi-pass nanopore; threshold = picking the right amino acid out of 20 at a **known** position; category = classification accuracy.
**NUMBERS as relayed:** "28% for all 20-way amino acid classifications (compared to 5.5% for a dummy model)"; "from 28% (without re-reads) to 61% (with 10 re-reads)". The highlighter's own closing judgement: single-residue sensitivity has so far been shown "in very synthetic and artificial contexts". De novo sequencing is named a future goal.

**My own arithmetic, labelled as mine, with the sources' own hedges carried through.** Mordret's rate is "around one misincorporation event per 1,000 translated residues" — an order-of-magnitude statement, not a point estimate. On a 300-residue chain that is lambda ~0.3, so Poisson P(>=2) = 1 - e^-0.3(1.3) = **~3.7% of molecules** — the record's "a few percent", confirmed. For a measurement to resolve that 3.7% the instrument's **false** substitution calls must contribute far less: at a per-residue false-call rate of 10^-4 a 300-mer accrues 0.03 spurious calls and P(>=2 spurious) ~4x10^-4, comfortably under the signal; that is roughly the floor required. Taking S2's 61% at face value gives a per-position error of 39%, i.e. ~117 miscalled residues per 300-mer — **three to four orders of magnitude** away from that floor. S1's spectacular <1-in-10^6 does **not** close the gap, and treating it as if it did would be exactly the scope transfer this ledger warns about: it is a **3-way** choice among known variants at **one** known site on a **26-mer** with >30 re-reads, not a **20-way** de novo call at **~300 unknown** sites. Carry the hedges: the 61% is relayed secondhand and was measured at a *designed* position in *repeat* constructs, so it is not even a de novo per-residue rate — and that is the point. **No source I opened publishes a per-residue de novo accuracy for a full-length native chain at all.** The instrument figure the candidate's presupposition 1 needs is itself an unmeasured quantity.

## Single strongest killing point

The candidate names its instrument in the question, and the instrument does not exist at the regime the question needs. Both enabling-route papers — left unopened by the record, opened by me — read **synthetic** strands of 26 and 59 residues with the substitution at a **designed** position, scoring 87% on a 3-way known-variant choice per read and 61% on a 20-way call at a known site with 10 re-reads; the celebrated "< 1 in 10^6" is that 3-way discrimination after >30 re-reads, not a de novo read. By my arithmetic a per-residue false-call floor near 10^-4 is needed to resolve the ~3.7% of molecules expected at 2+, and 61% leaves ~117 spurious calls on a 300-mer — a 3-4 order-of-magnitude gap — while **no opened source publishes a per-residue de novo accuracy for a full-length native chain at all.** It is not fatal, because the record conditions on the capability instead of assuming it and makes establishing the floor step one; but it means the candidate is today an unanswerable question with a well-specified answer, and the honest reading of its "Reason for silence" is that the instrument boundary it invokes has not yet moved.
