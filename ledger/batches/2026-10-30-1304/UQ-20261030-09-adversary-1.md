# UQ-20261030-09 — Gate 4, adversary #1

## Restatement (my own words, before reading the record's framing back)

Take a pancreas cancer that was hit with chemotherapy before it was cut out, with gemcitabine in the regimen. Cut the resected tumour, and in the same tissue find (i) the tumour cells that are still alive rather than treatment debris and (ii) the places where bacteria are actually sitting, at a grain of tens of microns. Then ask a geometry question about that tissue: as you move away from a bacteria-occupied spot, does the chance that a tumour cell there is alive fall off? And is that fall-off still there once you hold constant how much of the neighbourhood is dead tissue, how hypoxic it is, and how far it is from a blood vessel. The headline number is a distance-graded odds of local tumour-cell survival per unit of local bacterial occupancy. The motivating idea is that bacteria chew up the drug locally, so bacteria mark drug-free pockets where cells live through treatment; a competing idea with the same geometry is that bacterial contact parks nearby cells out of cycle, where an S-phase poison cannot reach them.

## DEPTH CHECK: **Pass**

- **Object test completion:** "The answer would be a fact about **human tumour tissue — the joint arrangement of bacteria and still-living tumour cells inside a resected block**." Tissues, cells and bacteria are on the pass list.
- **Deletion test:** if every paper, registry and protocol vanished tonight, the archived block still has living tumour cells at some set of distances from bacteria-occupied microniches. The arrangement fixes the answer; only the means of reading it would be lost, which the rule says is irrelevant. Pass.
- **Rows test:** one row = one tissue microregion (or one tumour cell with its distance to the nearest bacterial niche). Not a specimen report, not a pathology record, not a paper. Pass.
- **Clauses:** matches none of (a)–(e). It is not a methodology audit — it asks what a bacteria-plus-host instrument shows in a tissue regime nobody has put it in, which the rule exempts in terms. Not (b): no records set is counted. Not (c): nothing in the estimand is a conformance, denominator or log-behaviour question; the contamination/detection-floor limb is audit-shaped and is carried as a labelled prerequisite, and removing it leaves the terminal limb unable to speak. Not (e): no published estimate or assigned label is being moved — a pathologist's regression grade is the *source of the viability call*, not the quantity.
- Adversarial note on depth, recorded and not sustained: "residual-and-viable" is operationalised by a marker rule nobody has stated, which is a well-posedness defect (below), not a depth failure. The viability of a cell is a fact about the cell.

## Promotion condition — what kind of document, and whether my nominee is that kind

To settle attack 2 the document must: cover human tumour tissue with a bacterial channel at ≤100 µm grain; include specimens or cohorts with a chemotherapy exposure axis; and **print** either a viability/residual-tumour endpoint against bacterial proximity, or state in its own words that it does not. Galeano Niño et al. 2026 is that kind of document on the first two criteria — human spatial bacterial profiling with microniche comparisons and a neoadjuvant-response cohort — and the open question is only the third, which is why it must be read at full text (doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2025.09.010, then sciencedirect/cell.com) before any grade above Provisional.
