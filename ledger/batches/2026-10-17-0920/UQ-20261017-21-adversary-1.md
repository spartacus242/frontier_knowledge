# UQ-20261017-21 — Adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my own words)
Aircraft weather reports (AMDAR, and winds/temperatures derived from Mode-S EHS / ADS-B surveillance messages) depend on the aircraft's navigation solution for the wind value (ground vector minus air vector) and, for part of the stream, for the position tag. Since 2022 large regions (Baltic, eastern Europe, eastern Mediterranean, Middle East) have had documented GNSS jamming/spoofing. Has anyone measured whether reports from those regions are worse against the NWP background (bias, scatter), are mis-positioned (detectable as impossible jumps within one airframe's track), and how many of the bad ones get through operational QC into the analysis — compared with similar clean airspace, using an exposure map that is not itself built from the aircraft's GNSS indicators? Or are they indistinguishable within a stated tolerance?
