# Bierbrier, Lo, Wu. JMIR 2014;16(2):e32 — first-hand notes
Read level: FULL TEXT via jmir.org/2014/2/e32 (2 fetches, 0 searches; page passed through a summariser, so quotes are short fragments, none stitched).
1. Counts: 14 apps (of 1,603 found), 13 functions tested, 10 cases per function, 1240 tests total ("10 cases for each function for a total of 1240 tests"). Note 14x13x10 = 1820, so not every function was on every app (1240/10 = 124 app-function pairs). Methods also mention 15 functions after narrowing from 147 of 476; 13 tested.
2. Case selection: "tested using the same 10 variations of data input, including 2 extremes and 8 middle values"; "validated with clinicians for face validity". FOUND: 2 extremes per function. NOT described as deliberately placed at boundaries, unit-conversion points or missing values. MELD cases with creatinine >4 mg/dL appear in Results, but not described as a design choice.
3. Reference: Excel calculation with allowance for rounding error (Methods); Abstract says "manual calculations". Scored 1/0; tested twice; incorrect scores rechecked by a second person.
4. Results: overall accuracy 98.6% (17 errors / 1240). 6 of 14 apps 100% accurate. 11 of 13 functions 100% accurate on all apps. "Approximately half" clinically significant; 8/17 (47%) changed prognosis. No explicit formal rule beyond prognosis change seen. Child-Pugh: errors on 2 apps, 4 errors, 1-point differences, did not change Child-Pugh class. MELD accuracy 95%; Child-Pugh 97%. 8 of 14 apps made similar incorrect MELD scores for creatinine >4 mg/dL; one example 52.6% -> 71.3% predicted mortality.
5. Between-app agreement statistic: NOT FOUND (full text read). Threshold/category-crossing rate: NOT FOUND as a rate; only the Child-Pugh no-class-change statement and the 8/17 prognosis-change count, plus the MELD example.
6. Definition ambiguity: partly. Errors attributed to "incomplete application of United Network for Organ Sharing" modifications (UNOS cap creatinine at 4 mg/dL); this is framed as app error, not as an ambiguity in the published score. No wider discussion of ambiguous definitions: NOT FOUND.
