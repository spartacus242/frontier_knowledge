## Restatement (my words)

Take one fixed open-weight model and one multiple-choice / exact-match benchmark. Run it, one item at a time, under several kinds of change that practitioners treat as not changing the model: different 4-bit quantizers at the same bit width, bf16 vs fp16, different attention kernels, different sampling seeds at a fixed temperature — with several interchangeable instances of each kind. For every item record whether its correctness flips. The question: do the different KINDS of change tend to flip the same items (a common fragile subset), over and above what you would expect if each item just had its own flip rate as estimated from the instances within a kind? Does any such common subset remain after you account for how close the item's top-two logits were to begin with? And how much of the reported accuracy is carried by the most fragile items?

Note on the estimand as the record states it. V0 asks for sharing "beyond what item-specific independent Bernoulli flipping predicts". If the item-specific rate p_i in that null is common to all perturbation types, the null already contains a shared fragile stratum (the items with high p_i), and nothing "beyond" it is identifiable from one flip per item per type. If p_{i,t} is type-specific (R1's "within-type estimates p_i"), then the quantity R1 names — "the BETWEEN-TYPE component of per-item flip propensity" — under the ordinary variance-components reading is the item x type interaction, i.e. the degree to which types flip DIFFERENT items, which is the negation of a shared stratum; yet R1 calls a non-zero value the "shared stratum exists" outcome. The coherent repair is to read "between-type component" as the item random effect common across types (Var(a_i) in logit p_{i,t} = mu + a_i + b_{i,t}). I attack the repaired version below, but the record's own text does not say this, and it matters for attacks 4, 5 and 8.

Rejected. No attack on 1, 2 or 4 is Unresolved, so Gate 4 alone does not reject; but the Gate 2 ground (novelty Low) stands and is strengthened by a prior the record missed — Hu et al. 2022 (arXiv:2204.04220), which in SE "disagreement" vocabulary already shows per-input quantization flips concentrate at small top-two margin (AUC-ROC >0.9 in 23/30 settings) and tabulates the union of flipped inputs across four quantization configurations — and the reason-for-silence mechanism is contradicted by that same literature, which under the LEDGER's Gate 4 rule makes the novelty finding suspect.
