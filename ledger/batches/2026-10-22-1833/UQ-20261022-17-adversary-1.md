## Restatement (my own words)

Take the half-dozen-or-more most-downloaded open multilingual base-model tokenizers on the Hugging Face hub, pin each to a commit, push every document of one fixed, dated, script-labelled multilingual sample through encode-then-decode, and count how often the text comes back changed — once demanding byte-for-byte identity, once forgiving whatever normalisation the tokenizer's own config says it applies. Break the changed documents down by script and by the Unicode feature that got mangled (NFKC-unstable characters, combining marks, zero-width joiners, whitespace runs), sort them into "same meaning" vs "meaning changed", and attribute each to a cause (normaliser, control stripping, no byte fallback, decoder whitespace rule). Then re-score per-language loss on only the documents that survived the round trip and ask what fraction of one specific published cross-language loss gap that re-scoring moves.
