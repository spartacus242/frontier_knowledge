## Restatement (own words)
Take one raw Common Crawl pool. Filter it T>=2 times, each time with a quality classifier whose training labels came from a different teacher LLM, the teachers chosen to be equally capable but from different families, keeping the same fraction of documents each time. Train A>=3 architectures/recipes on each filtered corpus at equal FLOPs and tokens, S>=2 seeds per cell, one pre-declared eval suite. Ask: does "which architecture wins" depend on which teacher graded the corpus, beyond seed noise (interaction variance / seed variance, with CI), and how many pairwise architecture rankings flip sign across teachers at a pre-registered effect size?
