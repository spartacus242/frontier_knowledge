## What verification corrected underneath the verdicts

Four verdicts did not move and very little under them survived untouched. Twenty-two readers, no errors.

**A misquotation by cut, carrying the batch's strongest objection.** UQ-01's boundary-coincidence leg rested on Genoud, Isler & Martin 2018 identifying the postabsorptive state by "a respiratory quotient compatible with a postabsorptive state (i.e. approaching 0.7)". The string is verbatim. The entry cut both ends: the governing clause is "measurements done on artiodactyls fasted for variable durations have shown that", the predicate is "can be reached well before starvation", and the content is attributed to Silver et al. 1969. The sentence is about how long a fast must last in artiodactyls. Further: **RQ is not a SELECT inclusion criterion** (SELECT is E = 1 or 2 across thermoneutrality, rest, postabsorptive state, resting phase, adult, non-reproductive), the abbreviation never occurs in the 35 pages, and no empirical RQ distribution is printed anywhere in it. What survives the correction is textbook physiology, not a citation. Table 1's SELECT beta is confirmed at 0.729 ± 0.010 (exponent of body mass, N = 549 species, mlO2/h against g) — but the printed t-test is **against 0**, not against 3/4, so "intervals claimed to exclude both 3/4 and 2/3" was the record's reading.

**A sign inversion in a support clause.** UQ-02's fatal row added that the protein-measurement method was "flagged as a deficiency in Papers 2-4". **A regime failure in the leg the entry had named its best survivor.** UQ-01 singled out Walsberg & Hoffman 2005 as the leg that would survive if the boundary coincidence fell, "because it rests on printed numbers from an opened primary source in the candidate's own taxa". ## The entry that tested its own reopening condition

UQ-04 had named the one document that could overturn it: the peer-reviewed version of the multi-pass nanopore work, with the condition that a printed de novo per-residue figure clearing ~10^-4 on a native chain should reopen the rejection at Gate 4. It was opened for the first time at verification and the condition is **not met**.

The article is Motone, Kontogiorgos-Heintz, Wee, Kurihara, Yang, Roote, Fox, Fang, Queen, Tolhurst, Cardozo, Jain and Nivala, "Multi-pass, single-molecule nanopore reading of long protein strands", *Nature* **633**, p. 662 (2024), doi 10.1038/s41586-024-07935-7. The published title **drops** the preprint's "with single-amino acid sensitivity". It prints no de novo per-residue accuracy, no limit of detection and no substitution false-positive rate, and the Discussion forecloses the direction in the authors' own voice: "directly determining amino acid sequences from the nanopore signal (de novo sequencing) will be ... even more difficult". Measured instead: pairwise 100%, 3-way 95%, 5-way 86%, 20-way 28% against a 5.5% random-guess baseline, top-5 67%, top-8 81%; per-read whole-strand ~62%/42% (HDKER) and ~51%/21% (AVLIM). **The 61% is simulated** (Supplementary Note 3; Extended Data Fig. 7d is captioned "Simulated effect of rereading..."). Every construct is a designed PASTOR carrier and the titin item is an engineered 89-residue variant.

So a rejection that stood on an absence in a preprint now stands on an absence in the reviewed text plus the authors' own statement of difficulty. Its vulnerability has narrowed to one document, the Supplementary Information, and the reopening condition transfers to it unchanged.

Two smaller findings in the same entry cut against the candidate and for it. **Gomes 2007 Figure 4c was opened** (adversary 3 could not read it) and it is a genuine per-molecule 0/1/2/3 count distribution under an explicit binomial — so a published per-molecule count-distribution construct has existed since 2007, prior art for the construct, with a sharply different regime (three pre-specified CUG sites in one gene under programmed sense-codon ambiguity). And **Landerer's ">15% carry at least one"** is an Introduction-level inherited citation hedged with "imply", not the model's output, which deepens the gap rather than closing it.

## Route lessons

- **nature.com fails both ways even for open access.** The Nature 633 version of record was served by the **NSF Public Access Repository mirror**, `par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/<id>`. That is now the first route to try for an NSF-funded Nature paper.
- **MIMAVE opened in full at the Genome Biology route** after failing for all three adversaries (302 to link.springer.com, PMC hard-blocked). It names replicates nowhere, its verb strengths are "we urge" (raw counts) / "we recommend" (pre-normalisation scores) / "we strongly recommend" (raw reads), and it is **identical in substance to arXiv 2306.15113** — so any force drawn from a preprint-to-published change is struck.
- **Weller 2016 opened at the SAGE PDF route** where opus4.kobv.de served a bot interstitial. It is usable for framing only: no number, its 44% and "<50%" are about reagent **identifiability** rather than dose, and its ">75% nonspecific" is an unnamed company's unpublished test relayed from a comment on Baker 2015.
- **Withers (2001)** is *Australian Journal of Zoology* 49(4):445-461, doi 10.1071/ZO00057, abstract only. Its abstract moves against UQ-01's cancellation derivation: configurations exist where one flow is measured and the other calculated from gas composition, flow differs in parts of the excurrent circuit, and the equations vary with configuration. So the cancellation is **conditional on a shared-flow degree-one structure and exact where that holds**, not general.
