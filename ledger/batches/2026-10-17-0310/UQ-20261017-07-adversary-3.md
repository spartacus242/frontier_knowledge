## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Take one raw Common Crawl pool. Filter it two or more ways where the ONLY difference between filters is which LLM wrote the quality labels (same rubric, same keep fraction, teachers of equal ability but different lineage). Train several architectures/recipes on each filtered corpus at equal compute, several seeds each. Does "who graded the data" change "which architecture wins" by more than run-to-run noise, and how many pairwise architecture verdicts flip sign at a declared minimum gap?

Reduced further: is the teacher of a quality filter a nuisance factor that interacts with model design, or is it exchangeable?

## Recommended verdict

Provisional at best — Unresolved on attack 3 (Combinatorial novelty), no Unresolved on 1, 2 or 4. Novelty must be re-graded from Medium toward Low–Medium: the record's own conditional on arXiv:2609.11917 triggers (opened; sign-reversing dense/MoE x data-repetition interaction, filter-share x architecture statement, seed appendix present but unseen), and DataDecide (ICML 2025, not found by the record) already instantiates same-pool classifier variants at matched 10% keep rate x 14 scales x 3 seeds with the crossover-vs-variance problem named, falsifying the record's "Not Low" grounds about matched keep rate and seed replication. Promotion conditions: (i) a Gate 2 re-grade written against DataDecide and 2609.11917 explicitly, stating the architecture axis as the sole uninstantiated factor; (ii) any evidence — even observational keep-set overlap between two capability-matched teachers at one fixed rubric — that the isolated teacher factor is not null by construction (my query 5 found none either way); (iii) the record's P1 wording corrected (only FineWeb-Edu has a single identifiable LLM teacher of per-document labels).

Single strongest killing point: both halves of the proposed design already exist as public factorials — DataDecide crossed same-pool, different-classifier corpora at matched keep rate with a model factor and three seeds and reported that crossovers are hard to separate from variance, and arXiv:2609.11917 crossed dense vs MoE with data-side factors and reported a sign reversal — so the candidate is the unrun cell of two existing experiments, and the novelty grounds it stated ("nothing ... matches keep rate across teacher-filtered corpora, replicates cells across seeds") are factually wrong.
