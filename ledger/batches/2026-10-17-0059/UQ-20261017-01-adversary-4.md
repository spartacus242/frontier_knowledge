## Attacks

### 1 Triviality: PARTIAL
- For the candidate: no opened source states the sign or size of E[Δ]. Ambroz's only directional statement (given fact 1) is that criteria-selected values for sub-7 A IRMOFs "would be higher" than standard-range values, which, if anything, points to NEGATIVE Δ for habitual-window authors on micropores. So the unconditional sign is not predictable from what I read.
- Against: a positive proximity slope is predictable WITHOUT any incentive. S2 states "the predicted area is highly dependent on the region of the isotherm that is chosen" and BET "overestimates ... significantly" in some cases; the record itself (P2) says the overestimate is worst for mesopore plus 10-20 A micropore MOFs and that frontier MOFs are disproportionately such materials. High reported area in the MOF class goes with large pores (S3: record holders are mesoporous), so a window-by-pore-size effect would produce Δ rising with area in the class. Adjusting for "material class" does not remove within-class pore-size variation. The record names the confound but only proposes class stratification, which is coarser than the confound. Needed: a pore-size/pore-class covariate computed independently (e.g. from structure) and the slope reported conditional on it.
- Own reasoning (not sourced): Δ = R/ref - 1 and proximity is an increasing function of ref, so ref sits in both. Any error in ref that is not shared by R induces a NEGATIVE spurious covariance (if ref = true(1+e), R = true, Δ ≈ -e, proximity rises with e). R1 removed the positive tautology but introduced this opposite-signed coupling; the record does not address it. It is an errors-in-variables problem needing e.g. instrumenting proximity with an independent quantity.

### 3 Combinatorial novelty: PARTIAL
- Each ingredient exists (BETSI reference, reimplemented and matching to machine precision on 18 of 19 isotherms per S5; archived isotherms; record series; recomputation-audit method). The candidate's contribution is the conjunction plus an incentive covariate. A conjunction is genuine novelty only if it yields a quantity not obtainable from the parts; it does (a signed, claim-linked offset). But the strongest piece of the conjunction, the incentive covariate, is the part with the least data (frontier stratum), see attack 4. Evidence of non-obviousness beyond the queries: none.

### 4 Presupposition failure: UNRESOLVED
- Reported-BET harvesting: given fact 3 (ISODB stores no reported-BET field) means the frame is hand-built; the record concedes this might give hundreds. No count exists in the record or in anything I read.
- Frontier stratum: the frontier is a handful of papers (S1 reports NU-109E 7010 and NU-110E 7140 m2/g; S3 says record holders exceed 7000). Whether their isotherms are deposited in an archive is not established by anything opened. The primary coefficient lives in this stratum.
- The frontier authors state they used consistency criteria: S1 says areas are "subject to well-defined "consistency criteria"" (cited in passing; criteria and window not in the text I received). If frontier authors applied the same criteria as the reference, Δ near the frontier is expected near zero by construction of their practice, so the incentive-linked "upward exercise of window choice" is least plausible exactly where the question puts its weight. Contrasting datum from S1: reported NU-109E 7010 vs simulated NU-109SE 6950 m2/g (own arithmetic: 60/6950 ≈ 0.9%), but both are BET-method outputs so this is not evidence about bias.
- Presupposition that authors' areas come from the deposited isotherm and that habitual windows are in force is carried over, not shown. The record's best response (declare exclusion rule) is procedural, not evidence of feasibility.

### 5 Vagueness: PARTIAL

Residual undefined items: functional form of "proximity" (ratio, rank, log?), "isotherm quality" metric, "material class" taxonomy, linear versus other form of "increase", and period over which "to date" frontier is evaluated; each is a researcher degree of freedom in the audit itself, which the record says it wants to eliminate. ### 7 Reason for silence: UNRESOLVED
- Mechanism 1 (tools only recent) is weak: the consistency criteria for MOFs date to at least 2006-2007 work (AIChE 2006 abstract 381a title seen in Q7; S1 cites criteria in 2012); the criteria can be applied by hand or script to dozens of papers, and the record itself says a hand exercise was feasible per paper. BETSI is a convenience, not an enabling condition.
- Mechanism 2 (disciplinary boundary) and mechanism 3 (accusation taboo) are asserted from the absence of search hits; the argument "it would persist even after tools arrived" is circular, since the only evidence is the absence it is meant to explain.
- Unconsidered mundane reasons: (i) deposited raw isotherms attached to a stated BET window are rare, so the frame is tiny (consistent with given fact 3 forcing manual harvesting); (ii) the field may regard the answer as known and uninteresting (S1 suggests record-claim authors already cite the criteria); (iii) such an audit may exist unpublished or inside the unopened items. None is excluded.
- Record's claim that the round robin "deliberately anonymised its participants" is not verified by me (UNVERIFIED).

### 8 Dissolution: PARTIAL
- The question does not dissolve as a measurement: E[Δ] and a slope are well-defined and informative whatever the cause. It does dissolve as an INCENTIVE claim: if reported areas come from instrument-software defaults or a habitual 0.05-0.30 window (Ambroz, given fact 1), there is no discretionary choice being "exercised directionally"; Δ then measures default-window error as a function of pore size, and the incentive reading is not identified. Also Δ compares against a criteria-based reference that S2 and P2 say can itself overestimate, so the sign of Δ is method-versus-method, not analyst-versus-truth. The record's negative control (C-constant) is a partial answer but is unevidenced as discriminating.

## Strongest objection
The estimand cannot separate incentive from mechanics: the proximity slope is confounded with pore-size-dependent window error (frontier MOFs are mesoporous and high-area), is contaminated by ref appearing in both Δ's denominator and proximity (own reasoning, opposite-signed), and is to be estimated on a tiny frontier stratum whose authors say they used consistency criteria.
