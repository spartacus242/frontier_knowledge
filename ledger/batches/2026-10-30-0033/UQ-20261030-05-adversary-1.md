# Gate 4 — Adversary #1 log — UQ-20261030-05

## Restatement (mine, before reading the record's framing back to itself)

Take one fixed reporter protein carrying a classical import signal, expressed from one promoter, and ask how many molecules of it each nuclear pore passes into the nucleus per second, in each of five kinds of cultured cell. Count the pores in those same nuclei so the number has a real denominator. Then compare two spreads: how much the per-pore rate varies from cell type to cell type, against how much the pore count per nucleus varies from cell type to cell type. If the rate barely varies while the count varies a lot, cells build pores in proportion to the traffic they carry; if the rate varies as much as or more than the count, pore number is not matched to traffic and may be a bottleneck in some cells.
