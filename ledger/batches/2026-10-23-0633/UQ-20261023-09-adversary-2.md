## 1. Restatement in my own words (before attacking)

Three named autonomous retinal-screening AI devices are cleared in the US and issue a patient-facing verdict — refer / don't refer / can't tell — off fundus photographs taken in primary care by whoever is on shift. The accuracy numbers on the box come from a trial where trained photographers shot the images to a protocol. The candidate asks: if you photographed the same patient a second time in the same visit, with a different member of staff on another camera of the same model, how often would the device change its verdict — and is that churn bigger or smaller than the statistical wobble (the confidence interval) on the sensitivity and specificity the device was cleared on?

So the candidate is asking for (a) a within-visit, operator-swapped, same-camera-model **decision-disagreement rate** for a cleared autonomous device in routine use, with a CI, counting "insufficient quality" as its own outcome; and (b) an explicit comparison of that rate against the device's own clearance uncertainty. Its implicit charge is that the cleared accuracy overstates what an individual patient's single acquisition buys.

Two things I will hold the candidate to that its own framing blurs: the unit it asks about is the **patient-visit**, and the setting it asks about is **routine deployment**, not a pre-market sub-study.

### C7 in full, because it is the clause the question cannot survive as written

The canonical wording asks how the flip rate compares with **"the uncertainty margins on the sensitivity and specificity in the device's own clearance study"**, as though each device's file supplied one such margin. Two primary files **of the same device family**, opened:

- **K200667** states its precision quantity as **three-category overall agreement, OA = (PP+NN+UU)/N**, on **eye-pairs with printed denominators** (169/180, 56/62), beside accuracy on **subjects** against a reading centre.
- **K223357** states its precision quantity as **average positive agreement and average negative agreement** — a **two-state** pair of metrics that has no ungradable term — and prints them with **no denominators at all**, beside accuracy that *does* carry denominators and is split by camera.

So across two clearances of one device, the precision measurand itself changes (3-category OA on eye-pairs → APA/ANA with no stated unit), while the accuracy estimand stays an accuracy-against-reference on a different unit. **My own reasoning, labelled as mine and not a source finding:** a flip rate on re-acquisition and a CI on accuracy-against-reference are not two estimates of one quantity, so "compare the flip rate with the uncertainty margin" has no defined operation; and the candidate does not say whether the margin is the CI half-width, its lower bound, or the distance to a pre-specified performance goal. What grounds this is not my logic but the printed metric definitions and the absent denominators in the two files I opened. The search that would have strengthened it further — a regulatory-statistics source stating whether a precision agreement and an accuracy CI are comparable for a qualitative device — I did **not** run; my budget went to the primary clearance files.

**Does the measurand exemption save C7? I checked, and it does not — and I say so out loud rather than grading around it.** The exemption applies when a question *reports* a contested clause as a primary result instead of assuming it. The candidate's Gate 1 record **identifies this very defect** ("the comparison limb is not operationalised") and then **declines to cure it** — the reformulation allowance is explicitly marked NOT USED, and the canonical wording still asks the comparison as though the margin were given. Nothing in the question reports the heterogeneity of interval or precision constructions as an output. So C7 is assumed, not measured, and attack 4 reaches it. (Had the question asked "what share of cleared autonomous devices' files print a precision measurand commensurable with their accuracy interval?", the exemption would have applied and this attack would have failed.)

**And I tested Gate 1's repair as hard as the original, as required.** The record's recorded-but-unused repair asks for "the patient-visit-level flip rate in routine deployment [matched against] the eye-level inter-operator agreement printed in the device's pre-market precision study". That repair **adds** presuppositions rather than removing C7's problem: it presupposes (i) that eye-level agreement can be lifted to a patient-visit decision, which needs the worse-eye combination rule and the per-eye contingency tables — Table 13 is now confirmed to exist and to print only 56/62 with no cell detail in what was served to me, so the lift is **UNVERIFIED**; and (ii) that a deployed version can be re-fed its own stored image sets under the same version, which neither file I opened supports. The repair is not a cure.

**Attack 4 status: Unresolved** — C7 is a clause the canonical question cannot survive without, it comes back CONTESTED-to-FALSE on two primary documents I opened, and the question assumes it rather than reporting it.
