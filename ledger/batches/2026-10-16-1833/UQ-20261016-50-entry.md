Domain(s): AI architecture — data layer (pretraining quality filtering, data-exhaustion forecasting); method from measurement/reliability theory (inter-rater reliability, G-theory, no-gold-standard latent-class accuracy).
Question (canonical): Treating each published pretraining quality filter as a measurement instrument applied to one fixed Common Crawl sample at a matched keep rate, how much do the filters agree on which documents are "high quality" — pairwise agreement, the size of the all-filters intersection, and the share of the keep decision attributable to the document rather than to the filter — and by how much does the field's estimate of how much high-quality text the web still holds move across equally defensible filters?
Alternative formulations: (1) Is "high-quality web data" a property of documents or of the classifier that picked them? (2) If two teams filter the same crawl with their own published classifiers at the same keep rate, what fraction of the kept corpus is common, and do models trained on each keep-set and on the intersection differ beyond seed noise?
Motivating gap: Curation discards most of the web on a classifier's say-so; the intake held that no one treats filters as instruments with a reliability, and nominated Nemotron-CC as ensembling scorers in a way that presupposes material disagreement "while never reporting how much". Verified by reading: that central claim is false — quantifying the disagreement is the paper's stated justification for ensembling, and the number is in its Table 8. The "comparisons rank filters by downstream score" claim survives.
Presuppositions: P1 filters publicly runnable on a common sample — supported (DCLM fastText and two NVIDIA scorers released; FineWeb-Edu carries a licence question, excluded from Nemotron-CC's ensemble for that reason). P2 matched keep rates are the right footing — contested; all priors operate at native rates. P3 agreement interpretable with no gold standard — contested, conceded as part of the point. P4 exhaustion forecasts depend on a variable quality criterion — mixed: Villalobos defines quality by downstream performance via a filter-retained fraction (10–40%), but its 95% interval [22T, 490T] (~22x) is already built from inter-filter disagreement, so a per-filter spread is pre-absorbed. P5 one snapshot generalises — untested by any prior. P6 document-level is the right unit — contested. P7 (added at Gate 2) chance-corrected agreement computable from published data — partially refuted: Nemotron-CC Table 8 is union-restricted (cells sum exactly to the printed total), so it supplies no true-negative cell.
What would count as an answer: (1) an agreement matrix on one fixed stratified CC sample at matched and native keep rates (pairwise agreement, kappa, |∩K| as a share of each keep-set, document-vs-filter variance decomposition, with CIs); (2) per-filter sensitivity/specificity against a latent quality class under a no-gold-standard latent-class model, or demonstrated unidentifiability; (3) the remaining-high-quality-token stock recomputed per filter as a max/min spread against the forecast's declared uncertainty; (4) optionally, fixed-compute models trained on each keep-set, the intersection and the symmetric difference against a pre-registered seed-variance band. High agreement answers as firmly as low. Gate 2 finding against this list: (1) is published for K=2 at native rates; (3) is absorbed by the forecast's own ~22x interval; (2) and (4) are untouched.

Nemotron-CC (arXiv:2412.02595) Table 8, "High-quality documents overlap analysis", on one snapshot (CC-MAIN-2021-21): union 11,359,655 (100.0%), intersection 1,152,821 (10.1%), FineWeb-Edu only 4,022,294 (35.4%), DCLM only 6,184,540 (54.4%); the three cells sum exactly to the union total, so the table is union-restricted and supplies no true-negative cell, and the percentages sum to 99.9% by rounding. Appendix B and the Table 11 caption give 368 of the top 1k domains in the intersection. Two refinements this entry now carries: (i) keep rates are NOT matched and the keep-set sizes differ (about 5.18M against 7.34M), confirmed from the printed counts, so the matched-rate limb of the candidate is genuinely untouched by the prior; (ii) the paper's stated reason for ensembling is recall and diversity of high-quality tokens, with the low overlap cited as supporting evidence — the word "disagree" does not appear — so this entry's Motivating-gap correction is sharpened rather than reversed: quantified overlap is published and is used to justify ensembling, but it is not framed as a reliability finding. No kappa, no chance-corrected agreement and no document-versus-filter variance decomposition are reported, which is what leaves the residue described below.

Strongest remaining objection: Attack 4. The filters are not competing instruments for one construct — FineWeb-Edu scores "educational", DCLM instruction-likeness, the Nemotron scorers their own annotators' taste — so inter-filter agreement is not a reliability coefficient, low agreement is the predicted and uninformative outcome, and this objection bites the surviving residue (matched-rate kappa, K≥3, variance decomposition) as hard as it bites the published headline.
