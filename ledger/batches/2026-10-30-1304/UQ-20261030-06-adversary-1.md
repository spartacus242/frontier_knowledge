## Restatement (my own words, written before reading the record's framing conclusions)

Take one person. They have two of a thing — two breasts, two lungs, two kidneys, two testes, two ovaries. One side is usually bigger. Cancer shows up more often on one side than the other. The question asks whether that side-preference is *nothing but a head-count*: if you knew how many at-risk epithelial cells sat on each side of that one person, would the cancer hazard on a side be simply proportional to that side's cell count (exponent exactly 1.0), or would a left/right term still be left over after the head-count is paid off? The headline number is the exponent beta fitted **within person** on **measured per-side epithelial cell number**, plus the sign and size of any residual side term, plus whether beta is the same across the five organs. Because a person contributes a cancer to at most one side, Gate 1 repaired it into a case-only conditional likelihood: among unilateral incident cases, model P(left) as a function of beta·log(C_L/C_R) plus a side intercept.

What the question is *not*: it is not "is there laterality" (known), and it is not "did somebody adjust for organ size" (that would be a clause (a) audit). It is a scaling exponent and a residual, fitted on bodies.

## DEPTH CHECK — **PASS**

- **Object test completion:** *The answer would be a fact about* **human paired organs — their per-side at-risk cell populations and the hazard of malignant transformation in them.** That is on the explicit pass list ("a human body, its tissues, cells and physiology pass"). No dataset, release, institution or publication name is needed to make the blank true.
- **Deletion test:** if every paper, registry row and protocol vanished tonight and bodies stayed as they are, each person's two sides still have their cell counts and their cancers, so beta and the residual side term still have values. The rule reads the test as whether the answer would still **exist**, not whether anyone could still measure it — so the fact that the needed instrument may not exist (see attack 4, clause 2) is *irrelevant here* and I do not import it into this test.
- **Rows test:** one row = one organ-side of one person (left breast of woman *i*). An instance of the natural object, not a registry row standing in for one.

(c) no — it requires a **new** measurement (per-side epithelial cell density), not a re-denominatoring. (d) no. (e) **live tension, same one the record found:** alternative formulation 1 ("do the laterality excesses amount to nothing more than…") is shaped like a consequence-of-correction question about published laterality ratios. I answer it from the **canonical** wording, whose headline quantities are a newly fitted exponent and a residual asymmetry in bodies, not a published figure being moved. I agree with the record here and reached it independently.
- **Depth verdict: PASS.** This is not a Gate 0 rejection. A Pass is not a point in the candidate's favour and changes nothing below.
