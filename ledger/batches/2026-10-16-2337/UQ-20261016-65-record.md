Domain(s): Medicine — radiology care delivery, diagnostic quality and safety; clinical informatics; imaging protocol governance

Question (canonical) — Gate 0 intake version:
What fraction of diagnostically intended imaging series that are acquired and archived are never displayed to any interpreting reader before the report is signed, and what is the rate of actionable findings in a blinded re-read of a sample of those never-displayed series?

Question (canonical) — Gate 1 reformulation (ONE reformulation, for well-posedness only; both versions recorded):
In sites whose viewer/PACS logs emit series-level display events, and under a provenance map and series taxonomy both declared before any data are pulled: (i) among series classed as *diagnostically intended* (excluding localizers/scouts, dose reports, failed or repeated acquisitions, and auto-generated reformats/parameter maps), what proportion carry no display event attributable to any reader in the interpreting chain (resident, fellow, attending, second reader) within the window from study-available-in-PACS to report signature — counting a displayed derived series as coverage of each source series the declared provenance map says it encodes; and (ii) in a stratified sample of those never-displayed series, what is the per-series rate of actionable findings under a pre-specified actionability taxonomy, read by >=2 blinded readers with adjudication and with the original report withheld?
(The reformulation changes nothing substantive. It fixes three things the intake version left to inference: *who* counts as a reader, the exact time window, and that a viewed reformat counts as coverage of its source rather than leaving P3 to swallow the whole estimand.)

Alternative formulations:
- Does anyone look at all the images we acquire, and does it matter if they do not?
- Treating the PACS display log as the instrument, how much acquired image data is unexamined, and is the unexamined fraction diagnostically empty?

Motivating gap:
The literature is dense on acquisition (protocol, sequence, dose, contrast timing) and dense on reader search behaviour (eye tracking, satisfaction of search), and silent on the join: whether an acquired series was opened at all. The logs that would answer it exist in every PACS but are kept for access control and compliance, are owned by IT and privacy offices rather than radiology research, and are often purged on a short cycle; hanging protocols routinely display a subset of what was acquired. Confirmed during Gate 2: the one opened account of a department mining its own PACS audit logs (Maine Medical Center, SCAR 2003) used them for suspicious-access detection, VIP-access review and turnaround-time benchmarking, and states the vendor purged logs after 30 days — the instrument's custodial purpose and retention are exactly as the gap predicts.

Presuppositions (as intake, with Gate 1/2 annotations):
P1 Viewer/PACS logs record series-level display events attributable to the interpreting reader and timestamped against report signing — CONTESTED and LIMITING; vendor-dependent; sites logging only study-level access cannot answer the question. Gate 2 note: the one PACS usage-log study I could reach logged *commands and command classes per case*, not series display, which is evidence that series-level attribution is not the default export.
P2 Series can be partitioned by a rule declared in advance into diagnostically intended vs technical/derived — NECESSARY for non-triviality. Gate 2 note: load-bearing, because the localizer/scout re-read literature (see priors) already owns the uninteresting answer.
P3 Non-display implies non-examination — CONTESTED; reconciliation substudy required. The reformulation absorbs the derived-reformat case into the estimand via the provenance map; second-workstation and logged-out viewing remain for the substudy.
P4 A blinded re-read can establish actionable findings reliably — plausible; needs multi-reader adjudication, a pre-specified actionability taxonomy, and handling of the re-reader's extra context (the original report must be withheld; downstream outcomes must not leak).
P5 Report signing is the right cut-off — flagged; later views at MDT meetings or by referrers are a different question and are counted separately. Gate 2 note: this is also what separates the candidate from the "unreviewed imaging/reports after signature" family that search repeatedly surfaced.
P6 Enough series are never displayed for the quantity to be interesting — CONTESTED; limb (i) measures it, and a near-zero result closes the question. This is the question's falsifiability, and also its largest risk: nothing I found bounds the quantity in either direction.
P7 Someone would act — plausible and specific; see Significance/audience.
P8 The quantity is not already published as a vendor or informatics operational metric — PARTIALLY CHECKED, NOT CLEARED. Queries 10 and 11 found department-dashboard/BI literature with no "series displayed" metric, but found US8566122B2 "Method and apparatus for navigation to unseen radiology images" — so the *engineering concept* of an unseen series is patented prior art in the vendor space, while no published population estimate of the fraction was found. Vendor analytics documentation is largely not web-indexed, so P8 is weakly checked rather than established.

What would count as an answer:
In >=2 sites with series-level display logs, a pre-registered estimate of the proportion of diagnostically intended series never displayed before report signing, with its distribution by modality, protocol, shift and reader workload; plus a blinded multi-reader re-read of a stratified sample giving actionable-finding yield with an interval; plus the P3 reconciliation substudy. All three outcomes act: near-zero never-displayed fraction closes the question; substantial fraction with near-zero yield argues for stopping the acquisition; substantial fraction with non-trivial yield defines a new, measurable, attributable class of missed finding.

Significance — consequence:
Three distinct actions follow, and the question cannot return an uninformative answer. If the never-displayed fraction is near zero, the question closes and a recurring worry about hanging-protocol coverage is retired with a number. If it is substantial and the blinded re-read yield is near zero, the acquisition is being paid for in scanner time, dose (for CT), contrast and archive cost without ever entering a diagnostic decision — which converts protocol pruning from an expert-opinion exercise into an audited one. If it is substantial and the yield is non-trivial, the result defines a missed-finding class that is, unusually, both measurable and attributable at the series level: it is detectable prospectively from logs, before harm, which no current QA instrument does (discrepancy meetings and peer review act on reports, not on unopened data).
Significance — audience:
(1) Protocol committees and modality leads, who already prune sequences for throughput and cost — the opened RSNA QI project "Reducing Unnecessary Scan Acquisitions at Abdominal MRI" and the brain-MRI protocol-optimization literature show this committee exists and acts, currently without display-coverage evidence. (2) Radiology QA/patient-safety and medico-legal functions, for whom "acquired, archived, never opened, finding present" is a new and documentable exposure. (3) PACS/enterprise-imaging vendors and informatics groups: US8566122B2 is direct evidence that a vendor thought "navigation to unseen radiology images" worth patenting, so the feature side of the audience is real; limb (i) tells them whether to ship an unviewed-series gate before signing. (4) Compliance/IT custodians, for whom a positive result is a reason to extend log retention and widen the schema from study-level to series-level.
Significance — fecundity:
Opens: does the never-displayed fraction rise with reader workload, shift, and list length (a dose-response test of workload on coverage, independent of report error)? Is display coverage a better leading indicator of diagnostic error than turnaround time? Can an AI triage be aimed specifically at series the reader did not open, which is a far smaller and better-defined target than "second-read everything"? Does hanging-protocol design explain between-site variance, making coverage a configuration-level rather than reader-level defect? Does a declared-in-advance series taxonomy become a reusable instrument (a "coverage" denominator) for other display-log questions?
Significance — anchoring:
Touches satisfaction of search and the volumetric visual-search coverage results (which measure the within-series version of the same deficit); the unreported/unread-examination and follow-up-recommendation-never-reviewed literatures (the same failure one and two levels up the hierarchy, P5's separate question); the incidental-findings-on-localizers/scouts family (the same failure one level down, excluded by P2); dose and throughput optimization; and the DICOM/ATNA audit-trail standard, which is where the instrument's schema is actually decided.

Reason for silence:
A custodial-purpose split across three owners plus a retention wall, not an absence of imagination. The log exists only because access control requires it: DICOM/ATNA-style audit trails and HIPAA access monitoring are specified for *who looked at whose record*, not *which series was displayed*, so series-level display events are an incidental by-product whose presence is vendor-dependent (the one PACS usage-log study I could reach exports commands per case, not series display). The log is owned by IT/privacy, the acquisition decision by the protocol committee, and the display decision by the reader, and none of the three is funded or credited for the join. Retention closes the door even where the schema is adequate: the opened 2003 account reports vendor purging of audit logs after 30 days, which forbids retrospective linkage to report signatures and outcomes and forces any study to be prospective and negotiated with compliance. Finally, there is no reporting hook — no quality measure, accreditation item or billing code makes "series displayed" a reportable quantity — so the metric has no owner even inside radiology informatics, where dashboards track volume and turnaround instead. Secondary mechanism: the uninteresting sub-case (localizers and scouts) has absorbed the question's attention for two decades, which lets the general form look already-asked and already-answered.

Well-posedness note (why 3, and the residual): the primary estimand is log-defined, pre-registrable, and falsifiable in the closing direction (P6: a near-zero fraction ends it); the series partition, the reader set, the time window, the provenance map and the actionability taxonomy are all declared before data are pulled. This is a validity limitation of a well-specified quantity, not vagueness about what is being asked.
