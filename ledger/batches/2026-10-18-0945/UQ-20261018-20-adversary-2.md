## Restatement (my own words, before engaging the record's framing)

Take one healthy tissue in one healthy animal. Of the cells in it that are dying right
now, sort them by which named death programme they are running — apoptosis,
necroptosis, ferroptosis, pyroptosis, and the rest of the committee's list — and report
the proportions, with an honest bucket for cells that fit more than one and for cells
that fit none. Then, knowing how long each kind of corpse stays visible before it is
eaten, turn those standing proportions into deaths per day per unit tissue, and check
that the per-programme deaths add up to the tissue's independently measured total
turnover.

Short form: the cell-death field has a catalogue of ways to die and no census of which
ones actually do the killing in a normal body. The candidate asks for the census.

Two things I note about the framing before attacking it. (a) The record concedes most of
the ground an adversary would normally take: presuppositions 1, 2, 3 and 7 are all
marked CONTESTED, well-posedness is self-scored 2/3, and the strongest objection is
pre-written. That makes the easy attacks unavailable and pushes the real fight to
attacks 2, 4 and 8. (b) V2 is a protocol, not a question: most of its length specifies
how to answer rather than what is asked. V1 is the question; V2 is V1 plus a method.
I attack V1's content and V2's answerability separately where it matters.
