## Gate 0 — Intake

Question (canonical): Treating each published pretraining quality filter as a measurement instrument applied to one fixed Common Crawl sample at a matched keep rate, how much do the filters agree on which documents are "high quality" — pairwise agreement, the size of the all-filters intersection, and the share of the keep decision attributable to the document rather than to the filter — and by how much does the field's estimate of how much high-quality text the web still holds move across equally defensible filters?

Alternative formulations:
1. Is "high-quality web data" a property of documents or of the classifier that picked them?
2. If two teams filter the same crawl with their own published quality classifiers at the same keep rate, what fraction of the kept corpus is common, and do models trained on each team's keep-set and on the intersection differ beyond seed noise?

Gate 1 reformulation (one permitted; recorded, not replacing — well-posedness only): *On one named, stratified Common Crawl snapshot sample, with K ≥ 4 named released filters (DCLM fastText, FineWeb-Edu classifier, the two released Nemotron-CC scorers, a perplexity filter, Gopher heuristics), each run twice — once at its native operating point and once at a keep rate matched across all K — report (i) pairwise percent agreement and Cohen/Fleiss kappa with CIs, (ii) |∩K| as a share of each filter's keep-set and of the union, (iii) a document-vs-filter variance decomposition of the keep decision, (iv) per-filter sensitivity/specificity against a latent quality class under a no-gold-standard latent-class model, or a demonstration of unidentifiability, and (v) the remaining-high-quality-token stock recomputed per filter, reported as a max/min ratio and compared against the ratio 490/22 ≈ 22 already carried by the published 95% interval on that stock.* The reformulation was needed because "by how much does the field's estimate move" has no fixed comparator as originally written; it also makes both answer directions pre-specifiable. It does not rescue novelty — limbs (i) and (ii) for K = 2 are the published result, and limb (v)'s comparator turns out to absorb the finding.

Motivating gap (as intaken, and as corrected by Gate 2): The intake held that curation pipelines discard most of the web on a quality classifier's say-so, that the literature is dense with how-to-filter and empty of any treatment of filters as instruments with a reliability, and that every published comparison ranks filters by downstream benchmark score. The first and third claims survive; the second does not. The intake's own nominated "telling detail, to be verified by reading rather than assumed" — that Nemotron-CC ensembles scorers in a way that presupposes material disagreement while never quantifying it — was verified by reading and is **false**: quantifying the disagreement is the paper's stated justification for ensembling ("ensembling different classifiers can increase the recall of high-quality documents"), and the number is in Table 8.

Presuppositions (post-Gate 2 status):
- P1 The filters are publicly available and runnable on a common sample — **SUPPORTED, and more strongly than intaken.** DCLM fastText is released; Nemotron-CC states "The quality classifier models have been released as well" with two NVIDIA Hugging Face models and a NeMo Curator reference implementation. One adverse detail: Nemotron-CC excluded the FineWeb-Edu classifier from its ensemble *for licensing reasons*, so a K ≥ 3 comparison including it carries a licence question, not a technical one.
- P2 Matching keep rates is the right common footing — **CONTESTED, as intaken, and the contest is live:** every prior found operates at native rates (FineWeb-Edu 8% vs DCLM 11% high-quality share over 13 snapshots; AboutMe's percentile cutoffs break to 5.2% and 8.7% on score ties, and Gopher uses rule-specific cutoffs). The candidate's requirement to report both is the right call and is the one design element no prior satisfies.
- P3 Agreement is interpretable with no gold standard — **CONTESTED, and conceded as part of the point.** The no-gold-standard estimator family is mature and named in clinical diagnostics (latent-class diagnostic accuracy, Bayesian LCA; MetaBayesDTA, arXiv:2211.05949) but no transplant to corpus filtering was found. Identifiability on binary keep decisions from K filters with correlated errors is a real risk; the candidate pre-declares unidentifiability as itself a result, which is correct practice.
- P4 The data-exhaustion forecasts depend on a quality criterion this exercise can vary — **MIXED on reading, and this is the limb that moves most.** The intake's worry (that the forecasts might define high quality by *source class*) is REFUTED: Villalobos et al. define quality by downstream performance and operationalize it as a filter-retained fraction, "between 10% and 40% of deduplicated web data can be used for training without significantly compromising performance", anchored on two filtering studies that disagree (Penedo et al. 2023, ~30% removed; Marion et al. 2023, ~50% optimal pruning). But the follow-on premise — that a filter-induced spread would be news against the forecast's declared uncertainty — is ALSO largely refuted: the quality-adjusted stock is 100T with a 95% interval of [22T, 490T], a span of ~22x, and that interval is built from precisely the inter-filter variation the candidate proposes to measure. A per-filter recomputation landing inside that band is pre-absorbed by construction.
- P5 One Common Crawl sample can generalise across dumps and languages — **UNTESTED by any prior found.** Nemotron-CC's overlap figure is one snapshot (CC-MAIN-2021-21) with a separate 13-snapshot share comparison; whether the 10.1% is stable across dumps is not reported. English-only scope would be a stated limitation; the one multilingual agreement paper found (arXiv:2512.18834) measures source overlap, not filter overlap.
- P6 Document-level is the right unit — **CONTESTED, as intaken.** Unaddressed by priors; line/paragraph-level filters make a document-level matrix a partial view.
- P7 (added at Gate 2) Chance-corrected agreement is computable at all from what the field publishes — **PARTIALLY REFUTED for the published data.** Nemotron-CC's Table 8 is restricted to the *union* of the two keep-sets (the three cells sum exactly to the printed total of 11,359,655 — verified arithmetic), so it supplies no true-negative cell and no kappa can be computed from it without the full sample denominator.

What would count as an answer: three numbers and one optional experiment, per the intake — (1) an agreement matrix on one fixed stratified CC sample at matched keep rates (pairwise agreement and kappa, |∩K| as a share of each keep-set, and a document-vs-filter variance decomposition, each with CIs and each repeated at native rates); (2) the same under a no-gold-standard latent-class estimator giving per-filter sensitivity/specificity against a latent quality construct, or a demonstrated unidentifiability; (3) the remaining-high-quality-token estimate recomputed per filter as a spread, compared against the uncertainty the forecasts already declare; (4) optionally, fixed-compute models trained on each keep-set, the intersection and the symmetric difference, against a pre-registered seed-variance band. High agreement answers as firmly as low.
**Gate 2 finding against this list:** (1) is published for K = 2 at native rates (intersection share and domain-list overlap); the kappa, matched-rate and variance-decomposition parts are not. (2) is untouched by anything found. (3) is published in the only form that matters — the forecast's own 95% interval already spans ~22x and is derived from inter-filter disagreement — so the limb is absorbed rather than open. (4) is partly done in a different filter family (arXiv:2510.00866 trains 350M models on keep-sets from six different reference sets) but never on the intersection or symmetric difference.

## Gate 3 — Significance

Consequence: Heavily discounted by A. The candidate's dramatic branch — "filters barely agree" — is **already the published state of the world**: 10.1% Jaccard between the two filters the field actually uses, and 368/1000 shared top domains. So the answer's direction is not in doubt, and the "high agreement" branch the intake wanted to keep open is effectively closed. What remains consequential is narrower and genuinely unsettled: whether that 10% is *chance-level or far above it* (no kappa exists), whether it is an artefact of mismatched native keep rates (8% vs 11%) or survives rate-matching, and whether the keep decision is mostly filter-specific variance — which would mean the quantity curation pipelines believe they are thresholding does not exist at the document level. A "mostly filter variance" finding would make every single-filter corpus an instrument artefact; a "mostly document variance with low agreement" finding would instead mean the filters measure different real document properties, which is a different and more benign conclusion. No prior distinguishes these two readings, and they recommend opposite actions (ensemble for recall, as A does, versus report a measurement error).

Audience: Real and named, but mostly already in possession of the headline. NVIDIA's curation group computed the overlap and acted on it (ensembling); HuggingFace FineWeb/FineWeb-Edu, AI2/Dolma, the Common Crawl Foundation and the DCLM authors would act on a matched-rate reliability figure; Epoch AI would act on an attribution of its [22T, 490T] interval to named filters. The audience that has *not* been reached is the one that owns the method — psychometrics/generalizability theory and no-gold-standard diagnostic accuracy — and the honest statement is that this audience has no stake in web corpora and no reason to act.

Fecundity: Moderate. Opens the internal-disagreement question for A's own ensemble; line- versus document-level agreement; whether agreement is stable across dumps and languages (A's 10.1% is one snapshot); whether an exclusion/keep propensity is gameable by publishers; and whether "the high-quality web" can be an estimand at all rather than a per-instrument artefact. Weakened by the fact that the most striking downstream question — does training on the intersection differ from training on either keep-set — is a compute-bound experiment the method-owning fields cannot run and the corpus-owning ones have no incentive to.

Anchoring: The data-exhaustion scaling literature (D, and its ~22x declared interval); the IR assessor-disagreement result, now verified real (Voorhees SIGIR 1998, revisited in arXiv:2502.20937), which is the precise prior the intake said must be re-derived or refuted; the absent sampling frame for the web; and identifiability limits on latent-class models with correlated errors.

Score: Significance **2/3** (consequence real but headline published and forecast limb absorbed; audience real but already served on the headline). Well-posedness **3/3** (a named sample, named filters, named statistics, both keep-rate regimes, both answer directions pre-specified, and unidentifiability pre-declared as a result — the reformulation fixes the one loose comparator).

## Reason for silence

Not applicable to the headline, which was not silent: A published the intersection number in 2024 and B the multi-filter fixed-sample design in 2024. For the residue, two mechanisms, neither evidenced beyond the texts read:

1. **An incentive gradient inside the data-owning field, visible in how A frames its own number.** A computed exactly the candidate's quantity and read it as a *recall deficit* to be fixed by ensembling — "ensembling different classifiers can increase the recall of high-quality documents" — rather than as the unreliability of an instrument. Under that reading, disagreement is a resource (more documents clear the bar) and there is no reason to compute a kappa, match keep rates, or decompose variance; the shipping criterion is a downstream benchmark score, so a filter is judged by the model it trains, never by its agreement with another filter. That also explains why A never measured agreement among its own three ensemble members: the ensemble's value to it comes from their disagreement, so quantifying it would have been beside the point.
2. **A disciplinary boundary with no traffic in this direction, plus one licensing artefact.** The method that would own the residue lives in diagnostic-test accuracy without a gold standard and in generalizability theory, and query 8 found that family entirely inside medicine with no transplant to text corpora; query 7 found the psychometric vocabulary attached only to human-versus-LLM-judge agreement. The timing mechanism is weaker than it looks — the filters have been jointly available since 2024 — but there is a concrete obstacle: A excluded the FineWeb-Edu classifier from its ensemble *for licensing reasons*, so the one group with both the motive and the compute to run a K ≥ 3 matched-rate comparison was barred from including the field's most-cited filter. That is an artefact of licence terms rather than of thought.
