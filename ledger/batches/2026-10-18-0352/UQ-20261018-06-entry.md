Question (canonical): For one measurand per network, over a pre-declared frame of (station, event-window) pairs with exogenous event windows and each pair's realized peak y assigned from evidence independent of that station's instrument (with a declared error model): (a) what is the hazard h(y) that the instrument ceases to report inside the window, in the measurand's own units, with CIs, and is h flat in y; and (b) re-fitting GEV/POT with that censoring modelled explicitly — as a non-ignorable selection model, or by treating a lost peak as interval-censored below by the last recorded value — what is the signed change in the 50- and 100-year return levels against the conventional fit on the published annual-maxima series? Alternative formulations:
