## Restatement (my own words)

Nuclear-data libraries (ENDF/B, JEFF, JENDL, and formally adjusted libraries) are advertised as agreeing well with the ICSBEP criticality benchmarks — but some of those same benchmarks were used, in one way or another, to build the libraries. So: how much of that advertised agreement is self-congratulation? Concretely, if you take each benchmark back out of whatever process let it influence the library and recompute, (a) how far does calculated-over-experimental k_eff move, in pcm and in multiples of the benchmark's own stated error bar, (b) how much bigger do the bias and the bias *uncertainty* that criticality-safety analysts derive from that C/E population get, and (c) how much less conservative does the ANSI/ANS-8.24 Upper Subcritical Limit turn out to have been? V2 adds a triage step: do the holdout arithmetic only for libraries whose integral fitting is actually refittable (regime A), and for libraries tuned by expert judgement (regime B) settle for a correlation between "this benchmark was used in production" and "this benchmark's residual is small."

Two things the restatement makes visible. First, the question's quantitative core is ordinary held-out validation of a model fitted to the data it is scored on. Second, the limb that carries the practical payoff (the USL) and the limb that is computable (the holdout) are attached to *different libraries*: USLs in US criticality-safety practice are derived with un-adjusted evaluated libraries, which are exactly the regime-B libraries where the record concedes no refit exists.

## Regime statement for each source before it grounds a grade

**Rochman et al. 2018.** Population: random TENDL-style file realisations for three actinides. Unit: the file realisation, scored per benchmark. Instrument: χ²-weighted assimilation (BMC, BFMC, Mocaba, best-single-file, linear combination) on 14 integral quantities from 12 benchmarks. Endpoint: C−E and χ²/P. Comparator: prior average and nominal file 0. Threshold: author-stated χ²/P. Benefit category: fit quality of adjusted vs prior data — **no bias uncertainty, no margin, no USL.** Is the candidate inside it? **Regime-A limb: yes.** The candidate defines regime A as "documented formal assimilation/adjustment with a retrievable benchmark set, covariances and sensitivity profiles" — Rochman's assimilation is exactly that, its benchmark set is enumerated, and its product is an adjusted nuclear-data library. The candidate's *endpoint* for that limb (derived bias and bias uncertainty, in pcm per stated uncertainty) is outside; its *question* (is in-sample integral agreement optimistic, and what happens held out) is inside and answered. **ΔUSL limb: outside** — Rochman is silent there, and silence is not support in either direction.

**SCALE/TSURFER manual.** Population: an applicant's own suite of benchmark experiments plus one or more application systems. Unit: the experiment (active or passive response) within a single GLLS adjustment. Instrument: generalized linear least squares with χ²-per-degree-of-freedom filtering. Endpoint: **the application bias and its uncertainty** — the same endpoint family the candidate's ANS-8.24 limb needs. Comparator: initial vs adjusted response estimates. Benefit category: criticality-safety bias determination. Is the candidate inside it? **Partly.** The manual's manipulation is holding an experiment out of the *applicant's* adjustment, not out of the *library evaluation's* integral feedback. That is a different object from the candidate's, and I do not treat it as a duplicate of the candidate's primary limb. What it does establish, inside its own regime, is that hold-out checking of a predicted bias is documented practice in the code that produces criticality-safety biases, and that the field's own manual warns against extrapolating adjusted libraries.

**Chadwick, CSEWG 2006.** Population: ENDF/B-VII candidate evaluations. Unit: the evaluation decision (nubar, elastic angular distributions) and the critical assembly it was tuned against. Endpoint: predicted k_eff of critical assemblies. Benefit category: evaluation quality. Inside the candidate's regime? It is regime B, in the candidate's own index library's own working group, and it is a *question-asked* document rather than a measurement — which is precisely what attack 7 needs.
