## Restatement (my own words, before any attack)

Take five named kinds of cultured mammalian cell. Put the same NLS-tagged fluorescent reporter into each of them, watch molecules of it cross the nuclear envelope, and work out how many molecules one nuclear pore passes per second in each cell type. Separately count how many pores each nucleus actually has. Then ask whether the per-pore rate is much more alike across the five cell types than the pore counts are. If it is, cells are building pores roughly in proportion to the traffic they have to carry; if it is not, some nuclei are running their envelope near saturation while others hold spare capacity, and pore number is a limiting resource in the saturated ones.
