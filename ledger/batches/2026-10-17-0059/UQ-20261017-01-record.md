Domain(s): Other STEM — physical chemistry / porous-materials characterisation (adsorption metrology). Adjacent: measurement science (laboratory bias), research metascience.

Question (canonical) [R1, adopted at Gate 1]:
Over a pre-declared frame of archived nitrogen/argon adsorption isotherms (NIST/ARPA-E ISODB plus journal SI) each linkable to a source paper that reports a BET area computed from *that same* isotherm, with the reference area fixed by one pre-registered consistency-criteria implementation (BETSI-class) whose tie-breaking and no-admissible-window rules are declared before any data are examined: is E[Δ] positive, where Δ = (reported area − reference area)/reference area, and does E[Δ] increase with proximity-to-frontier — proximity computed from the RECOMPUTED reference area, not from the reported value, against the highest area published to date for that material class in a declared journal set — after adjustment for material class, publication year and isotherm quality?

Question (V0, as submitted at Gate 0):
Among published nitrogen/argon adsorption isotherms that are archived in machine-readable form, does the discrepancy between the authors' reported BET surface area and the area recomputed under one fixed, pre-registered consistency-criteria procedure grow with the proximity of the reported value to the contemporaneous highest-reported area for that material class?

Why R1 was needed (the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation, well-posedness only):
(a) V0 says "discrepancy", which is unsigned, while the hypothesis and the stated answer are directional; R1 fixes Δ as signed.
(b) V0 is circular in a way that would manufacture its own answer: proximity-to-frontier is computed from the reported value, which is also the numerator of Δ. For a fixed reference area, a larger reported value mechanically raises both Δ and proximity, so V0's coefficient is positive by construction and is not an estimate of anything. R1 defines proximity on the recomputed reference area. This is the single most important change and any protocol that keeps V0's definition is answering a tautology.
(c) R1 moves the frame, the admissibility rules and the degenerate cases (several admissible windows, none admissible) into the question, since each is a discretionary choice that would otherwise reproduce inside the audit the very degree of freedom being audited.

Alternative formulations:
- Is the analyst's freedom in choosing the BET pressure range exercised directionally — upward — when the resulting number is close to a record, rather than symmetrically as noise?
- Does the published BET area of a porous material depend on how ambitious the claim it supports is, after the isotherm itself is held fixed?

Motivating gap:
Osterrieth et al., "How Reproducible Are Surface Areas Calculated from the BET Equation?" (chemRxiv 10.26434/chemrxiv.14291644; published version Advanced Materials 2022) distributed 18 already-measured raw isotherms to 61 laboratories and obtained "a wide range of values", and released BETSI, which "expands on the well-known Rouquerol criteria and makes an unambiguous BET area assignment possible" (abstract read via core.ac.uk/outputs/558625868; FULL TEXT COULD NOT READ — see access note). That study's estimand is dispersion across analysts on a fixed isotherm. Nobody found here has asked whether the dispersion is directional and incentive-linked — whether the signed offset from a criteria-fixed reference is larger and positive for values sitting near the field's record frontier, which for MOF surface area is a dated, enumerable claim series (Northwestern's NU-109/110 "is the sky the limit" record line, JACS 2012, 134, 15016). Two things make the quantity newly computable: machine-readable isotherms with source-paper linkage (ISODB), and a mechanical reference computation (BETSI, 2021/22).

Presuppositions (status after Gate 1):
1. A usable number of archived isotherms carry BOTH the authors' reported BET value and the source paper. CONTESTED and UNVERIFIED in this run — I did not confirm that ISODB stores a reported BET area as a field, as opposed to the isotherm plus adsorbent metadata. This is load-bearing: if the reported value must be read out of each paper by hand, the achievable frame may be hundreds, not thousands, and the frontier stratum thinner still. First thing an answerer must check.
2. Archive coverage is itself selected by journal, era and material class, so the frame is not "the literature". CONTESTED, flagged, and handled only by declaring the frame rather than generalising from it.
3. One consistency-criteria procedure yields a reproducible reference area. CONTESTED: such tools return several admissible windows or none; pre-specified tie-breaking and a no-admissible-window rule are required, and the no-admissible-window rate is itself a reportable number.
4. A dated record frontier per material class is enumerable. MILDLY CONTESTED: record claims are contested in real time, so the frontier must follow a pre-declared rule (highest value published to date in a declared journal set).
5. The reported value was computed from the deposited isotherm, not a different run or batch. CONTESTED and material; exclusion rule must be stated in advance and the exclusion rate reported.
6. Upward shifts of the selected pressure window mechanically raise the computed area. MECHANICAL, established — and the direction is corroborated independently: Gómez-Gualdrón, Moghadam & Snurr (AIChE 2016, 339a) find on simulated isotherms that the BET calculation "can significantly overestimate the true monolayer loading even when all criteria are satisfied", worst in MOFs combining mesopores with 10-20 Å micropores.
7. NEWLY ADDED AT GATE 1, and contested: the authors' *implied* pressure window is recoverable from (deposited isotherm, reported area). It is not uniquely identified — in general a set of windows reproduces the reported area within rounding. So strengthening element (i), the window-offset mechanism check, is at best partially identified and must be stated as a set-valued or bounded quantity, not a point offset. An answerer who reports a single implied window per paper is over-claiming.
8. BET area functions as a competitive figure of merit, so proximity-to-record is a meaningful incentive covariate. Reasonably established by the existence and press treatment of the record-claim series.
NOT presupposed: that any inflation is intentional, or that any author violated a standard. The estimand is directional bias in a discretionary analysis step.

What would count as an answer:
A pre-registered recomputation over the declared frame: per entry, signed Δ and the (set-valued) offset between the authors' implied window and the reference window. Primary answer = the coefficient of proximity-to-frontier in a model for Δ with material-class and publication-year effects and isotherm-quality controls, as a signed effect size with a confidence interval. A coefficient indistinguishable from zero is an equally publishable answer and retires the hypothesis. Three pre-specified strengthening elements: (i) the window-offset, not just Δ, scales with proximity (subject to presupposition 7); (ii) a negative control reported in the same papers that carries no record competition (BET C-constant, or low-pressure fit residual) shows no proximity effect; (iii) falsification route — if Δ is centred at zero or tracks only isotherm noise, the answer is no. Practice-changing deliverable: the size of the correction implied for the published record frontier, or, in the null case, an uncertainty interval to attach to it.

Novelty — how this differs from each:
vs P1: same reference computation, different unit, endpoint, comparator and estimand. P1's unit is a participating analyst in a designed exercise; the candidate's is a published paper in the wild. P1's endpoint is a participant's number; the candidate's is the ORIGINAL AUTHORS' published number, signed against the reference. P1's estimand is spread; the candidate's is a regression coefficient of spread's sign on an incentive covariate (proximity-to-frontier) that P1 has no way to construct, because a round-robin isotherm supports no record claim and carries no author with anything at stake. P1 establishes that the degree of freedom is large; the candidate asks whether it is exercised asymmetrically where it pays to be.
vs P2: P2 is about whether BET is the right instrument for a known structure — method bias against geometric ground truth on SIMULATED data, with no author, no reported value and no literature frame. The candidate holds the method fixed (the criteria-based reference IS the yardstick, warts and all) and asks about the human analysis step. P2's direction-of-error finding is in fact a confound the candidate must control for, not a prior answer: because BET overestimates most in mesopore-plus-large-micropore materials, and frontier MOFs are disproportionately such materials, material-class adjustment is load-bearing and P2 tells an answerer exactly which classes to stratify on.
vs P3: closest on frame — the same archive, the same literature-meta-analysis move, the same group that owns adsorption data quality. But P3's endpoint is isotherm loadings, its comparator is internal consensus among replicates, and its estimand is an unsigned disagreement rate. It never recomputes a BET area, never compares against what the original authors reported, never assigns a sign, and never relates deviation to any property of the claim. P3 is the strongest evidence that the candidate's frame is buildable and that the obvious adjacent study was done without asking this question.

Significance — consequence:
If yes (positive, non-trivial coefficient): the published porosity frontier is partly an artefact of analysis freedom concentrated at the frontier, and the correction is computable and specific — a revised frontier series with intervals. The policy follow-on is cheap and concrete: require the BET pressure window and a criteria-checked value alongside any record or near-record claim, which journals already have the deposition machinery to enforce. It would also convert a reproducibility finding into a bias finding, which is a different class of problem with different remedies (correction versus uncertainty propagation).
If no (coefficient indistinguishable from zero): the round-robin's spread is exonerated as analyst noise rather than directional bias. That is also actionable and arguably more useful day to day: it licenses treating BET dispersion as an uncertainty to propagate into every reported area — including the record series, which would then carry an interval instead of a correction — and it removes an insinuation that currently floats free in the field's informal talk without ever being tested.

Significance — audience:
(i) Porous-materials synthesis groups and the MOF/COF/porous-carbon community, who compete on this exact number; (ii) NIST/ARPA-E ISODB and the Sholl/Walton data-quality line, who would gain a new quality axis (reported-value fidelity) over an archive they already curate and who have already built the replicate machinery (P3); (iii) standardisation bodies governing BET determination (ISO 9277, IUPAC), for whom "the discretionary step is exercised directionally" is the argument that turns a recommendation into a required reporting field; (iv) journal editors at the venues that print record claims; (v) metrology, where laboratory bias is a mature quantity (Analyst 1997) that has never been modelled as a function of the claim it supports; (vi) metascience, which owns the recomputation-audit method (query 9) and has never applied it to instrument data reduction in the physical sciences.

Significance — fecundity:
The estimand generalises to every figure of merit in materials science whose value depends on an analyst-chosen window over a raw curve: pore volume via t-plot or DFT kernel choice, electrocatalyst ECSA, Tafel-slope fitting range, gas-uptake extrapolation, battery capacity at a chosen rate, thermal-conductivity fit windows. Each becomes a testable instance of "is the discretionary step exercised directionally near the claim frontier". It also makes askable the reverse question — whether the criteria-based reference itself drifts with era as software defaults change — and the design question of what a frontier claim would have to deposit for its number to be audit-stable. This ledger already holds a sibling in UQ-20261016-43 (electrocatalyst durability runs read as censored lifetimes), which suggests a family of "the published figure of merit is partly an artefact of a reporting choice" questions rather than a one-off.

Significance — anchoring:
The live BET-validity debate for micropores (P2: overestimation of monolayer loading even when criteria are satisfied); the MOF record series and its press treatment (JACS 2012, 134, 15016, "is the sky the limit"; Northwestern world-record releases); the archive-quality programme over ISODB (P3's ~15-16% outlier rates); the Osterrieth round-robin and the standardisation push it triggered; and the researcher-degrees-of-freedom literature in metascience, which has never been given a physical-science case with a mechanical reference value and an enumerable record frontier — a combination this domain has and psychology does not.

Reason for silence:
Three mechanisms, in increasing strength. (1) New instruments and data: the mechanical reference computation dates from 2021/22 (BETSI) and source-linked machine-readable isotherm archives matured only in the late 2010s, so before that the recomputation was a hand exercise per paper and the frontier-wide version was infeasible. (2) Disciplinary boundary, visible in the search record: the dispersion was framed as a metrology problem and handed to the natural owner of that framing — an inter-laboratory round-robin, whose designed estimand is a spread and whose community's vocabulary (query 12) treats bias as a property of a laboratory. "Is the dispersion directional and incentive-linked?" is a metascience/forensic-econometrics estimand (queries 9, 11) with no owner inside physical chemistry, and the two literatures do not cite each other. (3) The strongest: an incentive and taboo gap in the output rather than the method. The round-robin deliberately anonymised its participants; this recomputation cannot, because its unit of analysis IS the published paper and its most interesting stratum is the handful of record claims, each attached to a named, prominent group. The deliverable is accusation-shaped even when the finding is null, which makes it a study the field's own members are structurally poorly placed to publish and outsiders lack the domain access to run. That is a reason for silence that would persist even after the data and tools arrived — which is consistent with the tools having arrived in 2022 and the study still not existing in 2026.

Scores: Well-posedness 2/3 · Significance 2/3
Well-posedness 2/3, not 3/3: R1 removes the circularity and the sign ambiguity, but two soft spots survive. Presupposition 7 (the authors' implied window is not uniquely identifiable from the isotherm plus the reported area) means strengthening element (i) is only partially identified and must be restated as a bounded quantity. Significance 2/3, not 3/3: the audience is real and the deliverable concrete, but the practice-changing limb — the size of the correction to the record frontier — rests on a small number of frontier materials, so even a clean positive coefficient may imply a correction too small or too uncertain to move the series. The null answer is genuinely publishable, which protects the question from being significant only if it comes out one way.
