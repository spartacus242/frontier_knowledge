Question (canonical — Gate 0 intake version):
How much of the output of a spatial-transcriptomics analysis — spatially variable gene calls, spatial-domain and niche partitions, and inferred cell-cell communication — is determined by the chosen aggregation support (bin size and shape, or segmentation unit) rather than by the tissue, and is there a support at which these outputs become stable?

Question (canonical — Gate 1 reformulation, the version carried forward; both recorded per Gate 1):
For a pre-declared set of Visium HD / Stereo-seq datasets re-binned to a ladder of square supports, and for outputs that are well defined on an arbitrary areal support (SVG rank list; spatial-domain partition at fixed k), (a) what is the variance decomposition of each output into a tissue-identity component and an aggregation-support component, estimated against a within-support resampling noise floor, and (b) does there exist a support s* above which the support component falls below that noise floor by a criterion declared before any data are pulled? Segmentation-based pipelines and cell-level cell-cell communication inference are treated as a SEPARATE limb, because they presuppose cells and so are not defined on an arbitrary support.
(The reformulation was taken only for well-posedness: it supplies the estimand the intake version left implicit in "determined by ... rather than by the tissue", and it quarantines the outputs that are not support-general.)

Alternative formulations:
- Do spatial-transcriptomics conclusions survive a change of support, in the sense geostatistics means it?
- What is the modifiable-areal-unit sensitivity of published spatial-omics analyses, and can a support-stability criterion be defined for them?

Motivating gap:
The aggregation unit became a free user parameter in this instrument class only in 2024. Visium HD ships 2 um bins with 8 um recommended and 16 um in common use; the Bioconductor OSTA book publishes separate "Visium HD (binned)" and "Visium HD (segmented)" workflows (https://lmweber.org/OSTA/pages/seq-workflow-visium-hd-bin.html), so the same raw data are routinely analysed at several supports by convention rather than by argument. The downstream stack was inherited from the fixed-support era and takes the support as given: SVG tests, domain-detection models and ligand-receptor scoring are all defined on whatever unit is handed to them. In geography and geostatistics this is a named, formally treated problem with an apparatus for how a statistic transforms under aggregation. The intake screening (two queries) returned only vendor tutorials, OSTA chapters and tool papers, suggesting no MAUP treatment existed in the field.
**That suggestion did not survive Gate 2.** The gap is real as an EMPIRICAL gap and false as a CONCEPTUAL one: the question has been posed in print, by name, twice, and the 2024 posing says in terms that the answer is "completely unknown and ignored."

Presuppositions:
1. Re-binning a high-resolution dataset to coarser supports is a faithful stand-in for having captured at that support. [CONTESTED — real coarser capture also changes molecular sensitivity, probe kinetics and diffusion, whereas re-binning changes only aggregation; measured support-dependence is therefore a LOWER BOUND on the true one and must be reported as such.]
2. The named outputs are well defined at every support compared. [CONTESTED and acted on at Gate 1 — cell-level methods such as ligand-receptor inference presuppose cells, so the segmentation arm is now a separate limb rather than a point on the same support ladder.]
3. "Stability" can be defined without a ground truth — e.g. as the support above which a partition or ranking changes by less than its own resampling noise. [CONTESTED and load-bearing; must be declared in advance or the question is unfalsifiable. Note that the method-owning field already has a formal instrument for exactly this — S-maup, a statistical test for MAUP sensitivity, PLOS ONE 2018 — so the criterion need not be invented, which weakens the candidate's claim to be opening new conceptual ground.]
4. A non-trivial body of published conclusions rests on a default support. [TO BE ESTABLISHED by audit; a limb of the question. Not established here.]
5. MAUP has not already been imported into this field under another name. [CHECKED AT GATE 2 AND **FALSE**. It has been imported under its own name — not a disguise, an explicit import. See priors 1 and 2.]
6. Tissue biology has a characteristic length scale at all, so that a stable support can exist. [CONTESTED but harmless — if structure is scale-free over the accessible range, branch (ii) is still an answer.]

What would count as an answer:
For a pre-declared set of datasets, methods and outputs, a support-output curve with a resampling-based noise band, yielding either (i) a support or support range above which outputs are stable by the declared criterion — an answer with a number in it, directly usable as a reporting standard — or (ii) a demonstration that outputs keep changing across the whole accessible support range, in which case support becomes part of the result and cross-study comparisons at different supports are not licensed. Both branches are substantive, so the question cannot be answered "whatever the data show."

Significance — consequence:
If branch (i) holds and a stability support s* exists, the field gets a number it can put in a reporting standard: analyse at or above s* for these output classes, and cross-study comparison above s* is licensed. That is directly actionable — it would settle a parameter currently chosen by tutorial default. If branch (ii) holds and outputs keep moving across the whole accessible range, the consequence is sharper and more disruptive: bin size becomes part of the result rather than a preprocessing detail, every Visium HD paper owes a support-sensitivity panel, and the common practice of comparing an 8 um analysis against a 16 um one — or against a segmented one — is not licensed. Either way a live convention either gains a justification or loses one.

Significance — audience:
(a) Spatial-omics method developers and benchmark authors — would add support as an axis to benchmark designs that currently vary method, platform and organ only (queries 4, 10 show the axis is absent). (b) The OSTA/Bioconductor and Seurat/scverse workflow authors, who currently publish parallel binned and segmented chapters without an argument for either, and whose defaults propagate into hundreds of analyses. (c) 10x Genomics, whose "8 um recommended" would acquire or lose an evidential basis. (d) Journal reviewers and editors, who would gain a concrete thing to ask for. (e) The GIScience group that posed it (Comber/Cockell/Zormpas) — they have said in print they want this done, which is direct evidence the audience is real rather than asserted. Against that: the same evidence shows the audience has had the question for two years and has not acted, so the stated demand has not yet produced supply.

Significance — fecundity:
Opens: does s* differ by tissue, by output class, by biological process grain? Is there a Krige's-relation analogue for expression statistics — a predictable transformation of an SVG statistic under aggregation, which would let results be converted between supports rather than recomputed? Does optimal support track a measurable tissue length scale (cell diameter, crypt/lobule pitch), making it predictable a priori? Can an optimal-zoning/regionalisation approach replace fixed square bins with data-adaptive supports? And the inverse question: if segmentation and binning disagree, which is the ecological fallacy and which the individualistic one?

Significance — anchoring:
Touches the segmentation-free vs segmentation-based dispute (currently argued on cell-purity grounds, not support grounds); the reproducibility of spatial-domain partitions across studies; the interpretability of niche/neighbourhood definitions; and, on the geography side, the long-standing MAUP literature, which would gain a new and unusually tractable empirical arena — biology can re-aggregate the same tissue at many supports from one capture, which census geography essentially never can.

Reason for silence (candidate mechanism):
Two mechanisms compound, and together they explain the actual pattern found — which is not silence but inaudibility. (1) New instrument: the support only became a free user parameter in 2024 with Visium HD's 2 um bins, so the problem is roughly two years old in its current form. (2) Disciplinary boundary, in a specific and documentable form: the question WAS asked, but in AGILE: GIScience Series — a GIScience conference series — which is not indexed or read by the spatial-transcriptomics community, so the posing did not land where the practitioners are. The same group's bioinformatics-venue follow-up (STExplorer) carried the MAUP mention into the field's own literature but shipped a tool rather than the audit, because a tool is what a bioinformatics venue rewards and a negative-sounding sensitivity audit of everyone's defaults is not. (3) Incentive gap: the answer's most likely form — "your defaults move your results" — invalidates work rather than enabling it, and has no obvious home as a tool paper. Note that this mechanism is well evidenced, which under the Gate 4 rule is normally reassuring; here it cuts the other way, because the mechanism explains why the question is UNANSWERED, not why it is UNASKED, and the ledger only admits the latter.
