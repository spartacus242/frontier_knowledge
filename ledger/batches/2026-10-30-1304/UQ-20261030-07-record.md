## Gate 0 — Intake and Depth

Question (canonical, V1 as submitted):
Is the width of the transmission bottleneck a repeatable property of the DONOR — do the several recipients infected by one donor show correlated founding-population sizes — and what fraction of between-pair variance in bottleneck width sits at the donor level?

Alternative formulations:
- Do some people pass on many virions and others only one, consistently across everyone they infect, so that bottleneck width is a host trait rather than a property of the route?
- Decomposed into donor, recipient and pair components, where does the variance in the size of the founding viral population actually sit?

Motivating gap:
The settled claim being inverted is that the transmission bottleneck for acute respiratory viruses is narrow — one to a few genomes — and that it is reported as a number per PATHOGEN or per ROUTE. That reporting presupposes the bottleneck is a property of the virus and the route rather than of the individual donor. One-to-many transmission clusters (households, workplaces, superspreading events) with deep sequencing on both sides already contain the information to test it. If bottleneck width is donor-repeatable, then WHICH hosts transmit decides how much within-host diversity — minority drug-resistant, immune-escape or antigenically novel variants — is handed on at all.

CORRECTION TO THE SUBMITTED GAP, made at first hand in this gate: the intake says the presupposition "has never been tested". That is too strong, and I withdraw it. A donor-side determinant of founding-population size HAS been tested and found, in HIV-1: medRxiv 10.1101/19013524, read in full at the PDF route, prints "acute stage transmissions are less likely to lead to single particle infections compared with chronic stage transmissions (27% vs. 80%, P = 0.0005)" — the opening words scope this to a comparison of source-partner infection STAGE, and the clause ASSERTS the quantity. What has not been done, in anything I opened, is the variance decomposition with repeated recipients nested in one donor. The candidate survives on its estimand, not on its "never tested" framing.

Presuppositions (as submitted, with my assessment at first hand):
1. Sequenced transmission studies contain donors who infected two or more recipients with directionality established, not merely inferred. CONTESTED, and PARTLY SUPPORTED by one source I opened: bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.02.22.432096 covers "39 transmission pairs involving 43 samples" in the Austrian SARS-CoV-2 superspreading data, and its Figure 1D control excludes random recipients that are "not a known recipient of that donor sample" — a fragment whose opening scope is an EXCLUSION RULE for a null construction, and which implies some donors have more than one known recipient without stating how many. The direction-of-transmission worry stands: in the HIV prior, pairs enter only where "the direction of transmission is reported from The Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) HIV sequence database", i.e. direction is inherited from a database annotation, not established.
2. Bottleneck width is estimable per pair with enough precision for a variance decomposition. CONTESTED, and the one dataset I opened is evidence AGAINST adequate precision: in bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.02.22.432096 the same corpus gives "the remaining 25 pairs having N_b estimates of 115-5000" at a 3% iSNV threshold, while under stricter filtering "The maximum likelihood estimate for N_b was 1 for 12 out of these 13 transmission pairs". A three-order-of-magnitude swing in the candidate's own measurand, driven by the threshold, is the sharpest well-posedness problem the candidate has.
3. The donor's sampled compartment and timing approximate the population actually transmitted. CONTESTED; not examined at first hand by me.
4. Bottleneck width is a meaningful per-event quantity rather than an artefact of selection during early within-recipient expansion. CONTESTED — and now sourced against, in the candidate's own direction of worry. bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585 (read in full from INTRODUCTION onward) states that "losses of diversity are primarily driven by events that occur during the expansion of infection", that "a severe bottleneck occurs in contact animals 1-2 days after the initiation of infection", and that "early events in the recipient host impose a stringent and stochastic bottleneck"; its companion clause "The bottleneck acting during the first stage can be very loose" has an opener scoping it to the FIRST of two stages, inter-host transfer. Direction of the clause: that paper ASSERTS a recipient-side bottleneck and DENIES that the transfer step is what sets the narrow one. So it cuts against the candidate's yes-branch rather than supporting it, and the candidate's restrict-to-early-recipient-samples remedy may not separate the two stages, since the severe bottleneck is placed 1-2 days INTO the recipient's infection.
   MEASURAND EXEMPTION DECLARED, not merely considered: presupposition 4 is the candidate reporting the very failure mode an attack would want to call a false premise. An attack that says "the bottleneck is really imposed in the recipient" is restating presupposition 4, which the question already flags as its dissolution risk; it is a reason the answer may be a null, not a reason the question is ill-formed.
5. Transmission events from one donor are conditionally independent given the donor. CONTESTED; the household/route/dose/environment confound is correctly flagged, and the remedy demanded (donors who infected recipients in different setting types) may empty the frame.
6. At least one existing dataset has enough one-to-many clusters for the variance component to be estimable at a pre-declared precision. UNVERIFIED BY ME IN BOTH DIRECTIONS. I opened no source that enumerates donors with two or more deep-sequenced recipients. This is a feasibility gate, not a finding, and it must be checked before the analysis, as the intake itself says.

## Gate 1 — Well-posedness

What would count as an answer (as submitted):
A fitted donor-level intraclass correlation of bottleneck width, with interval, across a pre-declared frame of one-to-many transmission clusters from existing deep-sequenced studies, under one pre-specified bottleneck estimator and one variant-calling threshold, with a null built by permuting recipients among donors within setting type; plus the change in donor-level variance when donor viral load, symptom status and days-since-onset are added. ICC indistinguishable from zero means the bottleneck is a route/recipient property and per-pathogen reporting is justified; ICC clearly above zero makes it a host trait, and the follow-on question is which donor-side physiology sets it. Sensitivity across two further estimators and two thresholds is part of the answer, not an appendix.

This is a crisp answer-condition with both branches interpreted in advance, and that is the candidate's strength. Three defects:
- DEFECT 1, the pre-declared set. "A pre-declared frame of one-to-many transmission clusters from existing deep-sequenced studies" is named and nowhere enumerated or generated by a rule. LEDGER.md Gate 1 requires one or the other. I supply a RULE (not an enumeration, and I say which): all published studies that (i) deep-sequence consensus-plus-iSNV data from both members of at least one human transmission pair of an acute respiratory virus at >=100x coverage with raw reads deposited in SRA/ENA, and (ii) report, or allow reconstruction of, a transmission network in which at least one individual is the inferred source for two or more others. Members verified at first hand: ONE — the Austrian SARS-CoV-2 superspreading corpus as reanalysed at bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.02.22.432096 (39 pairs, 43 samples), verified for clause (i) and only IMPLIED for clause (ii) by the Figure 1D exclusion wording quoted above. The count of qualifying donors in it is unknown to me.
- DEFECT 2, precision. Presupposition 2's threshold swing (115-5000 vs 1) means a donor ICC could be dominated by threshold-induced noise rather than by donor biology. The candidate's pre-specification of one estimator and one threshold handles the arbitrariness but not the noise.
- DEFECT 3, the null already has a precedent. The candidate's permutation null — permuting recipients among donors — is close in construction to the control already run in bioRxiv 10.1101/2021.02.22.432096, which pairs donor samples against random individuals that are "not a known recipient of that donor sample". That is a point against the novelty of the candidate's METHOD step, not of its estimand, and I record it as such rather than as a kill.

REFORMULATION (the one permitted at this gate; both versions recorded).
V2: Across the frame defined by the rule above, restricted to recipient specimens collected within three days of the recipient's symptom onset, what is the donor-level intraclass correlation of log10 bottleneck size N_b — N_b estimated under one pre-specified beta-binomial exact-estimator at a 2% iSNV calling threshold with >=100x coverage at called sites — with its interval against a null built by permuting recipients among donors within setting type, and how much of the donor-level variance survives adjustment for donor viral load, symptom status and days-since-onset? The frame is admitted to the analysis only if it yields at least 15 donors each with two or more qualifying recipients; below that the reported result is "not estimable at the pre-declared precision", which is a statement about the available events and not an answer to the question.

WHAT V2 NOW PRESUPPOSES, tested as hard as what it replaced:
- (a) That at least 15 donors with two or more deep-sequenced recipients exist in the pooled published record. UNVERIFIED BY ME. This is V2's load-bearing new presupposition and it is exactly the thing I could not establish; it is a promotion condition, not a fact. The failure mode is real: both barcoded-influenza animal studies I opened or part-opened have exactly ONE recipient per donor by design — bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585 says "we placed a naïve animal with each inoculated animal" — so the design that would answer this directly has not been run even in the animal literature, and the human datasets are the only source of one-to-many structure.
- (b) That one estimator and one threshold are applicable across pooled datasets of differing depth and platform. Not verified.
- (c) The three-day window is a stipulation, not a sourced cut, and bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585 places the severe bottleneck 1-2 days after initiation of infection in the recipient, which is INSIDE that window — so the window does not demonstrably separate transfer from early expansion. V2 does not repair defect 3 of the depth-adjacent worries; it makes the stipulation explicit.
DEPTH RE-APPLIED TO V2: Object — the founding virus populations transmitted between human hosts; Deletion — hosts and transmitted virions would still fix it; Terminal estimand — donor-level ICC of log10 N_b; Rows — one transmission event. V2 does not buy well-posedness by turning a world question into a record frame: the frame rule is a sampling rule over transmission EVENTS, and the studies are the instrument. PASS.

It is a question, not a thesis: the yes and no branches are both stated and both informative, and the candidate names the no-branch as a vindication of current practice.

## Gate 3 — Significance

CONSEQUENCE, argued. If the donor ICC is clearly above zero, bottleneck width is a host trait, and the per-pathogen and per-route numbers the field reports (one to a few genomes) are averages over a distribution of host types rather than constants of the virus. The operational consequence is specific: the probability that a minority variant in a donor — drug-resistant, immune-escape, antigenically novel — is handed on at all becomes a function of WHO the donor is, so the rate at which new lineages seed from infected people is not uniform across them, and the hosts from whom novel variants establish become identifiable in advance by a donor-side measurement rather than retrospectively by sequencing the lineages that happened to spread. If the ICC is indistinguishable from zero, the field's reporting convention is vindicated against a live alternative, the search for donor-side physiology is closed off, and bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585's recipient-side account gains the human-data support it does not currently have — a null here is not a non-result, because the HIV prior's 27%-vs-80% contrast predicts the opposite.
AUDIENCE, named. Within-host viral genomics and phylodynamics groups that estimate N_b per pair (the Koelle-group estimator line; the Lowen barcoded-influenza line; the SARS-CoV-2 household-sequencing groups) would change what they report: a per-pathogen N_b with a donor variance component beside it, rather than a single number. Variant-emergence and vaccine-escape modellers would change a parameter they currently set as a scalar. Outbreak epidemiologists who already stratify transmission by host (superspreading, k) would gain a second host-level axis.
FECUNDITY. If the ICC is non-zero: which donor-side physiology sets it — viral load, exhaled-particle production, mucus rheology, symptom type, immune status; and the question I find most interesting, whether the SAME hosts are extreme on both axes, i.e. whether overdispersion in offspring NUMBER and overdispersion in transmitted DIVERSITY are the same host trait or two. That joint object does not exist in any literature I searched. If the ICC is zero: the recipient-side account becomes the thing to decompose, and the candidate's own design turns around to ask whether bottleneck width is a repeatable RECIPIENT trait.
ANCHORING. It touches the superspreading/k-overdispersion literature (variance in offspring number, never joined to variance in founding population size); the unresolved threshold dependence visible inside prior 2 (N_b of 115-5000 versus 1 on one corpus); the Varble route-and-recipient framing; and the HIV source-stage result, which is the one piece of evidence in any pathogen that a donor covariate moves founder multiplicity.
SIGNIFICANCE SCORE: 3/3 — both branches change what a named community reports and does, and the fecundity limb names a new object rather than a further decimal place.

## Reason for silence

Three mechanisms, none of them "no one thought of it".
(1) A DISCIPLINARY BOUNDARY IN THE STATISTICS. Bottleneck width grew up as an ESTIMATOR problem — how to get N_b out of one pair's iSNV frequencies — and the apparatus for asking whether a trait recurs within an individual (repeatability, intraclass correlation, variance components) lives in animal breeding, behavioural ecology and quantitative genetics, not in phylodynamics. My query 4, which put the two vocabularies in one string, returned no document joining them; that absence is a fact about my sweep, but it is consistent with the boundary.
(2) THE EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN THAT WOULD ANSWER IT WAS NEVER RUN, because the design question was route. Both barcoded-influenza studies in this line pair each donor with exactly one recipient — bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585 states "we placed a naïve animal with each inoculated animal" — which is the right design for comparing aerosol with contact and the wrong one for comparing donors. One donor to several recipients is standard in veterinary transmission work, so the design exists; it was simply never pointed at this question.
(3) THE NESTING IN THE HUMAN DATA WAS TREATED AS A NUISANCE RATHER THAN AS THE SIGNAL. The datasets that do contain one-to-many clusters were analysed pair-by-pair with the pair as the independent unit: prior 1 states "our model assumes a single transmission event between each source and recipient partner", and prior 2 fits per-pair estimates and groups them by nothing. Prior 2 goes further and uses the donor-recipient structure only to build a NULL — excluding individuals "not a known recipient of that donor sample" — which is the candidate's own permutation idea turned inside out: the structure was handled as something to control for, not something to measure. A quantity that every relevant analysis explicitly assumes away is the classic shape of an unasked question.
