## Restatement (my own words)

Take a crystal thin enough for multislice electron ptychography, containing isolated point defects of one declared kind (a substitutional foreign atom or a vacancy). For the atoms in the first, second, ... coordination shells around ONE such defect, measure each atom's thermal mean-square displacement u² by ptychography, then change the specimen temperature, re-find the very same defects, and measure again. Compute what harmonic lattice dynamics predicts for a supercell that already contains the same defect. Ask three things: (a) is the measured u² at shell n different from that harmonic prediction; (b) does the ratio measured/harmonic change between the two temperatures (the question asserts a harmonic perturbation forbids this and a locally anharmonic or resonant mode requires it); (c) over how many shells does any difference fade back to the host value. A dose series is carried to bound beam-induced motion.
