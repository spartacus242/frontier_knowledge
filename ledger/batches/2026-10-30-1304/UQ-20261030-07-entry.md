Question (canonical): Across human one-to-many transmission clusters of an acute respiratory virus, what is the donor-level intraclass correlation of log10 transmission bottleneck size N_b — i.e. do the several recipients infected by one donor show correlated founding-population sizes, and what fraction of between-event variance in bottleneck width sits at the donor level? (Judged on the Gate 1 reformulation V2, which pins one beta-binomial exact estimator, a 2% iSNV calling threshold, >=100x coverage, recipient specimens within 3 days of recipient symptom onset, a permutation null within setting type, adjustment for donor viral load / symptom status / days-since-onset, and a frame-admission gate of >=15 donors each with >=2 qualifying recipients; V1 as submitted is in batches/2026-10-30-1304/UQ-20261030-07-record.md, Gate 0.)
Alternative formulations: Do some people pass on many virions and others only one, consistently across everyone they infect, so that bottleneck width is a host trait rather than a property of the route? / Decomposed into donor, recipient and pair components, where does the variance in the size of the founding viral population actually sit?
Motivating gap: Presupposition inversion. Bottleneck width for acute respiratory viruses is reported per PATHOGEN or per ROUTE, which presupposes it is a property of the virus and the route rather than of the individual donor. If it is donor-repeatable, WHICH hosts transmit decides how much within-host diversity (minority resistant, immune-escape or antigenically novel variants) is handed on at all. The record withdraws the intake's "never been tested": a donor-side determinant of founder multiplicity is established in HIV-1 (medRxiv 10.1101/19013524, read in full, prints "acute stage transmissions are less likely to lead to single particle infections compared with chronic stage transmissions (27% vs. 80%, P = 0.0005)"). What is untested is the variance decomposition with recipients nested in one donor.

Significance — consequence: If the donor ICC is clearly above zero, the per-pathogen and per-route numbers the field reports are averages over host types rather than constants of the virus, and the probability that a minority variant is handed on at all becomes a function of who the donor is — making the hosts from whom novel lineages seed identifiable in advance by a donor-side measurement. If it is indistinguishable from zero, the reporting convention is vindicated against a live alternative (the HIV 27%-vs-80% contrast predicts the opposite), and the recipient-side account of bioRxiv 10.1101/2023.11.19.567585 gains human support it lacks.
Significance — audience: The groups that estimate N_b per pair would report a donor variance component beside the pathogen number. Adversary 2 sharpens this from notional to named: the audience is the authorship of the two papers it read in full — the estimator-owning group (Sobel Leonard, Weissman, Greenbaum, Ghedin, Koelle) and a published comment re-estimating another group's bottleneck on the same corpus — a community that argues publicly about exactly this number. Variant-emergence and vaccine-escape modellers set N_b as a scalar and would gain a second parameter; outbreak epidemiologists who stratify transmission by host (superspreading, k) gain a second host-level axis.
Significance — fecundity: If non-zero: which donor-side physiology sets it (viral load, exhaled-particle production, mucus rheology, symptom type, immune status), and whether overdispersion in offspring NUMBER and overdispersion in transmitted DIVERSITY are the same host trait or two — a joint object absent from every literature searched. If zero: the recipient side becomes the thing to decompose, and the same design turns around onto recipient repeatability.
Significance — anchoring: Superspreading / k-overdispersion (variance in offspring number, never joined to variance in founding-population size); the unresolved threshold dependence inside bioRxiv 2021.02.22.432096 (N_b 115-5000 at 3% versus 1 for 12 of 13 pairs under stricter filtering); the Varble route-and-recipient framing; the HIV source-stage result.
