## Restatement (my words, before attacking)

Take the numeric settings that recur across biology protocols (37 C, 5% CO2, 10% serum, 4% PFA for 10 min, 1:1000 antibody, 12/12 h light...). Choose the set by a pre-registered frequency rule over a dated protocol corpus, flagging the ones physics forces. For each, trace the documentation back to where the number came from and code it as (i) a real optimisation experiment under conditions still in use, (ii) a real optimisation under conditions now superseded, (iii) a non-evidential source (vendor sheet, textbook, older protocol), or (iv) untraceable. Report the four rates with intervals and inter-coder agreement. Then, for a random sample of (ii), redo the optimisation under today's conditions and see whether the optimum moves past a pre-set margin.
