The refutation first. Ambroz et al., Small Methods 2018 (doi 10.1002/smtd.201800173) contains no pore-size-dependent sign change in the habitual-minus-criteria BET difference. Read through the UCL open-access copy, the text says the standard range "should not be used for MOFs with pore sizes below 7 Å" and that the resulting areas "can be incorrect", and its ONE directional statement runs the opposite way to the kill: for the sub-7 Å IRMOFs the criteria-selected values are expected to be higher than standard-range ones ("we expect the values would be higher"), which makes the habitual window an underestimate there, not an overestimate. The word "underestimate" does not appear anywhere in the paper, and the sentences the adversaries read as overestimation are about micropore pore filling and about NU-1103/1104's inflated monolayer loading ("the monolayer loading is overestimated resulting in excessive surface area") — a different mechanism from pressure-window choice.
