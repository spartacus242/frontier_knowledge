## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Haplotig-purging tools (purge_dups, purge_haplotigs, hifiasm's built-in purge) are run by default in EBP/VGP-style assembly pipelines to delete contigs that look like a retained second haplotype: roughly half-depth read coverage plus very high identity to another contig. The candidate claims that very young, near-identical gene duplicates share that signature, and asks (a) what fraction of real young duplicates the purge step itself deletes (pipeline otherwise fixed, with an independent per-locus copy-number instrument on the same individual), and (b) whether putting them back measurably moves published CAFE-style gene-family gain/loss rates and the youngest bin of Ks/identity distributions, even after CAFE's epsilon error term is allowed to absorb what it can.
