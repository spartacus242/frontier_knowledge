# Gate 4 adversary (late, independent pass) — UQ-20261016-54

Adversary ran with the Gate 0-3 record only. Budget used: 8 web searches (all NEW vocabularies), 4 fetches. No search was refused.

## 0. Restatement in my own words (before touching the generator's framing)

Take a named set of open-weight model releases. Enumerate every publicly posted model derived from them — re-quantizations (GGUF/MLX/AWQ/GPTQ etc.), fine-tunes, merges, and "uncensored"/abliterated variants — sort them into those four buckets in advance, and give each one a weight equal to how many times the hub says it was downloaded at a fixed date. Now score each artifact on a fixed capability suite and a fixed refusal/jailbreak-resistance test, and ask: how far is the download-weighted average score from the score of the original released weights, where the original is re-measured through the same inference engine and precision as the bucket it is being compared with? Report that gap per metric against a margin declared beforehand, with confidence intervals, a measured census coverage error, the same computation under at least three other weightings, and a split of the variance into between-bucket and within-bucket (different uploaders, same nominal setting).

Plain version: **is the model that gets benchmarked representative of the models people actually download, and by how much is it off?**

## 3. What the three new sources actually do (unit / endpoint / reference / estimand)

**arXiv:2608.15046, "Certifying Compressed Language Models: An Audit and a Statistical Toolkit".**
UNIT: 1,707 paired model-by-task cells mined from public per-item evaluation dumps (1.3B–405B), plus an audit of 17 published equivalence claims from method papers, model cards and vendor docs, plus the author's own controlled GPTQ-vs-AWQ experiment. Not third-party hub uploads — the summariser could not confirm hub provenance, and no download data appears.
ENDPOINT: net accuracy delta vs **per-item churn** (per-item disagreement between compressed and original), and per-item agreement.
REFERENCE: the uncompressed original, paired per item.
ESTIMAND: paired equivalence at a **declared margin**.
Exact strings verified one at a time: "That quantity is least informative when two models are most alike"; "median per-item churn is about five times the median net accuracy delta"; "Not one of the 16 declares a numerical tolerance in advance".
No download weighting, no ecosystem population, no refusal/safety metric (confirmed by the fetch: "Refusal/safety measurement: Not mentioned").

**arXiv:2608.18086, "Position: Current Model Cards Are Insufficient for Downstream Governance of Open-Weight Foundation Models".**
UNIT: 500 Hugging Face model cards. ENDPOINT: documentation content and licence/AUP enforceability — "standard open-source licenses (OSLs) are not well suited for OWFMs and may weaken the enforceability of AUPs". REFERENCE: none. ESTIMAND: none; position paper. Fetch confirms: download counts not mentioned, no measured capability or refusal numbers, no download-share figures.
**Consequence: the record's named highest residual risk is now discharged. It does not contain a derivative-population measurement.**

**arXiv:2503.15222, "Model Hubs and Beyond: Analyzing Model Popularity, Performance, and Documentation".**
UNIT: 500 Hugging Face *sentiment-analysis* models (~80,000 human annotations). ENDPOINT: measured task performance vs popularity (downloads, likes, recency), and documentation completeness ("approximately 80% of the models analyzed lack detailed information about the model, training, and evaluation processes"). REFERENCE: none — models are compared with each other, not against a base release. ESTIMAND: whether popularity tracks performance; headline "model popularity does not necessarily correlate with performance".
This is the closest thing yet to *joining downloads to measured behaviour on a hub*, and the record missed it. But it is not the candidate: no derivative population of a named release, no partition into quantization/fine-tune/merge/abliteration, no weighting (popularity is a correlate, not a weight), no reference-weights comparator, no safety endpoint, one task.

## 4. The eight attacks

### Attack 1 — Triviality. Objection → Response → **Resolved**
Objection: the answer is deducible in a page. The download mass is obviously dominated by faithful re-quantizations; quantization degradation is already characterised; abliteration's direction is already fixed by Alice.io. So D ≈ small, concentrated in a small abliterated tail, and no study is needed.
Response (concrete, and it defeats the objection): the simplest sub-limb of this — whether a compressed artifact is behaviourally equivalent to its original at all — needed an entire 2026 paper over 1,707 model-by-task cells to settle even qualitatively (arXiv:2608.15046), and its result was counter-intuitive rather than deducible: "median per-item churn is about five times the median net accuracy delta". Second, the fraction limb is not deducible from anything published: query 4 here and query 3 in the record both returned no published derivative-class download share, and the independently-established facts for this candidate record that even Model Pulse's figures could not be confirmed as published. Third, the within-class/between-uploader term is empirical and currently only in substack posts. A page of reasoning yields a sign, not a magnitude, a mass-location or a decomposition.
**Resolved.**

### Attack 2 — Disguise. Objection → Response → **Resolved**
Objection: this is a known question in other words — container-registry security measurement, generic-drug equivalence surveillance, popularity-vs-performance on model hubs, compressed-model certification.
Response: 8 new queries across Chinese, Japanese, container-registry, pharma-regulatory, supply-chain-audit, and exact-phrase weighting vocabularies, and the three nearest hits opened. Each fails a different load-bearing limb:
- 2608.15046 has the declared-margin machinery and the reference comparator and the compression class, and has **no population, no weights, no safety endpoint** (fetch: download counts "Not mentioned", refusal "Not mentioned"). It is the method without the population.
- 2503.15222 has downloads and measured behaviour on a hub, and has **no derivative population, no weights, no base comparator, one task**. It is the data without the estimand.
- 2608.18086 has 500 hub artifacts and the governance framing, and measures **documentation only**.
- Docker Hub work selects the most-pulled images rather than weighting by pulls, and has no behavioural reference.
- Pharma equivalence is per-generic against the RLD, not market-share-weighted population expectation.
Nothing I ran names a release's third-party derivative population as its unit, weighted by acquisition, with a behavioural endpoint against the reference weights. **Resolved**, on a negative as concrete as a negative gets: 8 new vocabularies, 3 near-misses opened and dissected rather than assumed.

### Attack 3 — Combinatorial novelty. Objection → Response → **Resolved**
Objection: survey sampling × Hugging Face. Bolt unequal-probability estimation onto a scraped hub table; no problem lives at the interface.
Response: four interface problems that neither parent field hands you.
(a) **Format–engine confounding**: a GGUF artifact and a safetensors reference cannot share an inference engine, so the naive gap is partly a llama.cpp-vs-vLLM artifact. The record's R1 invents a format-matched control for this; survey sampling has no analogue because its units do not come with incommensurable measuring instruments.
(b) **The weights are endogenous to packaging**: per the independently-established facts, the hub counts a download as a server-side request to a query file and for GGUF repos *every* `.gguf` file is a query file, while per-file counts are not documented as exposed. So the sampling weight depends on how many quant levels an uploader packed into one repo. Unequal-probability estimation assumes known selection probabilities; here the probability instrument is contaminated in a class-correlated way. That is a genuine open problem at the interface, and (see attack 4) it is also the candidate's deepest wound.
(c) **The frame is a DAG, not a list**: a quantization of a fine-tune, and a merge with a parent outside R, have no natural class; the partition has to be legislated.
(d) **The endpoint itself is contested in this substrate**: 2608.15046 shows the net-delta endpoint is the wrong one for compressed-vs-original pairs. So even the "metric" slot is not off-the-shelf.
**Resolved.**

### Attack 4 — Presupposition failure. Objection → Response → **Partial**
Two contested presuppositions, one amplified and one *missing from the record's own list of seven*.

(i) Amplified. The record's P1 ("downloads proxy use") treats the hazard as mirrors, CI and bots. It never addresses the sharper hazard: the counter's **per-file multiplicity**. Given facts for this candidate: a download is a server-side request to a query file, and in a GGUF repo every `.gguf` file counts. A repo shipping Q2…Q8 therefore accumulates counts at a multiple of acquisitions, and the multiple differs systematically between the re-quantization class (many query files per repo) and the fine-tune/merge classes (safetensors shards). So the *primary* weight is biased in exactly the direction that decides which class holds the mass. R1's "stated within-repo allocation rule" is the right instinct but is a free parameter, and none of the three offered sensitivity weightings (likes, provider catalogues, router shares) corrects it — they are different populations, as the record itself concedes.

(ii) Missing from P1–P7. The candidate presupposes that the **net population expectation is the decision-relevant functional** — that D = E_w[m] − m(ref) summarises the thing governance cares about, and in particular that a null D licenses the conclusion the record stakes its NO branch on ("vindicates reference-weight evaluation as a population proxy"). An opened source in the candidate's own field, on exactly the compressed-vs-original pairs that constitute the candidate's dominant class, contradicts that: "That quantity is least informative when two models are most alike", with "median per-item churn is about five times the median net accuracy delta" (arXiv:2608.15046). **Scope check, because this is where adversaries on this ledger have gone wrong**: that paper's regime is compression vs original on capability benchmarks, 1.3B–405B, which *is* the candidate's class (i) and capability metric; it does **not** cover fine-tunes, merges, abliteration, or the refusal endpoint, and it says nothing about download weighting. So the objection bites the candidate's capability limb and its NO branch, and leaves the refusal limb and the YES/magnitude limb standing.

Best response available to the candidate: the estimand is deliberately defined on the quantity the field actually reports and decides on (net benchmark numbers, net refusal rates), so a download-weighted version of that same number is the apples-to-apples object; and the class partition plus between/within decomposition surfaces where the mass sits. That response is plausible and not circular — it is simply unevidenced that any allocation rule recovers acquisition from the multiplicity-inflated counter, and it concedes that the NO branch must be restated as "the reported number is reproduced in expectation", not "reference-weight evaluation is a valid proxy". Note also that the variance decomposition R1 specifies is over *artifacts*; churn is a per-*item* quantity, so no amount of between/within-class decomposition recovers it.
**Partial.** Not Unresolved: the question survives with the functional presupposition flagged and the NO branch's claim narrowed. Not Resolved: a presupposition that an opened on-regime source contradicts, and which the record did not enumerate, is not defeated.

### Attack 5 — Vagueness. Objection → Response → **Partial**
Objection: R1 is a protocol schema, not a question. R (which releases), the capability suite, the refusal measure, the decision margins, the allocation rule and the three alternative weightings are all "pre-specified" — i.e. specified by the answerer, not by the question. The YES/NO verdict is a function of those free choices: pick an abliteration-heavy family and a tight margin and you get YES; pick one well-behaved release, MMLU, and a 5-point margin and you get NO. So it can be answered either way without the evidence changing.
Response: once a design is declared the estimand is identified and both branches are stated and falsifiable; and the deferral is deliberate pre-registration rather than evasion — the same structure as "what is the in-use-vs-type-approval NOx gap", which also fixes fleet, pollutant and margin at design time. Supporting: 2608.15046 audited 17 equivalence claims and found "Not one of the 16 declares a numerical tolerance in advance", so demanding a prospective margin is a recognised corrective in this exact literature, not hand-waving.
**Partial.** The response is plausible; what is unevidenced is that the gap is robust to the design choices, and the record constrains none of them — not R, not the suite, not a single margin.

### Attack 6 — Significance inflation. Objection → Response → **Partial**
Objection, in two parts.
(a) The NO branch is the record's claimed load-bearing negative ("retire a live governance worry cheaply"). Per 2608.15046 that inference does not hold in the candidate's dominant class: a null net delta is "least informative when two models are most alike". So the branch the record counts as half its significance cannot deliver the conclusion it promises without per-item work the question does not specify.
(b) For the safety audience the decision-relevant quantity is a **tail mass**, not a mean: a releaser writing a safety case or AISI assessing marginal risk needs "what download share goes to safeguard-removed variants", which a download-weighted mean dilutes by construction — and the mass is overwhelmingly faithful re-quantizations, so the mean is near-dominated by the least interesting class. A statistic that averages the hazard away is a poor instrument for the audience named.
Response: R1 does require each metric component judged against its own pre-declared margin *and* a between-class decomposition, so the abliterated class is reported separately and the mass-location is recoverable; the record's own fecundity field names "which derivative class carries the download mass" as the first follow-on. The audience is evidenced from print (TMLR 2026, AISI, procurement practitioners) rather than asserted. The record already holds significance at 2/3 for a related reason.
**Partial.** The audience is real; the headline estimand is mismatched to what that audience would act on, and the repair (report the tail, not the mean) changes the question rather than answering the objection.

### Attack 7 — Reason for silence. Objection → Response → **Partial**
The attack must explain why nobody **asked**, not why answering is hard. The record's mechanism — a unit-of-analysis norm (ML evaluation keys everything to a release) plus a disciplinary boundary (the method is owned by survey sampling, fleet metrology and registry measurement) plus an incentive gap plus instrument lag — does address asking, and it has one genuinely evidential anchor: ATOM, the first work to use hub download weights at ecosystem scale, wrote the candidate's population out of scope *by rule* ("exclude local-inference re-uploads (e.g., GGUF, MLX)"). An exclusion rule is evidence of a norm in a way that an absence is not.
But two competing mechanisms, both of which the record does not consider, are at least as good:
(a) **The asking has already started, minus the weights.** arXiv:2608.15046 (Aug 2026) asks precisely "is the compressed artifact equivalent to the reference, at a declared margin?", audits the field's published equivalence claims, and builds the statistical toolkit. So the un-asked residue is narrower than the record's mechanism implies: not "nobody frames artifact-vs-reference as an estimand" but "nobody has weighted it by acquisition".
(b) **Informed silence about the weight.** Because a hub download is a request to a query file and GGUF repos multiply query files per acquisition (given facts), practitioners who know the counter know it is not an acquisition count. A question whose primary weight is a known packaging artifact is one people decline to ask, which is a credible mechanism — and a less flattering one, because it implies the silence is justified rather than accidental.
**Partial.** A credible mechanism exists (so the novelty finding is not thereby suspect, and this is not Unresolved), but the record's mechanism is only partly the right one and the competing account it missed cuts against the question's value.

### Attack 8 — Dissolution. Objection → Response → **Partial**
Objection: "the model evaluated is not the model run" dissolves on analysis. Nobody runs the average artifact; each downloader runs exactly one, and what they need is that artifact's number, which the population mean does not give. And since a null D does not establish equivalence (2608.15046), the single number D does not answer the underlying worry either. What looked like one question is a bookkeeping aggregate plus a set of per-artifact questions already being answered piecemeal (localbench, kaitchup, 2608.15046).
Response: the aggregate has a determinate referent — the expected metric value of a uniformly-drawn download — and that referent is exactly what the mature analogues report (sales-weighted fleet averages, in-use vs type-approval values, which the record's query 5 locates in ICCT/JRC work). A population mean of a per-unit quantity is not a dissolved concept in any field that regulates a fleet. So the *question* does not dissolve.
**Partial.** The question survives; the *framing claim* — that answering it settles whether reference-weight evaluation is a valid proxy — does partly dissolve, for the reason in attack 4(ii).

## 5. Verdict from this pass

No attack Unresolved; attacks 1, 2 and 3 Resolved on opened sources and an 8-vocabulary negative sweep; five Partials, the heaviest on attack 4.

**Provisional.** Not Rejected: Disguise swept clean in vocabularies the record never tried, the record's single named highest residual risk (2608.18086) was opened and does not contain the measurement, and the interface problems are real. Not Validated: the primary weight is packaging-dependent in a class-correlated way, and the headline functional is contested by an on-regime source the record had not seen.

**Single strongest surviving objection (key kill, non-fatal):** the candidate's headline estimand is a *net* delta, and arXiv:2608.15046 establishes for exactly the compressed-vs-original pairs that dominate the candidate's download mass that "That quantity is least informative when two models are most alike", with "median per-item churn is about five times the median net accuracy delta" — so the NO branch, on which the record rests half its significance, cannot support "vindicates reference-weight evaluation as a population proxy". Compounding it, the primary weight is a per-file request counter whose multiplicity differs systematically between the GGUF and safetensors classes, which is the very comparison that decides where the mass sits.

**Promotion conditions:** (1) enumerate the functional presupposition explicitly and either adopt a per-item/churn-style endpoint or narrow the NO branch to "the reported number is reproduced in expectation"; (2) fix the within-repo allocation rule against the documented query-file counting behaviour and show the class ordering is invariant to it; (3) add a declared tail statistic (download share and mass of behaviour-modification derivatives) alongside the mean; (4) carry arXiv:2503.15222 and arXiv:2608.15046 as nearest priors — the record has neither; (5) open arXiv:2608.11803, 2512.03073, 2512.13655 and hf/papers/2607.17427, none of which was opened by Gate 2 or by this pass.
