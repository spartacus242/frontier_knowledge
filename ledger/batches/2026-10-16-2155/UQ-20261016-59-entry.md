Domain(s): Biology — population/conservation genomics of long-lived modular organisms (trees, corals, clonal plants); somatic mutation. Neighbouring: cancer multiregion genomics, survey-sampling variance components, forest/aquaculture clone identity.

Question (canonical): In long-lived modular organisms, how much do standard population-genomic estimands — individual heterozygosity, pairwise relatedness/kinship, allele frequencies and the Ne derived from them — change depending on which module of the same individual was sampled, and is that within-individual sampling variance large relative to the between-individual variance those estimands are meant to measure?
Question (Gate 1 reformulation, carried forward): On a crossed design of N individuals x M >= 3 spatially separated modules per individual in >= 2 taxa (one long-lived tree, one colonial/clonal marine organism), sequenced at matched depth with module provenance recorded, what is the ratio of the between-module-within-individual to the between-individual variance component, with an interval, for each of four pre-specified estimands (per-individual observed heterozygosity; pairwise kinship; per-site allele frequency; one LD-based Ne), after a technical component is estimated from replicate libraries and chimeric individuals are classified and reported separately from mosaics? Target of estimation is the module-conditional estimate plus its between-module variance, not a single per-individual "true" value.

Alternative formulations:
- Does it matter which branch of the tree you sequence?
- Treating within-individual somatic mosaicism as a measurement-error term rather than as biology: how large is the between-module variance component in population-genomic estimates for trees, corals and clonal plants, and which published conclusions are sensitive to it?

Motivating gap: Within-individual somatic variation is now directly measurable and mapped in named organisms (Pando aspen mosaic; per-branch somatic variant counts in oak and Populus), and theory exists for indexing it (Iwasa, Tomimoto & Satake 2024, Quantitative Plant Biology 5:e11, doi 10.1017/qpb.2024.9 — theoretical, three within-tree distance indexes, no estimand). The literature treats mosaicism as biology or as a Discussion-section caveat; no opened source propagates module choice into the estimands the field reports. Gate 2 established the caveat has been voiced explicitly in the coral literature since 2012 and never quantified.

Presuppositions:
1. Somatic variation detectable above sequencing error at population-study depth. CONTESTED — and materially weakened at Gate 4: Schmid-Siegert et al. (oak, 40X WGS) ran 314,865 candidate discordant sites to 17 confirmed; Oury et al. 2024 (46X capture) report IGV counts "extremely sensitive to the threshold defined".
2. Somatic variants reach within-module frequencies high enough to move an individual-level estimand. CONTESTED, the crux, and the ground of the rejection (see Adversarial log, attack 1).
3. Multi-module sampling at matched depth with provenance recorded is feasible. ESTABLISHED (Oury 2024: 93 colonies x 3 nubbins + replicates; Herrera 2022: 15 plants x 5 modules; Pando: 184 samples).
4. The estimands remain defined for a mosaic individual. Dissolution risk, handled by the module-conditional target — but not retired for the Ne limb (Pando: "The unit of effective population size here can be thought of in terms of cellular lineages").
5. Module provenance recoverable in some published datasets. PARTLY CONTESTED; now partly satisfied (Oury 2024 data public, Zenodo 10633675).
6. Chimerism separable from mosaicism. NECESSARY, and shown fragile at Gate 4 (threshold-dependent antimode; mosaic-class discordance density implausible for point mutation).

What would count as an answer: A variance-components analysis on multi-module sequencing in >= 2 taxa partitioning each estimand's variance into between-individual, between-module-within-individual and technical components, with the between-module share and interval per estimand and chimeras reported separately; plus a sensitivity arm re-running a published analysis with modules swapped, reporting how often a conclusion-relevant threshold is crossed. A measured near-zero share answers it in the negative and licenses single-module practice explicitly.

Verification (post-batch, fresh-context checker, log UQ-20261016-59-verification.md): the arithmetic that carries the kill is CONFIRMED from one paper and one pipeline; two decorations around it are not.
