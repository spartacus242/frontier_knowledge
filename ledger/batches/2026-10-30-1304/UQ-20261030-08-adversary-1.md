# UQ-20261030-08 — Gate 4, adversary #1

## Restatement (my own words, before reading the record's framing again)

Take a human carcinoma that has been cut out whole. Ask whether the cells in its outermost shell carry only a thinned-down draw of the clones found deeper in the tumour — because the advance is driven by the few cells that happen to sit at the very edge, so each step forward is a small founder draw (a "pulled" front) — or whether the edge carries essentially the clonal variety of the bulk, because the advance is fed from behind (a "pushed" front). Quantitatively: along distance from the invasive margin inward, how much clonal diversity is there at the outermost bin relative to the bulk, over what distance does that ratio recover, and is the shape of that profile what loss of clones by chance at a moving front would produce rather than what one clone sweeping along the edge would produce. And then: is the answer the same in different organs' carcinomas, or does the class differ by tumour type.

The one permitted reformulation keeps that estimand but (i) restricts the tumour-type set to one generated by a rule over PUBLISHED spatially resolved series ("three organ sites for which published multi-region or spatially resolved sequencing series of grossly circumscribed primary carcinomas with recorded sample-to-margin geometry exist at n >= 10 samples per tumour"), and (ii) requires the profile to be matched against a drift-driven-front prediction with an edge-localised sweep estimated separately.
