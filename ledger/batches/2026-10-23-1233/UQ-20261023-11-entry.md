Significance — consequence: if the common-cause excess is large, every capability and completeness product inherits an optimism that no amount of per-station compliance reporting can reveal, because the governed unit is the station marginal while the failure mode is the joint, and mitigation changes category from adding stations to diversifying telemetry, power and firmware. If it is about zero, the field gains a published null it does not have and threshold monitoring can be read as per-station-independent in availability. Deflated by Gaebler & Ceranna: for the IMS globally the smallest margin in the grid (0.2 m_b) is reached only near 50% random loss and the current 12% shortfall costs under 0.05 m_b, so the stakes are bounded globally and live regionally, where a clustered loss of the three governing stations is not a global-average effect.
