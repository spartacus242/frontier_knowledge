Domain(s): Biology — microbiology / microbial systematics and biological resource centres; secondary comparative genomics, research-reagent reproducibility. Method-owning neighbour: reference-material metrology (between-unit homogeneity / stability, ISO Guide 35).

Question (canonical):
V2 (the one Gate 1 reformulation, adopted as canonical). Over a frame of co-held microbial accessions declared before any sequencing — strains linked as "equivalent" across two or more independent collections (ATCC, DSMZ, NCTC, CIP, JCM, NCIMB) by catalogue cross-reference or StrainInfo identifier matching — sequence the material as distributed by each collection (freshly ordered ampoules, primary arm; distributor-provenanced public assemblies, secondary arm) and report (a) the pairwise cross-collection divergence distribution partitioned into SNV / indel / IS / plasmid / prophage classes; (b) the share of accession pairs exceeding a comparator measured inside the same study — the divergence between two independently revived ampoules of one lot from one collection — not a stipulated threshold; (c) the dependence of divergence on transfers-since-deposit and years-since-deposit, under a pre-declared rule separating post-deposit drift from cross-referencing error.
(V1, superseded at Gate 1: same question with "a pre-declared within-strain comparator", which was undefined and so answerable by stipulation.)

Alternative formulations:
- (lay) Is "the same strain" one organism across the world's culture collections, or has each collection's freezer grown its own version?
- (metrology) Treating each co-held accession as a reference material issued by two or more producers, what is the between-producer inhomogeneity of the genomic property value versus each producer's within-lot inhomogeneity?

Motivating gap: Divergence between nominally identical holdings of one strain is reported case by case and never aggregated as a population quantity. Desroches et al. 2018 sequenced the ancestral Escherich E. coli strain as held by NCTC, CIP, ATCC and DSMZ — 164/803/878 SNPs and 127/227/230 indels against NCTC86_Dunne, i.e. four collections' holdings are four lineages — and calls for no broader survey. StrainInfo welds 717,337 identifiers / 476,193 deposits onto 314,035 strains, verifying matches by comparing sequence *accessions*, never sequence content. READ LEVEL caveats from the record: prior (B) read as preprint; four PMC-hosted leads unread at Gate 2.

Presuppositions (post-Gate-4 status):
P1 Enough co-held accessions exist (K) — supported by StrainInfo's deposit:strain ratio; exact K unpublished, must be extracted from the frame as step 0.
P2 Each collection's distributed material is obtainable — established for the prospective arm; retrospective arm contested and demoted to secondary.
P3 Divergence well defined under a symmetric reference-free variant/SV protocol — established for bacteria; contested for fungi/protists, to be excluded or stratified.
P4 A comparator exists — repaired by V2's internally measured within-lot replicate baseline; needs an interval, not a point.
P5 Transfers-since-deposit retrievable — WORSE THAN CONTESTED after Gate 4: DSMZ's catalogue History field is defined as history *before* deposit, the wrong side of the event for limb (c); limb (c) stays secondary.
P6 Divergence is post-deposit drift rather than two wrongly linked isolates — contested, load-bearing; reported as a result under the declared separation rule.
P7 One ampoule stands for the accession — contested; lot-to-lot variance needs its own arm.
P8 A cross-reference claims one physical lineage — NOW EVIDENCED, and it cuts the candidate's way: NCIMB's own curator page states it is "generally accepted amongst major collections" that equivalent cultures "will be indistinguishable from one another", that "genetic drift may alter the properties of the strains between collections", and that these are "rare occurrences" — an unquantified rarity claim, which is the candidate's estimand. But DSMZ's field guide defines "Other collection numbers" only as "Accession numbers in other collections", so the claim is NOT uniform across the frame (see strongest objection).

What would count as an answer: (i) the frame fixed before any data pull, with K reported; (ii) per accession, assemblies of each collection's distributed material plus the within-lot replicate pairs; (iii) one pre-declared symmetric reference-free variant+SV protocol; (iv) the divergence distribution with class partition; (v) the exceedance share with an interval under pre-committed bands (CONCENTRATED-NEAR-ZERO / MODEST-DRIFT / LINEAGE-DIVERGENT) so a null is informative; (vi) regressions on transfers and years for the subset with retrievable histories, with its coverage fraction; (vii) an explicit mis-cross-reference rate under the declared rule. A distribution tight near zero is equally publishable.

Significance — consequence: If divergence is concentrated near zero, administrative equivalence is validated as genomic equivalence and cross-reference tables become usable as scientific statements. If a material share exceeds the within-lot baseline — and (A)'s 878 SNPs shows the upper tail is not hypothetical — "the strain" is a collection-specific lineage, and four practices lose a premise: the ICSP type-strain principle, QC-strain-based assay standardisation (CLSI/EUCAST organisms sourced from whichever collection is local), accession-based industrial and probiotic identity claims, and the reproducibility reading of thousands of methods sections. It also bears on whether a genome sequence can stand as nomenclatural type or must be tied to one named holding.
Significance — audience: instrumented and demonstrably acting. Collections and their federations (WFCC/WDCM, ECCO, StrainInfo as the graph's maintainer) — available action: flag or withdraw a cross-reference, publish per-accession diffs; RIKEN JCM already does this one accession at a time by public notice (JCM 12229T, JCM 1633T withdrawn and replaced), which is exactly the case-by-case practice a rate would systematise. ICSP / IJSEM / SeqCode on what a type is. CLSI, EUCAST, ISO/EURL reference-material producers who already run between-unit homogeneity studies for every other property. Reusing laboratories deciding whether to sequence on arrival. Probiotic and biocontrol regulators. Discount recorded: ATCC is already applying authentication pressure without the cross-collection axis.
Significance — fecundity: a drift rate per transfer or per year makes passage budgets computable; makes "how much divergence before MICs shift" answerable; opens whether drift is random or convergent (mutator alleles, IS activation, prophage loss at the same loci across collections would be laboratory domestication, not noise); makes a cross-collection coalescent datable as an archival record; exports to cell-line, antibody, mouse-line and plasmid analogues.
Significance — anchoring: the type-strain-as-physical-object problem and the genome-as-type debate; (A)'s mutator acquisition in collection passage; (B)'s authenticated-vs-crowd-sourced discordance; the pre-genomic "strain degeneration on subculture" literature, never converted into a population estimate; and reference-material metrology's between-unit homogeneity framework.

Reason for silence: Gate 4 re-ordered the record's list and the surviving mechanisms are structural, not "no one thought of it". Strongest, supplied by adversaries 1 and 2: the field holds a consensus answer by assertion — a major collection states in public catalogue guidance that equivalent cultures "will be indistinguishable" and that drift between collections is a "rare occurrence", with no study, number or citation behind it, and a quantity everyone treats as already characterised is not asked about. Second: a boundary inside the only institutions that could answer it — the equivalence graph sits in collection informatics, sequencing sits in each collection's own QC, and the comparison is between peer depositories, so no single actor owns the pair. Third: a systematics norm that reads variation as a curation error to fix case by case rather than a distribution to estimate — (A) narrates four non-identical holdings as one strain's "odyssey" and proposes no survey; JCM absorbs each counterexample as a withdrawal notice. STRUCK on adversarial review: the record's lead mechanism ("public metadata names a strain, not a distributor") is a why-answering-is-hard for the secondary arm only, since the record itself concedes ampoules are sold; and the reputational mechanism weakens to "publishing a caveat is cheaper than publishing a measured failure rate about a peer".
