# UQ-20261017-53 Gate 4, judge 1 (late)

Verdict: PROVISIONAL. No attack is Unresolved. Attacks 1, 2, 4 are not Unresolved, so no Rejection.

Classification: not a disguised known. It is partly a method never applied to the unit (richness/unseen-class estimator on isoform classes) and partly a question the prior literature did not pose (is "isoforms per gene" an estimand: finite, depth-stable across admissible contamination models, with held-out validation). The nearest posed-and-unanswered item is Pickrell 2010 ("no sign of saturation", graphical only), which answers direction of depth effect, not asymptote, interval or estimandhood. That is the residual open-problem risk.

My own checks (this pass)
- Kim et al. 2004 Nat Genet 36:915 (read via fetch): estimates a per-species alternative-splicing RATE by a modified Ewing-Green gene-count ratio across an alignment threshold. No zero-class/unseen-isoform estimator, no interval, no held-out prediction. Not the same question.
- BIISQ (arXiv 1703.08260; Nat Commun 2018): hierarchical Dirichlet process isoform discovery on SHORT reads. Catalogue is unbounded by construction; no predictive count of unobserved isoforms, no held-out library prediction, no explicit false-positive term (paper concedes counts "could be confounded by erroneous splice junctions"). Nearest isoform-discovery nonparametric work, but different question; it ASSUMES unboundedness rather than testing it.
- Searches for Chao/Good-Turing/rarefaction applied to isoforms per gene returned nothing beyond Schertzer 2025 (observed D0/D1 only).
- Supplied facts: Wang&Lindsay (genes), Garcia-Ortega&Martinez 2015 (genes; admits splice variants as "genes" ambiguity), preseq (reads/junctions), Chiu&Chao 2016 (spurious singletons, no RNA), Willis (no calibration), Melamud&Moult, Pickrell, Houseley&Tollervey, Schertzer, Monzo/Frankish/Conesa.

Attack grades
1 Triviality: Resolved. Needs deep multi-library long-read data, a calibrated contamination model, and a held-out test; not a one-page answer. Pickrell's graph and Monzo's qualitative statement show the answer is not known.
2 Disguise: Partial. Evidence of non-disguise is strong in sources opened (no isoform richness estimator with FP term, no finite-vs-unbounded test, no held-out prediction). But most "NOT FOUND" is abstract-level/shallow (PMC4479379, Chiu-Chao preprint, Glinos published version, 2025 BMC Genomics GTEx survey unread; Schertzer not full-text checked). Not Unresolved: prior legs of the void adversary rest on Wang&Lindsay (genes, not isoforms) and Melamud/Pickrell (no estimator), which the facts defeat. Cannot be Resolved given unread items.
3 Combinatorial novelty: Resolved. The interface has a real problem: isoform individuation (junction-chain identity at tolerance), RT/template-switch artefacts, singleton inflation. Ecology-side repair (Chiu-Chao, Willis) exists without transcript data, transcriptome-side gene-level use exists (Garcia-Ortega); the join is nontrivial, not decorative.
4 Presupposition failure: Partial. P5 ("real" = reproducible across libraries and donors): the claim that artefacts reproduce across donors is unsourced; Houseley&Tollervey tested only same-substrate repeats, so it neither supports nor refutes. The second leg (noise isoforms individually rare so reproducibility is depth-indexed) is substantive: with enough libraries even rare noise splices recur, so "real" shifts with depth. But the candidate's part (c) explicitly allows the outcome "depth-indexed, not an estimand", so the presupposition is partly the object tested. P2 (spike-in-calibrated artefact rate transferable to endogenous reads) is contestable (SIRV spike-ins lack biological splicing noise and endogenous RT-structure effects) and unevidenced either way. P6 concedes lower-bound status, which is honest but weakens (c): a Chao-type lower bound is finite whenever f2>0, so finiteness must be argued from coverage/parametric models, not the lower bound. Plausible, unevidenced, so Partial not Unresolved.
5 Vagueness: Partial. Falsifiable parts: (b) held-out prediction error, (a) intervals. Soft part: "admissible contamination models" and "depth-stable" need pre-declared definitions and thresholds; as written a finite answer or a "not closed" answer are both reachable, but rules for each are not fixed.
6 Significance inflation: Partial. Annotation community (GENCODE-type, Monzo/Frankish/Conesa) cares how much remains to find and how to stop; Schertzer says catalogue "almost certainly underestimates". Gate score 2/3 and the "real" count may matter less than reproducible-isoform yield per library, which part (b) covers.
7 Reason for silence: Partial. Credible mechanisms: deep replicated long-read data with spike-ins is recent (ENCODE4, GTEx long-read), short-read isoform individuation is ill-posed, ecological estimators lived in microbiome/ecology, annotation field framed by novelty counts. But gene-level richness estimators were used in RNA-seq in 2015 and EST in 2005, so the 10-year gap needs the individuation argument, which is inferential, not sourced.
8 Dissolution: Partial. Known theory: unseen-class richness is not identifiable without parametric assumptions, so (c) may end as "not identifiable", which is arguably a dissolution. Counter: the held-out test (b) and the pre-declared depth-indexed outcome make that a result, not an escape; unverified empirically.

Strongest remaining threat: attack 4 (reality criterion depth-dependent; spike-in transferability), followed by attack 2 residue (unread sources) and attack 8 (non-identifiability).
