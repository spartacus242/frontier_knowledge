## Restatement (my own words, before engaging the record's framing)

Take the published EPDs for construction products and sort them into like-for-like bins (same declared unit, same strength/performance class, same reference service life, same life-cycle stage set, A1–A3 at minimum). Then ask three things.

(i) When two EPDs in the same bin report different kg CO2e, how much of the gap is the **factory** — kiln, furnace, grid, mix design — and how much is the **paperwork**: which PCR version, which allocation rule, which background database and vintage, which declared-unit conversion, which programme operator, which verifier. The method half is to be measured from the subset of products declared more than once under *different* rule sets, as a within-product contrast.

(ii) If you forced every declaration in a bin onto one named reference configuration and recomputed, what fraction of head-to-head "A beats B" calls flips sign, and what fraction of pass/fail verdicts against the GWP limit actually in force on the declaration date (e.g. a Buy Clean maximum) crosses the line.

(iii) How much of the frame you had to discard for absent method metadata, reported field by field.

In one line: **are EPDs measuring plants or measuring methods, and does the answer change who wins the contract.**

## Single strongest killing point (closest I got)

Method metadata in this corpus is not merely missing, it is **present and false**, and the question's pre-declared safeguards count only the missing kind. Anderson & Moncaster, coding hundreds of concrete and cement EPDs, found that "many EPD state economic allocation but make none… implying economic allocation has been used when in fact no allocation has been used", that for many EPDs the treatment of kiln waste CO2e (37–122 kg CO2e/t, mean ≈77, ≈7% of total) "is not at all clear", and that factor-of-1000 unit errors had to be corrected with programme operators. A machine-extracted frame therefore carries misclassification in the explanatory variable of limb (i), which attenuates the estimated method component toward zero — so the candidate's own admitted null becomes the likeliest finding for reasons of data quality rather than physics, while limb (iii) reports only dropout by field and would not detect it. This is a Partial, not a kill: the within-product re-declaration contrast keys on observed configuration changes rather than on the self-reported allocation label, so it is substantially insulated, and the bias direction is at least known. But it is the defect the question should be amended to meet before any answer is believed.
