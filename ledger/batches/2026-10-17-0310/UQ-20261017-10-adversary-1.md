## My own arithmetic (labelled as such, not from any source)

For a per-sequence-mean reduction over B sequences with gradient-bearing lengths L_s, each token in sequence s gets w_i = 1/(B L_s). Then sum_i w_i^2 = (1/B^2) sum_s 1/L_s, so Kish ESS = B^2 / sum_s (1/L_s) = B x HM(L), nominal count = B x AM(L), and the ESS ratio of limb (b)(ii) is exactly HM(L)/AM(L), a functional of the length histogram alone. For a per-micro-batch token-mean with G micro-batches of token counts T_m, the same algebra gives HM(T_m)/AM(T_m). Under packing to a fixed sequence length with padding ratio p, T_m is constant up to p, so the ratio is 1 - O(p). Under token-count normalization over the whole window (HF >= PR #34191, Axolotl, Cerebras "num tokens" mode), w_i is constant and the ratio is 1 by construction. No uncertainty attaches: this is algebra, not an estimate.
