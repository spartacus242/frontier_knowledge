## Findings
1. Hanidu A, Kovalenko M, Usman S, Monasta L, Zamagni G, Stampalija T, Lees C. "Intrapartum ultrasound for cervical dilatation: Inter- and intra-observer agreement." Acta Obstet Gynecol Scand 2024;103(12):2455-2464. DOI 10.1111/aogs.14970.
2. Numbers all exist.
 - 80.3%: 118/147 paired TPUS-DVE observations differing by <=1 cm (0 cm: 34%, 1 cm: 46.3%; 34+46.3=80.3). Denominator = paired observations (not women; 110 women analysed). Not a "tolerance" chosen post hoc in a stated sense; Discussion says "80%" within 1 cm.
 - -0.925 cm: Results 3.1, "mean difference of -0.925 cm (95% LOA = -0.777 to 2.628 cm) between DVE and TPUS". Abstract rounds to -0.9 cm. Direction of subtraction NOT stated (only "between DVE and TPUS"). LOA are internally inconsistent (lower limit -0.777 is above the mean -0.925; midpoint ~+0.93; looks like sign error). Fig 7B legend gives no axes.
 - ICC 0.85: TPUS vs DVE, 95% CI 0.75-0.90, p<0.001, 147 observations; unitless. ICC model not specified. Authors label 0.75-0.90 "good".
3. Comparison: transperineal ultrasound (2D AP cervical diameter, offline from cine-loops) vs digital vaginal examination, i.e. method comparison; DVE is the reference/clinical comparator, not an instrumented gold standard. Also inter-/intra-observer agreement among ultrasound readers on same cine-loops (inter ICC 0.939/0.969; intra 0.995/0.996). Not a simulator study. DVE done by treating doctors/midwives; TPUS by two researchers; stated blinded to each other.
4. Real nulliparous labouring women (37-42 wk, active labour, singleton cephalic), London single centre, May 2021-Nov 2022. 206 consented, 96 excluded, 110 analysed, 147 pairs, pairs within 60 min of each other (TPUS followed DVE; not strictly simultaneous). No models/phantoms/simulators examined. 
5. No. No partograph/labour curve/dystocia threshold analysed. Only background: 0.5 cm/h progress rate used to justify 60-min window (not computed from data); NICE active-labour definition used, not tested.

## Caveat
The paper is about TPUS vs DVE, not about "accuracy of digital assessment" per se; DVE is the comparator. Mean difference of -0.9 is not a measure of DVE error against truth. Read via summariser; no verbatim stitching.
