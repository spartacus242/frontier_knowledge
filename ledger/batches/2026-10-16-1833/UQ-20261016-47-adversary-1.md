## Restatement (my words)

Take one pretraining corpus and randomly carve out several equal-sized slices. Train one BPE (or Unigram) tokenizer per slice with algorithm, pre-tokenizer and vocabulary size held fixed. (a) How different do the tokenizers come out — vocabulary overlap, merge order, tokens-per-word on held-out text — and how does that spread shrink as the slice grows? (b) If you pretrain one LM per tokenizer on the same bytes at the same FLOPs, is the spread in bits-per-byte and benchmark scores caused purely by *which slice the tokenizer saw* as large as (i) ordinary seed noise and (ii) the gaps papers attribute to "BPE vs Unigram" or "33k vs 100k vocab"? In short: is a tokenizer a reproducible artifact or a sample statistic with an unreported standard error, and does that error matter downstream?
