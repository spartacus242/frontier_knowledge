MY OWN REASONING, not a finding: the simulations appear to sit on a 10 K grid (−32, −22, −12, −2 K are all named). −27 K is the midpoint of the interval (−32, −22) in which the height-distribution bimodality disappears; −17 K is the midpoint of the interval (−22, −12) in which the steps disappear and the distribution turns unimodal. The caption and the text may therefore be reading two different diagnostics of the same boundary. The "Step free energies" sentence, placing the sharp rise at ΔT ≈ −20 to −30 K "somewhat below the estimated smoothening temperature", fits an estimate warmer than −20 K (the caption's −17 K) better than −27 K. What would ground this: the paper's list of simulated temperatures and Fig. 4/Fig. S maps, which I did not fetch. On the brief's own premise that an inconsistent source's headline figure "cannot be decomposed": the discrepancy is decomposable in the hypothesis above, but as a printed source the paper does state two different prism values, and the decomposition is mine, not the authors'.

### (3) Estimand: smoothening (surface phase transition) temperature, with the habit changes as the experimental comparator

The computed quantity is the temperature of a surface phase transition on a simulated facet, named by the paper a "smoothening temperature" ("at a smoothening temperature, $\Delta T_s \approx -4$ K", Source A; "at a smoothening [CUT] temperature, Ts ≈ −4 K", Source B). The habit transitions enter as the comparator taken from the literature: caption sentence 2 (Source B): "changes sharply from platelike to columnar at ca. −4 K" ... "from columnar to platelike at −10 K" ... "somewhat less sharply from platelike to columnar at −40 K (8)." — a cited (ref. 8) description of the Nakaya diagram, not a computed result. The agreement sentence ("Interestingly, these two temperatures, together with the full completion of a second bilayer at $T_l = -12$ K are in very close agreement with the crystal habit changes observed in the Nakaya diagram.") ASSERTS agreement between computed surface-transition temperatures and observed habit changes; it does not compute a habit transition temperature. Abstract (Source A): "crystal growth rates of basal and prismatic facets exhibit an enigmatic temperature dependence." and "as temperature is increased, the crystal surface transforms from a disordered phase with proliferation of steps, to a smooth phase with small step density."

Qualification the entry should carry: the paper does not stop at smoothening temperatures. It derives step free energies and infers relative growth rates, and in Source B, section "Relation to crystal growth", it prints a predicted growth-rate crossover: "The next crossover is predicted to occur at the temperature T = −26 K" (ΔT, my reading; single summariser fragment; the sentence's subject and surrounding text were not returned, so what "next" follows is NOT LOCATED by me). So the paper PREDICTS a growth-rate crossover temperature as well as computing smoothening temperatures; it still MEASURES no habit diagram and PRINTS no habit transition temperature of its own. The entry's "estimand = surface smoothening temperatures per facet" is right for the headline quantities and the record's regime line ("inferred relative growth rates") already covers the rest; the entry's "Prior 1 predicts facet smoothening temperatures in one model" understates by omitting the predicted −26 K crossover.

A further observation for the judge, outside items (1)–(5) but about Prior 1's numbers: the paper's own caption enumerates the observed habit changes at −4, −10 and −40 K, citing (8). The entry's anchoring sentence, "the gap between Prior 1's simulated prismatic smoothening (≈ −27 K) and the observed −22 °C transition", measures the gap against the entry's −22 °C convention, which is not the comparator Prior 1 prints in its caption. I did not sweep the text for "−22" as a Nakaya value (the −22 K occurrences I saw refer to simulated behaviour), so whether the paper anywhere uses −22 °C is UNCHECKED.

## 7. Bearing (no verdict on the entry)

The entry's Prior 1 block is accurate on substance: the three numbers are printed as the entry gives them (two of three confirmed in both versions), the prism value genuinely differs between the Figure 1 caption (−17 K) and the running text (ca. −27 K) in both the arXiv and published versions, the computed quantity is a surface smoothening temperature with habit changes as a cited comparator, and no isotope term occurs in either version at summariser-read level. The defects are presentational and bibliographic: the record flagged the inconsistency without printing either value; the entry's title is the arXiv-announced one rather than the published one; there is no DOI; the paper also prints a predicted growth-rate crossover at ΔT = −26 K that the entry's "predicts facet smoothening temperatures" omits; and the paper's own caption places the third observed habit change at −40 K (8), not at the −22 °C the entry's anchoring sentence measures Prior 1 against.
