## Provisional entries

**UQ-20261017-11** — In reference genome assemblies from the standard Earth BioGenome-era pipeline, what fraction of genuine recent gene duplicates is removed by the haplotypic-duplication purging step, and by how much does that removal shift published gene-family expansion and contraction rates?

Three adversaries each ran all eight attacks and 27 Disguise queries between them; the third sourced a fatal presupposition failure that the first two had reached and graded only Partial for want of a source. Four checkers went at it. **The kill does not stand.** Its first leg held that purge_dups tests only contigs below the haploid/diploid coverage transition, so a fixed young duplicate at diploid depth cannot be purged as a haplotig at all. Coverage gating is real and quoted — "Contigs with average coverage below mid coverage are tested for haplotypic duplications" — but "cannot be removed at all" appears in neither the paper nor the README; the code as relayed carries an alignment-only HAPLOTIG branch (`flt_by_bm_mm`) with no coverage test; and the tool's own authors concede the failure mode the candidate asks about, that dropping `get_seqs -e` "may delete false positive duplications" and that of the middle-sequence removals "Some of them are real, while others may not." Its second leg — that relocating a hemizygous copy to the alternate assembly is the haploid-reference convention rather than an error — is **UNVERIFIED**: Rhie et al. 2021 failed by DOI and at nature.com, the EBP standards paper was never opened, and the NCBI glossary defines neither "primary assembly" nor "pseudohaplotype". Per the ledger's own rule an unrun check never rejects, so this names a promotion condition and could yet restore the objection.

Two of the entry's own claims were also wrong, both favouring the candidate, and are corrected: hifiasm's asmgene does **not** publish limb (a) in weakened form (the figures are confirmed as printed — CHM13 76.9, HG00733 67.3, HG002 74.8 percent retained — but the metric pools assembler collapse, purge removal and real individual-versus-reference CNV and Table 2 has no same-sample purged-versus-unpurged pair), and the "purge_dups dropped for badger and black rhino as significantly worse" passage is about **HiCanu**, in Supplementary 1.2, with no figures. The Gate 2 neighbour is also weaker than the adversary claimed: Guiglielmoni et al. 2021 runs on one ~102 Mb rotifer genome, measures overpurging only as Illumina k-mer completeness, has no ground truth for genuine duplicates and no gene-family limb — so it does not supply the estimand, though it does show the Gate 2 record incomplete. What now holds the entry at Provisional is attack 7, Reason for silence, Unresolved from two independent adversaries: two of the three silence mechanisms are contradicted by opened sources and purge_dups' issue tracker shows the weaker limb asked repeatedly with numbers attached (#42 is literally "Optimise purging without losing too many genes", BUSCO C 97.0→95.3%). **Its draft routing row is withdrawn** under rule (c).

**UQ-20261017-15** — Across the assay families used to map protein interactions, what region of the affinity-by-abundance space can no current method detect, and what share of plausible interactions falls in it?

And it maps no systematically invisible region: the closest statements are that "many PPIs remained undetected as Y2H, like all characterized assays" and that weak or transient interactions are hard to detect — qualitative remarks, not a boundary. So the unread neighbour supports novelty. **UQ-20261017-14** (do molecules carrying a somatic mutation differ in their own local methylation from mutation-free molecules at the same locus?) — the batch's whole story in one entry: rejected, rescued, and rejected again on better ground.

The batch killed it at Gate 2 as answered by MethylSaferSeqS (PNAS 2023). Verification withdrew that: the paper's mutant-versus-wild-type methylation comparison is **two patients**, presented as a demonstration that the method works, at loci the authors chose because the mutation sits "in a CpG dyad that affected the methylation status of the strands" — the mutation creates or destroys the very CpG being measured, which is why the text reads "As expected" — and the paper describes no flanking window at all. The second prior the entry leaned on was struck outright: Kusmartsev et al. 2020 runs the opposite direction of inference, a population-level association in which methylation predicts flanking SNP incidence ("nucleotides in the close vicinity (+/-3 bp) of methylated cytosines mutate less frequently").

Its own strongest objection then turned out to carry a second quantitative leg nobody had checked, so it was checked before any promotion. The numbers hold and the inference does not. Confirmed verbatim: "average conversion efficiency of 77% (IQR: 75% to 79%)", and the rejected 16-hour condition that reached over 98% conversion at the cost of "loss of half of the original DNA template molecules". But the unit of that figure is the **library**, the paper reports no per-molecule conversion rate and no per-read methylation-call error rate, and the per-molecule error correlation that would defeat a window-level estimand is the adversary's own derivation. The paper's one in-text measurement of the artefact — 3.1% apparent methylation at a cytosine the mutation had already taken out of CpG context — is about **sevenfold below** the ~23% the objection derives from the library average. HiDEF-seq, named as the conversion-free escape, was then read and **does not call 5mC at all**.

Because the original kill was a Gate 2 kill, `LEDGER.md`'s rule bound: a candidate rejected at Gate 2 "has no Gate 4 at all", so a withdrawn Gate 2 ground means all eight attacks must run from scratch. Two cold adversaries did, and both found the same thing in a vocabulary eleven Gate 2 queries and nine first-pass adversary queries had never searched. **epialleleR** (GigaScience 12:giad087, 2023; Bioconductor) ships `generateVcfReport`, which "tests for the association between SNV and epiallelic status using Fisher Exact test". A third fresh-context checker opened the vignette independently and confirmed the strings and, decisively, the output's own shape: per-strand Fisher p-values (`FEp+`, `FEp-`) over per-strand reference and alternate read counts prefixed methylated and unmethylated. That is the candidate's unit, endpoint, comparator and estimand in one function, with Fisher exact on a single locus's 2×2 being the exact conditional test that matched conditional logistic generalises. The paper names "single-nucleotide variations or mutations within the immediate proximity" as cis-factors for epimutations, and the preprint states the capability — "allows to connect DNA methylation status with potential underlying cis-factors" — and runs no such analysis.

The two adversaries graded that differently, which is worth recording rather than smoothing over. Adversary 1 called attack 2 **Unresolved**, on the ground that what the candidate adds is design hygiene on an asked question. Adversary 2 called it **Partial**, because no sweep found the contrast *executed* and its best cross-field hit (Beaulaurier et al., PMC4490391) defeated its fetch cap. Both are honest readings of the same facts, and the rule settles it twice: Unresolved on attack 2 from any adversary is fatal, and Gate 0 defines a disguised known as a covariate swap on an asked question, "Rejected **even if the exact variant is unmeasured**". Attack 7 is Unresolved from **both** cold adversaries independently and would cap the entry even without attack 2 — the record's silence mechanism was that the quantity "is not in anyone's results tables", and it has been a documented function call since 2023. Adversary 2's phrasing is the right one: the silence is **unexecuted intent, not an unposed question**.

Both adversaries also declined, independently of each other and of the judge, to carry the conversion objection the prompt had put in front of them. Adversary 1: its per-molecule correlation step "is asserted, not sourced; I found no primary source for it within budget and I will not score a kill on it."

One genuine scope limit survives on the kill, confirmed verbatim twice: the vignette's own code comment reads "# NA values are shown for the C->T variants on the \"+\" and G->A on the \"-\"" "# strands, because bisulfite conversion makes their counting impossible", so under bisulfite the tool is blind to C>T at CpG — the deamination class with the strongest mechanistic prior, and the class the candidate's motivating gap is built on.
