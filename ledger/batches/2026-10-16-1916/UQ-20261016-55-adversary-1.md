# UQ-20261016-55 — Gate 4 adversary #1 log

## Restatement (my own words)

Take a fixed time window and four kinds of public record that purport to show someone demanding that content be removed from LLM training data: DMCA notices on Lumen aimed at dataset/model hosts, dataset withdrawals (court-ordered or voluntary), documented consent withdrawals from research corpora, and DOI-resolvable retractions. Count them, stratify by legal basis, record how many artifacts each names and how often they arrive, and for the subset that can be located in an open corpus (Dolma, peS2o) measure document count, token share and how many retained documents share text with the target. Put a bound on the demands that never appear in public. Then ask whether the forget sets of TOFU, MUSE, WMDP and DUSK look like draws from that distribution or lie outside it.
