## Findings

The title in the task ("...course of ablation") differs from the page heading; the URL slug is truncated at "course", so the task title looks paraphrased. Authors: HF Pitschner, P Reinesch, H Bahavar, U Jung, H Hassdenteufel, M Kunis, A Koenig. Citation line: "European Cardiovascular Disease 2007;3(1):83-8", DOI 10.15420/ecr.2007.0.1.83. The site/journal is now European Cardiology Review (ECR); in 2007 titled European Cardiovascular Disease. Year, volume, issue, pages match.
Q2 Unit: catheter interventions in patients. "we assessed 202 catheter interventions in patients"; "241 catheters were used" in the reprocessed group; new group "231". So 202 is the number of interventions and 241/231 the catheters, which matches the premise (note 231 + 241 = 472 catheters over 202 interventions). Recurrence denominators of 100 (new) and 102 (reprocessed) sum to 202 and imply patient-level groups, though group patient counts were not stated directly. Non-randomised; samples drawn by a non-medical technician on consecutive days from 356 reprocessed / 298 new catheters available. Registry years 2000 and 2003.
Q3 Recorded: YES. Quote: "The number of reprocessing turns (one to six times) was read from the barcode of the catheter" (sentence truncated by summariser; remainder says it was read after the procedure and unknown to technician/physician beforehand - the latter from summary, not verbatim).
Q4 Exposure use: NOT FOUND in the text read. No outcome by cycle, no regression, no dose-response. Analysis is new vs reprocessed two arms (t-tests, Fisher exact, 5% level, whole sample only; subgroups too small and not tested). The only cycle-related data are reprocessor scrap rates by cycle (Figure 12: "the percentage scrap quota continually drops from the first to the fifth cycle"), not a clinical result. Caveat: Tables/figures not seen, so a per-cycle table cannot be excluded entirely, but the text says "up to six times (see Figure 1)" only as a description.
Q5 Endpoints: procedure time, fluoroscopy time, number of RF deliveries; severe procedure-related complications (pericardial effusion/tamponade, third-degree AV block, peri-procedural thromboembolism, death); arrhythmia recurrence. Follow-up six months ("Procedure-related complications were picked up over a time period of six months"). Complications: 1 AV block III with new catheter; 1 pericardial effusion without puncture with reprocessed catheter; no deaths/thromboembolism/septic events. So patient-outcome endpoints exist, 6 months, with 2 events total.

So 6.9% does not match the stated numerators; probable typo or arithmetic slip, or Table 2 differs (not seen). Reported as nonsignificant.
