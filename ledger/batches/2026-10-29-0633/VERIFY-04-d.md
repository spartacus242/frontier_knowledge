## 6. The discriminator leg, in one line

UNADDRESSED: neither paper prints a measured-versus-harmonic ratio at any temperature — Chen computes no amplitude at all (its DWFs are XRD/QCBED inputs subtracted in quadrature to find the instrument's residual blur), and Zhang compares measured amplitudes to anharmonic classical MD and to a literature first-principles value at 300 K alone with no ratio printed — so the two papers neither support nor contradict the claim that bulk anharmonicity and force-constant error in the null would make R temperature-dependent without a defect, and the leg stands exactly where the entry already puts it: on the adversaries' own coth-identity algebra with unmeasured magnitudes.
