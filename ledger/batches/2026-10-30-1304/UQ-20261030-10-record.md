## Reason for silence

The honest mechanism is **not** that nobody asked — Prior 1 asked, which is why this is an open problem rather than an un-asked question. The silence is in the *doing*, and it has three named causes. (i) **New instruments, barely arrived:** per-crypt colibactin attribution in normal colon is a 2023 capability (Prior 2) and the signatures were catalogued as SBS88/ID18 only recently; the age-and-geography analysis that motivates the design is 2025. (ii) **A disciplinary boundary with no shared unit of analysis:** the birth-cohort framing lives in cancer epidemiology, whose row is a person-year of incidence in a registry, while per-crypt sequencing lives in cancer genomics, whose row is a crypt — Q8 and Q1 return disjoint literatures, and nothing in Q6 or Q11 names the bridge as a method. (iii) **Cost and consent structure:** the design needs normal mucosa from people *without* cancer across three birth decades under one protocol, which is a prospective recruitment problem, whereas every existing normal-crypt colibactin dataset (Prior 2) was assembled opportunistically from cancer patients' adjacent tissue — the cheap frame is exactly the one the question forbids.
