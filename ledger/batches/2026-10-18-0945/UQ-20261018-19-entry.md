Question (canonical): In a genome-scale single-gene CRISPRi perturbation atlas with single-cell transcriptomic readout, what fraction of the robustly expressed transcriptome is not moved by ANY single-gene perturbation at a declared minimum effect size and declared per-gene power, and does that non-responsive set recur across cell lines? Full V2 text in the record.)

Alternative formulations: How much of a cell's transcriptome is reachable by perturbing one gene at a time, and is there a part of it that no single-gene knockdown touches? / Reading a genome-scale Perturb-seq response matrix along the GENE axis rather than the perturbation axis, how many measured genes show no detected response to any perturbation after each gene's own detection power is accounted for, and do the same genes recur in a second line?
