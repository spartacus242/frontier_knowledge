## 1. Restatement (my own words)

Take a published human single-cell atlas whose raw reads and pipeline code are public. Spike real, foreign cell populations into its raw data at known frequencies and at known transcriptional distances from the resident populations, re-run the atlas's own pipeline at its own resolution, and record how often the spiked population comes out as its own reported cluster. Plot that recovery rate as a surface over (frequency, distinctness), draw the 50% contour, compare contours across atlases, and integrate the shortfall against a prior to estimate how many detectable populations each atlas failed to report.

Stripped of the astronomy vocabulary: "What is the rare-subpopulation detection power of atlas X's clustering pipeline as a joint function of subpopulation frequency and separability, and how many subpopulations did it therefore miss?"
