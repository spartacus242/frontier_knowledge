Question (canonical): In a plasmid repository holding, per plasmid, (a) a depositor-supplied map, (b) its own full-construct sequencing of the distributed material and (c) a citing-publication list: over the sequenced catalogue, what is the joint distribution of (discrepancy class: ORF-altering / regulatory / backbone / selection marker / silent) x (number of distinct citing publications whose methods describe the construct by map rather than by catalogue number), with CIs; and in a pre-specified subsample, does orthogonal re-sequencing plus a function assay of discrepant and as-mapped constructs confirm the predicted consequence and establish whether the discrepancy predates or postdates the depositor's publication?
Gate 1 version note: V1, adopted at Gate 1; V0 superseded (see the line below).
(V0, superseded at Gate 1: among research plasmids whose repository sequencing differs from the depositor's published map, what is the joint distribution of discrepancy class by functional consequence and citing-publication count — how many published results were built on a construct other than the one their methods describe? V1 names the three data objects, demotes the bare discrepancy rate because it is published, restricts the exposure denominator per P8, and makes direction-of-error an experimental arm.)

Alternative formulations:
- (lay) When the plasmid in the freezer isn't quite the plasmid in the paper, how often does the difference matter, and whose results rest on it?
- (cross-field) What is the plasmid-catalogue equivalent of the "ghosts of HeLa" count — how much of the literature rests on deposited material that differs from its own description, and in what way?

Motivating gap: Intake held that repository full-construct sequencing newly makes map-versus-reality measurable at catalogue scale while the same repositories count per-plasmid citations, making downstream exposure countable. Gate 2 held that the first half was falsified by Bai et al. 2024 and McGuffie & Barrick 2024. Verification (2026-10-16; independent fresh-context checker, 21 queries, 2 refused, log in batches/2026-10-16-1033/UQ-20261016-29-verification.md) overturns that for Bai and leaves it standing only for the classification limb: NEITHER paper is map-versus-material at catalogue scale, so the candidate's own premise is not established by the cited work either (see the P3 note below). What stands is that the catalogue-scale discrepancy classification has been run over 51,384 fully sequenced Addgene plasmids — McGuffie & Barrick 2024 — and that the same paper explicitly names publication tracing as deferred labour, which is the ground this rejection now rests on.

Presuppositions: P1 repository sequences accurate enough to be ground truth — CONTESTED at repeats/low-complexity (Bai et al. treat independent sequencing as arbiter but do not quantify their own error rate); P2 deposited material is the material used in the published work — CONTESTED and part of the question (Bai et al. propose the common 11-bp AAV 5' ITR deletion arose independently in several labs and spread, with some labs possibly adopting the mutant as reference, so the direction can run either way or be circular); P3 depositor maps retrievable in machine-comparable form at scale — PARTLY SUPPORTED, UNVERIFIED IN DETAIL (multiple per-plasmid sequence records inferred from result titles; no sequences page opened); DOWNGRADED TO CONTESTED on verification — neither cited paper performs a map-versus-material comparison at catalogue scale (Bai et al.'s true map-versus-material arm is the 49-plasmid Addgene AAV subset; McGuffie & Barrick compare sequences against canonical part databases and sequenced no material themselves), so nothing opened establishes that a repository holds depositor maps joinable to its own full-construct sequencing across a catalogue; P4 per-plasmid citing lists reasonably complete — CONTESTED (self-reported plus text-mined); P5 functional consequence coarsely stratifiable from sequence class — DEFENSIBLE, but a variant of a published scheme (Bai et al. Table 1 tiers: destroy / compromise / context dependent); P6 discrepancies not confined to unused plasmids — ESTABLISHED against the candidate's favour (5' ITR mutated in 34/49, 69%, of >100-request Addgene AAV plasmids); P7 catalogue composition informative about wider practice, or finding scoped to deposited material — CONTESTED (Bai et al.'s vendor-intake arm is the wider-practice evidence); P8 NEW, load-bearing: published methods describe the construct in a way that *can* disagree with the repository sequence — where a paper cites only "Addgene #X" the described construct IS whatever the repository distributes, so no mismatch exists to count and the exposure denominator collapses toward map-stating papers; unquantified anywhere opened, the weakest link; P9 NEW: maps are depositor-supplied, usually not published in the paper, correcting V0.

What would count as an answer: (i) the joint table over the sequenced catalogue — per discrepancy class, plasmid counts and distinct citing publications inside the P8 denominator, with CIs, plus a class-by-consequence cross-tab; (ii) pre-committed bands so a negative is real (NEGLIGIBLE / MARKER-ONLY / SUBSTANTIAL ORF EXPOSURE); (iii) a validated subsample assaying discrepant and as-mapped constructs; (iv) a direction-of-error arm comparing the archived original deposit against an independently obtained depositor-lab aliquot, orthogonally re-sequenced, so pre- versus post-publication origin is assigned rather than assumed. Without (iii) and (iv) the result reduces to the rate-plus-annotation exercise already published.
