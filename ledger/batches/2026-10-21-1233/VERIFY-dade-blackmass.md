## (b) Samples: how many, whence, how prepared

Two short sentences carry the whole sample description, and both returned **whole, un-clipped** (single fragments, no reconstruction needed):

> "For this study, three different BMs from different suppliers and recyclers were used."

> "The studied BMs were produced by treatments such as pyrolysis, dry shredding and sieving."

That is the entirety of what the Methods state about count, provenance and upstream processing. Notes on each element of the claim:

- **How many: three.** Verbatim "three different BMs".
- **Whence: "different suppliers and recyclers"** — no names, no countries, no plant identities.
- **Preparation (upstream): pyrolysis, dry shredding, sieving** — and the paper hedges the list with **"treatments such as"**, i.e. it does not assert that these were the only treatments, nor which BM got which. The claim's "shredding" is **"dry shredding"** in the source; dropping "dry" loses a specification the authors made.
- **Size fraction: not stated in Methods for these samples.** The <500 µm figure is a general characterisation of Black Mass given in the Introduction, not a sieve cut applied to these three samples:
  > "These BMs consist of fine particles (<500 µm) that are rich in valuable elements such as Ni, Co, Mn and Li"
  and
  > "In this paper, a refinement of fine (<500 µm) BM particle chemistries and textures was carried out"

  A related but different figure in §2.3: *reconstruction from 2 consecutive fragments* — "The aim of the SEM/QEMSCAN® analyses was to investigate, in detail, particles and phase relationships / within individual particles that rarely exceed sizes of 100 µm."

- **Preparation (in the lab)** is a separate protocol, in §2.2 with Figure 1. Verbatim single fragments: "As shown in Figure 1, each BM is embedded in an epoxy resin (a)." — *reconstruction from 2 fragments*: "When the resin has set, the polished section is cut vertically in half (b and c) and embedded one more time / into epoxy resin (d and e)." — *reconstruction from 2 fragments*: "Several polishing steps were performed by means of a Struers RotoForce-3/RotoPol-31 device using silicon / carbide paper < 5 µm, then with a diamond polishing suspension on a cloth <1 µm size."

**Grammar-on-both-sides check.** If "(<500 µm)" is quoted as the sample's sieved fraction, the cut is on the wrong side of the clause: in both sentences it is an attributive description of BM in general and of the particle population studied, not a reported sieve stage in the sample-prep chain. ## (c) What the paper measures

Unit of analysis: **the individual particle and the phase domains inside it**, per polished section of each of three Black Masses.

- Instrument and scale, verbatim (single fragment): "This specific QEMSCAN® database contains 260 subgroups, which comprise major and minor chemical variations of phases." / "The database was created using millions of point analyses."
- Per-sample measurement effort, BM1, *reconstruction from 2 fragments*: "The characterisation was performed on 10.5 million X-ray analyses using 5 µm steps on a total of / 753,000 particles." The same construction is used for BM2 (74.2 million analyses, 1.6 million particles) and BM3 (11.8 million analyses, 1.4 million particles). Abstract-level total, *reconstruction from 2 fragments*: "To attain a high statistical representativity QEMSCAN® analyses were performed on a total of 3 million / particles".
- Units: area-based measurement converted to mass. Verbatim: "The QEMSCAN® element compositions are recalculated in mass percent, which differ from the area percent". Bulk chemistry is reported separately by wet chemistry: "Chemical compositions (wt.%) of the three BMs quantified by ICP-OES and C/S analyser." (Table 1 caption; Ni, Co, Mn, Li, Al, Cu, C).
- Endpoints actually reported: phase/cathode-type identification and classification, particle size and shape, **liberation** and free-surface distributions, and element distributions among phases — e.g. "The liberation of Cu with 72 wt.% liberated at more than 80% is good"; "fine grained particles (<20 µm) contain very small amounts of Cu (≈3 mass%) and reduced amounts of Al"; "On the contrary, 97 mass% of NMC from the initial BM3 are contained in the fraction <20 µm".
- Comparator: there is **no external comparator population**. The only comparison is internal validation against the same samples' wet chemistry: "QEMSCAN® analyses match very well with ICP-OES generated analyses (r² > 0.99)" — a correlation of two assays of the same material, not an uncertainty estimate.
- Li is **not measured** by the primary instrument: §2.5.2 has it "quantified by inference" from stoichiometry.

**So: per-sample phase and cathode-type abundances, yes — expressed in mass percent recalculated from area, for three polished sections. Chemistry shares by mass of ARRIVING batteries, no.** The measurand is the composition of the Black Mass *as received*, after an unquantified upstream pyrolysis/shredding/sieving chain, for three samples of convenience.

**Plainly absent** (checked by direct search of the text for each):
- **Sampling frame** — none. "three different BMs from different suppliers and recyclers" is the whole frame; no population of plants, batches or tonnages is defined, and nothing says how the three were chosen from any larger set.
- **Region / geography** — nothing on this. Suppliers, recyclers and countries are unnamed.
- **Time window** — nothing on this. No sampling dates, campaign period or production years of the feed batteries.
- **Field sampling protocol at the recycler** — nothing on this. The only protocol described is the in-lab anti-segregation embedding/polishing protocol (§2.2), which governs how a sample is sectioned, not how it was drawn from a stream.
- **Variance budget** — none. No error bars, confidence intervals, replicate sections, or between-batch variance on any abundance. The nearest statement is the un-numeric "This yields a high statistical analytical precision", resting on point counts, i.e. counting precision on one section, not sampling uncertainty about any population.
- **Battery types / formats of the feed** — not stated; inferred from observed phases only (e.g. "This association, corresponding to the initial cathodes of cell phones, shows the weak liberation").

## (f) Regime of the result, and convertibility to arriving-battery mass shares

**Regime established.** Population: **three Black Mass samples of convenience**, from unnamed, different suppliers and recyclers, each already through an upstream chain the paper describes only as "treatments such as pyrolysis, dry shredding and sieving". Unit of analysis: **individual particles and intra-particle phase domains**, on **polished 2-D sections**, with results recalculated to mass percent. Instrument/condition: **SEM + QEMSCAN®, 5 µm step X-ray mapping**, against a **prototype 260-subgroup database**; the particle population of interest is fine, "rarely exceed[ing] sizes of 100 µm", within BM generally described as <500 µm. Categories: phases, cathode active-material types (LCO, NMC variants, NCA, LFP), deleterious elements, liberation classes. Thresholds: those the authors state themselves — e.g. liberation "at more than 80%" for the 72 wt.% Cu figure, and the "<20 µm" fraction for the 97 mass% NMC and ≈3 mass% Cu figures. Any figure quoted from this paper must carry the author's own threshold with it; the stated uncertainty is only the r² > 0.99 assay agreement, as there is no interval on any abundance.

**Could its measurand be converted to arriving-battery mass shares without further per-chemistry or per-format yield factors? On what the paper itself supplies, no.** The paper supplies **nothing on this**: no mass balance, no recovery yield, no record of the mass or type of batteries entering, and no statement connecting Black Mass composition back to input composition. The only mass-reduction statement in the text runs the other direction, about prospective beneficiation: "This potentially reduces the mass of material to be processed," "and thereby the amount of chemicals and energy." Because the samples sit **downstream of pyrolysis, dry shredding and sieving** — steps that partition Al, Cu, casing, plastics, binder and electrolyte differently by cell chemistry and format — a share measured in Black Mass is a share of *that stream*, and the per-chemistry and per-format yield factors needed to invert it are exactly what the paper does not provide. A result established on three polished sections of post-treatment Black Mass is **silent** about shares of arriving batteries, not supportive of them.

## Defects

1. **"Sieving" with no size fraction attached to the samples.** The Methods state no sieve cut for these three BMs; "<500 µm" is an Introduction-level description of Black Mass in general and of the particle population studied. Presenting it as the samples' prepared size fraction is a cut on the wrong side of the clause.
2. **"Such as" dropped.** The source says "treatments **such as** pyrolysis, dry shredding and sieving" — a hedged, non-exhaustive list, not a specified process chain; and "dry shredding", not "shredding".
3. **Representativity equivocation.** The paper's representativity statements are (i) a cited general methodological point attributed to Vanderbruggen et al. [9] and (ii) its own hedged claims about 2-D sectioning against density segregation and about counting statistics over 3 million particles. Neither is a claim that three samples represent a feedstock population; treating the cited sentence as the paper vouching for sampling representativity misattributes a claim it makes about someone else's methodological concern.
4. **Precision read as sampling uncertainty.** "This yields a high statistical analytical precision" and "r² > 0.99" are within-sample counting precision and assay agreement on the same material. There is no variance budget, no replicate, no interval on any abundance — so no basis for an uncertainty on a population-level share.
5. **No sampling frame at all** — no region, no time window, no field protocol, no tonnage, no battery types or production years of the feed. These are absences in the source, not omissions in the claim's phrasing, and must be reported as absences rather than inferred from "different suppliers and recyclers".
6. **Regime mismatch if used for arriving-battery shares.** The measurand is per-particle, per-section, mass-recalculated-from-area composition of post-treatment Black Mass. Converting it to shares of arriving batteries needs per-chemistry and per-format yield factors the paper does not supply, and the paper explicitly declines process modelling.
7. **Li is inferred, not measured** (quantified from stoichiometry, §2.5.2); any Li share taken from the QEMSCAN results carries that assumption. Graphite and plastics likewise come from external C/S analysis, which the Conclusions flag as a gap to be closed.
8. **Front-matter dates not served.** The received/revised/accepted dates were not on the page as fetched; they are unverified rather than absent, and should not be quoted from a secondary short form.
9. **Silence noted where it is silence.** No LCA or modelled-feedstock comparison appears anywhere; the stoichiometric comparisons and the §4 simulations are different things and should not be reported as filling that gap.
