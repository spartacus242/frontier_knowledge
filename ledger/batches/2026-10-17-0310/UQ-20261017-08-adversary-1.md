## Restatement (my own words, before attacking)

Two questions bolted together. (a) Of the big open-weight pretrained models, how many actually publish the Adam/AdamW moment tensors (m, v) next to the weights, at which steps, in what file layout, under what licence? (b) Give one attacker the checkpoint's weights plus its moment tensors and an otherwise identical attacker the weights only, same compute, same shadow models, same queries. How much better does the first one do at (i) telling which documents were in the last stretch of training, (ii) telling which sources were present, (iii) pulling out planted canary strings — and how fast does any advantage fade as a function of how many steps before the checkpoint the data was seen? The claimed shape of the answer: whatever leaks should sit in m (horizon ~10 steps) and be nearly absent in v (horizon ~1000 steps).
