Domain(s): Biology — experimental methods and research-reagent practice (immunoassays in cell and molecular biology). Adjacent: measurement metrology, reporting standards, meta-research.
Question (canonical): For a pre-declared panel of commercial research antibodies, each pinned to a catalogue number and a dated datasheet of record: (i) is the vendor-recommended working dilution for a named application predicted by the product's stated stock concentration, or by a measurable binding constant plus the nominal target abundance in the recommended sample type, under a comparison declared in advance; and (ii) across a pre-declared sample of published immunoassays using that panel, in what fraction is the mass-per-volume or molar antibody concentration in the incubation computable at all from the paper plus that datasheet, and among papers quoting the same nominal dilution for the same catalogue number, what is the spread in computed concentration? [R1, adopted at Gate 1; V0 superseded there for well-posedness — both versions in the record.]
Alternative formulations: (a) Where does "1:1000" come from — the antibody's biophysics, or the bottle's packaging? (b) Biology's most-used reagent class is specified by a dilution rather than a concentration: how often is the dose recoverable from the published record, and is the recommended dilution a property of the antibody at all?
Motivating gap: Methods sections specify antibodies by dilution, and a dilution is a dose only if stock concentration is known — which many products do not state. The titration how-to literature tells users to optimise empirically and is silent on where the vendor's figure came from. Claim driving the candidate: two papers both reporting "1:1000" may differ in dose by orders of magnitude.
Presuppositions: P1 datasheets state a recommended dilution and state/omit stock concentration recordably — not measured here; no datasheet was opened by the record or by any of three adversaries. P2 binding parameters obtainable for enough of the panel — contested, no survey of affinity-data availability found. P3 a polyclonal has a single meaningful affinity — contested, handled by stratification. P4 the selling vendor sets the figure — contested, OEM relabelling; Voskuil 2014 (read by adversary 3) notes the same clone sold "in known mg/ml concentration" and "without any specifications". P5 dose materially affects results — partly established; NordiQC CD8 run 54 (read) shows optimal IHC across a typical 1:25–1:200 range when retrieval/detection are adjusted, which bounds it in that regime. ## Verification of this entry's sources (post-batch, 2026-10-22)

Three fresh-context checkers, each handed one claim and its source and none of them this entry's reasoning. Notes: VERIFY-datasheet-concentration-form.md, VERIFY-weller-2016-antibodies.md, VERIFY-blind-spots-westerns.md.

VERDICT REVERSED, Rejected to Provisional, because the fatal ground's premise was tested for the first time and failed. Leg A is an algebraic identity CONDITIONAL on a dated datasheet yielding one scalar stock concentration per catalogue number. Nobody in the batch had opened a datasheet. A checker opened three, across three formats, and 0 of 3 gave an unconditional scalar: Cell Signaling Technology prints an exact per-LOT value on a separately versioned certificate of analysis that carries no dilution ("Concentration: 36 ug/ml", product 5125 lot 8, "version 2025-04-11"); Sigma-Aldrich gives "~2 mg/mL" with a pointer to a lot or batch certificate; Synaptic Systems 266 011 gives a MASS (100 ug) reconstituted at a volume the laboratory chooses, so its "1 mg/ml" is the vendor's own arithmetic conditional on the user adding 100 ul. Three of the five forms the entry itself anticipated - range, lot-dependent, lyophilised mass - turned up in three pulls. n = 3, convenience-selected, so this is first evidence against the premise and not a frequency claim; the honest reading is that the rejection rested on an unmeasured premise with adverse first evidence, not on an established identity.

LEG B FAILS ON TWO OF ITS THREE CLAIMS. "CST adjusts the vial concentration instead" is nowhere on the CST post: the post holds the ASSAY COUNT constant, says "no matter the concentration", and attributes concentration variance to antibody activity, sensitivity, purity and vendor. The inverse-tuning mechanism was the entry's inference presented as the vendor's statement. And "CST prints no stock concentration" is true of the post and FALSE of the company, since CST's own lot certificate prints one - which contradicts attack 6's stronger wording that the convention "structurally cannot supply a concentration". What survives of Leg B is only that the post is undated, which was correctly recorded.

THE LEG-A REBUTTAL IS ITSELF REFUTED. Adversary 3 dismissed "the spread was meant to capture lot-to-lot variation" on the ground that it "needs lot data the frame excludes". Lot certificates are published and openable, and the checker opened one. So the within-product spread is a function of lot-CoA dispersion, which is measurable, and limb (ii) is not degenerate: two papers quoting the same nominal dilution of the same catalogue number compute different doses whenever they used different lots. That is why well-posedness is restored to 2/3 rather than left at 1/3.

A THIRD CATEGORY NOBODY IN THE BATCH NAMED, and it is now the entry's strongest objection: some vendors recommend a working CONCENTRATION range and no dilution at all (Sigma A2228, 0.5-1 ug/mL for Western blot), so limb (i)'s response variable is undefined for part of any panel - independent of the range problem adversary 2 filed, and a real specification gap rather than a kill.
