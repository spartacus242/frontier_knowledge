## 1. Restatement in my own words

Take a list of released open-weight LLMs, fixed in advance, and a fixed prompt set. Let the model generate freely, feeding its own output back in (not along a forced human-text path). Two vocabulary subsets are fixed in advance by detectors: U, items the model was effectively never trained on, and R, items the tokenizer can never emit as the encoding of any text. Then ask three numbers, U and R reported separately and per detector:

(i) before any truncation, how much of the next-token probability sits on U, and on R — mean across steps and in the upper tail;
(ii) after applying that particular release's own declared default truncation rule (read out of its `generation_config.json` or model card; a declared substitute rule where the release ships none), at what share of generation steps does at least one member of U (resp. R) still sit inside the sampling pool;
(iii) how many members of U and of R actually come out, per million generated tokens, against a control set of vocabulary items matched on unigram frequency profile.

In one line: the detection literature feeds these tokens IN; this asks how often the model puts them OUT, and whether the shipped sampler already stops that.

**C3. Each release declares a default decoding rule, including a truncation rule, machine-readably per artifact.** → **TRUE on the one instance I opened (S3)** — `do_sample: true`, `temperature: 1.0`, `top_p: 0.8`, `top_n_sigma: 0.05`, `top_k: -1`. TWO things follow, and they point opposite ways. (a) In favour of the question: the declared rule here is richer and stranger than the record's own arm list — `top_n_sigma` is a spread-dependent cut and `top_k` is explicitly disabled — so "the release's own declared default truncation rule" is a non-trivial per-artifact object, which is exactly the question's framing. (b) Against a tidy execution: honouring that arm requires an engine that implements each declared rule, which is an implementation burden the answer-shape does not budget for. Neither is a false premise. **MEASURAND EXEMPTION DECLARED, not merely considered:** the canonical wording already converts this clause into a reported quantity — "an explicitly declared substitute arm recorded as such for releases that ship none" — so the share of releases shipping no declared rule is a result of the study, and attack 4 does not reach C3.

**C4. The full pre-truncation step-wise distribution is observable for these artifacts.** → **UNVERIFIED by any document I opened**, uncontested in substance (open weights expose logits). Kills nothing.

**C5. Members of U and R are identifiable in a generated token stream.** → **UNVERIFIED by any document I opened**, uncontested in substance (token IDs are the emitted objects). Kills nothing.

**C6. A null "vocabulary subset matched on unigram frequency profile" is constructible for U and for R.** → **CONTESTED BY MY OWN REASONING, NOT BY ANY SOURCE I OPENED → graded UNVERIFIED for kill purposes.** The argument, labelled as mine: if R is defined as items never produced by tokenizing text (the record's quoted definition, which I did not open myself), then every member of R has unigram frequency identically zero in tokenized text, so a frequency-matched control must be drawn from the zero-frequency stratum — and that stratum is where under-trained candidates are mined from in the first place. Control and treatment then come from the same stratum, and the null is weakly identifying rather than absent: it can still be built from zero-frequency items that a detector certifies as well-trained, but the question does not say so, and limb (iii)'s "against a null" is doing less work than it appears to. This is the only clause where I can construct a real dent, and I cannot source it, so it is Partial-grade and not a kill. It would also be cheap for the generator to close by stating the matching stratum explicitly.

**C7. The declared-default arm is executable in a standard stack.** → **TRUE-with-friction (sourced, S3):** the declared rule includes a parameter outside the temperature/top-p/top-k/min-p family the record's answer-shape names. Friction, not falsity.

**Attack 4 verdict: Partial.** No clause the question cannot survive without came back FALSE, or CONTESTED on a source I opened. C6 is the clause a kill would have to land on. WHAT KIND OF DOCUMENT WOULD SETTLE C6, written before nominating anything: it must cover the vocabulary of at least one shipped tokenizer at item level, must VARY corpus frequency independently of under-training (so that zero-frequency-but-well-trained items can be shown to exist in usable numbers), and must PRINT a joint distribution of corpus unigram frequency against a trained/under-trained indicator. I can name no source I have seen that is of that kind — arXiv:2405.05417 prints counts of verified tokens, not a frequency-crossed table, as far as the record's read reports — so I do not nominate one. That is a gap in my log, stated as a guess-free absence rather than dressed as a lead.
