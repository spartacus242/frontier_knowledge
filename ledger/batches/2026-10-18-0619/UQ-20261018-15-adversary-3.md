## Restatement (my own words)

When an LLM pretraining run enters its final learning-rate decay (cooldown / annealing / "stage 2"), benchmark scores jump. The candidate asks, per benchmark: what fraction of that jump could be obtained from the PRE-cooldown weights merely by rescoring them (temperature rescaling, length-normalised or rank-based option scoring, label-format correction; no weight update), to be called "sharpening/format"; whatever remains, as read by a fixed probe panel, is to be called "knowledge". Then: do published annealing data mixes re-rank when scored on the "knowledge" part alone? Pre-committed bands: small format share + no re-ranking licenses current microanneal practice; large share + re-ranking indicts it.

## Sources opened, with the lines that matter

S1. Dominguez-Olmedo, Hardt, Mendler-Dünner (2024/ICLR 2025 oral), arXiv:2407.07890v1 (HTML, opened). Extracted via the fetch tool; short fragments are verbatim.
- Definition: the term "describes a growing set of techniques to include task-relevant data in the pretraining stage"; "training on the test task is not a malpractice"; Sec. 2.2: "training on the test task is a distinct phenomenon from data contamination".
- Adjustment: "fine-tuning each model under comparison on the same task-relevant data before evaluation"; result: "instances of emergent behavior largely vanish once we adjust for training on the test task"; Sec. 7: "training on the test task removes the unpredictability and discontinuity associated with emergence".
- Cause named: Sec. 2: Qwen 1.5, Olmo 1.7 and MAP Neo "include instruction data during pretraining"; Gemma's pretraining mixture "was determined partially based on downstream benchmark evaluations".
- SHARE quantified (mediation): Sec. 2.2: recent models "outperform older ones by over 7 accuracy points in MMLU and 17 accuracy points in GSM8K"; after adjustment "no evidence for a significant difference"; Appendix B / Table 2 (as extracted): mediated effect 0.071 (MMLU) and 0.168 (GSM8K), direct effect not significant; Table 3 caption: "newer models attain much higher benchmark scores because of training on the test task". MY ARITHMETIC, not the paper's wording: 0.071 against a >7-point gap and 0.168 against a >17-point gap means the mediated share is close to the whole gap; the extraction gave no interval, so I carry no uncertainty figure.
- Mechanism = format: Sec. 3.2: standard MMLU evaluation "conflates knowledge-testing with testing a models' ability to answer multiple choice questions"; newer models score higher "largely because they are better at multiple-choice question answering"; Sec. 1: gains attributed to "an improved ability to comprehend MMLU-style prompts".
- The paper does NOT mention annealing, mid-training or cooldown (checked explicitly). Llama 3 appears only as a performance comparison.

S2. Yano, Kiyono, Kobayashi, Takase, Suzuki, "Pre-training LLM without Learning Rate Decay Enhances Supervised Fine-Tuning", ICLR 2026 (abstract page opened). Decay schedulers "may exhibit better performance after pre-training" but "WSO consistently outperforms decay-based schedulers in terms of performance after SFT"; "decay-based schedulers lead models into sharper minima"; "applying LR decay to improve pre-training metrics may compromise downstream adaptability". Says nothing about format/knowledge/calibration. Abstract only.

S3. Phase: instruction tuning only. "Instruction-tuning data are judged by quality metrics, and tuned models are judged by benchmarks" ... "this interface confounds both measurements"; "Data quality and model capability are interface-conditioned quantities"; "a skill that raises accuracy by more than 40 points under the training format can be nearly invisible under every other"; "correcting a single generation budget flips the measured effect of fine-tuning on GSM8K from a gain into a large loss". S4. Guo, Pleiss, Sun, Weinberger (ICML 2017), "On Calibration of Modern Neural Networks", arXiv:1706.04599 (PDF opened). Sec. 4.2: temperature scaling uses "a single scalar parameter T > 0 for all classes", logits divided by T before the softmax; "Because the parameter T does not change the maximum of the softmax function" ... "In other words, temperature scaling does not affect the model's accuracy." SCOPE CHECK (mine): this is stated for a single softmax over classes. It therefore covers (a) single-token label-emission MC scoring (MMLU-style letter choice) exactly, and (b) greedy decoding, since greedy takes the per-step argmax and each step's argmax is preserved for any T > 0 (my own elementary extension, not Guo's sentence). It does NOT cover summed multi-token option log-likelihoods (completion-formulation scoring), where per-position normalisers differ and the ranking can move with T, nor sampled decoding.

## Verdict

Recommended: **Rejected** (Unresolved on attacks 2 and 4; also Unresolved on 7). Key kill: V2's two-way partition is mis-specified and non-exhaustive — the "sharpening" instrument provably cannot move argmax-scored accuracy (Guo et al. 2017 §4.2), and the dominant known driver of cooldown gains in the very run named (FLAN and GSM8K-derived data in OLMo 2's stage-2 config) is "training on the test task", which V2's probes would book as "knowledge" and band (i) would then license — while Dominguez-Olmedo et al. (ICLR 2025 oral) have already computed the test-task-versus-capability share of LLM benchmark gains with MCQ-answering ability as the mechanism, leaving the candidate as that question with the comparator relabelled.

Routing (already-asked): Dominguez-Olmedo, Hardt & Mendler-Dünner, "Training on the Test Task Confounds Evaluation and Emergence" (arXiv:2407.07890) — the cooldown is a natural new comparator for their adjustment, not a new question; the AI2 format-correction line (OLMES; Bunn et al. 2025); Gan et al. arXiv:2609.02015 for the SFT-phase version.

What a successor candidate would need (pointer only): hold the data fixed and vary only the schedule (cooldown vs continued constant LR on the same mix), score with a non-MC, non-greedy endpoint or with completion-formulation scoring where temperature is not inert, and treat in-task training data as a declared third component rather than part of the residual. That is a new intake, not a repair of this one; the permitted reformulation is spent.
