Motivating gap: The settled claim inverted is that L1 insertion-site selection is set by local, one-dimensional features (ORF2p nick-site consensus, accessibility, replication timing, base composition). Its unexamined presupposition is that the relevant geometry is one-dimensional — that the target is chosen without reference to where in the nucleus the element was made. L1's cis-preference is a property of the machinery, and for retroviruses integration-site choice is known to be steered by nuclear architecture.
