## Gate 3 — Significance (argued, not asserted)

**Consequence — yes/no, and a third outcome.** If beta = 1 with no residual side term, the independent-cell-of-origin model gains its cleanest possible confirmation: the one comparison in which genotype, age and systemic exposure are held exactly constant, with laterality reduced to bookkeeping and the century-old left-breast/right-lung/right-testis observations explained away. If a residual side term survives with a sign, there is a side-of-body asymmetry in human carcinogenesis that no current theory predicts, and the candidates for it are developmental (Nodal/Pitx2-lineage left-right patterning, asymmetric lymphatic and venous drainage, asymmetric organ-level immune surveillance) rather than epidemiological. If beta is significantly below 1 across several organs at once, that is evidence of clonal competition or field-level constraint suppressing the per-cell hazard as tissue grows — which would bear directly on Peto's paradox at the within-organism scale. Three distinguishable outcomes with a pre-set rule is a genuine strength.
**Audience.** Cancer epidemiologists (laterality would move from a footnote to either a nuisance or an exposure), evolutionary oncologists working on Peto's paradox and cell-number scaling, and developmental biologists who study left-right patterning and have no oncological endpoint to test it against. All three are real communities with existing literatures, two of which I opened.
**Fecundity.** It makes askable: does the residual invert in situs inversus totalis? Does it track handedness, sleeping posture or arm dominance? Does it appear in paired organs of other mammals where left-right size asymmetry differs in sign? Does per-side epithelial cell density differ systematically, which nobody has measured and which would be a finding in its own right before any cancer endpoint?
**Anchoring.** Peto's paradox; the across-tissue stem-cell-division explanation of risk variation; the obesity/organ-size mechanism (slope 1.17 on strata); the height-and-cancer literature; and the unexplained breast and ovary laterality residual that PRIOR 1's own abstract leaves standing.
**Score: 2/3.** Capped below 3 because the descriptive form of the headline question already has a published answer, so what changes is the grain of an existing finding rather than whether the finding exists.

## Reason for silence

The candidate's own silence mechanism **fails: the question is not silent.** It was asked in 2006 for all five organs and in 1993 within person for the breast. What IS silent is the cell-number-resolved version, and that has one credible mechanism, which my presupposition-2 check grounds: **per-side epithelial cell density has never been measured in living people.** Every instrument that exists per side gives tissue VOLUME or MASS or projected AREA — Volpara dense volume, CT lung volumetry, autopsy organ weight, mammographic planimetry — and none gives epithelial cell count, so the denominator the question needs has not been constructible, and the field has substituted tissue mass for cell number without flagging the substitution. That is a genuine instrument gap, which is an acceptable mechanism on this ledger. A second, weaker mechanism is a disciplinary boundary: the people who measure per-side organ volume are radiologists building risk models for case/control discrimination (PRIOR LEG C's AUC endpoint), not epidemiologists fitting scaling exponents.
