# Working notes: is "machine-generated" a document-level binary? (7 searches, 6 fetches)
Read levels are via summariser (WebFetch), so none is verbatim FULL TEXT in the strict sense; "fetched page" = summariser view of full page.
- Ahrefs blog (ahrefs.com/blog/what-percentage-of-new-content-is-ai-generated): fetched page (summariser). 900k English pages, April 2025, one per domain, in-house bot_or_not. Pure AI 2.5%, pure human 25.8%, mixed 71.7%; headline 74.2% = pure AI + mixed. Mixed sub-bins 11-40% 25.86%, 41-70% 20.50%, 71-99% 15.51% (sum 61.87%, gaps/denominator unclear). No accuracy/FPR given. No sensitivity of headline to mixed assignment reported (summariser's "~10 points" is its own inference, not a finding).
- Pew methodology 2026-08-20: fetched page (two routes). Continuous score 0-1, single cutoff 0.2 = "meaningful" signs of AI authorship/editing. 490,000 pages (10k x 49 CC snapshots, Jan 2021-Jul 2026); post-ChatGPT estimate uses only dated pages (~10-15%). Open Pangram editlens_Llama-3.2-3B; 62,370 pages from 7 crawls vs Pangram 3.3: 96% agreement, kappa 0.61. 35% of July 2026 dated pages. No mixed share, no other cutoffs reported.
- EditLens arXiv:2510.03154 (html v1): fetched page. Human / AI-edited / fully AI; regression on edit-similarity score; says binary detectors "not well-suited" to mixed authorship. No web prevalence, no sensitivity of prevalence.
- Graphite (via Axios 2026-10-14 secondary; Graphite page itself appeared in search results only, SEARCH SNIPPETS ONLY/not opened): binary, AI if Surfer finds <=50% human; ~65,000 CC URLs, Jan 2020-May 2025; Surfer FPR 4.2%, FNR 0.6% on GPT-4o vs pre-ChatGPT; ~48% AI by May 2025; no mixed share, no other thresholds.
- Pangram 3.3 model card: fetched page. ai_assistance_score 0-1, four bands: Human <=0.25, Lightly AI-assisted 0.25-0.5, Moderately 0.5-0.75, AI >=0.75. No performance numbers for assisted/mixed bands. Note: Pew's 0.2 cutoff is for Open Pangram score, not the 3.3 band edges.
