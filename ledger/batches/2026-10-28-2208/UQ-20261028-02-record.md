Domain(s): Mathematics — arithmetic statistics (class groups of quadratic fields, Cohen-Lenstra heuristics, reflection principles)

Question (canonical): Beyond the inequality forced by Scholz's reflection principle, are the 3-class groups of the reflection-linked pair Q(sqrt d) and Q(sqrt -3d) statistically independent as d ranges over positive fundamental discriminants, or is there a residual correlation that the single-field Cohen-Lenstra heuristics, being one field at a time, neither predict nor forbid?

Alternative formulations:
- Does a two-field Cohen-Lenstra heuristic exist whose marginals are the standard ones and whose support is exactly Scholz's inequality, and does it match the computed 3-class groups of reflection-linked pairs?
- Does knowing the 3-class group of a real quadratic field tell you anything more about its reflection partner's 3-class group than Scholz's rank inequality alone?

REFORMULATED: "Over positive fundamental discriminants d <= X, does the measured conditional probability P(r_3(-3d) = r_3(d)+1 | Cl_3(Q(sqrt d)) isomorphic to G) agree with Dutarte's predicted 3^-(a+1) at a = r_3(G), for every finite abelian 3-group G of order at most 27 (the stated rule: all such G, enumerated by order), or does it depend on G beyond its 3-rank — i.e. does the joint law of the reflection-linked 3-class groups factor given rank, as it provably does not for the 4-ranks of Q(sqrt d) and Q(sqrt -d)?"
