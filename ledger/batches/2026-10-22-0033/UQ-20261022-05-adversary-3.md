## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

The candidate asks two things about commercial research antibodies. (i) Take a fixed list of products, each pinned to one dated datasheet. For a named application (say western blot), does the "recommended dilution" printed on that datasheet follow from the concentration the vial ships at — so that the dilution is a fact about the bottle — or from the antibody's binding constant together with how much target is in the recommended sample — so that it is a fact about the biophysics? Decide by regressing log dilution on both, with format and vendor as covariates. (ii) Take a sample of papers that used those products. In what share of them can you turn the paper's "1:1000" into a µg/mL (or molar) dose using the paper plus the dated datasheet, and, among papers that quote the same dilution of the same catalogue number, how much does the computed dose vary?

My reading of the hinge: limb (i) presupposes the dilution is the free variable that something predicts; limb (ii)'s second quantity presupposes that the computed dose can differ between two papers that quote the same dilution of the same product under one datasheet. Both presuppositions are tested below.

## Single strongest killing point (the leg the verdict stands on if the others fell)

Attack 4, Leg A: as written, limb (ii)'s "spread in computed concentration among papers quoting the same nominal dilution for the same catalogue number" is identically zero, because the record pins each catalogue number to one dated datasheet and defines the computed concentration as that datasheet's stated stock concentration times the quoted dilution. Every escape (lots, revisions, paper-stated µg/mL) requires data the question's own frame excludes or that the record's own P6 says is unavailable in 99% of papers. The motivating "orders of magnitude" claim lives in a different comparison (across catalogue numbers) from the quantity operationalised (within one). This needs no external source; it is a defect in the candidate's text that survived a Gate 1 reformulation whose stated purpose was well-posedness. Leg B (CST fixes the dilution and tunes the concentration, Source 1, full text, regime CST) is independent and sourced, and would on its own cap the entry at Provisional with "re-specify limb (i)" as the promotion condition.
