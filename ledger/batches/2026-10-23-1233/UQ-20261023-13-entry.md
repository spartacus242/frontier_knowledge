Question (canonical): For a declared frame of systems each carrying two or more independently published CALPHAD assessments whose full parameter sets are retrievable, and on one commonly digitised experimental corpus with every datum attributed to its source study: (a) what is the Kendall tau between the ordering of the competing assessments by their apparent residual, recomputed on the pooled corpus under one pre-declared common metric, and their ordering by leave-one-study-out predictive residual under that same metric; (b) what is the distribution of the predictive-minus-apparent gap, in the metric's units and as a ratio; and (c) in how many systems, and which, does the top-ranked assessment change?
Reformulation note: this is R1, the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation, adopted in the record. Strongest remaining objection: The verdict stands on ONE leg — the definitional-plus-algebraic leg of attack 4. Leave-one-study-out is a train/test split; with the parameter set frozen, an assessment's prediction for study k is unchanged by whether k is withheld, so the leave-one-study-out residual equals the apparent residual datum for datum, the gap of limb (b) is identically zero and the tau of limb (a) is identically 1, for every system and every corpus. The question as worded answers itself before any evidence is collected, and R1 has already spent Gate 1's single reformulation, so the re-fit that would make it non-trivial cannot be written in. The OTHER legs are weaker and the verdict does not need them: the weights-irreproducibility leg is sourced in the ESPEI regime only plus two full-text instances that print no weights, and would not survive a traditional-assessment paper that printed per-dataset weights and its free-parameter list; the per-datum study-attribution leg (attack 4's C3/C4 in adversary #2) rests on one full text that R1's frame would exclude by construction; and the unverified-frame-member leg is Unverified, which under this ledger's own rule caps a status and never rejects.
