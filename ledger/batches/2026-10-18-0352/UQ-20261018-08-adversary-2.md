## Restatement (my words, not the generator's)

Has anyone ever checked, with real fire incidents instead of models, whether buildings signed off through the performance-based / "alternative solution" / equivalency route actually contain a fire as well as comparable buildings built straight to the prescriptive code? And, as a prerequisite that is itself an answer: for how many buildings can you even find out which route they were approved under, and do enough of those buildings burn to measure anything? Limb (A) is a feasibility/counting question about regulatory records; limb (B) is a matched or weighted non-inferiority comparison on spread beyond the compartment of origin, with the margin declared before any data pull.

Two things I note before attacking. V2 is *conditional* ("in a jurisdiction holding a retrievable register ... that can be joined to individual buildings"), so limb (B) only has content if some jurisdiction satisfies an antecedent that neither the record nor I could instantiate. And V2 treats design pathway as a two-arm exposure, which is the point where the field records and the question part company (see attack 8).
