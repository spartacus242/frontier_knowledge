## The signature defect: performed but not reported is not published

The 2026-10-18 batches' signature was the clipped quotation; the 04:52 batch's was a result cited outside the scope it was proved in. This batch's is adjacent to both and has its own shape. **Two of the three Gate 5 rejections rested on a paper that PERFORMS the candidate's analysis without ever REPORTING its quantity**, and in both cases the reviewer read the performance as publication.

| Where | What the source does | What it was read as |
|---|---|---|
| UQ-09, Prosperi et al. 2023 | ROC curves in the title, TPR and FPR defined in equations (2) and (3), eight ROC panels plotted with the AUC shaded, its own optimal cut-offs derived and printed (beta 4.77 and 3.67 mm/m) — and **not one absolute discrimination number anywhere**: no AUC value, no sensitivity, no specificity, no PPV, no prevalence, and a Table 1 that is an empty symbolic TN/FP/FN/TP template | "publishing the candidate's central estimand", i.e. the ROC discrimination of damage by angular distortion |
| UQ-07, Sloley AIChE 2019 166c | announces a Monte Carlo analysis and reports none of its results on the page; the analysis is over fouling RATES and compares two MODELLING CHOICES | "the distributional version is announced in prior 1's own abstract", i.e. the candidate's distributional limb already asked |

The rule this yields: **an analysis that appears in a paper without its number cannot make a quantitative question a disguised known.** A plotted curve, a defined metric and a derived threshold are evidence that the method arrived in the field; they are not the measurement. The test to apply is the one the ledger already applies to figures — name the number and where it is printed — and when the answer is "it is shaded in a figure" or "the paper says the analysis was performed", the candidate's quantity is unpublished.

It is worth being exact about what this does and does not withdraw. Prosperi's proximity is real and it still caps UQ-09 at Provisional: same object, same buildings, same predictor, published cut-offs of the same order as the literature value. What fell is the claim that the candidate's estimand is in print.

## The second finding: the classical limits were never scored as classifiers

The deciding fact behind the reversal is one no pass had made explicit. Prosperi et al. cite Skempton-MacDonald and Burland-Wroth for DEFINITIONS only — their limiting values are never evaluated and never even stated as numbers — and run Boscardin-Cording's 3.3 permille THROUGH the fragility curve to yield 5% (shallow) and 2% (deep), which is the opposite direction of travel from classification. No TPR or FPR is computed at 3.3 permille. Peduto et al. 2019, the other leg, cites Skempton-MacDonald without applying it: its rotation is a DInSAR footprint profile slope, not an angular distortion between adjacent frame points.

So on the question of whether anyone has scored the published settlement limits as damage classifiers against surveyed damage, the two closest papers in the field are silent rather than supportive, and that silence is now the candidate's novelty. Both were read at full text, independently of the record, by readers who were handed the source and the claim and not the reviewer's reasoning.

## What to carry forward

1. **Performed is not published.** When a kill says a paper publishes the candidate's quantity, name the number and where it is printed. A plotted curve, a defined metric, a derived threshold or an announced analysis is not the measurement.
2. **A definition citation is not an evaluation.** Prosperi cites two of the three threshold families for definitions only; a kill resting on "the paper uses the Skempton-MacDonald criterion" has to say whether it applies it, evaluates it, or merely borrows its algebra.
3. **Run a threshold the way its own author stated it, in the direction of travel the candidate asks for.** Converting a published limit into a probability through a fitted curve is the inverse of scoring it as a classifier, and reads as the same thing to a skimming reviewer.
4. **A multi-leg kill needs every leg sourced.** Both of UQ-09's checkable legs fell; the third was never a validation claim. The v8 preamble carries this rule and it earned its place.
5. **Assert the ledger's tail separator before appending**, and read the index count as a check. One entry short with no stderr warning is this failure.
6. **Paste the entry's citation into every verification brief.** Three passes, three manufactured defects, all from briefs built out of something else.
7. **OpenAlex is how to end a mirror hunt honestly.** `is_oa` false with a null best location is a finding; five more searches are not.
8. **A statutory reporting obligation is not a published measurement, and its class scheme is where the granularity hides.** 2025/2289 carries a chemistry axis and is still silent within lithium-ion.
