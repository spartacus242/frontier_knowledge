Question (canonical, V2 after the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation):
Over a frame of research-biology protocol parameters enumerated BEFORE any tracing by a pre-registered rule (frequency of exact numeric-plus-unit strings in a dated protocols.io + Methods-section corpus, with physically or instrumentally forced parameters flagged in advance), and traced under a written, dual-coded rubric to exactly one of four states — (i) primary optimisation found, under still-current conditions; (ii) primary optimisation found, under superseded conditions; (iii) traced to a non-evidential source (vendor document, textbook, earlier protocol); (iv) chain unresolvable — (a) what are those four rates, with intervals, a coder-agreement statistic, and forced parameters reported separately; and (b) for a random subsample in state (ii), does re-running the original optimisation under current conditions move the optimum beyond a pre-declared margin?

Question (V1, as intaken — recorded per Gate 1): For the canonical default parameters of experimental biology protocols, what fraction can be traced to a primary determination that actually optimised a measurable endpoint, and among those, what fraction were optimised under conditions — cell lines, media, reagents, instruments, endpoints — that are no longer the ones in use?
(Reformulation was for well-posedness only: V1 leaves the denominator, the coding rule and the re-determination limb implicit, and reads as purely bibliometric although its own answer-criterion requires limb (b).)

Alternative formulations:
- A1: Where do biology's default numbers come from, and how many of them were ever optimised at all rather than inherited?
- A2: Treating each ubiquitous protocol constant as a claim, what is the citation-chain depth to its primary evidence, and what share of chains terminate in no evidence at all?
  NOTE (load-bearing): A2 is the WEAKEST of the three on novelty. Standvoss et al. 2024 (PLOS Biology, opened — see priors) occupies most of A2's territory at the citation level. The canonical V2 survives because its unit is the parameter VALUE, its endpoint is evidential status against an optimisation criterion, and it carries limb (b). If the question is ever reduced to A2 it should be re-graded Low.

Motivating gap: Textbook silence map. Protocol repositories, vendor manuals and Methods sections specify thousands of exact parameters — 37 C / 5% CO2, 10% FBS, 4% PFA for 10 min, 1:1000 primary antibody, 95 C for 5 min, 12/12 h light cycle, 20-24 C vivarium, 70% ethanol — and for almost none is there a living literature asking what the primary determination was, what endpoint it optimised, or whether it still holds. Dense in "how", empty of "why". The class is demonstrably non-empty and consequential: two members have been re-examined one at a time by accident of individual interest — ambient-O2 gas phase (physioxia literature; Alva et al., Cells 2022, opened: standard incubator headspace ~18% O2 vs 2-6% in most tissues in vivo, with downstream effects on ROS, lifespan, respiration and drug response) and sub-thermoneutral vivarium temperature (mouse phenotype). Neither re-examination was prompted by a survey; the population of defaults has never been enumerated, so nobody can say which default to re-examine next. Gate 2 confirms the asymmetry: the literature contains single-parameter critiques and citation-level method-reporting audits, and no parameter-level provenance audit.

Presuppositions (each marked):
1. A defensible frame of "canonical defaults" can be enumerated before any tracing (e.g. exact numeric-string frequency in a dated corpus). DEFENSIBLE, and the enumeration rule is itself the first deliverable and must be registered; a hand-picked list makes the rate an artefact of the picker. Gate 2 note: the extraction tooling exists and is mature (WNUT-2020 shared task on entity/relation extraction from wet-lab protocols), so the frame is constructible, which it was not a decade ago.
2. Tracing is feasible — chains terminate often enough for a rate to be estimable. CONTESTED. Pre-1970 literature and vendor documentation are patchily digitised, so "chain unresolvable" is its own reported category and must never be pooled with "no evidence exists". Standvoss et al. found chains that dead-end rather than resolve, which is weak corroboration that the category is non-trivial in size.
3. "Optimised a measurable endpoint" is codable with acceptable reliability. CONTESTED — requires a written rubric, dual coding and a reported agreement statistic, or the headline fraction is one coder's judgement.
4. A default that is untraceable or was optimised under superseded conditions may be wrong. CONTESTED and EXPLICITLY DISCLAIMED. Many inherited parameters are robust. The audit measures EVIDENTIAL STATUS; the claim is only that this status is currently unknown and is the prerequisite for prioritising re-determination.
5. Someone would act on a prioritised list of defaults whose primary evidence is absent or obsolete. WEAKEST presupposition and the one the question must defend. Physioxia and thermoneutrality show that re-examining a single default moves a field; neither case shows that an audit is what triggers such work. Gate 2 result sharpens this against the candidate: PRO-MaP (Batista Leite et al., PLOS Biology 2024, opened), the most recent multi-stakeholder consensus on methods reporting, recommends sharing, citing and versioning protocols and says nothing about where parameter values came from or whether they were ever optimised — so the body with the most natural interest in this audit is currently not asking for it.
6. The frame's parameters are genuinely conventional rather than instrument- or reagent-forced. CONTESTED PER PARAMETER. 5% CO2 is coupled to the bicarbonate buffering of the medium (itself a default, so the coupling relocates rather than dissolves the question); 37 C is forced for homeotherm-derived cells in a way 1:1000 antibody dilution is not. Forced parameters must be identified in advance and reported separately, since their "evidence" lies in a physical constraint rather than an optimisation.

What would count as an answer: Two limbs; not well-posed without the second.
(A) Audit: N defaults enumerated by the pre-registered rule, each traced under the written protocol to exactly one of the four states, dual-coded with an agreement statistic, reported as rates with intervals, forced parameters separate, "chain unresolvable" never pooled with "no evidence".
(B) Validation: for a random subsample of defaults in state (ii) (superseded conditions), re-run the original optimisation under current conditions and report whether the optimum moved by more than a pre-declared margin.
"The defaults are evidenced" = most trace to current-condition optimisations AND the re-determinations reproduce the optima within margin. "They are not" = a ranked, actionable list of defaults whose primary evidence is absent or whose optimum has moved — the output protocol repositories and reporting-standard bodies could use and currently cannot construct. An audit without limb (B) answers a bibliometric question, not this one.

Significance — consequence: If most defaults trace to primary optimisations under still-current conditions and limb (B) reproduces the optima within margin, that is a substantive negative result against a popular reproducibility narrative — it would say the inherited-parameter worry is anecdote generalised from two famous cases, and would redirect reproducibility effort away from parameter provenance toward reporting and analysis. If instead a large share lands in states (ii) and (iii), the output is a ranked re-determination worklist: a named set of parameters where re-examination has the physioxia/thermoneutrality prior behind it, which is the input to re-determination studies that currently get started only by accident of individual curiosity. Either way the rate itself is new information: at present nobody can even state the order of magnitude, and PRO-MaP's silence shows the field has no estimate to work from.
Significance — audience: (i) protocol repositories (protocols.io, Bio-protocol, STAR Protocols) — a provenance field on a parameter is implementable only against an audit that shows the states are codable and how often each occurs; (ii) reporting-standards and guideline bodies (ARRIVE, MDAR, NC3Rs, the PRO-MaP group itself, who published recommendations in 2024 without this information); (iii) funders of metascience and reproducibility programmes, who would gain a prioritisation rule for re-determination grants; (iv) core facilities, cell-culture standards groups (e.g. physioxia incubator adoption) and vendors who write the manuals that state (iii) traces to. Caveat retained: presupposition 5 is the weak one, and PRO-MaP's omission is evidence that the audience is not currently asking.
Significance — fecundity: Opens (a) which classes of default are fragile vs robust under re-determination, and whether fragility is predictable from the state code; (b) whether default obsolescence correlates with replication-failure rates in the subfields that use them; (c) a typed provenance schema for protocol parameters (a "parameter RRID"), and what it would cost to populate; (d) whether forced parameters relocate conventionality rather than remove it (5% CO2 / bicarbonate); (e) the half-life of a parameter's optimising conditions as a measurable quantity; (f) whether the same audit run in chemistry, materials or clinical laboratory practice gives different rates, which would be the first cross-field comparison of evidential status of method constants.
Significance — anchoring: the reproducibility and generalizability literature in preclinical biology (protocol harmonization vs heterogenization); the physioxia literature; the thermoneutrality literature in laboratory animal science; P1's shortcut-citation finding and PRO-MaP's recommendations; metrology's traceability chain; the "recommendations without supporting evidence" tradition in guideline appraisal; requirements engineering's lost-design-rationale problem.

Reason for silence: Three mechanisms, none of them "no one thought of it".
(1) Disciplinary boundary at the method, not the data. The audit design is owned by metrology (traceability chain), guideline appraisal (citation-chain audit of recommendations) and requirements engineering (design-rationale recovery). Gate 2 found all three vocabularies mature and none of them pointed at biology protocols; experimental biologists do not read them, and their practitioners have no reason to care which number a Methods section prints. Each discipline's audit object stops at its own boundary: metrology at the clinical measurand, guideline appraisal at the clinical recommendation.
(2) Incentive and credentialing gap. In experimental biology a parameter is credentialed by working, not by provenance: a protocol that yields a publishable figure needs no origin. Nothing funds or rewards auditing a number nobody disputes, and a negative result ("the defaults are fine") is unpublishable in the fields that would have to do the tracing, while the positive result is a service output rather than a finding. P1's journal-policy numbers corroborate the incentive reading from the other side — 0-2% of journals ask authors to fully describe previously-described methods, so the publication system actively rewards passing the parameter along by reference.
(3) The frame was until recently not constructible (new-instrument mechanism). Enumerating defaults by frequency requires a dated, machine-readable protocol corpus and reliable extraction of numeric-plus-unit strings from Methods sections; that tooling matured only with the wet-lab-protocol NLP work and the growth of dated protocol repositories. Before it, any list of "canonical defaults" was a hand-picked list, which makes the headline rate an artefact of the picker — exactly the objection that would have sunk an earlier attempt. So the question's pre-condition, not the question, is what was missing.

Scores: Well-posedness 2/3 · Significance 2/3
Well-posedness 2/3, not 3/3: the four-state coding, the dual-coding requirement with an agreement statistic, the separate reporting of forced and unresolvable parameters and limb (B)'s pre-declared margin are all specifiable, and the enumeration rule is registrable. Two residual defects: (a) "the fraction" is not a rule-independent estimand — the denominator is whatever the pre-registered enumeration rule admits, so the headline rate is conditional on an arbitrary (if honest) rule and is not comparable across studies; (b) "conditions that are no longer the ones in use" has no sharp criterion applicable uniformly across thousands of heterogeneous parameters, and limb (B) is only executable for the subset whose original optimisation is documented in enough detail to re-run, which is a biased subsample of state (ii) by construction.
Significance 2/3, not 3/3: consequence and fecundity are argued from opened sources and the audience is nameable, but presupposition 5 is undefended — the two cases that motivate the question (physioxia, thermoneutrality) were both triggered by individual curiosity, not by an audit, and the most recent multi-stakeholder consensus document in exactly this space (PRO-MaP 2024, opened) does not ask for provenance information, which is direct evidence that the named audience is not currently demanding the deliverable.
