## Restatement (my words, before reading the attacks)

Take an open-weight release R. On the hub there are hundreds of third-party derivative weight files (GGUF requantizations, fine-tunes, merges, abliterated variants), each with a download count. Evaluate each on a fixed capability suite and a fixed refusal measure under an engine matched to the reference run. How far does the download-weighted mean score of that derivative population sit from the reference weights' own score, and how is the deviation split between derivative classes and between uploaders within a class? Short: what is the download-weighted benchmark score of the model people actually fetch, versus the score of the model that was evaluated?
