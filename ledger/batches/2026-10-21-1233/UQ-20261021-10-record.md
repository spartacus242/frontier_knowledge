## Question (canonical)

**V1 (as handed over at intake; recorded, not scored):** For end-of-life lithium-ion batteries actually arriving at recycling plants in a declared region and window, what is the measured distribution of feedstock cell chemistry, state of health, format and non-battery contamination under a declared representative-sampling protocol, and how does it compare with the input composition assumed in the published recycling-process and life-cycle-assessment studies on which recovery-efficiency and recycled-content targets are modelled?

**V2 (the one Gate 1 reformulation, for well-posedness only; scored as canonical):** For a declared region and window and a frame declared before any data pull, with the comparison carried on **one pre-declared crosswalk layer — cathode-active-material chemistry shares by mass of arriving battery mass, with element content of the black mass made from it as a second, separately reported layer** — (a) what is the measured arrival-composition distribution, estimated under a sampling protocol declared in advance and reported with its fundamental-sampling-error budget, its grouping-and-segregation component and its detection limits; (b) over a literature frame declared in advance and restricted to studies that state an input composition, what is the distribution of **assumed** compositions on that same crosswalk layer, with the share of frame studies excluded for not stating one reported as a primary result; and (c) what is the signed gap between (a) and (b), and what does that gap do to the recovery-efficiency and recycled-content figures those studies report when their own stated models are re-run on the measured composition? **Format** is carried as a stratifying variable of the frame, not as a separate endpoint. **State of health** is defined only on the sub-stream arriving as intact packs or modules and is to be reported as *not evaluable* on the shredded, discharged or damaged remainder, with that remainder's mass share reported.

**What V1 → V2 fixed, and why the reformulation was needed.** V1 is not well-posed as written, on four counts that its own presupposition list half-concedes and its canonical sentence then ignores. (i) *Unit mismatch inside the question.* V1 asks for one "distribution of composition" while the measured side is routinely reported on black mass (a post-pyrolysis, post-shredding, sub-500 µm product) and the modelled side on cell or cathode designs; without a declared crosswalk the "signed gap" is a difference of two different measurands. V2 names one primary layer and demotes the other to a separately reported layer. (ii) *Four endpoints bundled as one.* Chemistry, state of health, format and contamination have different instruments, different achievable frames and different variances; V2 keeps chemistry and contamination as endpoints, demotes format to a stratifier and makes state of health conditional with a declared not-evaluable class. (iii) *The protocol was a preliminary in V1 and is an output in V2.* Since the method-owning community is sampling theory, a composition without its fundamental-sampling-error budget is not a measurement, so V2 requires the variance budget in the answer. (iv) *The propagation step was unconstrained.* "Propagated into recovery efficiency" is only executable where a study's own model can be re-run; V2 says so, which makes "the frame's models are not re-runnable" an admissible finding rather than a failure.

Alternative formulations (from intake, retained): What is actually in the arriving stream and in the black mass made from it, and does the published process and LCA literature assume a feedstock that the real stream does not supply? / Over a declared frame of published Li-ion recycling process and LCA studies, what feedstock composition does each assume, and what is the signed gap to the composition measured at operating plants?

## Significance — consequence

**If the answer is a gap:** every recovery-efficiency and recycled-content figure computed on the assumed composition carries a sign and a magnitude of error that is currently unpriced, and the error is not symmetric across targets. Element-specific obligations and mass-based overall obligations respond differently to the same compositional shift: a stream shifting toward lithium iron phosphate removes recoverable nickel and cobalt from the feed as a matter of stoichiometry (LiFePO4 contains neither), so element-specific recovery denominators shrink while the mass-based denominator does not, and a process train optimised on an NMC-dominant assumption is being assessed against a feed it was not designed for. Capital allocation follows the same figures: hydrometallurgical plant sized on an assumed nickel-cobalt grade is sized on a revenue line. **If the answer is no gap:** the worry closes, the existing process and LCA literature inherits an empirical warrant it currently lacks, and the regulatory feasibility assessments stand as computed — which is a substantive result, not a null. **If the answer is that assumed compositions cannot be coded:** the finding relocates from the stream to the literature, and the actionable object becomes a reporting requirement rather than a process redesign.

## Significance — fecundity

Once an arrival distribution with a variance budget exists: whether black-mass price formation tracks assay or nameplate, and with what lag; whether chemistry-specific LCAs should be reweighted by the arrival mixture before being used in policy appraisal, and what that does to published comparative rankings of routes; whether a composition-conditional recovery target is better behaved than a fixed mass-based one; what sampling intensity a compliance assay needs to hold a stated false-declaration rate, which is the standard question the method-owning community asks of any new material; whether the same gap exists in the other direction — processes designed for contamination the stream does not actually carry; and whether the production-scrap-dominant current feed means today's measured arrival distribution has almost no information about the end-of-life distribution of the 2035 window, which would make the window declaration the most consequential part of the design.

## Significance — anchoring

Touches four live things. The stoichiometric tension between mass-based recovery obligations and an LFP-shifting chemistry mix. The reported origin inversion in the feedstock (production scrap now, end-of-life vehicles from about 2035, per the Fraunhofer ISI projection), which is an anomaly for any study that treats "recycling feedstock" as end-of-life material. The occurrence of the phrase "an unspecified mixed battery waste stream" in a peer-reviewed LCA paper, which marks the assumed-input problem as already visible inside the modelling literature. And the black-mass assay standardisation gap that both a trade column and a 2026 project are reported to be addressing (**both title-only and unopened**, so recorded as consistent context, not as evidence).

## Reason for silence

Three mechanisms, in descending order of how much work they do. **(1) An incentive and access gap that is also a pricing mechanism.** The arriving stream's composition is the thing its assay prices. A recycler that publishes its intake composition publishes its margin and its suppliers' grade; the opened literature is consistent with this — every characterisation study obtained material from suppliers described without names (three unnamed suppliers in Dadé, "industrially sourced" in Bologna, "from Europe" in Donnelly), which is what a commercially sensitive feedstock looks like in print. This is not "no one thought of it": it is a standing reason why the measurement is made privately and reported as samples rather than as streams. **(2) A disciplinary boundary that separates the method from the data from the model.** The method belongs to minerals-processing sampling theory (its venue is the World Conference on Sampling and Blending), the material belongs to battery and chemical engineering, the assumed-composition question belongs to industrial ecology and LCA, and the target belongs to recycling policy. The one paper found that crosses method into material — the WCSB10 paper — lists a sampling strategy as an aim and asks for more samples; it did not reach the modelling literature, and the modelling literature's assumed inputs did not reach it. A question whose answer needs all four is nobody's next paper. **(3) A missing standard, which is being built right now.** Without an agreed sampling and assay method for black mass there is no common denominator in which a measured and an assumed composition could be compared, so the comparison was not merely unasked but unexpressible; the standardisation effort reported in 2026 (title-only here) would remove that obstacle, which places the candidate at the moment the question becomes askable rather than long after. Supporting, but not sufficient alone: **(4) a new-instrument component** — automated mineralogy with a large phase database, X-ray CT and LA-ICP-MS for lithium are what made per-particle chemistry attribution in black mass routine, and the opened studies using them are from 2021–2025.
