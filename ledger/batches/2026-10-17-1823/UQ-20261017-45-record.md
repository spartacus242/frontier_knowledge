Question (canonical):
V2 (Gate 1 reformulation — the one permitted, for well-posedness; V1 below) — On a pre-declared, machine-extracted frame of published EPDs for construction products, partitioned into functional classes by a normalisation fixed before any data pull (declared unit, performance/strength class, reference service life, life-cycle stage set A1-A3 at minimum), and with the method-effect estimator declared in advance as the within-product contrast across repeated declarations of the SAME product under differing method configurations (PCR version, allocation rule, background-database identity and vintage, declared-unit conversion, programme operator, verifier): (i) what share of the within-class variance in declared GWP is attributable to method/background-data configuration versus to producer/plant identity; (ii) under a harmonised recomputation that fixes the method configuration at one declared reference, what fraction of pairwise within-class product comparisons reverse sign, and what fraction of compliance determinations against the threshold actually in force at the time of declaration (e.g. a Buy Clean maximum acceptable GWP) cross the threshold; and (iii) what fraction of the frame had to be dropped for missing method metadata, reported separately by metadata field?

V1 (intake wording, retained per Gate 1): In the now-enumerable corpus of Environmental Product Declarations used by low-embodied-carbon procurement, how much of the declared-GWP difference between competing products of the same functional class is attributable to methodological and background-data choices — product category rule version, allocation, LCA database vintage, declared-unit conversion — rather than to production differences, and what fraction of threshold-based procurement decisions would reverse under a harmonized recomputation?

(What V2 adds: it names the estimand's identification strategy as part of the question rather than as an implementation detail; it fixes the reversal limb to a stated reference configuration and to the threshold in force at declaration date, so the limb cannot be answered differently by choosing a convenient harmonisation target; and it makes the metadata-dropout report a limb of the question rather than a reporting courtesy, because a decomposition on the well-documented minority answers a different question.)

Alternative formulations:
- Do EPDs measure factories or methods, and does the answer change who wins a Buy Clean procurement?
- For products carrying multiple declarations, how large is the method-induced component of declared global-warming potential relative to the plant-to-plant component, and does it exceed the margin at which procurement thresholds are set?

Motivating gap:
A topic saturated with how-answers and structurally empty of the why-question. The standards regime (EN 15804 / ISO 21930 plus product category rules) specifies how to compute and verify a declaration; every declaration carries a printed non-comparability caveat; procurement policy then compares declared values against numerical thresholds anyway. The caveat is how the field declines to ask what the declared number is a measurement OF, and because it is printed, the absence reads as prudence rather than as an open question.
This gap is now documented, not recalled. The California Department of General Services' statutory *Buy Clean California Act global warming potential adjustment study* (1 Jan 2025) sets the maximum acceptable GWP limits that bind state procurement, finds discrepancies between its own facility-EPD averages and the industry-wide values, and states that these "could be attributed to" multiple programme operators, inconsistent review periods, modelling assumptions, and datasets and software — then quantifies none of it, and chooses industry-wide EPDs on grounds of "consistency" rather than on an attribution analysis. The field that sets the threshold names the confound and declines to measure it. Meanwhile the tool that mediates most US low-carbon material selection (EC3) propagates method uncertainty by *assigned expert-judgment factors* — a flat 3% term for LCIA-method difference, with allocation and characterisation differences named as sources but explicitly "not modeled" — and the methodology document states it "has not been extensively peer-reviewed." What is new is that the corpus is machine-readable at the scale of tens of thousands of declarations, so the split is estimable for the first time, and the stake is a live allocation of public money.

Presuppositions:
1. An enumerable, linkable corpus exists with enough declarations per functional class and extractable method metadata (PCR version, background database and vintage, verifier, declared unit). CONTESTED and binding: metadata completeness, not corpus size, is the constraint. Partially supported by what was opened — the DGS study compiled facility-specific EPDs from "several repositories" but with only 9-13 per material for most categories and one for flat glass, so a usable per-class n is a real question for small classes while plausible for concrete, cement and steel.
2. Functional equivalence across declarations can be enforced by a declared normalisation (declared unit, performance/strength class, service life, fixed stage set, A1-A3 minimum). Supported: Konradsen et al. 2023 shows service-life and module-D choices drive the largest divergences, which is why fixing the stage set and RSL is prerequisite rather than optional.
3. A harmonised recomputation or an identified method-correction is possible. CONTESTED: EPDs publish results, not inventories, so identification may rest entirely on the subset of products declared more than once under different method configurations, and that subset may be unrepresentative. This is the binding threat to limb (i), and the reason V2 pre-declares the estimator and the dropout report.
4. Plant-level production differences are real and non-negligible, so the decomposition is not degenerate by construction. Supported: the DGS counts show facility-level spread within a material category wide enough that 13-18 of 27 rebar facilities fall on different sides of candidate limits.
5. Procurement thresholds are known, binding, and applied to declared values as published. Checked and supported from the policy document: BCCA limits are numeric, statutory, and were in fact changed on 1 Jan 2025 (rebar 890 to 755 kg CO2e; mineral wool 3.33 to 2.68 and 8.16 to 6.82).
6. Declared values are not already strategically selected by producers (choice of PCR, verifier, or which plant to declare), which would make the "method component" partly behavioural rather than technical. CONTESTED, and the reason the estimand must be stated as a decomposition of *declared* values with the behavioural channel named, not as a decomposition of a physical quantity.
7. PCR committees, programme operators and procuring agencies would act on a measured method component. CONTESTED: a noisy but settled rule may be preferred to re-opening the rulebook. Weak evidence for action-readiness: DGS declined to lower four of seven limits in 2025 partly on data-quality grounds, which is the behaviour of a body that would use such a number.

What would count as an answer:
Three things together, with the identification strategy declared before any data pull:
(i) a variance decomposition of declared GWP within functional classes into method/background-data components and producer/plant components, estimated on the enumerated corpus using repeated declarations of the same product under different method configurations as the method-effect estimator, with the transportability of that estimator from the repeat-declared subset to the frame reported as a sensitivity rather than assumed;
(ii) a decision limb: the share of pairwise within-class comparisons and of threshold determinations that reverse under harmonised recomputation, with uncertainty;
(iii) an explicit null that counts as an answer: method components small relative to plant components with few reversals, which would mean declared values are substantively comparable in practice and would settle by measurement a question the field currently answers only by disclaimer.
An answer must report what fraction of the frame was dropped for missing method metadata, by field, because a decomposition computed on the well-documented minority answers a different question.

Novelty — nearest prior formulations (3, with sources actually opened):
P1. Konradsen, M. et al. (2023), "Same product, different score: how methodological differences affect EPD results," *International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment*, DOI 10.1007/s11367-023-02246-x (read at link.springer.com; SI at zenodo.org/records/10017801).
 - Unit of analysis: ONE hypothetical product — a 1.23 x 1.48 m triple-glazed wood/aluminium-clad window from a fictive Danish manufacturer — under 10 LCA scenarios built from the rule sets of four programme operators (EPD Denmark, EPD Norway, EPD International, IBU).
 - Endpoint: declared GWP in kg CO2-eq per 1 m2 at U = 0.5 W/m2K over a reference service life, by stage and total A1-C4 (plus 13 impact categories).
 - Reference/comparator: two base scenarios REF-r and REF-e (EPD Denmark rules, EN 15804+A2, residual vs green-certificate grid mix).
 - Estimand: the percentage change in declared GWP caused by each methodological choice relative to the base scenario. Reported effects: IBU-b 43% lower overall and ~50% lower on climate change via service life; DEN-r ~17% lower; module D -6/-7 vs -34/-35 kg CO2-eq; NOR-b A1-A3 12% lower with B2 83% higher, offsetting in total. Explicitly NOT a variance decomposition and NOT a count of reversed comparisons.
P2. California Department of General Services (2025), "Buy Clean California Act global warming potential adjustment study," dated 1 Jan 2025, prepared under Public Contract Code s.3502(c) (read at dgs.ca.gov).
 - Unit of analysis: the BCCA-eligible material category (seven of them), populated by industry-wide and facility-specific EPDs, with manufacturers and production facilities as the counted units.
 - Endpoint: simple average declared GWP per category, and the count of compliant manufacturers/facilities.
 - Reference/comparator: the maximum acceptable GWP limit currently in force, against candidate alternative limits (the industry-wide value and the study's own calculated facility average).
 - Estimand: whether each limit should be lowered, operationalised as compliance counts at alternative limits — e.g. hot-rolled structural steel 6/6 manufacturers and 13/14 facilities at 1,010 vs 4/6 and 11/14 at 819.7; hollow structural sections 6/8 and 15/23 at 1,710 vs 5/8 and 11/23 at 1,648; rebar 7/7 and 27/27 at 890 vs 18/27 facilities at 755 vs 13/27 at 689.1; heavy-density mineral wool 1/1 at 8.16 vs 0 manufacturers at 6.82. Adopted: rebar 890->755, mineral wool 3.33->2.68 and 8.16->6.82. Sample 9-13 facility EPDs for most materials, one for flat glass and for heavy-density insulation.
P3. Hasika, V., DeRousseau, M. & Northcott, P. (Building Transparency / C-Change Labs) (2023), "EC3 Uncertainty General Methodology," v1.1, 2 Feb 2023, building on Waldman, B., Huang, M. & Simonen, K. (2020), "Embodied carbon in construction materials: a framework for quantifying data quality in EPDs," *Buildings and Cities* 1(1) (methodology read at buildingtransparency.org; the Waldman framework identified via F2 but not itself opened).
 - Unit of analysis: a single EPD, scored for the specificity of its underlying LCI.
 - Endpoint: an uncertainty factor per specificity group — the percentage increase from the median to the 80th-percentile GWP — combined by root sum of squares into UFtotal.
 - Reference/comparator: the EPD's own median declared GWP; the 80th percentile is chosen as "a conservative value that incentivizes EPD data transparency and specificity."
 - Estimand: a forward-propagated, expert-assigned uncertainty multiplier over seven groups (supply chain, facility, product, batch, manufacturer for industry-wide EPDs, LCIA method, residual) — NOT an empirical variance decomposition. Methodological choices are essentially unmodelled: a flat 3% LCIA-method term, with allocation and characterisation differences named as sources but not modelled. Worked example: one copper cable EPD, 17.6% supply-chain + 2.4% batch -> 18.0% total. Other assigned terms: 10% product, 3% residual, cement batching at 80% of ASTM C94's +/-2%. Self-described as "not extensively peer-reviewed."

Significance — consequence:
If method/background-data components dominate within functional classes and a non-trivial share of threshold determinations cross under harmonisation, then Buy Clean-style limits are partly sorting producers by their LCA consultant, PCR vintage and database licence rather than by their kiln, furnace or grid — and the policy instrument is mis-specified in a way that is fixable (harmonised recomputation as a procurement requirement, or thresholds set as method-robust bands). The magnitudes already on the table make this live rather than hypothetical: P1 finds 12-50% swings from single method choices and 43% from a full rule-set swap, while P2's adopted BCCA changes move limits by ~15% (rebar 890->755) and that ~15% move takes rebar facility compliance from 27/27 to 18/27. A method component of P1's size against a policy margin of P2's size is the condition under which reversals are common; nobody has checked.
If the null holds — method components small relative to plant components, few reversals — the printed non-comparability caveat is revealed as over-conservative in practice, which licenses direct comparison, removes the main technical objection to EPD-threshold procurement, and would let EC3-style conservative adjustment factors (which currently penalise industry-wide data by assigned judgment) be replaced by measured ones. Either answer settles by measurement a question the field currently answers only by disclaimer; that asymmetry-free structure is why this is a question rather than an agenda.

Significance — audience:
(a) Threshold-setting agencies that must act on a number: California DGS under Public Contract Code s.3502(c) (it revises limits on a statutory clock and in 2025 declined to lower four of seven limits partly on data-quality grounds), GSA and FHWA low-embodied-carbon programmes, EPA under IRA s.60503/60506 (its interim determination and the open RFI on how to set "substantially lower" thresholds are exactly where a measured method margin would enter), and EU analogues under the revised Construction Products Regulation and GPP criteria. They would act by widening, banding, or conditioning thresholds, or by requiring a declared method configuration for award-relevant EPDs.
(b) PCR committees and programme operators (IBU, EPD Denmark/Norway/International, UL Solutions, NSF, ASTM, EPD Australasia — the last already publishes guidance on EPDs of similar products): a measured split tells them which specific rule degrees of freedom to close first, in priority order, instead of harmonising everything or nothing.
(c) Tool and database operators whose adjustment factors are currently stipulated: Building Transparency / C-Change Labs state their EC3 uncertainty methodology is "not extensively peer-reviewed" and leave allocation unmodelled behind a flat 3% LCIA term; limb (i) supplies the empirical replacements, and limb (ii) answers the unanswered CLF-forum question about whether their adjustment inflates apparent savings for industry-wide versus product-specific comparisons.
(d) Producers and trade associations (AISC, CRSI, polyiso and mineral-wool bodies already publishing comparability explainers), who would learn whether declaring per-plant rather than industry-wide is worth its cost at the margin that actually decides awards.

Significance — fecundity:
Opens: whether a repeat-declaration panel can serve as a standing audit instrument for the EPD system (a measurement-system analysis for declarations, which metrology owns and this literature has not borrowed); whether method-effect correction should be a verified field on the declaration itself; whether producers' observed choice of PCR, verifier and declaring plant is strategically responsive to the threshold in force, which converts limb (i)'s "method component" into a measurable behavioural elasticity and makes threshold gaming an empirical question in a procurement literature that currently has none; how to set thresholds that are robust to the method noise rather than nominally below it; and whether the same decomposition applies to the other declared indicators (acidification, eutrophication, primary energy) whose method sensitivity P1 suggests differs by category.
Anchoring: the EN 15804 / ISO 21930 non-comparability caveat and what it is actually claiming; the unvalidated status of EC3's uncertainty factors; DGS's explicit non-attribution; the mature but untransplanted rank-reversal / composite-indicator sensitivity literature (JRC, EPI sensitivity analyses) and metrology's variance-components framework, neither of which has been pointed at this corpus.

Reason for silence:
A disciplinary-boundary plus incentive mechanism, with a new-data component.
(1) The printed caveat discharges the question. A non-comparability disclaimer on every declaration converts an open empirical question into a satisfied compliance obligation: the LCA community has met its duty by warning, so no one in it owes an answer, while the procurement community needs a scalar and takes the declared one. The absence therefore reads as prudence, which is precisely why it is invisible as a gap.
(2) The parties able to compute the split are the parties the answer would impugn. Programme operators' product is comparability-within-rules; the dominant tool vendor assigns method uncertainty by judgment and says its methodology has not been extensively peer-reviewed. A measured method component either shows the assigned factors wrong or shows the rulebook's degrees of freedom decisive; neither is a result an operator or vendor is incentivised to produce about itself.
(3) The method lives in a field that does not read this one. The estimand is a variance-components / measurement-system-analysis problem with a rank-reversal decision limb — mature named families in metrology and in composite-indicator sensitivity analysis (JRC/EPI tradition). Query 11 confirms the families exist and are not applied here; query 6 found no LCA round-robin reproducibility study at all. The construction-materials standards literature owns the data and does not own the method.
(4) Only recently answerable. Corpus-scale extraction of method metadata from tens of thousands of declarations is new, and the repeat-declaration subset that identifies the method effect only exists once products have been re-declared across PCR and database generations — a condition the EN 15804+A1-to-+A2 transition and successive ecoinvent vintages have just created.
