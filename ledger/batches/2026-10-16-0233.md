**Provisional detail.**

*UQ-20261016-06 (serving-stack numerics and agentic scores).* Well-posed 3/3, significance 2/3, and no adversary left a fatal attack Unresolved. It is held at Provisional because both adversaries independently surfaced a close within-field prior that Gate 2's 11 queries missed, and the run session then verified it directly rather than relying on their characterisation: **arXiv:2506.09501**, Yuan et al., "Understanding and Mitigating Numerical Sources of Nondeterminism in LLM Inference" (2025-06-11, v2 2025-10-24), which names evaluation batch size, GPU count and GPU type as sources of differing outputs, traces them to "the non-associative nature of floating-point arithmetic", and reports up to 9% accuracy variation under bfloat16 greedy decoding. That makes the variance limb asked and partly answered, and falsifies the candidate's "new instrument" reason for silence. What the verification also established is why this is Provisional and not Rejected: the paper does not isolate batch *composition* in the serving stack, its fix is LayerCast (FP32 compute) rather than batch-invariant kernels, and it neither establishes a mean deficit against a pinned-path reference nor touches long-horizon agentic tasks or any growth with rollout length. The "NeurIPS 2025 oral" venue attached to this citation in the Gate 2 record was not verifiable from the arXiv listing and is flagged as unconfirmed in the entry.

*UQ-20261016-07 (inherited findings in follow-up radiology reports).* The only candidate in seven batches to score 3/3 on both well-posedness and significance. Novelty is Medium rather than High because VisualCheXbert (CHIL 2021) names the mechanism in print — report labellers "have access to clinical report history, which biases their diagnoses", and "diagnoses were carried forward from prior studies" — while measuring only total report-vs-image disagreement, with no series-order variable and no index-vs-follow-up contrast. The strongest surviving objection is a confounder the question does not control: a blinded single-image panel cannot separate "restated but no longer present" from "still present but now below the detection threshold of a supine portable film", and the index-exam noise floor does not fix it because index positives are systematically more conspicuous than follow-up residual ones. The question's own predicted signature is therefore produced just as well by declining conspicuity with no inheritance at all. Promotion needs severity, view and conspicuity matching written into the question itself.
