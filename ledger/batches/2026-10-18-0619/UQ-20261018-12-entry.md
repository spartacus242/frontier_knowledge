Question (canonical): In shipped low-precision training configurations (BF16 parameters with BF16 or block-quantized 8-bit optimizer state, versus FP32 master weights), what fraction of (parameter, step) update attempts leaves the parameter bitwise unchanged ("annihilation") or materially deviant from an FP32-master-weight replay ("distortion"), is that fraction concentrated on identifiable parameter groups (rare-token embedding rows, normalization gains, small-magnitude weights, attention vs MLP), and does it rise as the learning rate decays? (V2, the one permitted Gate 1 reformulation, declares the configuration frame, a bitwise annihilation definition, a pre-declared distortion threshold, the parameter partition and a seed band; full text in batches/2026-10-18-0619/UQ-20261018-12-record.md.)
Alternative formulations: (1) How many of a run's updates never land, and which parameters quietly stop learning before the run ends? (2) Numerical-analysis words: what is the realized stagnation rate of the weight-update recurrence in shipped low-precision optimizers, stratified by operand magnitude? (3) Distributed-systems words: what per-step weight-update sparsity does BF16 absorption induce, and is the pattern structured by module?
Motivating gap: Inverts the presupposition that a computed update changes the parameter it is applied to. The claimed gap was that the ecosystem ships mitigations (Kahan summation in torchao/optimi, stochastic rounding, FP32 master weights by default in Megatron/DeepSpeed, bitsandbytes block-quantized state plus stable-embedding layer) whose necessity has never been quantified in a real LLM run. FALSE AS STATED: the realized rate is published at five scales with the ulp mechanism named (arXiv 2602.03839), independently at seven scales (arXiv 2602.11456), by module group and magnitude decile at the end-checkpoint unit (arXiv 2606.13657), and with matched compensated/FP32-master arms on held-out perplexity at 125M-7B two years earlier (arXiv 2405.03637, COLLAGE, ICML 2024).
