### (7) Subtype convention and internal consistency — CONFIRMED by the paper's own usage; the rule itself is never stated in the paper.
The paper never defines the direction of the Rubin et al. (2007) scale. Its usage: Sec. 3.1.1 EET 96029 (2.5) "is the least altered meteorite used in the present study"; Sec. 3.1.1 "SCO 06043 (CM2.0) is the most highly aqueously altered of the studied meteorites"; Sec. 1.2 Murchison "is one of the least aqueously altered CMs known (i.e., CM2.5; Rubin et al., 2007)"; Sec. 4.7.1 Nogoya (CM2.2) and MET 01070 (CM2.0) as Rubin's highly altered examples. The entry's reconciliation is therefore the paper's own, not an external inference.
