**Rochman et al. 2018 — in what regime was the result established?**
- *Population:* random (TMC/TENDL-style) nuclear-data file realisations, I = 10,000 per isotope, for exactly three actinides (²³⁵U, ²³⁸U, ²³⁹Pu). Not released evaluated libraries.
- *Unit of analysis:* the random library realisation, scored per benchmark. (The candidate's unit is the *(library, benchmark) pair* for released libraries.)
- *In-sample set:* 14 integral quantities from 12 ICSBEP benchmarks. *Held-out set:* 51 further ICSBEP criticality benchmarks, by class 18 hmf / 18 pmf / 7 imf / 3 mmf / 4 mcf / 1 icf — i.e. **fast-metal-dominated**; one thermal-compound and no LCT/thermal-solution class in the holdout.
- *Operating condition / instrument:* χ²-weighted assimilation of random files (BMC, BFMC, Mocaba, best-single-file TMC selection, linear combination of files), scored by calculated k_eff and spectral indices F28/F25, F49/F25 against ICSBEP values.

- *Benefit category:* fit quality of adjusted versus prior nuclear data. **No margin, no USL, no ANS-8.24.** Relayed verbatim from the fetch: "None of these is mentioned."

## Gate 3 — Significance (argued, scored on V2)

**Consequence.** If the answer is "yes, large": every criticality-safety validation that derives a bias and bias uncertainty from a C/E population overlapping the evaluation's own integral inputs has an understated bias uncertainty, and the USL is non-conservative by a computable amount — a licensing quantity, not a scientific one. If "no": the worry retires and benchmark-derived bias keeps its current standing, which is itself worth having stated. Prior 1 has already delivered a directional answer *for assimilated libraries* — held-out performance degraded relative to the unadjusted prior — so the consequence of the regime-A limb is now "how much", not "whether", which deflates it. The regime-B and ΔUSL limbs remain genuinely open and are where the consequence now lives: no document opened connects a held-out degradation to a margin.

**Audience.** Real and named: criticality-safety analysts and the ANSI/ANS-8 working groups who own ANS-8.24; the US NCSP (whose own WANDA 2026 performance-assessment talk asks for "No significant regression in performance" with no numbers and no independence test); OECD/NEA WPEC SG45 (VaNDaL) and SG46, and WPNCS SG14, whose live exercise already manipulates which experiments enter assimilation. These bodies would act: a demonstrated held-out degradation would change benchmark-selection guidance and the bias-uncertainty construction. Their actual engagement with the independence question is, on Prior 3, nil — which is evidence the audience exists but has not been addressed, not evidence it would not care.

**Fecundity.** Moderate. ## Reason for silence

A **disciplinary boundary with an incentive gap on each side.** The evaluation community owns the adjustment and reports its fit in χ² and C−E; its own methodological mandate document (Prior 3) does not list independence of validation experiments among the criteria it sets, so holding benchmarks out is nobody's deliverable there. The criticality-safety community owns the USL and takes the library as a given input: the LANL/ORNL USL methodology literature returned by query 6 is about *selecting and weighting* benchmarks for an application, never about whether those benchmarks informed the library. Neither side owns the join, and the join is where the margin is. The incentive half is sharp and visible in the sources: a library release paper's function is to show improved benchmark performance, and a held-out *degradation* has no venue in a release paper — Prior 1's finding that the unadjusted prior beat every adjusted file out-of-sample appears in a methods paper in EPJ Plus, not in any release documentation, and was not picked up by the WPEC feedback report or the 2026 performance-assessment deck. That is the mechanism, and it also explains why the silence is *partial* rather than total: the measurement was made once, in the one place with an incentive to make it, and then not propagated.
