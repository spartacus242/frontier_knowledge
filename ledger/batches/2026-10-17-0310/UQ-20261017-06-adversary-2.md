## Restatement (my own words, not the record's)

Take one fixed MoE-LM pretraining recipe and run it three or more times changing nothing but the random seed (router/expert init and data order). Each run induces, at any checkpoint, a map from probe-corpus tokens to the top-k experts that process them — i.e. a partition-like labelling of tokens by expert, per layer. Expert labels are arbitrary, so first fix a rule for deciding which expert in run B is "the same expert" as a given expert in run A (Hungarian/linear-assignment matching on per-expert activation profiles over a fixed probe corpus). Then ask one empirical question with two limbs:

- **Limb 1 (identity).** After matching, how much do two runs' token-to-expert labellings actually agree (ARI/NMI), (a) at the very early checkpoint where OLMoE says routing has already "saturated" (~1% of pretraining) and (b) at the end? Is that agreement distinguishable from agreement manufactured by the matching procedure itself (nulls: relabelling; matching to an unrelated run)? And how does it compare to the *within*-run number OLMoE published (a run's agreement with its own future, ~60% at 1%)?
- **Limb 2 (prognosis).** Of whatever spread in final held-out loss and benchmark scores exists between seeds, what share is already readable off the 1%-checkpoint routing statistics?
- Plus: how much of all of the above moves when you change the load-balancing auxiliary-loss weight.

In short: is the thing that freezes in the first 1% of MoE pretraining a property of the *recipe* (same twice, up to relabelling) or of the *run* (a one-off lottery), and does it foretell how good the run ends up? Note what the question is *not*: it is not about whether experts are semantically interpretable, not about whether two runs are linearly connected in weight space, and not about routing stability within one run.

## Recommended verdict

**Provisional** — nothing Unresolved (so not Rejected), but Partial on attacks 2, 4, 6 and 8, plus Medium novelty with an acknowledged close neighbour and one Unverified check (2607.28308's three-seed training appendix unread), which under Gate 5's own criteria blocks Validated. Promotion conditions: (i) read arXiv:2607.28308 Appendices S3 and S6.3 to confirm its three same-recipe seeds carry no between-seed routing comparison; (ii) add a marginal-preserving null (expert-mass/token-frequency-preserving) so the headline cannot be manufactured by routing-mass concentration, and separate the unrelated-run null's two factors; (iii) record Gurnee et al. 2401.12181 as a named neighbour in the novelty section and re-grade with the cross-seed-universality vocabulary included; (iv) obtain or cite a between-seed terminal-quality SD for MoE so limb 2 has a known denominator; (v) fix the Prior 1 author-name order.

**Single strongest killing point:** the headline statistic may be manufactured by routing-mass concentration rather than by canonicity — Hungarian matching returns the maximum-weight assignment by construction, and Prior 3's own opened numbers (ECI Gini 0.8957/0.9207/0.9465; 4 experts covering 66.3% of ECI in shallow DeepSeek-V2-Lite layers; cross-domain top-k Jaccard 0.8735 in OLMoE-1B-7B, the anchor model) say a handful of experts carry most of the routing mass, so matching them first drives A_cross up for reasons any two runs of any recipe would share; neither declared null (random relabelling, which does not pass through the same maximization; unrelated-run matching, which changes recipe and correspondence together) isolates that. Non-fatal under the gate rules, but it is the objection the design must answer before the number means what the question wants it to mean.
