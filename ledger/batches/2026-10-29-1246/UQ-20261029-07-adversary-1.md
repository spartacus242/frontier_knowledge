## Attack 4 — numbered conditioning clauses, tested literally

1. **An 8-state HMM's entropy rate can be computed to a tolerance at least 10x finer than 0.01 nats/symbol (i.e. 0.001).** UNVERIFIED by anything I opened. (I recall that HMM entropy rate has no closed form and is computed numerically via the mixed-state process to high precision, but I opened no source; this clause kills nothing.)
2. **The PCFG's per-terminal entropy rate equals a closed form "from production entropies and expected derivation length".** CONTESTED — BY MY OWN REASONING, NOT A SOURCE FINDING. That closed form (the Grenander/Chi derivational-entropy formula) is the entropy of the distribution over DERIVATIONS per expected yield length. The terminal string is a deterministic function of the derivation, so H(string) <= H(derivation), with strict inequality whenever two derivations with positive probability share a yield, i.e. whenever the grammar is ambiguous. CNF does not imply unambiguity, and Version B declares no unambiguity condition. Under an ambiguous declared grammar, H as the candidate computes it OVERSTATES the true entropy rate and Delta is biased downward (possibly negative), and the ±0.01 band is then meaningless for that limb. The searches that would have grounded this (queries 2 and 9) returned N06-1043 and J99-1004, which I did not open. Grade: CONTESTED on my own labelled reasoning; the repair is trivial (declare an unambiguous grammar, or compute H by the inside algorithm as P1 does), so this is a well-posedness defect of Version B's wording, not a fatal presupposition.
3. **Per-token cross-entropy of a finite-context model and the infinite-conditioning entropy rate are on "the same conditioning".** CONTESTED — MY OWN REASONING. A model with context window T achieves at best H_T = H(X_n | X_{n-1..n-T}) >= H; H_T − H is a property of T, which Version B does not declare and L(N,D) does not scale. For an 8-state HMM with positive transitions the convergence is exponential and negligible; for a PCFG with nested long-range dependencies it may not be below 0.01 nats. Not sourced; kills nothing.
4. **The two architecture families and matched-FLOP training exist.** TRUE on common knowledge; not contested.
5. **A FLOP-matched ladder's fitted E isolates an ARCHITECTURE property rather than a training-duration property.** CONTESTED — SOURCED (S1, full text): "when trained for sufficiently long, a transformer with a fixed depth and 1 head per layer is able to achieve low test loss ... which contradicts previous findings". Earlier-reported gaps to the optimum on Markov data closed with longer training, so a positive E on a FLOP-matched ladder is ambiguous between an architecture floor and under-training at the ladder's step budget. The candidate's only sensitivity arm varies N at infinite data and does not vary training duration at fixed N. **The question does NOT convert this into a reported quantity** (no training-length arm is declared), so the measurand exemption does not reach it. This is the one sourced leg of attack 4. It is CONTESTED (sourced one way, with the candidate's design silent), not FALSE, and S1's regime (random chains per sequence, in-context optimum) differs from the candidate's fixed-source regime, so I grade attack 4 **Partial**, not Unresolved.
6. **Measurand exemption DECLARED:** the clause "a strictly positive floor would be indistinguishable from data entropy in every published fit" is what the question exists to test; it is a measurand, and I do not grade it as a presupposition.
