# Gate 4 — Adversary #1 log — UQ-20261030-03

## Restatement (my own words, before reading the record's framing back)

Take one lytic virus and one permissive adherent human cell line. Infect cells one at a time, in a synchronised window just after each cell has divided, and then kill each cell separately and count how many *infectious* virions (PFU) it made. Two numbers are wanted. (i) How heavy-tailed is that per-cell yield — expressed as the negative-binomial dispersion parameter k, with a confidence interval, the same parameter epidemiology fits to per-person onward transmission. (ii) How much of that spread was already decided before the virus arrived — measured as the correlation of log yield between two sister cells that came from the same division and were infected in parallel, scored against non-sister pairs matched on cell-cycle phase and dose. A near-zero sister correlation means the high-output cell is made at the moment of infection; a high one means a pre-infection cell state predicts it.

## DEPTH CHECK — **Pass**

**Object test completion:** the answer would be a fact about *individual infected human cells and the infectious virions each one produces, and about how much of that output two freshly divided sister cells have in common.* Cells and virions are on the pass list.

**Deletion test:** if every paper, database and protocol vanished tonight, an infected cell would still make the number of infectious virions it makes, and two sisters would still either resemble each other in that number or not. The dispersion and the sister correlation are properties of the cell population, not of anybody's records. Pass.

**Rows test:** one row is one infected cell with its titred infectious output; one row of limb (ii) is one sister pair. Not a paper, record, certificate or run. Pass.

The record's own "honest wrinkle" (the ICC is defined against a matched null, so it could be called a fact about the design) does not fail the block: matching is the instrument and the variance component is the estimand. Depth is **not** my killing ground.
