Domain(s): Biology — developmental biology and organ-size control; quantitative neuroanatomy and whole-organ atlas construction. Adjacent: compositional data analysis, variance-component estimation, canalization/quantitative genetics, morphological/phenotypic integration (the method-owning field).

Question (canonical, Gate 0 as intaken):
Across genetically identical, identically reared individuals, is the absolute cell number of an organ regulated as a total — with compensatory negative covariance between its constituent regions or cell types — or are the parts regulated independently, so that the variance of the total is the sum of the parts' variances?

Question (canonical, Gate 1 reformulation — the version graded; one reformulation used, for well-posedness only):
In a cohort of n genetically identical, identically reared individuals, with a nested technical-replicate layer and a pre-declared partition granularity, does the across-individual covariance matrix of absolute regional cell counts of one organ carry negative off-diagonal structure large enough to put Var(total) significantly below SUM Var(parts) once the technical component is subtracted — and does the sign replicate in a second line or species and hold under both an atlas partition and a registration-free marker partition?
Reason for reformulation: the intake version asserts "regulated as a total," which the intake's own fourth presupposition concedes is not identifiable from covariance (negative covariance is equally consistent with neutral partitioning of a shared precursor pool). The reformulation states the question purely as a variance-structure question, which is what the stated evidence can settle. Both versions are recorded; the reformulation is strictly weaker in claim and identical in evidence demanded.

Alternative formulations:
- When one brain region of an inbred mouse happens to have fewer cells than average, do other regions have more?
- Does the across-individual covariance matrix of whole-organ cell counts carry the negative structure a conserved total implies, and is the total's coefficient of variation smaller than that of any of its parts?

Motivating gap (as intaken, and its defect):
Intaken claim: what is newly measurable is the COVARIANCE, not the counts; classical stereology sampled one or a few regions per animal, so it could report per-region means and CVs but "could not estimate cross-region covariance within individuals," leaving a field dense in CVs and empty of the one statistic that separates a regulated total from independently regulated parts.
DEFECT FOUND AT GATE 2, and it is load-bearing: this premise is false. The isotropic fractionator (Herculano-Houzel & Lent 2005) counts every dissected structure of a single brain, and Herculano-Houzel et al. 2015 (opened, read) used it on 19 same-stock, same-week-born mice to estimate exactly the across-individual between-structure cell-number covariance the candidate calls newly measurable, and published the pairwise correlation table. Separately, Elkind et al. 2023 (opened, read) already hold 369 inbred C57BL/6J brains with absolute cell counts over 532 atlas-registered regions per animal AND a split-hemisphere technical-noise layer — the candidate's stated precondition — so the instrument gap, the technical-layer gap and the n gap are all closed in print. What remains genuinely unpublished is narrower than the candidate claims: the explicit Var(total) vs SUM Var(parts) contrast with intervals, the interpreted sign of the full region-region covariance matrix, and cross-line replication of that sign.

Presuppositions (status after Gate 2):
1. Automated whole-organ counts have per-animal technical error characterisable enough to separate biological from shared technical covariance. CONTESTED at intake; now ESTABLISHED as feasible — Elkind et al. 2023: "Cell count estimates in the right and left hemispheres served as a proxy for technical noise," with regions exceeding a 15.5% left-right difference excluded (33 regions dropped). The candidate's "precondition, not an improvement" framing is correct and is already standard practice in the nearest dataset.
2. The partition is well defined and identical across individuals. CONTESTED, and the candidate's pre-declaration of two partitions (atlas + marker-defined) does not fully repair it. A further defect the candidate does not address: Var(total) − SUM Var(parts) = 2·SUM_{i<j}Cov(i,j) is not partition-invariant in GRANULARITY, not merely in boundary placement. Five dissected structures (Herculano-Houzel 2015) and 532 Allen regions (Elkind 2023) can return opposite verdicts on the same brains, because SUM Var(parts) shrinks relative to the covariance sum as the partition refines. The question must pre-declare a granularity or report the statistic as a function of it; as posed it does neither. This is the main well-posedness deduction.
3. Genetically identical, identically reared individuals are available at usable n. ESTABLISHED and stronger than intaken: 369 inbred C57BL/6J brains in one published cohort.
4. Negative covariance is identifiable as evidence about regulation. CONTESTED and correctly flagged by the intake; handled by the Gate 1 reformulation, which drops the mechanism claim.
5. Published regional cell-count phenotypes are interpreted per region, so a compositional covariance structure would change analysis and power. PARTLY ESTABLISHED, PARTLY WEAKENED: Elkind et al. already benchmark every per-region effect against inter-individual variability ("inter-individual variability was always greater than the effect size of a single qualifier"), so the field is less naive about the spread than the intake assumes, though it still does not model the total as a constraint.
6. Residual environmental variation among "identically reared" animals (litter, rack position, cage) is measurable and blockable. CONTESTED, unaddressed by any prior opened; neither Herculano-Houzel 2015 nor Elkind 2023 blocks or models litter/cage as a shared factor, so this limb of the design is genuinely novel even though the question is not.

What would count as an answer:
An estimated across-individual covariance matrix of absolute counts over n isogenic individuals with a nested technical-replicate layer, plus a pre-declared comparison of Var(total) against SUM Var(parts) with intervals, and CV(total) against CV of each part. "Regulated as a total" = Var(total) significantly below the sum once the technical component is subtracted. "Independent parts" = indistinguishable from the sum. "Neither" = positive residual covariance beyond the technical estimate, pointing at a shared growth factor rather than compensation — a distinct, reportable third answer. Declared in advance: the sign must replicate in a second line or species, and the result must be reported under both the atlas and the registration-free partition. Gate 1 adds: and at a pre-declared partition granularity, or as a curve over granularity, since the statistic is not granularity-invariant.
Note on answer-criteria status: these criteria are crisp and pre-declarable — the candidate is NOT rejected for ill-posedness. It is rejected because the question they would settle has already been put and answered once, at lower power.

PRIOR 1 — Herculano-Houzel S., Messeder D.J., Fonseca-Azevedo K., Pantoja N.A. (2015), "When larger brains do not have more neurons: increased numbers of cells are compensated by decreased average cell size across mouse individuals," Frontiers in Neuroanatomy 9:64. https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fnana.2015.00064/pdf — OPENED AND READ.
- UNIT OF ANALYSIS: the individual mouse. n = 19 adult male Swiss mice, all ~2 months old, all born in the same week (one stock, common rearing).
- ENDPOINT: absolute neuron and non-neuron cell NUMBER per CNS structure — cerebral cortex incl. hippocampus, cerebellum, olfactory bulb, "remaining areas" (brainstem/diencephalon/basal ganglia), spinal cord — all measured in the SAME individual by isotropic fractionator, plus whole-brain totals.
- REFERENCE/COMPARATOR: across-individual, between-structure comparison within the stock; and whole brain against its structures.
- ESTIMAND: the pairwise across-individual correlation of cell number between structures. Reported: cerebellum–olfactory bulb rho = 0.4734 (p = 0.0071); cortex–spinal cord rho = 0.3796 (p = 0.0321); rest-of-brain–spinal cord rho = −0.4820 (p = 0.0060); cortex–cerebellum not significant. Stated conclusion: neuron numbers are "regulated and allocated independently to different brain structures," with "no evidence of compensatory changes in the distribution of neurons across brain structures." Max/min neuron ratios: cortex 1.77x, cerebellum 2.90x, olfactory bulb 4.33x, remaining areas 3.48x, whole brain 1.63x — the total varies less than any part, which is the candidate's second limb, present as a ratio rather than as a CV.

Significance — consequence: Real but partly discharged. If the covariance structure is negative enough to put Var(total) below SUM Var(parts), then every per-region cell-count phenotype is a compositional readout against a constrained total, and the large literature of single-region count comparisons is both mis-powered and mis-interpreted — a change in how such studies are analysed. If indistinguishable from the sum, per-region analysis is licensed and Herculano-Houzel's 2015 conclusion is confirmed at proper power and in an isogenic line. The third outcome — positive residual covariance beyond the technical estimate — would point at a shared systemic growth factor and would make "identically reared" a measurement claim rather than a husbandry one. The discount: one answer is already in print, so the consequence of the yes-branch is "overturn an under-powered published negative," not "settle an open question."
Significance — audience: quantitative-neuroanatomy groups building region-resolved count atlases (the Zeisel cohort above; CUBIC/CUBIC-Cloud and ClearMap user communities), mouse-phenotyping consortia that report per-region counts as phenotypes, and developmental organ-size-control labs that argue about progenitor-pool limitation (the Stanger/Melton pancreas-vs-liver line). They would act by modelling the total as a constraint in region-level tests, by powering on covariance rather than means, and by reporting the covariance matrix they already compute instead of consuming it inside an embedding.
Significance — fecundity: once the sign is fixed, it becomes askable whether the constraint is imposed at the progenitor-pool stage or later; whether it is heritable, i.e. whether the covariance structure itself differs between lines; whether a perturbation (diet, enrichment, early stress) moves the total or only redistributes it; and whether the same structure appears in non-neural organs with clonal cell lineages.
Significance — anchoring: the concerted-versus-mosaic brain-evolution dispute, currently argued across species, brought inside a single genotype; canalization and developmental stability in quantitative genetics; Herculano-Houzel's cell-number/cell-size compensation, which is the same compensation logic applied to a different pair of variables; and the progenitor-pool-limitation results in organ-size control.
Significance score: 2/3 — argued, with a named audience and concrete actions, but one limb of the consequence is already answered in print and the "field treats the spread as mere noise" premise is weakened by Elkind et al. explicitly benchmarking every effect against inter-individual variability.

Reason for silence: Not needed to carry this entry, since the question was not silent — it was asked in 2015. The relevant mechanism is for the UNDER-REPORTING of the statistic rather than for silence about the question: region-resolved count atlases are funded to detect effects (genotype, sex, drug, behaviour), so the across-individual covariance matrix is computed as nuisance structure or as an embedding input and never promoted to an estimand — Elkind et al. 2023 is the clean instance, computing the matrix and spending it on a tSNE. A disciplinary boundary compounds it: the Var(sum)-vs-SUM-Var statistic belongs to morphological/phenotypic integration and variance-component estimation, fields that work on sizes and distances and do not read whole-organ counting papers, while the counting field does not read theirs. Under the ledger's own rule, the existence of a credible under-reporting mechanism but no credible silence mechanism is itself corroboration of the Low novelty grade rather than a defence of the candidate.
