## Question (canonical)

**Version B (Gate 1 reformulation — the canonical form; one permitted reformulation used, for well-posedness only):**
For one pretraining recipe at a scale at which several parallelism configurations are feasible, run a crossed design of C ≥ 3 parallelism configurations × S ≥ 3 seeds, holding global batch size, the sequence and membership of global batches, seed, numeric formats and token count fixed, and restricting C in advance to the *matchable set* — configuration changes that provably do not alter the mathematical objective, excluding changes that do (e.g. tensor-parallel degrees under a vocab-parallel loss whose normalisation is computed on the local vocabulary, Megatron-LM #7464, or TP degrees that do not divide the vocabulary, #1754): what are the between-configuration and between-seed variance components, each with a confidence interval, and what interval covers their ratio σ²_config/σ²_seed, for (a) held-out loss at a fixed token count and (b) each member of a pre-registered downstream metric set; is the upper confidence limit of σ²_config/σ²_seed below 1 (the invariance verdict); and which configuration axis — device/rank count, micro-batch count, or sharding style (TP/PP/SP/EP placement of reductions) — carries any excess?

**Version A (Gate 0 intake, recorded as required):**
Holding recipe, data order, seed, global batch size and numeric formats fixed, is the trained model invariant to the parallelism configuration — and if not, how does parallelism-induced divergence in held-out loss and in a pre-registered downstream metric set compare with the seed-to-seed divergence the field already treats as its noise floor?

Reason for the reformulation: Version A asks whether the trained model "is invariant", which is trivially false at the bit level and so answerable in a sentence; it also compares two point estimates ("divergence vs divergence") when the comparator is itself estimated with error, and it does not say which configuration changes are admissible. Version B converts the target into a variance-components contrast with an interval on the ratio, fixes the endpoints, and pre-declares the matchable set. Both versions are recorded; Version B is canonical.

## Alternative formulations

1. Is "same recipe, different GPU layout" the same experiment, and by how much is it not?
2. How much of the difference between two training runs is attributable to how the run was sharded across devices rather than to anything in the recipe?
3. (systems vocabulary) The elastic-training literature ships a binary predicate — a reconfiguration either "preserves accuracy consistency" or is "relaxed consistency". What is the magnitude behind the predicate, and where does it sit relative to seed noise?

## Motivating gap

The inverted presupposition is the one every distributed-training paper and framework relies on and none measures: that the parallelism configuration is a throughput knob leaving the computed object unchanged once global batch, data order, seed and formats are fixed. Gate 2 established that the field does **not** actually believe this, and still does not measure it:

- FlexTrain (MLSys 2026, oral) states that elastic training "has seen little production use because of accuracy inconsistency", prefers changing the pipeline-parallel degree "to preserve deterministic computation and maintain accuracy consistency", and offers data-parallel scaling only "when accuracy consistency is relaxed". So non-invariance is a named production blocker with a two-tier engineering predicate attached — and no magnitude, no comparator, no seeds anywhere on the page read.
- NVIDIA's own NeMo-RL guide quantifies one instance in a deployed stack: reward-model validation loss 0.6035 / 0.6010 / 0.5864 / 0.6021 at TP = 1 / 2 / 4 / 8, "different TP plans lead to slight but noticeable inconsistencies in the validation loss", cause attributed to "how different TP plans partition and aggregate computations across devices" and, for row-wise plans, to floating-point addition being non-associative so "changing the summation order can change results". n = 1 per TP degree; no seed replicates; no interval.
- Tenplex (arXiv:2312.05181) asserts the invariance that the candidate questions — "the convergence of the DL job is unaffected", "convergence is equally unaffected" for pipeline and tensor parallelism — on eyeballed loss curves (Fig. 16), one run per arm, no numeric loss difference reported, no seeds, no variance.

So the mechanism claims that were NOT SOURCED at intake are now read out of specific named stacks: Megatron-LM (#1754 "Tensor parallelism produces divergent loss with non-divisible vocabulary sizes"; #7464 vocab-parallel label smoothing using the *local* vocabulary size and local mean log-probability — a semantic, not merely numeric, dependence on TP degree; #5176 inconsistent loss across runs with identical configurations), NVIDIA NeMo-RL (TP plan → validation loss, with FP non-associativity named), and DeepSpeed (#5717: under ZeRO 1/2/3 multi-GPU, *different seeds produced the exact same loss curve*, while single-GPU runs did not — the parallelism configuration can also suppress seed variation, which bears directly on the yardstick). What remains unmeasured is the magnitude as a variance component against the seed floor, and which axis carries it.

The secondary disclosure limb is weaker than intake assumed. "(Mis)Fitting: A Survey of Scaling Laws" (arXiv:2502.18969), opened: the survey reports no parallelism or hardware disclosure rate at all and never gives TP/PP/DP degrees or device counts; its nearest numbers are 40 of 51 papers "with a description of training setup", 23 of the surveyed papers not describing the optimisation process, 15 not describing how FLOPs/parameters are counted, and 19 of 42 with analysis code. The confounds it does discuss (from Porian et al. 2024) are warmup, decay, parameter counting and hyperparameters — none hardware or parallelism. That is a usable negative (the quantity is not published, and the frame to compute it in exists), but the "published ladders change parallelism with scale" presupposition stays unsourced.

## Presuppositions

1. **Non-invariance mechanisms are real in deployed stacks (reduction order and accumulation tree change with parallelism degree).** NOW ESTABLISHED, in two vendor stacks, sourced: NeMo-RL guide ("partition and aggregate computations across devices"; non-associativity of FP addition; TP → validation-loss table) and Megatron-LM #1754/#7464. Intake flagged this as unsourced; it no longer is.
2. **A parallelism change can be made while holding global batch, data order, seed and numeric formats fixed.** CONTESTED and now sharpened rather than resolved. FlexTrain's own design concedes the asymmetry — PP changes keep computation deterministic, DP changes require "relaxed consistency" — and Megatron-LM #7464 shows some TP changes alter the *objective*, not only the summation order. Version B therefore pre-declares a matchable set and excludes objective-altering changes; within-batch grouping cannot be held fixed when micro-batch count changes, but that is the treatment under test, not a defect.
3. **Seed-to-seed divergence is an appropriate yardstick.** CONTESTED, and newly complicated: DeepSpeed #5717 reports multi-GPU ZeRO runs in which different seeds gave identical loss curves, i.e. the seed floor is itself configuration-dependent, so the yardstick must be estimated *within each configuration* and not borrowed. Version B does this by crossing C × S. A second pre-declared yardstick ("smallest published difference the field acts on") remains advisable.
4. **Divergence, if present, does not self-cancel over training.** OPEN. Unaffected by anything opened; Version B measures both endpoints (loss and downstream set) for exactly this reason.
5. **Published ladders do change parallelism with scale, and disclose enough to tell.** CONTESTED, and the disclosure half is now partly answered in the negative: (Mis)Fitting reports no parallelism/hardware disclosure rate and lists no device counts, so the retrospective version of the question is bounded by non-disclosure. This limb should be demoted to a secondary, clearly-labelled sub-question.
6. **This is not UQ-20261016-53 in new clothes.** HELD, on the stated estimand. UQ-20261016-53's unit is a published headline claim, endpoint is column estimability under a reconstructed design matrix, comparator is an alias/VIF threshold, estimand is a population share plus inter-coder kappa. This candidate's unit is a training run, endpoint is held-out loss and a downstream metric set, comparator is the within-configuration seed component, estimand is a variance ratio with an interval from new runs. Disjoint. BUT: the ladder/disclosure limb (presupposition 5) *is* an instance of -53's genre, and -53 was rejected. The limb must be carried as a labelled secondary at most, and Gate 4 should test whether the candidate leans on it.

## What would count as an answer

A crossed C ≥ 3 configurations × S ≥ 3 seeds paired experiment at a scale where several configurations are feasible, everything in the matchable set held fixed, reporting a variance-components decomposition with intervals: σ²_config, σ²_seed, and an interval on their ratio, for held-out loss at fixed token count and for each member of a pre-registered downstream metric set. **Invariant** if the upper confidence limit of σ²_config/σ²_seed lies below 1 on all declared endpoints; otherwise the answer is the magnitude and the axis decomposition (rank count vs micro-batch count vs sharding style), reported per endpoint since loss may converge while downstream metrics reorder. Secondary, cheaper and clearly labelled as such: the disclosure rate of per-rung parallelism in published scaling ladders, which bounds whether the retrospective version is checkable at all.

## Novelty — nearest prior formulations (3, with sources, all opened)

**P1. VirtualFlow: Decoupling Deep Learning Models from the Underlying Hardware** — arxiv.org/pdf/2009.09523 (2020), opened.
- Unit of analysis: one training job under one hardware configuration (one run per configuration as reported).
- Endpoint: final validation accuracy (ResNet-50 top-1; BERT-BASE GLUE task accuracy) plus per-step convergence curves in figures.
- Reference/comparator: a literature target value (ResNet 76.26 top-1; BERT targets from the authors' own large-batch runs) and naive TensorFlow with batch size scaled by GPU count (TF*).
- Estimand: not formally stated; implicitly whether final accuracy and the convergence trajectory are invariant to GPU count and GPU type at fixed global batch size, judged by closeness to a target within an asserted ±0.5% tolerance.
- What it reports: at global batch 8192, top-1 = 75.92 (1 GPU), 75.96 (2), 75.99 (4), 75.83 (8), 75.68 (16), 76.01 (2× RTX 2080Ti) — a spread of ~0.33 points; GLUE spreads QNLI 90.86–91.05, SST-2 91.86–92.35, CoLA 82.45–84.08. States VirtualFlow "preserves not only the final accuracy but also the entire convergence trajectory". No seeds, no repeated runs, no variance decomposition, no confidence intervals; the ±0.5% tolerance is asserted without derivation.

**P2. Tenplex: Dynamic Parallelism for Deep Learning using Parallelizable Tensor Collections** — arxiv.org/pdf/2312.05181 (SOSP 2024), opened.
- Unit of analysis: a single training job that undergoes a resource/parallelism change at step 100.
- Endpoint: the training-loss trajectory over steps (BERT-large on OpenWebText).
- Reference/comparator: the same job run without a resource change.
- Estimand: a qualitative invariance predicate — "the convergence of the DL job is unaffected" — judged visually from loss curves (Fig. 16). Reports no numeric loss difference, no final quality, no seed variance, no significance test. §2.3 concedes that a naive change means "the final trained model may have e.g., a different accuracy".

**P3. FlexTrain: Scalable Hybrid-Parallel Training with Elastic Resource Utilization and Consistent Accuracy** — mlsys.org/virtual/2026/oral/3776 (MLSys 2026 oral), abstract page opened; full paper not read.
- Unit of analysis: an elastic training job under a scheduler.
- Endpoint: throughput/speedup (up to 1.73× consistent, 2.27× relaxed), with accuracy consistency as a side constraint.
- Reference/comparator: non-elastic scheduling.
- Estimand: a binary consistency predicate attached to the configuration-change *type* — PP changes "preserve deterministic computation", DP scaling requires "relaxed consistency" — never a magnitude, and the page defines neither the tolerance nor the measurement. No mention of seeds or loss equivalence.
- Also bearing on Gate 2: it names "accuracy inconsistency" as a reason elastic training "has seen little production use", i.e. the field states the problem and leaves the quantity blank.

Also opened and bearing on novelty but not graded as priors: the NVIDIA NeMo-RL DTensor TP accuracy guide (deployed-stack instance, n=1 per TP, reward-model fine-tuning, no seed comparator) and (Mis)Fitting (arXiv:2502.18969, the disclosure-limb frame, which does not contain the quantity). **PolyPythias** (fifty Pythia pre-training runs; the natural source of the seed yardstick) was surfaced at query 10 but NOT opened — fetch budget exhausted — so it grounds no grade here and is flagged for Gate 4.

## Novelty — how this differs from each

- **vs P1 (VirtualFlow):** the closest match found, and genuinely close on the measured object — accuracy across device counts at fixed global batch. It differs on three of four attributes. Comparator: a literature target value, not the within-configuration seed component. Estimand: closeness to a target inside an asserted tolerance, not a variance ratio with an interval. Design: n = 1 per configuration, so the 0.33-point ResNet spread cannot be separated from run-to-run noise at all — exactly the comparison the candidate exists to make. Treatment axis: device count and device type under a virtualisation layer *built to erase the effect*, not TP/PP/SP/EP sharding style, micro-batch count or collective implementation as crossed factors. Scale and era: 2020 CV/BERT-base, not LLM pretraining. VirtualFlow is prior art on the presupposition (someone has compared configurations) and not on the estimand (nobody has decomposed the variance).
- **vs P2 (Tenplex):** same treatment factor (parallelism reconfiguration), same endpoint family (loss), opposite epistemic posture. Tenplex asserts invariance as a correctness property of its own system and demonstrates it by eye on one run per arm; the candidate asks for the magnitude of the residual with replicates and intervals, and adds the downstream endpoint Tenplex never measures. Tenplex's comparator is "the same job without a change", which cannot answer "is the change smaller than the seed".
- **vs P3 (FlexTrain):** FlexTrain converts the question into a design constraint and routes around it (change PP, not DP) rather than measuring it. Its endpoint is throughput; consistency is a binary predicate on change type. The candidate asks for the quantity FlexTrain's predicate is standing in for, and its C × S design would tell FlexTrain whether its "relaxed consistency" DP mode costs anything detectable above seed noise.
- **vs the in-ledger template (UQ-20261016-49):** that entry crosses numeric format against seed variance in matched pretraining runs; this one crosses parallelism configuration against seed variance. Same comparator architecture, different and non-overlapping treatment factor. Precedent, not duplication.
- **vs UQ-20261016-53:** disjoint on all four attributes for the primary estimand (run vs published claim; variance ratio vs column estimability; seed component vs alias threshold; new runs vs literature share). The ladder/disclosure limb overlaps -53's genre and must stay secondary and labelled.

## Significance — consequence

If the answer is **invariant** (upper limit of σ²_config/σ²_seed below 1 on all endpoints): the field's regression-test practice is vindicated and, for the first time, calibrated — framework teams get a published tolerance to set their "loss curves match" gates against instead of eyeballing, cross-configuration comparisons become defensible, and FlexTrain's "relaxed consistency" tier can be declared free. If the answer is **not invariant**: every comparison whose arms differ in layout carries a treatment-correlated nuisance factor, which bites hardest where parallelism moves with the treatment — scaling ladders (small rungs on one node, large rungs across many), and recipe ablations run at one sharding and shipped as general advice, a candidate mechanism for the field's documented non-transfer of recipe results. Release notes asserting "no convergence impact" become unquantified claims, and the axis decomposition says what to fix: if the excess sits on micro-batch count, a fixed accumulation tree fixes it; if on rank count, deterministic collectives do; if on sharding style, the invariance claim has to be weakened in the frameworks' own documentation.

## Significance — audience

(i) Distributed-training framework teams (Megatron-LM, DeepSpeed, TorchTitan, JAX/MaxText, NeMo) who own the regression tests the folklore lives in, and who would set gate thresholds from a measured floor rather than by eye — NVIDIA has already written a guide on this for one axis without the number. (ii) Elastic-training systems researchers, who currently ship a binary consistency predicate (FlexTrain) or an eyeballed curve (Tenplex) as their correctness argument and would get a protocol and a yardstick; FlexTrain itself asserts the unmeasured quantity is what keeps elasticity out of production. (iii) Scaling-law practitioners, who would have to report per-rung parallelism — and, if the component is non-negligible, re-examine fits whose rungs changed layout. (iv) Reproducibility and evaluation methodologists assembling a total error budget for a published model number, who currently have the seed term (PolyPythias, Signal-and-Noise) and the inference-nondeterminism term (UQ-20261016-06) and no training-layout term.

## Significance — fecundity

Does the configuration component grow or shrink with scale — if it grows, large ladders are worse than they look and the retrospective confound is worse than the prospective one? Is it monotone in rank count, giving a cheap extrapolation rule? Does it concentrate on a single axis, so one invariant-collective or fixed-accumulation-tree change buys invariance? What does a bitwise-invariant *training* mode cost in throughput — the training analogue of batch-invariant inference kernels? Should the parallelism configuration be part of a model's identity for reproducibility claims, as the harness version now is for evaluations? Does the configuration component predict downstream-metric rank reordering at equal loss, i.e. is the loss endpoint sufficient? And is the seed floor itself configuration-dependent, as DeepSpeed #5717 suggests — which would make "seed variance" an ill-defined constant and require it to be reported per layout?

## Reason for silence

A disciplinary boundary with an incentive gap on both sides of it, which is why the quantity sits in folklore and release notes. The systems community owns the configuration change but needs only a pass/fail regression gate: one run per configuration, compared by eye, is the cheapest thing that satisfies a reviewer, and a null result ("nothing changed") is a non-publishable outcome for a systems paper whose contribution is throughput. The ML-science community owns replication and varies seeds, but treats the parallelism configuration as infrastructure chosen by the cluster scheduler rather than a scientific factor, so it never enters the design matrix. Nobody's venue rewards the C × S crossed design, whose cost is multiplicative in full pretraining runs and whose headline is either "your layout doesn't matter" or "other people's published comparisons are confounded". The method that would settle it is owned by a third field entirely (variance components / repeatability-vs-reproducibility studies in metrology; processor-count ensemble-consistency testing in climate modelling), and query 8 shows that vocabulary has never been pointed at training runs. Compounding it: the practitioners best placed to notice are the ones whose regression tests already absorb the effect, declaring it "within noise" without the noise ever having been estimated.
