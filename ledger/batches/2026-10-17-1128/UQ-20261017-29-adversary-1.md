# UQ-20261017-29 — Adversary 1 log

## Restatement (my own words)
Take every public SFT and preference dataset (pinned to a dated snapshot) and treat the union as one object. (a) How much do the datasets overlap at the example level once formatting is stripped and a near-duplicate rule is applied at three thresholds — pairwise, as a deduplicated pool size, and as "how many datasets does each distinct example sit in"? (b) For a few published mixtures (Tulu-style), if you charge each example to its components under a declared rule, do the component proportions and their rank order change versus the declared weights? (c) Repeat (a) stating how much of the pool could not be fetched.

In short: is the open post-training "pool" mostly re-exports, and do declared mixture weights therefore weight records rather than content?
